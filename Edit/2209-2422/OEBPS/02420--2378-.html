Chapter 2378 Ugly Face

Zhang Yu missed all of Taliyah's skills, and his mentality was a bit broken.

Lin Feng's Kaiyin fared even worse. After transforming into assassin form, he was as fragile as paper. He was left with very low health after being hit by the cannon a few times and could only shrink back into his shadow.

Meanwhile, the rest of Wang Ling's team were not idle either.

Guo Hao's Zelas activated his ultimate ability, Arcane Ritual, from a distance, unleashing three ultra-long-range projectiles that all landed precisely on Varus!

Xia Zhinuan's Neeko transforms into a little cannon to confuse the opponent, and then suddenly flashes into the fray to activate her ultimate skill, [Rage]!

The team fight was one-sided.

Ten seconds later.

Velus was the first to fall.

"The gunner Mailin has destroyed the Arrow of Punishment!"

Immediately afterwards, Kai was killed.

Taliyah was killed by the cannon.

Ornn, despite his tough skin and high health, couldn't withstand the combined damage of five people and eventually fell to the ground.

Sona, now with only a sliver of health remaining, fled back to the fountain.

"Aced!"

Total wipeout!

The system's rousing notification sound echoed throughout the canyon.

The viewing hall was deathly silent.

Then, a deafening roar erupted!

"Holy crap! That little cannon! His movement is like a ghost!"

"How can he be so good at dodging? He dodged all the attacks!"

"Did you see his movement speed? He's practically a blur behind him!"

"And that healing...it heals while I'm fighting? What kind of rune is that?!"

Chen Chao was so excited in his voice that he was almost incoherent: "Lingzi! Awesome! Absolutely awesome! It was so much fun using my Udir as a cannonball! It shot through in a flash! Haha!"

Kobayashi Yu exclaimed admiringly, "Lord Wang, your tactics are truly remarkable!"

Xia Zhinuan looked at the little cannon on the screen, its feet moving with incredible speed, her feelings complicated.

This person is ridiculously strong.

Moreover, the unquestionable confidence he exuded when giving instructions was completely different from Luo Xinghe's affectedly assertive performance.

...

Luo Xinghe stared at his black-and-white screen, his fingers trembling.

0/1/0.

He gave away first blood again.

And it was in front of Sun Rong.

It's simply shameful.

What he found even more unacceptable was Wang Ling's incomprehensible actions.

That movement speed, that positioning, that health regeneration...

"He must have used some kind of trick again..." Luo Xinghe gritted his teeth: "A normal person couldn't possibly have that kind of reaction!"

He took a deep breath, forcing himself to calm down.

"It's alright, we have a good lineup, we can fight in the later stages," he said in a voice message, trying to reassure the team.

But none of his teammates responded.

Liu Kaichuan, Zhang Yu, and Lin Feng all fell silent.

In that team fight just now, they missed all their skills and were played like dogs by a Tristana.

This feeling of defeat is more painful than losing the game.

Sun Rong said softly, "Let's... develop first."

Her Sona has been resurrected and is walking outside.

Luo Xinghe glanced at her, a hint of annoyance flashing in his eyes.

In the team fight just now, Sun Rong's Sona provided healing and shields, but her crucial control and speed boosts always seemed to be a step too slow.

If she had accelerated earlier, I might have been able to create some distance between us.

If she can unleash a powerful ultimate ability, she might be able to control the little cannon.

But she didn't have any of them.

"Sun classmate," Luo Xinghe said, his tone relatively gentle, but the meaning behind his words was not so polite: "In the next team fight, you need to use your skills more timely. Especially your ultimate and E skill, as they are related to my output environment."

Sun Rong was stunned for a moment, then realized he was being sarcastic.

She pursed her lips, didn't refute, and simply nodded: "...I understand."

...

The match lasts for fifteen minutes.

Third round of Hextech Rune Draw.

Wang Ling looked at the position where the roulette wheel pointer stopped, his eyes flickering slightly.

【One After Another】: Every time you attack or cast a spell on the same hero for the third time, it will deal additional true damage and grant you a 15% movement speed bonus for 3 seconds.

The third rune.

Combined with the movement-based health regeneration from [Tap Dance] and [Endless Recovery].

In addition to the items he has already built: Attack Speed Boots, Zeal, Aether Spirit, and the newly acquired Stark Statikk Shiv.

A gameplay system centered around "extreme speed" has been fully formed.

Wang Ling returned to the city and acquired [Phantom Dance]: Provides a shield when health is below 30% and grants a movement speed bonus when attacking.

"That's about it," Wang Ling thought to himself.

He controlled the cannon to return to the lane.

On the other side, Luo Xinghe also upgraded his equipment.

Varus built Manamune and Berserker's Greaves, preparing for a poke build.

He glanced at the little cannon's item bar and scoffed, "Statikk Shiv and Phantom Dancer? No Infinity Edge? What a trash build."

Sun Rong whispered a reminder: "He moves very fast, his skills are hard to hit."

"What's the use of moving fast?" Luo Xinghe disagreed: "The damage is low and he can't hit the front row. Our Ornn is so weak, can he move?"

As if to prove his point, Liu Kai's Ornn had already built Sunfire Cape and Thornmail, with an astonishingly high armor level.

"In the team fight later, Ornn will be in the front, and I, Varus, will deal damage from the back. Taliyah and Kayn should look for opportunities to flank the backline. Sona, please protect me." Luo Xinghe instructed the tactics: "I'll handle their Tristana."

He spoke with great confidence.

But Sun Rong had a bad feeling.

...

The match lasted eighteen minutes.

The second large-scale team fight broke out in the middle of Howling Abyss.

This time, it was the Luo Xinghe team that initiated the attack.

Liu Kai's Ornn is calling for sheep again!

Lava goats galloped in!

Zhang Yu's Rock Sparrow cast a wall, blocking the road!

Lin Feng's Kaiyin sneaks into the shadows, his target Zelas and Neeko in the back row!

A perfect first move.

However, Wang Ling's cannon activated [Ghostly Step].

Movement speed increases dramatically in an instant!

Then, he controlled the cannon and started attacking.

The goal is not crispy skin.

It's Ornn.

"He ordered Ornn?" Luo Xinghe was stunned: "Has he lost his mind?"

Ornn has high armor, high health, and Sunfire Cape damage reflection.

With such low attack power, Tristana's attacks on Ornn are like tickling him.

But soon, Luo Xinghe's smile vanished.

Because the small cannon's movement speed is ridiculously fast.

Attack speed boots, Phantom Dancer, Statikk Shiv, Aether Spirit, plus the cycle bonus from Tap Dance and the burst movement speed from Ghost Step...

The cannon's movement speed began to increase at an astonishing rate.

600, 700, 800...

Furthermore, due to the [Successive Attacks] effect, every third attack that hits Ornn will trigger additional true damage and a 15% movement speed bonus.

Realistic damage ignores armor.

Ornn's health is actually dropping!

But what's even more terrifying is the little cannon's positioning.

It quickly became a phantom.

No one's skills could hit Wang Ling!

"How the hell am I supposed to fight this?!" Liu Kaichuan was going crazy.

He couldn't even touch the small cannon.

The little cannon, like a fly, buzzed around him, occasionally poking him.

It doesn't cause much damage, but it's extremely insulting.

Taliyah's skills are all useless.

Kayn tried to cut into the back row, but was held back by Udir and Riel.

Varus's Q skill requires charging, making it impossible to hit targets with such low movement speed.

Luo Xinghe's expression grew increasingly grim.

He finally realized something was wrong.

This little cannon's build and runes are not for dealing damage at all.

It's for the sake of extreme speed.

Speed, in this chaotic battle map, is itself a weapon.
