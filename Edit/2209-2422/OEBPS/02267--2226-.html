Chapter 2226 Evil People Will Be Punished by Other Evil People

The nun's voice wasn't loud, but it seemed to carry an invisible magic, giving the impression of being deafening.

Sun Rong turned her head slightly and saw that the other three male cultivators in the coffee shop were startled when they heard the sound. A look of curiosity appeared on their faces, and then they calmly returned to their work, their fingers slowly tapping on the keyboard. However, the speed at which they tapped the keyboard was much slower than before, obviously because they had been attracted by the sudden sound.

Clearly, listening to gossip is a common human trait.

You can go out for a coffee and catch a great show at the same time. This coffee is totally worth it!

Sun Rong thought to herself, a faint smile appearing on her lips.

"This girl is no ordinary person." Watching the amusing scene unfold downstairs, Liu Qingyi, dressed in a light purple dress, sat elegantly on the second-floor sofa, a fragrant cup of coffee in her hand. She took a small sip, a playful glint in her eyes. Her gaze held a worldly-wise composure, as if she could see through everything.

After all, she was a thousand years older than Sun Rong and Liu Qingyi, and her romantic experience was quite extensive.

Before meeting Wang Zhen, Liu Qingyi had also drifted through the long river of time, personally experiencing many different kinds of men. Those memories, whether sweet or bitter, were like twinkling stars in the night sky, leaving a deep imprint on her heart.

Of course, she has also witnessed the breakups and reunions of many of her friends, and those stories of joy and sorrow have given her a deeper understanding of love.

Based solely on the conversation between the boy and girl, she was immediately certain of one thing: the girl's dating experience was far more extensive than the boy's.

This move of "returning the token" may seem like a breakup on the surface, but it is actually a desperate measure to cut off the source of the problem and advance by retreating.

The goal is to make one's partner doubt themselves, thereby prompting them to deeply reflect on whether they have done anything wrong.

This guy clearly has little dating experience and is a "love-obsessed" person, and he's also someone who's very prone to self-destructive behavior.

A hint of confusion and helplessness was visible in his eyes.

Liu Qingyi used the "Team Transmission Technique" to analyze her conjectures to Sun Rong and Jiu Gong Liangzi. Her voice was soft yet firm, as if she were telling a story of a predetermined ending.

That is, even though the girl may seem to break up with you very readily on the surface, she may not actually mean it that way.

Sure enough, the moment the girl decisively handed over the wedding ring, the boy couldn't hold back his tears.

He gazed at the wedding rings on the table, recalling the sweet moments of their past relationship. Those beautiful memories flashed through his mind like a movie, filling him with pain and reluctance.

At that moment, the girl handed him a tissue, her expression changing from sharp and sarcastic to gentle, a sly glint in her eyes: "Don't cry. You're a grown man, aren't you ashamed? If outsiders see this, won't they say I'm bullying you?"

"Then... could we talk this over properly..." The male cultivator took the tissue handed to him, wiped his tears haphazardly, his voice trembling slightly, and his eyes revealing a hint of expectation.

Seeing that her goal had been achieved, the female cultivator smiled, her smile radiant: "Of course, otherwise why do you think I agreed to meet you today?"

The boy was silent for a moment, took a deep breath to calm himself down, and then said, "I'm sorry, I've just been under a lot of pressure lately, I shouldn't have said these things to you..." His voice was full of guilt and self-blame, like a child who had done something wrong.

"I also made mistakes. Baby, let's not fight anymore, okay?" The female cultivator held the male cultivator's hand, gently stroking the back of his hand with her fingers, and whispered.

"Okay." The male cultivator shook hands with him back, and a smile returned to his face.

"Since you were mean to me just now, shouldn't you give me some compensation?" Tu Qiongbi finally revealed her purpose after going around in circles, a hint of smugness flashing in her eyes.

"That...that's natural..." The male cultivator hesitated for a moment, then nodded, a hint of helplessness in his eyes.

"Baby, my younger brother is currently facing a bottleneck and needs a Rejuvenation Pill."

The female cultivator said in a tea-tea-like tone, with a hint of coquetry in her voice, "I know this pill isn't cheap, but you've always been the one who loves me the most, haven't you?"

A Guiyuan Pill costs 50,000 spirit stones. For wealthy young ladies like Sun Rong and Jiugong Liangzi, it's nothing special, but for ordinary cultivators, 50,000 spirit stones is a huge sum of money.

It should be known that 10,000 spirit stones can enable an ordinary cultivator's family to live a relatively affluent life for two months.

"Big brother, why don't you just refuse..." Sun Rong and Jiu Gong Liangzi watched everything happening downstairs, their eyes revealing a hint of anxiety and helplessness.

"He won't refuse." Seeing this, Liu Qingyi couldn't help but sigh, her eyes revealing a hint of pity and helplessness. Love-struck, truly hopeless.

"..." Sun Rong and Jiu Gong Liangzi couldn't help but open their mouths wide upon seeing this. For these two young ladies who were ignorant of the ways of the world and lacked certain experience in love, the scene in front of them was a bit too exciting.

Liu Qingyi's analysis was spot on!

With just a few words, the female cultivator had precisely controlled the male cultivator in front of her, placing herself in a superior position.

Sun Rong kept a close eye on the red countdown above the two people's heads. After the female cultivator's tugging, the numbers above their heads had been steadily increasing, first from a few minutes to three days, and then to a month. A hint of doubt and curiosity flashed in her eyes as she pondered the meaning of the countdown.

She now fully understands that this is the countdown to their romance.

Once the timer resets to zero, it means the relationship will be completely over.

But why are there different colors?

Sun Rong frowned slightly, deep in thought, a hint of confusion in her eyes.

The countdowns for Jiu Gong and Liu Qingyi were both gold, but the countdown for the male and female cultivators downstairs was red.

Does this foreshadow some kind of emotional crisis?

Just as Sun Rong was in a daze, she suddenly saw Liu Qingyi, who was drinking coffee next to her, gracefully flip over from the second floor and come to the front of the two cultivators.

Her movements were clean, crisp, and full of power.

Seeing the monk's submissive demeanor reminded her of her past self, and a feeling of sympathy and pity welled up in her heart.

I really couldn't bear to watch anymore.
