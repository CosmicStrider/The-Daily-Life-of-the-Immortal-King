Chapter 2317 Intelligent Game Theory

Zhao Qian's face was ashen. He forcibly suppressed his surging anger and stared at Liuli and the sixty people with a sinister look.

In this situation, the second game is crucial and cannot be lost!

He absolutely refused to believe that he could lose to another person of the same age in terms of breadth of knowledge and agility of thought.

"Lord Jingling!"

Zhao Qian stepped forward, his tone filled with suppressed dissatisfaction: "Intelligence games demand fairness and impartiality! I hope this test will truly treat everyone equally, and not let certain...unfounded reasons affect the judgment!"

His words were loaded with meaning, and everyone present understood them perfectly.

Liuli's little face fell, and she was a little unhappy.

This guy actually dared to question her impartiality?

"snort!"

She snorted and raised her head: "This realm's spirit has been in charge of the secret realm for a thousand years, and has its own rules! The test of wisdom tests one's comprehension and knowledge base, how can it be easily interfered with by external forces? Rest assured, this realm's spirit... will make a fair judgment!"

While she said "fair," she was secretly calculating her own options.

With a big shot here, how can helping their team be considered favoritism?

That's called following the will of Heaven!

Yes, that's right!

Outside the venue, teachers, students, and judges from various schools who were watching via a light screen were also discussing the event.

"Tsk tsk, Zhao Qian from Qingyun High School is getting anxious."

"How can I not be anxious? My face is practically swollen from being slapped. But then again, that spirit's bias in the last round was way too obvious..."

"Haha, I find this quite satisfying! This is how you deal with people who resort to dirty tricks!"

"Look at this clever little girl, Jingling. I wonder what kind of trouble she'll cause this test of wisdom."

Wang Muyu sat in the Sun family's viewing area, swinging his short legs, his mouth stuffed with a spirit fruit, and mumbled to the Sun family bodyguard next to him, "This Sister Liuli is so much fun!"

The Sun family bodyguard couldn't resist and secretly went up to rub the little wooden fish's head.

It's amazing how cute this little guy is; he even bears a resemblance to Miss Sun Rong...

...

Inside the secret realm, Liuli ignored Zhao Qian's fiery gaze.

With a wave of her hand, two simple stone answering platforms rose from the center of Mingxin Terrace, corresponding to Number 60 High School and Qingyun High School respectively.

"The second test, a battle of wits, begins now! The rules are as follows!"

Liu Li cleared her throat: "The spiritual generals of this realm will take turns posing questions, and both sides will answer at their respective answering stations. The questions will cover all aspects of cultivation. One point will be awarded for each correct answer, and no points will be awarded for incorrect answers or failure to answer within the time limit. The first team to obtain five points will win this round! Now, please have the first person from each side take their place!"

At Number 60 High School, the group exchanged glances.

"For the first question, let's play it safe. Li Jun, you go first," Sun Rong whispered the instructions.

Letting Li Jun score first can greatly boost his confidence and the team's morale.

Li Jun took a deep breath, nodded vigorously, and walked to the front of the answering table.

Having weathered the first and previous rounds of public opinion storms, his gaze was much more resolute than before.

Upon hearing this, Zhao Qian's gaze darkened.

Cultivation knowledge is his area of expertise!

Born into a family of cultivators, he read extensively from a young age. Coupled with the powerful spiritual consciousness brought about by his near-infant cultivation level, his deductive memory ability far surpassed that of his peers.

At this moment, Zhao Qian seemed to already see Li Jun scratching his ears and cheeks, making a complete fool of himself.

"This time, I'm personally involved!"

Full of confidence, Zhao Qian took a step forward, determined to get his revenge here.

"First question, please listen carefully! Li Jun from Number 60 High School, please prepare to answer!" Liuli's voice echoed in all directions.

"Question 1: Please briefly describe the specific content of the three stages of foundation building and explain their importance in consolidating the foundation."

The question caused a brief moment of silence, followed by a burst of exclamations.

This question... is way too basic!

The three stages of Foundation Establishment are crucial points that almost every Qi Refining cultivator is instructed on by their teachers when attempting to establish their foundation.

Detailed information can be found in any book on basic training.

This is practically a free point question!

Li Junming was visibly taken aback, seemingly not expecting it to be so simple.

He immediately composed himself and answered fluently: "The three stages of foundation building are condensing qi into liquid, opening the purple palace, and the initial manifestation of spiritual roots. Their importance lies in..."

His words were clear and well-organized. He not only answered the key points but also added his own understanding and insights, clearly demonstrating that he had truly grasped the essence of this stage.

"...Therefore, successfully passing through the three stages of foundation building is a solid foundation for future cultivation. That concludes my answer."

"Correct answer! One point is awarded for a score of 60!"

Liuli announced it almost immediately, a kind smile appearing on her small face.

...

Observation area outside the secret.

"puff!"

One of the teachers, who was drinking water, burst out laughing: "The three stages of Foundation Establishment? Is Jing Ling afraid he won't be able to answer the sixty questions?"

Another judge laughed and slapped his thigh: "But Li Jun answered really well. His foundation is very solid. He didn't just memorize it."

Everyone could see that the water was being released so much that it was almost like a waterfall!

...

On the Mingxin Platform, Zhao Qian's face was as black as the bottom of a pot.

He suppressed the urge to curse his mother and stared intently at Liuli.

"For the first question, Zhao Qian from Qingyun High School, please prepare to answer!"

Liuli turned to Zhao Qian, her kind expression vanishing instantly, replaced by a businesslike demeanor.

"Please explain the principle behind the phenomenon of 'stellar spiritual energy reversal' that may occur at the intersection of the energy nodes of 'Primordial Star Power' and 'Acquired Five Elements Spiritual Energy' when constructing the 'Zhou Tian Star Array', and state at least two solutions that ancient cultivators used to deal with this problem, and evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of the solutions."

Zhao Qian: "???"

The students of Number 60 High School: "???"

The audience, both inside and outside the venue, shouted: "!!!"

The entire scene fell into a deathly silence.

What the hell is this question?!

The Great Array of the Celestial Stars?

That's the legendary protective array of the ancient demon race's heavenly court!

Stellar psionic energy reversed?

Even many newbies haven't heard of this term, right?

What about the ancient cultivators' response?

Is this knowledge that should be mastered in high school?!

Zhao Qian opened his mouth, but felt his throat was dry and his mind went blank.

He prided himself on being well-read and from a family with a rich cultural heritage, but this topic was completely beyond his knowledge. He had only vaguely seen fragments of some of the concepts in the topic in some of the family's secret collections.

The Qingyun High School team members behind him were also dumbfounded, looking at each other blankly, none of them able to answer.

"What? Can't you answer?"

Liuli tilted her head, her tone cruel: "Time's almost up."

"You...you've gone beyond the syllabus! Seriously beyond the syllabus!" Zhao Qian roared, trembling with rage, pointing at Liuli.

"Beyond the syllabus?" Liuli blinked her innocent big eyes: "Intelligence game tests the depth and breadth of understanding of the path of cultivation. What syllabus is there to talk about? Could it be... that the geniuses of Qingyun High School only know how to read books by rote?"
