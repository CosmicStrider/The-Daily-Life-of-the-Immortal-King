Chapter 2275 Bai Zhe's Gaze

The immortal boat landed smoothly at the dedicated cultivation transportation hub in Songhai City.

The spiritual energy of Songhai is far less pristine and abundant than that of the inner world, but it still evokes a sense of relief and emotion in everyone present.

Stepping onto the familiar ground, everyone breathed in the exceptionally reassuring spiritual energy and breathed a sigh of relief.

Chen Chao stretched exaggeratedly: "Ah! It feels so good to be home! I feel my golden core is full of power again!"

"Come on, you slept the best on the way back, you almost drooled all over Brother Li's armor." Guo Hao mercilessly exposed him.

Upon hearing this, Li Changzhe immediately inspected his armor, whose luster had not yet been fully restored, with a pained expression, and muttered, "The repair bill is almost equal to my family's annual GDP! I'm never going to be at the front again..."

However, despite the heartache, everyone knows that all the magical artifacts damaged or lost during the mission in the Underworld will be completely destroyed at the Underworld Project base, ensuring that the mission participants do not suffer any inexplicable losses.

"Thank you all for your hard work on this exploration of the Earth's core."

As a social butterfly among various universities, Sun Rong naturally took on the responsibility of coordination: "The school has granted us three days off, so everyone should rest and recover. I will draft a basic version of the report, and everyone can supplement it according to their own experiences. Please pay attention to the confidentiality clause."

As she spoke, her gaze inadvertently drifted towards Wang Ling. This report was actually primarily written for Wang Ling; otherwise, she wouldn't have wanted to mention anything about the Earth's core project.

In the subsequent report, Sun Rong was required to "invisibly" assist Wang Ling in this operation, which required a bit of subtle and euphemistic writing.

Sun Rong was actually reluctant to do this, but for Wang Ling's sake, she felt it wouldn't hurt to sacrifice some of her brain cells.

Wang Lingzheng was looking down at his phone.

The message on the screen was from the owner of the snack shop he frequented, asking him what the "Ghost Salt and Pepper" and "Hellfire" flavors he had mentioned before tasted like. The owner had tried making them several times but they didn't quite work.

Wang Ling frowned slightly.

Because it's hard to describe.

Because the material samples from the inner world are controlled, they cannot be taken out. Even if Wang Ling could open a passage to get them directly, if the boss is reported, the source of these material samples may be traced, which would probably make things even more troublesome.

Wang Ling knew that Teng Luchen's suspicions had not disappeared; it was simply because Sun Yiyuan and his old friends were too powerful, making him hesitant to act rashly in the short term.

"Let's go, Reiko! What are you daydreaming about!"

Chen Chao put his arm around Wang Ling's shoulder: "Go back and get a good night's sleep! I'll be waiting for you in the game dungeon tonight!"

Wang Ling stiffened almost imperceptibly, silently shifting the priority of "thinking about the flavor description of instant noodles" to the back of his mind, and nodded.

Students from various schools were dispersed at the hub.

The students from Number 60 High School said goodbye to Li Changzhe from Jingmen Number 8 High School and Zhang Linyan from Dishitian High School, and agreed to keep in touch online in the future.

Wang Ling didn't use any supernatural powers. Like a wounded person who had truly "overdrawn" his spiritual energy, he obediently squeezed onto the spiritual energy bus back to the eastern suburbs with Sun Rong, Chen Chao, and Guo Hao.

The bus swayed and rocked, and the familiar street scene outside the window was breathtaking.

The setting sun cast a golden glow on the skyscrapers, vendors pushed carts loaded with low-grade spiritual fruits and called out their wares, and cultivators hurried off to and from get off work or school.

This kind of trivial yet authentic everyday life finally allowed Wang Ling's slightly tense nerves to relax completely.

While he was "unconscious" in the medical room at the Palm Cliff base, he had already used his divine sense to scan his home and confirm that everything was alright.

But seeing it in person still feels different.

The bus stopped at the station in the urban-rural fringe area. Wang Ling said goodbye to the noisy Chen Chao and Guo Hao, and watched as Sun Rong's luxurious car silently glided in front of her.

"Wang Ling." Before getting into the car, Sun Rong turned around, took out a cutely packaged box from her exquisite storage bracelet, and put it in his hand: "I still have a lot of these sweet crystal berry candied fruit. When we were at the center of the earth... thank you."

Her cheeks flushed slightly, and before Wang Ling could respond, she quickly jumped into the car.

Wang Ling stood there, holding the box that still carried a faint fragrance and was warm, watching the flying car disappear into the distance.

He looked down at the box, then thought about the unopened "Spicy Dragon Scale" flavored instant noodles in his pocket.

He hesitated for three seconds.

He opened the instant noodles and carefully put the candied fruit box into his pocket.

"Crack, crack..."

Chewing on the spicy and crispy pancake, Wang Ling slowly walked towards home.

This flavor makes him feel more at ease.

As I opened the door, the familiar, faint fragrance of medicinal plants wafted towards me.

"My darling son is back!" Wang's mother, wearing an apron, poked her head out of the kitchen, holding a bunch of shimmering chives in her hand. "Was the Earth's core fun? I heard it's dangerous? Are you alright? Let Mom see you!"

Mr. Wang rushed out of the study, his face filled with exaggerated worry: "Lingling! I heard you've run into a lot of trouble? Who dares to bully my son? Tell your father, and I'll immediately write him into a novel and arrange a thousand different ways for him to die a miserable death!"

Wang Ling: "..." He was used to it.

He shook his head, indicating that he was fine, just a little tired.

This statement perfectly fits his "seriously injured" persona.

"It's alright, it's alright!" Wang's mother breathed a sigh of relief, then chided, "You always make such a fuss whenever you go out. Today when I was buying groceries, someone even asked me if I had found some ancient heirloom outside. How come you're always so lucky..."

Wang Ling's lips twitched almost imperceptibly.

"Oh, right, go check on your sister!" Wang's father lowered his voice, his face mysterious: "Nuan Nuan seems to miss you a lot these past few days. She keeps looking in the direction of your room and making little noises, and she's even playing with a little rabbit made of shadow... although that rabbit looks a bit distorted and abstract..."

Wang Ling changed into slippers and headed towards the nursery.

Inside the crib, Wang Nuan was holding a baby bottle and drinking it happily.

Sensing Wang Ling's presence, she immediately released the bottle, her big, dark eyes sparkling as she looked over, waving her chubby little hands and making indistinct syllables.

The brother and sister exchanged only a glance, and everything that happened to Wang Ling in the Earth's core world converged into an incredibly clear torrent of memories, flowing directly into Wang Nuan's brain.

This experience wasn't important to Wang Ling himself, but it was a good opportunity for his younger sister to gain more learning experience. Wang Nuan was still young and needed to absorb more knowledge. Moreover, Wang Nuan was also aware of the information about Bai Zhe and the Outer God, so this information had to be shared with her as well.

In the upcoming battle of destiny, Wang Nuan will also be one of the important combat forces.

At this moment, seeing Wang Nuan looking somewhat "drunk" from absorbing a large amount of memories, a soft emotion quietly crept into Wang Ling's heart.

He walked over and gently touched his sister's soft hair.

Wang Nuan squinted her eyes in enjoyment, then reached out her small hand and grabbed one of Wang Ling's fingers.

In an instant, the shadow at Wang Ling's feet seemed to come alive, twisting slightly and splitting into a small ball. Then, that ball of shadow rolled on the ground and turned into the shape of a crispy noodle bag. It even tried to simulate a "crack" sound effect, but only made a faint rustling sound.

Wang Ling noticed that Wang Nuan's shadow acting skills were becoming more and more proficient.
