Chapter 2414 Before the Quarterfinals

The report's conclusion is simple: Wang Ling, early stage of Foundation Establishment, possesses outstanding martial arts talent, a skill rating of S+, and shows no signs of concealing his cultivation.

"No signs of concealing cultivation..." Luo Xinghe sneered, "What a lie."

He had personally witnessed Wang Ling's performance in the e-sports camp; that kind of skill and awareness was something that ordinary high school students could never achieve.

And today's match.

The technique of catching the leg and throwing was so exquisite that even the judges were amazed. Could this have been figured out by someone in the early stages of Foundation Establishment?

"Mo Xuan," Luo Xinghe said to the air.

"Here." Mo Xuan's voice came from the shadows.

"Have you read the report?"

"I've seen it."

What do you think?

Mo Xuan was silent for a few seconds, then said, "Two possibilities. First, Wang Ling is indeed a rare martial arts genius, with skills strong enough to make up for the gap in cultivation level. Second, he has some kind of hidden method that we cannot detect, which can even deceive the competition system."

Which possibility do you think is more likely?

"The second type." Mo Xuan said without hesitation: "While martial arts geniuses do exist, the skills this person has displayed have already exceeded the scope of geniuses. That kind of control over force and angle cannot be achieved without decades of hard training. He is only eighteen years old."

Luo Xinghe nodded: "That's what I think too."

"But the problem is, we have no evidence," Mo Xuan said. "All testing methods show that he is only at the early stage of Foundation Establishment. The judging panel has also given its official conclusion. If we continue to question him, it will only make us seem unreasonable."

Luo Xinghe clenched his fist.

He was unwilling to accept it.

After spending so much money and pulling so many strings, this is the result we got in the end?

"Is there any other way?" he asked.

Mo Xuan thought for a moment: "Yes, but the risk is very high."

"say."

"Let's test him directly," Mo Xuan said. "Arrange an accident to force Wang Ling to take action. If he is truly hiding his strength, it will definitely be exposed."

Luo Xinghe turned around and stared at the shadowy figure: "How exactly do we do it?"

"I will arrange for someone to create chaos at the quarterfinals next weekend," Mo Xuan said calmly. "At that time, Wang Ling will likely have to use his true strength to protect those around him. As long as he exposes himself once, we will have something to hold against him."

Luo Xinghe's eyes lit up.

But then he frowned: "What about the risks?"

"It's a big deal," Mo Xuan said. "If Wang Ling really has someone backing him, what we're doing is tantamount to a direct provocation. Besides, the stadium has tight security, and creating chaos would require a lot of resources."

Luo Xinghe remained silent for a long time.

He was weighing the pros and cons.

Ultimately, hatred overwhelmed reason.

"I'll do it," he said through gritted teeth. "I'll take responsibility if anything goes wrong."

"Understood." Mo Xuan's voice disappeared into the shadows.

Luo Xinghe looked out the window again, his eyes cold.

Wang Ling, I will definitely expose your true colors!

At 10 p.m., Wang Ling returned to the Wang family villa.

In the living room, Wang's parents were watching TV. When they saw him return, Wang's mother immediately stood up: "Lingling's back? How did the competition go?"

"I disguised myself and barely won," Wang Ling said.

"Hey, you have to be extra careful when you're disguised, or you might accidentally kill someone!"

Wang's father said with a smile, "If Wang Ming hadn't said that the talisman's current condition is very stable, I wouldn't have allowed you to participate in the competition!"

Wang Ling: "..."

He went upstairs to his room, closed the door, and threw his schoolbag onto a chair.

Then she walked to the bedside and took out the huge instant noodle gift box from the storage bag.

Opening it reveals five compartments, neatly stacked with one hundred packets of different flavored instant noodles.

Wang Ling looked at it for a few seconds, then reached out and took out a bag of durian cheese flavored drinks.

This is a treasure flavor he discovered today.

Upon opening the packaging, a strong durian aroma wafts out.

He broke off a piece and put it in his mouth, chewing slowly.

Today's match is replaying in my mind.

Li Haoran's sword, Liu Zixuan's fire, and Zhao Wuji's fist.

They're all too weak.

He was so weak that he had to deliberately hold back, so weak that he had to calculate every bit of force, so weak that he had to pretend to have "narrowly won".

I'm a little tired.

But it's quite interesting.

Especially Zhao Wuji's final kick, the power, speed, and angle were all at the peak of the Foundation Establishment stage.

When he received that kick, Wang Ling actually had a hundred ways to retaliate, but he chose the most reasonable one—using the force of the kick to throw the opponent.

It allows you to win without revealing your true strength.

Perfect.

After finishing his instant noodles, he folded the packaging bag into a small square and placed it on his bedside table.

There were already more than a dozen small cubes piled up there, all of which were for today's meals.

Then he lay down and turned off the light.

In the darkness, he closed his eyes.

Three seconds later, even breathing sounds rang out.

Beside his pillow, the crystal he had brought back from Disneyland, radiated a gentle light in the night.

Inside the crystal, two shadows stand side by side in front of the mirror.

Meanwhile, Chen Chaojia.

Chen Chao lay in bed, tossing and turning, unable to fall asleep.

The scenes from today's match kept replaying in his mind, especially the moment when Wang Ling caught his leg and fell.

He's handsome, but something just doesn't feel right.

Chen Chao wasn't stupid; he could sense that Wang Ling had a secret.

And it's not a secret.

But he also remembered his father's words: The Wang family are all kind-hearted and have never harmed us.

I also remember Guo Hao's words: The most important thing between brothers is honesty. But if the other person doesn't want to talk, then don't ask.

"Sigh..." Chen Chao sighed, sat up, and thought of Luo Xinghe's one million spirit stones again.

That's money that an ordinary person could never earn in a lifetime.

Luo Xinghe gave it to him like that, so that he could test Wang Ling.

Chen Chao got out of bed, walked to his desk, and turned on his computer.

Log in to the donation page of Yanran Angel Hospital.

Enter the amount: one million spirit stones.

Donation confirmed.

The page redirects to show that the donation was successful.

Looking at the words "Thank you for your kindness" on the screen, Chen Chao suddenly felt relieved.

Who cares?

If Wang Ling has a secret, then so be it.

Who doesn't have secrets?

As long as he's still the brother who shares his instant noodles with him and silently stands up for him when he's bullied, that's enough.

Chen Chao turned off the computer and lay back down on the bed.

This time, he fell asleep very quickly.

In his dream, he saw Wang Ling standing on the podium, holding a championship trophy in one hand and instant noodles in the other, distributing them to everyone in the audience.
