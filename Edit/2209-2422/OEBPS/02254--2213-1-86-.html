Chapter 2213 Instant Cowardice

In this great era of scientific cultivation, thanks to the influence of modern technology, the new generation of cultivators no longer needs to follow the path of ancient cultivators.

Ancient cultivators improved their cultivation in the following ways.

One method is to join a sect and accumulate reputation through various sects to obtain cultivation resources.

Secondly, they may encounter various fortuitous events, such as finding an old man hidden in a ring who will guide their cultivation. However, such cultivators who have fortuitous encounters are ultimately few and far between.

Thirdly, one could join a group of independent cultivators, each contributing a small amount of money to explore the secret realm, and then splitting the magical treasures within. However, in reality, once the secret realm was successfully explored, there was never any true fairness. It was often a meritocracy, with the most senior cultivators taking more and more, which would then lead to various grudges and feuds, eventually escalating to murder and robbery.

Of course, it's not limited to the three points mentioned above. In the past, cultivators, especially those living at the bottom, had very limited ways to obtain resources. Apart from a few lucky encounters, most people had a rather difficult life.

But things are completely different now.

Zhijiu Nian is a living example of a new generation of cultivators who has grown up entirely thanks to modern technology. He has accumulated wealth through modern live streaming methods, accumulating a large amount of rewards and live streaming income, and finally using this wealth to transform cultivation resources.

I must say, he's a real talent.

Unlike Mr. Zanghu, who liked to popularize science online, Mr. Zanghu devoted most of his live streaming and video income to supporting public welfare, and was dedicated to helping more cultivators understand cultivation science more scientifically.

Such a righteous person is extremely rare.

Wang Ling doesn't actually watch live streams or short videos, but he had heard of the streamer "Cai Shu Xue Qian De Jiu Jiu," who became an internet sensation by live streaming various handicrafts and food from the countryside.

If it weren't for this incident, who would have thought that such a popular online streamer would be directly related to the illegal extraterrestrial smuggling?

...

In the distance, Zhijiu Nian, who was preparing for the next live broadcast, suddenly felt the atmosphere around him turn chilly.

"This is... the feeling of fear?"

Zhijiu couldn't believe it.

He actually felt a long-lost sense of terror.

He was clearly a cultivator at the ninth level of the True Immortal Realm. Although he had not yet reached the peak, this level was still extremely rare, even after Earth completed its upgrade.

At least within the scope of Earth, a cultivator who has reached the True Immortal Realm can, in most cases, roam freely.

To ensure his live stream wouldn't be disturbed, Zhijiu bought hundreds of acres of surrounding farmland, decorated and improved it in various ways to match the theme of the live stream, and set up many barrier arrays nearby to isolate the atmosphere and provide defense.

Within his hundred-acre farmland, if anyone trespasses on his land, their presence will be immediately detected and reflected in the spiritual signal radar in his hand.

But now, the radar in his hand is clearly malfunctioning; the radar searchlight is spinning wildly everywhere.

"Bang!"

The next second, the electronic radar started smoking and then exploded in Tomohisa's hand.

This scene made Zhijiu Nian gasp.

"Boss..." The girl who had been helping Zhijiunian with the live stream was also surprised.

"Don't panic, it's just that some foolish thief broke in."

Zhijiu comforted his female partner, Lu Xiao, who was standing next to him.

Those who work around him are completely unaware of the grand plans he is actually devising. This includes the siblings Lu Xiao and Lu Jing, whose relationship with Zhi Jiunian is merely that of boss and employee.

They have to do whatever you tell them to do.

The brother and sister had lost their parents at a young age and had no one to rely on. They were completely clean and had no ties with other sects or forces. This was precisely why Zhijiu had taken a liking to the brother and sister in the first place.

Even though Tomohisa is young, strong, and calm, it is still unknown who the person who broke in is and how strong they are.

"Today's live stream can't be delayed. You'll go live from here in a bit to stall for time, understand?" Zhi Jiu frowned and said to Lu Xiao.

"Okay, boss." Lu Xiao bowed.

The live stream must continue.

No one can stop you from making money.

With Zhijiunian's current fan base, a single live stream selling agricultural products can generate millions or even thousands of sales, which is a substantial income.

Tomohisa is currently short of money.

So even if someone breaks in now, he can't stop the live stream. He still needs his "family" in the live stream to raise the relevant expenses for his big plan.

Zhiji took a deep breath and finally made up his mind to face this unknown opponent alone.

He flew into the air, closed his eyes, and carefully sensed the energy. He felt that the energy was unusually strong, filling the surrounding air and making it impossible for him to discern the specific direction.

A hundred-acre estate...

This person's aura was so strong that it could cover such a wide distance that Zhijiu was so shocked that his lips turned pale.

Just then, he felt a sharp gaze shooting straight at him from behind. In the dense forest below, he finally sensed the warning from Wang Ling.

This was Wang Ling's deliberate gaze, intended to allow Zhi Jiunian to see him.

When Tomohisa realized that the visitor was just a teenager, his expression became even more horrified.

What do kids eat to grow up these days?

No...

He's probably not a simple child; perhaps he's just an old monster who's preserved his youthful appearance...

In just a moment, Tomohisa thought of many possibilities.

He approached Wang Ling, but dared not get too close, maintaining a distance of about fifty meters.

As Wang Ling took a step forward, his body involuntarily took a step back.

Originally, Zhi Jiunian still planned to take action, but now, facing Wang Ling and this endless pressure, the extreme fear made him regain some of his rationality.

"I don't know what I did to offend you, senior, that you have to come to me in person to confront me." Zhi Jiunian asked, "I'll be frank. The work I do behind the scenes can't be discussed openly, but if there's any conflict of interest with you, please feel free to speak frankly. I can change!"

The words had barely left his mouth.

This completely baffled Wang Ling and Sun Rong, who were secretly watching from behind a big tree.

"......"

Wang Ling had already prepared to take direct action to eliminate Zhi Jiunian and hand him over to Zhuo Yi.

But I didn't expect Tomohisa to apologize so quickly.

This caused Wang Ling to fall silent for a moment.

Then, he fixed his gaze on Zhijiunian, intending to use the King's Eye to directly probe Zhijiunian's past, wanting to directly understand the reasons behind Zhijiunian's assistance in setting up this grand formation...
