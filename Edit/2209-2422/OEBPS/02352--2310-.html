Chapter 2310 Silent Erosion

Wang Muyu's return has injected unprecedented vitality into the Wang Family Villa.

At the dinner table, Wang's mother wanted to pile all the dishes on the table into Wang Muyu's bowl.

Wang Muyu looked like a little squirrel chewing nuts. Seeing his cheeks bulging as he diligently chewed, the whole family couldn't stop laughing.

Mr. Wang was temporarily relieved from the torment of starting the new book and curiously looked at the child who looked very much like his own precious son and was exceptionally well-behaved.

"You know what, you really do know... Little Wooden Fish, it really looks like Lingling's child with another man's daughter," Wang's mother exclaimed.

Wang Ling: "..."

Wang's father laughed and said, "You can't say that, Lingling probably doesn't have those kinds of thoughts either."

"I was just joking!" Wang Ma stared intently at the little wooden fish, her gaze never leaving it.

Although it may seem inappropriate to discuss gender issues now, to be honest, Wang's mother does hope that Wang Ling will one day get legally married and bring home a grandson or granddaughter.

Wang Muyu felt uneasy under his mother's gaze and just buried his head in his rice.

This rice, it really is rice...

"Little wooden fish, has anyone bullied you at school? Tell your Uncle Wang, and Uncle Wang will have your brother Wang Ling take care of it!" Father Wang patted his chest, feeling that he had become much more impressive in front of his child.

Wang Ling silently ate his rice, with Wang Nuan sitting on one side of his shoulder, chewing her nails, and Wang Muyu leaning close to him on the other.

He felt like he wasn't home, but rather in some kind of daycare.

"No, Uncle Wang! Everyone is fine!" Wang Muyu replied in a childish voice, shaking his head like a rattle-drum.

What he really thought was: In the Wuji Sword Sect, his master Yi Jianchuan spoiled him rotten, who would dare to bully him...

Wang Ling gently tapped Wang Muyu's forehead with the end of his chopsticks, signaling him to concentrate on eating.

The little guy shrank his neck and sat obediently, but the excitement in his eyes was almost overflowing.

This noisy yet warm daily life was something Wang Muyu had never experienced in the ancient ruins of the Dragon Clan or in the deep space of outer space.

He comfortably savored the lively atmosphere, and the vast dragon power within him seemed to become more gentle and peaceful as a result.

After the meal, in Wang Ling's room.

Wang Muyu and Wang Nuan sat side by side on the soft carpet, with piles of cultivation toys given to them by Sun Rong in front of them.

It's hard to believe that two children with godlike fighting abilities would be interested in worldly cultivation toys and have a great time playing with them.

Wang Nuan waved her little hands excitedly, and the shadows around her feet changed into various shapes.

Wang Muyu carefully manipulated a wisp of seven-colored dragon power and began building Le Di blocks.

Wang Ling leaned against the bedside, watching this scene, a rare hint of satisfaction appearing in his dead fish eyes.

But this tranquility did not last long.

His consciousness always operates at minimal power consumption and covers the entire globe.

Just now, he accurately caught a faint noise.

And the source is none other than Number 60 High School.

...

The following day, Number 60 High School.

When Wang Ling arrived at the school, he found that the entire campus was shrouded in an invisible sense of tension; the thing he was worried about had happened.

"Have you heard? Director Ma from the Academic Affairs Office suddenly had a mental breakdown in his office yesterday evening and almost destroyed his inner core! Luckily, Teacher Pan and the old antique happened to pass by and suppressed it in time!" During the break, Chen Chao lowered his voice and shared the gossip he had just heard.

"Director Ma? Isn't he known for his stable mind? He's been stuck in the late stage of the Golden Core realm for decades and has never made a mistake." Guo Hao expressed his doubt.

"It's absolutely true! I saw Director Ma being picked up by his family this morning. He looked sallow, like he'd been soaked in water, like a bloated, soggy dried sea buckthorn!"

Chen Chao confidently stated, "According to reliable sources, he recently used a lot of unorthodox elixirs in order to break through to the infant stage, which is probably a side effect."

Upon hearing this, the surrounding classmates all showed expressions of realization and sympathy.

Impatience in cultivation can lead to inner demons turning against one's true nature, which is not uncommon in the cultivation world.

Only Wang Ling, who was by the window, turned his gaze away from the playground outside and gave Chen Chao a brief, indifferent glance.

Unorthodox elixirs?

Perhaps.

But the root cause lies in that forcibly implanted, fanatical will.

Bai Zhe's "shadow hunters" have already begun their activities.

Moreover, the target is no longer just students; even school faculty and staff have not been spared.

Director Ma is probably just the tip of the iceberg.

Sun Rong walked over carrying several thick volumes of "An Introduction to Advanced Runeology" and gently placed them on Wang Ling's desk: "These are new study materials; I printed an extra copy."

She paused, her voice softening slightly, tinged with a hint of worry: "Wang Ling, regarding Director Ma's matter... do you think it's related to what happened before?"

Although there was no explicit warning from Aohai, her accurate intuition and her unconditional trust in Wang Ling made the girl unconsciously connect the two things.

Wang Ling looked at the concern in her clear eyes, paused for a second, and nodded.

Sun Rong's heart sank slightly.

Even school teachers have been infiltrated? The scope is wider than she imagined! More terrifying!

"Is there anything I can do for you?" she asked immediately.

The power of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group can be mobilized at any time.

Wang Ling shook his head, his meaning clear: not for now, the other party is in the dark, it is not advisable to alert them.

He needs clearer clues to find out the identification methods and activity patterns of the "spotters".

Large-scale investigations can cause panic, and the other party may have counter-surveillance capabilities.

Just then, a commotion broke out at the classroom door.

"Li Jun? What brings you here?" a classmate asked in surprise.

Li Jun, a student from the regular class in the first year of high school, stood at the door of the elite class classroom. He looked somewhat reserved, but his eyes were unusually firm.

He held a jade slip that looked quite old in his hand.

Meanwhile, his gaze searched the classroom, eventually landing on Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

"Sun Rong, classmate."

Li Jun stepped forward and handed the jade slip to Sun Rong: "I've been researching something, but I can't think of anyone in the school who could help me, so I'm taking the liberty of bothering you..."

Sun Rong took the jade slip, probed it slightly with her spiritual sense, and a look of surprise appeared on her face: "Is this a prototype of a method for identifying mental abnormalities based on spiritual energy analysis?"

"Um."

Li Jun nodded, somewhat embarrassed: "My cultivation level is low, so I can only create a framework. Since last time... I've been thinking about whether I can do something to prevent more people from going astray. This method can theoretically detect whether a person's mind is distorted, but I haven't verified it yet, and it requires massive computing power and database support..."

His idea was simple: if those devices could distort people's minds, then there should also be a way to detect the traces of distortion.

However, this method still has many incomplete elements, which Li Jun alone cannot realize. Therefore, the first person he thought of was Sun Rong.

At this moment, Sun Rong's beautiful eyes lit up, moved by Li Jun's thoughtfulness.

She didn't think that Li Jun's cultivation level would allow him to develop a method with any specific effect, after all, this man beside her who loved eating instant noodles knew tens of thousands of Heavenly Dao principles.

If you really want to know what kind of magic can be used to distinguish them, then Wang Ling definitely has plenty of ways.
