Chapter 2287 Deep Within the Dragon's Nest

Before the dizziness from the spatial shift had completely subsided, a sense of oppression, a mixture of dragon might and decay, washed over us.

Wang Muyu was tightly imprisoned by the Chains of Annihilation, held in someone's hand like a piece of merchandise.

He desperately suppressed the surging and roaring dragon power within his body, restraining it under the illusion of near depletion.

The golden dragon eyes barely opened a crack, cautiously examining the legendary "Dark Hall, an ancient dragon ruin."

What came into view was an incredibly magnificent and extremely bizarre scene.

The enormous hall seemed to be built in the core of an ancient star, with its dome reaching the sky and countless dragon runes emitting a dark light floating above it.

What supports the hall are not stone pillars, but huge, jet-black dragon bones that gleam with a metallic luster, winding and coiling, with ghostly blue flames burning in the eye sockets of the dragon heads, silently gazing down.

The air was filled with the pure yet cold aura of the dragon race's origin.

But deeper still, there lingers a sticky and greedy feeling that is extremely uncomfortable, as if countless eyes are watching from the shadows.

Wang Muyu, with his keen insight, knew that this was the aura of a void nightmare.

It's hard to believe that the once mighty cosmic overlord has now colluded with the forces of the Outer Gods, eroding this once sacred dragon palace into such a state.

The ground was a dark golden metal, engraved with intricate ravines. What flowed within it was not energy, but dragon blood, like molten gold, radiating boundless vitality, yet it was forcibly restrained here by some force, filled with resentment.

This place is just one of Bai Zhe's strongholds.

It is far more evil and ancient than one might imagine.

"Hmph, the Son of Ten Thousand Dragons, is nothing more than that."

Huang Yan's voice broke the silence. He transformed back into his human dragon form, flicked his wrist, and seemed to still be savoring the feeling of punching Wang Muyu away: "What a waste of such a big effort. Jueqi, aren't your stats a bit exaggerated?"

Jueqi did not answer, but his molten gold vertical pupils coldly swept over him.

He carried Wang Muyu and strode into the depths of the hall. Shenming and Xunfeng followed closely behind, equally vigilant as they surveyed their surroundings.

Clearly, the bizarre scene in the universe had left them with lingering fear.

Wang Muyu snorted inwardly and continued to play the role of a "weak captive," even deliberately letting a wisp of dragon power escape uncontrollably, only to quickly wither away, making it appear even more realistic.

"See Lord Juejiao! Lord Huangyan! Lord Shenming! Lord Xunfeng!"

Along the way, they encountered many patrolling dragon descendants. When they saw the four imprisoned, especially Wang Muyu who was imprisoned, they all bowed and paid their respects, their eyes revealing awe and fervor.

These four are the strongest fighters among the Dragonborn today, and their prominence has even surpassed that of Yan and Jingze, who are considered elders among the Dragonborn. They are enough to attract attention wherever they go.

Deeper into the hall lies a more open area.

A colossal throne, constructed from countless pale bones, stands in the center.

The throne was empty.

But in front of the throne was a huge pool of blood, not blood, but boiling dark red energy liquid, in which various unfinished dragon bodies were soaked, appearing ferocious and terrifying.

Beside the blood pool stood several men in black robes with gloomy auras; they were the high-ranking members of the Primordial Organization.

Their faces appeared as a black mist. This was not unrelated; they were a new race created by evil human cultivators who "voluntarily renounced their human status," and were distorted by combining some of the essence of dragon blood with the aura of outer gods.

Bai Zhe called these black-robed people "Black Dumplings" and gave them important positions in the Primordial Organization.

Upon seeing Jueqi and the others return, especially Wang Muyu, the black-robed leader let out a hoarse laugh: "Welcome back, esteemed lords! Is this the Son of Ten Thousand Dragons handpicked by Lord Bai Zhe? Indeed... his foundation is extraordinary!"

He could sense the immense potential that remained even under confinement.

"Enough with the nonsense." Jueqi coldly interrupted him: "Target captured. Initiate preliminary testing and reinforce the containment immediately. Where is Lord Bai Zhe?"

"Lord Bai Zhe is currently at the core of the Dragon God Altar, engaging in deep communication with the will of the Darkness in preparation for the upcoming fusion ceremony."

The man in black robes respectfully replied, "My lord instructed that after capturing the target, it should be sent directly to the Dragon Transformation Pool for deep confinement and energy analysis. All preparations must be completed before it awakens."

Hualong Pond?

Wang Muyu's heart skipped a beat; just from the name, it didn't sound like a good place.

He felt Jueqi carrying him toward the boiling pool of blood.

"High concentrations of dragon blood essence, external god erosion energy, and various unknown law binding fields were detected."

The protective will left by Wang Ling within Wang Muyu's body, like the system notification sounds implanted in those novels, silently analyzed and transmitted the information.

"Threat level: High. Can be converted into energy replenishment. It is recommended to absorb it moderately to maintain the disguise."

Just as Jueqi was about to throw Wang Muyu into the Dragon Transformation Pool—

"Wait a minute." Shenming suddenly spoke, his icy blue eyes fixed on Wang Muyu: "Jueqin, do you think the capture process... went too smoothly?"

Xunfeng swayed, his fingertips entwined with the breeze: "Yeah, Brother Jueqi, that sudden movement in the universe really startled me. This little guy, could he have a backup plan? I have a feeling someone is watching us."

The Absolute paused for a moment, then the Molten Gold Vertical Eyes refocused on Wang Muyu, scanning the beam of light to examine him closely.

Wang Muyu's heart skipped a beat, and he almost lost his breath.

He coughed hard, forcing out a little more golden dragon blood. His breath grew weaker, his eyes became unfocused, as if he were already in a dying, twilight state.

The scan data from Absolute came back: "Target's vital signs continue to decline... Energy fluctuations are below the threshold! Soul fluctuations are weak and scattered! No abnormal energy connections were found!"

On Earth, Wang Ling sighed softly.

The concealment techniques he used on Wang Muyu combined various Great Dao disguises, making him far beyond the ability of these minor Ancestral Realm cultivators to detect.

"Excessive caution," Jueqi said coldly. "The severe backlash after the target's explosion is logical. Everything was calculated."

He seemed to be convincing his companion, but more likely he was convincing himself of his own inexplicable unease.

Without further hesitation, he swung his arm and threw Wang Muyu directly into the boiling Dragon Transformation Pool!

Thump!

The dark red energy liquid instantly engulfed Wang Muyu.

An extremely domineering energy immediately surged from all directions, attempting to penetrate his limbs and bones, and erode his dragon soul.

At the same time, countless strange rune chains emerged from the bottom of the pool, coiling upwards to completely lock him to death at the bottom of the pool!

Songhai City, Number 60 High School.

The first period in the afternoon is a practical class on talisman studies.

The classroom was filled with sparks of inspiration as the students were engrossed in drawing basic symbols.

Wang Ling sat by the window, a psionic pen held between his fingers.

On the talisman in front of him, an incredibly complex invisible rune was quietly taking shape. The brushstrokes were so steady that there was not the slightest deviation, yet the spiritual energy was perfectly controlled at the level that a Foundation Establishment stage cultivator should have.

His gaze seemed to be fixed on the talisman, but in reality, most of his mind had already traversed endless space.

The moment Wang Muyu was thrown into the Dragon Transformation Pool, Wang Ling's finger, which was drawing the final stroke of the rune, paused almost imperceptibly.
