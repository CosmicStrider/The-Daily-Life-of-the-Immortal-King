Chapter 2359 Confrontation Match

Chen Chao and Guo Hao exchanged a glance, both seeing the shock in each other's eyes that said, "This kid really dares to speak his mind."

Wang Ling finally finished his yogurt, accurately tossed the empty box into the trash can three meters away, and then pulled out a pack of unopened instant noodles from his pocket.

He seemed not to hear.

Or rather, they heard it, but didn't understand it.

Or perhaps, they understood, but it was sealed away and suppressed.

In short, his face remained expressionless.

Ryoko Kugimiya gently tapped the table with her folding fan, breaking the awkward silence: "What do kids know? Alright, finish your meal and rest a bit. We have a competition this afternoon. Go back to your rooms and get ready."

Everyone felt as if they had been granted a pardon and rose to their feet.

Sun Rong practically fled the cafeteria.

Wang Ling followed slowly behind her, keeping a distance of about three steps.

...

It was 1 p.m. in a small meeting room in Building A.

The Love Strategist Alliance held another emergency meeting.

Zhuo Yi, Jiu Gong Liangzi, Wang Zhen, Liu Qingyi, and Sun Rong sat around the conference table.

"The first phase was very effective." Zhuo Yi spoke first: "I have already sent the synchronization data of Master and Junior Sister Sun during the ice-breaking games this morning to Mr. Wang Ming. After analysis, he believes that this level of physical coordination and instinctive reaction indicates that Master subconsciously pays close attention to Junior Sister Sun."

Ryoko Kugimiya added, "Furthermore, based on my observation, although Wang Ling did not show any obvious reaction after Luo Xinghe and Xia Zhinu appeared, his spiritual energy fluctuations were somewhat abnormal. This indicates that the seal was working and suppressing certain emotions."

Sun Rong pursed her lips: "So... he still doesn't feel anything?"

"Not necessarily." Wang Zhen shook his head: "The sealing mechanism of Ling Zhenren is very special. This process has actually already happened. It's just that we can't see the part in between where the emotion was generated."

"Yes." Ryoko Kugimiya tapped the table with her folding fan: "Like boiling a frog in lukewarm water. We need to gradually let Wang Ling's emotional modules adapt through continuous and gentle stimulation, until one day, the seal can no longer suppress him, or he learns to deal with these emotions on his own."

At this point, everyone knew in their hearts that this was likely to be a protracted war that would last a very long time.

After all, the problem of sealing is indeed thorny, and we can't actively break the seal of the King's Decree, otherwise the psychic energy will explode, which is no different from mass suicide.

Now they are just doing their best and leaving the rest to fate, trying to get as much data and information as possible about Wang Ling, so that they can at least know that Wang Ling has feelings for Sun Rong, and at the same time, it can bring some help to Wang Ming's subsequent research and development of new talismans.

Sun Rong knew that everyone had already done their best, and everyone was doing their utmost to help her.

She naturally had nothing to complain about; instead, she was filled with gratitude: "So, what are the plans for this afternoon?"

"I've already arranged the draw for the qualifying rounds this afternoon."

Zhuo Yi pulled a piece of paper from his pocket, on which was drawn a simple battle diagram: "Eight teams, randomly paired into four temporary teams. But in reality, I have adjusted the random algorithm."

He pointed to the drawing: "Team A: Sun Rong, Wang Ling, plus Zhou Rui from Xuanming High School, and... Luo Xinghe."

"Team B: Chen Chao, Guo Hao, Li Changzhe, Wang Muyu, plus Liu Kaichuan from Chihuo High School, and Xia Zhinuan."

"Team C and Team D are mixed teams from other schools, so they are not important."

Wang Zhen's eyes lit up: "So, Sun Rong and Ling Zhenren's team added Luo Xinghe? And Chen Chao and Guo Hao's team added Xia Zhinuan..."

"That's right." Zhuo Yi nodded. "This arrangement serves several purposes: First, it allows Luo Xinghe to interact frequently with Junior Sister Sun during the competition, thus stimulating Master. Second, placing Xia Zhinuan on the other team means that if she shows concern for Master, it will also create external stimulation. Third, your two teams are very likely to meet in the finals, and then..."

He paused, a meaningful smile appearing on his face: "The interaction when on the same team and the confrontation when facing opponents will produce completely different chemical reactions. We need to observe Master's reactions from all angles. I will discuss this arrangement with Chen Chao separately later. He doesn't know the secret of Master's seal, and keeping it from him is also to protect him."

Ryoko Kugimiya added, "I also think this method is good. The team's voice recordings during the competition are recorded throughout. We will analyze whether Wang Ling's tone, speed, and word choice changed subtly when he heard Luo Xinghe speaking to Sun Rong."

Sun Rong's cheeks flushed as she listened.

This plan... is too meticulous.

The meticulousness made her feel like a lab rat being observed.

But for the sake of the king's decree...

She gritted her teeth and nodded: "I understand."

"Remember," Zhuo Yi instructed, "everything should be natural. Don't force it. Junior Sister Sun, you must remember to be yourself, just be yourself, don't overact. Let everything flow naturally."

"Yes, I understand, Senior Zhuo Yi!" Sun Rong nodded in assurance.

Before the meeting ended, Ryoko Kunomiya suddenly called out to Rong Sun.

"Rongrong, this is for you."

She took a small silver earring out of her handbag and handed it to Sun Rong.

"What is this?"

"A miniature psionic sensor," Ryoko Kugimiya explained. "It's worn on the ear and is inconspicuous. It can monitor your heart rate, psionic energy fluctuations, and other physiological data in real time and synchronize them to the backend. We need to compare your data with Wang Ling's to see if there is a correlation between your reactions when certain interactions occur."

Sun Rong was shocked; this level of professionalism was almost unbelievable...

But Sun Rong took the earring, somewhat hesitant: "Isn't this... a bit too much?"

"Scientific research needs data support." Ryoko Kumiya said seriously, "Don't worry, the data is absolutely confidential and will only be used for analysis."

In the end, Sun Rong still put it on.

These small, silver earrings have a simple design and look like ordinary jewelry.

But Sun Rong knew that from this moment on, her every move, even every heartbeat, became an analysis sample for the Love Strategist Alliance.

...

At 1:50 PM, in the Battle Hall of Building A, Room 1.

All eight teams, with a total of forty members, were present.

In the center of the hall stood a huge transparent lottery box containing forty numbered spirit jades.

Zhuo Yi stood beside the lottery box and announced loudly, "The rules are simple. Each person will draw a spirit jade, numbered 1-10 as Team A, 11-20 as Team B, and so on. After the draw, each team will have ten minutes to determine their positions and discuss tactics. The competition will officially begin at 2:30."

"Now, please have each team captain come forward to draw lots."

The team leader of Number 60 High School is Sun Rong.

She stepped forward, reached into the lottery box, and pulled out a warm, smooth jade.

Turn it over, number: 7.

Team A.

She subconsciously looked at Wang Ling.

Wang Ling also came up, casually touched it, and found the number: 3.

Also Team A.

Next, Chen Chao drew number 15, Team B. Guo Hao drew number 16, Team B. Li Changzhe drew number 17, Team B. Wang Muyu drew number 18, Team B.

Perfect grouping.

At least on the surface, it appears to be random.

Only those in the know understand that Zhuo Yi had tampered with things.

Other teams also drew lots one after another.

When all the lottery results were displayed on the big screen, a murmur of discussion arose from the audience.

"The students from Number 60 High School have been separated."

"Sun Rong and Wang Ling are on the same team, and Luo Xinghe has joined... How should the bottom lane be split up?"

Zhuo Yi clapped his hands: "Alright, the grouping is complete. Now, each team will proceed to their designated areas for tactical discussions. Team A will be in discussion area number one on the left, and Team B will be in discussion area number two on the right. The other teams will proceed to areas number three and four."

The crowd began to disperse.

Sun Rong, Wang Ling, Luo Xinghe, Zhou Rui, and six other team members from different schools, a total of ten people, headed towards the first discussion area.

The tactical discussion officially began.
