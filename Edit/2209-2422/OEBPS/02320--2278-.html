Chapter 2278 Daily Life

Songhai Number 60 High School.

Before the morning class, Sun Rong naturally came to Wang Ling's side and placed her newly developed beverage on the corner of Wang Ling's table: "Want to try it? It's a new product developed by the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group based on my description of the Inner Earth Juice."

Wang Ling glanced at the bottle of beverage, then at Sun Rong.

In the inner world, being fed seemed to have become a habit, and this habit was carried over into daily life.

He silently picked it up, inserted the tube, and took a breath.

The sweet fruit flavor mixed with the aroma of spiritual plants is surprisingly delicious.

"Thank you." The voice was as flat as ever, but at least the caller answered.

Sun Rong's eyes narrowed slightly, and she started drinking as well, her gaze seemingly drifting unintentionally out the window, a small sense of relief washing over her.

Every time I hand him something, it feels like embarking on a delicate adventure. I both anticipate his acceptance and fear disturbing him.

She found this cautious feeling somewhat amusing, yet she couldn't control it.

"Does Nuan Nuan like the new projector? I read the instructions, and it should be able to simulate the process of small star field collapse and birth. I wonder if it's too complicated?" She started a conversation about Wang Nuan, something that always made her feel more at ease.

Wang Ling recalled how his sister had looked at the room full of starlight last night, her shadows twisting excitedly into various nebula shapes, and nodded: "Yes. I like it."

He paused, then added, as if completing a task report, "She played for a long time."

"That's good!" Sun Rong smiled. "If I finish my report this weekend, I'll go see her and bring her the new starry sky puzzle."

"Okay," Wang Ling replied.

With someone to play with his sister, he can relax.

At this moment, more and more students are filling the classroom, and the air is filled with the vibrant energy unique to young people.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao were surrounded by a group of classmates; their Golden Core cultivation made them the focus of the class.

The two men were modest in their words, but their smugness was impossible to hide, as they gestured to describe the ferocity of a certain "lava giant lizard" from the Earth's core—of course, an artistically embellished version.

Wang Ling sat in his corner by the window, as if he were in an invisible, silent barrier.

My fingertips tapped unconsciously on the table at a constant frequency, as if calculating some kind of cosmic law.

In the first lesson, Teacher Pan explained the "Formulas for Material Stress and Spiritual Energy Conduction" in basic refining, his voice rising and falling with intonation.

For Wang Ling, this knowledge was already ingrained in his soul, and it was not as challenging as figuring out how to accurately describe the taste of the new instant noodles to the snack shop owner.

Furthermore, most of his mind had already pierced through the clouds and been projected towards the Wuji Sword Sect's dojo in the distant void.

In his "perception," that area was bustling with activity at that moment.

...

Several squads, personally dispatched by Lord Diu Lei and led by elite elders of the Battle Sect, are using various cutting-edge cultivation technologies and formations to precisely intercept the primordial evil cultivators attempting to approach the Wuji Sword Sect.

Most battles take place in spatial crevices or on the edge of desolate secret realms, where the light of psionic explosions is like silent fireworks, briefly illuminating and then quickly disappearing.

Zhan Zong's movements were efficient and covert, fully embodying the guiding principle of "both practicing and generating revenue" of Diu Lei Zhenjun.

Just as one team used the "Binding God Rope Matrix" to trap three evil cultivators who were trying to use the "Blood Sacrifice Boundary Breaking Technique," another team next to them started working on the spot using the "Memory Extractor," attempting to interrogate them to extract information about the strongholds of the Primordial Organization and clues about the ancient secret realm.

All of these scientific cultivation devices were designed by "Wang Ming." Wang Ming provided a great deal of assistance in the Zhanzong sect's integration of modern cultivation technology.

"Tch, this team of Netherworld Shadow Escape Techniques is quite good, but unfortunately they ran into our newly upgraded Heavenly Net System. They can only consider themselves unlucky." Inside a War Sect mobile outpost disguised as an asteroid, True Monarch Thunder Drop crossed his legs, looked at the huge tactical spirit screen in front of him, sipped his spiritual tea, and commented with great interest.

The War Sect has become adept at using this disguise technique. In earlier years, some extraterrestrial civilizations even attempted to disguise their aircraft as meteorites or comets to test the development of Earth's civilization.

"Sect Master, a group attempting to harass the Sword Sect's outer patrol disciples with the Illusionary Sound Bell was discovered in Area C. We have countered them with the Heavenly Silence Array, capturing five people and seizing one magic bell in excellent condition. It is estimated that it can fetch a good price," an elder reported.

"Well done! Make a note of this. After the war, when we reward our merits, 20 percent of the profits from this batch of spoils, after deducting costs, will be distributed as bonuses!" Lord Thunderclap waved his hand, full of enthusiasm.

He enjoyed the feeling of being in charge, and even more so the sense of accomplishment from generating revenue for the War Sect.

Of course, he knew perfectly well that all of this stemmed from the absolute power endorsement of that "elder brother" hidden in the classroom of Number 60 High School.

Without this confidence, he wouldn't dare to so casually use the primordial evil cultivators for training and revenue generation.

However, not all heretical practitioners are so easily manipulated.

On the screen, a high-level alarm suddenly lit up in an edge area.

A team of evil cultivators, consisting of three sinister-looking old men dressed in ancient attire, forcibly tore through a spatial maze set up by the War Sect and rushed toward a subordinate secret realm at an extremely fast speed.

An eerie, decaying aura emanated from them.

Wherever it passed, even the spiritual energy of the void seemed to be drained of its vitality.

"Hmm? These three old guys... their cultivation is actually close to the level of Dao Immortals? The Primordial World has really gone all out this time!"

Dao Immortal is a major realm after Immortal Saint! After that, there are Dao Venerable and Dao God!

After the Dao God comes the Dao Ancestor, who symbolizes entering the Ancestral Realm!

Since Earth's upgrade, many of the original True Immortal Realm cultivators have advanced to the Immortal Venerable or Immortal Saint realms, while only a few have reached the Dao Immortal level.

Lord Diu Lei sat up straight, his eyes sharpening: "Notify the third squad of the Dark Guard to intervene, activate the forbidden magic domain, prioritize capture, and if the resistance is fierce, authorize the use of the Star-Shattering Crossbow!"

As soon as the order was given, the three old men seemed to sense something. The leader suddenly turned around, his withered lips moving as he uttered a string of obscure incantations.

Buzz!

A gray ripple that stretched for tens of thousands of miles suddenly spread out, its target the War Sect's dark force squad that was preparing to encircle them!

This ripple contains the terrifying law of "annihilation," which will deplete the spiritual power and shorten the lifespan of ordinary cultivators who touch it.

"Not good!" Lord Thunder's expression changed slightly; the power exceeded expectations.

Just as the gray ripples were about to touch the dark squad—

Click.

A faint sound, as if it came from the depths of the universe's background noise.

The domineering gray ripples disappeared inch by inch, as if erased by an invisible eraser, without even a ripple.

The sinister smiles on the faces of the three old evil cultivators froze instantly, replaced by utter astonishment and bewilderment.

What happened?!

How did their combined attack... just disappear?

Before they could figure it out, the Zhanzong Dark Guard seized this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.

The "Forbidden Law Domain" descended instantly, and the specially made "Immortal Suppressing Chains" quickly coiled around it, binding the three bewildered old monsters like dumplings in an instant.

"Done! We're done!"

The captain of the dark unit breathed a sigh of relief. Although he didn't know what had just happened, he was sure that the sect leader had arranged a backup plan!

Inside the mobile outpost, Lord Thunderclap was stunned for a few seconds before reacting. He cupped his hands in a gesture of respect towards the air, his face beaming with joy: "Thank you for your protection, brother!"

He unceremoniously attributed the credit to himself and Zhan Zongqiang's powerful "hidden contingency plan".

In the classroom, Wang Ling, expressionless, blew away a strand of hair that had fallen onto the table and continued tapping the surface.
