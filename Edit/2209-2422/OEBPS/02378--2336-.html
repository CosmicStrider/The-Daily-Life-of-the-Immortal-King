Chapter 2336 The Unbelievable Matching Mechanism

Ma Yu's cries echoed through the esports arena, filled with the helplessness of having her dreams utterly shattered.

Mr. Ma stood beside the gaming booth, saying nothing more, only gently patting his son's back.

His face was exhausted, but there was a sense of relief in his eyes.

Perhaps this painful lesson is far more effective than his own countless sermons.

After a long while, Ma Yu's crying gradually subsided.

He wiped away the tears and snot from his face with the back of his hand, stood up from the gaming cabin, and, forcing himself to stand, bowed deeply to Wang Ling, Chen Chao, and the others.

"Big brother, big sister... I... I'm sorry!" His voice still had a heavy nasal tone, but his tone was more sincere than ever before: "I was too arrogant..."

At the same time, his gaze lingered on Wang Ling for a while longer, his eyes filled with nothing but awe.

Ma Yu still can't understand how Wang Ling acted like he was possessed by a ghost.

But he was acutely aware that there was an insurmountable chasm between him and the other party.

Chen Chao wanted to say a few more sarcastic remarks, but seeing Ma Yu's miserable state, he simply waved his hand and said, "Alright, alright, I forgive you. Remember not to be so arrogant when playing games in the future, and be more civilized and polite."

Guo Hao added, "Esports is not just about operation; mindset, awareness, and overall perspective are all indispensable. Your operation skills are actually not bad, but your mindset is too impatient and you are prone to getting carried away, which is a big taboo."

Ma Yu nodded vigorously, making sure to remember these words.

Seeing this, Mr. Ma quickly stepped forward and thanked everyone again: "Thank you so much, classmates! Xiao Yu is spoiled rotten by his mother. It's his good luck to meet you all today! I will definitely discipline him properly when we get back!"

After exchanging a few polite words, Ma's father helped Ma Yu leave the Fantasy Esports Arena.

After watching the father and son leave, the venue returned to calm, but the atmosphere was different.

Chen Chao let out a long sigh of relief and stretched vigorously: "Hey, I never thought that playing a game could turn me into a life mentor! It feels pretty good!"

Guo Hao laughed and said, "The main reason is that your son was too ruthless in those two matches, directly shattering that kid's Dao heart. But it's good, this wave has boosted morale!"

Sun Rong smiled at Wang Ling and said softly, "Wang Ling, thank you."

She knew that, given Wang Ling's personality, the reason he disciplined Ma Yu was most likely because the other party had insulted his instant noodles.

However, objectively speaking, it did establish the team's authority and was very beneficial for the subsequent training.

Wang Ling nodded slightly in response.

"Alright, let's get down to business!" Sun Rong clapped her hands, drawing everyone's attention back: "Our team is now complete, and our goal is to win the Songhai City e-sports qualifier. Next, we need to clarify our roles and conduct team coordination training."

She walked to the holographic tactical board in the center of the venue, tapped it lightly, and brought up an aerial view of the classic map "Summoner's Canyon" from "Glory of the League".

In fact, Sun Rong was not a professional, but the coaching team behind the Huaguo Water Curtain Group had already summarized all the information obtained from the battle and reported it to Sun Rong in real time through spiritual transmission.

"Based on their performance just now, I'll make a preliminary assignment of positions."

Sun Rong's gaze swept over everyone: "Chen Chao, top lane. Your ability to withstand pressure and your laning details are very good, you're suited for the solo lane."

"No problem!" Chen Chao patted his chest, full of confidence.

"Guo Hao, you'll play jungle. You have a great sense of rhythm and are good at leading the team."

"Leave it to me, don't worry." Guo Hao nodded; he had a wide pool of jungle heroes.

"Wang Ling..." Sun Rong looked at Wang Ling, who was silently taking out a second pack of instant noodles, and asked gently, "ADC position, is that okay?"

Wang Ling paused for a moment, then nodded.

For him, any position is about the same.

After all, one can "invite the gods".

"Great!" Sun Rong smiled and then looked at Wang Muyu: "Then, we'll leave the mid lane to our Little Wooden Fish, how about that?"

"Me?" Wang Muyu looked up, a little nervous. "Can I do it? I'm not very good at it yet..."

"It's okay, everyone starts from scratch." Sun Rong squatted down and patted Wang Muyu's head: "The mid-lane position is crucial. You need to clear the lane quickly, support the side lanes, and deal burst damage in team fights. I believe that with your learning ability, Muyu, you will be able to get the hang of it quickly."

Chen Chao also came over: "Yeah, Muyu, don't be afraid! Your brothers are here, don't be afraid!"

Guo Hao analyzed, "Muyu is young and has a fast reaction speed, which is an advantage when playing mid lane. Once he becomes familiar with the heroes and basic gameplay, he has huge potential."

Wang Muyu was overjoyed, clenching his little fists: "Yes! I will study hard!"

Sun Rong stood up and pointed to herself last: "Then I will be in charge of the support role, protecting Wang Ling, and cooperating with Guo Hao in roaming and setting up sights."

The lane assignments are now officially confirmed: Top laner Chen Chao, jungler Guo Hao, mid laner Wang Muyu, ADC Wang Ling, and support Sun Rong.

Next, Sun Rong used the tactics panel to bring up the hero selection interface.

Sun Rong: "Muyu, you're not familiar with mid-lane heroes yet. Let me introduce you to a few more traditional heroes first, and you can see which one you like."

On the holographic projection, three-dimensional models of several heroes and skill demonstrations appeared one after another.

"This is Clockwork Spirit."

Sun Rong pointed to the young mage surrounded by a metal sphere: "She controls a magic puppet to attack and release skills, which requires excellent positioning and puppet control skills. She plays a significant role in team battles."

Wang Muyu stared at the sphere flying around and blinked.

"This is the Dark Sovereign, also known by players as the Ball Girl."

Sun Rong then switched to a female hero surrounded by dark orbs: "She can control the dark orbs through her skills, with both burst damage and crowd control."

Wang Muyu's eyes lit up slightly as he watched Syndra conjure a dark orb with a wave of her hand and blast the virtual dummy to pieces.

"And then there's the Desert Emperor, or Tsar for short." Sun Rong showed a hero guarded by sand soldiers: "He can summon sand soldiers to attack, but he requires a high level of skill and becomes more powerful in the later stages of the game."

After the heroes finished their demonstrations, Sun Rong gently asked, "Muyu, which one do you like best?"

Wang Muyu tilted his head and thought for a moment, then finally pointed to Syndra: "Sister Sun Rong, I want to try this... Syndra."

He enjoyed the feeling of controlling dark power and unleashing destruction with a wave of his hand.

"Great, Muyu!" Chen Chao praised, "This hero's damage is explosive after the update, but he's a bit squishy, so you need to position yourself carefully."

Guo Hao nodded in agreement: "The key is that it's not difficult to operate, making it very suitable for playing with a wooden fish. It can also help improve skill accuracy."

"Alright, let's start with Syndra as your beginner champion."

Sun Rong wrote it down: "Of course, once you're more proficient, you can try other heroes to expand your hero pool."

With the team composition and hero selection for Muyu finalized, the next step is to practice in actual combat.

"No amount of theory can compare to a real battle."

Sun Rong suggested, "Let's play a match first to get familiar with each other's playstyles and teamwork. The key is to let Muyu experience the rhythm and basic gameplay of the game."
