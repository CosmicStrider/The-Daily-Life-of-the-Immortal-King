Chapter 2348 Liu Kaichuan's Nightmare

Liu Kaichuan lay slumped in the expensive psionic e-sports cabin at the headquarters of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, staring blankly at his 0/10/2 score on Varus.

Draven's spinning axe seemed to still be dangling before his eyes.

He has lost before; he has repeatedly studied the videos of professional athletes. Defeats are the food for growth.

But like today, being treated like a mobile ATM, having your price slashed from 300 yuan to 20 yuan, you're not even as good as a lowly soldier...

This is no longer a game match.

This is one-sided psychological torture and personal insult!

Especially at the very last high ground tower, Draven flashed in, ignoring all his teammates, and just focused on hacking away at him, a 20-gold Varus... Liu Kaichuan almost crushed his mouse at that moment.

"Hoo..." He exhaled a long breath of turbid air, feeling as if half of his soul had been cleaved apart by Draven's axe.

...

In the office break area, Sun Yiyuan could see the "massacre" that had just taken place through the internal monitoring screen.

Instead of showing the slightest sympathy for Liu Kaichuan's miserable state, he stroked his chin, his face beaming with a smile, his eyes gleaming.

"Good! Well done!" Old Master Sun couldn't help but murmur his praise.

The coffin reappeared silently beside him, handing him a newly printed document.

"Chairman, these are the preliminary investigation results regarding the game ID 'L'. His real identity is highly suspected to be Wang Ling, a student in the elite class of the first year of high school at Songhai Number 60 High School."

"Wang Ling? It really is him!" Sun Yiyuan took the documents and quickly glanced at them.

Although the information above regarding Wang Ling's game records is pitifully scarce, it only further confirms his conjecture.

He recalled Wang Ling's unassuming performance during the previous Earth Core incident, and combined it with the incredible skill he had just displayed in the game...

"This kid always manages to surprise me!"

Sun Yiyuan was in high spirits: "As expected of the person I've chosen, even playing games is so talented! Rongrong's eye for talent is just like mine, so sharp!"

He now looks at Wang Ling like a mother-in-law looks at her son-in-law, becoming more and more satisfied with him.

Wang Ling's special care for Liu Kaichuan and his decision not to kill Sun Rong in the game were automatically interpreted by him as his concern and protection for his own granddaughter.

"Not bad, not bad, Wang Ling has finally learned to protect his food. That's progress." Grandpa Sun thought to himself with satisfaction, and even began to consider whether he should invest more money in the Dream Esports Arena to upgrade the equipment and make it easier for the children to "cultivate their friendship".

...

The door to the gaming cabin slowly opened, and Sun Rong stepped out with a somewhat apologetic expression, looking at Liu Kaichuan, whose gaze was unfocused, in the other cabin.

"President Liu, are you... alright?" Sun Rong asked somewhat embarrassedly.

Although the misunderstanding was caused by Grandpa and the coffin, it was ultimately her fault, and she even made them suffer so much.

Liu Kaichuan mechanically turned his head, his eyes filled with confusion: "Sun... classmate Sun... is that classmate Wang Ling usually... this brutal?"

"No, no, no... He actually loves world peace. It's just that he might be taking it more seriously today."

Sun Rong looked apologetic: "I'm so sorry, President Liu, it was all a misunderstanding today, and I've made you..."

"It's alright!" Liu Kaichuan shook his head violently, forcibly breaking free from the fear of being dominated by Draven.

At the same time, the competitive spirit of the e-sports club president was reignited: "Victory and defeat are common in war! ADC is not my main position, so it's normal to lose!"

He stood up, stretched his stiff fingers, and his eyes regained their fighting spirit: "Sun, that last game doesn't count! That Draven was too monstrous! Let's play again! This time I'll play top lane, my main position! You jungle to help me, and with our mid, top, and jungle synergy, we're sure to put on a spectacular show!"

He refused to believe that he would be utterly defeated in his area of expertise.

As the saying goes, "every profession is like a mountain to climb," and the top laner's understanding of lane control, minion wave management, and split pushing is not something that can be grasped overnight.

Seeing Liu Kaichuan's renewed fighting spirit, Sun Rong hesitated for a moment.

Out of consideration for social etiquette, she felt she should play another round with Liu Kaichuan to ease the awkwardness of the situation.

A strange intuition told her that it might be... very interesting.

"Okay." Sun Rong nodded: "Then President Liu, you go top, and I'll jungle."

"Alright!" Liu Kaichuan confidently lay back down in the gaming cabin: "Watch me, Fiora, poke their top lane to pieces!"

...

On the other side, Wang Ling and Chen Chao also left the game room.

"Awesome! That's so awesome, Lingzi! Did you see that Verus? He didn't even dare to leave the fountain in the end!" Chen Chao shouted excitedly in the voice chat.

Wang Ling grunted in response, his reaction indifferent.

"But Lingzi, I still want to ask, why do you keep letting Lulu go?" Chen Chao asked, winking and making faces. Even he, who was slow to react, could sense something was up.

Wang Ling paused for two seconds before offering an impeccable reason: "Sun Rong was just playing around. They're all classmates. It's too cruel and not good."

Chen Chao: "...Awesome." He couldn't refute this reason.

Just then, in Wang Ling's friend list, the statuses of [Rong Guang Huan Fa] and [Xuan Feng Qing Qing Zi] changed to "in the queue" again.

Wang Ling's gaze lingered on the two side-by-side IDs for a moment.

Then, he calmly moved the cursor and sent a team invitation to [the number one brute in the Number 60 High School].

"Chao, continue."

"Come on! Lingzi, what should we do this game? Let's continue to crush them in the bottom lane?" Chen Chao rubbed his hands together, eager to get started.

Wang Ling did not answer, but directly clicked "Start Matching".

The invisible laws of nature once again quietly shifted the pointers of the matching system.

...

Inside the game room, Liu Kaichuan instantly locked onto [Fiona, the Peerless Swordswoman].

This is his trump card, his signature hero, whom he is confident can defeat anything.

Sun Rong chose the Void Queen jungler, a strong pick in the current meta, to focus on supporting the top lane.

"Don't worry, Sun! If you can gank for me just once this game, I can completely dominate the enemy top lane!" Liu Kaichuan declared confidently. "Fiora is a counter to all melee champions!"

However, when the red icons on the fourth and fifth floors of the opposite building lit up, Liu Kaichuan's smile instantly froze.

Fourth floor (red square): [Weapon Master Jax]!

Fifth floor (red section): [LeBlanc, the Enchantress]!

Then I looked at the ID...

Fourth Floor: [L]!

Fifth floor: [The number one brute in Number 60 High School]!

"Holy crap?! It's them again?!"

Liu Kaichuan nearly jumped up from his gaming chair, his voice trembling: "This is like a persistent ghost!"

Sun Rong was also stunned, then her lips twitched slightly, and she thought to herself, "This is too much of a coincidence..."

She was almost certain that this could not be explained by a typical matching mechanism.

[All] Whirlwind Qingqingzi (Peerless Swordswoman): ...Brother, what a coincidence!

[Everyone] L (Weapon Master): ...

Wang Ling's response was still shockingly concise.

Liu Kaichuan took a deep breath, forcibly suppressing the turmoil in his heart, and said to Sun Rong in the team voice chat: "It's okay! Sun! Weapon Master is indeed theoretically a counter to Fiora, but that's just theory! It's all about the ID! I've played Fiora thousands of times, my skill level is overwhelming! Watch how I crush him with my skills!"

His fighting spirit was rekindled. A weapons master? Just a Tigger!

Before Lady Fiona's dueling dance, they are all toys!

Looking at Liu Kaichuan's inexplicably confident demeanor, Sun Rong couldn't help but remind him, "President Liu, Wang Ling... he's very meticulous in his operations, so be careful."

"Don't worry!" Liu Kaichuan said dismissively, "Just because you're good at playing ADC doesn't mean you can handle top lane! Top lane is a very complex game, and he can't handle it!"

...

Game loading screen, Sword Maiden VS Weapon Master.

Chen Chao asked curiously in the voice chat, "Lingzi, why are you suddenly playing top lane? And you even picked Jax? This champion is strong in the late game, and it's not easy to lane against Fiora in the early game."

Wang Ling responded calmly: "Train heroes."

Chen Chao: "..." I don't believe you! Draven was practicing a champion just now?

The match begins.

Liu Kai's Fiora played very aggressively, attempting to use her Q skill [Sky Slash] to poke and harass the enemy from level one.

However, Wang Ling's weapon mastery was exceptionally cunning, always using minions to block the Sword Maiden's most comfortable attack angle.
