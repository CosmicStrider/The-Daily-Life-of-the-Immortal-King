Chapter 2371 The Strategist's Actions

In the Starry Sky Esports Center, moonlight streamed through the windows at the end of the seventh-floor corridor in Building B, spreading a cold, silvery sheen across the floor.

In room 709, Wang Ling lay on the bed with his hands behind his head and his eyes staring at the ceiling.

In the next room, 708, Sun Rong turned over, and the mattress made a slight creaking sound.

Wang Ling could hear her breathing, which was a little faster than usual, so she probably wasn't asleep yet.

He turned to the side and picked up the bag of limited edition instant noodles that Sun Rong had given him from the bedside table.

The packaging is exquisite, featuring a gold-stamped logo celebrating Ling Shi Fu's anniversary. Inside are several flavors... all limited editions that are not easy to find on the market.

Wang Ling tore open the spicy crayfish flavored packet.

The spicy aroma mixed with the sweetness of seafood filled the room.

He picked up a piece and put it in his mouth.

It has a moderate level of spiciness, with a slightly sweet aftertaste.

It tastes better than the regular flavor.

Wang Ling chewed slowly, his gaze fixed on the crescent moon outside the window.

The emotional module of the sealing talisman began to gnaw at my chest, sending a familiar burning sensation through me.

It was as if something wanted to pop out from inside, but was forcibly pushed back in.

Wang Ling frowned.

He placed the remaining half-pack of instant noodles on the bedside table, turned over, and closed his eyes.

...

Meanwhile, on the third floor of Building A, there is a small meeting room.

The lights were dimmed, with only the holographic projection screen in the center of the conference table emitting a faint blue light.

The four people, Jiu Gong Liangzi, Zhuo Yi, Wang Zhen, and Liu Qingyi, sat around.

Xia Zhinuan sat near the door, holding a cup of hot water in both hands, her face still somewhat pale.

"So, Luo Xinghe wants to meet you at the Nebula Cafe in the city center tomorrow afternoon?"

Ryoko Kugimiya tapped the table lightly with her folding fan, her tone calm.

"Yes." Xia Zhinuan nodded: "He said there are some things that can't be explained clearly over the phone."

"What did you say to him?" Wang Zhen asked.

"I said... I would go." Xia Zhinu bit her lip. "But I'm scared. That post on his Weibo was clearly trying to influence his fans. I'm worried that if we meet, he'll force me to do something even worse..."

"He won't," Zhuo Yi said calmly, taking a sip of tea. "At least until he gets the evidence he wants, he won't do anything to you. He needs you as an inside man to prove that Wang Ling is the one with the problem."

Immediately afterwards, the members of the Strategist Alliance started to complain about Xia Zhinuan using the "team communication technique" right in front of her.

Liu Qingyi crossed her arms, snorted coldly, and transmitted her voice: "A clown jumping around. If you really want to touch him, you won't even need to lift a finger."

"But we can't let Master make a move." Zhuo Yi looked at Liu Qingyi and transmitted his voice, "A war of words must be fought in the manner of a war of words. Master's identity cannot be exposed, and the peace of Number 60 High School must be maintained. Therefore, Xia Zhinuan's role is crucial."

At this moment, he turned to Xia Zhinuan, his tone softening: "You don't need to be afraid. When we meet, someone will secretly protect you. You just need to show guilt and wavering, but don't agree to any of his requests. Just stall him and find out his specific plans."

"Specifically... how do I apply it?" Xia Zhinuan asked nervously.

Ryoko Kugimiya opened her tablet and pulled up a document: "This is based on Luo Xinghe's psychological analysis report. He will most likely test whether you really regret it first, and then he may propose a few options: such as asking you to continue acting in the subsequent competition, or asking you to secretly film Wang Ling's abnormal moments, or... asking you to post some ambiguous words on social media."

She pushed the tablet toward Xia Zhinuan: "What you need to do is show hesitation, and finally delay it by saying that you need time to consider or that the risk is too great. But at the same time, you need to reveal that Wang Ling is indeed a bit strange, to arouse his interest and make him feel that you still have value to him."

Xia Zhinuan quickly glanced at the report, her pupils dilating in shock.

The report detailed every word Luo Xinghe might say, how she should respond, and even provided suggestions on tone and facial expressions.

They're frighteningly professional.

"This...is all based on your analysis?" she couldn't help but ask.

"Love... no, it's interpersonal psychological analysis, which is our team's area of expertise." Ryoko Kugimiya lied without changing her expression, covering her mouth with her folding fan: "Anyway, follow this, and there shouldn't be any major problems."

Wang Zhen stepped forward to add, "Oh, right, remember to turn on your phone's recording function. Although Luo Xinghe might check it, you have a backup plan."

He took a small, button-sized silver ornament from his pocket and handed it to Xia Zhinuan: "This is a psionic miniature recorder that can be blocked from regular detection. Tuck it in your collar or inside your pocket; it won't be noticeable."

Xia Zhinuan took the "button," her fingers trembling slightly.

She suddenly realized that the struggle she had been drawn into was on a much higher level than she had imagined.

Zhuo Yi noticed her unease and said gently, "Xia Zhinuan, you are doing the right thing. Luo Xinghe's behavior has crossed the line. If it continues, it will not only harm Wang Ling, but also the reputation of the training camp, and even affect other innocent team members. Your choice now is to stop the damage."

These words calmed Xia Zhinuan down.

She gripped the recording button tightly and nodded vigorously: "I understand. I will do it well."

"Very good." Ryoko Kugimiya closed her tablet. "Tomorrow at 2 PM, Nebula Cafe. We will have people in the next booth and around the perimeter. If anything goes wrong, your bodyguards will intervene immediately. Remember, safety first."

The meeting lasted for another ten minutes or so, during which some details were refined.

It was almost 11 p.m. when the meeting ended.

Xia Zhinuan left first, and Jiugong Liangzi and the others sat for a while longer.

"How are the preparations going on the public opinion front?" Wang Zhen asked.

Zhuo Yi brought up another screen, which displayed real-time data monitoring from several social media platforms.

"Luo Xinghe's Weibo post has been forwarded more than 3,000 times and commented on over 10,000 times, and its popularity is slowly rising. Several big fans in his fan group are leading the speculation, focusing on the training camp's dark secrets, preferential treatment for those with connections, and suspected cheating."

He swiped the screen and pulled up a prepared press release: "I've already contacted several gaming media outlets we regularly work with, and we'll release a batch of positive reports tomorrow morning, focusing on the scale of this training camp and the diversity of the participating teams. My mentor doesn't like high-profile events, so I'll keep his information as clean as possible and report it in the collective name of Number 60 High School. Use positive information to dilute negative speculation."

"Not enough." Ryoko Kugimiya shook her head: "Luo Xinghe is a streamer, good at stirring up fans' emotions. Positive press releases are too official, and fans don't buy into that. We need a more grassroots voice."

She looked at Zhuo Yi: "Can you mobilize the navy resources?"

Zhuo Yi grinned: "This was prepared a long time ago. Three batches of accounts: one batch pretending to be casual fans to praise the training camp and Number 60 High School, one batch pretending to be rational fans to analyze the match recordings to refute the cheating theory, and another batch... pretending to be Luo Xinghe's haters to dig up his past dark history of swearing during live streams."

Liu Qingyi raised an eyebrow: "Fight black with black?"

"That's called shifting the focus," Zhuo Yi said. "When public opinion turns to the fact that Luo Xinghe himself is not clean and that Luo Xinghe can't afford to lose, who cares about what rhythm he originally wanted to set?"

After listening, Liu Qingyi nodded: "Okay. But we need to be careful not to let it turn into large-scale cyberbullying. Our goal is to resolve the crisis, not to create new conflicts."

"Don't worry, I know what I'm doing," Zhuo Yi said, patting his chest.

"Also," Ryoko Kugimiya said, remembering something, "as for Sun Rong... shouldn't we be moving on to the third phase tomorrow?"

At this moment, Zhuo Yi glanced at the schedule: "Well, according to the original plan, tomorrow morning's team combat training will simulate a sudden crisis. I asked the system team to add a little something to the competition, so Junior Sister Sun will encounter some minor trouble."

Wang Zhen's eyes lit up: "A hero saving a damsel in distress?"
