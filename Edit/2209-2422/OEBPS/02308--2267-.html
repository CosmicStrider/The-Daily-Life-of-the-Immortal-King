Chapter 2267 The Gift from the Spirit of the Ancient Land

Inside the Earth's core cavity, time and laws seemed to be gripped tightly by Wang Ling's gently clasped fingers, finally freezing in place.

Absolute silence reigns supreme.

Those black shadows that were previously baring their fangs and radiating endless malice and curses are now like paintings cast into nothingness, their "meaning" being brutally erased by an invisible giant hand.

There was no explosion, no screams, not even a single particle of light dissipating.

They simply... no longer exist.

From the most basic constituent particles, to the evil will they carry, and to all the traces left in this world at this point in time.

Everything was completely "negated" under Wang Ling's single thought.

It's like deleting erroneous code from a system with the highest privileges—clean, efficient, and without leaving any redundancy.

Sun Rong's pupils reflected this incomprehensible scene.

The scabbard of the Ohe River sword fell silent on her wrist, as if it too was awed by this supreme power and dared not utter the slightest hum.

She felt her throat go dry, and a sense of awe, stemming from an instinct for life, swept over her, making her feel almost suffocated.

This is not fear, but the inevitable tremor that lower-dimensional beings feel when they glimpse the manifestation of the supreme law.

Wang Ling's expression remained unchanged, and even the shimmering seven-colored Daoist aura in his eyes quietly disappeared, returning to his lifeless, expressionless eyes.

He patted his hands, which weren't even dusty, as if he had just finished a trivial chore.

Although it was just a thought.

Their gaze fell upon the area beneath the massive heart of the planet.

Without the continued erosion and curse of the Outer God's shadow, the heartbeat, condensed from the pure energy of the earth, seemed to have gained a little strength.

The filthy energy threads that entangled it were still there, but they dimmed considerably in that sudden moment.

Below, there is a hunched and ancient figure composed of light and shadow—the Spirit of the Ancient Land.

After it manifested, its painful groans ceased.

It slowly and with great difficulty raised its head. On its blurred face, the two points of light representing its eyes flickered intensely, filled with disbelief and shock, and... a faint hope that had been dormant for countless ages.

"...Is it you?" An extremely old and weak, yet infinitely vast thought resonated intermittently between Wang Ling and Sun Rong's hearts, carrying a heavy feeling as if rocks were being rubbed together.

It seemed to recognize Wang Ling's presence, but for a moment it wasn't entirely sure. In the end, it simply buried all the shock deep within its heart.

Faced with Wang Ling, it dared not speak nonsense.

"An existence that transcends the rules..."

Wang Ling did not respond, but simply watched it quietly. Back then, the monk Jin Deng lost an eye in order to see his presence clearly, and Wang Ling never expected that the spirit of the ancient land would resonate with him.

Sun Rong took a deep breath, trying to calm herself down from the shock. She stepped forward and bowed respectfully: "Sun Rong, a cultivator from one of the sixty sects on the surface, greets the senior spirit of the ancient land. We are here to help you."

The spirit of the ancient land slowly swept over Sun Rong, especially lingering for a moment on the Ao Hai in her hand and the red lotus in her arms, with a touch of gentleness and recognition in that spirit.

"The pure power of the earth and... a protective heart..."

"Thank you all for helping me..."

Its will was still weak, but much more coherent than before.

"Senior, how are you? Those evil things..." Sun Rong asked with concern, while glancing around warily.

Although the dark figures were gone, there seemed to be cold eyes peering out from behind those honeycomb-like holes, but no one dared to make any further moves.

"For now... it's alright..."

The spirit of the ancient land carried a deep weariness in its thoughts: "They are the minions of the [Void Nightmare]... subsisting on the very essence of the devouring stars..."

"Void Nightmare?" Sun Rong memorized the name.

"An ancient aggregate of greed...from the abyss beyond the star's core..."

The spirit of the ancient land was filled with disgust and fear: "It has its eye on me... attempting to assimilate me into a nest that will erode all worlds..."

"Did Bai Zhe bring them here?" Sun Rong immediately thought of that name.

"A traitor to the Dragonborn..."

The spirit of the ancient land suddenly surged with intense emotion, filled with rage: "He tore open the tiny rift, attracting the initial attention... but the arrival of the darkness was not his doing alone... there were hidden dangers deep within the earth, he merely detonated them..."

The amount of information was enormous, and Sun Rong was trying her best to digest it.

It seems that Bai Zhe was the trigger, but the Earth's core itself also has problems.

Wang Ling seemed uninterested in this history. His gaze swept over the filthy chains that still clung to the planet's heart and the spirit of the ancient land, and he frowned slightly.

These things are an eyesore.

He raised his hand and lightly traced a line in the air with his fingertips.

There was no sound, no light effects.

But those incredibly tough chains of filth, strong enough to render even Immortal Venerables helpless, melted silently from the point of contact with the planet's heart, like snow basking in the sunlight, and then rapidly spread upwards.

The process was incredibly fast.

In almost the blink of an eye, all the entangled black chains vanished, as if they had never existed.

The planet's heart shuddered violently, and a vast and boundless life energy surged out like a burst dam, instantly filling the entire cavity!

A golden-red light suddenly shone, rendering everything sacred and warm.

"Ugh!" The spirit of the ancient land let out a long cry that sounded like both pain and liberation. Its hunched, luminous body solidified at a speed visible to the naked eye and suddenly straightened up a bit.

Although still blurry, it no longer appears so fragile.

The atmosphere of despair and pain that had enveloped this place was instantly dispelled!

Bathed in the pure energy of Mother Earth, Sun Rong felt completely relaxed. The Aohai Sword Core in her dantian spun wildly, its golden-red flame patterns absorbing energy and becoming increasingly dazzling.

She even felt that the bottleneck in her cultivation was beginning to loosen!

"Thank you... sir..." The spirit of the ancient land was filled with gratitude.

This time, its message was delivered exclusively to the king, with utmost respect.

Wang Ling then nodded almost imperceptibly, accepting the gratitude.

After he finished doing all this, his expression remained unchanged, as if he had just casually cleaned up some trash on the roadside.

His gaze shifted to the honeycomb-like holes at the edge of the cavity, and his brows furrowed slightly.

These "eyes" are still watching.

The spirit of the ancient land understood his meaning, and its grand will resounded once more, this time with an undeniable majesty: "This place... is off-limits to prying!"

Buzz—!

An invisible wave swept through all the holes, and those "windows" connecting to the outside world instantly blurred, distorted, and were eventually completely cut off.

The scattered images from the Thousand Caves City, Lava Ridge, and even the surface base have completely disappeared.

The entire core cavity of the Earth has temporarily become an absolute "sealed room".

After all this was done, the light and shadow of the Ancient Land Spirit seemed to dim a bit more, clearly indicating that it had consumed a lot of energy.

It looked at Wang Ling and Sun Rong again, its thoughts carrying a hint of pleading: "Powerful beings, merciful Miss Sun Rong... I beg you..."

The light and shadow raised a hand and pointed in a certain direction from the cavity.

What appeared to be a solid crystal wall gradually rippled under the guidance of the ancient spirit, revealing a hidden entrance to a narrow passage that could only allow one person to pass through at a time.

Deep within the passageway, an even more ancient and pure earthy atmosphere permeates the air.
