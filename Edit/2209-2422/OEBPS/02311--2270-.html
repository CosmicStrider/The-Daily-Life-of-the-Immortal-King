Chapter 2270 You're disturbing my instant noodles!

Before the slight dizziness from the spatial shift had completely subsided, Sun Rong was already shocked by the horrific battle before her eyes.

Inside the main passage of Thousand Caves City, the sounds of frenzied explosions and roars of energy light were incessant.

The purple light around Liu Qingyi was not as intense as it had been at the beginning, but her fist energy was still fierce, repelling the attacking subterranean creature. Her breathing was noticeably heavier.

Cracks spread across the array plate in Ryoko Kugimiya's hand, and the defensive light barrier that it was maintaining flickered, as if it would shatter at any moment.

"Liangzi! Those shadow bats on the right are about to self-destruct!" Liu Qingyi shouted urgently.

Ryoko Kunomiya gritted her teeth and laboriously traced lines on the array plate with her fingers. A thin wall of light rose up, but it was clearly insufficient to completely block it.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao held their ground at a distant pass, both covered in wounds. Guo Hao had almost run out of talismans, and Chen Chao had to lean on his sword to stay upright, but they still managed to hold off several lava hounds that tried to charge at them.

Zhang Linyan's arrows were still accurate, but her quiver was already half empty.

Li Changzhe's Myriad Scale Armor was dimming, clearly indicating that his spiritual energy reserves were almost depleted.

Su Xingyue's swordsmanship was still formidable, but her brows were furrowed with exhaustion.

The entire team was like a small boat that could capsize at any moment in a storm.

The appearance of Wang Ling and Sun Rong instantly caused a strange ripple.

The first thing to change was not the monsters, but the maze itself.

The moment Wang Ling stepped onto the ground of the passage, a seemingly ordinary rock beneath his feet suddenly sank an inch without warning.

Click.

A very faint click of a mechanism was heard.

Buzz—!

Above everyone's heads, a previously unremarkable dome-shaped rock wall suddenly lit up with countless ancient runes!

These runes had clearly been dormant for countless years, but at this moment they were inexplicably triggered, instantly condensing into a vast amount of earth vein energy, transforming into an incomparably thick earth-yellow light shield that enveloped everyone!

Bang bang bang bang!

Countless frenzied subterranean creatures were caught off guard and crashed into the suddenly appearing ancient protective wall, their heads bleeding and their heads spinning!

Even the few shadow bats that wanted to self-destruct crashed headfirst into the light shield, completely isolating them from the explosive force!

Everyone: "???"

Ryoko Kugimiya stared blankly at the shattering array plate in her hand, then at the incredibly solid, rune-flowing ancient light barrier, her mind momentarily blank: "This is... the ancient defensive array of Thousand Caves City? It's been ineffective for a long time... how could it suddenly..."

Liu Qingyi also froze, realizing that it must have been that man who made the move!

The beast tide's offensive stalled. The subterranean creatures and the Glass Clan seemed bewildered by this sudden turn of events, temporarily ceasing their attacks and anxiously roaring and pacing around the light barrier.

Wang Ling stood still, seemingly startled by the sudden appearance of the light shield. He subconsciously took a half step back, only to have his heel "coincidentally" hit another loose stone brick.

Gurgle!

The stone brick rolled and hit an inconspicuous protrusion on the wall.

Boom!

Suddenly, the rock wall on one side of the passage split open, revealing a narrow tunnel hidden behind it. A pure vortex of psionic energy surged out from it, instantly blowing over the exhausted group.

Blown by this psychic wind, everyone felt a surge of energy, and their depleted spiritual power began to recover rapidly! Even their wounds started to itch slightly and began to heal!

"It's the wind of the revitalized spiritual veins!" Ryoko Kunomiya exclaimed, "How could the long-dried-up branches suddenly...?"

Everyone's eyes involuntarily focused again on Wang Ling, who had just "accidentally" kicked the stone brick.

Wang Ling stared blankly at the suddenly appearing passageway and the surging spiritual wind, seemingly confused about the situation.

To cover his embarrassment, he silently took out a packet of Jade Heart Jelly from his pocket, opened it, and took a sip.

Sun Rong: "..." She forced herself not to laugh. Ao Hai and she were of one mind, and a subtle sense of resonance came through them, making her vaguely feel that these two "coincidences" were probably not so simple.

But she wisely didn't point it out.

"Who cares! Heaven is on my side! Recover quickly!" Chen Chao roared, seizing the time to absorb the spiritual wind.

However, this good fortune did not last long.

After the initial chaos, the subterranean creatures and the Glass Clan seemed to be driven by some kind of will, becoming even more frenzied.

They began to relentlessly attack the ancient light shield, and the ripples on the shield became increasingly dense.

Worse still, several enormous burrowing beetles, their carapaces shimmering with a glassy luster, burst forth from the earth. They opened their massive jaws, and dark red destructive energy began to condense, their target aimed directly at the light shield!

"Oh no! It's the Glass Insect Guardians! Their power can corrode the array!" Ryoko Kugimiya's face turned pale.

Just as the light shield was about to give way, Wang Ling seemed to be startled by the frenzied attacks outside, causing his hand to tremble. The half-eaten jade heart jelly suddenly flew out of his hand, tracing an arc, and just happened to pass through a tiny energy fluctuation gap in the light shield caused by the continuous attacks, before falling out.

Snap.

The jelly box fell to the ground, and the remaining jelly and juice splashed out, landing precisely on the eyes and mouthparts of the glass beetles that were gathering energy.

hiss--!

A strange, corrosive sound of strong acid erupted!

Suddenly, the glass beetles let out an extremely shrill scream. The parts of them splashed with jelly juice emitted puffs of white smoke, and their hard glass shells were quickly corroded into small holes. The dark red energy that had been condensed to half its maximum also went out of control and exploded violently in their mouths!

Boom boom boom!

With several muffled thuds, these strongest insect guards were blasted into pieces by their own out-of-control energy!

The aftershocks of the explosion also swept through a large area of subterranean creatures, clearing out a vast region.

The entire battlefield fell into a deathly silence once again.

Everyone stared in disbelief at the mess outside, then slowly turned to look at Wang Ling, who was looking at his fallen jelly with a pained expression.

What... what kind of incredible luck is this?!

A jelly that slipped from a hand passed right through a tiny gap, splashed right onto the most critical enemy's vital spot, and even triggered an energy backlash.

"Holy crap!" Guo Hao muttered, "Wang Ling! Your luck is beyond lucky charm! It's so precise!"

Chen Chaoshen agreed: "From now on, when going into dungeons, we must bring the token! We don't have to do anything, we can just stand there and eat snacks!"

Looking at the completely melted jelly outside, Wang Ling showed a hint of genuine regret, after all, it was quite delicious.

Then he quietly took out a new bag of snacks from his pocket—instant noodles with the words "Hellfire" printed on them.

He unpacked the package and began to habitually crush the bread, making a "crunch crunch" sound.

However, this seemingly ordinary "click" sound, in the quiet environment at this moment, seemed to carry a strange rhythm.

The sound traveled through the light shield, and the previously agitated and furious subterranean creatures suddenly became somewhat... sluggish.

They shook their heads, as if they had heard a sound that made them extremely uncomfortable.

The few members of the Glass Clan who were hiding among them, attempting to infiltrate through their disguises, covered their ears in pain. The disguises on their bodies began to become unstable, flashing with seven-colored light, thus revealing their identities.

"This sound..." Liu Qingyi noticed the abnormality immediately.

Ryoko Kugimiya immediately activated the array: "An extremely high-frequency psionic resonance wave has been detected! Although it is very weak, it is precisely what is interfering with the pseudo-stability of the Glass Clan and the neural perception of the subterranean creatures!"

The source was... the sound of Wang Ling squeezing instant noodles?

She didn't dare to continue...

Everyone was puzzled, wondering what had happened.

Only the crunchy bag of instant noodles in Wang Ling's hand echoed in the air.

Wang Ling seemed completely unaware that he had once again "unintentionally" performed a great service.

He focused intently on kneading the dough, and when he felt it was almost done, he nodded in satisfaction, then picked up a pinch, ready to put it in his mouth.

At that moment, the mutation occurred again!

Deep within the maze, the spatial fluctuations that had been hidden for so long finally reached their peak!

An irresistible, immense spatial force instantly enveloped everyone!

"Forced teleportation!" Ryoko Kumiya screamed, "Watch out!"

A flash of light!

An ancient light barrier! The winds of the spiritual veins! The beast tide outside... all the sights vanished in an instant!

When everyone's vision returned, they found themselves in a completely unfamiliar, enormous, circular arena-like environment!

The arena is surrounded by countless honeycomb-like cave entrances, as if countless eyes are peering into the darkness.

In the center of the arena stood a tall humanoid figure made of crystal glass—a projection of Bai Zhe's clone!

He clapped his hands, the sound echoing coldly through his glass-like body:

"A fantastic performance, and... amazing luck."

"But fortunately, I won't always be on your side, Wang Ling."

Bai Zhe's clone stared intently at Wang Ling, who was looking around blankly with a handful of instant noodles in his hand. His tone was filled with suppressed anger and a hint of barely perceptible fear.

"Now, let's begin... the real game."

Wang Ling looked at Bai Zhe's clone, then at the instant noodles he was holding in his hand. After hesitating for a moment, he still put the crumbs of noodles into his mouth.

Click.

He chewed for a moment, then looked calmly at Bai Zhe and whispered to him, "You're bothering me, I'm eating instant noodles."
