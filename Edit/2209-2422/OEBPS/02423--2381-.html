Chapter 2381 The Art of Backstabbing

The competition is still ongoing.

However, Luo Xinghe's team fell into a brief silence in their internal voice chat.

Chen Chao cleared his throat: "So, how do we go about it? Continue to stick together?"

He asked the question casually, and there was no trace of seriousness in his tone.

"We can only stick together." Luo Xinghe forced himself to calm down, his voice suppressing his anger: "Even if the clown gets fed, he's still just an assassin. If the five of us go together, he can't kill everyone instantly. Thresh and Lee Sin, focus on protecting the backline. Li Changzhe's Ornn, look for opportunities to initiate team fights."

He spoke clearly and logically, which is indeed the mindset a professional anchor should have.

But no one responded.

After clearing a wave of minions, Guo Hao slowly began to speak: "From a data perspective, Shaco currently has the best equipment in the game, and with the Hextech rune effect, his burst damage is already overflowing. Even if we group up, as long as he can kill one of our cores, we will collapse."

"Then tell me how to fight?" Luo Xinghe asked impatiently.

"Delay it," Guo Hao said, shrugging. "Wait until his period of strength is over, or until our equipment is complete. But judging from the current pace, the probability is not high."

This is tantamount to saying nothing at all.

Luo Xinghe took a deep breath, suppressing the urge to curse.

He glanced at the scoreboard.

Wang Ling's Jester is currently at 7/0/3, with 76 last hits. The [Phantom Dancer] item is fully crafted in his inventory, and he is currently working on [Draaxsa's Duskblade].

With this kind of equipment, a clown can sneak up and send a 0/4 Kai'Sa back to the fountain with a single slash.

"I'll develop on the side," Luo Xinghe decided to avoid battle for now.

He carefully maneuvered Kai'Sa along the edge of the map, keeping close to the wall with every step, as if afraid a clown might suddenly jump out from the shadows.

But what you fear most is what happens most often.

Just as he reached the bushes in the middle of the map, preparing to use his W skill to finish off a siege minion from a distance—

"Hehehe..."

A strange laugh came from the air.

Luo Xinghe's heart tightened, and his fingers unconsciously pressed on the Flash button.

The next second, two clowns appeared in his field of vision, one after the other!

It's the real body plus an illusion again!

"Damn it!" Luo Xinghe cursed. Kai'Sa used her E skill to accelerate backward, while simultaneously using her W skill [Void Seeker] to try and mark the true form of the Shaco.

But Wang Ling's actions were ridiculously fast.

The Joker moved to the side, and the energy pulse almost grazed the edge of his painted mask.

Then it simply vanished into thin air!

Luo Xinghe felt a chill run down his spine and frantically pressed Flash to try and create distance.

But in the instant he made his move—

The Joker appeared behind him!

Backstab!

Basic attack!

[Deception] Summoner's skill resets invisibility, then basic attack!

Two slashes, just two more!

Kai'Sa's health bar was instantly emptied!

"You have been slain!"

0/5.

Luo Xinghe stared at the black and white screen, his fingers trembling so much he could barely hold the mouse.

He didn't even see how the Joker appeared behind him.

That speed, that anticipation...

"He's cheating, isn't he?!" Luo Xinghe roared in the voice chat, "How could he possibly predict my flash location?!"

The team chat was completely silent.

A few seconds later, Chen Chao said quietly, "Xinghe, this isn't the first time your son has predicted a flash. Don't make a fuss. Just because you can't do something doesn't mean others can't."

These words were like a slap in the face, landing hard on Luo Xinghe's cheek.

He opened his mouth, wanting to refute, but couldn't utter a single word.

In everyone's eyes, Luo Xinghe was like a fool, manipulated by Wang Ling.

"..." Luo Xinghe closed his eyes and leaned back in his chair in despair.

...

At the same time, from Wang Ling's perspective.

Sun Rong's cat perched on the clown's shoulder, watching as the clown swiftly defeated Kai'Sa with two slashes and then silently disappeared, the whole process flowing smoothly.

"Wang Ling, you're so amazing..." she couldn't help but whisper, the cat's tail swaying gently on the clown's shoulder.

"Hmm." Wang Ling responded, controlling the clown to burrow into the bushes and wait for the skill to cool down.

Although he almost killed someone without taking any damage in the previous wave, the cat still habitually gave him an E skill, "Extreme Energy".

A pale green healing light shone on the clown.

"Actually, you don't need to heal me," Wang Ling suddenly said.

"Huh?" Sun Rong was taken aback: "Why?"

"My health is full."

"......"

Sun Rong blushed, only then realizing that she had been so nervous that she hadn't even checked her health bar before randomly unleashing her skills.

"I'm sorry..." she said softly.

"It's nothing." Wang Ling paused for a moment, then added, "Thank you."

The two words were spoken very softly, but Sun Rong heard them clearly.

Her heart skipped a beat, and the corners of her mouth involuntarily turned up.

The cat happily wagged its tail on the clown's shoulder, its little purple paws gently resting on the clown's shoulder armor.

Xia Zhinuan's swordswoman witnessed this scene.

She was clearing minions near the mid lane when she caught a glimpse of the cat wagging its tail on the clown's shoulder out of the corner of her eye, and her eyes flickered slightly.

As a witness who was once used by Luo Xinghe but has now "returned to the right path," Xia Zhinuan understands better than anyone the strange atmosphere between Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

She pursed her lips and whispered a reminder in the team voice chat: "Wang Ling, Luo Xinghe's mentality has already collapsed. But given his personality... he might go even further."

"Hmm." Wang Ling's reply remained concise.

He knew.

From Luo Xinghe's gaze in the cafeteria yesterday to the online storm this morning, this person has gone astray.

But Wang Ling didn't care.

What he cares about is...

At that moment, Sun Rong's voice came: "Wang Ling, how long until your next ultimate move? I feel like they might be grouping up and pushing hard."

"Ten seconds." Wang Ling glanced at the skill bar.

"Then I'll come down and get a view first," Sun Rong said, maneuvering the cat to jump off the clown and drift towards the river.

But the moment she left the clown—

From the shadows on the flank, a hook suddenly flew out!

It's Zhang Yu's hammer!

It turns out that the remaining four members of the Luo Xinghe team did not retreat, but instead used Kaisa's death as bait to ambush on the flank!

"Sun Rong, be careful!" Xia Zhinuan exclaimed.

Sun Rong reacted quickly; the cat immediately tried to use W to re-attach to the nearest Irelia.

But Hammerstone's hook came too suddenly!

It was about to hit!

Just then—

"bass!"

A flash of golden light.

The clown flashed in front of the cat!

The hook struck the clown, dragging him toward where Hammerstone was!

"Wang Ling!" Sun Rong exclaimed, her eyes wide.

Wang Ling reacted even faster.

The instant he was hooked, he pressed the R key!

【phantom】!

The Joker's true form was split in two during the dragging process, and the illusion appeared on Hammerstone's face.

The control effect of the hook was taken over by the illusion!

"What the hell?!" someone in the spectator hall exclaimed.
