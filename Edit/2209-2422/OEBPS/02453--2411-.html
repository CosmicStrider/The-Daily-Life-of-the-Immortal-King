Chapter 2411 Luo Xinghe's Test

Seeing the boy calmly eating instant noodles, Chen Chao opened his mouth, but in the end, he swallowed back the words of advice.

"So... how do you plan to play?" he asked in a different phrasing.

Wang Ling put the last piece of instant noodles into his mouth, thought for a moment, and said, "I'll see how it goes."

Chen Chao: "..."

This answer is the same as no answer at all.

The starting hand seals for the first three forms are the Separating Fire Seal, the Yang Flame Seal, and the Burning Heaven Seal. If you can interrupt him before he completes the hand seals, you will have a chance."

Wang Ling looked at Guo Hao and nodded: "Mm."

I don't know if they listened or not.

Li Changzhe was so anxious that he scratched his head and cheeks. The matter of Number 60 High School had nothing to do with him. If it weren't for his experience in the inner world and the feelings he had developed with Wang Ling, Li Changzhe would not have gotten involved in this mess.

Although he thought it was unlikely that Wang Ling could hide his true level, Li Changzhe still tried his best to help, based on the principle that they were brothers: "Wang Ling, how about we discuss a tactic? For example, you can rush in at the beginning and not give him a chance to cast a spell?"

Wang Ling shook his head: "Too troublesome."

"What should we do then?"

"Let's see."

"..." The three of them were completely at a loss.

2:50 p.m.

There are ten minutes left before the game starts.

A large crowd had already gathered around the E-03 battle stage.

After news spread that Wang Ling had defeated Li Haoran with ordinary martial arts in the previous match, many students who hadn't paid attention to the competition came over to see if this "oddball" from Number 60 High School could create another miracle.

In the third row of section B of the audience seats, Sun Rong held her drink, feeling even more nervous than if she were playing the game herself.

Ryoko Kugimiya noticed her emotions and gently comforted her, "Rongrong, don't worry."

"I know..." Sun Rong said softly. What she was most worried about was not Wang Ling's cultivation level, but whether Wang Ling's sealing talisman could still hold up. If it went out of control, the Foundation Establishment stage cultivator on the other side would probably be turned to ashes.

Zhuo Yi smiled and said, "Don't worry, Junior Sister Sun, he knows what he's doing."

Sun Rong was taken aback and looked at Zhuo Yi.

Zhuo Yi didn't explain further, but instead turned his gaze towards the waiting area, his eyes filled with a certain certainty.

Meanwhile, in the VIP section, several city leaders and competition judges were also talking in hushed tones.

"Wang Ling from Number 60 High School had a very impressive performance in the last match." An elderly man with gray hair stroked his beard: "To defeat a mid-Foundation Establishment cultivator with just ordinary martial arts, his body control is not simple."

The middle-aged woman wearing glasses next to him nodded: "Indeed. However, this opponent is Liu Zixuan, whose fire spirit root purity is 72%, which is already an excellent level of talent. If Wang Ling continues to use evasive tactics, it may not be enough."

"Let's wait and see." The old man squinted. "I think this kid might have a backup plan."

...

2:55.

Liu Zixuan walked out of the waiting area and stepped onto the battle stage.

Today he wore a red combat uniform specially made by the Experimental High School, with golden flame patterns embroidered on the cuffs and collar. His short hair was neatly styled, and he had a confident smile on his face.

The spiritual energy fluctuations of the late Foundation Establishment stage spread out without any concealment, and the air around him was slightly distorted, which was a natural manifestation of the overflow of fire-attribute spiritual energy.

Wow, what a powerful aura...

"Is this the pressure of a 72% pure fire elemental root? I can feel the heat even from this far away."

"Wang Ling from Number 60 High School is in trouble."

There was a buzz of discussion in the audience.

Liu Zixuan enjoys this attention.

He stood on the stage, arms crossed, eyes closed, seemingly resting, exuding the air of a master.

A few seconds later, Wang Ling also walked out of the passage.

He was still wearing that ordinary Number 60 High School uniform, zipped up to his chest, holding a half-eaten bag of barbecue-flavored instant noodles in his hand, and pouring crumbs into his mouth as he walked.

Liu Zixuan opened his eyes and frowned slightly when he saw Wang Ling's appearance.

He was somewhat displeased.

It's disrespectful to take a competition so seriously when the other side is so casual about it.

Wang Ling walked to the other side of the battle stage, finished the last of his instant noodles, folded the packaging bag and stuffed it into his pocket, then licked the pepper powder clinging to his fingertips.

The referee walked to the center of the platform, looked at the two men, and asked, "Are you both ready?"

Liu Zixuan nodded, his hands already in the starting stance.

Wang Ling nodded.

"The match begins!"

The moment the referee finished speaking, Liu Zixuan moved.

Instead of rushing to attack, he took three steps back to create distance.

At the same time, his hands quickly formed hand seals, and as his ten fingers flew, crimson spiritual energy flowed through his fingertips.

"Blazing Sun Heart Technique - Firewall Technique!"

call!

A wall of flames, three meters wide and two meters high, appeared out of thin air and stretched across the center of the battle arena.

A wave of intense heat swept over them, and even those in the front rows of the audience felt the temperature rise sharply.

"He started with an area-of-effect spell!" Guo Hao frowned from the audience. "He wants to restrict Wang Ling's movement."

Chen Chao stared intently at the stage.

Firewalls are indeed a lot of trouble.

The battle arena itself is only twenty meters square, but this firewall takes up a third of the space.

If Wang Ling wanted to get close, he would have to cross the firewall.

However, the temperature of the firewall is high enough to burn the protective spiritual power of a Foundation Establishment cultivator.

If you go around them, the activity area will be further reduced.

A very standard fire-based suppression strategy.

Liu Zixuan stood behind the firewall, a smile playing on his lips.

His hands continued forming hand seals without stopping.

"Blazing Sun Technique - Chain Fireballs!"

Three fireballs, each the size of a fist, flew from his palm, circled the wall of fire, and shot towards Wang Ling from three different angles.

Left, right, and top – this effectively blocked most of the space for dodging.

A low murmur rippled through the audience.

"Perfect coordination!"

How do I evade Wang Ling?

On the stage, Wang Ling watched the three fireballs flying towards him, but did not dodge.

He raised his right hand and made three virtual taps in the air with his index finger.

Puff, puff, puff.

The three fireballs suddenly went out when they were still a meter away from him.

Like a candle being blown out, it vanished into thin air without even a spark.

The entire room fell silent for a moment.

Liu Zixuan's smile froze.

"What happened?" he blurted out.

The audience erupted in uproar.

"How did you put out the fireball?"

"What kind of magic did Wang Ling use? I didn't see any hand seals!"

"And there wasn't even the slightest fluctuation of spiritual energy!"

In the VIP section, several judges sat up straight.

The white-haired old man squinted: "That was just now..."

"Spiritual energy being released outwards? No, there's no spiritual energy fluctuation." The bespectacled female judge frowned. "It's more like... interference with the spiritual energy structure inside the fireball."

"How is that possible?" another middle-aged judge questioned. "Although the Fireball Technique is a basic spell, its internal spiritual energy structure is very stable. To interfere with it from the outside, one needs a profound understanding of fire-attribute spiritual energy and precise control. This requires at least the Golden Core stage..."

He didn't finish speaking, but the meaning was clear.

This cannot be done in the early stages of foundation building.

On stage, Liu Zixuan's expression turned serious.

He stared at Wang Ling, reassessing his opponent.
