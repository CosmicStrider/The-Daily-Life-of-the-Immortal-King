Chapter 2366 Luo Xinghe Turns Evil

"Purification?" Luo Xinghe was taken aback.

Did Kuchi bring purification?

When...

He had no time to think, because Kuch had already turned around, and Gatling gun bullets rained down on him.

"You want to fight back?" Luo Xinghe sneered, "I have an ultimate shield, how are you going to beat me when you're at half health?"

Kai'Sa continues to deal damage.

But soon, Luo Xinghe realized something was wrong.

Corki's damage is too high!

Each basic attack carried additional damage from Sheen, and with the machine gun's continuous output, Kai'Sa's shield was instantly shattered.

Corki himself, although with lower health, moved extremely cunningly, causing half of Kai'Sa's basic attacks to miss.

"How is this possible..." Luo Xinghe looked at his rapidly decreasing health and finally realized the problem.

Corki's forward W move just now wasn't to escape, but to adjust his position.

Adjust to a position where he cannot easily retreat.

Kai'Sa is currently deep in the red side's jungle. To escape back to her tower, she must pass through Corki's gun.

And Kuchi is stuck on that only possible route.

Luo Xinghe was incredulous; he had fallen for the same trick twice...

"This is outrageous!" Luo Xinghe felt a chill run down his spine: "It was a trap from the very first missile."

He deliberately got hit by the skill, then intentionally let himself use his ultimate to rush in, and then counterattacked to surround him.

"You..." Luo Xinghe spoke into the channel, his voice dry and hoarse: "You plotted against me?"

Wang Ling didn't reply, but simply raised his modified rifle and fired a final normal attack, killing Luo Xinghe.

"Bang!"

Luo Xinghe died for the second time.

0/2.

"......"

Luo Xinghe stared at the black and white screen, his fingers trembling uncontrollably.

Shame.

Moreover, it's a humiliation of being intellectually crushed.

The viewing hall.

After a brief silence, a buzz of discussion arose.

"This time... Luo Xinghe is completely overwhelmed by the show."

"Kuchi brings purification? Why didn't I think of that?"

"Cleansing away Kai'Sa's W mark and preventing the plasma from stacking is ruthless."

Chen Chao stood up excitedly: "Your son! Amazing! This awareness! Luo Xinghe has been completely manipulated!"

Guo Hao also praised, "It's not just about awareness. If you watch the replay, Corki's three ultimates were carefully designed in terms of angle. The first one forced him to reposition, the second one blocked his retreat, and the third one forced out Kai'Sa's W. Then he deliberately took the W, luring Kai'Sa to use her ultimate to close the gap... It was a whole set of interconnected plans."

Zhou Rui added, "Moreover, the Cleanse was brought from the start. This shows that Wang Ling had already planned how to counter Kai'Sa when he was selecting champions."

Li Changzhe clicked his tongue: "It's only been six minutes, and you've already calculated to this extent... Wang Ling's talent for cultivation is lacking, but his talent for games is top-notch!"

"..." Sun Rong silently lit a row of candles for Luo Xinghe.

...

At this moment, in the back-end monitoring room.

"Mental stress level?" Ryoko Kunomiya asked.

"Wang Ling: 105%. Luo Xinghe: 341%, triggering a red alert, the system forcibly injects calming spiritual power," the analyst reported.

"Red alert..." Wang Zhen raised an eyebrow: "Luo Xinghe's mental state has collapsed."

Liu Qingyi crossed her arms: "You've only died twice and you're already broken? Your mental fortitude is too weak."

"It's not that he has poor mental fortitude." Zhuo Yi shook his head. "It's that the contrast is too great. He's a big streamer with millions of fans. He usually streams by stomping noobs or carrying high-level games. Today, he was outplayed twice in a row by his master, a high school student. His pride can't take it."

Ryoko Kugimiya tapped her palm with her folding fan: "Moreover, he suspects that Rin is cheating, but he can't find any evidence. This feeling of powerlessness will further intensify his emotions."

"Then next..." Wang Zhen looked at the screen.

"Next, Luo Xinghe might do something irrational." Zhuo Yi took a sip of tea. "Notify the monitoring team to pay close attention to his psionic energy fluctuations. If he uses cultivation abilities illegally..."

"What will happen?" Liu Qingyi asked.

Zhuo Yi smiled and said, "Master will teach him how to be a person."

...

Inside the illusion.

Luo Xinghe is resurrected.

This time, he stood in the spring for a full ten seconds.

0/2.

If you die again, the game will be over.

"We can't lose," he whispered, his eyes bloodshot. "We absolutely can't lose."

Losing to Wang Ling means that everything he said in front of everyone has become a joke.

This means that his dignity as a professional anchor has been completely trampled on.

What will his fans think of him? What will Sun Rong think of him?

Thinking of Sun Rong, Luo Xinghe's anger flared up even more.

From the very first time they met, his attention was involuntarily drawn to the girl.

She's beautiful, comes from a good family, has a gentle personality, and is also good at games.

The perfect match.

But her gaze remained fixed on Wang Ling.

That wooden guy who munches on instant noodles all day and has a blank expression.

On what grounds?

"I refuse to accept this." Luo Xinghe clenched his fist.

He glanced at the equipment bar.

We have enough money; we can release [Fever].

But that's not enough.

To turn the tide, you need stronger equipment and external help.

Luo Xinghe's gaze fell on a hidden function of the illusion system.

【Psionic Overload Mode】

[Note: Consumes extra mental energy to significantly boost hero attributes for a short period. However, this will cause a rapid increase in mental load; exceeding the safety threshold may result in permanent damage.]

[WARNING: This function is for emergency use only and is not recommended to be enabled.]

This is a feature set up in the Illusionary Realm Simulation Battle to deal with "sudden crises," originally intended for players to protect themselves when encountering bugs or system errors.

However, the rules do not explicitly prohibit its use.

"As long as you don't interfere with the game directly without using cultivation abilities, it's not considered a violation." Luo Xinghe's eyes flickered: "Psychic overload is a built-in function of the system and falls within the rules..."

He took a deep breath.

Then, silently recite in your mind: "Activate psionic overload mode, level: three."

[WARNING: Level 3 overload will cause the mental load to rise above 500%, and if it continues for more than ten seconds, it may cause loss of consciousness. Confirm?]

"Confirm."

[Command received. Psionic Overload Mode activated, duration: 30 seconds]

Buzz!

Luo Xinghe felt a surge of heat rush from his spine to his brain.

The field of vision instantly became clear, and every detail of Kai'Sa's movements was presented before my eyes as if in slow motion.

"This is... the feeling of surpassing limits." Luo Xinghe's lips curled into a grotesque smile.

He manipulated Kai'Sa to leave the fountain.

This time, he's going to win.

...

Middle lane.

Wang Ling is clearing the line.

Suddenly, he sensed something.

Look up and look across.

Luo Xinghe is flying from the direction of the spring.

The speed is very strange.

It's much faster than a normal Kai'Sa.

Moreover, the bio-armor on Kai'Sa's body faintly emitted an abnormal purplish-red glow.

"Psionic overload?" Wang Ling instantly realized.

He had seen the description of this feature in the rules manual, but he didn't expect that anyone would actually use it.

Level 3 overload will cause mental stress to spike to a dangerous level.

Luo Xinghe is risking his life.

"Interesting," Wang Ling thought to himself.

Kuchi stopped clearing the line and slid backward a short distance.

Kai'Sa landed and immediately activated her E skill to accelerate and charge forward.

The speed is incredibly fast!

Wang Ling manipulated Kuchi to pull him backward, simultaneously launching a phosphorescent bomb in anticipation.

But Luo Xinghe easily dodged with a strange right-angle movement.

"The reaction speed has increased by at least three times," Wang Ling assessed.

Not only movement speed, but also attack speed and skill casting speed have all been increased.

Kai'Sa's attributes now completely overwhelm Corki's.

You can't win by fighting head-on.

"Then let's delay it."

Wang Ling controlled Kuchi to fly towards his own jungle.

Overload mode has a time limit; if it lasts longer than 30 seconds, Kai'Sa will become weakened.

But Luo Xinghe clearly wouldn't give him a chance.
