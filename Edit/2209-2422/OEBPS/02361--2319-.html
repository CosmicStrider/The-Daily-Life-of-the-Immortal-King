Chapter 2319 The Home of the Shadow Stalkers

The controversy surrounding Li Jun's plagiarism incident came to an end with Qingyun High School being expelled from the secret realm.

The students of Number 60 High School returned in triumph, bearing the core mark of the secret realm and the honor of official recognition. The controversy inside and outside the school regarding the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" vanished, replaced by amazement at Li Jun's comeback and a reassessment of Number 60 High School's strength.

In the past year, the school ranking of Number 60 High School has improved rapidly. It has not only achieved excellent results in various competitions, but also made great contributions in the recent Inner Earth World Trial.

Now, throughout Songhai, whenever someone mentions Number 60 High School, they are full of praise for it.

Many parents have even started using their personal connections in order to get their children into Number 60 High School.

After the Secret Realm competition ended, Wang Ling carried the excited Wang Muyu on his shoulder, while Sun Rong followed beside them. The three of them walked home in the sunset like a family of three.

At this moment, Wang Ling's spiritual consciousness had already penetrated through layers of space and landed deep within the Azure Netherworld Secret Realm, landing on the short "Shadowman" who was bound like a dumpling by seven-colored chains.

...

The Azure Netherworld Secret Realm, a core forbidden area.

Inside the cage constructed of seven-colored light, the short team member huddled on the ground.

His dark aura was suppressed by the power of Liuli's secret realm, making him like a venomous snake whose fangs had been pulled out.

Liuli floated in front of him, her small hands behind her back, her small face full of majesty that did not match her appearance.

"Speak! Who ordered you? How many of you have infiltrated the school?" Liuli's voice resonated with the laws of the secret realm, striking straight to the soul.

The short teammate's eyes were unfocused, and his lips were trembling, as if he was resisting.

But the seal, reinforced by the Celestial King, prevented him from even annihilating himself.

Under the continuous mental pressure from Liuli and the purifying power of the secret realm's origin, his deepest fears were amplified infinitely.

"The House of the Shadows..."

He squeezed out broken syllables from his throat.

Overwhelmed by fear, he eventually revealed all the information he knew, albeit in fits and starts.

Liuli frowned, carefully noting down these fragmented words, and using the power of the laws to forcibly decipher the memories deep in her mind, beginning to organize the information she had already grasped.

A moment later, her expression changed slightly, and she immediately used the mental channel left by Wang Ling to transmit the information to Lord Diu Lei.

...

Soon, Wang Ling's phone started vibrating.

Lord Diu Lei sent an urgent encrypted communication request, with the highest priority.

"Brother! There's been a major breakthrough!"

As soon as the call connected, Diu Lei Zhenjun's voice came through urgently, and in the background, the rapid tapping of Wang Ming's keyboard could be heard.

"That fellow Daoist from the Azure Netherworld Secret Realm has extremely efficient interrogation methods. That short shadowy figure revealed quite a bit of crucial information!"

Wang Ling subtly moved his phone a little further away, causing Wang Muyu on his shoulder and Sun Rong beside him to perk up their ears.

"Get to the point," Wang Ling said succinctly.

"Good! According to the confessions, the ‘Shadow Stalker' program run by the evil cultivators of the Primordial Era is more systematic and more evil than we imagined."

Lord Diu Lei said in a solemn tone: "They are not only developing downlines, but more like they are building a distorted 'breeding ground' for gu."

"Raising Gu?" Sun Rong couldn't help but repeat in a low voice.

"That's right! All the transformed Shadows are not treated equally. They have a strict internal hierarchy to select and cultivate core members."

Wang Ming's voice interrupted rapidly, and at the same time, a holographic projection unfolded in front of Wang Ling, displaying a simple yet sinister level chart:

[Shadowman Level System] (from low to high)

Gray-Clad: The lowest rank, newly converted or low-contribution infiltrators. They can only accept simple instructions, infiltrate the lower strata of society, and are responsible for observation and transmitting basic information. They have extremely limited authority and may not even know the organization's complete structure.

Black Robe: Core Member. Having completed initial "spiritual purification," they demonstrate high loyalty to the organization. They are able to proactively recruit subordinates and carry out more complex infiltration and sabotage missions. They have begun to explore some basic methods of using evil power.

Bloodline: An elite tier. Those who excel or make significant contributions in numerous missions are bestowed with a "bloodline," branded in a hidden part of their body. Their strength is significantly enhanced, allowing them to mobilize greater evil power and manage regional networks of shadow agents. They also possess knowledge of parts of the core plan.

Dark Stars: High-ranking administrators. Few in number, they report directly to the Primordial Core or powerful Dragonborn. They possess immense autonomy and resource allocation capabilities. They are also rumored to acquire some of the dragon's abilities.

As Lord Diu Lei explained the ranking system, he added at the end, "There is also a highest level whose name is unknown, which we will tentatively mark as a question mark. Information about this level is missing, but it seems to be directly related to 'ascension'."

"What are the promotion methods?" Sun Rong pressed.

She realized that the existence of this system greatly stimulated the "enthusiasm" of the shadows, and also made the organization more difficult to eradicate.

"Mainly through his performance at the ‘House of Shadows'," replied Lord Thunder Drop.

"The House of Shadows?" Wang Muyu tilted his head; the name sounded a bit strange.

"That's the key!" Wang Ming chimed in.

The holographic projection changed, showing a vast villa area brightly lit but shrouded in blood-red moonlight.

This is a concept drawing constructed by Wang Ming using simulation technology, based on the memories read by the short lurker.

It may differ from the real scene, but the accuracy of the recreation is over 80%.

"This is an independent little world created by a powerful evil cultivator from the primordial era, a sacred place in the hearts of all hidden shadows."

As the image zooms in, you can see villas of various shapes, all exuding a sinister yet luxurious feel.

There was no sun in the sky, only a huge red moon.

"All snorkelers are qualified to enter the ‘Snorkelers' House.' Once inside, they do not operate under their original identities or identities."

Wang Ming's tone carried a hint of disbelief: "According to the rules, they will draw lots to randomly form families with other potential infiltrators and share a villa. Each initial family will have no more than five members."

"Start a family?" Sun Rong frowned. She hadn't expected the hierarchical order behind the Shadow Walkers to be so complex.

"Yes! Moreover, in this small world, their inner darkness will be amplified, giving rise to a 'second personality'. They will live together as a new family, but their real purpose is to complete the mission issued by the 'Gate of the Abyss'."

The projected image focused on the center of the villa area.

One after another, enormous doors, distorted and shrouded in an ominous aura, stood there.

"This is a concept drawing of the Abyss Gate simulated by Brother Wang Ming."

Lord Diu Lei's voice was deep and resonant: "This door connects to various secret realms that have been corrupted or controlled by evil cultivation forces. Behind each door lies an independent world filled with the laws of death. Family members must enter the designated door, survive for a sufficient number of days, and find a way to survive in order to pass the test. Upon successful completion, the villa will receive points, which will be displayed in real time on the leaderboard in the sky."

He paused, then emphasized, "These points are directly related to resource allocation, level advancement, and ultimately, the qualification for ascension!"

"Ascension..." Wang Muyu caught the word.

"Yes, this is the highest reward. According to the prisoner, when the points accumulate to a certain level, reaching the question mark level, there is a chance to be granted 'Dragonborn genes,' transforming into a true dragonborn and gaining near-eternal life and world-destroying power. However, this information is strictly sealed, and he only knows a vague part of it."

There was a moment of silence on both ends of the call.

This intelligence report contains a huge amount of information.

Moreover, the operating model of this "House of Shadows" is simply an extreme combination of the dark side of human nature and the cruel competition of raising Gu.

Wang Ling's gaze fell on the concept image on the holographic projection, his dead fish eyes showing no emotion.

But those who know him well know that he has made a decision.

"Brother, what are your plans..." Lord Thunderclap asked tentatively.

Wang Ling did not reply and directly cut off the communication.

He needs to go and see for himself.

It's better not to go there yourself, as that would make too much noise and easily alert the enemy.

He has a million ways to sneak in unnoticed.
