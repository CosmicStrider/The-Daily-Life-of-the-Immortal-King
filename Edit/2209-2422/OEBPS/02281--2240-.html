Chapter 2240 Those who are clean will clean themselves up

In the quiet medical records area, the only sounds were the rustling of turning pages and Wang Ling's soft breathing.

The soft, simulated starlight streamed in through the window, falling on the two people sitting side by side, making the moment seem almost unreal.

Sun Rong's thoughts were repeatedly pulled between the strange rules on the book pages and the breathing of the boy beside her, and the pages she was holding with her fingertips curled slightly at the edges.

In this subtle and tense stillness, the female researcher wearing a white lab coat and black-rimmed glasses walked to the desk opposite the two and sat down.

Her movements were slightly stiff. After sitting down, she did not immediately start working. Instead, she lowered her head and stared blankly at the table, as if she was trying her best to suppress something.

An indescribable sense of tension permeated the air.

Wang Ling's fingers paused slightly as he flipped through "Creatures in the Earth's Core".

That ominous premonition was not unfounded.

His divine sense was incredibly sharp; the moment the female researcher stepped into the medical room, he detected a barely perceptible, malevolent spiritual imprint on her.

It wasn't her own; it was more like a mark that had been tainted on her.

Sure enough, the silence didn't last long.

The female researcher suddenly raised her head, her face rapidly shifting between pale and flushed, the emptiness in her eyes replaced by a mixture of panic and deliberate shame and anger.

She abruptly stood up, her trembling finger pointing at Wang Ling, who was quietly reading a book. Her sharp voice instantly pierced the tranquility of the medical room:

"You...you pervert! What...what are you doing under the table with your hands!" A suppressed scream, filled with grievance and anger.

Her words were like a bomb dropped into a calm lake.

Wang Ling remained engrossed in his reading, his eyelashes not even twitching, as calm as if he hadn't heard a thing.

At that moment, he was flipping through the books on the table with his right hand, while his left hand, naturally, was under the table preparing to sneak a bite of instant noodles...

The crevices of my fingernails still carried a strong smell of pepper.

But Sun Rong raised her head in an instant, and her eyes, which had been slightly misty with shyness, suddenly became sharp as cold stars.

She didn't look at Wang Ling, but instead fixed her gaze on the poorly performing female researcher opposite her, her voice calm yet carrying an undeniable chill: "What did you say?!"

"Just... just now! I saw it, his left hand... under the table, it looked like it was wriggling!"

The female researcher shyly covered her face, tears quickly welling up in her eyes, her acting skills were nothing short of exaggerated. "I want to report you! You... Wang..." She wanted to say Wang Ling's name, but at the crucial moment, Wang Ling's "Great Shielding Technique" was activated, making it impossible for her to remember the name the mastermind had told her.

So sometimes, the person who wrongs you knows better than anyone how wronged you are... They can even throw dirty water on you without even knowing your name.

"Fine! Whoever you are... you have to give me an explanation! I'm going to report you for sexual harassment to Director Teng!"

The intention behind this serious accusation is obvious—to smear Wang Ling, completely expel him from the base, and force the reputation of the Sixty Gates to be ruined.

It felt as if a fire was burning in Sun Rong's chest.

Because this blatant frame-up dared to be placed on the person she liked!

She slowly stood up, her cold gaze sweeping over the female researcher like a real ice spike, causing the woman to subconsciously take a half step back, her "grief and indignation" on her face stiffening slightly.

"Oh? You mean Wang Ling did something indecent to you under the table?"

Sun Rong's voice was devoid of emotion, yet it possessed a penetrating power that seemed to see through everything. "Madam researcher, please tell me, where did this seriously injured person, who you claim has just experienced a spiritual pressure impact far exceeding their own limits, resulting in the depletion of their spiritual energy and damage to their meridians, requiring assistance to move and now struggling even to turn the pages of a book, find the strength and leisure to do such a thing, and precisely sexually harass you sitting at the table opposite me from afar?"

Her counter-questions were clear and logical, each word like a hammer blow to the other party's riddled lies.

This directly transformed Wang Ling's medical diagnosis of "inability to move" into the most powerful alibi.

Wang Ling remained the quiet "seriously wounded" man; his current weakness became his strongest shield.

The female researcher was clearly unprepared for Sun Rong's calm and sharp rebuttal. Her prepared words caught in her throat, and her face grew even more flustered: "I...how would I know! Maybe he was faking it! Anyway, he...he did it! Are you going to protect him?! You're from the same school; your testimony is completely unreliable."

"Protect him?"

Sun Rong's lips curled into a cold smile, and the powerful aura unique to the young lady of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group instantly erupted: "No, I'm telling you the price of slander! Do you have any evidence?"

Sun Rong's voice suddenly rose, clear and forceful: "Trying to smear someone's innocence with nothing but empty words? Who gave you the audacity? You accuse him of harassment, produce physical evidence! Surveillance footage! Or a third witness who was present at the time! Do you have any?"

The female researcher was rendered speechless by her barrage of questions. Her eyes darted around, and she stammered, "At that time... we were just... we were reading here... there was... no one else..."

"No evidence?" Sun Rong sneered. "Then it's slander, huh? Very well! On behalf of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group and myself, I formally file a complaint against you! I accuse you of maliciously defaming Wang Ling, a participant in the [Project Center of the Earth] national key project base, with the intent to disrupt the project's stability and damage his reputation! I will demand that the base immediately retrieve all surveillance footage from that area and simultaneously report it to Teng Lao, the chief commander of the Project Center of the Earth! If you have the ability to smear me, you should have the ability to bear all the consequences!"
