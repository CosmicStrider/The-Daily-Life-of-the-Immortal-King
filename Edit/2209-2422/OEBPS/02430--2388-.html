Chapter 2388 The Labyrinth of Light and Shadow

The entrance to the light and shadow maze is designed like a flowing gate of light.

Seven-colored particles swirled and flowed within the door frame, emitting a soft humming sound.

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe crouched down, hiding behind a giant, glowing mushroom-shaped decoration on the side of the entrance.

"Did they go in?" Chen Chao poked half his head out.

"We just went in two minutes ago." Guo Hao glanced at the time on his bracelet: "The average time to complete the maze is forty-five minutes. If we go in now, we'll most likely run into each other inside."

Li Changzhe hesitated for a moment: "Brother Chao, is this... really a good idea?"

"What's wrong with that!" Chen Chao said confidently. "Don't you want to know what it's like when Lingzi and Sun Rong are alone together? If that blockhead Lingzi says something wrong and makes Sun Rong angry, we can step in and smooth things over! So we need to see how things go!"

At this point, Chen Chao rubbed his hands together and said, "Let's go, let's go, let's wait until they're a little further away before we go in, so we don't get caught."

The three waited another five minutes before sneaking towards the entrance.

The staff member at the entrance was a young man wearing glasses. Seeing the three of them huddled together, he twitched his lips and asked, "Are the three of you together?"

"Yes, yes, yes!" Chen Chao nodded. "How do you play this maze?"

"The Light and Shadow Maze is divided into three areas: the Memory Corridor, the Choice Illusion, and the Mind Image Corridor. Each area has puzzles or challenges that require teamwork," the staff explained. "The bracelet will record your progress. Once you complete all the areas and find the exit, you will receive a stamp."

He handed over three pairs of specially made glasses: "These are light and shadow sensing glasses. Only by wearing them can you see the real path and puzzle clues inside the maze. In addition, some paths inside the maze are randomly generated, so everyone's experience may be different."

Chen Chao took the glasses and put them on; the scene before him immediately changed.

The once unremarkable entrance passage is now adorned with flowing light patterns on the walls and arrows pointing the way on the ground.

"That's interesting," he muttered.

The three of them walked into the Gate of Light.

...

The maze was more spacious than I had imagined.

The walls on both sides of the passage resemble liquid light, slowly flowing and changing colors.

Above us was a simulated starry dome, twinkling with starlight.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong have already grown apart.

"These light patterns... seem to follow a pattern." Sun Rong stared at the flowing patterns on the wall, analyzing them carefully.

Wang Ling walked half a step beside her, his gaze calmly sweeping over their surroundings.

He could actually see through the entire maze's structure at a glance—which paths were dead ends, which mechanisms were traps, and which lights and shadows were clues. He could even sense that several staff members were manipulating parts of the illusion deep within the maze.

But he didn't say it.

Because Sun Rong seemed to really enjoy the process of solving puzzles.

"Look here." Sun Rong stopped at a fork in the road and pointed to a pattern on the wall of the passageway on the left: "Don't these dots of light look like an arrow when connected?"

On the wall, the pale blue dots of light did indeed form a vague right arrow shape.

Wang Ling nodded: "Mm."

"Shall we go to the right?" Sun Rong asked.

According to conventional puzzle-solving logic, if the clue points to the right, it often means that the correct path is in the opposite direction.

Wang Ling glanced at the "arrowhead" again.

Actually, that was a misleading sign deliberately set up by the staff. If you really go to the left, you will enter a circular corridor and it will take you ten minutes to get out.

"The right side," he said.

Sun Rong chose the right-hand passage without hesitation.

After the two went inside, the light patterns on the wall suddenly changed, forming a small smiley face pattern.

"We made the right choice!" Sun Rong's eyes lit up as she looked happily at Wang Ling. "How did you know?"

Wang Ling pulled a packet of instant noodles from his pocket, tore it open, and said, "I guessed."

He would never say that it was because he overheard a conversation between staff members in the control room thirty meters away...

"That super good-looking couple went to the right! Quick, turn on the heartwarming mode of the 'Memory Corridor'!"

"Got it! By the way, that guy was so handsome, and the girl was super cute too..."

"Focus on your work! Miss Kugimiya specifically instructed us to take good care of this couple!"

Wang Ling chewed on his instant noodles, his mind completely unmoved.

He had long since noticed Ryoko Kumiya's little scheme.

But it doesn't matter, he wanted to collect stamps to exchange for a large gift pack of instant noodles anyway.

As for spending time alone with Sun Rong...

At this moment, Wang Ling's seal was slightly hot, and as always, it cooled down quickly after generating some fluctuations.

...

"Wait, why are there two roads here?"

On the other side, Chen Chao and his two companions stood at a crossroads, looking at the mini map displayed on their wristbands with concern.

The map only showed the areas they had passed through; the area ahead was completely dark.

"How about we split up?" Li Changzhe suggested.

Guo Hao immediately objected: "The maze rules recommend that teams of two or more people work together. Splitting up could lead to someone getting trapped. Besides, our purpose in coming in is to observe Wang Ling and Sun Rong. What's the point of observing them if we split up?"

Chen Chao stroked his chin: "Shall we vote? I think we should go to the left; the light pattern looks brighter there."

"There is no direct correlation between light intensity and path accuracy," Guo Hao calmly analyzed. "Judging from the architectural structure, these types of mazes usually place attractive visual elements on misleading paths."

"Then let's go to the right?"

"The airflow in the right-hand passage is more pronounced, and it is likely the main route to the open area."

As the three were arguing, they suddenly heard faint voices coming from the passage on the left.

"...This mechanism seems to require pressing two buttons at the same time."

It's Sun Rong's voice!

Chen Chao immediately raised a finger and snorted, lowering his voice: "It's on the left! Sneaky over there!"

The three of them tiptoed into the passage on the left.

At the end of the passage was a circular room with walls covered in glowing buttons, each with a different symbol.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong were standing in the center of the room.

Sun Rong looked up at the wall: "These symbols... are somewhat like ancient cultivation script, but not quite the same."

Wang Ling glanced at it.

It's actually just some random drawings with a few simple Bagua symbols.

The so-called "solution" is simply pressing the buttons for the three symbols "sun, moon, and star" in sequence.

But he still waited for Sun Rong to find out on her own.

"Look, this looks like the sun, this is the moon, and there are stars..." Sun Rong noticed it: "Could it be in this order?"

As she spoke, she stood on tiptoe to press the "sun" button on the wall.

But the button was a bit high, so it was a little difficult for her to reach it.

Wang Ling walked up behind her naturally and reached out his hand.

His hand reached over Sun Rong's shoulder and easily pressed the button.

This position was almost an embrace from behind, so close that Sun Rong could feel Wang Ling's body temperature.

Her ears turned red instantly.

"Thank you, thank you..." Sun Rong said softly.

Wang Ling grunted in response, withdrew his hand, and took a half-step back.

But that brief moment of closeness caused the seal to scald again.

Hiding around the corner of the passage, Chen Chao excitedly grabbed Guo Hao's arm: "Did you see that?! Your son's finally figured it out! He even knows to press buttons for people from behind!"

Guo Hao expressionlessly pried his hand away: "From an ergonomic point of view, Wang Ling's height advantage is indeed more suitable for completing this operation. Moreover, he maintained a reasonable social distance, and his half-step back was very polite."

"You have absolutely no romantic sense!" Chen Chao rolled his eyes.

Li Changzhe whispered, "But Sun Rong's face is so red..."

Inside the room, Sun Rong pressed the "moon" and "star" buttons.

All the buttons on the wall lit up at the same time, forming a light gate.
