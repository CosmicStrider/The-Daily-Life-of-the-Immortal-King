Chapter 2290 The Four Extreme Dragon Lock Formation

In the depths of space, the ancient Dragon Clan ruins have become the core battlefield for five top-tier Celestial Ancestor level experts.

Wang Muyu's small body was bursting with boundless spiritual energy.

After devouring the Dragon God's son, the surging power within him almost burst forth, and the seven-colored dragon scales released a powerful protective glow that flowed across his body.

The pair of newly sprouted dragon horns on its forehead were crystal clear, radiating a terrifying aura of ten thousand dragons.

"roar!"

Wang Muyu no longer suppressed his emotions and let out a dragon's roar filled with fighting spirit.

The illusory image of ten thousand dragons behind him solidified, as if a true ancestor of ten thousand dragons had descended, his dragon eyes indifferently gazing at the four insects who had offended his majesty.

The attack of the four members of Jueqi has begun!

Huangyan took the lead, scorching the space in its path with twisted black marks; its terrifyingly high temperature was enough to instantly vaporize planets.

The extreme cold rays from the deep sea arrive first, capable of freezing everything directly.

The wind blades of Xunfeng appeared strangely from all directions, cutting through space and blocking all escape routes.

The final silence of the Absolute is the most deadly.

That speck of gray light, imbued with the essence of annihilation, silently pierced towards Wang Muyu's dantian, attempting to obliterate him in one fell swoop.

This is a world-destroying combined attack from the four Heavenly Ancestors, and their coordination is extremely perfect!

Wang Muyu neither dodged nor avoided; the chaotic energy he had just absorbed gave him boundless confidence.

"Disperse them!"

He mimicked the all-controlling posture of Wang Ling from his memory, his small fist enveloped in rich seven-colored dragon power, and he threw a punch forward!

There is no method, only power!

Boom!

A massive, seven-colored beam of light erupted and collided head-on with the blazing Black Flame Demon Dragon.

laugh!

There is absolutely no need to consider any elemental advantage or disadvantage. Wang Muyu, who possesses the power of ten thousand dragons, delivers a strike that inherently counters any type of dragon attribute!

The energy emanating from Huangyan was instantly disintegrated by Wang Muyu's seven-colored light pillar!

The collision of the two unleashed an energy surge powerful enough to destroy a planet!

Huang Yan's expression changed. He never expected that his Hellfire Black Flame would be so easily neutralized!

At the same time, the seven-colored protective light emanating from Wang Muyu surged wildly, automatically transforming into a seven-colored shield that enveloped his entire body when attacked, directly blocking the extreme cold rays from the deep sea.

This is the autonomous protective effect of the power of ten thousand dragons. Even if Wang Muyu did not deliberately resist, the seven-colored protective light seemed to have intelligence and autonomously resisted the tricky attacks from various angles.

A flicker of disbelief finally crossed Shenming's cold eyes.

This little kid's current strength seems extraordinary, surpassing any Dragonborn!

It's hard to describe with just the word "too sinister"!

As for Xunfeng's ubiquitous wind blades that rip through the air, they are more of a comic relief.

The wind blades that cut into Wang Muyu's dragon scale phantom were instead shattered by the protective radiance of Wang Muyu's body.

Finally, there was that final shot.

With his hands in his pockets, Little Wooden Fish didn't know who his opponent was. He had almost no intention of deliberately blocking, but the seven-colored protective light automatically began to absorb the chaotic energy in the universe, transforming into a shield in front of him and blocking the attack from the Absolute Thorn at that moment!

Buzz!

The spear tip struck the shield, unleashing a burst of annihilation power, but it was quickly annihilated by the shield, which combined Wang Muyu's protective dragon power with the power of chaos.

"These four people seem to be no match for me at all." Wang Muyu's little face gradually became excited.

He actually managed to withstand the attack of four bad guys!

Moreover, without even taking it seriously, these four dragon descendants at the Celestial Ancestor level couldn't even scratch him!

"His power... has undergone a qualitative change!"

Absolutely sheathed his spear, and the data stream in his molten gold vertical pupils refreshed wildly. His voice was more solemn than ever before: "After devouring the son of the Dragon God, the various supreme characteristics of his power of ten thousand dragons have been further enhanced! He has developed extremely strong resistance to any single-attribute attack, and can even assimilate and absorb it!"

The other three were startled upon hearing this.

In their eyes, Wang Muyu, who had already fused with the power of ten thousand dragons, was already seen as the reincarnation of a demon child.

Now this demon child has eaten another demon child and become Demon Child PLUS!

"There's definitely a way to break it! He probably hasn't fully digested this power yet, we still have a chance!"

Huang Yan roared furiously, slamming his hands together and bulging the muscles beneath his dark red dragon scales: "Everyone! Form the Four Extreme Dragon Lock Formation! Trample him! Exhaust him!"

The four dragon descendants reached a consensus instantly.

If elemental attacks are ineffective, then crush them with absolute power and formations!

The four figures flashed and occupied positions on all four sides, surrounding Wang Muyu in the center.

"I will defend my throne! I will burn the heavens and boil the seas!"

The blazing flames roared, and towering black flames surged from his body, not as an attack, but as a massive pillar of fire that reached the sky and sealed off a space.

"I guard the Kan position! The icy hell is the abyss!"

With a gentle touch of her jade hand, Shenming unleashed an extremely cold dragon's breath, completely transforming another cosmic space into a frozen domain, where even light seemed to be frozen.

"I guard the Xun position! The inescapable wind prison!"

Xunfeng's figure completely dissipated, transforming into countless invisible threads that intertwined and formed a massive wind prison, sealing off all possibilities of spatial leaps.

"The Gen position! Utter annihilation and return to nothingness!"

The phrase "Absolute Imprisonment" is concise and to the point. He planted the Spear of Final Silence into the void, and the Realm of Annihilation collapsed to its extreme around him, eventually transforming into a gray swamp capable of annihilating all spiritual energy. It floated below Wang Muyu, cutting off his ability to absorb any energy from the outside world.

The Four Dragon Lock Formation is complete!

In an instant, the four celestial-level domain powers of flame, ice, wind rift, and annihilation superimposed on each other!

A huge, transparent cubic cage with four colors flowing around it trapped Wang Muyu in the center.

Inside the cage, the space was completely isolated, and the aura of the laws crashed and ravaged from all directions, continuously eroding and consuming Wang Muyu's power.

Wang Muyu suddenly felt immense pressure!

The seven-colored dragon light surrounding his body was suppressed to flicker unpredictably.

Those four forces were so pervasive that he felt as if he had been thrown into a cosmic crusher.

He exerted all his strength, and the seven-colored beams of light continuously struck the four walls, but they only caused the cage to tremble violently, and it was difficult to break through it in a short time.

Even more terrifying is that the enormous energy within his body is being rapidly consumed by this terrifying formation!

"Let's see how long you can hold out!" Huang Yan laughed menacingly, maintaining the array's output.

"Strip him of his origin and offer it to Lord Bai Zhe." Shenming's tone remained icy.

Xunfeng's figure appeared and disappeared in the wind prison, emitting mocking laughter.

Jueqi, on the other hand, did everything in his power to maintain the formation and weaken Wang Muyu's foundation.

Meanwhile, in Songhai City, Number 60 High School.

The old man was explaining a complex psionic geometry problem on the platform, the chalk scratching across the blackboard.

Wang Ling sat by the window, his gaze seemingly fixed on the other students in the playground outside who were having a physical education class.

Most of his mind was focused on the four-colored dragon lock cage, which was trapped inside and looking somewhat lost, through endless time and space.

Wang Ling sighed.

Ultimately, the little wooden fish was too young and lacked combat experience.

After all, they were facing four Heavenly Ancestors, who were also Bai Zhe's capable subordinates. A quick victory was the best course of action.

Although in Wang Ling's eyes, this Four Extreme Dragon Lock Formation was as crude as some students' homework that they copied carelessly when they ran to school in the morning.

The loopholes are so numerous they're like a sieve.

Almost instantly, Wang Ling's mind conjured up tens of thousands of ways to break the formation.

From the simplest and most brutal method of breaking through the formation with brute force, to the most exquisite and effortless method of using minimal force to overcome great strength.

But Wang Ling didn't choose any of them.

"I need to toughen him up and give him a little lesson," he thought.

What Wang Muyu lacks is not strength, but the ability to adapt to desperate situations and the kind of personality that makes him a little complacent when he has strength.
