Chapter 2361 Driven by Jealousy

The inexplicably low temperature in the air seemed to drop two degrees the moment Sun Rong scanned the QR code to accept Luo Xinghe's friend request.

Sun Rong rubbed her arms, her gaze involuntarily drifting towards Wang Ling.

He stood in the corner and took out a pack of instant noodles from his school trouser pocket.

This time it comes in a limited edition gold-colored packaging.

The words "Crab Roe Flavor" were a little dazzling under the lights in the training hall.

"Rule adjustments for the second round of qualifying."

Zhuo Yi's voice drew everyone's attention back to the center of the hall.

He wore a standard official smile and held up three fingers:

"First, draw lots again, completely disrupting the system."

"Second, a global ban/pick (BP) system will be adopted. Once a hero is selected in the entire second round, neither side can use it again. In addition, all heroes that appeared in the previous round will be automatically added to the ban pool for this round."

As soon as he finished speaking, a loud "buzz" erupted in the hall.

"Now this is a real test of everyone's champion pool!" Chen Chao shouted the loudest: "In the end, will we have to use Garen for jungle and Barbarian for mid?"

"It's definitely more difficult now." Zhou Rui pushed up his glasses, the lenses reflecting the light. "But considering the position allocation and the strength of the current version, there won't be more than three or four T1 or T2 heroes available for each position."

As he said this, his gaze swept over Wang Ling, intentionally or unintentionally.

The ADC position is already a disaster area in terms of champion pool.

In the previous round, Wang Ling used Luxembourg, Sun Rong used Nami, Guo Hao used Xayah, and Wang Muyu used Luo...

With the bottom lane being the only relevant option, five popular choices have already been eliminated.

"Third," Zhuo Yi continued, dropping another bombshell, "This round will feature real-time voice chat for the entire team. All team voice chat will be connected to the spectator system for other team members to learn from and communicate with."

"Learning to communicate?" Li Changzhe muttered to himself, "I guess it's learning how to stay calm in front of a microphone..."

Sun Rong did not respond.

The silver earrings on her earlobes were slightly hot.

Ryoko Kyuu had just secretly told her via telepathy that this "voice live stream" was actually something the Strategist Alliance specifically suggested Zhuoyi add in order to monitor Wang Ling's reactions during team communication in a comprehensive way.

Love science research requires support from multidimensional data.

This is how Yoshiko Kunomiya explained it in a very serious manner.

Sun Rong almost rolled her eyes at that moment.

"Now, let's begin the second round of the draw."

The transparent lottery box reappeared, with forty spirit jades circulating inside.

Sun Rong stepped forward and reached in.

The jade was warm and smooth; she closed her eyes, touched it, and took it out.

Number: 19.

Team B.

She turned to look at Wang Ling almost immediately.

Wang Ling just pulled out a spirit jade from the box and was looking down at it.

His expression remained unchanged, but Sun Rong noticed that he gently rubbed his thumb on the surface of the jade, as if confirming something.

Then he looked up.

The lottery results are updated in real time on the big screen:

Team A: Xia Zhinuan (Xingyao), Liu Kaichuan (Chihuo), Zhang Yu from Titan High School, Zhao Ming from Saint Science Academy, and Lin Feng from Emperor Shitian.

Team B: Sun Rong (Number 60 High School), Luo Xinghe (Xingyao High School), Guo Hao (Number 60 High School), Li Changzhe (Jingmen Number 8 High School), Jiudao and Gaozhong Xiaolin You.

Team D: Wang Ling (Number 60 High School), Chen Chao (Number 60 High School), Wang Muyu (Number 60 High School), Xuanming Zhou Rui, Jiu Dao and Gao Zhong Yamaguchi Ken.

Team C's roster consists of unfamiliar names; they're not important.

The important thing is—

Sun Rong is in Team B.

Luo Xinghe is also in Team B.

Wang Ling is in Team D.

They were separated, and in this way.

Sun Rong's heart skipped a beat.

She looked at Wang Ling.

Wang Ling had already put away the spirit jade, put his hands back in his pockets, and slowly walked towards the assembly area of Team D.

As he passed by her, he seemed to pause for a moment.

Is this about focusing on oneself?

Sun Rong pursed her lips.

...

Team B discussion forum.

"Guo will play top lane, I'll jungle, Li will play mid lane, Luo Xinghe will play ADC, and Sun Rong will play support," said Kobayashi Yu from Jiudaohe.

His Mandarin had a Sun Island accent but was very fluent:

Then, he paused for a moment and added, "I can see that although Sun played support in the last round, his fundamentals during the laning phase were very solid. This time, paired with Luo Xinghe, the game will definitely go smoothly. Moreover, Wang Ling is on the opposing team this round. If he also plays ADC, your matchup will be very interesting."

That statement was perfectly flawless.

But Sun Rong keenly noticed that when Xiao Linyou said "Wang Ling is over there," his gaze momentarily drifted towards Team A—more precisely, towards Luo Xinghe, who was chatting and laughing with his teammates.

Another "actor"?

Sun Rong understood the situation, but remained outwardly calm: "I can play support. However, regarding the hero pool, I wonder what heroes Mr. Luo will choose?"

"I'm fine with anything." Luo Xinghe shrugged and smiled nonchalantly.

"Caitlynn, Ashe, and Ashe are still available." Guo Hao quickly checked the strategy board: "Or we can try some unconventional picks—anyway, everyone is short of heroes in the global ban/pick phase."

"I want to play Yasuo!" Li Changzhe raised his hand, his eyes shining.

"Yasuo is in the ban pool." Guo Hao mercilessly retorted, "Someone used him in the last round."

"Ah..." Li Changzhe was deflated.

...

Team D discussion forum.

The atmosphere was a little awkward.

Chen Chao, with his arm around Wang Ling's shoulder, was spitting as he spoke: "Lingzi! What are we going to do this round? Global draft! Do you even have enough ADC champions? Lucian is gone, Ezreal is gone, Jinx is gone, Draven is gone too..."

He counted on his fingers, growing more and more desperate with each count.

Wang Ling slowly munched on the crab roe-flavored instant noodles without saying a word.

"Wang Ling." Zhou Rui pushed up his glasses and turned the tactical tablet toward him: "I've done a count, and there are only seven ADC heroes that are still viable and ranked above T2 in the current meta: Caitlyn, Jinx, Ashe, Tristana, Kai'Sa, Vayne, and Twitch."

He paused for a moment: "Among them, Caitlyn, Smite, and Ashe will most likely be picked first or banned. Our pick order is after Team A, so we may not be able to get these three."

Chen Chao wailed, "Then there are only four left? Kai'Sa, Vayne, Tristana, Twitch... Lingzi, are you familiar with these four?"

After finishing the last piece of instant noodles, Wang Ling folded the packaging bag into a neat little square and stuffed it back into his pocket.

Then he looked up and calmly scanned the data on Zhou Rui's tablet.

"I don't play ADC," he said.

"Huh?" Chen Chao was stunned.

Zhou Rui was also taken aback for a moment: "Then you hit..."
