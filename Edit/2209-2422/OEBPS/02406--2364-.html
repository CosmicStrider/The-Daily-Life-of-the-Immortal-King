Chapter 2364 Illusion Simulation Battle

The night at the Starry Sky Esports Center is much quieter than in downtown Songhai City.

Outside the window, the outline of Qingyun Mountain was blurred, and the moonlight shone on the mountain ridge, coating it with a layer of silver.

Wang Ling lay on the bed in room 709, his hands behind his head, his eyes staring at the ceiling.

In room 708 next door, Sun Rong turned over, and the mattress made a slight creaking sound.

Having excellent hearing can sometimes be a problem.

Wang Ling could clearly hear Sun Rong's breathing.

It's a bit fast; it doesn't look like it's asleep.

She was nervous.

Because of tomorrow's illusion simulation battle?

Or is it because of... Luo Xinghe?

Wang Ling frowned slightly.

He picked up a pack of unopened barbecue-flavored instant noodles from the bedside table and began to habitually use the noodles to relieve stress.

The familiar smell calmed him down a little.

But that inexplicable restlessness still lingered around my heart.

The way Luo Xinghe looked at Sun Rong.

The deliberately gentle tone in Luo Xinghe's voice.

Sun Rong took the WeChat QR code when Luo Xinghe handed it out.

He didn't know what to call this feeling.

It's sealed in something slightly hot, trying to suppress something.

Soon, Wang Ling returned to calm.

He sat up and walked to the window.

Looking at the Starry Sky Esports Center in the night, several lights were still on in the training area of Building A.

At this time, some people are still practicing extra.

Wang Ling's gaze fell on the third floor of Building A.

That's the main venue for tomorrow's simulation battle.

His gaze was calm, but a glint of gold flashed deep within his pupils.

...

At the same time, on the third floor of Building A, in Battle Hall No. 1.

Luo Xinghe stood in the center of the empty hall, looking up at the holographic projection screen that was suspended in mid-air.

The screen is playing a loop of this afternoon's match footage.

Olaf slapped his face.

Female police officer dies suddenly.

0-10.

Super weird.

Every death was like a slap in the face, landing hard on his cheek.

"Xinghe, aren't you going to rest yet?"

Xia Zhinuan's voice came from behind her.

She carried a cup of hot spiritual tea to Luo Xinghe's side and handed it to him: "Have some, it'll calm your nerves."

Luo Xinghe took it, but didn't drink it; he just held it in his hand.

The tea was warm, and the warmth traveled through the cup to my palm.

"Zhi Nuan," he suddenly spoke, his voice slightly hoarse, "Do you think Wang Ling... is normal?"

Xia Zhinuan was taken aback: "What do you mean?"

"His skills." Luo Xinghe stared at Olaf's precise, millisecond-level movement on the screen: "Predicting Caitlyn's E skill with a right-angle movement, predicting my Flash landing point with his Q skill, always targeting me first in team fights... This can't be explained by mere skill."

He turned his head and looked at Xia Zhinuan: "It's like he can see right through me."

Xia Zhinu pursed her lips: "Maybe he just has a particularly good intuition? Some talented players do have this ability."

"Intuition?" Luo Xinghe laughed, but his smile was a little cold. "Zhi Nuan, we've been streaming for three years, and we've played at least eight hundred, if not a thousand, high-elo games. Have you ever seen a genius player play a bottom-tier hero like this?"

He paused, then said, word by word, "Unless, he used another method."

"You mean..."

"Cultivation techniques," Luo Xinghe said in a low voice, "If he used mental perception beyond the rules, or some kind of precognitive ability..."

He didn't finish speaking, but his meaning was already very clear.

Xia Zhinuan's expression changed slightly: "Xinghe, you can't say things like that. There's no evidence."

"So I need to find evidence." Luo Xinghe took a sip of tea, his eyes sharp. "I've already applied for monitoring permission for tomorrow's illusion simulation battle. As long as there's any abnormality in his psionic energy fluctuations, the system will definitely detect it."

"Even if we manage to capture him, it's possible he has an extraordinary talent..."

"Then let's test it." Luo Xinghe interrupted her: "The Illusion Simulation Battle has a mental stress test module. For normal players, the mental stress value will fluctuate within the safe range. But if he uses excessive mental power... the value will definitely explode."

Xia Zhinuan looked at Luo Xinghe and suddenly felt a little unfamiliar with him.

Normally, Luo Xinghe is sunny, humorous, and always has a smile on his face.

But now, there's something almost obsessive in his eyes.

"Star River," she said softly, "Are you... caring too much?"

Luo Xinghe remained silent for a few seconds.

"0-10." He said, "Zhi Nuan, I've never been beaten like this before. Never even once."

He looked up at Xia Zhinuan and remained silent for a long while.

"I will win it back."

Luo Xinghe's voice was calm, but every word seemed to be squeezed out from between his teeth: "And I want everyone to know that he didn't win cleanly."

Xia Zhinuan opened her mouth, but ultimately said nothing.

She simply patted Luo Xinghe on the shoulder: "Get some rest, you have a competition tomorrow."

As she turned to leave, she glanced back.

Luo Xinghe was still standing in front of the screen, head tilted back, motionless.

The moonlight shone on his profile, casting a soft, half-lit glow.

...

The next morning at 7:30.

The Starry Sky Esports Center cafeteria was already bustling with activity.

Chen Chao, carrying a mountain of food, plopped down opposite Wang Ling: "Lingzi! Did you hear? The rules for today's Illusion Simulation Battle have been drastically changed!"

Wang Ling was intently peeling a boiled spirit egg when he heard this and looked up: "?"

"First-person perspective!" Chen Chao exclaimed excitedly, gesturing wildly. "Consciousness connects with heroes! It's like we become heroes ourselves, fighting in the canyon! And we can even use cultivation abilities to a limited extent!"

Guo Hao added, "According to the rulebook released last night, the Illusion Simulation Battle combines the map data of Glory League with a specially made mental illusion. After a player selects a hero, their consciousness will temporarily merge with the hero template, gaining the hero's skills and attributes, while retaining some of their own cultivation."

He paused for a moment: "For example, when a Foundation Establishment cultivator enters an illusion, although their hero attributes remain unchanged, their reaction speed, dynamic vision, and mental strength will all be stronger than ordinary people. The Golden Core stage is even stronger. This is to simulate the state of real cultivators in battle."

Li Changzhe stuffed a small steamed bun into his mouth and mumbled, "Doesn't that mean the higher your cultivation level, the more advantageous it is?"

"In theory, yes." Zhou Rui continued, "But the rules also have a balancing mechanism. First, the use of cultivation abilities is limited to enhancing one's own control and cannot be directly applied to hero skills. For example, you can use spiritual power to enhance your hand speed, but you cannot use fireball to replace a hero's skill."

"Second," he continued, "there is a mental load monitoring system within the illusion. Overusing cultivation abilities will cause the load value to spike, and exceeding the safety threshold will force you to exit and be judged as negative."

Then, Chen Chao turned his gaze to Wang Ling: "Lingzi, what are you planning to do today? Still jungle? Or continue training Luo Xinghe?"

Wang Ling didn't say anything, but made a small gesture of slitting his throat.

Chen Chao chuckled and said, "Got it, got it!"

At this moment, Sun Rong and Jiu Gong Liangzi also came over with their plates.

Sun Rong wore a light blue tracksuit today, with her hair tied in a high ponytail. She looked refreshing and capable, but there were faint dark circles under her eyes, indicating that she hadn't slept well last night.

"Good morning." She sat down next to Wang Ling, her voice a little soft.

Wang Ling glanced at her and pushed another unopened bag of honey-glazed chicken wing flavored instant noodles to her.

Sun Rong paused for a moment, then took it: "Thank you..."

Ryoko Kugimiya sat on the other side of Sun Rong, half-covering her face with a folding fan, her eyes scanning back and forth between Wang Ling and Sun Rong, a meaningful smile on her lips.

"Wang Ling," she suddenly spoke, her Mandarin still carrying that Sun Island accent, "Please make sure you give it your all in today's match."

Wang Ling: "?"

"After all..." Ryoko Kumiya blinked, "Some people may need a little stimulation to understand their own hearts."

That statement is completely incoherent.

But Sun Rong's face flushed red instantly.

Wang Ling looked at Sun Rong.

Sun Rong was looking down, taking small bites of her steamed bun, her ears burning red.

...

8:50 a.m.

Building A, Hall 3, Illusion Battle Arena.

Unlike the previous battle halls, there are no rows of esports booths here.
