Chapter 2215 The Story of Crossing the Earth's Core

Faced with such a terrifying opponent full of unknowns, Tomohisa knew that his plan could no longer proceed smoothly.

In addition, with the involvement of powerful conglomerates like Huaguo Water Curtain Group, no matter how strong their backing capital is, how can they compete with the currently booming leading pharmaceutical companies?

"We were just one step away..." Zhijiu couldn't help but sigh.

He leaned weakly against the tree behind him, never feeling so exhausted. He thought he had overcome all obstacles, but he never expected to overturn at the last step.

Now that Sun Rong and Wang Ling have found him and mentioned the construction of the great formation, even if Zhi Jiunian wanted to pretend to be confused, he knew that this matter could probably no longer be hidden.

Only.

He confessed everything he knew to Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

Just as Wang Ling's investigation revealed, the construction of the grand array was intended to drain the spiritual veins beneath the entire Songhai Sea, and only in this way could the ultimate goals of Zhi Jiunian and the mastermind behind it be achieved simultaneously.

That is—while hindering Songhai from sending elite high school students to infiltrate the Earth's core, they can open a passage to the Earth's core first!

"So it was a war over the inner Earth?" Sun Rong was stunned upon hearing this.

These past few days, after she advanced to the Golden Core stage, she left the Mountain of Regression ahead of schedule. On her way to visit Old Huang, she and Wang Ling unexpectedly stumbled upon this suspicious matter.

Although Sun Rong had a vague feeling that this matter might be related to the inner world of the earth based on some details, she was still somewhat shocked to hear Zhi Jiu Nian say it with her own ears.

"The resources in the inner world are abundant. Have you considered the consequences of your actions?" Sun Rong asked.

These days, Hua Xiuguo has been selecting talents from major elite universities in Songhai. Ostensibly, this is to launch a new round of university ranking tests with the theme of the inner world, but the real purpose is to lay the foundation for Hua Xiuguo's voice in the inner world in advance.

After all, on the upgraded Earth, Huaxiu Kingdom is the first cultivation kingdom with the complete autonomy to open a passage to the Earth's core world, without exception.

For this reason, other powerful cultivation kingdoms like Mishu have been making a lot of "efforts" in recent times to master the secret method to the inner world of the earth, but in the end it has all been in vain.

The cultivators of Huaxiu Kingdom are still highly vigilant about the spies hiding among them.

Wang Ling hadn't expected that there would be a group of cultivators in the country who wanted to enter the Earth's core world ahead of time using their own power.

"Of course I knew the consequences, but fortune and honor are often found in danger."

Zhijiu Nian said, "Moreover, as far as I know, the boss behind the scenes is not after resources from the inner Earth."

"If it's not for resources, then why go to the inner world?" Sun Rong asked curiously.

"The spiritual energy in the Earth's core far exceeds that on the surface, as Miss Sun Rong must be well aware of."

Zhijiu Nian said, "The reason why the spiritual energy in the Earth's core world is always so rich is because since the first spiritual energy revival on Earth and the emergence of cultivators, whenever a cultivator dies on Earth, a portion of their spiritual energy returns to the dust and merges with the Earth."

"Over the years, the concentration of psionic energy in the inner Earth has far exceeded that on the surface, which is why many rare resources appear in the inner Earth."

"Of course, besides resources, there are other things, such as..."

"Spiritual body".

Wang Ling calmly answered.

"As expected of a senior, he is very knowledgeable."

Zhijiu Nian said, "The boss behind me went to the inner world to... find her brother's spirit."

"For my brother's spirit?"

Sun Rong has wrinkled eyebrows.

"yes."

Zhijiu sighed and began to recount the story he knew.

There was once a young man who fell in love. He was a true martial arts prodigy, with his skills in martial arts reaching their zenith. At the young age of twenty-one, he caught the attention of the Martial Saint, one of the Ten Generals, in a national martial arts competition. The prize money from various competitions also allowed him to accumulate a considerable fortune at such a young age.

He didn't come from a very wealthy background, and he could have used the bonus to live a more affluent life and achieve greater success.

until......

A girl named Su Jinxiang appeared in his life.

For a young man who had always focused solely on his own spiritual practice and had never been in contact with the opposite sex, Su Jinxiang's appearance was like a new ray of light in his life, illuminating his past and present.

That kind of warmth can probably only be understood by those who have never truly been in love.

From the very first second he fell for Su Jinxiang, the boy did everything he could to be better to her.

Whenever Su Jinxiang said that his cultivation resources were poor, he would unhesitatingly hand over a large portion of the spirit stones he had accumulated over the years to help him with his cultivation.

He asks for nothing in return, and every time he provides a spirit stone, he labels it as a voluntary gift.

Whenever Su Jinxiang says he can't accept the pain of being separated from his loved one, he expresses his deepest feelings.

The young man would ride his spirit sword, traversing mountains and rivers to pursue the girl he loved.

In order to be closer to the girl he loved, and also to provide Su Jinxiang with more financial assistance, he lived in the cheapest house in another unfamiliar city, eating the cheapest fasting pills...

The boy thought that every sincere effort he made was worthwhile, but to his utter surprise, all his sincerity only brought him deeper harm.

Everything he gave, all his sincerity, did not shake Su Jinxiang's sincerity in the slightest.

She continued to toy with him, trampling his dignity and sincerity under her high heels with each deception...

The young man, who was only twenty-one years old, chose to end his life in the most regrettable way at an age when he should have been fully blossoming.

Su Jinxiang, the woman who had been deceiving him all along, never showed the slightest remorse.

This also proves the saying that in a relationship, only the sincere party is always the most vulnerable to being hurt...

...

After Zhi Jiunian finished telling the story, Sun Rong, who was already very sentimental, felt a lump in her throat and tears welled up in her eyes.

Even Wang Ling, who was usually reserved and not easily moved by emotions, was touched the moment he heard the story.

He even hoped that Tomohisa had made up this story to deceive them.

But unfortunately.

For Wang Ling, who could see through everything, he knew that everything Zhi Jiu Nian was saying at this moment was the truth...

The boy in the story who was obsessed with love was ultimately hurt by love and chose to fall into eternal sleep.
