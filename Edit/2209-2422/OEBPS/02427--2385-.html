Chapter 2385 The Advisors Alliance's Paradise Plan

On the holographic screen, the match-up list of "Wang Ling vs. Luo Xinghe" was prominently displayed, particularly eye-catching.

The lobby was silent for a moment, then a series of barely suppressed whispers broke out.

"It's those two again..."

"This is a solo exhibition match, best-of-three format."

"Luo Xinghe was completely outplayed this morning, and he's still coming this afternoon?"

"Maybe we can turn things around? The mindset is different in exhibition matches and training matches."

These whispers pierced Luo Xinghe's ears like needles. He sat in the Xingyao High School area, his fingers gripping the edge of the tactical tablet tightly.

Then he faced the king's order.

Furthermore, the recording of this exhibition match will be made public...

In an instant, Luo Xinghe's mind was flooded with images that he could never accept:

For example, the post-match video was edited into a compilation titled "Luo Xinghe Gets Slaughtered Like a Pig by High School Students," which went viral on various video websites, garnering over ten million views. The comment section was filled with ridicule.

For example: his fan group disbanded, his live stream viewership plummeted, and his contracted company sent him a termination letter. People who used to call him "Xinghe Dada" now call him trash. In the ever-changing world of the internet, where people habitually create idols and then trample them underfoot, nothing is impossible.

...

"No...no way..." Luo Xinghe murmured softly, cold sweat beading on his forehead.

He forced himself to take deep breaths, trying to cultivate the qualities of a professional broadcaster: calmness, focus, and treating the match like any other game.

My heart was pounding like it was going to explode.

My stomach was churning, and the metallic sweetness of the blood I had just vomited in the elevator still lingered in my throat.

Even more terrifying, the notification of the failed Ghost Mission kept replaying in his mind.

"The target's danger level was severely misjudged... The executor suffered irreversible psychological trauma..."

The assassin, at the mid-stage of the Nascent Soul realm, suffered a mental breakdown before even touching the hem of Wang Ling's clothes.

What exactly is Wang Ling's cultivation level? What is his background?

"We are now conducting pre-match equipment adjustments. Please prepare yourselves, both athletes," a staff member announced over the loudspeaker.

Luo Xinghe mechanically stood up.

My legs feel a little weak.

He looked towards the opposite training area.

Wang Ling had already put down the instant noodles and was slowly walking towards the designated illusion access cabin.

Chen Chao was gesturing wildly and talking to Wang Ling, who occasionally nodded.

The man remained as calm and composed as ever.

It was as if what was about to take place was not a high-profile exhibition match, but an after-dinner stroll.

Luo Xinghe forced a smile, but it looked worse than crying.

He walked to his access cabin, the cover of which was already open, and soft guiding light emanated from inside.

Lie down inside, connect with consciousness.

"I can't lose... I can't lose..."

He muttered to himself, his fingers trembling as he pressed against the edge of the access cabin.

How can we win?

Compared to operational skills? Wang Ling's predictions and reactions are simply beyond the realm of human capabilities.

As for tactics, there are only so many routines in solo matches, and the opponent has already figured them out.

Luck? To be able to pull a Hextech ARAM exclusive rune, that's incredibly lucky, isn't it?

Unless... the king orders a lenient approach.

Is it possible?

A ridiculous thought suddenly popped into Luo Xinghe's mind: If I kneel down and beg him now, will he give me some face?

The moment the thought arose, he felt a strong sense of shame.

I, Luo Xinghe, a top streamer with millions of followers, actually think of kneeling down and begging for mercy?!

"Athletes, please get into your positions as soon as possible," a staff member urged.

Luo Xinghe gritted his teeth, lifted his leg, and stepped into the access cabin.

But just as he stepped halfway in—

"Buzz!"

My mind suddenly went blank.

Immediately afterwards, countless images flashed through Luo Xinghe's mind.

He recalled his childhood when his family was poor, and how other children would beat him on the ground and call him a "bastard without parents." He cried but made up his mind, telling himself that he must make something of himself and escape this fate of being at the bottom!

Then, he recalled his first time streaming a game. There were only three viewers. He talked for four hours straight, trying to liven up the atmosphere in front of the camera while playing the game, until his voice became hoarse.

Later, on the day he finally made it through and officially signed the contract, he cried all night looking at the contract.

Luo Xinghe's thoughts were racing. He even thought about the first time he saw Xia Zhinuan. The girl's undisguised praise made his heart skip a beat.

But in the very last scene of the slideshow, Wang Ling appeared.

That wooden man gnawing on instant noodles stole all the attention, stole the victory, stole... everything he had painstakingly built up.

"It's all because of him...it's all because of him!"

Luo Xinghe's eyes reddened.

Immediately afterwards, the chaotic thoughts in his mind turned into his own imagined scenarios of fear regarding Wang Ling:

He saw himself standing on the podium, with no one below. The live chat was flooded with comments like "Retire!" and "Don't embarrass yourself."

He saw Xia Zhinuan standing on Wang Ling's side and said coldly, "Luo Xinghe, you piece of trash! You will never be as good as him!"

Finally, he saw himself leaping off the high-rise building...

"puff!!!"

A mouthful of blood gushed from Luo Xinghe's mouth, splattering onto the inner wall of the access cabin, a glaring crimson stain.

He swayed and fell straight backward.

"Star River?!" The staff member was stunned.

"Luo Xinghe!"

"Medical team! Call the medical team immediately!"

The entire hall instantly erupted into chaos.

Everyone stood up and craned their necks to look at the Xingyao High School area. There lay Luo Xinghe on the ground, his face ashen, blood still trickling from the corner of his mouth, his body twitching slightly.

Several training camp doctors rushed over and quickly examined her.

"Disordered spiritual energy, damaged heart meridians... It's a backlash from inner demons!"

"Quick! Get him to the hospital!"

The stretcher was brought in, Luo Xinghe was quickly secured, and then carried out of the hall. The whole process took less than two minutes, but it left everyone stunned.

Before the competition even started, a contestant vomited blood and fainted?

What's going on?

Zhuo Yi strode down from the second-floor monitoring room, his face grave. He glanced in the direction Luo Xinghe had left, then at Wang Ling, who stood calmly beside the access cabin, and sighed inwardly.

The invisible pressure from the master... can really drive a person crazy.

"Everyone, quiet down." Zhuo Yi walked to the center stage, his voice resounding throughout the hall through the amplified magic instrument: "Due to a contestant's sudden physical condition, today's exhibition match final cannot continue."
