Chapter 2372 The Spark That Ignites Public Opinion

In the No. 1 battle hall of Building A, the holographic projection screen is already lit up.

The group list for the second round of the points competition was displayed high above, with the bright red "Third Team vs. First Team" standing out starkly.

"Wow, what a small world!" Chen Chao rubbed his fists together, eager for a fight. "Lingzi's going up against that brat Luo Xinghe again! This time, we'll teach him a lesson!"

Wang Ling sat on the first team's bench, slowly tearing open a packet of new flavored instant noodles.

This is the cheese flavor that Sun Rong gave me this morning.

He didn't say anything, but just looked up at the opposite preparation area.

The five members of the third team were huddled together discussing tactics.

Luo Xinghe stood in the center, holding a tactical tablet in his hand, his expression calm, talking to Liu Kaichuan and Xia Zhinuan beside him.

From his appearance, he had completely regained the composure of a professional anchor, as if the beating he suffered at the hands of Wang Ling yesterday had never happened.

But Wang Ling knew that Luo Xinghe was only appearing calm on the surface.

"He's faking it," Wang Ling thought to himself.

Sun Rong sat next to Wang Ling and whispered, "Wang Ling, in this round of the competition... Luo Xinghe might target you."

"Mmm," Wang Ling responded, continuing to eat his instant noodles.

Sun Rong continued, "From a roster perspective, the third team has three main players from the training camp: Luo Xinghe, Xia Zhinuan, and Liu Kaichuan. On paper, their strength is not weak. But Xia Zhinuan yesterday..."

She didn't finish her sentence, but the meaning was clear.

Xia Zhinuan betrayed Luo Xinghe by switching sides during the friendly match yesterday.

Today, with the two on the same team, how much chemistry they can still have is a big question.

Li Changzhe scratched his head: "Can Xia Zhinuan still act?"

"No." Wang Ling suddenly spoke up.

Several people looked at him.

Wang Ling put the last piece of instant noodles into his mouth, chewed it twice, and then slowly said, "She wants to win."

Sun Rong seemed to understand something: "You mean, Xia Zhinuan will take this seriously?"

Wang Ling nodded.

He has a very good eye for people.

Xia Zhinuan's play in the dragon pit team fight yesterday was a testament to her skill and her choice. The girl has made her decision, and she won't look back.

"That's even better!" Chen Chao grinned. "Fair competition! Let's crush them head-on!"

...

At this moment, the third team is in the preparation area.

Luo Xinghe was outlining tactics: "In this round of the competition, the system will randomly trigger interference factors. What we need to do is use these interference factors to create opportunities."

He looked at Xia Zhinuan with a gentle smile: "Zhinuan, you play mid lane, choose a hero with crowd control and roaming ability. Liu Kai will play top lane, choose a hero who can initiate team fights. I'll play ADC, Zhang Yu will support. Lin Feng will jungle."

This allocation is reasonable.

But Xia Zhinuan could sense that Luo Xinghe was subtly observing her reaction as he spoke.

He was testing the waters.

He was testing whether she would still listen to him.

"Okay." Xia Zhinu nodded, her expression natural: "I'll play Galio, he's quick to provide support and has good control."

"Okay." A hint of satisfaction flashed in Luo Xinghe's eyes: "Then our lineup will focus on mid-jungle-support synergy. In the early game, we'll rely on Lin Feng's rhythm, and in the mid-game, Zhi Nuan and I will engage in team fights."

As he spoke, he drew several attack routes on the tactical tablet.

Everything seemed so professional and so well-organized.

But Xia Zhinuan knew that beneath this calm surface, undercurrents were surging.

The silver button inside her collar was slightly hot.

This is the miniature recorder the Strategist Alliance gave her; it's working.

"Xinghe." Xia Zhinuan suddenly spoke, her voice softening, "About yesterday... I'm sorry. I've thought about it a lot since then, and I was indeed too impulsive. Wang Ling... he may really be strong, but I shouldn't have treated you like that."

That's half true and half false.

The apology was genuine, but the subsequent twist was orchestrated by Ryoko Kumiya.

Luo Xinghe paused in his hand movements.

He turned to look at Xia Zhinuan, his eyes filled with complex emotions: "Zhinuan, I'm glad you think that way. Actually, I was also wrong. I shouldn't have forced you to do those things. It's a competition, winning and losing are normal. I was just too serious."

He spoke sincerely, and even patted Xia Zhinuan on the shoulder.

But Xia Zhinuan could feel that the temperature of his palm was very cool.

"So... shall we play our best in today's match?" Xia Zhinuan asked.

"Of course," Luo Xinghe smiled. "We're going to win. And we're going to win beautifully."

He paused, then lowered his voice: "Zhi Nuan, you saw it yesterday too, Wang Ling's actions... weren't they a bit too outrageous? Especially that Flash-block, would a normal person have that kind of reaction speed?"

They've arrived.

Xia Zhinuan's heart tightened, but she remained outwardly calm: "It is quite outrageous. But after thinking about it, maybe he is just that kind of genius. There are always some people in the world whose talents we cannot understand."

"A genius?" Luo Xinghe scoffed, but quickly composed himself. "Perhaps. But Zhi Nuan, what do you think the training camp will do if we can find evidence that he used unfair methods?"

Xia Zhinuan pretended to think: "They'll probably... disqualify me, right? In serious cases, they might even be banned."

"Yes." Luo Xinghe sighed, "It's a pity we don't have any evidence. The entire match yesterday was monitored, and the data was all normal. The training camp officials also issued a statement saying that everything was in compliance with regulations."

He kept his eyes fixed on Xia Zhinu as he said this.

Xia Zhinuan knew that he was testing whether she knew more about the inside story.

"Then... let's just forget about it." Xia Zhinuan lowered her head: "It's just a training match anyway, there's no need to take it too seriously."

"Yes, you're right." Luo Xinghe nodded, but Xia Zhinuan could clearly see the coldness in his eyes.

He won't let it go.

He's just waiting for his chance.

...

"Attention all players, the second round of the competition is about to begin. A random interference factor will be triggered in this match, so please be prepared."

The system notification sounded in the lobby.

The two teams of ten people successively lay down in the illusion access cabin.

Consciousness connection.

When Wang Ling opened his eyes again, he was already standing in the spring water of the Summoner's Canyon.

He chose the top lane this round, and the champion was Fiora, the Grand Duelist.

A warrior who requires extreme skill and awareness.

"Lingzi, is your Sword Maiden any good?" Chen Chao asked in voice chat.

He played jungle this round and chose [Demacian Prince].

"Mm," Wang Ling replied simply.

Sun Rong played support [Jana, the Storm's Fury], Guo Hao played mid [Kassadin, the Void Walker], and Li Changzhe played ADC [Ezreal, the Explorer].

The opposing team's lineup is as follows: Luo Xinghe as ADC [Velusive Arrow], Xia Zhinuan as mid laner [Gario the Colossus], Liu Kaichuan as top laner [Renker the Butcher], Zhang Yu as support [Soul Lock Helmsman], and Lin Feng as jungler [Lee Sin the Blind Monk].

In terms of lineup, the third team is more focused on team fights and control, while the first team is more flexible, but has two core heroes, Kassadin and Fiora, in the late game.

"Interference factor extraction in progress..."

The system's voice echoed in the sky.

A few seconds later, the results were in:

[Distracting factor in this event: Mental interference]

[Effect: At the 8th, 15th, and 22nd minute of the match, a player will be randomly selected and inflicted with a "Confusion" status for 30 seconds. During this time, the player's commands will be randomly reversed or their target will be distorted.]

"Holy crap! Mind interference!" Chen Chao wailed in the voice chat, "If I were drawn, wouldn't I be sending myself to the tower?"

Guo Hao calmly analyzed: "Random selection has an equal probability. We must be prepared for interference and try to avoid high-risk operations at the time when interference is triggered."

Sun Rong said softly, "Wang Ling, be careful."

Wang Ling grunted in agreement and controlled the Sword Maiden to move towards the top lane.

He knew Luo Xinghe would target him.

And mental interference is the best opportunity.

...

The match begins.

In the top lane, Wang Ling's Fiora is facing off against Liu Kaichuan's Crocodile.

Liu Kaichuan lived up to his reputation as the main top laner of Chihuo High School. His laning details were excellent, and he used the Crocodile's strong period to constantly trade blows from level one.

But Wang Ling's Sword Maiden's positioning was outrageously cunning.

Every time Crocodile used his Q skill [Tyrant's Strike], Fiora dodged it with movement or her Q skill [Sky Slash].

Every time Crocodile tries to stun Fiora with his W skill, Fiora can always counter with her W skill, "Laurent's Eye."

At level three, both sides have only half their health remaining.

Liu Kaichuan reported via voice message, sounding like he was gritting his teeth: "The pressure in the top lane is immense, and Fiora's mechanics are very delicate."

Fiora is also one of his main heroes. He didn't expect Wang Ling to choose top lane this game and even use his main hero to fight him... How freak is this guy? He seems to be proficient in all lanes except support!

Luo Xinghe's voice came through: "Hold on, wait for the jungler. Lin Feng, gank top before level six."

"Okay." Lin Feng's Lee Sin was farming jungle camps when he heard this and marked the top lane.
