Chapter 2235 The Radiant Armor and the Central Government's Summoning

"Boom—boom boom boom boom—!!!"

The deafening, dense explosions shattered the tranquility at the entrance of the Earth Core Experiment Base. A destructive energy wave, far more violent than the three consecutive attacks it had suffered in the previous sixty missions, crashed against the protective shield, and the entire base seemed to be trembling violently.

The sixty people gathered on the spacious platform of the main hall were startled by the sudden series of bombardments and looked up through the huge glass dome.

The entrance area in the distance outside the window was almost completely engulfed by a series of bursts of crimson fireballs. Thick smoke billowed, and turbulent energy distorted the space.

"Holy crap! Ten shots?! Has Old Man Teng gone mad?" Chen Chao's eyes almost popped out of their sockets. Although he had just withstood three small Eastern Armor Cannons, he was well aware of their power.

Ten consecutive saturation attacks? That's practically enough to evaporate the newly arrived celestial ship, ship and crew, together!

"Who's so unlucky? They're being 'targeted' right after they arrive?" Guo Hao was still shaken. Even in the safe gathering hall, the residual energy waves transmitted through the protective shield and the thick rock walls still made his chest feel tight.

He subconsciously touched his nose, recalling how he had almost overexerted himself by performing the ultimate hand seals.

Sun Rong, Wang Zhen, Liu Qingyi, and others also had their brows furrowed.

Even the well-informed Fang Xing and the monk Jin Deng disagreed with Teng Luchen's "welcome ceremony," which was clearly driven by resentment and went far beyond common sense.

"It's Beijing Number 8 High School," Wang Ling's calm voice rang out.

His divine sense swept across the area, and he had already discerned the identity of the celestial vessel that was receiving special care.

Just as that destructive energy was about to completely engulf the immortal ship, an extremely dazzling white-gold light suddenly burst out from the side of the immortal ship!

"Buzz—!"

A metal cube, about the size of a palm and composed entirely of countless fine white-gold scales, was thrown out with great effort!

The cube instantly disintegrated in mid-air, and countless scales, like stars given life, frantically combined, extended, and stacked at an unimaginable speed!

In just half a breath, an enormous platinum shield, with its mysterious and intricate patterns and every scale exuding an aura of indestructibility, materialized out of thin air in front of the celestial ship!

The surface of the giant shield was covered with mysterious runic auras, as if they were formed from the scales of an ancient divine beast, and a vast and powerful defensive aura swept out!

"Hiss—! Saint-level defensive artifact [Ten Thousand Scale Armor]!" Guo Hao gasped. As a rune array enthusiast, he immediately recognized this top-tier item, which was extremely expensive and could only be owned by top sects or super-rich individuals.

"A single activation can form the 'Scaled Armor Formation,' which can last for at least half an hour, enough to withstand a full-force attack from a Nascent Soul stage cultivator! This guy... is too powerful!"

Chen Chao was also astonished: "Goodness... how many spirit stones does this thing cost? Does Li Changzhe's family own a spirit stone mine?"

He recalled how long he had scrimped and saved to acquire a suitable magical artifact, and then looked at how others casually tossed out a priceless Saint-level magical artifact—the difference was just too great.

The devastating explosions relentlessly bombarded the massive Scaled Armor Formation. The platinum shield, like a stabilizing pillar, remained unmoved under the ten earth-shattering barrages. Its surface shimmered with light, absorbing and annihilating all the violent energy impacts, leaving only ripples of expanding transparent energy. The immortal ship inside the Scaled Armor Formation barely even shook.

After the embers of the last cannonball dissipated, the light of the platinum shield slowly faded, automatically disintegrating into a slightly dim metal cube, which flew back into the immortal ship.

Although the spiritual light on the surface of the Jingmen Number 8 High School's fairy boat was slightly dim, it was otherwise unharmed and was swaying and accelerating toward the berth of the gathering hall. On the bridge, Li Changzhe's face, which was both angry and pained, and had a hint of relief after surviving a disaster, could be vaguely seen.

The students of Number 60 High School watched the slowly approaching fairy boat, their hearts filled with complex emotions.

Li Changzhe's extravagant emergency response, while solving the immediate crisis, perfectly confirmed his "wealthy" persona. Teng Luchen's venting-like actions, with their underlying targeting and probing intent, caused Sun Rong, Fang Xing, and Wang Zhen, who were in the know, to frown deeply. The monk Jin Deng clasped his hands together, uttered a low Buddhist chant, and a glint of shrewdness flashed in his eyes.

Just as the students of Number 60 High School were preparing to welcome Li Changzhe, who had just experienced a "big scene", a figure wearing a smart trench coat hurriedly walked out of the inner passage and came straight to the area where the students of Number 60 High School were gathered.

"Junior Brother Wang Ling..." The person who came was Zhuo Yi. Under the surprised gazes of everyone, he pretended to be calm and his expression remained unchanged. He secretly gave Wang Ling a wink, and his attitude was completely genuine.

Zhuo Yi's arrival, and his flustered appearance, surprised Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe, who had just passed the welcome test.

Zhuo Yi seemed oblivious to the reactions of the others, lowered his voice, and said quickly and clearly, "Junior Brother Wang Ling... it's like this, Commander Teng is in the core hub and specifically asked to see you."

Although most of the people here were acquaintances, there were still outsiders present. In front of a few outsiders, he still had to put on a show. He couldn't bring himself to call him "Master" right now, so he could only address him as "Junior Brother."

He paused, then looked at Wang Ling and added, "We require the elite core representatives of the 'Sixty Sects,' especially you, to go there as soon as possible."

Upon hearing this, the atmosphere in the sixty-member group instantly became tense. Sun Rong's heart tightened, and she immediately looked at Wang Ling. The Golden Lamp Monk maintained his kind and benevolent expression, but a hint of understanding flashed in the depths of his eyes.

Having just suffered a defeat at Number 60 High School, Teng Luchen vented his anger on Number 8 High School in Beijing, and then impatiently "called out" Wang Ling to go to the central hall...

This is absolutely not for the purpose of reward!

The old fox clearly hasn't given up. Having failed with long-range artillery probing before, he's now going for direct, "face-to-face" observation!

Wang Ling remained expressionless, seemingly unsurprised by the summons. However, his usually calm eyes flickered slightly, revealing a barely perceptible ripple of emotion.

He looked at Zhuo Yi, nodded slightly, and gave a soft "hmm." Although the voice was soft, it seemed to weigh a thousand pounds on everyone's hearts.

He glanced thoughtfully at the entrance area outside the window—there, the Jingmen Number 8 High School's fairy boat had just come to a stop, and Li Changzhe was straightening his somewhat disheveled clothes while muttering to himself with heartache, "What a huge loss, what a huge loss, isn't the Huaxiu Country's price control department going to do anything about it?"

Guided by the staff, Li Changzhe walked towards the lobby—then turned his gaze back to the entrance of the deep passage leading to the central hall.

The elite representatives of the Number 60 High School, led by Wang Ling, have all assembled.
