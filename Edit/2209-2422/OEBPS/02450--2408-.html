Chapter 2408 Three Brothers

Guo Hao stared at the densely packed menu on the screen, his heart pounding like a drum.

The system backend interface was much more complex than he had imagined. On the left was permission management, in the middle was the real-time data stream, and on the right were various parameter setting options.

His gaze swept quickly across the area, finally settling on a gray "Test Mode" button.

"Uncle, how do I use this test mode?" He tried to make his voice sound like simple curiosity.

Guo Mingyuan came over and pointed to the screen: "Look, click here, then enter the test duration, scan accuracy parameters, and simulation environment settings... But you don't need to worry about these, just take a look."

Guo Hao nodded while using the corner of his eye to memorize the operation path.

The final step requires the administrator's password.

Guo Hao's heart sank, but he still maintained a curious expression: "Uncle, is this password fixed?"

"Of course not, it's randomly generated every day," Guo Mingyuan said. "The system will automatically send the password for the day to the encrypted email addresses of the three of us supervisors. To enable test mode, all three of us must authorize it simultaneously; none of us can be missing."

We're doomed.

Guo Hao felt a chill run down his spine.

He originally thought he only needed to deal with his uncle, but he didn't expect it would require three people.

"So strict..." He chuckled dryly.

"Of course." Guo Mingyuan said proudly, "This is the core system of a key project in the city, with a very high security level. Not to mention you, even if I wanted to start the test mode alone, I would have to wait for the other two supervisors to be present."

Guo Hao's mind raced.

The competition is starting in three days, and he doesn't have time to plan carefully.

We must think of another way.

"Uncle, how do you usually perform system maintenance, such as upgrades or debugging?" he asked from a different angle.

"The maintenance methods are different."

Ming-Yuan Kuo walked to another terminal and brought up a blue interface: "The maintenance mode has lower privileges, so I can enter with just my card. However, in this mode, you can only view logs and check hardware status, but you can't change the core parameters."

Guo Hao's eyes lit up again.

I can't change the parameters, but I can view the logs.

Will there be any useful information in the log?

"Can I see the maintenance mode?" he asked.

"That works." Guo Mingyuan readily swiped his card to log in: "Anyway, it won't change anything, just treat it as a visit and learning experience."

The blue interface unfolds, with a menu tree on the left and a blank area on the right.

Guo Hao quickly browsed the menu:

Then open the System Log.

Lines of records popped up on the screen:

[System self-check passed at 14:32:11 on March 15]

[March 15, 14:33:05 User [Guo Mingyuan] logged into maintenance mode]

[Scanner calibration completed at 14:35:22 on March 15]

...

It's densely packed, keep scrolling down.

Guo Hao's eyes scanned the information rapidly, like a scanner.

Soon, Guo Hao understood.

The system runs a test mode every few days for a short time, about 20 to 30 minutes.

Moreover, judging from the logs, the scanning accuracy was adjusted for each test to simulate different cultivation environments.

This is an opportunity.

If he could find a way to get Wang Ling to complete the scan during the twenty or thirty minutes the system entered test mode on the day of the competition...

"Uncle, when do you usually do the tests?" he asked casually.

"Every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 9:00 AM," Guo Mingyuan said. "It's a fixed time, without fail."

Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

The match is three days later, on Thursday.

No.

Guo Hao frowned, but then he remembered that the competition was starting at 2 p.m., while the test was at 9 a.m.

The timing doesn't match.

unless......

"What if the system malfunctions and needs to be tested temporarily?" he persisted.

"Then we'll have to go through the emergency procedures," Guo Mingyuan said. "All three people must be on site, and we also have to report to the municipal bureau. It's very troublesome, and we usually try to avoid it if possible."

Guo Hao's heart sank again.

It seems that starting with the system itself is too difficult.

He needs Plan B.

"Uncle, I think I understand now." Guo Hao closed the interface, turned around, and flashed a bright smile: "Thank you so much! This knowledge will be a huge help to my thesis!"

Guo Mingyuan patted him on the shoulder: "Study hard, and you'll be more successful than your uncle in the future."

"Absolutely!" Guo Hao smiled, but his mind was racing.

It seems that the path of using a hacked system is not viable.

We need to think of another way.

...

It was 8 p.m. at Chen Chao's house.

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe reunited.

This time we didn't go to a barbecue restaurant; we stayed in Chen Chao's room.

A pile of documents was spread out on the table: competition rules, venue floor plan, system instructions, and a storage bag that Chen Chao had taken from Luo Xinghe, inside which 500,000 spirit stones gleamed.

"So... the system can't handle it?" Chen Chao frowned after hearing Guo Hao's report.

"We can't handle it." Guo Hao shook his head: "It requires authorization from three supervisors simultaneously, and the data needs to be uploaded to the municipal bureau's server in real time. If we try to cheat during the competition, we'll be caught in no time."

Li Changzhe scratched his head: "Then what should we do?"

The room fell silent.

Chen Chao stared at the bag of spirit stones and suddenly spoke up: "How about... we just tell Wang Ling?"

Guo Hao and Li Changzhe both looked at him.

"Tell Wang Ling that Luo Xinghe is plotting against him, and that the competition is a trap."

Chen Chao continued, "Given Wang Ling's personality, he'll definitely have a way to deal with it. Maybe he'll just skip the competition altogether. Let the school deduct points; it's better than revealing his true abilities."

"No way." Guo Hao shook his head. "First of all, how do you explain that you know all this? That Luo Xinghe contacted you and you even accepted a 500,000 yuan deposit from him? What will Wang Ling think?"

Chen Chao was speechless.

"Secondly," Guo Hao pushed up his glasses, "even if Wang Ling believes you and chooses not to participate in the competition, will Luo Xinghe let it go? He definitely has a backup plan. This time we knew his plan and were able to find a way to deal with it. Next time he'll be hiding in the shadows, and we'll be even more passive."

"Then what do you suggest we do?" Li Changzhe asked anxiously.
