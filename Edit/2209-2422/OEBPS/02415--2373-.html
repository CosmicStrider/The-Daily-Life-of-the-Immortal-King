[Multiple Interference Factors Warning: There is a low probability that this match may be affected by multiple interferences. Players are advised to be prepared.]

low probability?

Judging from Luo Xinghe's recent attack, the other side likely knows the approximate time when the interference will be triggered.

[Warning: System error... Unknown data stream detected...]

[The second interfering factor is forcibly triggered]

[Interference Type: Hero Replacement]

[Effect: Randomly select a player and replace their currently used hero with a random hero of the same level that did not appear in this match. Purchased equipment will be converted into an equivalent amount of gold, and the player will receive one free online shop purchase.]

[Replacement Target: Fiora, the Sword Maiden]

[New hero being randomly selected...]

[Randomly Completed]

New Hero: Zilean, the Guardian of Time
