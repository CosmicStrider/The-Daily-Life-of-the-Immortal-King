Chapter 2289 The Battle of Devouring the Abyss

The riot at the Dragon God Altar was like pouring cold water into boiling oil, causing an instant uproar!

"Awooo—!!!"

The boy inside the giant egg—the dragon god demon child—suddenly let out a piercing, insane scream.

The eggshell binding him made a cracking sound as it broke open under the strain, and dark red and black energy poured out like a bursting dam!

He tried to break free of the remaining eggshell, and on his naked body, the unstable phantom of dragon scales flickered wildly, its color rapidly changing between rainbow and pitch black.

Those molten gold pupils had no reason whatsoever, only the most primal hunger and destructive desire. In an instant, a wave of spiritual energy was released from his body, and the powerful spiritual pressure was like a mountain, almost forming a physical version that crushed the entire scene!

Even though it was a failed product, its terrifying psionic energy fluctuations still left everyone present speechless.

At this moment, in another dragon palace in the universe, Bai Zhe's consciousness was traveling billions of light-years to perceive the astonishing psychic power of the replica he had created.

"What a pity..." Bai Zhe frowned. He had actually been secretly studying Wang Ling's genes.

This is Bai Zhe's alternative plan. If he fails to capture Wang Muyu, he can use the scientific methods of the Primordial Era to analyze and replicate a gene sequence similar to Wang Ling. If successful, he can create an unlimited number of such combat weapons as all dragon-born beings!

Unfortunately, it seems that it was just a tiny bit short.

This dragon god demon child is now out of control; uncontrolled failures cannot be used by him.

Bai Zhe sensed Wang Muyu's aura from afar. He originally wanted to spare Wang Muyu's life, but now it seemed that existing science and technology could not analyze Wang Muyu's gene sequence.

"Sorry, Muyu, your real father... for the sake of his great ambitions, has no choice but to dissect you himself..." Bai Zhe revealed a cruel smile.

...

Meanwhile, on the other side, when Wang Muyu heard Wang Ling's calm yet undeniable words "absorb it," a hint of surprise flashed in his small dragon eyes, which was then replaced by a resolute determination.

Dad never makes baseless accusations!

If Dad says it can be absorbed, then it definitely can!

Although the aura emanating from that failed product was as violent and chaotic as a star about to explode, the part containing his father's genes made him instinctively feel a sense of resistance and rejection.

But more than anything, there's a strange sense of "closeness".

That is the mutual attraction between forces of the same origin.

"roar--!!!"

At this moment, in the center of the altar, the little dragon god demon boy let out an even more painful roar. His body twisted violently, and the dark dragon scale phantom flickered. Terrifying energy waves impacted the surrounding buildings, emitting a teeth-grinding sound!

"Suppress him! Suppress him quickly!" The black balls were terrified and frantically manipulated the altar array in an attempt to stabilize the energy.

Absolutely cleaving his vertical pupils away from the Dragon Transformation Pool, his gaze fixed intently on the rampaging clone: "Priority changed! Control the Dragon God's Son! We must not let him lose control!"

Huangyan, Shenming, and Xunfeng immediately realized the seriousness of the problem.

If the altar is destroyed, they cannot bear Lord Bai Zhe's wrath!

Huangyan roared and attacked first, his claws igniting with towering black flames, transforming into two enormous fiery dragon claws that grabbed directly at the giant egg, attempting to forcibly stabilize it with brute force: "Quiet down, you bastards!"

The deep sea opened its mouth and spewed out an extremely cold torrent, not to attack, but to try to freeze the rampaging energy around the giant egg and slow down its collapse.

Xunfeng's figure flashed and appeared above the giant egg. He waved his hands, and countless fine spatial cracks cut into the energy conduit connecting the eggshell and the altar, attempting to cut off the energy supply.

Jueqi raised the Endless Silence Spear high, and the Annihilation Domain expanded to its fullest extent, enveloping the giant egg, attempting to "annihilate" that most unstable conflict energy at the level of the laws.

The four celestial-level dragon descendants joined forces, their power earth-shattering, causing the entire Dark Palace to tremble, and their energy radiance engulfed everything.

However, that clone was a monstrous biochemical product created by Bai Zhe by gathering vast resources and fusing various supreme powers. The energy turbulence it generated when it went berserk far exceeded the ordinary!

The moment the blazing black dragon claws touched the eggshell, they were violently shaken by a fierce energy mixed with seven-colored dragon light and a deathly gray aura, making it difficult to grasp!

The extremely cold breath from the deep sea was constantly evaporated by the boiling, violent energy, making it difficult to freeze effectively.

The spatial cracks created by the Xun wind were largely neutralized by the dark will patterns that spontaneously appeared on the surface of the energy conduit, resulting in minimal effect.

The annihilation domain of the Absolute did have some effect; the chaotic energy spilling out at the edges was quietly wiped out. However, the boiling, destructive energy at the core... its strength subtly resisted the power of annihilation, and the speed of annihilation was far slower than its explosive speed!

"No! The energy core of the conflict is too strong! Forcibly suppressing it will only accelerate the explosion!" Jueqi instantly assessed the situation, his voice cold and urgent, "It must be channeled! Or separated!"

But how easy is it to talk about?

Those supreme powers are now almost completely intertwined!

Just as the four dragon descendants were preoccupied with the outbreak of the clones, consuming most of their energy—

At the bottom of the Dragon Transformation Pool, Wang Muyu moved!

"Now!"

The suppressed and disguised power within him erupted like a prehistoric beast unleashed from its reins!

Click!

The rune chains binding him were instantly shattered by the immense seven-colored dragon power and completely purified!

The dark red energy liquid in the Dragon Transformation Pool was displaced, creating a vacuum zone!

Wang Muyu's small body floated up, radiating a more dazzling and solid golden light than before. The pair of soft dragon horns on his forehead seemed to have hardened a bit, shimmering with iridescent light.

He no longer concealed himself, and the majesty of the Son of Ten Thousand Dragons was unleashed without restraint.

Although his cultivation level was still immature, the absolute suppression of his life level made all the dragon descendants present, including the dragon god demon child, pause for a moment!

"What is he trying to do?!" Huang Yan turned around abruptly, both shocked and furious.

"The target has escaped control!" A slight tremor appeared in Shenming's icy tone.

Xunfeng exclaimed, "Wow! This kid is definitely up to something!"

The molten gold pupils of the Absolute Confinement suddenly contracted, and the data analysis went into overdrive with alarms, instantly confirming all the previous unease and doubts!

He couldn't even care less about the clones anymore. He turned the Endless Silence Spear around, and the Annihilation Domain was about to envelop Wang Muyu!

But it was too late!

Wang Muyu had a clear objective and completely ignored the four people in Jueqi.

The enormous phantom of the ancestor of all dragons reappeared behind him, emitting an ancient dragon roar that shook the universe!

Then, he opened his small mouth and sucked hard at the giant egg in the center of the altar that was about to explode!

It is not a physical act of inhalation, but a domineering devouring that originates from the very source of one's bloodline!

This is one of Wang Muyu's unique dragon-blooded abilities... Ten Thousand Dragons Return to the Ruins!

This ability was an instinct awakened deep within his bloodline, which he had never fully unleashed before. Now, under Wang Ling's command and stimulated by the "tonics" before him, he instinctively used it!

An invisible vortex, seemingly capable of swallowing galaxies, suddenly appeared before Wang Muyu. It was more terrifying than a black hole in the universe, containing astonishing spiritual energy, as if everything caught in it would be annihilated!

A terrifying suction force erupted!

The target is the berserk energy core within the giant egg, which is a mixture of Wang Ling's genes, various dragon origins, and outer god energy!

"Sizzle sizzle—!"
