Chapter 2294 The Trap of Appetite

The forum conference themed "We are all here, we are still here," hosted by the Xibei Cultivation Food Group, was grandly opened at the largest convention center in Songhai City.

Huge holographic projection advertisements are displayed along the main thoroughfares of Songhai City, with highly enticing slogans: "Revolutionizing the taste buds! Ushering in a new era! Fulfilling all your fantasies about [Food Cultivation]! Food cultivation can also solidify your foundation and lead you step by step to immortality!"

In this scientific era of cultivation, the concept of food cultivation is familiar to every modern cultivator.

The reason why many children can break through to the Foundation Establishment stage or even the Golden Core stage at an early age is largely because, in the modern context, they have long since left behind the old era of cultivation where resources were scarce.

Furthermore, with the addition of certain technological means, the spiritual energy inherent in those foods can naturally inspire many cultivators to receive high-intensity spiritual energy replenishment from a young age, making their cultivation speed exceptionally rapid!

Therefore, the problem faced by most cultivators is not a lack of spiritual energy, but rather an unstable foundation.

The forum now set up by the XiBai Cultivation Food Group is a technological innovation for the "Food Cultivation Party," which allows people to undergo food cultivation training without worrying about an unstable foundation.

This alone attracted many monks who loved excitement to come and watch.

In conclusion, this forum was far larger than a typical new product launch, and was a meticulously planned carnival for all.

On the campus of Number 60 High School, this matter almost dominated the discussion during breaks.

"Have you heard? Hehe, they're releasing a bunch of groundbreaking new food-based health products! They even offer custom-made options! They'll tailor the perfect spiritual food to each person's constitution and preferences! If we eat them regularly, we might even have a chance to reach the Golden Core stage before graduation!" a well-informed classmate excitedly shared.

"Customized? That's just hype, isn't it? What kind of technology would that require? Wouldn't that be a devastating blow to the traditional elixir industry?" Some people expressed their doubts and secretly glanced at Sun Rong beside them.

Everyone knew very well that this was a business war within the cultivation world.

Once these food-based cultivation products are widely available, they can satisfy cultivators' appetites and increase their spiritual power. As a result, traditional elixirs, which offer little in the way of a sensory experience, will inevitably lose their appeal.

"Hey, you guys don't understand. There are free samples at the forum! Limited edition! It's said that after eating them, even ordinary spirit rice will taste like dragon liver and phoenix marrow!"

Chen Chao held his phone, watching the pre-event live stream of the forum, his mouth practically watering: "It's a pity our school wasn't invited, otherwise we would definitely have to go and try it."

Guo Hao was more rational: "Last time we visited their factory, didn't it break down at the end? I always felt that this company was a bit suspicious. Sun Rong, what do you think?"

Sun Rong was looking down at the news about the forum on her phone screen, her brows slightly furrowed.

The faint sense of warning emanating from the sea had not completely disappeared, but had become more subtle.

Hearing Guo Hao's question, she looked up and subconsciously glanced at Wang Ling by the window first.

Wang Ling remained aloof from worldly affairs, holding a bag of new flavored instant noodles in his hand, munching on it in small bites, as if the hustle and bustle outside had nothing to do with him.

"Maybe so. The bigger the hype, the more careful we need to be."

Sun Rong withdrew her gaze. Of course, she knew the implications. She replied softly to Guo Hao, "I just feel that something is not right about this kind of thing that completely caters to appetite."

As the heir to the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, her intuition told her that there must be some kind of conspiracy behind this grand forum, which made her wary.

"Wang Ling, what do you think?" Chen Chao approached Wang Ling and asked casually.

Wang Ling paused, glanced at Chen Chao, and then slowly shook his head.

His gaze seemed to drift unintentionally across Chen Chao's phone screen, onto the middle-aged man standing in the center of the podium, wearing a white research gown and sporting a confident smile.

This person is none other than Dr. Lynch, the chief scientist of the HiPai Group.

He was also the person in charge of scavenging "evil thoughts crystals" in the waste disposal area yesterday.

Today, however, he has shed all the gloom and appeared in a glamorous manner, showcasing a small silver wrist-worn device to the audience.

"Ladies and gentlemen, this is the groundbreaking product launched by our Xibei Group after ten years of research, combining the best of cultivation technology and bio-psychic energy technology: [Taste Master]!" Lin Qi's voice, full of passion, resounded throughout the venue through the amplified magic device.

"Simply wear it and connect it to our Hsi-Bai [Spirit Network]. The system will automatically analyze your physical condition, emotions, and even subconscious food cravings based on your lifestyle preferences and big data. The store will then recommend the most beneficial dietary products for your constitution!"

"In addition, as long as you wear this product, even the most bland and ordinary foods in your life will taste and feel delicious in your hearts! It can also greatly enhance the spiritual energy that food can absorb! After using it for a period of time, all users' cultivation levels will steadily improve! If it is ineffective, we will also provide a full refund!"

A gasp and enthusiastic applause erupted from the audience.

The more Wang Ling looked at it, the more it reminded him of some kind of pyramid scheme ritual...

And he really wanted to complain, wouldn't staying in this thing for too long turn a person into a pica sufferer?

At this moment, Lynch continued his demonstration with a smile.

He invited an audience member to come up and put the watch on his wrist.

Afterward, he had the audience member pick up a glass of spirit wine and take a sip.

The audience member immediately looked on incredulously: "My goodness, I don't like drinking alcohol! But this doesn't taste like alcohol at all! It tastes like my favorite fruit juice! And it feels full of spiritual energy!"

In front of everyone, the audience member picked up another psionic biscuit, took a bite, and exclaimed again, "It actually tastes like steak?! And the psionic energy I'm absorbing has definitely increased a lot compared to before!"

The on-site demonstration was extremely impactful.

After consuming ordinary psionic food multiple times, the audience member became energetic and radiant.

This means that as long as you have a "master of taste", you can theoretically say goodbye to all expensive spiritual foods and obtain a top-notch enjoyment with the most basic ingredients, and it is an enjoyment that is 100% tailored to your personal preferences!

This is a fatal temptation for the vast majority of low-level cultivators and ordinary people.

At this moment, Lin Qi changed the subject, his tone becoming more serious: "In the future, we at Hippie will also expand our community communication functions!"

"By connecting the [Taste Master] with the [Spirit Network], we will build a brand new shared taste community!"

"Users can share their own taste settings and experience other people's food experiences! We will break down the barriers of taste and achieve true food harmony!"

The vision was painted so beautifully, as if a utopian future was right before our eyes.

However, Wang Ling, who was sitting in the classroom, could clearly see the monstrous trap hidden beneath that beautiful vision through the screen.

The so-called spiritual network has the same core energy fluctuations as the evil thoughts that were destroyed yesterday.

It's just that the disguise is more sophisticated, connecting to the user through that small wrist-worn device.

It doesn't directly control the mind, but rather makes users unconsciously become dependent on the device through infinitely amplified and distorted "appetite" while enjoying the ultimate taste experience, thus making them dependent on the "spiritual network".

Ultimately, the user's emotions and even thoughts will be subtly guided by the "spirit network".

The so-called "shared taste community" is actually a huge cage.

It connects everyone's desires, influencing and amplifying each other, ultimately forming an irresistible torrent of collective consciousness.

"Is this what it means to exploit appetite itself..." Wang Ling recalled what he had heard yesterday.

Compared to adding trace additives to food, this method of directly affecting the senses and desires is more direct, more covert, and more terrifying.

It's not contaminating food, but rather "hijacking" everyone's sense of taste and desire.

Wang Ling could even foresee that once this equipment became widespread, the very foundation of society would be shaken.

Cultivation will no longer be about arduous practice in pursuit of the Great Dao, but rather about taking a shortcut through dietary cultivation, achieving improvement through desire.
