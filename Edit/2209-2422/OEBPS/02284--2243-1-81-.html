Chapter 2243 The Immortal King's Wilderness Survival

The noise and chaos were completely shut out behind the distorted spatial passage.

Absolute silence reigned all around, with only warm air currents carrying a thick layer of sulfur and primal spiritual energy, silently surging in the darkness.

Wang Ling brushed away non-existent dust from his school uniform, his gaze calmly sweeping across this ancient, dark world.

The strange minerals on the rock face emitted a dappled glow in cool tones.

The air was filled with a repressed yet primal scent.

He looked down at his feet.

The unknown spirit beast that had torn through space in the passage and dragged their team of elite representatives from multiple cultivation academies into the abyss of destruction was now kneeling before him in an extremely comical and humble posture.

Its enormous body trembled like sieve chaff due to the fear that penetrated deep into its soul. A dozen or so crystal bone spikes of various shapes clung tightly to its body, trying to shrink its size to the minimum.

The only exposed, enormous scarlet eye was now filled with fear and confusion, staring intently at the direction of Wang Ling's shoe tip, not daring to deviate even slightly.

It was as if looking at it even once more would lead to utter annihilation.

Wang Ling's face remained expressionless.

Buzz!

A mental pressure far clearer than the invisible gaze within the passage, like an absolute command from the abyss, silently seeped into the core of the beast's consciousness.

"Ugh...!?" The unknown spirit beast let out an extremely suppressed wail, its huge body instantly stiffening as if bound by invisible chains, even its trembling was forcibly suppressed.

Its soul was as if it had been thrown into an endless ice hell.

At that moment, Wang Ling's consciousness directly sensed a resistance and... concealment originating from the depths of the giant beast's soul.

This is a deliberate silence.

It is attempting to shut down a certain form of communication.

Wang Ling's eyes flickered slightly, and his fingertips lifted silently.

A more precise and unquestionable mental command bombarded the behemoth's consciousness: "Play deaf and dumb, die!"

The colossal beast's massive body shuddered violently! The fear in its crimson eye was instantly replaced by a deeper despair—Damn it! It's been seen through!

It resignedly released its mental barrier, and a rough, fearful voice, filled with endless flattery, rang out in Wang Ling's mind, like sand rubbing together: "Immortal, please spare me, I was wrong!"

Wang Lingping's calm gaze swept over the enormous body of the beast, then glanced at the relatively flat, open platform beneath it, which had been smashed open and had a huge, inverted crystal dome hanging above it.

The location is secluded, the space is large, the temperature is comfortable, and there is an underground river nearby with flowing water.

This place is nice.

To avoid having to pretend to be low-key under the watchful eye of that old man Teng, along with the rest of the troops.

"This place is mine." Wang Ling's thoughts were clear and concise.

"Yes! Yes! As you command!" The unknown spirit beast felt as if it had been granted a pardon. It quickly and clumsily got up, trying to curl up its huge body so as not to take up too much space.

Wang Ling ignored its fear and silently made plans in his mind.

Project Center of the Earth?

explore?

What does this have to do with him?

Their first task upon venturing into the Earth's core was to use the Earth's intelligent map to record all the spiritual plants, minerals, and spiritual beasts they encountered in the inner world, and then upload them to a large database for comparison.

It is said that the inner world contains rare spiritual plants that have disappeared from the surface, as well as mineral veins that can be sustainably mined.

Even materials like superstar spirit beasts and materials that regenerate horns are used to craft high-grade immortal artifacts and armor.

Wang Lingya had absolutely no interest in the mission.

He just wanted a peaceful daily life.

Forced to come here, rather than getting involved in those pointless tasks and surveillance, it's better to stay here and wait and see.

And the instant noodles are almost gone...

It's clean here.

Suitable for...to stay alive.

If we can build it directly into a blessed land, it would be nice to wait until the mission is over.

Moreover, he had "accidentally" destroyed the base's core monitoring system, which made things easier for him.

The unknown spirit beast peeked at this mysterious and terrifying being, who stood there, his eyes vacant, seemingly lost in thought.

Then, a terrifying scene that it would never forget happened.

Wang Ling didn't even raise his hand or make any hand gestures.

He simply stood there, his eyes slightly narrowed.

Buzz—!

An indescribable power instantly spread out from him, enveloping the huge underground platform and part of the adjacent cave!

The dark red rock beneath my feet softened, flowed, and reassembled silently!

Like the hand of God sculpting clay!

A flat alloy floor spread out, its precision far exceeding what the gravel crushers could have imagined in human creation!

The rocks in the cave walls have been squeezed and shaped inwards, making them as smooth as a mirror!

The walls grow out of the solid rock, creating independent room structures!

Beneath the enormous, inverted crystal dome, a bright hall has been carved out, where the cold light of the crystal is softened by some force, becoming warm and comfortable!

Even more terrifying, some scattered, strange metallic minerals seemed to have been given life, extracted from deep within the rock walls, melted and cast in the void, and then shaped!

Water vapor gathers and rises from the underground river, condensing into clear, clean water pipes that seamlessly connect to the rooms.

A strange force distorted space, causing some strangely glowing moss growing in the corner to grow faster, evenly covering the base walls and emitting soft natural lighting...

It even formed an independent warehouse protected by a small array.

The whole process was silent and swift, so fast it was almost unreal!

In just a dozen breaths, the vast underground space transformed from a desolate primitive cave into a super comfortable secret base that blends a sense of technology with the charm of cultivation!

Its extravagance is enough to make any expensive man-made underground structure pale in comparison!

The unknown spirit beast stared wide-eyed, its enormous body trembling violently with fear.

This power to change the world with a wave of its hand... it felt as insignificant as a speck of dust!

Wang Ling let out a soft breath and looked around at his masterpiece.

It's alright.

At least for the short term, it's comfortable to live here.

Having a warehouse can save you some trouble.

As for this big guy...

His gaze fell on the unknown spirit beast that had once again slid to its knees after witnessing the miracle.

"From now on, your name will be Mountain Moving." Wang Ling gave it a simple and easy-to-understand label.

The boy's thoughts swept over some excess abandoned rocks and previously smashed energy ores in the corner of the base.

"Yes! Obey! Mountain Moving... Little Beast Mountain Moving! I am willing to serve you, Immortal, to the death!" Having been given a new name, Mountain Moving immediately realized his identity as a hired hand and dared not hesitate for a moment.

To work for such a being is an incredible opportunity that it could never hope to obtain!

Its massive body instantly became energetic, and it carefully used its huge crystal claws to lift a piece of discarded ore that was half a person tall, and tiptoed toward the designated "garbage" dumping area outside the base.

Its posture was clumsy yet earnest, like a large dog that had been called out and was overly excited.

This made Wang Ling suddenly miss Erha.

With this lively character around, this place will be much more vibrant.

In a rare moment of calm, Wang Ling finished building the base and didn't even think about his teammates. Before landing, he had already scanned everyone with his spiritual sense and found no one injured. However, he still gave the students from Number 60 High School special training, leaving them spiritual markers that would lead them to the base. Whether they could find it successfully was entirely up to fate.

Wang Ling ignored this newly recruited "laborer".

He walked to the warehouse and his eyes fell on the stored snacks.

Hmm, with the new base completed, shouldn't we grab a pack of instant noodles to celebrate?

The moment he reached out his hand—a faint, cold, and filthy sensory fluctuation, like an ink droplet thrown into calm water, was captured by his powerful divine sense.

This fluctuation, carrying an extremely subtle aura of the underworld, silently attached itself to an inconspicuous patch of scales on the edge of the massive body of the "Mountain Mover," who was diligently transporting ore.

Wang Ling paused slightly as he reached for the snacks. His thoughts extended.

The fluctuation was extremely faint and concealed; if he hadn't been in a position of absolute dominance and intentionally scanning his surroundings, it would have been almost impossible to detect.

It's like a miniature marker, or more like an... spy anchor.

Buzz! Wang Ling's divine sense silently penetrated the thick layers of rock, ignoring the barrier of distance, and remotely locked onto a direction—dozens of miles away, at the edge of a bottomless precipice in the earth's core.

There, on a bizarre platform made of huge, pale skeletons, a figure shrouded in a cloak was kneeling on one knee.

This person was surrounded by death and soul energy far exceeding Meng Nian's by ten or even a hundred times. He was the messenger from the underworld who sent the Mountain Moving Sect to test the cultivators on the surface!

Its core consciousness is attempting to perceive the situation here through some kind of underworld esoteric art connected to the markings.

However... when the messenger from the underworld touched the outer perimeter of the base that Wang Ling had just completed.

An incomprehensible barrier of power, as boundless as the abyss of the universe, instantly isolated it!
