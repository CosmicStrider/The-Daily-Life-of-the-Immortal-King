Chapter 2415 Useless Little Actions

At 7:30 a.m. on Tuesday, before the Songhai City Gymnasium opened, several people dressed in maintenance worker uniforms pushed a tool cart into the venue through a side door.

The leader took off his hat, revealing Mo Xuan's expressionless face.

"Quick movements."

He gave a low order, and his three men immediately dispersed. Two went to the monitoring room, while the other followed him toward the E-03 battle arena.

The stadium is empty at this moment.

Mo Xuan squatted down and placed his hands on the specially made floor.

The spiritual energy seeped in, sensing the internal rune structure.

E-03 was the most watched venue in yesterday's competition. The venue was specially reinforced last night, with the addition of three layers of cushioning arrays and a layer of protective shield generators.

These changes were originally intended to ensure the safety of the athletes, but they have now been exploited by Mo Xuan.

"The jammer is located near the control module of the protective shield."

Mo Xuan took out a fist-sized, silver-white device from the tool cart. Its surface was covered with densely packed runes: "Disguised as a heatsink, it will not be detected during normal inspections."

The subordinate nodded, took the device, skillfully pried open the access panel in the floor, and crawled inside.

"Mr. Mo, it's installed."

A moment later, his subordinate crawled out from below and covered the access panel again: "Four interference devices are installed at the four spiritual energy nodes of the protective shield. They will activate simultaneously when the competition is about fifteen minutes in, causing local spiritual energy disturbances."

"Duration?"

"Three seconds," the subordinate said. "A brief period of disruption won't trigger a system alarm, but it's enough to cause the shield to malfunction temporarily. If there happens to be an aftershock of an attack at that moment..."

Mo Xuan nodded: "That's enough."

In three seconds, a surge of uncontrolled spiritual energy could fly towards the audience.

This is also sufficient to test Wang Ling's true reaction.

"Clean up the traces and leave."

The four men pushed the tool cart away from the venue, just like ordinary maintenance workers completing their routine inspection.

Five minutes after they left, the stadium's cleaning staff began to enter.

No one noticed that several things that shouldn't have been there had appeared under E-03.

...

At the same time, in the classroom of the elite class of Number 60 High School.

Chen Chao arrived exceptionally early; the classroom was empty except for him and Wang Ling.

Wang Ling sat in his seat, intently unpacking a new flavor of instant noodles.

"Lingzi, aren't you nervous?" Chen Chao came over and lowered his voice: "That Zhao Wuji, I heard he's been training for several days, his strength is definitely stronger than last time."

Wang Ling shook his head and tore open the packaging.

Click.

The crisp sound was particularly clear in the quiet classroom.

"Alright then." Chen Chao plopped down in the chair next to him. "Anyway, as long as you know what's going on. By the way, Guo Hao said yesterday that the lottery system might have been tampered with."

Wang Ling paused for a moment, then continued eating his noodles.

"You think there's something wrong too, right?" Chen Chao continued, "What a coincidence that you're facing Zhao Wuji in the quarterfinals. I suspect Luo Xinghe is behind this."

Wang Ling swallowed the instant noodles in his mouth and thought for a moment: "Maybe."

"Aren't you going to make some preparations?" Chen Chao said anxiously, "What if Zhao Wuji really gets enhanced, or Luo Xinghe uses dirty tricks during the competition..."

Without saying a word, Wang Ling directly handed Chen Chao a bag of classic flavored instant noodles.

"Eat," Wang Ling said.

Chen Chao: "..."

Looking at the instant noodles in his hand, he suddenly felt that his worries were a bit unnecessary.

...

9:00 AM, Songhai City Gymnasium Media Center.

Zhuo Yi, dressed casually, sat in front of the monitoring screen, holding a cup of coffee in his hand.

Ryoko Kumiya sat next to him, using a tablet to review the venue's surveillance footage from the previous night.

"At 4:27 a.m., four people entered the venue and left 45 minutes later." She pushed the tablet in front of Zhuo Yi: "They were disguised as maintenance workers, but their walking posture and movement habits were trained. They should be Luo Xinghe's people."

Zhuo Yi glanced at the screenshot on the screen and nodded: "As expected. Given Luo Xinghe's personality, he definitely won't let this go easily."

"Do we need to intervene?" Ryoko Kumiya asked.

"Not for now." Zhuo Yi took a sip of coffee. "Master should have already noticed. Besides, I want to see what Luo Xinghe is up to."

Ryoko Kumiya frowned: "What if he goes too far? There are so many students in the venue."

"With our master here, we won't get into trouble." Zhuo Yi smiled. "Besides, Senior Wang Zhen and Senior Liu Qingyi are also present. If something really happens, they can control the situation."

When Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi were mentioned, Ryoko Kugimiya's expression relaxed slightly.

She knew the strength of those two; they were absolutely reliable in crucial moments.

"Oh right, what about Chen Chao..." Ryoko Kugimiya suddenly remembered something: "He probably doesn't know the truth about Wang Ling yet, right?"

"I don't know." Zhuo Yi shook his head: "Besides, Master doesn't want him to know for the time being. Chen Chao is a loyal friend, but too naive. Knowing too much would only make him more vulnerable."

"That's true." Ryoko Kugimiya nodded. "Then let them continue to be the love advisors. Anyway, Rongrong and Wang Ling's progress definitely needs a push."

As the two were talking, the surveillance screen suddenly switched to a different image.

Below the E-03 battle stage, four red dots are slowly flashing.

"The jammer has been activated." Ryoko Kumiya narrowed her eyes. "So they wanted to break the protective shield. How despicable."

Zhuo Yi stared at the screen for a few seconds, then picked up the communicator: "Senior Brother Wang Zhen, there are four jammers on E-03, installed on the spiritual energy nodes of the protective shield. They are expected to activate about fifteen minutes into the match."

Wang Zhen's lazy voice came from the other end of the communicator: "Got it. Should I take it apart?"

"No need," Zhuo Yi said. "Let them start. I want to see how Master will handle it."

"Alright then." Wang Zhen yawned. "I'll watch the show from the audience. By the way, Qingyi said she'd like to sit in the VIP section, is that alright?"

"Okay, I'll arrange it."

Communication ended.

Ryoko Kugimiya looked at Zhuo Yi: "Aren't you worried?"

"What are you worried about?" Zhuo Yi retorted. "With Ao Hai around, Junior Sister Sun won't lose a single hair. As for the protective shield malfunctioning... do you think Master will just watch the aftershocks of the attack fly into the audience?"

Ryoko Kumiya paused for a moment, then smiled.

...

Noon, at the training hall of Ti-Hsiu High School.

With his upper body bare, Zhao Wuji punched the specially made force-measuring target.

Bang!

The target surface vibrated violently, and the numbers jumped wildly before finally stopping at "1897kg".

"Not enough." The white-haired old man standing beside him shook his head: "The third level of the Vajra Subduing Demon Body requires a punching force of at least two thousand kilograms. Let's try again."

Zhao Wuji was panting heavily, sweat dripping down his muscles.

He has been practicing all morning, from six o'clock until now, without stopping.

But that's not enough.

The thought of losing to Wang Ling last week still made him furious.

That loss was incredibly frustrating.

It wasn't that I was weaker or less skilled; it was that I was... being played.

Wang Ling's nonchalant attitude and the way he could easily neutralize his full-force attack made him feel like a joke.

"Grandpa," Zhao Wuji wiped his sweat, "that Wang Ling... is he really only at the early stage of Foundation Establishment?"

The white-haired old man, Zhao Tieshan, Zhao Wuji's grandfather, remained silent for a while.

"According to system testing, yes."

"But I don't believe it." Zhao Wuji gritted his teeth: "It's impossible for someone in the early stages of Foundation Establishment to have that kind of skill."
