Chapter 2280 Wang Muyu's Battle

Wuji Sword Sect, Lingpu in the back mountain.

Inside the crystal-clear sword cocoon laid by Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan.

Wang Muyu sat cross-legged on the bluestone, his face no longer showing the usual confusion and curiosity, but instead filled with an unprecedented solemnity.

The deafening shouts of battle, the booming sounds of psionic explosions, and the chilling aura of gray annihilation from the outside world crashed against the sword cocoon like a tide, creating ripples.

Although the sword intent protection remained stable, the pervasive withering power made Wang Muyu, who was naturally sensitive to energy, feel extremely uncomfortable.

He could hear the roars of the Zhan Sect members outside fighting desperately, and he could feel the frenzy of countless monsters being shredded by sword energy and then falling one after another.

And then there was that azure figure wielding a pale bone spear, eroding through the Sword Sect's barrier step by step—Jueqi.

Those vertical pupils, like molten gold, pierced through space, coldly locking onto him.

That wasn't the look of someone examining a child; it was the scrutiny of a precious object, a target that had to be captured.

"Bad guy..." Wang Muyu muttered under his breath, clenching his little fists.

He wasn't afraid, but rather angry that something of his was being coveted.

This is Master's place; it must not be destroyed!

Just then, the Endless Silence Spear in Jueqi's hand erupted with thick gray mist once again, piercing fiercely into the already dimmed sword intent barrier.

Sizzle—!

A tooth-grinding corrosive sound rang out, and the barrier shook violently, actually being eroded into a gap about one meter in diameter!

Although the gap began to slowly repair itself under the self-healing ability of the sword array, at that very moment, a gray shadow flashed in like a ghost!

It was exile!

He had no intention of completely destroying the barrier; he only needed a hole through which his main body could pass!

"Target confirmed. Execute capture command." Jueqi's emotionless voice echoed within the Spirit Garden, carrying a robotic quality.

The bone spear in his hand was pointed directly at Wang Muyu. The dark red liquid dripping from the spear tip landed on the spiritual plants, and those vibrant plants withered and carbonized instantly.

A terrifying realm of annihilation spread out from him, the starlight projections flowing within the spirit garden dimmed, and even the air became heavy and deathly still.

The sword intent left behind by the Sword Saint surged wildly toward Jueqi, attempting to drive him away or strangle him, but the gray, annihilating dragon breath surrounding him caused most of the sword intent to disintegrate and dissipate on its own when it got close!

Wang Muyu jumped down from the bluestone, his small body standing straight, staring back at Jueqi without fear.

He instinctively mobilized the power within his body, and a dragon's might far greater than his own size naturally emanated, counteracting the suffocating sense of annihilation.

His miniature swordsman's robes moved without wind, and faint, seven-colored dragon scale phantoms flashed beneath his skin.

"The Dragon Clan... the supreme bloodline..."

A barely perceptible glint of data flashed in Jueqi's vertical pupils, seemingly some kind of scanning analysis: "Immense potential, threat level assessment upgraded. Forced containment is recommended."

Without further hesitation, the Silenced Spear trembled slightly, and a gray spear light, like a venomous snake emerging from its hole, silently pierced towards Wang Muyu's shoulder, intending to restrict his movements first.

This seemingly ordinary shot contains the law of annihilation, which is enough to instantly render an ordinary Dao Ancestor Realm cultivator powerless to resist.

Wang Muyu's dragon bloodline surged instantly, and his fighting instincts were almost deeply ingrained in this bloodline.

Faced with the crisis, he subconsciously raised his small hand, imitating the action when the Sword Saint taught him, pointing his finger forward like a sword!

"Buzz—!"

The emerald green sword energy that had been gently surrounding him suddenly became violent and fierce, as if summoned by a king!

Thousands upon thousands of sword energies, as if receiving an absolute command, converged into a torrent and crashed fiercely into that gray spear light!

Boom!

The energies of emerald green and grayish brown collided violently, unleashing a tremendous shockwave that scraped away a thick layer of soil from the spirit garden!

The results were astonishing.

The torrent of sword energy that Wang Muyu hastily gathered actually withstood Jueqi's attack!

It must be known that the Zekes are currently the most powerful dragonborn known!

Although the emerald green sword energy was constantly corroded by the gray aura after contact, they kept coming one after another, their numbers astonishing, and they managed to exhaust the power of that spear light.

Jueqi paused slightly, seemingly somewhat surprised by the outcome.

"The energy intensity is beyond expectations. Phenomenological analysis... it contains various advanced dragon characteristics... interesting."

Wang Muyu's face turned slightly pale; that last strike had almost drained all the free sword intent around him.

He took a breath, and instead of backing down, his eyes brightened slightly as he successfully blocked an attack.

He hasn't actually cultivated much, and now Bai Zhe has sent the strongest dragon descendant to capture him.

Is that all there is to it?

...

Classroom at Number 60 High School, Songhai City.

Afternoon classes seemed exceptionally long.

Wang Lingyi remained gazing out the window, his fingertips lingering on the table for a long time.

His core consciousness had already transcended endless space, allowing him to see clearly everything that was happening in the Spirit Garden behind the Wuji Sword Sect.

The appearance of Jueqi, its unique annihilating dragon breath, and its "research-like" approach to capturing Wang Muyu made Wang Ling instantly realize that this was no ordinary evil cultivator or dragon.

This is a special weapon meticulously crafted by Bai Zhe, combining dragon genetic technology with the power of the Outer Gods.

Its purpose is probably not just to capture Wang Muyu, but may also involve a deeper mission: to analyze, replicate, and even strip away the part of Wang Muyu's original genes that originated from Wang Ling.

Wang Ling's lifeless eyes remained as still as an ancient well.

At this moment, centered on him, an invisible yet supreme "intention" has quietly spread out.

Like an infinitely large net, it gently covered the entire battlefield.

He couldn't just crush the insect; that would be too boring and wouldn't do anything to toughen up Wang Muyu.

But he could secretly help Wang Muyu...

At this moment, Wang Ling's gaze seemed to casually sweep over the old-fashioned person writing on the blackboard on the podium, and his right index finger twitched very slightly under the table.

...

Wuji Sword Sect, outside the battlefield.

Lord Diu Lei was frantically directing the Battle Sect team to fight against the seemingly endless tide of monsters, while anxiously watching Jue Jin break through the Sword Sect's barrier.

"Damn it! How can this turtle shell be so hard! All firepower! Concentrate your firepower on the area around that gap! Don't let any other trash sneak in!" Lord Thunderclap roared into the communication device.

However, the moment he gave the order, something unexpected happened.

The enormous flesh-and-blood mother nest that had been hidden in the void rift from which monsters were constantly emerging suddenly experienced a violent energy backflow at several key energy transmission nodes on its surface!

Thud! Thud! Thud!

With a few muffled thuds, the Flesh and Blood Mother Nest convulsed violently, and the speed at which it spewed monsters suddenly dropped by more than one level!

It's as if its "production line" suddenly jammed.

At the same time, several evil cultivators who were at the forefront and whose strength was comparable to that of an early-stage Dao Immortal were about to unleash a combined attack to bombard the Sword Sect's barrier.

The energy pathways circulating throughout their bodies inexplicably experienced a very slight deviation.

The deviation is so small that it can be ignored and will never cause problems under normal circumstances.

But at this moment, it was precisely this slight difference that caused their massive evil energy to not only fail to be unleashed, but instead to ignite a fierce conflict between them!

Boom!

A violent energy explosion erupted at the heart of the monster horde, instantly clearing out a large area.

Those unfortunate evil cultivator leaders were the first to be hit, blasted to pieces and seriously injured.

"??? What happened?!" A leader of the evil cultivators roared, spitting blood, completely bewildered as to why the circulation of spiritual energy had gone wrong.

Lord Thunderclap was also taken aback for a moment, but he reacted extremely quickly: "Good opportunity! Strike while the iron is hot! Seize the moment and kill him!"

Zhan Zong's pressure suddenly eased.
