Chapter 2407 Brothers and Plans

The smoky aroma from the barbecue restaurant, mixed with the fragrance of lamb skewers, lingered around Chen Chao's nose.

Guo Hao's words were like a wake-up call, echoing in Chen Chao's ears several times, and the knot in his heart was slowly smoothed out.

"You're right."

Chen Chao downed the rest of the fruit wine in his glass in one gulp: "I was overthinking it. Wang Ling is Wang Ling. No matter how powerful he is, he's still my brother."

Li Changzhe chuckled, grabbed a handful of skewers and stuffed them into his mouth: "That's more like it! Come on, let's celebrate our brothers working together to take down that scoundrel Luo Xinghe!"

Guo Hao wasn't as excited as him. He pushed up his glasses, picked up the jade slip, and looked at it carefully again.

"The competition will be held at 2 PM three days from now at the Songhai City Gymnasium." He pointed to the information that appeared on the jade slip: "The registration deadline... is noon tomorrow. You can withdraw before then. Luo Xinghe has left himself a backup plan. If we change our minds, we still have time to withdraw from the competition."

Chen Chao's eyes lit up: "Then why don't we just withdraw from the competition?"

"No way." Guo Hao shook his head: "Have you forgotten what Luo Xinghe said? Withdrawing from the competition will affect the school's rating. And I suspect that even if we withdraw, he will still find a way to get Wang Ling into the competition. Rather than passively responding, we should take the initiative to plan ahead."

He paused for a moment, then continued, "My plan has three steps. First, Brother Chao, you contact Luo Xinghe tomorrow, pretend to agree to cooperate, but raise the price. It's not 500,000, it's 1 million spirit stones."

"A million?!" Li Changzhe almost choked on his food: "Would he really give it to me?"

"Yes." Guo Hao was very certain: "For someone who can mobilize infant-level assassins, a million spirit stones is nothing. Moreover, the more he values this plan, the more he is willing to invest."

Chen Chao frowned: "But what would I do with so many spirit stones? It feels like I'm selling out my brothers..."

"This isn't betraying a brother, it's a tactic." Guo Hao looked at him seriously: "First, once we get the money, we can donate it to the Yanran Angel Children's Hospital. Second, by asking for a high price, Luo Xinghe will be more convinced that you're genuinely greedy and will cooperate with him. Third..."

A sly glint flashed in his eyes: "If things are exposed, you can say that you were only pretending to cooperate in order to swindle Luo Xinghe's money, and that you were actually protecting Wang Ling. That would make perfect sense."

Chen Chao was stunned for a moment, then couldn't help but give him a thumbs up: "Guo Hao, it's a pity you're not a strategist with a brain like that."

Guo Hao smiled and continued, "The second step, after obtaining the spirit stones, is to start preparing the black system. I need a day to research what model of the scanning system used in the competition is, and then get the license from my uncle. This step is the riskiest, but it must be done."

"Can your uncle really handle it?" Li Changzhe was a little worried.

"One of my uncles works at the Psionic Technology Bureau in Songhai City, and he is in charge of these competition systems."

Guo Hao said, "However, I can't tell him the truth directly. I have to make up a reason... I'll say that our school wants to test the system's stability in advance and do a research project."

Chen Chao nodded: "That's a plausible reason."

"The third step, on the day of the competition." Guo Hao lowered his voice: "We need someone to meet me near the system control room. Chao'er, you're responsible for keeping Luo Xinghe calm and making him think that everything is going according to plan. Li Changzhe, you keep watch in the stands. If you find anything unusual, send me a signal immediately."

The three of them discussed the details for another half hour, listing out all the possible problems and devising solutions.

It was past nine o'clock in the evening when we came out of the barbecue restaurant.

The night view of Songhai City is bustling, with neon lights illuminating the streets. Chen Chao was walking home, his mind still racing.

He recalled his father's words, Wang Ling's always calm face, and the time they spent skipping classes, playing games, and bragging together on the playground over the past two years.

"Who cares?" Chen Chao muttered to himself. "Brothers are brothers, why think so much?"

He took out his phone and sent a message to Luo Xinghe, using the words Guo Hao had taught him.

[Chen Chao: Mr. Luo, I've considered the deal you proposed. I can cooperate, but it requires one million spirit stones. Half will be paid as a deposit upfront. The other half will be paid on the day of the match after Wang Ling takes the stage.]

The reply came in less than three minutes after the message was sent.

[Luo Xinghe: Okay. Tomorrow at 3 PM, the third bench in Central Park, Songhai City. Cash transaction.]

Chen Chao looked at the screen and clicked his tongue.

They really are very wealthy and powerful.

...

The next day was Monday, and classes were held at Number 60 High School as usual.

When Chen Chao entered the classroom with dark circles under his eyes, Wang Ling was already sitting in his seat eating instant noodles.

He was still in that familiar posture, head down, intently tearing open the packaging, breaking the bread into small pieces, and putting them into his mouth little by little.

Sunlight streamed through the window and fell on him, his school uniform collar slightly open, revealing his clean collarbone.

"Good morning, Lingzi!" Chen Chao plopped down next to him, his voice booming.

Wang Ling looked up at him, nodded, and took out another package from the drawer, handing it over.

"Barbecue flavor, newly released."

Chen Chao took it, tore it open, and started eating, saying indistinctly, "Thanks. By the way, Lingzi, have you heard about that new generation cultivator exchange competition?"

Wang Ling paused for a moment, then continued eating his noodles: "Mmm."

"It's organized by the city, so it's pretty legitimate." Chen Chao observed his expression: "I heard the prizes are pretty good, with the first prize being 100,000 spirit stones and a third-grade spirit weapon."

Wang Ling responded with an "oh" and then didn't react further.

At this moment, Sun Rong came in through the front door, carrying two cartons of milk. She walked naturally to Wang Ling's table and put one carton down.

"Wang Ling, this is for you."

"Thank you." Wang Ling took it and glanced at Chen Chao.

Chen Chao quickly waved his hand: "No need, no need, I brought my own water."

Sun Rong blushed slightly and sat down in her seat.

As Chen Chao watched this scene, the gloom in his heart caused by Luo Xinghe dissipated considerably.

...

The bell rang, and the homeroom teacher, Mr. Pan, walked in carrying his lesson plan.

"Classmates, there's an announcement." Old Pan pushed up his glasses: "The city is going to hold a new generation of cultivators exchange competition, and each school can register five contestants. Although our Number 60 High School is not a key cultivation high school, we can't fall behind. Interested students can register here."

A commotion broke out in the classroom.

"One hundred thousand spirit stones! A grade three spiritual weapon!"

Wow, this reward is really generous!

"But can we beat those top high schools? They have students at the Golden Core stage..."

Old Pan tapped the blackboard: "Quiet! The competition is divided into high school and university groups. We'll register for the high school group. The cultivation level requirement is Foundation Establishment to early Golden Core stage. Anyone higher than that can't participate."

Chen Chao's heart tightened.

From the Foundation Establishment stage to the early Golden Core stage... If Wang Ling is truly at the Foundation Establishment stage, then he meets the requirements.

But what if his cultivation level is even higher?

He glanced at Wang Ling furtively; the latter was looking down, drawing something in the blank space of his textbook.

Upon closer inspection, it turned out to be a sketch of a crispy bread bag, with a note next to it stating "Suggested to increase spiciness by 30%".

Chen Chao: "..."

This person really has no interest in cultivation.

As soon as the bell rang and Lao Pan stepped out of the classroom, several boys gathered around the podium to ask about registration.

Chen Chao seized the opportunity to move closer to Wang Ling: "Lingzi, do you want to sign up?"

Wang Ling thought for a moment and shook his head.

"Why? The prizes are pretty generous," Chen Chao asked tentatively.

"Thank you for your help," Wang Ling said succinctly.

Chen Chao breathed a sigh of relief, but immediately became nervous again.

Wang Ling didn't sign up voluntarily, but Luo Xinghe had already registered him. When the school notifies him, he'll have to go whether he wants to or not.
