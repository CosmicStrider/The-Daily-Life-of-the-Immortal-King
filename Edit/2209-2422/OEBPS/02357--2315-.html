Chapter 2315 The Rules of Victory

The Azure Netherworld, deep within the Lost Forest.

With the help of Sun Rong's guidance, the 60th squad struggled through the illusionary woodland.

The ancient trees around seemed to possess life, their branches moving silently and subtly altering the path.

The ever-present thick fog seemed to want to trap intruders in this labyrinth forever.

"This place is even more eerie than the inner forest of the Earth."

Chen Chao punched away the creepy vines that were winding around him and complained.

His Golden Core stage spiritual energy was being consumed extremely quickly here, and the omnipresent mental interference around him was causing him great distress.

"Concentrate, Chao'er."

Guo Hao reminded him, "This forest amplifies inner anxiety. Li Jun, your 'Lingtai Qingming Mantra' is the most effective; please take care of everyone."

Upon hearing this, Li Jun immediately began to recite it in a low voice.

A soft, clear light emanated from him, enveloping the five members of the team.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao immediately felt their mental clutter and anxiety subside, and their spiritual energy flowed more smoothly.

Wang Ling glanced sideways, which could be interpreted as a slight acknowledgment of the spell's actual effect.

"The ripples in Mingxin Lake are becoming clearer. Stay close to me and don't fall behind." Sun Rong held the Ao Hai sword, its tip radiating azure light, pointing in a certain direction ahead.

Her voice was clear and reassuring.

As a special spirit sword reforged with materials from the Heavenly Cube and Wang Ling, this level of environmental interference has negligible impact on Ao Hai.

Just as the team was about to pass through a particularly dense wall of fog.

Sun Rong suddenly stopped in her tracks.

A buzzing sound!

O'Hay gave a wary, soft snort.

"There's a situation," she whispered, her eyes sharply fixed on the left front.

A cloud of mist billowed up, and several disheveled figures stumbled out.

It's a team from "Fenglei Cultivation High School".

Their clothes were torn and they bore wounds; their faces showed signs of shock.

Fenglei High School is not considered a particularly good cultivation high school; its ranking is even much lower than that of the current No. 60 High School.

Participation in this competition was solely at the request of the school leadership.

The Lingtai Qingming Mantra is a huge pie, and obtaining ownership rights means a considerable amount of sustainable future income, so many people want a piece of the pie.

"A fellow Daoist from Number 60 High School!"

Upon seeing Sun Rong and the others, the captain of Fenglei High School quickly stated his position: "I absolutely support Li Jun! But we have no choice but to participate!"

The team leader, sensing the aura emanating from Li Jun, grasped at it like a lifeline: "Please, everyone! Help us..."

Before he could finish speaking, the fog wall was suddenly torn open, and three strangely shaped creatures pounced out.

They resemble wild wolves, yet their entire bodies are composed of distorted shadows and broken mirrors.

At the same time, it was extremely fast, and with each swing of its claws came a piercing scream that tore through one's mind.

"It's a Heart-Sickness Beast! Be careful, their attacks target the soul directly!" Guo Hao's expression changed as he quickly made the judgment.

Chen Chao roared, unleashing his golden core spiritual power. The scorching fist wind blasted towards one of them like a cannonball.

However, the demonic beast's physical body instantly vanished, and the force of the fist pierced through it, only blurring its form for a moment before it pounced even more fiercely.

"Physical attacks have failed!" Chen Chao hurriedly retreated.

Sun Rong's eyes narrowed, and the body of the Ao Hai Sword gleamed.

She did not unleash any earth-shattering sword techniques; she simply made a seemingly casual forward slash.

A sword aura, as radiant as moonlight, rippled outwards, carrying a sword intent that could purify all evil.

As the sword energy swept past, the three Heart Nightmare Beasts let out silent howls, their bodies rapidly dissolving.

The students from Fenglei High School were dumbfounded.

The monster they had been fighting so hard to defeat was so easily defeated by Sun Rong?

"Sun...Sun Rong, thank you for your help!" Captain Feng Lei only recognized Sun Rong and Li Jun, and quickly expressed his gratitude. His eyes were filled with thanks.

Sun Rong nodded slightly and put away the Ao Hai: "It was nothing. What happened to you? How did you end up being chased so badly by these monsters?"

Captain Feng Lei explained with lingering fear, "We followed the map to Mingxin Lake and found an abandoned altar along the way. There was an ancient jade in the center of the altar that could glow. We tried to get closer to investigate, but that triggered these ghostly things, and their numbers kept increasing!"

"Ancient jade?" Sun Rong's heart skipped a beat, and she exchanged a glance with Guo Hao.

Guo Hao pushed up his glasses and analyzed, "My uncle gave me some extra lessons before I entered the secret realm. He said that the Azure Netherworld Secret Realm is a testing ground for ancient powerful beings to temper their minds, and its core is controlled by the spirit of the secret realm. According to the rules announced by the Hundred Schools Administration, the final winner is the one who controls the core of the secret realm. That ancient jade is very likely related to the spirit of the secret realm, or a clue leading to the core."

"That's right!" Captain Feng Lei nodded quickly. "We suspect that's one of the key items, but the Heart Beast guarding it is too powerful; we're no match for it. And..."

He paused, a hint of indignation on his face: "When we retreated, we thought we saw people from Qingyun High School watching us from not far away, but they didn't make a move at all. Instead, they took the opportunity to head towards the altar!"

The members of the 60th squad immediately understood.

Given Qingyun High School's understanding of the secret realm, there must be a master guiding them behind the scenes.

Thank you for letting me know.

Sun Rong asked Captain Feng Lei, "How are your injuries? Do you need to come along?"

Captain Feng Lei shook his head with a wry smile: "We've exhausted ourselves, so we're planning to find a safe place to rest and not be a burden to you. You'll definitely make it to the end and prove Li Jun right again. Just be careful of those guys from Qingyun High School!"

After thanking him again, he hurriedly left with his team.

"It seems Zhao Qian's goal is very clear: to find and control the core of the secret realm first." Chen Chao rubbed his hands together, eager to get started.

"It's not that simple." Guo Hao pondered, "If the spirit of the secret realm were so easy to subdue, it wouldn't be a decisive factor. Besides, Zhao Qian and the others definitely have something to rely on."

Li Jun looked at Sun Rong with concern: "Sun Rong, what should we do?"

Sun Rong's gaze swept over her teammates, finally landing on Wang Ling, who had been silent all along.

Wang Lingyi maintained the same expression, but Sun Rong saw a hint of "do whatever you want" in his eyes.

She took a deep breath, her mind made up: "Now that we have the clues, we can't let Zhao Qian succeed. We're going to that altar! We not only need to get the ancient jade, but also find out what Qingyun High School is up to. We must be careful, and the risks of the secret realm itself should not be underestimated."

She secretly channeled the power of the Ao Hai Sea, gaining a clearer understanding of the coordinates of Mingxin Lake and that altar.

Finally, the girl looked firmly in one direction and said, "Follow me, we'll take a shortcut."

Outside the secret realm, observe the scene.

A huge circular light screen floated in mid-air, divided into dozens of images, displaying the movements of various teams in the secret realm in real time.

Teachers from various schools, officials from the Hundred Schools Administration, and "special observers" like Wang Muyu, who are students from various schools, gathered here to pay attention to the progress of the trial.

Of course, many more celebrities from the cultivation world did not arrive at the scene, but watched the competition via live stream.

Within the Wuji Sword Sect, when Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan saw Wang Muyu's figure on the live broadcast, he immediately spat out a mouthful of tea.

He guessed that Xiao Muyu might have been delayed by other matters because he hadn't returned to the sect, but in the end, he became a member of the Sixty-One Sect.

At that moment, when the screen displayed the scene of Sun Rong purifying the Heart Nightmare Beast with a single sword strike, a gasp of amazement rang out from the audience.

"Tsk tsk, the young lady of the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group truly lives up to her reputation!"

"To possess such sword intent and purification power at the Golden Core stage, the Sun family girl is truly remarkable!"
