Chapter 2357 Wang Ling and Sun Rong's Synchronization Rate

Songhai City, Starry Sky Esports Center, early morning.

A slightly noisy psionic broadcast suddenly rang out.

"All exchange camp participants, please arrive at Building A, Room 3 before 8:30 a.m. to participate in the opening ceremony and icebreaker activities. Repeat, all participants, before 8:30 a.m., at Building A, Room 3."

Wang Ling lay on the bed in room 709, staring at the ceiling.

The sound of the broadcast came through the array inside the room clearly.

Icebreaking activities...

He remembered Sun Rong mentioning it last night.

trouble.

Thinking this to himself, Wang Ling slowly sat up, picked up the last pack of spicy crab-flavored instant noodles from the bedside table, and tore it open.

After eating, I washed up and changed into the Number 60 High School uniform that seemed to be welded onto my body.

As I was leaving, the door to room 708 next door opened at the same time.

Sun Rong came out.

Today she changed into a light pink hooded sweatshirt, paired with a white short skirt and knee-high socks. Her hair was tied into a neat high ponytail, with a few wisps of hair falling naturally in front of her forehead. She looked full of energy.

"Good morning, Wang Ling," Sun Rong greeted him with a smile, her cheeks slightly flushed.

"Good morning." Wang Ling nodded, his gaze lingering on her for less than half a second before turning towards the end of the corridor.

The two walked side by side toward the elevator.

They were the only ones in the elevator.

The enclosed space made Sun Rong a little nervous. She could smell the scent of sun-dried cotton on Wang Ling, as well as a faint but familiar aroma of pepper...

"Um... I wonder what the icebreaker game is today?" Sun Rong said, trying to make conversation.

"Um."

"I hope it won't be too difficult..."

"Um."

The conversation was extremely difficult.

Sun Rong sighed inwardly.

When will this piece of wood finally start saying something new?

The elevator arrives at the third floor of Building A.

As soon as the door opened, a cacophony of noise rushed in.

The No. 3 multi-purpose hall was already crowded with people. Looking around, there were boys and girls wearing various school uniforms or team uniforms. At a glance, there were at least fifty or sixty people.

The members of the Number 60 High School team gathered in a corner. Chen Chao was gesturing animatedly to Li Changzhe, while Guo Hao listened with his arms crossed. Wang Muyu stood on tiptoe, curiously looking around.

Upon seeing Wang Ling and Sun Rong enter, Chen Chao immediately waved to them with winks and exaggerated expressions.

"Lingzi! Sun Rong! Over here!"

The two walked over.

"Did you see that? Did you see that?" Chen Chao lowered his voice and gestured with his eyes to the small stage that had been temporarily set up in front of the hall: "Brother Zhuo and the others are over there."

Sun Rong looked in the direction he pointed.

Sure enough, Zhuo Yi was dressed in a sharp casual suit and talking with several people in staff uniforms.

Ryoko Kugimiya stood to his side and slightly behind, still holding the folding fan in her hand, her eyes casually scanning the entire room as if she were assessing something.

Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi stood in a corner a little further away, both with smiles on their faces as they watched the show.

The Love Strategist Alliance is in full force.

Sun Rong's heart began to beat faster.

At this moment, Zhuo Yi walked to the center of the stage and picked up a sonic amplification device.

"Class, please be quiet."

His voice resounded throughout the entire hall through the array.

The noise quickly subsided, and everyone's eyes were focused on him.

"Welcome to the first Starry Sky Esports Center Esports Rising Star Exchange Camp." Zhuo Yi wore a perfectly appropriate official smile. "I am Zhuo Yi, Director of the Hundred Schools Administration, and also the person in charge of this exchange camp. Over the next three days, we will train together here. There is only one goal: to improve our skills and prepare for the city-level selection competition."

He paused, his gaze sweeping across the audience: "But before we dive into the intense training, we need to break down barriers and get to know each other better. So, the first activity this morning is an icebreaker game."

A slight commotion arose from the audience, and most people felt somewhat embarrassed.

After all, most of the people who come here are e-sports enthusiasts, who are instinctively somewhat resistant to "social games".

"The rules of the game are very simple."

Zhuo Yi raised his hand, signaling the staff to bring up two uniquely shaped pieces of equipment.

They were two circular platforms, each about one meter in diameter, covered with a smooth, responsive material, with a ring of pale blue psionic light slowly flowing around the edge.

Above the platform, a semi-transparent holographic screen is suspended.

"This is the synchronization rate testing platform."

Zhuo Yi explained, "The game is called: Mirror Challenge. Two people form a team and stand on two platforms respectively."

"Once the game starts, random actions will be displayed on the main platform screen. These could be simple actions like raising a hand or lifting a leg, or they could be continuous body movements. Team members standing on the two secondary platforms need to pass on these action messages and mimic the actions of the main platform as synchronously as possible in the shortest amount of time."

"The system will score in real time based on the synchronization rate and delay time of the movements. The maximum score is 100 points. The final score is the average score of the three rounds of challenges."

The rules are indeed simple. In short, the system gives the instructions, one person follows the system, and the other learns from their teammate. The goal is to see who has the highest synchronization rate.

But some people in the audience had already started to murmur.

"What kind of icebreaking is this?"

"It also involves physical gestures... I'm a little embarrassed."

"How are the groups divided? Is it random?"

Zhuo Yi seemed to have heard these questions and smiled: "The grouping method will be randomly selected by the system. Considering the tacit understanding within each team, we will try our best to separate teammates to promote cross-school exchanges."

As soon as he finished speaking, a huge light screen was projected into the air above the hall, displaying the names and images of all the team members at high speed.

"Now, begin random matchmaking!"

The names on the screen jumped wildly, then slowly stopped after a few seconds.

The first set of matching results is displayed:

[Main Character: Liu Kaichuan, Akahira High School]

[Second Place: Lin Weiwei, Titan High School]

Liu Kaichuan paused for a moment, then flashed what he thought was a cool smile and walked toward a quiet-looking female team member from Titan High School who wore glasses.

The girl named Lin Weiwei seemed a little nervous. She pushed up her glasses and whispered, "Nice to meet you."

Display matching groups.

Chen Chao was matched with a chubby guy from the St. Science Academy. The two looked at each other and laughed.

Guo Hao's partner is Jiu Dao, a short-haired girl who is new to high school and has a serious expression.

Soon, Sun Rong's name popped up.

[Main Seat: Sun Rong, Number 60 High School]

The names for the secondary position were still scrolling, finally settling on a single one...

[Secondary Position: Wang Ling, Number 60 High School]

"Wow!" Chen Chao immediately exclaimed, "What a coincidence, Lingzi! A match made in heaven!"

A soft, friendly laugh rippled through the crowd.

Sun Rong's face flushed red, and she secretly glanced at Wang Ling.

Wang Ling's face remained expressionless; he merely glanced at the light screen, then at the platform on the stage, and frowned slightly.

On stage, Zhuo Yi smirked mischievously.

Random matching?

Oh.

He personally adjusted the matching algorithm last night. The core logic is simple: ensure that Wang Ling and Sun Rong are in the same group, and that Sun Rong is the primary player.

The first phase of the Love Strategist Alliance: Creating the Right Environment, has officially begun!

"Alright, grouping complete." Zhuo Yi clapped his hands. "Then, let's start with the first group. Liu Kaichuan and Lin Weiwei, please come up to the stage."

The game officially begins.

Liu Kaichuan clearly wanted to show off in front of the new girl he met, and he was eager to perform after stepping onto the main platform.

However, when the system activated, he was dumbfounded.

The first movement is very simple: put your left hand on your hip, point your right hand to the sky, and at the same time lift your left leg back.

Liu Kaichuan did as instructed.

Lin Weiwei, sitting in the audience, hurriedly imitated the movements Liu Kaichuan passed on to her.

But she was clearly nervous; she raised her right hand too slowly and her left leg was raised at the wrong angle.

Synchronization rate: 71%

[Delay: 0.8 seconds]

[First Round Score: 68]

Liu Kaichuan: "..."

Lin Weiwei blushed and lowered her head.

A suppressed laugh came from the audience.

Liu Kaichuan was mortified.

The second move was even more outrageous: it was actually a ballet segment similar to Swan Lake, requiring one to stand on one leg, lift the other leg back, and make an elegant curve with the arms.

"What kind of crazy move is this?!" Liu Kaichuan cursed inwardly, but under the watchful eyes of everyone, he could only grit his teeth and go for it.

The result was predictable.

He couldn't stand steadily on one leg, swayed a few times, and his arm movements became completely distorted.

Lin Weiwei had already given up on treatment and made some random gestures.

Synchronization rate: 29%

[Delay: 2.3 seconds]

[Third Round Score: 15]

Liu Kaichuan stepped down from the stage looking utterly humiliated, all his previous attempts to show off completely gone.

Lin Weiwei was on the verge of tears.

Zhuo Yi commented at the opportune moment: "Good, a good start. Remember, this game tests not only physical coordination, but also observation, reaction speed, and teamwork. Next group, Chen Chao..."

The games continued in groups.

Some people scored high, some scored low, resulting in many laugh-out-loud moments.
