Chapter 2393 A Silent Resolution

The performance on stage reached its climax.

The volcano erupted, lava shot into the sky, and the fiery phoenix struggled and screamed in the flames before finally turning to ashes.

The lights in the entire venue were dimmed to the extreme, with only a faint spark flickering in the center of the stage.

The music became somber and melancholic, and the audience fell silent, holding their breath as they awaited the moment of the phoenix's rebirth.

Right now!

Fu San's eyes flashed, and he suddenly drew out a smoke talisman with his right hand!

But just as she was about to erupt—

The thin line on my ankle suddenly tightened!

"Hmm?" Fu San's body stiffened, feeling a strange force surge up from the soles of her feet, instantly sealing off the spiritual energy flow in half of her body!

The talisman slipped from her fingers and fell to the ground.

At the same time, Lock 4 also encountered the same situation.

Just as he was about to activate the binding artifact, he discovered that his spiritual power was frozen and blocked!

"What's going on?!" Suo Si's expression changed drastically.

The two looked at each other and saw the shock in each other's eyes.

I've been infected!

When? Who did it?

Such a method of sealing spiritual power invisibly can only be done by cultivators at least in the Nascent Soul stage! And to do it so secretly, without even them, who are in the late Golden Core stage, what level must the person who made this move be?

Both Fu San and Suo Si broke out in a cold sweat.

At that moment, the spark on the stage suddenly burst into a dazzling light!

"唳——!"

The clear, melodious cry of the phoenix resounded throughout the theater!

A magnificent golden phoenix soared into the sky from the ashes, its outstretched wings almost covering the entire stage!

The audience erupted in enthusiastic applause and cheers!

The lights blazed on, and the music became grand and magnificent.

Reborn from the ashes, presented in perfect form.

Sun Rong clapped enthusiastically, "That was amazing! Wang Ling, did you see that? That moment of rebirth!"

Wang Ling nodded: "Mm."

He actually felt that the special effects could be made even more realistic. For example, the temperature gradient of the flames could be made more delicate, and the light and shadow refraction of the phoenix feathers could be optimized.

However, seeing how happy Sun Rong was, he didn't say anything.

As the performance drew to a close, the phoenix circled in the air a few times before transforming into countless points of light that scattered across the sky.

The audience gasped in amazement.

In this lively atmosphere, Suan San and Suo Si stood stiffly in place, not daring to move.

The thin lines on their feet had disappeared, and the seal on their spiritual power had been lifted.

But those few terrifying seconds made them realize completely: this mission was not something they could accomplish.

"Withdraw." Fu San squeezed out a single word from between his teeth.

Suo Si nodded without hesitation.

The two immediately turned around and blended into the crowd that was leaving the theater, leaving without looking back.

...

Shadow Seven also gave up on the second-floor control panel.

The backup array was only halfway set up when the core spirit stone shattered again.

This time he saw it clearly.

The spirit stone began to disintegrate from the inside without warning, as if it had been destroyed at the molecular level by some invisible force.

This is definitely not an accident.

There was a master present, a master they couldn't comprehend.

Yingqi decisively packed up all the equipment, erased all traces, and quietly left the control room.

The two people at the entrance to the equipment room below the stage also evacuated immediately after receiving an emergency message from their companion.

Six assassins arrived silently and left in a flustered and disarray.

Throughout, Wang Ling remained calm and composed.

...

"The performance was pretty good!" As she walked out of the theater, Sun Rong was still excitedly reminiscing: "How did they do that phoenix rebirth special effect? Was it real fire magic?"

"Hmm," Wang Ling said, "A combination of illusions."

"Can you do it?" Sun Rong asked curiously, then realized she had asked a bit too much.

Wang Ling actually thought about it seriously.

However, what he was thinking about was not this kind of stage effect, but to truly bring a phoenix back to life after death.

That would require reversing the rules of life and death.

He tried it once eight hundred years ago in a secret realm of the immortal world, in order to save an ancient fire phoenix whose origin had been corrupted by the demon race.

The process was a bit troublesome, but it was ultimately successful.

However, there's no need to say these things.

"As you would," Wang Ling replied simply.

"......"

Then, the two followed the crowd to the square outside the theater, where many tourists had already gathered to wait for the fireworks display.

The night sky was clear, dotted with stars.

Ten minutes until the fireworks begin.

...

"They're gone?" In the monitoring room, Ryoko Kumiya stared at the screen, somewhat puzzled. "They left without doing anything?"

Wang Zhen's voice came through: "They've indeed left. Six people left the amusement park in three different directions, very decisively. Should I follow them?"

"No need," Zhuo Yi's voice rang out. "Since they've given up, let's not create any more trouble. The important thing is to protect Master and Junior Sister Sun's safety."

"But why did they give up?" Ryoko Kumiya asked, puzzled. "Everything was already arranged."

Liu Qingyi said coldly, "They may have sensed the strength of Master Ling."

"But the real person didn't do anything..." Kugimiya Ryoko stopped mid-sentence.

She switched screens to replay the surveillance footage from inside the theater.

Slow down, 10x, 20x, 50x...

In a fleeting moment, she caught a very subtle movement from Wang Ling: he lightly tapped the ground.

Just for a moment.

Then, the assassin on the second-floor control panel frantically began changing the array plates.

The "couple" on the first floor froze for a few seconds before hastily leaving.

"This is..." Ryoko Kunomiya's eyes widened.

"Master made his move." Zhuo Yi's tone was tinged with amusement. "Although we don't understand how he did it, the result is obvious: those people gave up when faced with a challenge."

Ryoko Kugimiya took a deep breath and looked at Wang Ling's indifferent profile in the picture.

...

In the square, Chen Chao and his two companions finally "bumped into" Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

"Lingzi! Sun Rong! What a coincidence!" Chen Chao waved exaggeratedly, "You guys just finished watching the performance too?"

Wang Ling glanced at him but didn't say anything.

Sun Rong smiled and nodded: "Yes, the performance was exceptionally wonderful. How about you guys?"

"We saw it too! That phoenix was awesome!" Chen Chao gave a thumbs up, then moved closer to Wang Ling and whispered, "Lingzi, during the performance just now, did you... that?"

Wang Ling: "Which one?"

"It's just... taking the opportunity to do something!" Chen Chao winked. "Like handing someone a tissue, lending them a coat, or... holding hands or something?"

Wang Ling replied expressionlessly, "No."

"Oh my god, how can you be so dense!" Chen Chao said with exasperation, "What a great opportunity! Darkness, emotion, the atmosphere was spot on!"

Guo Hao also said helplessly, "Wang Ling may be more focused on the performance content."

"He wasn't paying attention to anything!" Chen Chao rolled his eyes. "He was eating snacks the whole time!"

Li Changzhe whispered, "Brother Chao, don't be so loud..."

Sun Rong listened from the side, her face turning red again. She quickly changed the subject: "The fireworks are about to start, let's find a good spot."

"Yes, yes, fireworks!" Chen Chao was immediately swayed: "I heard that Didoni's fireworks are specially made, incorporating cultivation runes, and the effects are explosive!"

The group found a slightly open spot and stood down.

More and more people gathered in the square, all looking up and waiting.
