Chapter 2402 The Undercurrents Beneath the Smoke and Fire

The crisp sounds of chewing echoed at the entrance of the gift center.

A dozen or so people gathered around, sharing the first pack of honey butter flavored instant noodles that Wang Ling took out of the gift box, each with a relaxed smile on their face.

Sun Rong took a small bite of the crispy noodles, but her gaze remained fixed on Wang Ling's profile.

The boy was looking down at the empty bag in his hand, and his usually indifferent face clearly showed his satisfaction at this moment.

As Sun Rong pondered this, a complex mix of emotions welled up within her.

There was tenderness, heartache, and a little...slight pride.

At this moment, Wang Ling glanced at the time: 3:40 PM.

There are still several hours until the evening fireworks display.

Chen Chao asked, "So what do we do now?"

"Keep playing," he said.

We've come all this way, and we've already gotten the gift box, so of course we should have plenty of time to play.

And... he glanced at Sun Rong.

The girl was looking down at the bracelet, with a faint pink tinge still lingering on the tips of her ears.

"Where shall we go?" Chen Chao immediately switched into strategist mode: "We've basically done all the must-do attractions in the park, so how about... we browse the souvenir shop? Or find a place to sit down and have a drink?"

He deliberately made his words "sit down and have a drink" sound meaningful.

Sun Rong looked up: "Anything is fine with me."

Wang Ling thought for a moment: "By the lake."

He remembered that the view of the small island in the lake from the Heart-to-Heart Bridge in the morning was quite nice, and he thought he could go there for a walk.

"Great idea!" Chen Chao immediately chimed in, "The lakeside scenery is beautiful, and there aren't many people, perfect for... a stroll!"

He deliberately emphasized the word "stroll".

Sun Rong blushed again.

So the team split into two groups.

Xiao Linyou and Liu Kaichuan wanted to try a few more thrilling activities, so they agreed to meet again during the fireworks that evening. Chen Chao naturally followed Wang Ling and Sun Rong, ostensibly to cover for them.

Wang Ling didn't mind Chen Chao following along, and Chen Chao was quite pleased with himself. He thought Wang Ling had finally figured it out and knew that he was following him to help him out. He put his nose in his mouth and practically wrote "shining my shoes" on his face.

"..." Wang Ling didn't bother with it, and the three of them headed straight for the central lake area.

...

The lakeside was indeed quiet in the afternoon.

Most tourists gathered in the amusement park area, with only a few couples strolling along the lake.

The lake surface shimmered, and occasionally a koi carp would leap out of the water, splashing up tiny droplets.

The glowing plants on the small island in the lake looked especially dreamy under the sunlight, with specks of light falling from them.

Chen Chao tactfully walked five or six meters ahead, pretending to be focused on taking pictures, but in reality, he was keeping his ears perked up, ready to "assist" at any time.

Sun Rong and Wang Ling walked side by side on the stone path by the lake.

A breeze blows, bringing the moisture of the lake water and the fragrance of flowers and grass.

"I had a lot of fun today," Sun Rong said softly, breaking the silence.

Wang Ling nodded: "Mm."

"I thought we would lose that duo challenge." Sun Rong couldn't help but smile as she recalled the events on stage. "I didn't expect our teamwork score to be so high."

Wang Ling glanced at her.

The girl's profile looked exceptionally soft in the sunlight, her eyelashes trembled slightly, and her cheeks still had a lingering blush.

The seal started getting hot again.

He looked away and took a candy out of his pocket.

It was still a fruit hard candy, the pale pink candy balls gleaming inside the transparent candy wrapper.

"Here you go." He handed it over.

Sun Rong took it, but this time she didn't peel it off immediately. Instead, she held it in her palm and felt the candy wrapper rubbing against her hand.

"Wang Ling," she suddenly asked, "besides eating instant noodles, what else do you like to do in your spare time?"

Wang Ling thought for a moment.

Cultivation? (It seems unnecessary; his cultivation level increases every day, and the rate of increase is stronger than yesterday).

Fighting (he hopes for world peace).

I occasionally watch anime (like the fifth season of "The Daily Life of the Immortal King," which I'm currently watching).

All that's left is to sleep...

But these things don't seem quite right to be said.

"It's nothing," he finally replied.

Sun Rong was not surprised.

Wang Ling is like that—quiet, mysterious, but always able to bring people a sense of security.

She peeled off the candy wrapper and put it in her mouth.

The sweetness melts on the tip of the tongue.

"So... what do you want to do in the future?" she asked again. "Go to university? Or continue your studies?"

Wang Ling remained silent for a few seconds.

He hadn't thought much about this problem.

With his abilities, going to university would be purely for the experience. However, he has already decided on his major: he wants to study food processing and learn how to make instant noodles.

"Let nature take its course," he said.

Sun Rong laughed: "What a perfunctory answer."

But she wasn't angry.

Because what matters is the present moment.

For example, right now, I'm walking by the lake, eating candy, and enjoying the breeze.

That's good.

"In the future..." Sun Rong suddenly said, "I want to open a dessert shop."

Wang Ling looked at her: "You also want to apply for the food processing major?"

"???" Sun Rong was taken aback: "It's that kind of dessert shop that incorporates spiritual food techniques. They use spiritual fruits as raw materials and use magic to control the temperature and time to make desserts that are both delicious and can help with cultivation."

She said, a little embarrassed, "Isn't it childish?"

Wang Ling shook his head: "No."

He thought it was pretty good.

Sun Rong is skilled in alchemy and has a thorough understanding of the properties of spiritual materials, so she should be able to make desserts very well as well.

"Then..." Sun Rong mustered her courage: "When my shop opens, how about you be the first customer? I'll make you a special crispy noodle flavored cake."

Wang Ling's eyes lit up slightly.

Crispy noodle flavored cake?

That sounds... kind of interesting.

"Okay." He nodded.

Sun Rong smiled happily, her eyes curving into crescents.

Chen Chao, who was walking ahead, saw this scene through the reflection of his phone screen and was overjoyed.

It's done!

A promise for the future! This is a major breakthrough!

He quickly pretended to be taking pictures of the scenery and secretly pressed the shutter a few times.

Of course, it's in silent mode.

...

Meanwhile, at the park's monitoring center.

As Ryoko Kugimiya watched the scene on the screen of Wang Ling and Sun Rong walking side by side, a smile played on her lips.

"A stroll along the lake offers a pleasant atmosphere."

Wang Zhen's voice came through the earpiece: "Those four assassins have all left. Two are injured, one is mentally traumatized, and the other... is still soaking in the lake, being attacked by fish at the Golden Core stage."

Ryoko Kumiya couldn't help but chuckle: "Is the effect of Rin's domain really that exaggerated?"

"It's even more outrageous than you think." Wang Zhen said with emotion, "I observed them all day long, and anyone who had any ill intentions towards Master Ling and Junior Sister Sun either fell down and had bad luck or suffered a bloody disaster."

"That's suppression at the rule level." Ryoko Kumiya's smile faded. "Have you found out who's behind this?"

"We've got a lead," Wang Zhen said. "Those killers belong to an organization called ‘Honghuang Assets.' On the surface, they make investments, but secretly they take on all sorts of shady deals."

Ryoko Kugimiya frowned: "Would Luo Xinghe have dealings with a company like that?"

"That's why I said there's someone behind this." Wang Zhen's voice was serious: "Someone is using Luo Xinghe to test Ling Zhenren."

"Then tonight..."

"Things should calm down tonight," Wang Zhen said. "Once those four assassins report back, their superiors, unless they're idiots, should know that this target is untouchable. At least for the short term, they won't send anyone else."

Ryoko Kugimiya nodded and looked back at the screen.

In the scene, Wang Ling and Sun Rong have walked to the bench by the lake, and Chen Chao is pointing at the small island in the middle of the lake and saying something, gesturing wildly.

"However, we still can't let our guard down," she said. "The fireworks display at night is crowded and confusing, so security personnel need to be on high alert."

"clear."

Communication ended.

Ryoko Kunomiya leaned back in her chair, gently fanning herself with a folding fan.

As the sunlight outside the window gradually slanted westward, the park lights began to illuminate one by one.

Night is coming.
