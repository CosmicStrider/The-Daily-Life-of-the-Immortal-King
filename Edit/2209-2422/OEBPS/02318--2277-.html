Chapter 2277 The War Sect is Deployed

In the early morning in Matsushima City, the spiritual energy is exceptionally fresh under the rising sun, as if the dust of yesterday and the tremors from the earth's core have been washed away.

Inside the Number 60 High School campus, shouts and cheers continued in the Psionic Simulation Battle Arena, but everything had returned to normal.

Wang Ling sat by the window in the classroom, his fingertips unconsciously tapping a rhythm on the desk that only he understood.

His gaze seemed unfocused, but it had already pierced through layers of space and landed outside the blessed land where the Wuji Sword Sect was located.

A few barely perceptible "lines" are becoming restless.

Those were causal threads interwoven with malice, greed, and chaos, quietly converging from the depths of the universe in the same direction—the Sword Saint's Limitless Sword Sect.

Bai Zhe's evil cultivator army has begun to mobilize.

Wang Ling's lifeless eyes remained expressionless, but his fingers, which were tapping the table, paused for a moment.

He did not intend to deal with these small fry himself.

On the one hand, this contradicts his principle of maintaining an "ordinary" life and is easily exposed;

On the other hand, he needed to take this opportunity to see the Battle Sect, or rather, to see the current limits of Thunder Lord's abilities.

It's time to let this self-destructive senior figure make a difference.

With a slight thought, a specially encrypted command containing a trace of supreme authorization was instantly sent to the Battle Sect headquarters via a magical artifact specially made by Wang Ming that could not be tracked by any existing technology.

...

The headquarters of the War Sect is a place that looks like a modern high-tech industrial park on the outside, but inside there is a whole different world. This is a new area developed by the War Sect, which integrates cutting-edge cultivation technology with classical cave secret realm.

These days, the War Sect has been recruiting outstanding recent graduates from various cultivation universities, and the demand for positions far exceeds the supply.

Inside the sect leader's office, True Lord Diu Lei was sitting with his legs crossed, enjoying a cup of this year's newly produced "Cloud Mist Spirit Tip" while browsing the latest issue of the "Risk Assessment Report on Smuggling of Wonders from All Realms".

He recently closed a big deal, acquiring a batch of ancient artifact fragments from a small, collapsing dimension. He's currently debating whether to disassemble and study them or auction them off as a whole to maximize his profits.

Suddenly, the seemingly ordinary watch on his wrist vibrated slightly, and a series of complex and dazzling runes flashed across its surface, followed by an extremely simple emblem that disappeared in an instant.

Lord Diu Lei suddenly sat up straight, his leisurely expression instantly replaced by extreme seriousness and a hint of barely suppressed excitement.

"Get out!" he said to the air.

The elders who were reporting in the office immediately bowed, silently withdrew, and activated the highest level of isolation barrier.

After confirming that it was safe, Lord Diu Lei carefully read the message with his spiritual sense.

The information is extremely brief:

[Target: The Primordial Evil Cultivator Army. Movements: Intending to interfere with the Wuji Sword Sect and capture Wang Muyu.]

[Requirement: Interception.]

[Permission granted: Temporary mobilization of all resources below the True Immortal stage, led by the Battle Sect Elder.]

[Time Limit: Immediately.]

[Note: This has been edited to conceal certain aspects and has been moderately polished.]

It was unsigned, but Lord Thunderbolt was all too familiar with this style.

It's your brother!

Moreover, this time they directly used extremely high temporary authorization! That was almost 70% of the War Sect's publicly known mobile forces!

"Primordial heretical cultivators? These persistent worms actually dare to plot against Senior Sword Saint? And they even dare to covet the person your brother is protecting?" Thunderclap True Monarch's eyes flashed with a sharp light.

That was not fear, but the exhilarating light of carrying out a great mission!

He immediately pressed a hidden button on his desk.

Buzz—

Throughout the entire War Sect headquarters, there are several different dimensional training grounds.

Specific members who are practicing formations, exchanging techniques, or even meditating all simultaneously receive a highest priority instruction on their identity badges or personal magical artifacts.

There are no lengthy explanations, only a clear outline of the objectives and a set of coordinates.

Within a few breaths, powerful auras rose silently from every corner of the headquarters, only to quickly disappear again.

They silently converged toward the command coordinates using different short-range teleportation arrays or spatial warp devices.

Lord Diu Lei himself excitedly rubbed his hands together, and with a flicker of his figure, he disappeared directly from the office.

How could such a grand occasion be complete without him, the sect leader?

...

The Wuji Sword Sect is located in a secluded Sword Intent Cave.

The peaks, like swords, pierce the clouds, their chilling aura omnipresent, sweeping away all filth.

This is the holy land of sword cultivators.

Ordinary cultivators would feel a stinging pain in their souls even if they got close.

Behind the main peak, next to a carefully cultivated shrine.

A little boy, about six or seven years old, with delicate features, was sitting cross-legged on a smooth bluestone.

He was wearing a miniature version of a sword cultivator's uniform, his small face serious, his eyes closed, as if he were contemplating something.

It was Wang Muyu.

Beside him stood Yi Jianchuan, the leader of the Wuji Sword Sect and a renowned swordsman in the cultivation world, with his hands behind his back.

Sword Saint looked at Wang Muyu, his eyes filled with relief and a hint of undisguised amazement.

"Mu Yu, the sword is the blade of the heart. When the mind moves, the sword is drawn. It is not about form, but about spirit..." The Sword Saint slowly explained the basics of swordsmanship, his voice calm yet containing profound sword principles.

He discovered that his apprentice's talent was simply astonishing.

He grasps almost any sword technique or key point instantly, learns it quickly, and often applies it to other situations, offering insights that require him to ponder for a moment.

What's even more peculiar is that the fierce sword intent around him didn't make Wang Muyu feel uncomfortable at all. Instead, it was like a gentle pet, affectionately surrounding him, and he even had a faint feeling of being naturally dominated by it.

The Sword Saint attributed this to Wang Muyu's rare "innate sword embryo" physique.

He had no idea that the child possessed the supreme bloodline of the cosmic overlord dragon race, whose life level inherently possessed absolute affinity and control over all kinds of energy.

The slight dragon aura that Wang Muyu unconsciously emitted was enough to make the sword intent here submit.

"...So, concentrate your mind and try to communicate with the nearest sword energy around you." The Sword Saint demonstrated by pointing his fingers together like a sword, and with a gentle gesture, a three-inch-long, gleaming white sword energy condensed at his fingertips.

Wang Muyu nodded as if he understood, and imitating the Sword Saint, he stretched out his white little finger, held it up to the air, his face turned a little red, and tried his best to "hmm" out.

His acting skills are superb.

As a result, nothing happened.

Sword Saint smiled slightly, about to comfort him by saying there was no need to rush.

Sudden--

Wang Muyu seemed to find this unfunny, his little brows furrowed, and he subconsciously waved his little hand impatiently at a Seven-Star Sword Heart Grass swaying in the wind a few meters away, as if to shoo away the little flying insects that were disturbing his listening.

The movements were clumsy and lacked any sense of technique.

However, in the instant his little hand was raised!

Buzz!

The entire spirit garden, with its thousands of Seven Star Sword Heart Grasses and the natural sword intent contained in the surrounding mountain walls, seemed to have received a supreme command, and suddenly emitted an extremely excited buzzing sound!

The next moment, countless fine yet incredibly condensed emerald green sword energies burst forth from the blades of grass, like an army undergoing inspection!

It instantly filled the entire space behind the mountain!
