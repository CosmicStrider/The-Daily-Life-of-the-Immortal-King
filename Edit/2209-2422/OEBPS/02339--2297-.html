Chapter 2297 The Rise of Taste Society

The school bell rang on time at Number 60 High School.

These past few days, Teacher Pan has not continued to extend class time, just so that the children can go home early before the big exam, review in the most familiar and comfortable environment, and get a good rest.

The curriculum at school is already very demanding, so the educational policy must be appropriately relaxed before major exams to prevent some students from underperforming due to high tension.

After all, for every student at Number 60 High School, whether they can successfully complete this stage of their studies and move on to the next stage depends entirely on this major exam.

Wang Ling packed his schoolbag at a leisurely pace, which was quite different from the slightly hurried atmosphere of the students around him who were facing their final exams.

His gaze seemed to casually sweep across the window, his invisible consciousness weaving an intricate network that enveloped the entire campus, focusing on monitoring the signal sources of those "specially cared-for" [Taste Masters].

The most active one was Li Jun, who was in the regular class of the first year of high school.

"Reiko, let's go, let's reserve a spot in the training hall!"

Chen Chao patted Wang Ling on the shoulder and said in a loud voice, "You'd better give it your all in the final exams. I heard that the top ten in the elite class will receive a special reward from the principal!"

"Chao'er, you're dreaming! We just came back from the Earth's core and have already entered the Golden Core stage. Don't compete with others for this reward. Especially since this special reward is probably related to your guaranteed admission to university." Guo Hao shrugged and said.

"Just guaranteed admission? Weren't we already guaranteed admission?" Chen Chao sneered.

Fortunately, there weren't many people around, otherwise such a Versailles-like remark would have surely earned him eye-rolls from his classmates.

Guo Hao continued in a low voice, "You have no idea how hard others can work with such resources! I heard that some students in the regular classes who used that XiBai thing have made rapid progress recently, and this big exam should be very exciting."

Even so, there was still a hint of worry in his tone.

Clearly, the amazing rumors about the "Taste Master" caused a certain psychological stir even in the elite class.

Sun Rong walked over to Wang Ling's side and whispered, "Don't worry about what methods others use; a solid foundation is the most important thing."

As she spoke, her gaze fell on Wang Lingping's calm profile, as if trying to draw some reassuring strength from him.

The faint sense of warning emanating from Ooha made her instinctively reject any overly convenient way of acquiring power.

There are no shortcuts in the path of cultivation. Even in the context of modern scientific cultivation, embracing new technologies is an inevitable trend.

However, Sun Rong still felt that it was more prudent to proceed step by step steadily. Otherwise, with the various powerful pills provided by the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group, it wouldn't have been difficult for Sun Rong to forcefully push her cultivation level to the late Golden Core stage before entering the Number 60 High School.

Wang Ling nodded, which was considered a response to the three of them.

He didn't care about those rumors because he saw things more clearly than anyone else.

The four of them followed the flow of people out of the teaching building.

She blends in with the students around her who are excitedly discussing "Taste Masters" and worrying about their final exams, yet they seem to come from completely different worlds.

...

On the other hand, Li Jun felt as if his life had been divided in two by that silver watch.

Before he put it on, his daily life was filled with depression and gloom.

After wearing it, the world seems to be filtered through a dazzling lens, and even every breath feels so free!

A fragrance similar to a donut lingered in my throat.

The experience of defeating Zhang Hao in the alley that day was deeply imprinted in his heart.

It wasn't fear, but an unprecedented excitement.

This damn power is so fascinating.

He was no longer satisfied with simply using "Taste Master" to "optimize" his meals. Instead, he turned his attention to the constantly flashing "Shared Taste Community" icon on the device interface.

Taking a deep breath, Li Jun's consciousness sank into it.

He felt as if he had instantly fallen into a bizarre and fantastical kingdom of senses.

Countless orbs of light, each representing a different taste experience, floated around.

He touched a ball of light labeled "Foundation Building Special: Fiery Lava Cake".

"Boom!"

My consciousness felt as if it had been thrown into a volcano!

The intense heat, with its exhilarating sensation of breaking through barriers, directly stimulated his taste buds, almost making him groan.

After exiting the experience, he discovered that he had actually gained a new understanding of a certain key point of the exercise.

He then tried another dish: "Pure Heart and Enlightenment: Ice Soul Snow Lotus Soup".

After a refreshing, cool sensation, his thoughts became unusually clear, and the previously obscure texts of the cultivation method seemed to come alive.

That's amazing!

This is not just a deception of taste; it is an aid that directly targets the source of cultivation!

The community is filled with enthusiastic sharing.

"None of my family members understand! Using the [Deep Sea Spirit Beast Sushi] taste template, combined with basic body-training techniques, my cultivation efficiency has tripled!"

"I'm sharing a self-adjusting template, [White Senior's Reiki Face], which greatly improves Reiki sustain. My mom no longer has to worry about me running out of mana halfway through my training!"

"Just wondering, is there anything that can make someone more confident?"

Surprisingly, there were quite a few replies to this last post, with people sharing all sorts of exciting taste templates.

Li Jun's heart pounded; he had found a taste template that could unleash his innate, domineering aura.

He tried it secretly.

At that moment, he seemed to transform into an ancient emperor looking down upon all living beings.

Zhang Hao's repulsive face flashed through my mind, but it no longer aroused any fear; instead, it evoked only an urge to trample him underfoot.

At the same time, the spiritual energy within Li Jun's body became restless.

But he was completely unaware of this.

On the contrary, I became fascinated by this feeling.

A crazy idea began to grow wildly in Li Jun's mind.

It's right within the Number 60 High School!

He wants to establish a club!

A club for those of them who lack talent in cultivation and are discriminated against by the other students! A utopia where they can share resources and encourage each other!

With the initial changes brought about by the "Taste Master" and the persuasive words he learned in the community, Li Jun easily persuaded several classmates who also came from ordinary families and were struggling with their training.

"Brothers and sisters! We may not be as talented as those prodigies in the elite class, and our resources may not be as good as those second-generation immortals! But we have the desire to become powerful! Heaven rewards diligence? That's a lie for fools! Now, we have a new opportunity, which is the [Taste Master]!"

In an abandoned storage room on campus, Li Jun stood on a platform made of several broken tables pieced together, passionately launching into his lecture.

The "Taste Master" on his wrist gleamed faintly, seemingly echoing his emotions.
