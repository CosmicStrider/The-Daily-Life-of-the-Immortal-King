Chapter 2338 Crimson Fire High School

Zhou Rui's invitation to Number 60 High School to participate in a training match sparked heated discussions within his own club.

Many team members felt that Zhou Rui had acted impulsively, to some extent.

"Brother Rui, are you sure you're not mistaken? A training match around Number 60 High School?"

Inside the club activity room, a boy with chestnut-colored hair was the first to jump out.

He was the top laner in the match just now.

"It's just a match loss, why are you making such a fuss? They're skilled in cultivation, every one of them is a hero after returning from the inner world, but playing games... that's a completely different matter!"

"Exactly!" The mid-laner playing Talon chimed in with a gloomy face, "Ashe got lucky and landed two arrows, Bard's ultimate was a bit too accurate, and Syndra just got her late-game items. It's a casual match, nobody was playing seriously, so it doesn't prove anything. Playing these kinds of non-traditional esports powerhouse practice matches is a complete waste of time."

The walls of the e-sports club activity room are covered with certificates and trophies won by Xuanming High School in various e-sports leagues over the years.

Zhou Rui's gaze swept over these testimonies that carried history and hard work, and finally settled on his teammates who looked dissatisfied.

He pushed up his black-rimmed glasses, his gaze behind the lenses calm and resolute.

"The outcome of a match is indeed not important. What matters are the details."

Zhou Rui's voice wasn't loud, but it silenced the surrounding club members involuntarily: "Didn't you guys notice? Those two ultimate abilities from Ashe weren't just luck. The first was predicting Talon's movement path blindly, and the second was predicting Varus's flash landing point. Can an ordinary high school student have that kind of awareness?"

He paused, then continued his analysis: "And then there's Bard, every time he used his portals, the timing and angle were perfect. The timing of Malphite's teleportation flank perfectly complemented Ashe's initiation and Bard's follow-up control. Most importantly, there's Syndra..."

Zhou Rui looked at his mid-lane teammate: "Jueying, you have the deepest laning experience with him. In the early game, he was indeed like a novice, clumsy and disorganized. But from the mid-game onwards, you should know how terrifying his positioning, skill accuracy, and even damage calculation speed are."

The boy opened his mouth, about to refute, but facts speak louder than words, and in the end he kept his mouth shut.

"This is not a team that relies on luck or a few brilliant moments from individual players."

Zhou Rui summarized: "They have a top-notch ADC and support, a mid-laner with amazing growth rate, a calm and resilient top-laner, and a jungler with a strong sense of rhythm. More importantly, their synergy is unlike that of a newly formed team. I think they might be the biggest dark horse in this year's qualifiers."

"A dark horse? Brother Rui, aren't you overestimating them?" The top laner was still somewhat unconvinced.

"Whether it's good or not, you'll only know after you try." Zhou Rui's lips curled into a meaningful smile: "It's not appropriate for us to directly arrange another meeting, lest you have any reservations. But there are definitely people who will be interested in a team like this."

He ignored his teammates' comments, picked up his phone, skillfully found a contact number, and dialed the number.

...

Matsumi City, Akahashi High School Esports Club.

The atmosphere here is even more intense than at Xuanming High School.

The trophies hanging on the wall are also significantly more prestigious, including the championship trophies from last year's and the year before's city-level selection competitions.

The team leader, Liu Kaichuan, whose ID is "Red Fire, Burning Sky", is leading the team members in reviewing the video of their most recent training match.

He is tall and has sharp eyes; he is recognized as one of the top top laners in the Songhai City high school e-sports circle.

Liu Kaichuan was somewhat surprised to see that his phone rang and it was Zhou Rui calling.

Xuanming and Chihuo are old rivals with a delicate relationship, characterized by both competition and mutual respect.

"Hey, Zhou Rui? What's up, want to arrange a practice match? We're on fire lately, aren't you afraid we'll break your mentality?" Liu Kaichuan answered the phone with a laugh, his tone witty and teasing.

However, Zhou Rui's words on the other end of the phone made his smile gradually fade, and he instead showed an expression of interest.

"A training match? No. I'm recommending an opponent to you."

Zhou Rui's voice came through: "A newly formed team, ranked 60th, just beat us in matchmaking. They seem... very unusual."

"Number 60 High School?" Liu Kaichuan raised an eyebrow. "That school that's really good at cultivation? They've started doing esports too? And they even beat up your Xuanming Sect?"

There was a hint of doubt in his words, but he understood Zhou Rui's character and knew that he was not one to speak without reason.

"Yeah, and it didn't feel like they were going all out."

Zhou Rui continued, "I'll send you their five game IDs. They should still be online. Can you organize a team right away and play a 5v5 match with them? Just to test the waters for us, and also to find some new practice partners for yourselves."

Liu Kaichuan pondered for a moment.

The fact that Zhou Rui so highly praised an unknown team, even going so far as to contact them to test them out, speaks volumes.

"Okay, send me your ID." Liu Kaichuan made his decision: "I want to see just how good this team is that you, Zhou Rui, are."

After hanging up the phone, Liu Kaichuan immediately issued a call to action in the group chat.

"Everyone, if you're not busy, log in immediately! There's an urgent mission; we've arranged a trial match with a squad!"

...

Inside the Dream Esports Arena, the students from Number 60 High School had just finished their battle against Xuanming High School and were still immersed in the excitement of victory.

"Muyu, your Syndra is simply invincible in the late game!" Chen Chao vigorously ruffled Wang Muyu's hair.

"Brother Wang Ling's arrows are still the most accurate..." Wang Muyu blushed slightly, lowered her head shyly, and secretly glanced at Wang Ling.

Wang Ling sat quietly in the e-sports chair and opened the third pack of instant noodles.

Sun Rong and Guo Hao discussed the field control and resource exchange they had just discussed.

Just then, a new invitation notification sounded on Sun Rong's game client.

[Crimson flames and blazing skies invite you to join a 5v5 draft mode match.]

"Crimson Fire... Burning the Heavens?" Sun Rong was slightly taken aback, a look of surprise on her face: "Why would they invite us?"

"Red Fire High School?" Guo Hao's voice rose eight octaves upon hearing this: "The city champions from last year and the year before? They're challenging us to a match?"

Chen Chao also came over, his eyes wide: "Holy crap! Really? Crimson Fire High School? That's a real powerhouse!"

Even Wang Ling, who had been expressionless the whole time, paused for half a second while chewing his instant noodles.

Wang Muyu asked curiously, "Sister Sun Rong, is Chihuo High School very good?"

"Yes, they are very strong." Sun Rong nodded solemnly. "They are recognized as one of the dominant teams in the Songhai City high school e-sports scene. Their tactical execution and individual skills are top-notch."

Her mind raced, and she immediately thought of Zhou Rui, whom she had just added as a friend.

"It seems that Zhou Rui recommended us to Chi Huo High School. He wants to use Chi Huo to further test our strength."

"Shall we fight?" Chen Chao rubbed his hands together, eager to try: "A chance to play against the champion team is a rare opportunity!"

Guo Hao was calmer: "We will definitely fight, but Chi Huo is not Xuan Ming. They are stronger and their research is more thorough. We just exposed a lot of things, and they will definitely target us."

Sun Rong looked at Wang Ling and Wang Muyu, seeking their opinions with her eyes.

Wang Ling gave a soft "hmm".

Wang Muyu nodded vigorously: "I want to try!"

"Okay." Sun Rong took a deep breath, her eyes hardening. "Since they've extended an invitation, we have no reason to back down. It's a good opportunity to test our overall ability when facing truly strong teams. Everyone, get ready; this match will be much more difficult than the last one."

She accepted the invitation, and five people from Number 60 High School entered the competition room again.

The five opponents all had "Red Fire" as a prefix in their IDs, exuding an invisible sense of oppression.
