Chapter 2281 Whetstone

Wuji Sword Sect, back mountain.

In Jueyu's vertical pupils, which were like molten gold, the cold killing intent was tangible.

The surrounding air was almost frozen.

The final silence fell, but the gun continued to hum. The dark red liquid dripping from the gun tip became hot and viscous, each drop carving deep holes into the gray-white ground, emanating an aura of annihilation.

"Destruction Mode, Authorization Confirmed."

Robots typically speak in a completely emotionless tone, forced from a choked throat.

He slowly raised the bone spear in his hand, locking onto Wang Muyu.

The plan is no longer simply about capture, but aims to completely destroy Wang Muyu's ability to resist, and even to destroy parts of his body.

Wang Muyu's face was pale, and the vast dragon power within his body was suppressed by the Annihilation Domain, making him crawl like a tortoise. The sword intent around him was also dim and thin.

However, the flames in his golden dragon eyes did not extinguish; instead, they were aroused by the other party's undisguised killing intent, which stirred up an even deeper fighting instinct.

He is a combination of Wanlong genes, a descendant of the cosmic overlord!

How can we allow others to manipulate us at will?!

"You bad guy!" Wang Muyu shouted in a childish voice, trying to dispel the chill in his heart.

He steadied himself and thought about the mental cultivation he had undergone with Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan these past few days. The trace of fear in his heart was gradually being dispelled by a powerful willpower.

Jue frowned, realizing that Wang Muyu was even more difficult to deal with than she had imagined...

This terrifying being, a fusion of the Wanlong gene and a small portion of that powerful man's genes, despite lacking extensive combat experience, experiences tremendous growth in almost every battle! Just like Wang Ling's ever-increasing cultivation level...

Its small body, after its spiritual energy was depleted, quickly replenished itself and swelled to a level even stronger than before.

"Indeed interesting." Jueji nodded to himself. He finally understood why his revered Lord Bai Zhe was so obsessed with this little guy!

Then, Jueqi saw Wang Muyu begin to imitate the starting stance that his master Yi Jianchuan often used. His short legs were slightly bent, and although the posture was somewhat comical, there was an indescribable sense of majesty gathering.

"Come, let's fight!" Jueqin moved.

There was no earth-shattering fanfare, just a single step taken.

His figure blurred like a ghost, and in the next instant he had already covered dozens of meters. The Final Silence Spear, carrying a chilling gray and deathly silence, pierced straight at Wang Muyu's dantian!

The speed is far greater than before!

If this spear strikes, it will not only cripple his cultivation, but the accompanying "law withering" effect will be enough to permanently damage Wang Muyu's essence!

Wang Muyu's pupils contracted sharply, a strong sense of crisis stimulating his nerves.

He wanted to hide, but his body felt as heavy as a mountain.

He wanted to block, but he couldn't muster enough strength.

At this critical moment—

"Buzz—!"

The ground beneath his feet, the gray and fragile earth eroded by the Annihilation Domain, trembled once more without warning!

A powerful, primal sword intent, carrying a hint of wildness, erupted like a sleeping dragon being forcibly awakened!

This force did not directly attack Jueqi, but rather precisely impacted the invisible annihilation domain force field surrounding Wang Muyu!

Click!

Like the faint sound of shattering glass.

The almost absolute confinement domain was actually forced open by this sudden surge of earth sword intent, creating a tiny, insignificant gap!

The gaps are extremely small and exist for such a short time that they can be ignored.

But for Wang Muyu, that was enough!

The moment the gap appeared in the domain, the dragon power that had been suppressed within him surged forth like a flood bursting its banks!

"roar--!"

A majestic yet immature dragon roar suddenly erupted from the depths of his bloodline, not from Wang Muyu's mouth!

The golden dragon's might spread out in a substantial way, forcibly expanding the gray domain that was about a foot wide around it!

Wang Muyu's eyes shone brightly with golden light, and the dragon tail that manifested from his fighting spirit swayed freely, with seven-colored dragon scales shining brightly on it!

He didn't have time to think about where the earth sword intent came from; his fighting instincts drove him to unleash the immense dragon power he had just released without any strategy!

He didn't use any techniques; it was just the purest release of power!

A thick golden beam of light, mixed with scattered earth vein sword intent, roared like an enraged young dragon and crashed head-on into the Absolute Silence Spear!

Boom!

This clash was far more intense than any previous one!

Jueqi was somewhat astonished. In this illusory realm on Earth, where the spiritual energy was far inferior to that of the Dragon Palace, a young dragon offspring could actually grow to such an extent!

A terrifying energy shockwave exploded in a ring, uprooting all the remaining spiritual plants in the spirit garden and turning them into dust! Even the sword intent protective cocoon set up by the Sword Saint shook violently, its surface covered with ripples!

His body, which was charging forward, suddenly stopped!

He no longer felt weak resistance from the spear tip, but rather a powerful, primal, and wild force of violence at an extremely high level!

This power, in essence, subtly suppressed his Annihilation Dragon Breath!

Although his quantity was far less than his, the pressure emanating from his level of life caused even his dragon-born body, born for battle, to tremble slightly.

"Supreme Dragon Blood... its purity exceeds expectations by 473 percent..."

The analysis system inside the enclosure was issuing alarms wildly, and the data stream was flashing erratically.

Wang Muyu was thrown away by the recoil and rolled several times on the ground before barely coming to a stop.

He was covered in dust, his arms and legs felt numb, and his blood was churning inside him.

A dragon descendant of the Celestial Ancestor level is indeed not easy to deal with.

But he immediately got up, his eyes wide open, looking at his slightly reddened little hands, and then at Jueqi in the distance, whom he had repelled for the first time.

He... he actually fought off that bad guy?

Although my hand is numb, it doesn't seem so scary anymore?

An unprecedented feeling began to sprout in his heart—confidence.

Jueqi slowly straightened up and looked down at his bone spear.

At the tip of the spear, a small patch of the dark red corrosive energy was temporarily purified, revealing the pale bone beneath.

When he looked up at Wang Muyu again, the killing intent in his molten gold vertical pupils was even stronger, but there was also an unprecedented solemnity.

"Threat level recalibrated...final sequence. Execute final cleanup protocol."

He no longer viewed Wang Muyu as a sample to be captured, but rather as an abnormal variable that had to be completely destroyed.

His goal was simply to bring back Wang Muyu's genes; whether he lived or died was irrelevant.

As long as even a single dragon scale is left behind, their side will make a huge profit!

Juejue slowly raised the Silence of the End Spear, and the pale patterns on the spearhead lit up one by one, as if performing some ancient and evil ritual.

The surrounding desolate domain intensified once more, even beginning to draw life force from the space itself, causing the light to dim even further.

Wang Muyu felt an even more terrifying pressure than before, his small face tense, but he did not back down. Instead, he tried to mobilize the boiling dragon power in his body, his big golden eyes fixed on Jueqi, trying to find the next opportunity.

He felt that there was still a great deal of untapped power within him, and that his potential was far greater than what he currently possessed...
