Chapter 2347 Draven's Trial

After the game loading screen finished, ten figures descended upon Summoner's Canyon, accompanied by the system's horn.

"Welcome to the Glory Alliance!"

Liu Kaichuan (ID: Xuanfeng Qingqingzi), controlling Varus, bought Doran's Blade and Bloodthirster while confidently telling Sun Rong (ID: Rongguang Huanfa) in team voice chat: "Sun Rong, don't worry. Although I'm not as proficient as a top laner as an ADC, I can still handle solo queue games. Let's lane normally, play it safe, and look for opportunities..."

But to be honest, Liu Kaichuan himself wasn't sure either. He had played against Wang Ling and also played ranked games together, and he knew that Wang Ling's ADC skills were at a professional level.

However, since he had agreed to help, Liu Kaichuan still had to show some of the professionalism expected of the president of Chihuo High School's e-sports club. Only in this way could he make Wang Ling on the other side "jealous".

He knew he was unlikely to beat Wang Ling, but he couldn't let Wang Ling have the same comfortable matchup as before, after all, he was a King-level player.

At that moment, before Liu Kai could finish his message, he saw Draven with the ID "L" and Thor with the ID "Sixty's Number One Reckless Man" walking straight towards the bottom lane.

That indomitable momentum exuded a menacing aura even through the screen.

"Wang Ling's Draven... seems a bit scary?" Liu Kaichuan muttered.

The cold gleam of Draven's spinning axe sent a chill down his spine.

Sun Rong stared at the familiar ID [L] on the screen and the brutal hero he had chosen, her heart racing inexplicably.

She responded softly, "Yes, we'll be careful."

She knew Wang Ling was strong, but she didn't expect him to choose Draven, a hero with such a strong sense of dominance and a desire to perform.

This is a stark contrast to his usual listless and unenthusiastic demeanor.

The lanes intersect.

The battle deviated from Liu Kaichuan's envisioned "normal laning" script from the very beginning.

Wang Ling's Draven learns the W skill [Blood Dash] at level one.

The first axe strike landed precisely on a low-health melee soldier, and the crisp sound of gold coins disappearing into a pocket rang out.

Almost the instant the axe landed, Draven flashed red, triggering his W speed boost. He moved sideways like a cheetah, easily catching the axe while simultaneously hurling a second axe with a whooshing sound towards Liu Kaichuan's Varus!

"hiss!"

Liu Kai instinctively moved aside, but the axe seemed to have eyes, and the edge still grazed him, causing him to lose a small amount of health.

"Wang Ling's skills are as fierce as ever!" Liu Kaichuan finally realized how much pressure the person who had faced Wang Ling before was under. These kinds of details can't be mastered without ten or eight years of gaming experience.

What he had no idea was that Wang Ling, as the Celestial King, was extremely quick to learn any game.

Moreover, Wang Ling's detailed operations went far beyond this.

At this moment, Wang Ling controlled his hero Draven to catch the axe, and his W skill instantly refreshed! He gained another speed boost!

Movement, axe swing, axe catch, then acceleration...

Wang Ling's Draven seemed to dance on a knife's edge, his movements incredibly agile.

With each movement, he precisely landed on the axe's landing point, while his throwing axe showed no mercy, relentlessly striking Verus.

Finishing blow? Suppression?

No, this was simply a rehearsal for a one-sided massacre!

Liu Kaichuan's Verus was forced to retreat repeatedly, not even daring to smell the experience.

He tried to harass from a distance with his Q skill, Piercing Arrow, but the obvious forward sway during the charge was like slow motion in the face of Wang Ling's inhuman reaction. Draven could easily dodge it with a small movement and then he would get hit by an axe instead.

"This guy... doesn't he even look at the circle on the ground when catching the axe?" Liu Kaichuan's forehead began to sweat.

He had played Draven and knew that the most difficult part of playing this hero was balancing positioning and catching axes in intense fights.

But Wang Ling's movements as he caught the axes were fluid and effortless, as if those axes were born to fall at his feet, his attention completely focused on how to chop people down.

Actually, by this point, Liu Kaichuan had already realized that something was wrong.

He chose Varus because the champion's Q skill has a long range, allowing for long-range poke. Even if he can't beat Wang Ling, he can eventually stabilize the bot lane by using long-range poke.

He thought he would only be slightly outmaneuvered by Wang Ling, but the situation did not develop as he had anticipated.

Sun Rong's Lulu attempted to use her Q skill, "Flash Spear," to slow down Draven and create space for Liu Kaichuan.

Actually, Sun Rong didn't want to take it so seriously, but as a person, you still have to be mindful of social etiquette. Since Liu Kaichuan helped her, she couldn't let him lose too badly.

However, Draven's axe skills were too unpredictable, and his positioning was even more astonishing, causing her skills to miss most of the time.

Even when it hits, the slight slowing effect is negligible compared to Draven's double W's acceleration.

Chen Chao's hammer strikes felt exceptionally comfortable.

He was initially worried that he might miss the target or not be able to keep up with the king's pace.

But he soon discovered that all he needed to do was stand in a position of intimidation, occasionally use his E skill [Doom Pendulum] to refresh his passive, or block some skills for Wang Ling.

Wang Ling's Draven doesn't need much protection; he can solve all problems on his own.

"First Blood!"

The system's cold female voice rang out; only three minutes had passed.

Liu Kai's Verruc made a positioning mistake, allowing Draven to catch up using his speed boost.

With three consecutive axe blows and the addition of Ignite, he was hacked to death under the tower, without even having time to use Flash!

"I..." Liu Kaichuan stared at the black and white screen, opened his mouth, but couldn't utter a single word.

He felt like a player who had just left the beginner village and encountered a boss with max-level gear and a special script.

Moreover, Liu Kaichuan now felt that he was losing face... because he knew that Sun Yiyuan, the chairman of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, was also watching the competition.

People praise him for being good at games.

Now... what a load of rubbish!

Left with no other choice, Liu Kaichuan typed to Wang Ling through the game's chat interface.

[Everyone] Whirlwind Qingqingzi (Arrow of Punishment): ...Brother, go easy on me?

Wang Ling's Draven stood beside Verus's corpse, calmly finishing off the tower with his sword, ignoring his words.

After being resurrected and returning to the online arena, Liu Kaichuan became more cautious, almost only daring to use his Q skill to last-hit minions from a distance.

However, the economic snowball has started rolling uncontrollably.

Draven's passive [Draven Alliance] grants him Exalted status, which stacks rapidly with every kill and last hit.

Upon returning home, Wang Ling immediately pulled out a Storm Greatsword and a Dexterity Cloak, resulting in a qualitative change in his damage output.

Seven minutes later, both junglers ganked the bottom lane at the same time.

Liu Kaichuan's jungler, Jarvan IV, attempted to use his EQ combo to block Draven.

Just as the flag fell, a golden light flashed across Draven's body!

Flash!

Instead of moving backward, he dodged to the side, avoiding the control while closing the distance with Velus!

"Damn it!" Liu Kaichuan thought to himself and immediately dodged backward.

But Wang Ling's Draven seemed to have anticipated his move.

With W providing acceleration, the E skill [Pathfinder Axe] was precisely unleashed, forcefully pushing the Flash-landed Varus, causing him to stumble and slow down!
