Chapter 2242 First Exploration of the Earth's Core

The heavy alloy gate, accompanied by the deep hum of the psionic engine, slowly rose from the depths of the Palm Cliff Experiment Base.

Strange spiritual runes intertwined on the surface of the gate, eventually converging into a huge ring with a diameter of over twenty meters.

The space inside the ring, like water with a stone thrown in, twisted and trembled violently before finally stabilizing into a deep blue vortex—the "Singularity Array" leading to the unknown inner world of the earth was officially activated.

Dean Jing Heqiu's calm voice resounded through the base's psionic broadcasting system across the platform assembled at the entrance of the passage: "Sequence activated! All units, take note! Spiritual hub stability: 98%! Spatial coordinate calibration complete! Gravitational parameters synchronized! First sequence—Huaxiu National Elite University Representative Team—teleportation initiation! All teams maintain formation, psionic shields activated! Three, two, one... Enter formation!"

The elite high school students, representing the top schools in Huaxiu Kingdom, wore adaptive psionic combat suits optimized according to their own attributes, and carried various magical artifacts, spirit swords, array plates, and puppets on their waists or backs.

With varying expressions on their faces, the group, guided by their respective instructors and base staff, entered the deep blue spatial passage in an orderly fashion, like streaks of light flowing into a vortex.

The sense of imbalance and slight tearing brought about by spatial transmission was something Wang Ling was already as used to as breathing.

He was positioned in the middle to rear of the Number 60 High School team, with Sun Rong slightly ahead of him. Chen Chao and Guo Hao were intently maintaining their hand seals to stabilize the shield around the team.

The inside of the teleportation channel was not absolutely empty; it was as if one were traveling through a tunnel of distorted light, with bizarre and fantastical scenery all around, and high-speed flowing fragments of alien space like a shattered kaleidoscope.

Everything seemed to be going according to plan.

until--

A roar that pierced through the psionic shield and reached the depths of the soul suddenly exploded!

In the turbulent energy flow ahead of the passage, a colossal creature suddenly tore through the spatial barrier and "squeezed" in without warning!

It appears to be formed from a mixture of pure energy and terrifying minerals from deep underground. Its main body resembles a twisted dragon-python, covered in dark purple crystal scales, with more than a dozen bone spurs of various shapes extending from its sides.

Its head resembled a bull but was not; a single, enormous eye occupied half of its face, its scarlet light like a searchlight, locking onto the elite college cultivators who were speeding through the transmission channel!

The oppressive force, mixed with the ancient and scorching aura unique to the Earth's core, far surpassed all previous simulation data!

Its single eye swept over the crowd, its greed and destructive intent undisguised, clearly regarding the vanguard carrying the hopes of the Huaxiu Kingdom as prey delivered to its doorstep!

"Warning! High-energy unidentified life form detected! Psionic energy fluctuations exceed threshold!"

"The source of interference has not been identified!"

"The stability of the spatial passage is rapidly decreasing! Gravitational parameters are out of control! Everyone, stabilize yourselves immediately!!" Teng Luchen's urgent and cold voice rang out in everyone's communication jade talisman, carrying a trace of undisguised shock and anger.

Chaos erupted instantly inside the passage!

"Hold on!"

"Form ranks! Defend!"

"Damn it, it's attracting spatial turbulence!"

"Wang Ling! What time is it? Stop eating instant noodles!"

Chen Chao roared, and the phantom of the dragon and elephant appeared and disappeared in the violently trembling light screen.

Sun Rong's expression changed slightly. The scabbard of the Ao Hai Sword hummed, and the azure water-patterned shield expanded, attempting to envelop more members of the team.

Su Xingyue rattled off a string of data coordinates at lightning speed: "The psionic core is at seven o'clock! Gao Tianming, left-wing interference!"

Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi looked solemn, their divine spiritual power surging around them, ready to erupt.

The chaotic energy flow, the piercing alarm, the shouts of teammates, and the roar of the behemoth shaking the passage all mingled together, forming a prelude to destruction.

Wang Ling remained standing towards the back of the group, his dead fish eyes calmly sweeping over the incomparably ferocious unknown spirit beast.

It feels... a bit noisy.

Its massive energy eye locked onto a spot not far from Wang Ling where energy was weak.

Judging from the trajectory and energy intensity of that claw strike, the two unlucky students from other universities nearby would probably be reduced to ashes immediately.

Tsk, quite arrogant.

Wang Ling felt completely unmoved, and even felt that it was somewhat delaying his mood to look for local-style instant noodles after landing.

He didn't even lift a finger. He just frowned slightly in his mind, looking towards the direction of the giant beast that had locked onto his vicinity and was exuding a violent and greedy aura.

Buzz—there was no earth-shattering burst of light, no deafening sound.

The entire space passage, which was trembling violently, seemed to have been filled with an invisible, absolutely still pebble.

The surging spatial turbulence froze instantly!

The violent stream of energy particles lost all kinetic energy and floated like dust.

An "unknown spirit beast" is waving its crystal claws and lunging forward menacingly.

The massive, scarlet eye froze instantly, as if a bucket of liquid nitrogen at absolute zero had been poured over it.

Then it transforms into a fear originating from the deepest, most profound will of life...

It "saw" it in an instant.

Behind that inconspicuous figure standing in the crowd, with a faint breath... that almost filled its entire perceptual "world".

That was a crushing force of will, more utterly empty than the void, more silent than the abyss, and more ruthless than the heat death of the universe!

It's the ultimate warning that even the concept itself seems to be frozen!

"Aww... Waaah—?!"

The deafening roar instantly transformed into a distorted, piercing wail filled with extreme terror!

The enormous creature, which was ferocious and overwhelming just a second ago, suddenly froze in mid-air!

All attack actions stopped instantly!

Its enormous, ferocious body twitched strangely in mid-air, as if its soul had been struck hard by an invisible hammer!

The next second, the "unknown spirit beast" did something that made all the elite students who were prepared to fight to the death almost pop their eyes out!

All its limbs, which were tearing through space, suddenly curled inward, and its huge, mountain-like body was forcibly rolled into a ball in mid-air!

Then, it fled in the fastest, most disheveled, and most desperate manner!
