Chapter 2350 The Love Strategist's Perfect Plan

Ryoko Kugimiya's words rippled through Sun Rong's heart.

"Next...next step plan?"

Sun Rong felt her cheeks burning, and the air in the student council office seemed to have become thick and sticky.

Looking at her decisive and efficient best friend, she felt both apprehensive about the unknown plan and a touch of embarrassment at having her secret exposed.

"That's right."

Ryoko Kugimiya gracefully opened her folding fan, partially obscuring her face, a slight smile playing on her lips. Speaking in a thick accent, she said, "Based on the information provided by Chen Chao, and my own analysis, Wang Ling's blockhead has been initially activated. Although he may not be aware of it yet, that instinctive reaction doesn't lie. Now we need to strike while the iron is hot, amplify this subtle emotion, and make him feel a clearer sense of crisis."

"So, the plan is?" Sun Rong took a deep breath, trying to calm herself down.

"A meticulously planned offline training match."

Ryoko Kugimiya closed her folding fan and said strategically, "I have already contacted my alma mater, Kudo High School's e-sports club. They will send four of their top members to fly to Huaxiu to play a 5v5 offline match with you at your Dream E-sports Arena, under the guise of exchanging and learning e-sports skills."

"Four...top members?"

Sun Rong blinked, vaguely sensing that things weren't so simple.

"Hmm." Ryoko Kugimiya took out a document from her seemingly small handbag and handed it to Sun Rong: "Look, this is their basic information, which I personally selected."

Sun Rong took the documents and turned to the first page. What came into view were four photos of different styles but all of them were quite handsome, with a brief text description next to them.

Fujiwara Ren: A cool and aloof high school boy. He has short, black hair, light brown eyes, and an aloof demeanor. He mainly plays mid-lane, has an extremely deep champion pool, and employs unpredictable playstyles. He is the president of the Kyudo High School esports club and holds a Challenger rank of 30 stars. Note: His ex-girlfriend was a classmate; they broke up amicably due to differing aspirations, and he once wrote 300 haiku for her.

Haruto Suzuki: A sunny, puppy-like type. Chestnut curly hair, a healing smile, and small fangs. Mainly plays jungle, specializing in rhythm-based heroes, a team facilitator, and a 15-star Challenger rank player. Note: He dated his childhood friend for three years, but they broke up when his family immigrated to Mishu. They still video chat once a week.

Tsuyoshi Takahashi: Dark-skinned, athletic type. Healthy complexion, well-defined muscles, and short, energetic hair. Mainly plays top lane, with an aggressive style and extremely strong lane dominance. Rank: Challenger 15 stars. Note: His ex-girlfriend was the president of the track and field club. During their relationship, he prepared a love-filled bento for her every day. Even after their breakup, she still referred to him as "the gentlest boyfriend."

Kenta Saeki: A muscular, fit male. He has a robust physique, a determined gaze, and well-defined features. He primarily plays support, possesses excellent protective instincts, and is decisive in initiating team fights. His rank is King 15 stars. Note: He dated his ex-girlfriend for two years, but they broke up due to their busy careers and limited time together. At the time of the breakup, he gave her an amulet engraved with the word "Wishing you peace."

Sun Rong looked at the four documents and was momentarily speechless.

This lineup...

Rather than an e-sports exchange, it's more like an exhibit that Ryoko Kumiya borrowed from some "Ideal Boyfriend Museum".

"Ryoko...isn't this a bit too much?" Sun Rong sighed, "It's just a training match, do we really need to find...four of them?"

"Of course we need it!"

Ryoko Kumiya confidently stated, "Stimulation requires layers and impact! A single handsome man might not be enough to evoke a strong sense of crisis, but the key is the visual and atmospheric impact of having four different types of high-quality men, all with stable dating experience, appearing around you and fighting alongside you!"

She paused, leaned closer to Sun Rong, lowered her voice, and gave Sun Rong a reason that she couldn't refuse: "Rongrong, think about it. You know Wang Ling's personality better than I do. This stubborn old man will be hard to get any results unless we use strong measures to break him down."

"This is a rare opportunity, and given the constraints imposed by my Jiugong family, plus the fact that these four individuals are of extremely high quality and will not overstep their bounds during the mission, their only responsibility is to display their charm and cooperate in the performance. Don't you want to see what other expressions Wang Ling will make in this situation?"

Sun Rong's heart began to beat faster involuntarily.

Of course she wanted to.

After returning from the inner world, there always seemed to be a thin veil between her and Wang Ling.

She could sense Wang Ling's protection, but she could never truly grasp the emotional pulse deep within his heart.

Curiosity, and the deep-seated expectation, ultimately triumphed over Sun Rong's last concerns.

"All right......"

Sun Rong nodded slightly, her cheeks flushed: "I agree."

Ryoko Kugimiya smiled triumphantly: "Very good. I'll arrange for them to set off immediately. The time is set for the afternoon of the day after tomorrow at the Dream Arena. I will personally oversee the event."

Meanwhile, after receiving news of Ryoko Kugimiya's "Plan," Chen Chao immediately began his own actions.

The first person he contacted was Zhuoyi, whom he now regarded as an "official cheat" in the Love Strategist Alliance.

The call connected, and Zhuo Yi's virtual projection appeared in Chen Chao's room. He seemed to be in the office of the Hundred Schools Headquarters, with a huge bookshelf and a sand table of Songhai City behind him.

"Master Zhuo!" Chen Chao exclaimed excitedly.

Upon hearing this, Zhuo Yi smiled and waved his hand: "Chen Chao, we are now comrades on the same side, so there's no need to be so formal with your address. If you don't mind, just call me Brother Zhuo."

"Yes, sir! Brother Zhuo!" Chen Chao readily complied, immediately changing his tune: "Jiugong has already taken care of things, Sun Rong has agreed. Now it's our turn to form teams! What do you think..."

Zhuo Yi smiled slightly, as if he had been prepared. He tapped his fingers lightly in the air a few times, and two encrypted messages were sent to Chen Chao's communicator.

"These are the personal contact details for Zhou Rui from Xuanming High School and Liu Kaichuan from Chihuo High School. In addition, as the director of the Hundred Schools Administration, I have extended an invitation to them to 'assist in the e-sports exchange activities of Number 60 High School,' and they will not refuse. As for how to persuade them to willingly become tools, that's up to you."

"Brother Zhuo is awesome!" Chen Chao cheered.

With this official endorsement, things became much easier.

He first contacted Zhou Rui from Xuanming High School.

On the projection screen, Zhou Rui's tone carried a hint of caution: "Chen Chao? The Outstanding Governor of the Hundred Schools Administration has indeed conveyed the message. May I ask what the specific arrangements are for your school's exchange activity this time? Who is the opponent?"

Clearly, after experiencing the fear of being dominated by Wang Ling last time, Zhou Rui has become more cautious.

Chen Chao chuckled and dropped his trump card: "Their opponents are a team led by Sun Rong. They've brought in four foreign players from Sun Island's Ninth Road High School. As for us..."

He paused deliberately, then lowered his voice: "ADC is Wang Ling."

"Wang Ling?!" Zhou Rui's eyes lit up instantly: "Specific time and place?"

"The day after tomorrow afternoon, Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group, Dream Esports Arena."

"Okay! I'll be there on time!" Zhou Rui agreed without hesitation.

Next, Chen Chao contacted Liu Kaichuan from Chihuo High School.

When Liu Kaichuan's virtual projection appeared, his face still carried a hint of lingering gloom, clearly indicating that he hadn't fully recovered from being dominated by Jax in the top lane.

"Chen Chao? What's wrong? If it's just words of comfort, then forget it."

"President Liu, what are you talking about? Do you think I'm that kind of person?" Chen Chao said with a grin, "There's a chance for revenge... oh no, I mean, a chance for exchange and learning. The day after tomorrow afternoon, at the Fantasy Esports Arena, we're playing an offline 5v5 match against Sun Rong's team. They've brought in four foreign players from Sun Island. Our ADC is Wang Ling."
