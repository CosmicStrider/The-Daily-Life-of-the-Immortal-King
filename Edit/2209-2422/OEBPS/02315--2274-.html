Chapter 2274 Wang Ling's Nonsense

Palm Cliff Base, Exit of Evacuation Passage.

The silver light dissipated, the intense feeling of weightlessness faded, and the sensation of being grounded returned.

The air that filled everyone's nostrils was no longer the sweltering, sulfurous, and primal psionic air of the inner world, but rather the refreshing scent filtered by the base's internal circulation system.

"We're back...we're really back!" Chen Chao shouted first, so excited he almost jumped up, but he forgot that his leg injury had just healed and he stumbled, only to be quickly supported by Guo Hao next to him.

"Be careful! Don't lie back down right after you've just come out!" Guo Hao complained, but he couldn't hide the relief on his face.

The students from Number 60 High School, Li Changzhe from Number 8 High School in Beijing, Zhang Linyan from Dishitian High School, Su Xingyue, and the remaining students from the Holy Science Academy walked out of the passage one after another.

Everyone looked exhausted, their clothes were more or less torn, and they were covered with dust from the earth's core and traces of battle.

But their eyes mostly shone with the light of surviving a disaster.

The intensity of the inner Earth was terrifying, leaving everyone with lingering fear when they recalled it, and they couldn't even find the right adjectives to describe their feelings.

The medical team, which had been waiting in the exit hall, immediately rushed over, and various testing instruments and healing auras swept over the crowd.

"Vitality signs are stable!"

"Your spiritual energy is severely depleted; you need to rest!"

"There are multiple external injuries; treat them immediately!"

The medical staff skillfully carried out triage and initial treatment.

Injured players such as Chen Chao and Guo Hao received special attention.

Su Xingyue helped a pale-faced student from Shengke and gently comforted him.

While undergoing the examination, Li Changzhe painfully touched his dull-looking armor.

He was on the verge of tears: "My Myriad Scale Armor... it's burned out!"

Zhang Linyan cautiously surveyed her surroundings, as if she had not yet fully recovered from the tension that had gripped her from the depths of the earth.

Wang Ling followed at the end of the group, his expression the calmest among them all. However, he knew very well that his flawless appearance would likely arouse suspicion, so before stepping out of the Earth's core, he secretly smeared some mineral dust from the Earth's core on his face.

A medical worker scanned him several times with a scanning device, his brows furrowing deeper and deeper: "Strange... Foundation Establishment stage? His spiritual power level is almost that of an ordinary person? But his physical data is abnormally perfect and harmonious, with no internal or external injuries, and even his mental fluctuations are as stable as a stagnant pool... He only has a layer of gray on his face, how is this possible?"

Wang Ling remained expressionless, his face showing complete composure.

Sun Rong stood not far from him. Hearing the medical staff's mutterings, the corners of her mouth turned up slightly, but she quickly suppressed it.

She was actually in very good condition. After returning, the power of the Ocean Sword Core and the Earth Core Crimson Lotus seemed to resonate more harmoniously with the Earth's environment. Her spiritual power not only fully recovered, but even improved.

But she still cooperated with the examination, her gaze constantly and subtly fixed on Wang Ling to make sure he was safe and sound.

Soon, Dean Jing Heqiu arrived with a group of base officials.

Welcome back! You've worked hard!

Jing Heqiu's voice was filled with genuine relief and lingering fear: "All personnel should immediately proceed to the advanced medical area for in-depth examinations and recovery! The base has prepared the best resources for you!"

His gaze swept across the crowd, lingering for a moment on Wang Ling, his expression filled with complex emotions that were hard to conceal.

The contrast between the completely out-of-control monitoring data and high-level energy reactions in the later stages of Earth's core and the excessively calm boy in front of him was truly shocking.

Everyone else had some injuries, but Wang Ling... was covered in dust.

Teng Luchen did not come to the exit in person, but everyone knew that he must be in the core hall, watching everything through countless surveillance cameras.

On the suspended passageway leading to the medical area, everyone remained silent for a long time; the heaviness experienced in the Earth's core could not be dispelled in a short time.

"Lingzi, do you think... we've done something meritorious?" Chen Chao leaned against the suspended stretcher and asked Wang Ling, who was walking beside him, in a low voice.

Wang Ling glanced at him but didn't say anything.

Guo Hao chimed in, "Of course it counts! But why do I feel like I've been through a dream, and I can't remember many of the details..."

This is Wang Ling's subtle 50% amnesia technique.

This directly obscured the memories of everyone involved in the Earth's core event, including the Glass Clan, the Underworld Clan, the Mountain Moving Clan, the Ancient Earth Spirit, and Bai Zhe's collusion with the Outer Gods. Leaking even a trace of these things would cause Wang Ling great trouble.

Apart from a few people who knew the truth, such as Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, Sun Rong, and Liu Qingyi... Wang Ling had no choice but to blur this memory.

However, there is also a reasonable scientific explanation for it.

A monk who has experienced such a life-or-death battle often suffers from "post-traumatic stress disorder," which causes him to have a vague memory of what he experienced, thus making him forget the feeling of being on the verge of death.

"It's good enough that you survived." Li Changzhe sighed, stroking his armor with heartache. "It's just that the price was a bit high."

Zhang Linyan then looked at Sun Rong, Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, and the others: "The subsequent reports... I'm afraid they will be difficult to write."

She was alluding to those "good luck" and "coincidences" that cannot be explained by common sense.

And for some reason, after coming out of the inner Earth, many of my memories started to blur...

Wang Zhen smiled lazily: "Just write the truth, say we're lucky, that God has blessed us with good fortune." He glanced at Wang Ling, who was looking down at his fingers, seemingly studying the thin slice of seasoning powder that had just touched them.

Fang Xing succinctly replied, "Agreed."

Sun Rong nodded slightly: "Yes, we said... that we supported each other, were lucky enough to complete the mission, and encountered incomprehensible abnormal phenomena."

Her statement both blurred the focus and left a reasonable buffer zone for any abnormalities that Wang Ling might expose.

The premium medical area consists of private rooms with luxurious facilities and a vibrant atmosphere.

Wang Ling was placed in a separate room.

As soon as the door closed, cutting off outside observation, the "weak Foundation Establishment stage" expression on Wang Ling's face vanished instantly. Although he still had little expression, his aura became incredibly profound and distant.

He walked to the bedside, sat down, took out a brand new packet of black pepper instant noodles from his pocket, and skillfully tore it open.

This flavor is still the most reliable.

Just as he pinched her twice, a soft knock came from outside the door, followed by Sun Rong's voice: "Wang Ling, are you alright? I... can I come in?"

Wang Ling paused for a moment, scanned the room with his mind, and confirmed that only Sun Rong was outside the door. He quickly stuffed the bag of instant noodles under the blanket, and his face instantly returned to its slightly tired and bewildered "weak" state before he whispered, "Come in."

It wasn't to guard against Sun Rong, but because Wang Ling knew that Teng Luchen was definitely monitoring him, so he had to pretend.

Sun Rong pushed open the door and came in, carrying a plate of sliced spiritual fruit. Her face was full of concern: "The medical team said you need to replenish your spiritual energy and vitamins, so I brought you some."

Her gaze swept quickly across the room, but she didn't find anything unusual. She just felt that the spiritual energy in the air seemed particularly gentle.

"Thank you." Wang Ling took the fruit plate, his fingers inadvertently touching Sun Rong's fingertips.

Sun Rong felt as if she had been stung by a weak electric current, and quickly withdrew her hand, her cheeks slightly flushed.
