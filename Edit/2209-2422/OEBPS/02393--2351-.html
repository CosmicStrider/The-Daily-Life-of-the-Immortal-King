Chapter 2351 Shura Field

At this moment, the air inside the Dream Esports Arena seemed to freeze.

Wang Ling had his hands in his pockets and his eyelids drooping. When his gaze swept over the direction where Sun Rong was, his steps unconsciously paused for a fraction of a second.

Surrounding Sun Rong were four boys, each with a distinct style but equally eye-catching.

Ren Fujiwara, the president of the Kyudo High School e-sports club, has black hair, light eyes, and a cool and aloof demeanor.

He was handing a cup of warm spiritual juice to Sun Rong, his voice gentle: "Senior Sun Rong, it's easy to get thirsty after warming up for a long time. This juice is made from green berries, a specialty of Sun Island, and it can soothe your nerves."

Beside him, Haruto Suzuki, with his chestnut-brown curly hair, smiled brightly, revealing his signature little fangs, and dangled a custom-made wristband in his hand: "Senpai! This is a lucky wristband I brought specially. It's engraved with runes that enhance focus! It'll feel amazing when you wear it later!"

On the other side, Gao Qiaogang, with his dark skin and bulging muscles, remained silent but had already adjusted the seat height and armrest angle of the gaming cabin in front of Sun Rong to the optimal position, confirming that everything was correct.

Meanwhile, the composed Kenta Saeki stood a little further away, his gaze quickly sweeping across the entire layout. He whispered to Sun Rong, "Senior, based on my divination today, I suggest you choose a camera position on the east side. The feng shui there is better, and you won't be easily disturbed."

The four had a clear division of labor and showed great care, making Sun Rong feel like a princess surrounded by stars.

Chen Chao watched from the side with a wildly wide smile: "Holy crap! Liangzi's move! Amazing! Four top-tier handsome men providing all-around service, the visual impact, the atmosphere... Lingzi, let's see how long you can keep up this!"

He nudged Li Changzhe beside him with his elbow.

Although Li Changzhe's skill level is only at the diamond level, he is not confused about interpersonal relationships.

He squinted and transmitted his voice to Chen Chao: "Brother Chao, what's this all about? I smell a vinegar factory opening for business... Wang Ling and Sun Rong..."

Chen Chao chuckled and replied telepathically, "Chang Zhe, you see through it but don't say anything. Just wait and see the show. This training match today is full of juicy gossip, guaranteed to be sweet!"

Meanwhile, Liu Kaichuan and Zhou Rui, who were meeting Wang Ling in person for the first time, were caught in another complex emotion.

Liu Kaichuan was originally eager to make a good relationship with the great Wang Ling today, and it would be best if he could recruit Chi Huo as an outside helper.

But when he actually stood in front of Wang Ling, the temperature of the air inexplicably dropped a few degrees.

He found that his legs were actually a little weak.

Zhou Rui pushed up his black-rimmed glasses, trying to use the calmness of a data analyst to resist the discomfort brought on by this intense battleground.

He silently observed Wang Ling, his heart filled with even more suspicion.

It felt like the temperature around this powerful figure, Wang Ling, was a few degrees lower than elsewhere.

The two club presidents, who were once powerful figures in the high school e-sports scene in Songhai City, were now as helpless as children in front of Wang Ling, with even their prepared words stuck in their throats.

...

The monitoring room is located on the second floor of the esports arena, behind a one-way mirror.

Zhuo Yi crossed his legs, holding a cup of spiritual tea, and watched the "spectacle" in the venue below with a smile.

Beside him, Wang Zhen stroked his chin, his eyes filled with excitement, while Liu Qingyi stood with her arms crossed, pouting and saying, "They've made quite a show of it. I wonder if Master Ling's blockhead can be enlightened."

Behind them was a group of professionals in white coats with serious expressions.

These were all achieved by Kugimiya Ryoko using the resources of the Kugimiya family to bring in a team of experts from the top psychology institute on Sun Island. They were specifically responsible for capturing Wang Ling's micro-expressions, body language, and even the subtle fluctuations of the surrounding spiritual energy through high-definition cameras and conducting real-time analysis.

"When the target's pupils made eye contact with Ms. Sun Rong, they contracted for 0.3 seconds, and there was a slight twitching of the cheekbone muscles, lasting approximately 0.1 seconds..."

A chief analyst wearing wire-rimmed glasses stared intently at the data stream on the screen, speaking rapidly: "At the same time, a faint spiritual energy fluctuation was detected within three meters of the target, consistent with the characteristics of emotional fluctuations..."

Wang Zhen leaned over and glanced at the dense data, asking curiously, "Does this mean that the True Man has reacted?"

The lead analyst pushed up his glasses, a hint of confusion in his voice: "There was indeed a reaction. But this fluctuation... is very peculiar. As soon as it appeared, it was instantly smoothed out by a stronger suppressive force, and the data quickly returned to baseline, as if something had forcibly formatted his emotions. This is a case we have never seen before."

Zhuo Yi took a sip of tea, a playful smile playing on his lips: "It's not that simple after all. Master's state of mind is no longer something that can be easily shaken by mortal emotions. But... as long as there's a reaction, even if it's just for a moment, it proves that we're on the right track."

Liu Qingyi snorted: "So, you mean he ate the vinegar, but digested it as soon as he tasted it? What kind of talk is that?"

Zhuo Yi had a playful smile on his lips, his gaze sharp.

He glanced at the four Sun Island boys on the monitor who were still "dutifully" playing the perfect teammate, a shrewd glint in his eyes: "Notify Ryoko to proceed according to plan. During the match, you can appropriately increase the level of interaction."

Zhuo Yi gave the order.

...

Downstairs, the awkward atmosphere was finally broken by Ryoko Kumiya.

She walked to the center of the venue, dressed in a smart women's suit and carrying a folding fan.

"Alright, now that everyone's here, both teams, take your positions. Friendship first, competition second, and of course, winner takes all." Her gaze swept over both sides, lingering especially on Wang Ling's face for a moment, before announcing: "5V5 custom draft mode, best of three to determine the winner. Ready to enter the equipment!"

Both teams headed to their designated psionic gaming cabins.

Before entering the cabin, Sun Rong couldn't help but look back at Wang Ling one last time, only to see him walking straight to his cabin, his back still calm, as if nothing had happened.

She felt a slight sense of disappointment mixed with a hint of expectation. She took a deep breath and lay down inside the cabin.

Wang Ling's team composition is as follows: Top laner Liu Kaichuan, jungler Zhou Rui, mid laner Li Changzhe, ADC Wang Ling, and support Chen Chao.

Sun Rong's team composition: Top laner Takahashi Tsuyoshi, Jungler Suzuki Haruto, Mid laner Fujiwara Ren, ADC Sun Rong (60 points), Support Saeki Kenta.

The two parties' consciousnesses were connected, and a huge holographic projection screen lit up in the center of the venue, displaying the hero selection interface!

The hero banning phase has begun.

The blue team, led by Sun Rong, took the initiative without hesitation, immediately banning Draven, the champion Wang Ling had played brilliantly!

Next up, the second ban is [Ezreal]!

The third ban is for Ashe!

The fourth ban is for [Jinx, the Unholy Phantom]!

The fifth ban is for [Glory Executioner]!

All five bans were targeted at Wang Ling! They banned almost all the strong ADC heroes he had recently used!

"Holy crap! That's ruthless!" Chen Chao exclaimed in the voice chat, "Lingzi, you've really drawn that hatred far too far! They're going to target you relentlessly!"

Liu Kaichuan and Zhou Rui's expressions also hardened.

This level of targeting is rare even in professional competitions, clearly indicating that the other party is extremely wary of Wang Ling.

Li Changzhe clicked his tongue: "Wang Ling, do you... have enough heroes in your pool?"

Everyone looked at Wang Ling on the fifth floor.

Wang Ling's gaze slowly moved across the hero list, his face remaining expressionless.

Just as the countdown was about to end, his cursor stopped on the head of a Yodl.

He was a hero carrying a giant bomb.
