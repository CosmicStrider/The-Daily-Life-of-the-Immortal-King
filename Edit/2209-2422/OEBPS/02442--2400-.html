Chapter 2400 The Promise in the Time Chamber

At the restaurant, Wang Ling ordered a set meal for three people.

While eating, Chen Chao excitedly planned: "After dinner, let's go to the 'Heart to Heart Bridge' first. I heard that project is very suitable for couples, and the scenery is beautiful. Then we'll go to the 'Time Capsule House' to record what we want to say. We can get stamps from both projects, and they're not tiring."

Sun Rong sipped her soup, her ears turning red again.

Wang Ling focused intently on his meal, seemingly unresponsive.

"Oh, right, Wang Ling." Chen Chao suddenly remembered something: "Someone slipped a strange card into my room last night."

Wang Ling looked up: "Hmm?"

"He said he wanted to know your true identity and asked me to meet him at the Horror Castle at 11 PM tonight." Chen Chao scoffed, "I'll just tear him up. What kind of person is he, all mysterious?"

Wang Ling paused for a moment with his chopsticks.

Sun Rong frowned: "Could it be Luo Xinghe?"

"Possibly," Chen Chao sneered. "That kid lost the match and vomited blood, he's definitely not happy about it and wants to cause trouble. But I'm not falling for it."

Wang Ling continued eating without saying a word.

But his senses spread out and enveloped the entire restaurant.

There were no abnormal energy fluctuations.

It seems the person who slipped the cards last night didn't come today.

Or rather... they came along, but because of the Misfortune Realm, they're currently having a run of bad luck and don't have time to cause trouble.

"Never mind him." Chen Chao waved his hand. "Let's do our own thing. We'll collect all the stamps this afternoon, watch the fireworks tonight, perfect!"

After lunch, the three rested for a while before setting off for the "Heart to Heart Bridge" at 1:30 p.m.

That's a glass walkway in the eastern part of the park, spanning an artificial lake, and about 200 meters long.

The bridge surface is made of transparent reinforced glass, allowing you to see the lake water and fish directly below when you walk on it.

The bridge is equipped with sensors on both sides. When two people walk side by side, different light and shadow effects will be projected on both sides of the bridge according to the synchronization of their steps and the distance between them.

The official tagline is: "Walk with the people who matter, and witness moments of mutual understanding."

There were many people in line, mostly couples.

Chen Chao stood in the queue, watching the couples ahead of him walk hand in hand onto the bridge, where pink heart-shaped lights immediately illuminated both sides. He couldn't help but rub his hands together and said, "Lingzi, Sun Rong, when you walk later, remember to stay close together! Keep your steps in sync! That way, you can trigger the best special effects!"

Sun Rong looked at the transparent bridge, feeling a little nervous.

Wang Ling glanced at the bridge's structure.

The bridge isn't actually very high; it just has some illusionary effects added to the glass interlayer to create fear and tension, but the design itself is safe.

However, Wang Ling could still sense that one of the bridge's support pillars had been altered.

Someone must have tampered with it last night or this morning.

Is it that killer?

Wang Ling remained expressionless, his fingertips twitching slightly.

An invisible spiritual force silently spread out, enveloping the tampered support pillar.

No matter what kind of trap it is, it won't work under his nose.

The procession moved forward slowly.

When it was their turn, a staff member smiled and asked, "Would the three of you like to walk together?"

"No, no, no!" Chen Chao quickly waved his hand, "They go, I'll follow behind!"

The staff member understood: "Okay. Please put on the synchronization bracelets."

Another bracelet.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong put them on.

This time, the bracelet has more complex functions. It can not only measure heart rate and mood, but also monitor the distance between two people, the frequency of their steps, and even the number of times their eyes meet.

"Once you're on the bridge, please try to stay in sync," the staff member said. "The bridge's sensors will project different lights and shadows based on how well you two are in sync. If you reach the 'Perfect Match' level, you'll even get a hidden stamp!"

"A hidden seal?!" Chen Chao's eyes lit up: "Go for it, guys!"

Wang Ling nodded and took the lead onto the bridge.

Sun Rong stood beside him, about thirty centimeters apart.

The bridge surface is transparent, and you can see the emerald green lake water and swimming koi below when you look down.

Because of the illusion, Sun Rong was initially afraid to look down, but after taking a few steps, she found that it wasn't so scary after all.

Sunlight streamed through the glass, casting shimmering ripples on the lake's surface.

The wind blows, bringing moisture and the fragrance of flowers.

"The scenery is beautiful," Sun Rong said softly.

Wang Ling hummed in agreement and slowed his pace to match her speed.

The two walked side by side on the transparent glass bridge, their shadows overlapping beneath their feet.

The sensors on both sides of the bridge started working.

At first, it was a faint blue halo, which gradually turned into a soft pale gold as their steps synchronized.

After walking about fifty meters, the color of the light began to change.

Wang Ling noticed that Sun Rong's gaze kept drifting towards him, and she quickly looked away.

He thought for a moment and then spoke up: "Look over there."

Sun Rong looked in the direction he pointed.

To the right of the bridge, in the center of the lake, there is a small island covered with luminous spiritual plants, which are now swaying in the wind, scattering specks of light dust.

"So beautiful..." Sun Rong was mesmerized.

Just then, the light and shadow on both sides of the bridge suddenly changed!

It transformed from pale gold to a dazzling rainbow of colors, with countless points of light converging in the air to form two overlapping heart-shaped patterns!

"Wow!" exclaimed the tourists watching from the bridgehead.

"The 'Heart to Heart' effect was triggered so quickly?!"

"They've only walked less than a hundred meters!"

Chen Chao waved excitedly at the bridgehead: "Yes, yes, yes! That's it! Hold on!"

Sun Rong blushed as she looked at the two glowing hearts in the sky.

Wang Ling remained calm.

He had just figured out the trigger condition for this effect: it would activate when both people's attention was focused on the same beautiful scenery and they were in a good mood.

It's very simple.

But Sun Rong didn't know that.

She thought this was proof of their tacit understanding.

I felt a sweet warmth in my heart.

The two continued walking forward.

When we reached the middle of the bridge, something unexpected happened!

"Snap—"

A soft cracking sound came from beneath my feet!

Sun Rong looked down and was horrified to find a crack in the glass beneath her feet!

Moreover, the cracks are spreading rapidly!

"Wang Ling! The glass is cracked!" she exclaimed in shock.

Chen Chao, who was at the bridgehead, also saw it and his expression changed drastically: "What's going on?!"

The staff panicked: "That's impossible! This glass can withstand 200,000 kilograms of pressure, how could it crack?!"

The tourists were in an uproar.

Sun Rong instinctively tried to back away, but Wang Ling grabbed her wrist.

"Don't move," he said calmly.

Sun Rong felt the warmth from her wrist, and her panicked heart instantly calmed down.

She trusted Wang Ling, and then subconsciously took the initiative, grabbing Wang Ling's arm.

Wang Ling was taken aback.

At this moment, the cracks in the glass were still spreading, making a teeth-grinding "crack" sound.

Strangely, the cracks stopped spreading to Wang Ling's feet.

It's like being blocked by an invisible barrier.

Wang Ling looked down at the cracks beneath his feet.

He sensed that the cracks originated from the tampered support pillar beneath the bridge.

Someone installed explosive runes there, which are remotely triggered.

But the explosive rune was just wrapped in his spiritual power, so it shouldn't be effective.

Unless... someone forcibly triggers it a second time, even at the cost of the rune self-destructing, just to create an accident.

That's ruthless.

Wang Ling's eyes turned cold for a moment.
