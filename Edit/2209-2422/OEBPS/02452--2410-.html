Chapter 2410 Wang Ling's Run-and-Run Style

Chen Chao sat on the sink, staring at the bank transfer notification on his phone screen.

Just now, the other 500,000 spirit stones that Luo Xinghe promised were deposited into his anonymous account in full.

The string of zeros following the number made him dizzy.

"They've really come..." he murmured.

Guo Hao leaned over and glanced at it, then chuckled, "It seems that although Luo Xinghe is unwilling, he still keeps his word."

"Keep your word, my foot!" Li Changzhe scoffed. "He thinks money can solve everything. What are you planning to do with this money?"

Chen Chao stared at the screen for a few seconds, then turned off his phone, jumped off the sink, and dusted off his pants: "Donating it to Yanran Angel Hospital can save a lot of children. Consider it Luo Xinghe doing something worthwhile."

Guo Hao nodded, agreeing with this approach.

"However..." he changed the subject, "Luo Xinghe won't let this go so easily. He spent so much money and only got a Foundation Establishment stage result; he'll definitely suspect there's a problem with the system, or that we tampered with it."

Chen Chao's heart tightened: "What should we do?"

"Let's wait and see," Guo Hao said. "The match is about to start, let's go watch Wang Ling's match first. We'll talk about it on the spot."

The three left the restroom and headed towards the waiting area in Zone A.

The corridor was already packed with people, with participants and their teachers from various schools making their final preparations.

The announcer's voice came over the loudspeaker, reminding the participants to enter their respective battle zones according to their assigned numbers.

Chen Chao tiptoed, searching for Wang Ling's figure in the crowd.

Soon, he spotted that familiar figure in a corner of Area A.

Wang Lingzheng was leaning against the wall, holding a bag of instant noodles in his hand, eating them in small bites.

"Wang Ling!" Chen Chao squeezed forward: "Isn't it your turn to go on stage soon?"

Wang Ling looked up and glanced at the time displayed on his bracelet: "Hmm, 2:10, E-03 battle arena."

"Who is the opponent?" Li Changzhe asked.

"Li Haoran, Songhai Number 3 High School," Wang Ling announced the name in a calm tone.

Chen Chao quickly took out his phone to check the information.

This was Guo Hao's homework, which he had done in advance by collecting information on all possible participants.

"Li Haoran, mid-stage Foundation Establishment, specializing in swordsmanship, was among the top eight in last year's city-wide youth swordsmanship competition..."

As he read the information, his brows furrowed: "Lingzi, this opponent is not weak."

Theoretically, the middle stage of Foundation Building is one small realm different from the early stage.

Moreover, the opponent is a swordsmanship expert with rich combat experience.

Wang Ling simply gave a calm "hmm".

Looking at his calm demeanor, Chen Chao suddenly remembered the results displayed by the scanner.

Initial stage of foundation building.

If this result is true...

"Lingzi, how about..." Chen Chao hesitated for a moment, "How about we just admit defeat? It's just an exchange match anyway, winning or losing doesn't matter."

He was genuinely worried.

If Wang Ling really only has the cultivation level of the early Foundation Establishment stage, he is likely to be injured when facing a sword cultivator at the mid-Foundation Establishment stage.

Although he knew that Wang Ling had some special methods, such as that talisman and the strange good luck that occasionally appeared...

But that was a competition, and under the rules, many methods couldn't be used.

Wang Ling glanced at Chen Chao and shook his head: "It's okay, just play normally."

Actually, Wang Ling was somewhat moved to see how much effort Chen Chao and the other two had put into their affairs.

But it's just a small competition, so it's not a big problem.

After Wang Ling finished speaking, he walked directly towards the battle arena.

Chen Chao wanted to follow, but was stopped by a staff member: "Student, this is the contestant entrance, no unauthorized personnel allowed."

He could only watch helplessly as Wang Ling's figure disappeared at the end of the passage.

"What should we do?" Li Changzhe asked anxiously, "What if Wang Ling really can't win?"

Guo Hao stared at the aisle, remained silent for a few seconds, and then said, "Let's go to the audience seats. Where is platform E-03?"

"The best view is from the third row of section B," Chen Chao recalled Sun Rong's earlier comment. "Sun Rong is there too."

"Walk."

...

Section B, third row.

Sun Rong gripped the energy drink bottle tightly, her eyes fixed on the E-03 battle stage below.

The stage was not large, about twenty meters square, and the ground was covered with specially made cushioning material. There was a transparent spiritual energy shield around it to prevent the aftershocks of the battle from injuring the audience.

At this moment, a tall boy wearing the uniform of Songhai Number 3 High School was standing on the stage, holding a standard training sword and doing warm-up exercises.

It was Li Haoran.

"Rongrong, don't be nervous." Ryoko Kugimiya's voice came from the side.

Sun Rong turned her head and saw that Jiu Gong Liangzi and Zhuo Yi had sat down next to her at some point.

"Senior Sister Jiugong, Senior Brother Zhuoyi," she greeted softly.

"Come watch Wang Ling's match?" Zhuo Yi smiled and said, "Don't worry, he'll be fine."

Sun Rong nodded, but her hand still gripped the bottle tightly.

She wasn't actually worried that Wang Ling would lose.

She was worried that... Wang Ling would be exposed.

Although she didn't know how strong Wang Ling really was, it would be troublesome if he accidentally used power beyond the early stage of Foundation Establishment during the competition and was discovered by the referee or the monitoring system.

"The match is about to begin." Ryoko Kumiya pointed to the audience below the stage.

The host's voice was amplified and carried throughout the venue:

"E-03 Arena, Wang Ling from Number 60 High School versus Li Haoran from Songhai Number 3 High School. Both sides, prepare—"

Wang Ling walked out of the passage and slowly stepped onto the battle stage.

He was still wearing his school uniform, not his combat uniform, and he wasn't carrying any weapons.

Li Haoran frowned: "Classmate, where's your weapon?"

Wang Ling thought for a moment, then pulled out an unopened packet of instant noodles from his pocket: "This one."

Li Haoran: "???"

A burst of laughter erupted from the audience.

"He used instant noodles as a weapon?"

"Is this a joke?"

"Is Number 60 High School out of people? They sent out such an oddball..."

Chen Chao, watching anxiously from the audience, exclaimed, "What is Lingzi doing?! At least bring a training sword!"

Guo Hao seemed thoughtful: "Perhaps... he has his own plans."

On stage, even the referee couldn't stand it anymore: "Wang Ling, the competition rules allow the use of standard weapons. Are you sure you want to use... snacks as weapons?"

Wang Ling nodded: "Mm."

The referee's lips twitched, but since the rules didn't prohibit snacks, he had no choice but to announce, "The game begins!"

As soon as he finished speaking, Li Haoran moved.

He shifted his stance, his figure shooting out like an arrow, the training sword in his hand flashing a cold light as it pierced straight towards Wang Ling's chest!

This sword strike was extremely fast and pierced through the angle, clearly a deadly move that had been practiced countless times.

Exclamations erupted from the audience.

Sun Rong subconsciously covered her mouth.

But the next second, everyone was stunned.

Wang Ling took a step to the side.

Just one step.

Not too much, not too little, just enough to avoid the tip of the sword.

Li Haoran's sword grazed the hem of his clothes and missed, causing him to stumble due to the excessive force.

"A coincidence?" Li Haoran steadied himself, a hint of doubt flashing in his eyes.

He refused to believe it and drew his sword again.

This time it was a triple stab, the sword shadows raining down on Wang Ling's vital points.

Wang Ling made another move.

He moved half a step to the left, took a step back, and then turned to the side.

All three swords missed.

The movements were very small, but each time they were precisely timed to the gaps in the sword's stance, avoiding the attack with minimal movement.

The audience was silent for a few seconds, then erupted into even louder discussion.

"This footwork..."

"It doesn't seem like a coincidence!"
