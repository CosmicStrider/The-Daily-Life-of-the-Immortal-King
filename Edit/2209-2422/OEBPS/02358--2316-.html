Chapter 2316 The Spirit's Favoritism

On the shore of Liuli Lake, the air seemed to freeze.

The little girl, the embodiment of the spirit of the secret realm, maintained the aloof demeanor of a spirit as her seven-colored eyes, which had originally held a sleepy look and a touch of majesty, swept over the crowd by the lake.

As an existence that has been asleep for thousands of years, she needs to maintain her dignity, especially knowing that there are many magical artifacts from the outside world aimed at this place. As the spirit of the secret realm, it is very important to create a sense of prestige.

"Wang Ling, what exactly is the essence of the spirit of the secret realm?" Sun Rong asked telepathically.

This knowledge is actually quite complicated to explain.

But since the person asking the question was Sun Rong, Wang Ling almost subconsciously gave her preferential treatment.

With a thought, without any communication, a piece of ancient knowledge that had already been organized was transmitted into Sun Rong's mind.

There are many types of cultivation realms.

Its basic spatial properties are two.

Firstly, there are naturally formed spaces, which usually contain many rare and precious materials that are extinct in the outside world, and the concentration of spiritual energy is dozens of times that of the outside world.

Secondly, these are spaces created by ancient cultivators through artificial means. These secret realms are mostly secluded cultivation places for ancient cultivators. During their seclusion, which can last for hundreds or even thousands of years, ancient cultivators would often create their own comfortable space inside to ensure the smooth progress of their cultivation, and even leave behind many magic arrays and magical artifacts. After they successfully emerged from seclusion, the secret realm space was left behind for later generations to explore.

The Azure Netherworld is a rather special case, possessing both of these characteristics.

First it was a natural secret realm, and then it was opened up and explored by an ancient cultivator. In the modern scientific cultivation world, the exploration value of such special secret realms is often greater.

As for the spirits of the secret realm, they are a kind of spirit born from the spiritual energy of heaven and earth based on the attributes of the secret realm, and their own cultivation is at least a thousand years.

Let's rewind a few dozen seconds.

The little girl's gaze first fell on the ancient jade in Zhao Qian's hand, and she nodded slightly, which was considered as acknowledging the validity of the token.

Then, she looked at the 60th squad led by Sun Rong, her eyes calm and unwavering, preparing to announce the test rules according to the established procedure.

However, when her gaze inadvertently swept over the boy standing behind Sun Rong, who was eating instant noodles, she noticed something.

A voice suddenly rang out in her mind.

"Liuli".

Wang Ling actually called out her real name directly through telepathy!

"Buzz!"

Liuli's entire spirit body trembled violently! This was a genuine sense of dread!

In her perception, the seemingly ordinary boy's body was like an immeasurable abyss, like a universe that contained all the stars in the sky!

A supreme will that transcends the rules of her realm and is incomprehensible to her made her, the spirit of the realm, feel a tremor from the depths of her soul!

What...what is this thing?!

Why did you appear in my secret realm?!

Liuli screamed wildly inside, and the feigned maturity on her face instantly crumbled, leaving only visible terror.

She even subconsciously shrank back a little, her little hands tightly gripping the hem of her colorful glass dress, as if that would give her a little more sense of security.

Wang Ling simply looked at her calmly, and even tilted his head slightly because he thought the little girl's reaction was a bit exaggerated.

It was this subtle movement that almost caused Liuli's spirit to disintegrate on the spot.

Is the boss unhappy with me?!

I...I...I didn't offend him, did I?!

"Lord Jingling?" Zhao Qian looked at the spirit of the secret realm whose face had suddenly turned pale, full of doubt, and couldn't help but ask a question.

This is completely different from the scene he had envisioned when the spirit of the realm solemnly announced the test!

Liuli was startled awake by Zhao Qian's voice. She forcibly suppressed her fear, and her small chest heaved a few times.

She dared not look at Wang Ling anymore, and tried to focus her attention on Sun Rong and Zhao Qian, but her tone had unconsciously become somewhat cautious.

"Since... now that both sides are present, the triple test will begin. This test will be best two out of three!" Liuli's voice trembled slightly.

She cleared her throat, trying to regain some authority: "The first test is the steadfastness of one's character!"

With a wave of her small hand, the waters of Liuli Lake surged and condensed into two enormous gates above the lake, resembling mirrors.

Inside the doorway, light shimmered, obscuring the view of the interior.

"This is the [Refining Heart Corridor]."

Liuli explained, glancing furtively at Wang Ling. Seeing no reaction from him, she continued, "Within the corridor, the fears, anxieties, obsessions, and other negative emotions of the intruders will be reflected and amplified, forming illusions of inner demons. The test is simple: the team that can get through the corridor and reach the [Mind-Clearing Platform] on the other side the fastest wins."

The rules have been announced.

Zhao Qian's lips curled into a cold smile.

Inner demons and illusions?

Qingyun High School was well prepared!

He glanced at the short teammate next to him, who nodded slightly.

"We're going ahead!"

Without further hesitation, Zhao Qian, along with his four teammates, confidently stepped through the mirrored doorway that belonged to them.

"Let's go too."

Sun Rong took a deep breath, and the Aohai chime in her hand emitted a faint, clear sound. She was the first to step through another doorway.

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Jun followed closely behind.

Wang Ling was the last one to go in.

Before stepping through the door, Wang Ling glanced in the direction of the spirit of the secret realm and gave it a brief but friendly look.

That gaze made Jing Ling Liuli's heart clench again.

What does this mean, sir?

Do you think my test was too frivolous?!

...

Inside the Heart-Refining Corridor.

The five members of the 60th squad suddenly found themselves in an endless passageway.

The light walls on both sides presented a distorted illusion.

Various noisy whispers and terrifying hallucinations began to assault their minds.

Chen Chao recalled the scene of his cultivation failing to break through and being ridiculed by countless people.

Guo Hao, on the other hand, fantasized that his own spirit beast farm would go bankrupt because of his own poor decision-making.

Li Jun faced the vicious online attacks that accused him of plagiarism. Countless insults were amplified a thousandfold, surging in like a tsunami.

"Concentrate! Circulate the Clarity Mantra!" Sun Rong shouted in a low voice.

She manipulated the Olympus sword, causing it to emit a deep blue aura that formed a protective shield, enveloping the team members within it.

After calming himself down, Li Jun immediately began to recite the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" with all his might.

The pure light of the mantra blended with the blue light of the ocean, greatly offsetting the spiritual erosion of the environment.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao immediately felt the pressure ease and calmed their minds, focusing intently on their spiritual realm.

"This illusion is somewhat clever, but it doesn't seem that scary?" Chen Chao wondered.
