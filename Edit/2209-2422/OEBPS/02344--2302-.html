Chapter 2302 After the Big Exam

The final exams at Number 60 High School have come to an end, and the campus is filled with a unique sense of relaxation after the exams.

In the morning, only a few "brothers" were checking their answers one by one in the classroom.

Every time they find the correct answer, these people burst out "YES!" They seem incredibly excited, as if they've found the answers to this year's college entrance exam.

Compared to the restlessness of the "Brother Bi" group, Chen Chao and Guo Hao were much calmer.

They can be exempted from exams, and even the college entrance exam can be skipped... The key is that they have already entered the Golden Core stage, so whether they come to school or not is not very meaningful. Even if they don't listen in class, no teacher will care about them.

This is the kind of happy school life that everyone envies!

Sun Rong, who also received the same exemption from exams, knew that the real "Brother Bi" was the boy sitting in the back row by the window, from Wang's hometown, eating instant noodles...

As soon as the exam paper for "A Comprehensive Overview of Advanced Alchemy Principles" was handed out, a gloomy atmosphere filled the classroom.

"Oh no, oh no, I completely went off-topic on that last comprehensive question!" Little Peanut grabbed his hair, looking utterly hopeless.

His deskmate was relatively calmer: "I barely passed. The questions were too tricky this time; several of the knowledge points were from the extended reading materials."

"It would be good if you could just pass."

A student in the front row turned around, but instead of looking frustrated, he had a strange sense of satisfaction on his face: "Thanks to the 'Sleep Treasure' my dad bought me, I slept really well the days before the exam, and I remembered things exceptionally well! Otherwise, I wouldn't have even passed. You can wear this to sleep and go straight to the meditation session, absorbing the spiritual energy of heaven and earth in your dreams!"

"【Sleep Treasure】? That thing that looks like an eye mask and is said to allow you to skip the meditation process and absorb the spiritual energy of heaven and earth directly in your dreams?" Chen Chao became interested.

"Yeah, it's super popular now! After using it, the sleep quality is amazing! And you really do have wonderful dreams, and you wake up feeling refreshed!"

The student continued enthusiastically: "I feel that my cultivation efficiency has indeed increased a lot."

Sun Rong was organizing her notes when she heard this, and her delicate eyebrows furrowed almost imperceptibly.

First came "Taste Master," and now we have "Sleep Treasure"?

Why does she feel like the current cultivation technology tree is getting more and more crooked?

The girl subconsciously looked at Wang Ling by the window.

Wang Ling maintained his standard posture of looking out the window, his fingertips unconsciously twirling a small piece of crispy noodle crumb.

He remained calm.

"Is it really that miraculous?"

Chen Chao was clearly tempted: "My dad's been saying he can't sleep lately, how about we get him one to try?"

I advise you not to.

Guo Hao poured cold water on the idea: "It's only been a few days since the 'Xi Pei' incident. It's better to wait and see about these suddenly popular high-tech cultivation products."

"That's true..." Chen Chao scratched his head and gave an awkward smile.

Sun Rong noticed that more than one classmate was discussing "Sleep Treasure".

Even Peanut, the study committee member who usually stays up late with dark circles under his eyes, looked exceptionally energetic today.

However, his eyes seemed to have lost some of their former liveliness, and instead had a hint of emptiness.

A sense of unease crept into her heart in an instant.

The school bell rang.

A bustling crowd poured out of the teaching building.

"Lingzi, Lao Guo, shall we head to the training hall? Although Guo Hao and I have already entered the Golden Core stage, Lingzi, you can't go on like this. None of us in the Iron Triangle can fall behind in our cultivation!" Chen Chao hooked his arm around Guo Hao's neck and shouted.

"..." Wang Ling was deeply moved by Chen Chao's brotherly enthusiasm, but shook his head, indicating that he would not go.

"Tch, you're going home to keep your sister company again? Sigh... Women really do interfere with cultivation!" Chen Chao scoffed, not insisting, and pulled Guo Hao away.

Wang Ling: "..."

Sun Rong naturally walked to Wang Ling's side, and the two of them followed the flow of people out.

"Wang Ling?"

Sun Rong spoke softly, tentatively: "What do you think of that [Sleep Treasure]?"

Wang Ling did not stop walking, his gaze fixed straight ahead.

After a few seconds, he uttered two words in his characteristically flat tone: "Don't use it."

Sun Rong's eyes showed a hint of shock: "...So, this thing is another trap?"

"Hmm, a new gadget from the Primordial Era." This time, Wang Ling did not choose to hide it from Sun Rong.

In the Primordial World, there are countless plans to use modern cultivation technology to capture low-level human cultivators, so Wang Ling feels it's unnecessary for him to always fight alone behind the scenes.

Sun Rong's family's influence can still be of help in critical moments.

"I understand. If there's anything I can help with, just let me know." Sun Rong nodded solemnly and didn't ask any more questions.

"Yes, thank you," Wang Ling replied politely.

Looking at Wang Ling's well-defined profile, bathed in a soft golden light under the setting sun, the panic she felt due to the unknown subsided slightly.

The two parted ways at the fork in the road.

Wang Ling strolled home leisurely.

He really enjoyed the feeling of City Walk in the city.

Firstly, it was for the sake of peace and quiet, and secondly, it was to completely conceal their presence amidst the city's hustle and bustle.

At this very moment, Wang Ling's spiritual consciousness had already quietly spread out.

He saw countless energy waves carrying hypnotic and inducing frequencies rising from thousands of households, like wisps of smoke, converging towards an inconspicuous coordinate in the city center.

Indeed, Lingqi Technology Headquarters

Deeper still, a "dream core," like a neuronal structure, is constantly pulsating and quietly operating.

After the appetite comes the desire to sleep.

Wang Ling chuckled to himself.

The methods employed by the Primordial Era were endless.

...

Wang Family Villa, I am Wang Ling.

On the computer screen, Wang Ming's holographic projection appeared almost the instant Wang Ling entered the room.

"Lingling! Did you sense it?"

Wang Ming spoke urgently, his fingers flying across the virtual keyboard, bringing up a complex graph of spiritual energy flow: "The average nighttime [spiritual awareness activity] in Songhai City has plummeted in the past week! Especially among young cultivators! This is absolutely abnormal!"

Wang Ling nodded, sat down on the edge of the bed, and picked up a bag of new flavored instant noodles.

"I traced the path of energy loss and it eventually led to this company, Lingxi Technology."

Wang Ming zoomed in on the product image and structural perspective of [Sleep Treasure]: "On the surface, it uses so-called [spiritual waves] to promote sleep, but its core algorithm constructs an extremely realistic dream after the user enters deep sleep and begins to slowly extract the user's original spiritual energy and spiritual consciousness energy!"

Wang Ming pushed up his overheated glasses, his expression grave: "This thing is even more insidious than the [Taste Master]! Sleep is crucial for modern cultivators to consolidate their cultivation. If this happens, you'll feel better in the short term, but in the long run, your foundation will be hollowed out! Moreover, this dependence on building beautiful dreams will increase... It's very likely that you'll eventually fall asleep and never wake up again."

"Just like in that anime, what was it called again? Right! Infinite Tsukuyomi!"

"..." Wang Ling was silent for a moment.

User data analysis begins to scroll across the screen.

"Technical path analysis shows that the underlying structure of this thing is the same as the previous [Spirit Network]!" Wang Ming said decisively.

Wang Ling listened quietly, crunching away on his instant noodles.

His gaze pierced through the screen and landed directly on the massive "Dream Core" beneath the headquarters of Lingqi Technology.

The dark pink neuromorphic substance, entwined with countless dream threads and constantly pulsating, was transmitting the absorbed spiritual energy and consciousness through some spatial channel to the Dragonbone Fortress far away in the Abyss of Fallen Angels.
