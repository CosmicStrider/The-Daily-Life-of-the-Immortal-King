Chapter 2422 Eyes in the Dark

Saturday night at nine o'clock, at Chen Chao's house.

The air conditioner's outdoor unit was humming, and three leftover takeout boxes were spread out on the coffee table in the living room.

Chen Chao was nestled on the sofa scrolling through his phone, Li Changzhe was sprawled out on the floor mat digesting his food, and Guo Hao was sitting on a single sofa with a laptop propped up on his lap.

The TV was on, but no one was watching it.

"Guo Hao," Chen Chao suddenly spoke up, "who sent that anonymous message?"

Guo Hao's hands didn't stop typing: "I don't know. But the fact that they could get photos of us entering the internet cafe means we were there for at least half an hour."

"Damn." Li Changzhe rolled over: "I've experienced what it feels like to be targeted by a pervert today."

Chen Chao stared at his phone screen, his thumb unconsciously swiping down.

He remembered the photo from that afternoon.

The angle from across the street perfectly captured him frowning as he made the phone call.

He was telling his mother that he was safe and sound, and that he wouldn't be home for dinner that night.

If it were back then...

He shook his head, not wanting to think about it any further.

"Tomorrow is Sunday." Guo Hao closed his laptop. "I suggest we continue to stick together. We can do our homework at the library during the day and watch movies at night. We'll go back to school on Monday, but there will be too many people, and he won't dare to move."

"What about next week?" Chen Chao asked. "We can't keep hiding like this, can we?"

Guo Hao remained silent for a few seconds.

"We'll talk about it next week... after Wang Ling's match ends."

Mentioning Wang Ling made Chen Chao feel even more upset.

He had considered telling Wang Ling.

But what's the point of telling Wang Ling? To make him worry too? To distract him and affect the game?

"Alright." Chen Chao sat up straight, picked up the remote and turned up the TV volume: "I'll sleep at my house tonight. My dad went to the martial arts gym to keep watch, and my mom went back to her parents' house, so no one will be watching over me."

Li Changzhe sat up with a jolt: "Is there a bed?"

"There are empty rooms on the second and third floors. Please make yourselves at home. Just let me know if you need anything," Chen Chao said.

Guo Hao actually wanted to go home to check more information, but safety came first, so he stayed with them instead.

The night outside the window was getting darker, and the occasional sound of a car passing by could be heard.

At 11 o'clock, the three of them went to sleep one after another.

...

Meanwhile, in a high-end apartment in Songhai City.

Luo Xinghe stood in front of the floor-to-ceiling window, holding his mobile phone.

The screen displays the real-time location sent by Mo Xuan:

[At Chen Chao's home, all three lights were off.]

He stared at that red dot for a long time.

The red dot remained motionless.

Luo Xinghe's lips curled into a slight smile.

Ordinary people are just ordinary people; when faced with something like this, they'll just stick together and hide at home.

They thought that being in a crowd meant safety, and that staying indoors meant they would be safe.

Innocent.

He put down his phone and turned to walk into the living room.

Three sheets of printed paper were spread out on the coffee table, containing detailed information about Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe.

This includes home address, family members, daily activity patterns, frequently visited locations, and even a few candid photos of their daily lives.

Luo Xinghe picked up Chen Chao's photo.

In the photo, Chen Chao is standing at the entrance of Number 60 High School buying grilled sausages, laughing carefree.

"Laugh now," Luo Xinghe said softly, "but soon you won't be able to laugh anymore."

He put the photo back on the coffee table, walked to the wine cabinet, and poured himself a glass of red wine.

The wine swirls in the glass, reflecting his face.

He is 23 years old (by Chinese reckoning), in the mid-stage of the Golden Core realm, and a top-tier streamer in the professional industry.

Originally, he had a bright future, but because of a royal decree, he was publicly humiliated by the Sword Saint, summoned and warned by the Sect Master of the War Sect, and became a laughing stock in the circle.

On what grounds?

He took a sip of his drink and looked back at his phone screen.

The red dot is still at Chen Chao's house, motionless.

Luo Xinghe glanced at the time; it was 11:40 PM.

He made a phone call.

It rang five times before the call was answered.

"Senior Jiang," Luo Xinghe spoke, "can we make our move before Wednesday?"

There was a two-second silence on the other end of the phone.

"Xiao Luo, I said I'd wait until the match is over."

"I know," Luo Xinghe said calmly, "but I've changed my mind. I can't wait that long."

"......Why?"

"Because tonight is the best opportunity." Luo Xinghe stared at the red dot: "The three targets are together, and none of their parents are here. Chen Chao's father is on night watch at the martial arts school, and his mother has gone back to her parents' home. Guo Hao's home is far away, so he will definitely be sleeping there tonight. Li Changzhe is from out of town and has no relatives in Songhai."

He paused, then said, word by word, "Catch them all in one fell swoop."

There was a long silence on the other end of the phone.

Luo Xinghe could hear the breathing sounds from the other side of the river, as well as faint electrical noises.

"Are you sure?" Jiang Liu finally asked.

"determine."

"Okay," Jiang Liu said in a hoarse voice, "add more money."

"How many?"

"three times."

Luo Xinghe smiled.

He knew they would charge more.

But it doesn't matter, he can afford it.

"make a deal."

The call was disconnected.

Luo Xinghe raised his glass and gestured towards the night view outside the window.

"Wang Ling, don't you like to pretend? Then I'll show you what the price is for pretending."

...

1 a.m. Sunday, Songhai City, Old Town District.

A gray van was parked across from Chen Chao's residential complex.

The headlights were off, the engine was off, and even the breathing sounds inside the car were reduced to a minimum.

Sitting in the driver's seat was a man in his thirties wearing a black jacket, with a buzz cut and sinister eyes.

Two other people were sitting in the back row, equally silent.

The young man in the passenger seat was getting impatient and asked in a low voice, "Brother, when are we going to make our move?"

The man with the buzz cut glanced at him: "What's the rush? Wait until the target is sound asleep, at least after 2 a.m.

The young people shut up.

Silence fell again in the carriage.

Just then, the man with the buzz cut's phone vibrated.

He looked down and saw a message from Jiang Liu:

The target is on the third floor, in the east-facing bedroom. All three are there. Be quick and decisive, don't leave any loose ends.

The man with the buzz cut replied with two words: 【Understood.】

He put his phone back in his pocket and pulled a palm-sized metal device from under his seat.

The device's surface is engraved with fine runes, with a ring of dark red light slowly flowing in the center.

"A spirit energy shield," he explained in a low voice. "Wearing this will prevent you from triggering the neighborhood's spirit energy alarms when you take action. Within three minutes, all spirit energy fluctuations in the entire building will be shielded."

The two people in the back each took one and clipped it to their waists.

Time passed by minute by minute.

1:45 a.m.

The man with the buzz cut glanced at the time on the dashboard and was about to say "Get ready to move" when his phone suddenly vibrated again.

This time it wasn't a message, it was a phone call.

Caller ID: [Brother Jiang]

The man with the buzz cut frowned and answered, "Brother Jiang?"

The voice on the other end of the phone wasn't as steady as usual, carrying a hint of suppressed urgency: "Stop. Retreat immediately."

The man with the buzz cut was taken aback: "What?"

"I said get out!" Jiang Liu's voice was low, but every word seemed to be squeezed out from between his teeth: "Are you fucking deaf? Get out of here now, immediately!"

The man with the buzz cut changed his expression.

He didn't ask any more questions, hung up the phone, and started the car.

The van silently slid out of the parking space, turned into the alley next door, and disappeared into the night.

The young man in the passenger seat looked bewildered: "Brother, what's wrong?"

The man with the buzz cut didn't answer; he just stared intently at the rearview mirror.

In the mirror, a black SUV had appeared where they had just parked.

The license plate was covered.

The car windows were tinted, so you couldn't see who was sitting inside.

But the man with the buzz cut instinctively told him that the car appeared after they left.

And... they came specifically for them.
