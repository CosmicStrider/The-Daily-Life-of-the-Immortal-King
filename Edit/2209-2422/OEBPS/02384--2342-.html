Chapter 2342 You can't suddenly fall in love with peace when you can't win.

The coastal defense line was filled with the deafening sounds of battle.

With the support of their faith, the Hua Xiu cultivators managed to withstand the overwhelming onslaught of the radiant sea beasts and even began to push back.

However, everyone knew that the key to the outcome of this simulated battle was not how many sea monsters they had resisted, but the figure bathed in brilliant golden light, speeding toward the ship heading toward Sun Island—Zhou Ziwei!

"Stop him! Stop that guy who looks like Ultraman Tiga at all costs!"

On the ships of Sun Island, the commander roared at the top of his lungs.

In an instant, countless intense beams of psionic energy, magic orbs, and crossbow bolts imbued with elemental energy, like a storm, completely engulfed the sky where Wang Ling was located!

The flames from the explosion merged into one, and the shockwave generated by the convergence of various psionic energies caused the seawater below to surge wildly.

On the coastline, Shi Tie, Zhang Potian, and the others who were fighting fiercely had their hearts in their throats.

"Brother Zhou!" Zhang Potian couldn't help but exclaim in surprise.

Such a dense barrage of saturation attacks would probably blast even a Nascent Soul cultivator to smithereens!

However, the next second, a scene that chilled all the cultivators of Sun Island appeared.

Amidst the endless explosion of light and flame, the golden figure emerged unscathed!

Wang Ling's speed did not decrease by even the slightest.

All attacks, upon contact with that protective golden light, were either silently annihilated or violently repelled!

A psionic beam that was deflected was instead struck by a nearby Sun Island frigate, causing a violent explosion.

The ship immediately burst into flames and then began to capsize.

Wherever Wang Ling passed, it seemed to bring with him an invisible rebound field. The more fierce the attack from Sun Island, the more severe their own losses became!

"What...what kind of monster is this?!"

"Our attack... is completely ineffective!"

"What exactly is his cultivation level?!"

Panic began to spread among the fleet on Sun Island.

The originally orderly formation began to show signs of demoralization due to Wang Ling's unreasonable advance.

"Hahahaha! Did you see that! This is the might of my Hua Xiu!" Shi Tie waved his one arm, his face flushed with excitement, and smashed the head of a small sea beast in front of him to pieces with a punch, his momentum growing even stronger.

"Brothers! Senior Zhou is already defeating the enemy! How can we fall behind! Kill!" Zhang Potian roared, his movements as fast as a ghost. Wherever he went, the assault cultivators from Sun Island fell one after another.

The morale of Hua Xiu's side was pushed to an unprecedented climax by Wang Ling's miraculous performance!

Just then, the sea began to churn violently once again.

"roar--!!"

Three distinct fifth-grade radiant sea beasts, as if receiving a death order, simultaneously leaped out of the deep sea, forming a triangular formation to block Wang Ling's path toward the flagship.

The combined pressure emanating from them almost froze the air in this area.

"It's a three-headed lord-level sea beast!"

"Oh no, Senior Zhou has been stopped!"

"Their aura... is so strong!"

The morale that had just been boosted was suddenly put to a standstill.

Faced with these three enormous creatures, Wang Ling finally stopped flying for the first time.

He hovered in mid-air, the golden light remaining steady, his face obscured, only a chilling calm emanating from him.

One of the sea serpents attacked first, opening its massive jaws large enough to swallow an aircraft carrier, spewing out a torrent of green venomous mist that swept towards Wang Ling.

The second giant crab followed closely behind, its huge right claw suddenly snapping at Wang Ling. Although it looked clumsy, it was surprisingly agile, moving so fast that it left only a blur in the air.

The last bat-eel emitted a silent, sharp mental cry, and invisible ripples shot straight into Wang Ling's sea of consciousness.

The three-way attack sealed off all escape routes!

Along the coastline, countless people held their breath.

However, Wang Ling simply raised his right hand slowly.

Together they resemble a sword.

He made a light, casual stroke forward.

A thin, hair-like golden thread spread out from his fingertips.

The golden thread transformed into sword energy, gently brushing past the towering poisonous fog.

The poisonous mist was instantly dissolved and evaporated by the sword energy.

The golden sword energy continued forward, slicing past the giant crab claws that were powerful enough to crush mountains.

The shell, harder than refined gold, silently split in two, the cross-section as smooth as a mirror.

Finally, the sword energy swept past the bat that had released its mental shock.

The batfish's enormous body stiffened abruptly, then split cleanly in the middle. Before the filthy blood and internal organs could even gush out, they were cleaved into nothingness by the supreme sword intent attached to the sword energy.

It was just a sword.

The three-headed fifth-rank lord sea beast that had filled the Huaxiu cultivators with despair was instantly killed!

But this is not the end.

After slaying the three-headed sea beast, the golden sword energy continued its momentum and gently landed on the dark blue sea below.

"laugh--"

A soft sound.

Centered on the point where the sword energy landed, the vast sea was forcibly pushed apart by an invisible, powerful force!

A massive, bottomless chasm, hundreds of meters wide, appeared on the sea, stretching straight towards the distant flagship of Sun Island!

The seawater has formed water walls up to 100 meters high on both sides of the ravine, but they cannot be closed, as if supported by an invisible force.

The mud, rocks, and even the wreckage of shipwrecks on the seabed are clearly visible.

One sword, and the sea is split!

"......"

The entire battlefield fell into a deathly silence.

Whether they were Chinese cultivators or invaders from Sun Island, everyone was so shocked by this scene that they lost the ability to speak.

This is no longer the power of a cultivator; this is a miracle!

Wang Ling's figure, in the separated sea passage, started up again, flying slowly towards the flagship.

On both sides are hundred-meter-high water walls, and below is a smooth underwater road, like a god on a procession.

...

On the "Rizhao" ship on Sun Island.

Seated beneath the canopy, Layman Che Li finally revealed his undisguised fear.

The jade cup in her hand was crushed to pieces with a "crack".

"How is this possible... When did Huaxiu produce such a person?!" Her voice was dry and trembling slightly.

As the golden figure drew closer step by step, a shadow of death instantly enveloped her heart.

She withstood all kinds of magic, cleaved three beasts with a single sword strike, and parted the sea to travel... This was simply a power she could not contend with!

"Island Lord! What should we do? He... he's coming soon!" The adjutant beside him was pale and his voice trembled.

Che Li Jushi's eyes flickered rapidly.

There's absolutely no way we can win a fight.

Run? Can you escape from something like this?

In a flash, one of her usual tricks came to mind.

"quick!"
