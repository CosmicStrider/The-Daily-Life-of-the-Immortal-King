Chapter 2353 Team fight can be lost, but Teemo must die!

The moment Wang Ling locked down Teemo, the entire Dream Arena fell into a brief silence, followed by an even louder murmur of discussion.

Chen Chao's eyes nearly popped out of their sockets, and he gasped in shock.

It's obvious that Hakim... is building his happiness on the suffering of the four people opposite him...

Although he exclaimed in surprise, Chen Chao's hands didn't stop moving, and he almost instinctively locked onto [Fairy Witch Lulu].

This is a hero who can maximize protective abilities, perfectly complementing Wang Ling's Teemo's stealth ambush and counter-attack.

"I believe in Wang Ling." Li Changzhe took a deep breath. Although he was the lowest rank, he was also inspired by this "choice of God." He immediately picked [Time Guardian Zilean] for the mid lane.

Zilean's resurrection ultimate [Time Reversal] and speed boost undoubtedly provide Teemo with another layer of protection.

"Four protect one, protect Teemo... I've never played against such an outrageous lineup in my life." Zhou Rui, the jungler, pushed up his glasses, his eyes sharpening.

He chose Sejuani, the Wrath of the North, a hero nicknamed "Pig Girl".

Sejuani's strong crowd control, tankiness, and slowing field can greatly limit the opponent's advance, creating an environment for Teemo to deal damage.

Liu Kaichuan, the top laner, locked in Shen, the Eye of Twilight.

With global support and taunt protection, Teemo can be remotely shielded, enabling full-map support.

At this point, the red team's lineup was finalized: Shen (top), Sejuani (jungle), Zilean (mid), Teemo (ADC), and Lulu (support).

An extreme, almost performance art-like "four-protect-one" lineup has been assembled.

On the blue side, Fujiwara Ren and his three companions looked at the opposing team, their faces revealing annoyance at being slighted.

"Teemo ADC? Is he insulting us?" Haruto Suzuki's sunny smile faltered slightly. He had chosen Kha'Zix, a jungler with a stronger early-game tempo, for this game.

"Arrogant Hua Xiuren!" Takahashi Tsuyoshi muttered, choosing the equally strong Future Guardian Jess, who could fight in melee and harass from a distance.

Kenta Saeki then took out Astral Wanderer Bard.

Fujiwara Ren's face was gloomy. With EZ banned, he also chose his main ADC champion.

Sun Rong chose to go mid lane this time, avoiding a direct confrontation with Wang Ling, and locked onto Karma.

The final lineup for the blue team is: Jayce (top), Kha'Zix (jungle), Karma (mid), Xayah (ADC), and Bard (support).

In terms of lineup, the blue side still looks more reasonable, possessing poke, burst damage, flexibility, and team buffs.

The red team, on the other hand, placed all their tactical emphasis on the Yodl's mushroom and blinding blowgun.

...

"Welcome to the Glory Alliance!"

Let the game begin!

"Everyone, go outside and get your scanners! Buy some control wards too!" As soon as the game started, Fujiwara Ren immediately gave the order in the team voice chat, her voice cold and stern: "We can skip the level 1 team fight, but we must maintain good vision control. We absolutely cannot let Teemo have an easy time!"

The five members of the blue team uniformly replaced their wards with scanning lenses that can detect invisible units, and each of them also purchased one or two additional control wards.

Wang Ling's Teemo, with Doran's Blade and Red Buff, strode towards the bottom lane with his adorable little steps, while Chen Chao's Lulu followed closely beside him.

During the laning phase, Wang Ling played exceptionally steadily.

He used the extra damage from his E skill, Poison Shot, to precisely finish off each enemy.

When Fujiwara Ren's Xayah tried to use her feathers to harass her, Wang Ling's Teemo always landed a precise Q skill, Blinding Dart, rendering Xayah's basic attacks useless and leaving her extremely frustrated.

"This guy... he's gone too far!" Fujiwara Ren frowned, feeling powerless.

On the other hand, Wang Ling's finishing skills were on full display at this moment. Under Lulu's cover, he was almost flawless in his attacks, and the economy grew steadily.

All eyes were focused on Teemo's level.

Level 6 is the turning point for Teemo.

Wang Lingping steadily rose to level six.

After instantly learning the R skill, he didn't immediately plant mushrooms to clear the lane. Instead, he marked the river bushes to signal Chen Chao to prepare vision, and then disappeared into the fog of war.

"Teemo's gone! Watch out for mushrooms!" Kenta Saeki immediately typed the signal on the map.

Fujiwara Ren gritted his teeth: "Keep the scanner on, watch your step!"

Wang Ling's Teemo has begun his entire stadium greening project.

He did not hastily plant mushrooms at a common intersection.

His mushrooms were in an outrageously difficult location.

For example, the extreme blind spot near the middle wall of the blue team's F6 camp.

For example, in the top lane river bushes, right next to the very edge of the wall, a passing hero's body will slightly brush against it.

Sometimes, he would even deliberately peek out from the edge of the other person's field of vision, and then immediately go invisible, pretending to have planted a mushroom in the same spot.

The people on the blue side nervously scanned the area, but found nothing.

"Baka! You're messing with us again!" Haruto Suzuki's praying mantis missed its target in the river once again, and he couldn't help but mutter a curse under his breath.

The truly deadly mushrooms had already been planted undetected in places they never expected.

Eight minutes into the game, Takahashi Takeshi's Jayce pushed the minion wave into the tower in the top lane, preparing to recall.

He habitually walked to the relatively safe corner in front of the second tower and pressed the return-to-town button.

Just as the countdown for returning to town reached its final second!

"puff!"

A soft popping sound, accompanied by the green poisonous fog from the mushroom explosion.

Takahashi Tsuyoshi's Jayce health bar dropped drastically, and his recall was interrupted!

"What?!" Takahashi Tsuyoshi looked at the mushroom that had appeared at his feet, both surprised and angry.

He instinctively tried to move away from the toxic fog, but ended up taking a step to the side...

"puff!"

Another mushroom!

"Damn it!" This time he cursed out loud, his health instantly dropping to a dangerous level, forcing him to cancel his return to town and awkwardly knock out his health potion.

"Hahaha!"

Liu Kaichuan's Shen saw everything clearly from below the tower and slapped his thigh with delight: "Wang Ling is awesome! The way you grow these mushrooms is amazing!"

This is just the beginning.

Haruto Suzuki's Kha'Zix wanted to gank the bottom lane, so he jumped over the wall and into the river bush, his feet barely touching the ground...

"Pfft! Pfft!"

Two mushrooms exploded on his face in quick succession.

With the continuous damage from the toxins, his originally healthy health dropped to half in an instant, and the gank plan was immediately ruined.

Fujiwara Ren's Kasumi wanted to help fight the small dragon, and had just entered the bushes at the entrance of the dragon pit...

"puff!"

The mushroom explodes, slowing you down and exposing your field of vision.

"Damn it!" His face turned ashen, feeling like he was dancing on a minefield with every step he took.

The blue team's economy was gradually widened by the frequent purchase of true sight wards and scans, as well as the depletion of resources by mushrooms and the delay in development.

The red team then seized the opportunity to control the first dragon and the canyon vanguard.

"Calm down! Don't panic!" Fujiwara Ren growled in his voice, trying to quell the chaos: "Fight at our pace! Teemo is useless in the late game!"
