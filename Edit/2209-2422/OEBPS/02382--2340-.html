Chapter 2340 Protect Hua Xiu!

Inside the training room of Chihuo High School, Liu Kaichuan put down his phone.

The phone screen was still showing the chat interface with Zhou Rui.

He rubbed his temples, the battle just now replaying in his mind.

This team from Number 60 High School is like a rough diamond, with amazing potential.

"President, are we really going to arrange a practice match with them?" The jungler with the ID "Flame Claw" couldn't help but ask, his face still showing some reluctance.

"Otherwise what?" Liu Kaichuan raised his eyes, his gaze sweeping over the team members in the training room. "Losing isn't scary; what's scary is not knowing how you lost. Zhou Rui's judgment isn't bad. He's in such a hurry to push us onto the team, firstly to test us, and secondly to use us to further assess the strength of Number 60 High School. But why can't we use the other side to improve ourselves?"

He stood up, walked to the tactics whiteboard, and picked up a marker: "Pull up the replay of that match, especially the key moments in the bottom and mid lanes, and watch it repeatedly. There are many things we can learn from their teamwork, decision-making, and attention to detail. Also, notify the data analysis team to produce a preliminary analysis report on this team as soon as possible, focusing on Ezreal and Orianna."

"Yes, President!"

Meanwhile, the Xuanming High School e-sports club.

Zhou Rui watched as Sun Rong readily accepted his invitation to the training match, and a smile finally appeared on his lips.

He turned around and looked at the members behind him, whose expressions varied.

"I know that many people are still not convinced and think that the match doesn't count, and that they were just lucky." Zhou Rui's voice echoed clearly in the activity room. "But we just received news from Chi Huo that they have also arranged a training match with them."

Everyone immediately perked up their ears.

"Red Fire also made a date?"

"That's right." Zhou Rui uttered two words, and seeing the team members' expressions change instantly, he added, "Moreover, Liu Kai just admitted that the EZ player is a monster."

A collective gasp filled the activity room.

At this moment, all the remaining contempt and resentment were finally suppressed.

"So everyone..." Zhou Rui looked around at everyone, his tone serious: "In the upcoming training matches, give me your utmost attention! We are facing a dark horse that is capable of overturning the landscape of high school e-sports in Songhai City! Seizing this opportunity and becoming stronger together with them is what we should be doing right now!"

The atmosphere inside the Dream Esports Arena was equally vibrant.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao were thrilled to have defeated two veteran powerhouses, Xuanming and Chihuo, in succession.

"Hahaha! That was awesome! So awesome!" Chen Chao patted Guo Hao's shoulder hard: "Old Guo, did you see that? Chi Huo and his gang didn't say a word in the end! Our Number 60 High School is really going to be famous this time!"

Guo Hao was relatively calmer, but his face still showed joy: "You played really well. However, even though we won, there are still quite a few problems. Sun Rong, shall we review the replays of the last two games?"

Sun Rong nodded: "Okay. Everyone should summarize their performance in these two matches and identify areas for improvement."

The group gathered around the holographic tactics board.

Sun Rong operated the interface, bringing up data statistics and key node replays.

"First, Chen Chao." Sun Rong looked at Chen Chao: "You handled the pressure well. Even when you were at a disadvantage in the laning phase in both games, your CS wasn't too far behind, and your damage absorption and key crowd control in team fights were also very effective. But..."

She switched to a scene showing Chen Chao's Titan being tower-dived by Alligator and Mantis.

"Here, your Flash was a bit slow. If you had reacted faster, you might have been able to trade kills. We need you to not only play defensively in the top lane, but also have stronger on-the-spot reactions and the ability to trade blows in extreme situations. Also, your champion pool currently seems to be too tanky, so we need you to practice some fighter-type top laners, such as Fiora, Camille, and Irelia, to broaden our tactical options."

Chen Chao scratched his head and wrote it down carefully: "Understood! I'll train my warriors like crazy from now on! I guarantee I can pull them out and be a C before the selection competition!"

Sun Rong smiled slightly and looked at Wang Muyu: "Muyu, your learning ability has really surprised all of us."

Wang Muyu lowered his head somewhat embarrassed.

"From Syndra in the first game to Orianna in the second game, your learning and adaptability are amazing."

Sun Rong praised him generously, then changed the subject: "But your champion pool is still too shallow. We've only tested Syndra and Orianna so far. Against opponents with different styles, we need you to master more types of champions. For example, assassins like Ahri and LeBlanc, support champions like Twisted Fate and Galio, or even tanky champions like Ryze and Vladimir. A wide champion pool is the foundation of a top-tier mid laner."

"Yes! Sister Sun Rong, I will try my best!" Wang Muyu clenched his little fist, his eyes firm.

In order to fight alongside his father and mother, he was willing to put in a hundred times the effort to practice.

"Guo Hao." Sun Rong looked at the jungler: "Your sense of rhythm and overall game awareness have always been excellent, and you play Jarvan IV and Nidalee quite well. But your jungle champion pool could be explored further. Have you considered practicing a less common champion that can turn the tide in crucial moments?"

At this point, a glint of light flashed in Guo Hao's eyes: "Actually, I've been practicing Widowmaker recently. This hero is good at farming in the early game, but after level six, her stealth ganking ability is extremely strong, making her a great secret weapon to catch the opponent off guard."

"A widow?" Chen Chao's eyes lit up: "Great, Lao Guo! This hero is cunning, I like him!"

Sun Rong nodded in approval: "Very good, this can serve as our tactical reserve. But we must be careful to maintain secrecy during training."

Finally, everyone's eyes fell on Wang Ling.

Wang Ling remained sitting there quietly, eerily calm.

Chen Chao leaned over, hooked his arm around Wang Ling's shoulder, and exclaimed in amazement, "Lingzi, I never would have guessed! You're usually so quiet, but you're so fierce when you play games! That EZ was like he had a global cheat! Did you put all your cultivation talent into gaming? This is called... the law of conservation of spiritual energy! If your cultivation talent isn't high, your gaming talent will definitely explode!"

Guo Hao nodded in agreement: "That makes sense. It seems that God is fair."

Sun Rong: "..."

Wang Muyu: "..."

The two who knew the truth exchanged a glance and couldn't help but chuckle.

Sun Rong quickly tried to smooth things over, suppressing a laugh, and said, "Chen Chao, don't talk nonsense. Wang Ling is just... not very good at expressing himself. We just haven't seen his efforts."

Wang Muyu nodded vigorously, "Yes, yes! Brother Wang Ling is amazing!"

Wang Ling chewed on his instant noodles expressionlessly, letting out a silent sigh.

All he really wanted was that ten-year snack gift pack...

But Wang Ling was too lazy to explain, nor did he need to.

"Okay, stop fooling around."

Sun Rong steered the conversation back on track, "Since both Xuanming and Chihuo have extended training match invitations, this is an excellent opportunity for us. Here's a preliminary plan: for the next week, we'll each play a best-of-three (BO3) training match against Xuanming and Chihuo every other day. The remaining time will be used for targeted individual training and team cohesion. Does anyone have any questions?"

"No!" they all shouted in unison, their fighting spirit high.

However, Wang Ling knew that there were some things he had to face.

He took out his phone and connected to an encrypted video call with Diu Lei Zhenjun and Wang Ming.

"Listen!" Wang Ming's virtual projection appeared on the screen, his tone excited: "The data you sent back last time about the Shadow Cultivator's Home is crucial! Diu Lei and I have already identified several possible strongholds of the Primordial Evil Cultivators!"

Wang Ling nodded and said succinctly, "Tonight, we'll close the net."

"What do you plan to do?" Wang Ming asked, "Destroy it directly by force?"

Wang Ling shook his head, then extended his index finger and gently tapped it in the air.

A faint golden light spilled from his fingertips, pierced through space, and silently disappeared into the void.
