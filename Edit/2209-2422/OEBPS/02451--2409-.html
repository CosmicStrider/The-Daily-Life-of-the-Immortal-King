Chapter 2409 The Brothers' "Perfect Plan"

Time flies by in the blink of an eye.

A few days later at noon, the entrance of Number 60 High School was crowded with students.

Old Pan stood beside the bus, taking attendance from the name list.

"Wang Ling, Chen Chao, Guo Hao, Li Changzhe... have they all arrived yet?"

"Here!" Chen Chao shouted in response, his eyes secretly glancing at Wang Ling beside him.

The boy was wearing a school uniform jacket today, zipped up to his chest, with a simple white T-shirt underneath.

The dark talisman patch on the back of her neck peeked out from her collar, looking no different from usual.

"Lingzi, are you nervous?" Chen Chao nudged Wang Ling's shoulder.

Wang Ling shook his head, took out a packet of instant noodles from his pocket, and tore open the package.

"You've got a big heart." Chen Chao chuckled twice and took out a loaf of bread from his bag and started eating it.

Guo Hao and Li Changzhe stood not far away, checking the final details of the plan.

"Did you bring the patch?" Guo Hao asked in a low voice.

"I brought it." Li Changzhe patted his pocket. "Where's the remote?"

"Here you are." Guo Hao patted his inner pocket. "Remember, send a message as soon as you see Wang Ling enter the venue. I'll be waiting for your signal backstage."

"clear."

After taking attendance, Lao Pan waved and said, "Get in the car! Songhai City Gymnasium, about a 40-minute drive, so hurry up!"

Wang Ling chose a window seat, and Chen Chao naturally sat down next to him.

Guo Hao and Li Changzhe sat in the row behind them.

The bus started and drove out of the school gate.

Chen Chao looked out the window at the rapidly receding street scene, his palms sweating profusely, and no matter how much he tried to absorb it with tissues, they would quickly become wet again.

He took out his phone and sent a message to Luo Xinghe:

We set off and arrived at the stadium around 1:30 PM.

A few minutes later, a reply came:

【Very good. Remember, after Wang Ling enters the arena, the remaining 500,000 will be deposited into your account.】

Chen Chao stared at the screen, gritted his teeth, and stuffed his phone back into his pocket.

"Chao'er, what's wrong?" Guo Hao peeked over from behind.

"It's nothing." Chen Chao shook his head and lowered his voice: "Luo Xinghe is urging us."

Guo Hao pushed up his glasses but didn't say anything.

Li Changzhe came over: "Can our plan... really work?"

"It has to work." Chen Chao clenched his fist. "Otherwise, I won't feel at ease even if I get that 500,000."

Wang Ling sat by the window, quietly eating instant noodles, as if everything around him was irrelevant.

However, Chen Chao noticed that Wang Ling ate noodles slower than usual today.

He seemed to be thinking about something.

"Rin-chan," Chen Chao couldn't help but ask, "how are you planning to play in the match later?"

Wang Ling turned his head and looked at him: "Play normally."

"What if you encounter a formidable opponent?"

"Let it be," Wang Ling said matter-of-factly.

Chen Chao choked.

This answer is too much like a royal decree.

"Fine..." He chuckled dryly, "It's just a formality anyway, winning or losing doesn't matter."

Wang Ling nodded and continued eating his noodles.

...

At 1:25, the bus arrived at Songhai City Gymnasium.

A large crowd had already gathered outside the venue. School flags fluttered in the wind, and students in different school uniforms gathered in twos and threes, creating a tense yet enthusiastic atmosphere.

"Wow, so many people?" Li Changzhe's eyes widened.

Chen Chao was also taken aback.

He originally thought it was just a regular exchange competition, but he didn't expect it to be so large-scale.

At least a thousand people gathered in the square outside the venue, and media reporters were also conducting interviews with their cameras.

"It seems the city is taking this competition quite seriously." Guo Hao narrowed his eyes. "That's good. The more people there are, the less likely Luo Xinghe will dare to act recklessly."

Old Pan led the team from Number 60 High School to the registration area to sign in.

The staff checked the list and handed over several entry certificates and wristbands.

"Wang Ling, right?" The staff member glanced at Wang Ling again: "Your contestant number is A-07. The waiting area is in section A. Scanning will begin at 1:50, and the competition will officially start at 2:00."

Wang Ling took the bracelet and put it on his left wrist.

The bracelet is specially made with sensing runes on the inside, presumably for recording match data and location.

"Lingzi, keep it up!" Chen Chao patted him on the shoulder.

Wang Ling grunted in agreement, his gaze sweeping over the silver-white scanner at the entrance of the venue.

The device is two meters tall, resembling a standing security gate, and its surface is covered with intricate runes.

Participants need to pass through the middle and complete the cultivation scan before they can enter the waiting area.

Several students were already queuing up.

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe exchanged a glance.

The plan begins.

...

1:35.

As planned, Chen Chao stayed near the main entrance of the venue to "keep watch".

Guo Hao and Li Changzhe used the excuse of going to the restroom to sneak into the venue ahead of time.

"Brother Chao, we're in." Guo Hao sent a message to Chen Chao's phone: "I'm in the backstage aisle, I can see the scanner. Li Changzhe went to the audience seats."

Chen Chao replied: "Wang Ling is still listening to the instructions from Lao Pan, it will probably take a few more minutes."

He put away his phone and pretended to wander aimlessly, but his eyes were always fixed on Wang Ling's direction.

Not far away, Wang Ling was being pulled along by Lao Pan, and they were talking. Lao Pan had a serious expression and was gesturing in the air with his fingers, as if he were explaining tactics.

Wang Ling kept his head down, nodding occasionally.

Chen Chao couldn't help but laugh.

What tactics does Lingzi need? If he were serious, he could tear down the entire stadium.

However, this cannot be said.

"Chen Chao!"

A familiar voice came from behind me.

Chen Chao turned around and saw Sun Rong running over, carrying two bottles of water in her hands.

"Sun Rong? What brings you here?" Chen Chao asked in surprise.

"Senior Sister Jiugong gave me the ticket, saying that Wang Ling has a match today and asked me to come and cheer him on." Sun Rong's cheeks were slightly red as she handed a bottle of energy drink to Chen Chao: "This... could you give it to Wang Ling for me?" This water is specially made by their group, with safe ingredients and a refreshing effect.

Chen Chao took the water, his heart filled with emotion at Sun Rong's feelings for Wang Ling. These two were such a perfect match! He was practically swooning!

"Okay, I'll give it to him in a bit." Chen Chao nodded: "Where are you sitting?"

"Section B, third row. I don't want to be too high-profile this time." Sun Rong pointed to the venue: "Senior Jiu Gong and Senior Zhuo Yi are also here. They are in the VIP section."

Chen Chao's heart tightened.

Zhuo Yi and Jiu Gong are here?

This specification is way too high!

"Then...then you go in first, I'll wait for Wang Ling here," Chen Chao said.

Sun Rong nodded, glanced in Wang Ling's direction again, and then turned and walked towards the entrance.

As Chen Chao watched her departing figure, he suddenly felt an even heavier burden on his shoulders.

We cannot let Wang Ling be exposed.

Absolutely not.

...

1:40.

Wang Ling finally escaped Lao Pan's "tactical guidance" and walked towards the entrance of the venue.

Chen Chao quickly went to meet him and handed him the water: "Sun Rong gave it to me."

Wang Ling took it, unscrewed it, and took a sip. It was sweet and tasted pretty good.

"Lingzi, after we go inside, let's meet at the restroom next to the waiting area." Chen Chao lowered his voice: "Guo Hao and Li Changzhe are waiting for us there."

Wang Ling glanced at him and nodded.

The two walked toward the scanner.

There weren't many people in line; there were only three students ahead of them.

Chen Chao took out his phone and sent a message to Guo Hao:

Wang Ling is here; he's expected to arrive at the scanning area in three minutes.

A few seconds later, Guo Hao replied:

[Received! I'm in the restroom now. Li Changzhe is in the audience.]

Chen Chao put away his phone, and his palms started sweating again.

The first student stepped into the scanner.

The device emitted a soft blue light, scanning the student's entire body from top to bottom.

Three seconds later, data appeared on the side display:

[Name: Zhang Wei]

School: Songhai Number 1 High School

[Cultivation Level: Late Foundation Establishment]

[Matched Opponent: C-12]

The student came out, and the staff swiped his wristband to enter the data.

"Next one."

The second student went in.

Name: Li Na

School: Songhai Experimental High School Affiliated
