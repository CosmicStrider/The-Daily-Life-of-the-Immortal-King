Chapter 2327 Bloodstained Patterns Permeate Number 60 High Schools

In the early morning, Wang Ling opened his eyes and heard the sounds of Wang Nuan and Wang Muyu playing and frolicking.

Downstairs, Aunt Wang was busily preparing breakfast for the whole family, her movements skillful, and the air was filled with the smoky aroma of fried eggs.

Mr. Wang was watching a replay of a game competition, hoping to find some inspiration, but he ended up getting really angry early in the morning.

"Damn it! Do they even know how to play?! This foreign aid from the Southern Cold Kingdom is unreliable! What kind of nonsense are they playing?! Even though our region is weak to begin with, they can't just throw the game like that!" Wang's father grumbled, quite angry at the behavior of the foreign aid in the match scene.

This foreign player earns a huge salary in China and is invincible in the country. But when he encounters his own country's team in the World Championship, he immediately chooses to serve his country.

Is this a human?

Hearing his father's angry scolding, Wang Ling understood his father's agitated feelings.

If given the chance, he would love to have a showdown with Brother Li from the Southern Cold Kingdom and let the world see who is the strictest father.

At this moment, Wang Ling slowly got up and changed into the Number 60 High School uniform with the character "精" (jing) on it.

His movements remained languid, but his eyes held a more subtle focus than usual.

After breakfast, on his way to school, Wang Ling deliberately avoided teleporting or running to quickly reach the campus.

He walked slowly, observing as he went.

The aroma of spiritual plant buns wafted from breakfast stalls, busy office workers and cultivators hurried along, and parents chatted as they sent their children to school... All these scenes constituted the usual normalcy of Songhai City.

However, a trace of the power of the King's Eye had already quietly begun to circulate in Wang Ling's seemingly lifeless eyes.

An invisible spirit brushed past every passerby.

Most soul halos are normal, a mixture of anxiety, fatigue, and anticipation brought about by life.

But within these normal emotional undertones.

He accurately captured those discordant noises.

A young man riding a psionic shared bicycle, with bread in his mouth, who looked like an ordinary company employee, had several dark threads wrapped around the edge of his soul aura. That was the mark of the "gray-clothed" level among the shadow operatives. His aura was weak but stable.

A woman standing by a bus stop, oblivious to everyone around her, applying makeup in front of a mirror—the dark aura deep within her soul is approaching the threshold of becoming a "black robe."

Even as Wang Ling waited at a red light, a black psionic sedan drove past him.

With the car window half-rolled down, a cold-faced boy in a custom-made school uniform sat in the back seat. The aura emanating from his soul caused Wang Ling's gaze to pause for a moment.

It's a blood streak.

The dark aura surrounding the boy was no longer thread-like, but wriggling slightly like a living thing, outlining faint dark red patterns on the surface of his soul! Its intensity far surpassed that of the previous gray clothes and black robe!

Moreover, there is more than one.

During his short ten-minute walk to school, Wang Ling sensed at least three "Bloodmark" level Shadow Stalkers passing by him.

They came from different backgrounds.

Students, white-collar workers, and even a seemingly amiable community inspector.

"The spreading momentum hasn't been completely suppressed," Wang Ling concluded.

Li Jun's "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" is like a broad-spectrum antibiotic, clearing away most of the bacteria on the surface and inhibiting large-scale outbreaks.

However, the effect is limited for individuals who are already deeply infected.

The emergence of the "Bloodline" level, in particular, signifies that the core of the Shadowman organization has begun to take shape and has become more deeply integrated into the social structure.

This is indeed a protracted war.

As you enter the gate of Number 60 High School, the familiar hustle and bustle hits you.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao were arm in arm, discussing last night's match.

Upon seeing Wang Ling, Chen Chao, as usual, patted him on the shoulder in a casual manner: "Lingzi, you look great today! Did you watch that infuriating match yesterday?"

Wang Ling: "..."

He stayed up all night to dismantle a dungeon in another world; there's no way to respond to that.

Guo Hao, being more observant, noticed that Wang Ling seemed a little more dazed than usual, but simply assumed he was still half asleep.

Sun Rong was already waiting at the classroom door, still holding an exquisite little food box in her hand, which contained her family's spirit chef's specially made spiritual pastries.

Seeing Wang Ling, she smiled slightly and handed over the food box, saying not only to Wang Ling but also to Chen Chao and Guo Hao, "Everyone, have you had breakfast properly? Let's have a taste together?"

When Chen Chao and Guo Hao saw that there was food, they grabbed a piece and stuffed it into their mouths.

While chewing, he shouted that Sun Rong was his "godmother"!

Sun Rong: "..."

Liking someone is always a helpless feeling. Sun Rong gave Wang Ling food, a habit she developed after her experience in the inner world. Her original intention was simply to talk to Wang Ling a few more times.

But after learning from her previous experiences delivering food, she realized that it seemed a bit too deliberate to only deliver food to Wang Ling.

So this time, she simply included Guo Hao and Chen Chao's share as well.

Watching the two wolf down their food, Sun Rong smiled helplessly, thinking to herself that they were both useless.

After saying thank you as usual, Wang Ling would pick up a small piece and chew it elegantly and in small bites.

But then, his expression suddenly paused.

As if sensing something, Wang Ling's gaze suddenly pierced through the noisy crowd and landed on a boy who was quietly reading a book by the window at the end of the corridor.

The boy was wearing the same elite class uniform as them, only from another class.

He was tall and slender, wore gold-rimmed glasses, and had a calm and even somewhat scholarly air about him.

Surrounded by his lively classmates, he appeared exceptionally low-key and reserved.

However, in Wang Ling's vision, the boy's soul was like a lamp burning with a dark red flame!

Blood streaks again!

Moreover, this was not one of those newly initiated Bloodline Markers, whose auras were still somewhat unstable, that I had sensed along the way. The Bloodline Markers on this boy's soul were clear, and the dark energy he emitted was very stable, clearly indicating that he had been advancing for some time and had a solid foundation.

He turned out to be a classmate from the same school and in the same grade!

Wang Ling was somewhat surprised.

A Bloodline-level Shadow Stalker was lurking right beside him, going to and from class every day just like an ordinary student.

Wang Ling withdrew his gaze without making a sound, as if it were just a casual glance.

He sat down at his desk and his fingers flew across the screen of his phone.

An encrypted message with a photo was sent out.

Recipient: Wang Ming.

The photo was taken by Wang Linggang using the ability of "Wang Tong," and a perfect 3D model was instantly generated. Finally, the Bluetooth signal was simulated using spiritual sense, and the photo was directly transferred to the phone.

With this clear photo, Wang Ling doesn't need much description; he can directly pinpoint the target in the vast database through comparison.

The first lesson was Professor Pan's introduction to the science of refining instruments.

Teacher Pan stood on the podium, speaking eloquently about the key points of smelting a new type of psionic alloy.

Wang Ling appeared to be lost in thought, but in reality, his consciousness remained fixed on the boy with glasses.

He saw more details.

The boy listened attentively in class and took meticulous notes.

He would occasionally exchange quiet conversations with his deskmate, his expression natural, his smile gentle, showing no signs of anything amiss. The fluctuations of his spiritual energy were also controlled at around the mid-Foundation Establishment stage, perfectly in line with the normal level of an elite class student.

The disguise was flawless.

However, Wang Ling, through Wang Tong, directly peered into some fragments of his soul's surface.

For example, one late night, in a dimly lit room, a boy knelt on the floor, facing a shadowy figure radiating an oppressive aura. The shadowy figure's voice was cold and alluring: "Complete this trial, and you will be promoted to 'Dark Star' and gain true power..."

For example, the boy expressionlessly used a poisoned short blade to slit the throat of a competitor in black robes.
