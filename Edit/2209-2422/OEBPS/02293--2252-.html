Chapter 2252 The Glass Clan

In front of the cavern, the "bonfire" modeled from lava core ore flickered with warm yellow light, elongating the shadows of the people sitting around and casting them onto the cave walls that emitted a faint ethereal glow.

The air was filled with the aroma of roasted food, the refreshing sweetness of Yu Xin fruit juice, and a rare sense of relaxation after the battle.

Sun Rong's fair fingers held a perfectly grilled mushroom and meat skewer, the golden fat glistening slightly in the firelight, its aroma enticing.

She hesitated for a moment, her fingertips tightening and relaxing slightly, but finally she mustered her courage and gently handed the meat skewer towards Wang Ling.

"Wang Ling, want to try this? Guo Hao grilled it, and the taste... seems pretty good?" Her voice was very soft, with a hint of barely perceptible probing and expectation, as if afraid of disturbing something.

She raised her gaze, carefully placing it on his focused profile.

Wang Ling's gaze shifted from the half-crushed bag of instant noodles in his hand, his calm, dark eyes turning to the meat skewers handed to him, and then following the skewers to Sun Rong.

The firelight danced in his dark pupils, casting a faint glow, but revealing no emotion.

He silently reached out his hand, his well-defined fingers naturally taking the bamboo skewer.

In the very instant of the handover, his cool fingertips brushed against Sun Rong's warm hand.

The touch was as light as a feather brushing the surface of water, yet it instantly sent a tingling electric current through Sun Rong's hand, sending a numbing sensation straight to her heart.

"!" Sun Rong's heart skipped a beat, as if she had been scalded by the slightly cool touch.

She reflexively withdrew her hand, her fingertips curling up and hiding in her palm.

Their eyes met briefly in the warm yellow firelight.

Sun Rong's cheeks flushed a thin layer of crimson at a speed visible to the naked eye.

She hurriedly lowered her eyes, her long eyelashes trembling like the wings of a startled butterfly, concealing her inner turmoil.

She immediately picked up the glass beside her, pressed the slightly cool glass against her burning cheek, and pretended to focus as she sipped the sweet juice inside.

"Click...click..."

Wang Ling seemed completely unaware of the brief contact and Sun Rong's sudden change in demeanor.

He looked away, down at the meat skewer in his hand, paused for about a second, and then very naturally opened his mouth and took a bite.

The chewing motion remained steady, with his cheeks slightly puffed out, exuding a focused and calm demeanor that seemed out of place amidst the surrounding noise.

It was as if that fleeting touch was nothing more than the most ordinary physical contact when receiving an item, failing to stir even the slightest ripple in his heart.

The flickering light of the campfire danced on his serene profile, as if the subtle moment that had just disturbed his tranquility existed only in Sun Rong's accelerated heartbeat and slightly burning ears.

Taking advantage of the act of lowering her head to drink her juice, she took a deep breath, suppressed the pounding in her heart, and quietly activated the special magical artifact that Meng Nian had given her—an elegant red embroidered pouch.

A cool spiritual energy flowed from the embroidered pouch into her palm, attempting to smooth out the ripples that shouldn't have been there, and allowing her to glimpse some... threads about the future.

With a slight shift in her thoughts, her vision subtly changed.

The noisy scene around the campfire was superimposed on a strange sight in her eyes: apart from the Mountain Moving Corpse, which sat as still as a stone sculpture in the shadows, showing almost no energy reaction, everyone present—Zhang Linyan, Li Changzhe, Su Xingyue, Guo Hao, Chen Chao, Jiugong Liangzi, Liu Qingyi...

Even Wang Zhen and Fang Xing, who were organizing their equipment a little further away, had a semi-transparent countdown timer appearing above their heads, emitting a faint glow!

Even more peculiar is that each person has several thin, red threads extending from their body.

Sun Rong can easily determine who is a couple. By counting down the red numbers, she can tell who is about to lose contact, and by counting down the gold numbers, she can tell who will soon get married.

Of course, a small number of people are connected to one person at the same time.

Sun Rong judged that this should be a kind of unrequited love relationship.

For example, Zhang Linyan has a red thread connecting Chen Chao and Guo Hao above her head.

Sun Rong had already heard the whole story of how she and Li Changzhe rescued Guo Hao and Chen Chao on the way.

In a place as perilous as the inner Earth, the "bridge-building effect" is easily created. When you receive help from a beautiful and exceptionally capable girl in a moment of crisis, it's hard not to be moved.

Suddenly, Sun Rong noticed with curiosity that as the bonfire party continued, the red lines above many people's heads began to change.

Several girls' red threads actually shot towards Wang Ling!

Sun Rong was startled; this was the embroidered pouch's ability to foresee future connections taking effect.

To her surprise, when these red threads, symbolizing affection and unrequited love, touched the fog above Wang Ling's head, they instantly vanished as if they had disappeared into thin air.

"......"

Sun Rong was shocked.

Of everyone present, only she and Wang Ling had fog above their heads.

Is it still possible to do this?

What does fog represent?

Is the person destined to be an only child?

Looking around, she noticed Liu Qingyi's direction.

Despite the warmth of the campfire, Liu Qingyi appeared to be eating the burnt meat skewers Guo Hao had grilled, but her gaze kept darting sharply towards Wang Zhen and Su Xingyue.

Especially when Wang Zhen inquired about Su Xingyue's recovery, a palpable sense of jealousy filled the air.

Sun Rong subtly moved closer to Liu Qingyi, and under the guise of refilling her juice, whispered in a voice only the two of them could hear: "Qingyi, you're mistaken. Wang Zhen and Senior Su have absolutely no relationship. I've checked, Wang Zhen's main connection... isn't with Senior Su."

As she spoke, she glanced meaningfully at the top of Liu Qingyi's head.

Liu Qingyi was slightly taken aback, and her face immediately turned bright red in the warm glow of the campfire.
