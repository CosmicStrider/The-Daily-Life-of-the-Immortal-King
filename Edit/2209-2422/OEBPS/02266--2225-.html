Chapter 2225 The Countdown Above Our Heads

Sun Rong rubbed her eyes vigorously until they were slightly red. She stared intently at the sky above her head, her eyes filled with disbelief.

After checking several times, she finally confirmed that it wasn't her imagination; the bright golden countdown was indeed floating there.

Turning our attention to the tops of Ryoko Kunomiya and Qingyi Liu's heads, we noticed that their countdown times were quite long, with Ryoko Kunomiya's countdown being only slightly shorter than Qingyi Liu's.

Ryoko Kumiya's countdown: 8208:52:18

Liu Qingyi's countdown: 9072:48:16

The constantly ticking seconds, like an hourglass that never stops, relentlessly slipped away second by second. Sun Rong silently calculated in her mind that, in terms of days, Kunomiya Ryoko's countdown was about 342 days, while Liu Qingyi's was 378 days.

Sun Rong knew very well that if she were to tell anyone about this, they would find it utterly absurd, a complete fantasy, and probably no one would believe it.

This feeling of being tightly bound by a mysterious system, unable to break free, and a deep sense of powerlessness—if she weren't experiencing it firsthand right now, she would find it hard to believe that such a bizarre thing could happen to her.

She vaguely guessed that this mysterious golden countdown was definitely connected to the brocade pouch that Meng Nian had given her.

After all, last night's dream was too bizarre; everything seemed so real, yet tinged with an illusory quality. The white-haired, venerable old man in the dream seemed to be conveying some important information to her through subtle hints.

But what exactly is the meaning of this countdown?

Is this a sign that a huge disaster is about to befall us?

Or is it a countdown to someone's death, when their life is about to come to an end?

Or perhaps it's a countdown to a crucial moment when someone is about to break through a difficult bottleneck in their training?

Sun Rong stared intently at the numbers constantly changing above their heads, shook her head slightly, and thought to herself that these guesses didn't seem to make much sense.

"Here's the thing, I can see a countdown timer above each of your heads, and Ryoko's countdown time is shorter than Qingyi's." Sun Rong thought seriously for a while before finally deciding to reveal her hand directly to Ryoko Kunomiya and Qingyi Liu. Her expression was extremely serious, and her eyes were firm, showing no sign of joking.

Upon hearing this, Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi were stunned for a moment, their faces showing surprise. They looked at each other for a while, and then simultaneously put down their exquisitely crafted and beautifully designed coffee cups.

Based on their understanding of Sun Rong, they knew that Sun Rong rarely teased others, and even when she joked, she would never do it with such a serious attitude.

"So, the countdown you're seeing now is most likely because of that embroidery pouch."

Liu Qingyi lowered her head slightly, lost in thought. Then she earnestly analyzed for Sun Rong, "This embroidered pouch from the Immortal Mountain is definitely not an ordinary object. In my opinion, it is very likely a magical artifact with great power. Moreover, it is very likely to have a mysterious artifact spirit."

"So, the old man I saw in my dream last night... was the spirit of the embroidered pouch?" A hint of doubt flashed in Sun Rong's eyes as she asked impatiently.

"Very likely." Liu Qingyi nodded affirmatively, then continued, "This magical effect, which looks like it's been blessed by the system, is very likely the work of the artifact spirit. Miss Sun Rong, you must understand that we are now in the era of scientific cultivation, so the artifact spirits of magical artifacts will also change with the continuous development of modern science. In order to make it easier for cultivators to understand the function of magical artifacts, it is quite reasonable to provide services to the owners of magical artifacts through digital panels."

"But what exactly is this countdown?" Ryoko Kugimiya asked with a heavy accent and a face full of doubt, especially when she heard that her countdown time was less than that of Ryu Qingyi. Her mind was filled with questions, and her brows furrowed involuntarily.

She stared intently at Sun Rong, a hint of urgency in her eyes: "Then, besides ours, can you see other people's?"

"I'll give it a try." Sun Rong said, standing up and walking lightly out of the VIP room.

She leaned against the warm, slightly cool jade railing on the second floor, her gaze sweeping towards the lobby on the first floor.

Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi each held a cup of coffee, appearing calm and composed on the surface, but in reality, their hearts were churning with excitement and curiosity. They followed closely behind Sun Rong, like two children waiting to see the excitement.

The café wasn't very crowded today, making it seem rather quiet. There were only a few scattered customers in the main hall; upon closer inspection, there were five cultivators among them: four male and one female.

One of the male and female cultivators sat together, and judging from their expressions and demeanor, they seemed to be a couple. At this moment, they were drinking coffee with serious expressions, without a trace of a smile on their faces. They were clearly not in a good mood, and there seemed to be a tense atmosphere in the air, as if they had just experienced a fierce argument.

As for the other three male cultivators, they were just ordinary customers. Each of them occupied a small corner of the cafe, holding a notebook in their hands, with a steaming cup of coffee in front of them, and were focused on their work.

"I can only see the countdown of the male and female cultivators sitting opposite each other." Sun Rong cautiously told Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi using the "team chat technique," after all, peeking into other people's countdowns seemed a bit presumptuous.

She spoke with a hint of caution: "It's just that the countdown timers above their heads are all the same, and the time is very short, less than 10 minutes... oh no, now it's less than 9 minutes, and the countdown timer is a bright red."

"Is that strange?" Ryoko Kugimiya was taken aback, a look of surprise on her face. She carefully observed the male and female cultivators sitting opposite each other drinking coffee, her brows furrowing slightly, and said, "They should be a couple."

"Really." Sun Rong looked closely and nodded. She saw that they were wearing a pair of matching couple rings, both on the ring fingers of their left hands, which was undoubtedly a symbol of being in love.

The three people on the second floor quietly observed the couple. In the ensuing silence, the male cultivator spoke first. His expression was extremely conflicted, and his eyes revealed regret and reluctance. He then sighed heavily, "You don't care about me. Do you admit that?"

Upon hearing this, the female monk remained silent for a moment, a complex emotion flashing in her eyes: "I think I've done quite well. If you feel that I don't care about you, I'm sorry."

The male cultivator scratched his head, forcing a smile: "We've been together for half a year, and it seems you've never given me a decent gift."

He looked at the matching rings on their hands, a hint of helplessness in his eyes: "I bought the rings. All the expenses for the various resources we use in our cultivation, such as spirit stones, talismans, and pills, are also borne by me."

Seemingly touched a sore spot, the female cultivator's lips trembled slightly. She then took a deep breath, revealing a relieved expression. She slowly crossed her legs, crossed her arms, and looked at the male cultivator before her with a scrutinizing gaze: "But you are a man. I'm in a relationship with you, shouldn't you be doing these things?"

"But I think that if you truly like someone, then the effort you put in should be equal." The male cultivator's tone was firm, and his eyes revealed his seriousness about the relationship.

"What do you mean? Do you think I haven't done anything? I've told you many times that these are all temporary. If I become successful in the future, of course I will repay you. But you're arguing with me over such a trivial matter. Are you even a man?" The female cultivator chuckled, a hint of disdain on her face, and shrugged.

"Okay, I admit my daily expenses are a bit high, but what relationship doesn't involve expenses? If you really feel that being together is too stressful, then let's end it here." The female cultivator's tone was resolute, and her eyes revealed a hint of impatience.

Sun Rong saw the male cultivator's eyes immediately redden, his body tremble slightly, and his voice tremble as he said, "Break up... You say it so easily..."

"It's just as you wished." The female cultivator chuckled, her eyes showing no lingering affection. She then removed the ring from her hand and slammed it onto the table: "Here's your ring back."
