Chapter 2222 Meng Nian's Redemption

Meng Nian stood there blankly, her eyes vacant and lost. That brief reunion that had just transcended the boundaries of soul and body felt like a fleeting dream, leaving her unable to process it.

Images of her time with her loved ones kept replaying in her mind, but now, everything had vanished, leaving only endless pain.

For her, this process was undoubtedly another heart-wrenching experience of losing a loved one. The agonizing feeling of her heart being torn apart was like countless needles piercing her heart, leaving her completely immersed in a depressed mood from which she could not extricate herself.

After a long while, Meng Nian suddenly snapped out of her dazed state, a hint of urgency flashing in her eyes.

She stumbled and crawled to Wang Ling's side, gripping his trouser legs tightly with both hands, her knuckles turning white from the force.

Her voice trembled slightly and pleaded, "Immortal! Since you have such supernatural powers, why not do a good deed and help my brother and me reunite? Moreover, Aheng's soul has turned into a soul bead, like a pill. Is it possible... like Nezha's mother, Yin Shiniang, who was refined into a pill and could still have a chance to be resurrected?"

Wang Ling frowned slightly, sighed helplessly, and couldn't help but secretly criticize this hellish joke. He thought that the influence of this 3-year-old billion-dollar movie star was still too far-reaching, even such an example could be brought up.

Wang Ling does indeed possess the ability to fully resurrect a person, but he has previously mentioned the issue of the balance of power in the Heavenly Dao many times.

Simply put, if Meng Heng had lived on like this, then in the current human realm, there would definitely be an innocent cultivator who would be affected by the balance of the Heavenly Dao, have his causal laws triggered, and then die.

If he were to resurrect Meng Heng without any cost, it would be too unfair to the other cultivators who perished for various reasons.

Moreover, Meng Heng killed so many innocent lives in order to resurrect his younger brother, so Wang Ling had no reason to directly resurrect Meng Heng.

"Your cultivation is not low either. You should understand the principle of the balance of the heavens." Wang Ling looked at Meng Nian, who was kneeling beside him and pleading bitterly, and said indifferently.

Upon hearing this, Meng Nian's body trembled slightly. She fell silent for a moment, her face showing a pained and conflicted expression.

Her mind was filled with images of the tragic deaths of innocent people and the many wise words Meng Heng had spoken to her.

These words struck her like a hammer blow, waking her up completely and making her realize that she had committed an unforgivable crime.

From Meng Nian's perspective, the saying goes that every wrong has its perpetrator and every debt its debtor. After realizing her mistake, Meng Nian was filled with remorse. She felt that she should have gone to great lengths to find the female swindler and appease the heavens instead of dwelling on the death of her younger brother and causing harm to many innocent lives in her attempt to revive Meng Heng.

Her body went limp on the ground, her eyes filled with regret and self-blame.

"But the soul condensing into a soul bead, was that something Senior Brother Wang Ling did intentionally?" Sun Rong looked at the bead in Wang Ling's hand that emitted a dark blue light, a hint of doubt flashing in her eyes, and asked.

"Hmm, no." Wang Ling shook his head slightly, a complex look in his eyes.

Although he only found Meng Heng's soul and had Meng Heng persuade Meng Nian, his original intention was not to directly revive Meng Heng.

After persuading Meng Nian, Meng Heng's soul transformed into a soul bead, a result of many intertwined factors.

This is a symbol of cruelty.

All I can say is that during the time he was trapped in the void after death, Meng Heng wandered in darkness every day, with loneliness and fear following him like a shadow. He truly regretted it and realized that he shouldn't have given up his life so easily.

Secondly, he felt immense guilt and remorse after seeing his sister Meng Nian kill so many living beings in order to revive him.

Thirdly, there was Meng Heng's deep longing for Meng Nian. Every little detail from childhood to adulthood played back in his mind like a movie. These mixed emotions prevented Meng Heng's soul from completely dissipating, but instead transformed into a soul bead that remained.

Under the condition of abiding by the balance of the heavens, the method of restoring the soul pearl to its original form as a soul, and then reshaping the physical body to revive it, still exists in the royal decree.

It just requires Meng Nian to spend a lot of effort.

Wang Ling half-squatted down and gently handed Meng Heng's soul pearl to Meng Nian, his eyes showing a hint of gentleness: "I have a method."

Wang Ling looked around at the messy laboratory, with broken instruments and scattered debris in disarray, and a pungent smell permeating the air. He couldn't help but sigh.

Finally, he left a "life" mark on the Soul Pearl and told Meng Heng how to revive him.

Simply put, for every life Meng Nian kills, he needs to redeem a hundred times that number. The redeemed lives will contribute a portion of their soul energy to replenish the energy of the "Life" seal. When there is enough energy, Meng Heng can truly be resurrected.

"Thank you, Immortal..." Meng Nian's eyes almost immediately filled with tears. She carefully took the Soul Pearl and hugged Meng Heng's Soul Pearl into her arms as if it were a precious treasure, as if it were the most precious thing in her life.

"You must walk your own path to redemption from now on," Wang Ling said, nodding slightly.

This is actually the best approach than to arrest or kill Meng Nian directly.

And Meng Nian has also shifted from the path of killing to the path of redemption, which is not necessarily a bad thing.

Moreover, Meng Nian's identity is very special. Although she is only an outer envoy chosen by the Underworld, Wang Ling had a premonition that he might still have dealings with the Underworld after entering the Earth's core. Taking all factors into consideration, he spared Meng Nian's life, allowing her to atone for her sins in this way.

However, those creatures that were persecuted in the laboratory will never return...

At that moment, Wang Ling stepped onto the foul-smelling, sticky liquid on the ground and walked to the center. His body emitted a dazzling golden light, which, like warm sunlight, illuminated the entire dark laboratory.

The golden light quickly spread and filled the surrounding area. He made a hand seal with one hand and chanted, "Great Salvation Technique."

In an instant, the foul-smelling slime and various limbs and tissues decomposed by the spiritual liquid on the ground of the entire biochemical laboratory were enveloped by golden light and soared into the air. In the end, they transformed into countless golden butterflies, fluttering with countless golden particles, like twinkling stars, flying into the void... This was the last thing Wang Ling could do.

At the same time, the entire illusion laboratory collapsed, and the surrounding space cracks peeled off layer by layer like peeling paint, with warm sunlight seeping through the cracks and shining on everyone.

When Wang Ling and Sun Rong came to their senses, they returned to the ward.

Everything in the ward seemed so quiet and peaceful, a stark contrast to the terrifying laboratory we had just left.

As if she had just woken from a dream, Meng Nian held the Soul Pearl in her hands, her eyes filled with tears as she looked at Wang Ling and Sun Rong, her eyes brimming with gratitude.

Meng Nian had never imagined that there were such powerful human cultivators in the cultivation world today. A sense of respect welled up in her heart, and she immediately said to Wang Ling, "Immortal, rest assured, I will not do evil again... and I am willing to be your pawn in the Underworld."

Wang Ling nodded, a hint of approval in his eyes.

Meng Nian's words are enough.

"Don't worry, Sister Meng Nian. We've already used our resources to find that female swindler. I believe we'll have results soon and bring her to justice." Sun Rong smiled at Meng Nian, her face radiating confidence and determination.

"Thank you." Meng Nian wiped away her tears. Looking at the young man and woman in front of her, she felt that the two were a perfect match. After thinking for a moment, she took out a red embroidered pouch from her bosom and handed it to Sun Rong.

"What is this?" Sun Rong was taken aback, her eyes full of doubt. She took the embroidery bag and gently stroked the exquisite embroidery on it.

"Miss Sun, rest assured, this is not some kind of underworld artifact, but something I obtained from Mount Immortality."

Meng Nian whispered to Sun Rong with a mysterious smile on her face, "This item has magical power to enhance marriage prospects."

After saying that, Sun Rong's ears turned red, and her face became burning hot, like a ripe apple, full of shyness.

"Sister Meng Nian, you've misunderstood. Senior Brother Wang Ling and I are just fellow disciples..." Sun Rong stammered, a hint of panic in her eyes.

"Go forward boldly." Meng Nian interrupted Sun Rong directly, smiling with gentle encouragement in her eyes: "Cherish the people in front of you."

Then she looked at Wang Ling and said, "Immortal, you are too."
