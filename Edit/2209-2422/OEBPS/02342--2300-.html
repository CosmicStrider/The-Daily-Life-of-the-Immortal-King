Chapter 2300 Psionic Combat Assessment

The next day, the most important part of the final exams for the Number 60 High School, the "Practical Application of Psychic Energy" assessment, finally began in the gymnasium.

The huge circular field was already packed with students, and the air was filled with noise.

The air was filled with the sounds of tense breathing and heartbeats, as well as a restlessness hidden deeper within.

The students in the elite class were mostly calm and collected, whispering among themselves and assessing their potential opponents.

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, Sun Rong... because they had already entered the Golden Core stage ahead of schedule, they were directly granted exemption from the exam.

But Chen Chao wasn't particularly happy. He was a bum and didn't want to miss this opportunity to show himself.

"Why are we all exempt from the exam, but Lingzi still has to take it?" Chen Chao protested.

"After all, Wang Ling is still at the Foundation Establishment stage... so it's relatively fairer."

Sun Rong gave an awkward smile and tried to smooth things over for Wang Ling.

She stood beside Wang Ling, her gaze sweeping across the area of the regular classes opposite, lingering for a moment on the members of the "Taste Club" who wore silver watches on their wrists, a hint of worry on her pretty face.

Wang Ling remained as calm as ever, his school uniform zipper zipped up to the top, covering half his face, his hands in his pockets.

He spent most of his time detachedly observing the entire arena and the undercurrents lurking beneath the surface.

On the judges' panel, teachers such as Mr. Pan and Mr. Lao Gu Dong sat upright, and even the principal, Mr. Chen Tianxiang, came to watch the competition.

In addition, several leaders from the Taipei City Council of Schools and Colleges visited, creating a solemn atmosphere.

"Round one, random matchmaking! Keep it to a minimum, no intentional serious injury to your opponent! Now, the light screen will begin to scroll!" The physical education teacher in charge of the assessment spoke in a booming voice.

On the huge screen, names flashed rapidly before finally settling on a single image.

[Number 60 High School Regular Class]

[Li Jun from Class 3, Grade 11 VS Zhang Hao from Class 7, Grade 11]

A considerable commotion broke out in the audience.

These two rivals actually met in the very first round!

Li Jun took a deep breath and stood up from the waiting area for the regular class.

His face was still somewhat pale, but his eyes were unusually firm as he walked steadily toward the ring.

At this moment, Li Jun's wrist was empty; the [Taste Master] that he once treasured was completely locked in the cabinet.

On the other side, Zhang Hao, with a sly grin, easily jumped onto the ring.

The silver watch on my wrist reflected a dazzling light in the sunlight.

He looked at Li Jun provocatively and deliberately swayed.

"Hey, you poor bastard, I didn't expect to run into you in the first round?"

Zhang Hao lowered his voice, his tone filled with malice: "Without that thing, what are you? I won't show any mercy today!"

Li Jun ignored his trash talk and calmly gave a salute in response.

Then, the most basic starting stance of the "Sixty Basic Body-Strengthening Boxing" was adopted.

His movements were precise and meticulous, which drew snickers from Zhang Hao and his followers.

"Pretentious!" Zhang Hao spat, not even bothering to bow, and instantly unleashed his spiritual power!

The aura of a mid-Foundation Establishment cultivator erupted, though it was weak, it carried a fierce energy amplified by the [Taste Master]!

With a stomp of his foot, he moved with astonishing speed, throwing a punch straight at Li Jun's face. The wind from the punch was howling, and he had actually used the powerful "Stone-Splitting Fist"!

A gasp of surprise erupted from the audience.

Zhang Hao clearly used excessive force!

Sun Rong unconsciously frowned.

Chen Chao was so shocked that he stood up and looked around: "Has Zhang Hao taken the wrong medicine?"

Only Wang Ling looked calmly at the arena.

Faced with this fierce punch, Li Jun did not take it head-on. Instead, he used basic footwork to slide to the side and narrowly avoid it.

The force of the punch grazed his cheek, causing a sharp pain and leaving a wound.

"Hide? Let's see how long you can hide!" Zhang Hao, having gained the upper hand, was relentless, unleashing a torrent of punches and kicks!

His moves were ruthless, completely exceeding the scope of mere friendly sparring.

Li Jun was forced into a completely defensive posture, relying on his solid basic skills to accurately dodge within a narrow area.

He only managed to block when he couldn't avoid it, but Zhang Hao's strike was incredibly powerful, making his arm go numb and leaving Li Jun looking completely vulnerable.

"Useless piece of trash! All you know how to do is hide!"

"Li Jun, fight back! You won't get any points by just dodging!"

There was a lot of discussion among the audience.

Many people began to worry about Li Jun. Although Zhang Hao's momentum was strong, it had clearly exceeded the scope of the assessment. Most people were somewhat ashamed of such behavior.

Zhang Hao, however, became even more smug, shouting incessantly, "What's wrong? Where's that arrogance you had in the alley the other day? Bring it out, you piece of trash!"

He didn't realize that Li Jun's seemingly embarrassed eyes were growing brighter and brighter.

Having discarded the false perceptions and restless power brought by the [Taste Master], his five senses returned to their original state, and he became increasingly clear about the flow of spiritual energy and the trajectory of his opponent's movements.

Although Zhang Hao's attacks were fierce, in his eyes they were full of unnecessary movements caused by weak strength.

Its weaknesses became increasingly apparent after Li Jun got used to Zhang Hao's offensive rhythm.

Just as Zhang Hao swept a roundhouse kick toward Li Jun's waist!

Li Jun made his move!

He didn't use any fancy techniques; he simply poured all the most basic spiritual power in his body into his right fist, seized the fleeting opening, and delivered a simple, basic punch, striking first despite being behind!

"Bang!"

A dull thud resounded.

Zhang Hao's smirk froze instantly, revealing an incredulous look in his eyes.

He felt as if his calf had hit a hard iron stake!

In an instant, a sharp pain pierced my heart!

He lost his balance and fell backward with a scream.

Li Jun withdrew his fist, his body as steady as a mountain.

The entire room fell silent for a moment.

No one expected the situation to reverse so quickly!

Immediately afterwards, a huge bang erupted at the scene!

"Holy crap! Li Jun won? He only used one punch?"

"Wasn't he the one who kept getting beaten up?"

The teachers on the judging panel also showed expressions of appreciation.

A hint of relief flashed in Li Jun's homeroom teacher's eyes.

The teachers could all see that the spiritual power contained in Li Jun's punch was not strong.

But both the timing and the exertion of force were incredibly precise!

It demonstrates a profound understanding and application of basic exercises!

This is far more precious than any realm that can be forcibly elevated by relying on external means!

Zhang Hao rolled on the ground, clutching his legs, filled with shame and anger. He suddenly raised his head and stared intently at Li Jun, his eyes filled with resentment and unwillingness.

"You...you tricked me!" he roared, instinctively reaching for the [Taste Master] on his wrist, attempting to forcibly increase his power and get revenge.

However, the instant his consciousness touched the device—

"Ughhhh!"

Zhang Hao suddenly clutched his head and let out an even more agonizing scream.
