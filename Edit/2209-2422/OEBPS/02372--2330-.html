Chapter 2330 Murderous Intent

Under the cover of night, Seiryu High School was immersed in a deathly silence before a storm.

The atmosphere on the third floor of the faculty and staff dormitory was so oppressive that it was hard to breathe.

Wang Ling held the bamboo skewer that emitted a faint aroma of roasted sausage, lost in thought.

Having just gone through the previous Abyss Gate mission, he could roughly guess what it meant when a key item mentioned in such a rule suddenly appeared under the bed.

This is clearly a way of framing someone, and perhaps it will directly attract Lin Wanwan's vengeful spirit to come here at night.

The black-robed teammate in the same room dared not breathe, frozen in place, his eyes filled with despair.

"M-My lord...this...this thing can't be kept!" The black-robed team member's voice trembled, and he was almost crying.

Rule number eight is clearly displayed overhead, prohibiting grilled sausages and related items; these bamboo skewers are the most direct death warrant.

Wang Ling ignored his panic, and the sneer of Wang Ying inside him rang out: "Heh, you're trying to embarrass yourself with such a little trick? Looks like someone's getting impatient and wants to use that little girl's vengeful spirit to get rid of us."

"......"

Wang Ling could almost foresee the ending.

If Lin Wanwan really approaches him, then there will probably only be one outcome: he will be directly exorcised, and the mission will be completed on the spot.

Wang Ling himself was unable to gather any effective intelligence.

At this moment, Wang Ling's spiritual consciousness stirred slightly and spread out silently.

He had a suspicion in his mind and needed to verify its authenticity.

"More than one."

A moment later, Wang Ling spoke calmly, but it sent chills down the spines of the black-robed team member next to him.

"Wh...what, there's more than one?"

Wang Ling did not explain, but the spiritual consciousness he had spread out had already silently penetrated the wall and swept towards the neighboring dormitory and even further away.

From this "God's-eye view," he could clearly see the other shadow agents involved in the mission. Under their beds, he saw similar sausage skewers, but in varying numbers; some had only one skewer, while others had more than a dozen piled up under their beds.

In their room, there was only this one under his bed.

"Interesting." Wang Yingrao analyzed with interest: "It seems that this vengeful spirit kills people by sorting them by number. The more bamboo sticks there are, the easier it is to become a target. I wonder how many people this little girl can kill in one night."

Listening to Wang Ying's analysis, Wang Ling instantly understood the meticulous calculations of the person behind this scheme.

He not only wanted to use Lin Wanwan to kill people, but also wanted to use fear and suspicion to cause chaos among these shadow agents.

With rules in place, everyone is insecure, so who would bother to explore the school's secrets?

Seeing Wang Ling staring silently at the bamboo skewer, the black-robed man in the same room turned his fear into a hint of sternness: "Sir! How about... we throw it out? Or... frame those guys in Group B?"

In order to survive, the dark side of human nature begins to emerge.

Wang Ling glanced at him, a glance devoid of any emotion, yet it instantly silenced the black-robed team member.

The black-robed team member felt as if he had been swept away by a prehistoric beast, and his soul trembled.

Then Wang Ling went straight out the door to the dormitory at the end of the corridor, and then quickly returned. The whole process took no more than three minutes.

The man in black robes in the same room asked suspiciously, "Sir... what is this?"

"Don't ask, go to sleep."

Wang Ling remained as taciturn as ever, and then did something that left the black-robed team members dumbfounded.

He casually stuffed the deadly bamboo skewer back into its original position under the bed, even kicking it further in to make it more concealed.

"M-Master..." The black-robed team member was completely dumbfounded.

"You're in charge of keeping watch." Wang Ling assigned the task without further explanation, then lay down on the bed, closed his eyes, as if the thing under the bed didn't exist at all.

Black-robed team member: "..."

He looked at "Zhou Ziwei" who seemed to be really asleep on the bed, then looked under the bed and felt like he was going crazy.

He simply couldn't understand the thinking of this Bloodstained Lord!

But his strong will to survive and the pressure of his rank prevented him from disobeying. He could only return to bed tremblingly, cover himself with the blanket, and then stare intently at the door, his nerves on edge.

...

Meanwhile, things were not peaceful in the other dormitories as well.

When Zhang Yu found the bamboo skewer under his bed, his face instantly turned ashen.

He almost immediately thought of Zhou Ziwei's thoughtful expression during the day.

"Zhou Ziwei...he must have discovered something! These bamboo sticks, maybe he's the one who did it! He wants to get rid of me first?" Zhang Yu's eyes were sinister, and he subconsciously locked his suspicions onto his old rival.

In the competition among lurkers, such underhanded tactics are commonplace.

He didn't make a fuss, but quickly threw the bamboo skewers outside the dormitory.

He made up his mind that he must join forces with others tomorrow to take down this dangerous Zhou Ziwei.

The other teaching assistant dormitories were unusually quiet, as if they hadn't noticed the bamboo skewers. Their skewers were probably hidden in more concealed places or simply stuck to the bed boards.

Regardless, the seeds of suspicion had already taken root and sprouted in the two lurker families along with that small sausage skewer.

...

Just past midnight, a faint aroma of grilled sausages, carrying a strange yet alluring charm, suddenly began to permeate the corridors of the faculty and staff apartment building.

The aroma was much stronger than what I smelled during the day, making me swallow hard while a chill ran through me.

"They're here..." Wang Ling and Wang Ying instantly became alert.

The black-robed teammate in the same room quickly covered his mouth to prevent a scream from escaping his throat.

He was trembling violently, so frightened he could hardly breathe, his eyes fixed on the faint shadowy figure under the crack in the door.

The aroma of grilled sausages grew stronger and stronger, as if it were lingering right outside the door.

Immediately afterwards, a series of light footsteps echoed in the corridor.

These were clearly not the footsteps of a normal human being; the footsteps were ethereal and even carried a strong sense of menace.

Everyone who was awake had their heart in their throat.

The footsteps paused for a moment outside Zhang Yu's dormitory door.

Zhang Yu held his breath, gathered all his spiritual energy, and gripped a magical artifact he had brought out from the previous few abyssal gates, preparing for a desperate fight.

However, the ghost's footsteps only paused for a few seconds before continuing forward.

It swept past Wang Ling's dormitory without stopping for a moment, heading straight for the dormitory at the end of the corridor, where ten bamboo skewers were stuck under the bed.

"ah!!"

Immediately afterwards, a short scream suddenly erupted from the dormitory at the far end!

The entire floor fell into an eerie silence.
