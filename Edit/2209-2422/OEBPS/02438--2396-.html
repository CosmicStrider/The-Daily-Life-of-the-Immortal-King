Chapter 2396 Domain of Misfortune

Morning light streamed through the restaurant's floor-to-ceiling windows, illuminating the dining tables.

The VIP restaurant at Dituni Park offers a buffet-style dining experience with a wide variety of gourmet delicacies.

The crystal dumplings gleamed with a pale golden luster, the rice porridge steamed, and there were various adorable desserts.

Chen Chao held the plate, his eyes practically glued to the food.

"My goodness... this breakfast is better than even a five-star hotel!" He picked up a dragon-shaped bun, the dragon scales clearly visible, and bit into it to find a molten salted egg yolk and custard filling.

Guo Hao was more restrained and only chose a spiritual vegetable salad and a boiled egg: "Chao'er, consuming too much high-spiritual-energy food for breakfast may lead to excessive mental excitement in the morning."

"Who cares! Let's eat first!" Chen Chao picked up three more steamed buns.

Li Changzhe carefully carried a bowl of spirit rice porridge and sat down next to Wang Ling: "Wang Ling, is this all you're eating?"

On Wang Ling's plate were a bowl of white porridge, two boiled eggs, three slices of toast, and... five packets of different flavored instant noodles, neatly arranged around the edge of the plate.

"Hmm." Wang Ling tore open a packet of barbecue-flavored instant noodles and ate it with white porridge.

Sun Rong sat opposite him, sipping her juice, her gaze occasionally drifting towards Wang Ling.

There wasn't much on her plate today, but she specifically chose the same spirit rice porridge as Wang Ling.

"Um... Wang Ling," Sun Rong said softly, "The 'Mist Maze' requires teams of three later. Have you and Chen Chao teamed up yet?"

Before Wang Ling could speak, Chen Chao immediately chimed in, "It's all set! I'm in a group with Guo Hao and Li Changzhe! Lingzi, of course you're in a group with Sun Rong!"

As he spoke, he winked at Guo Hao.

Guo Hao sighed: "According to the park's rules, the Mist Maze does require a team of three or more. Wang Ling and Sun Rong need to find another person."

"I can do it!" Chen Chao raised his hand.

"You're not going to be in our group anymore?" Li Changzhe was stunned.

"Hey, we can make some adjustments!" Chen Chao winked. "Your son and Sun Rong are missing one of them, and as a brother, it's my duty to help!"

Sun Rong blushed slightly, said nothing, and lowered her head to drink her porridge.

Wang Ling tore open another packet of instant noodles; this time it was seaweed flavored.

"Either is fine," he said.

Chen Chao immediately made the decision: "Then it's settled! Me, Lingzi, and Sun Rong will be a team! Guo Hao, you and Li Changzhe can find someone else, like Xiaolin You or Liu Kaichuan!"

Guo Hao glanced at Chen Chao helplessly, but did not refute.

He actually saw through Chen Chao's thoughts.

The so-called "flexible adjustment" was simply a way to observe the interaction between Wang Ling and Sun Rong up close.

But it's good that Chen Chao is around; the atmosphere won't be awkward.

As the group was eating, a commotion broke out at the restaurant entrance.

Yoshiko Kunomiya walked in.

Today she changed into a pale yellow dress, her hair was simply tied up with a jade hairpin, and she still held that folding fan in her hand.

Two staff members in amusement park uniforms followed behind him.

"Good morning, everyone." Ryoko Kumiya smiled warmly. "Was breakfast to your liking?"

Chen Chao gave a thumbs up: "Jiugong, I could eat this for a year!"

Ryoko Kugimiya chuckled, a hint of satisfaction flashing in her eyes as she glanced at Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

"All the attractions in the park are ready today." She walked to the center of the restaurant and said clearly, "Also, I have some good news to share: the check-in challenge has added a teamwork scoring mechanism."

She gestured to the staff to turn on the holographic projection.

The newly added rule is displayed on the screen:

[For two-person/multi-person collaborative projects, the system will assign a teamwork score based on factors such as coordination, communication efficiency, and completion time. A score of S will earn an additional stamp.]

"So that means..." Sun Rong's eyes lit up, "If we coordinate well, we can collect all the seals even faster?"

"That's right." Ryoko Kumiya nodded. "Moreover, some projects also have hidden challenges, and you can get extra stamps by completing them."

Chen Chao rubbed his hands excitedly: "Lingzi, Sun Rong, let's try to get S-level on all of them!"

Wang Ling's eyes flickered slightly as he looked at the words "extra stamp" on the screen.

He's determined to get the instant noodle gift pack for 100 people.

The more stamps you have, the more certain you are to your decision.

"Also..." Ryoko Kugimiya's smile faded, and her tone became more serious: "Last night, the park's security system detected some abnormal energy fluctuations. Although it has been dealt with, please still be careful. If you encounter any suspicious persons or situations, immediately use the emergency call function on your wristband."

As she spoke, her gaze swept over Wang Ling almost imperceptibly.

Wang Lingzheng was focused on peeling and boiling the eggs, seemingly oblivious to what was happening.

But Ryoko Kumiya knew he must have heard it.

Moreover, she knew exactly how those "abnormal energy fluctuations" were handled last night.

"Alright, everyone, please continue with your meal." Ryoko Kunomiya turned and left. "The attractions will open promptly at nine o'clock. Have a great time!"

She walked out of the restaurant, tapping her folding fan lightly in her palm.

Wang Zhen's voice came through the earpiece: "Liangzi, those four guys are in position. They are at the entrance of the 'Mist Maze', the control panel of the 'Extreme Roller Coaster', the control room of the 'Abyss Drop Tower', and one is in the maintenance passage of the 'Heart-to-Heart Bridge'."

"Is there any news from Master Ling's side?" Ryoko Kugimiya asked.

"Although Master Ling didn't react much, I have a feeling he's already set everything up..." Wang Zhen's voice was tinged with amusement. "The one at the entrance to the mist maze slipped on a banana peel five minutes ago and tripped, and is now limping around rearranging the traps. I bet Master Ling has set up some kind of magic array to make him unlucky or something..."

Ryoko Kumiya couldn't help but laugh: "This is just the beginning."

"Do you need me to intervene?"

"No need." Ryoko Kugimiya shook her head. "Let them play by themselves. Our focus is on ensuring the safety of ordinary tourists, and... creating opportunities for Ren and Rongrong."

"clear."

Communication ended.

Ryoko Kugimiya stood in the corridor outside the restaurant, looking inside through the glass.

Wang Ling had finished breakfast and was putting the last piece of instant noodles into his mouth.

Sun Rong sat opposite him, whispering something, her eyes sparkling.

Chen Chao was gesturing wildly beside him, probably coming up with some bad idea again.

The scene is very harmonious.

Ryoko Kunomiya smiled.

"Today is going to be very interesting."

...

8:50 a.m.

A queue had already formed at the entrance to the mist maze.

Wang Ling, Sun Rong, and Chen Chao were in the middle of the line.

Chen Chao, holding the amusement park map, was analyzing: "The maze covers 2,000 square meters, with randomly generated paths, traps, and puzzles. The official recommended completion time is 45 minutes to an hour. Let's try to finish it in 30 minutes!"

Sun Rong was a little worried: "Won't it be too difficult?"

"With Lingzi here, what's there to be afraid of!" Chen Chao patted Wang Ling on the shoulder: "Lingzi's puzzle-solving skills are top-notch! Right, Lingzi?"

Wang Ling nodded: "Mm."

Actually, he didn't even need to solve the mystery.

In his perception, the entire maze was transparent; he knew exactly which path led to which mechanism, how to activate it, and which corner harbored staff.

But he won't say it.

Because Sun Rong seemed to be really looking forward to this challenge.

The procession moved forward slowly.

Two staff members were standing at the entrance; one was scanning the wristbands, and the other was explaining the rules.

"Enter in groups of three. There are seven checkpoints inside the maze. You can only find the exit after activating all of them. The wristband will display a map and checkpoint progress in real time. If you get lost or encounter problems, you can press the help button on the wristband."

Soon it was Wang Ling's turn.

As the staff scanned the wristbands, their gaze lingered on Wang Ling for a second longer, but they quickly resumed their professional smile: "Please come in, all three of you. Good luck."

The entrance to the maze is a rotating portal of light.

The moment I stepped inside, the surrounding scenery changed abruptly.

Before me lay a dark passage, its walls made of rough stones, with shimmering moss growing in the cracks.

The air was damp, carrying the smell of earth and rotten wood.

Above us was a simulated cave ceiling, with occasional water droplets falling and making a "drip-drip" sound.

The passageway was only wide enough for two people to walk side by side, so Chen Chao consciously walked to the front.

"I'll clear the way!" he said with great enthusiasm.

Sun Rong walked in the middle, with Wang Ling at the rear.

The wristband screen lights up, displaying a simple map—their current location is a dot of light, with a large area in front of them in darkness.

"The first checkpoint shouldn't be far," Chen Chao said, looking at the map. "Let's walk forward and take a look."

The passage wasn't long; after walking about thirty meters, we reached a fork in the road.

There is a road on the left and a road on the right.

Strange symbols, like some kind of ancient script, were painted on the wall of the passageway to the left.

Several wind chimes hang in the passageway to the right, making a crisp sound when a breeze blows through them.

"Which side should we choose?" Chen Chao asked, turning around.

Sun Rong carefully observed both sides: "The symbol on the left... I feel like I've seen it somewhere before."

She walked up to the symbol and touched it.

The symbol suddenly lit up with a pale blue light!

At the same time, the bracelet vibrated and displayed a message:

[Uncover the mystery: Decipher the runes and choose the correct path]

The symbols on the wall began to change, forming three different patterns.

One is Mars, one is Venus, and one is Jupiter.

"What does this mean?" Chen Chao scratched his head.

Sun Rong pondered, "Mars, Venus, Jupiter... could this be the order of the planets in the solar system?"

Wang Ling stood behind her and glanced at the symbol.

It's actually very simple: these three patterns correspond to three hidden psionic circuits. Just press and hold the correct three-second interval to open the corresponding channel.
