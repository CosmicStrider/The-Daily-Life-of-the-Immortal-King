Chapter 2344 The Shadow Warriors are completely wiped out

In Songhai, the night was deep. Zhou Ziwei suddenly sat up in bed, his forehead covered in cold sweat, his heart pounding violently.

He had a very long and incredibly vivid dream.

In his dream, he wasn't the quiet, good student in the elite class who wore gold-rimmed glasses, but a warrior draped in golden light, facing terrifying sea monsters and the Sun Island demon amidst a vast army!

At this moment, the deafening shouts of "Hua Xiu Immortal!" still seemed to echo in his ears...

That exhilarating feeling of fighting to protect his homeland was etched vividly into the depths of his soul.

"Strange...why would I have such a dream?" Zhou Ziwei muttered to himself.

He felt his mind was clearer than ever before, as if a huge burden had been lifted, and even the flow of spiritual energy within his body became much smoother.

Just as he was immersed in the lingering charm of his dream, a wisp of barely perceptible black smoke silently emerged from between his brows and then quickly dissipated into the air without leaving any trace.

Zhou Ziwei was slightly dazed, feeling as if he had forgotten something.

But he couldn't remember exactly what it was.

"Never mind, it's probably just some trivial matter." He shook his head and didn't delve into it.

At this moment, he felt completely relaxed, enveloped by a long-lost sense of tranquility.

He lay back down, pulled the covers up, and quickly drifted back into a peaceful sleep, a slight smile even playing on his lips.

...

In the distance, the Wang family's small villa.

Wang Ling calmly withdrew the spiritual consciousness that had enveloped the entire city.

The last trace of Zhou Ziwei's identity as a "Shadowman" had been completely purified, and his related memories vanished with the dissipation of the black mist.

It's not just Zhou Ziwei.

At this moment, tens of thousands of former "Shadow People" scattered throughout the country of Huaxiu are experiencing similar changes.

They might remember a thrilling "dream," but they would never remember anything about the "House of Shadows."

This "Shadow Shaders" organization, meticulously constructed by the evil cultivators of the Primordial Era, has been uprooted by Wang Ling in a decisive and ruthless manner.

"Finally solved." Wang Ling murmured, feeling extremely relieved.

Although one potential threat has been eliminated, Wang Ling is well aware that the evil cultivators behind the scenes will not let the matter rest.

Bai Zhe, and the external divine forces behind him, must have even bigger schemes up their sleeves.

However, at least until the next storm hits, he can enjoy a rare period of peaceful daily life.

Right now, the thing that brings him the most happiness in his daily life is the upcoming "Glory League" city-level selection competition and the tempting ten-year snack gift pack reward.

With this in mind, Wang Ling no longer hesitated.

He lay back down on the bed and closed his eyes.

There is absolutely no need for any bulky psionic e-sports cabins.

For a Celestial King who can cover the entire world with a single thought, precisely connecting his consciousness to a virtual game world created by humans is incredibly simple.

He concentrated his will intensely, imagining his consciousness detaching from his body and precisely projecting itself onto the server nodes of "Glory Alliance" along an invisible torrent of data.

Buzz—

The next moment, Wang Ling's vision suddenly opened up.

He found himself in a technologically advanced virtual space, which was the login screen for the game "Honor of Kings".

Majestic hero statues surround the area, and in the very center stands an ordinary-looking little figure holding a sword, with his unremarkable game ID displayed above his head—【L】.

Almost at the same time he went online.

"bite!"

He recalled President Liu from Chihuo High School, whose playing style was aggressive, and found it difficult to connect the two images.

After a moment's hesitation, he clicked "accept".

He does need to familiarize himself with the environment and rhythm of high-score games.

However, Wang Ling actually prefers to play solo...

【Whirlwind Qingqingzi】: No way, great master! Name your conditions! As long as I can do it! Don't let my high school student appearance fool you, I actually have some money, equipment, spirit stones, and even some cultivation resources... Or, what do you wish for?

Who is this person? A classmate from Number 60 High School?
