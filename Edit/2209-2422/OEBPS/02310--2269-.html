Chapter 2269 The Secret Deep Within the Source Eye

Within the Earth Vein Source Eye space, the chaotic mother fluid gradually calmed down, but the aftershocks of the forced extraction and subsequent backflow still left ripples in the air.

Sun Rong took a deep breath, trying to calm the shock in her heart.

The image of Wang Ling extinguishing Bai Zhe's manifested will and Dao Ancestor-level attack in one breath will probably be forever etched in her mind.

His gaze shifted to the Earth Vein Source Eye, which had returned to calm but whose light was slightly dim. He tilted his head slightly, as if he were thinking about something.

"Wang Ling, is this the source of all the energy in the Earth's core?" Sun Rong asked softly.

She could feel that the activity of the Ocean Sword Core and the power of the Crimson Lotus in this place was far greater than before, and the primordial stone given by the spirit of the ancient land was slightly hot in her palm.

Wang Ling nodded and squatted down at the edge of the source.

This time, instead of tasting the mother liquor, he extended his right index finger and gently touched the energy membrane on the surface of the source eye.

Buzz—

A gentle, seven-colored ripple spread out from his fingertip, instantly sweeping across the entire surface of the Source Eye.

The source eye, which was originally somewhat dim, seemed to be injected with new vitality. The chaotic light became bright and pure at a speed visible to the naked eye, even more dazzling than before it was corroded!

Sun Rong's eyes widened. She clearly sensed that Wang Ling was not only purifying the Source Eye, but also upgrading it in a way she couldn't understand!

After doing all this, Wang Ling stood up and looked down at a dark area directly below the Source Eye.

It appeared to be empty, but as the light from the source eye intensified, a complex three-dimensional array composed of countless interwoven light filaments gradually emerged.

The diagram was deeply imprinted in the rock, yet it seemed to connect to a deeper dimension.

"This is..." Sun Rong approached, and the scabbard of the Ao Hai sword emitted a faint, warning hum.

At the center of the array, there was a clear gap, the shape of which was exactly similar to the primordial stone in her hand.

Wang Ling looked at the pebble in her hand and nodded slightly.

Sun Rong immediately understood and carefully placed the warm, smooth pebble into the empty space in the center of the array.

A perfect fit!

The moment the stone was put back in its place, the entire array suddenly lit up!

Countless filaments of light flickered and flowed like an activated neural network, and a holographic projection unfolded above the array—

It was a distorted space beyond description, where countless viscous, black, tentacle-like substances entwined around a faintly shimmering sphere of light (the origin of the ancient spirit), while in the shadows nearby, a blurry dragon-shaped outline was constantly injecting some kind of dark red energy into the tentacles, accelerating the erosion process.

Even more alarming is that at the edge of this scene, dozens of similar tiny points of light are being swallowed by darkness, each representing a different world or plane!

"Void Nightmare...is it simultaneously eroding multiple worlds?"

Sun Rong gasped in shock, finally understanding the level of threat they were facing.

Wang Ling's gaze, however, fell on the claws of the white dragon-shaped creature in the projection.

There, he held a constantly spinning dark red crystal core, the inside of which seemed to seal a fragment of some extremely ancient will.

Just as the two of them were staring at the projection, the dark red crystal core suddenly flashed violently!

Buzz!

A terrifying will, far surpassing anything before, suddenly descended upon the Source Eye space!

This time it was no longer a manifestation of Bai Zhe, but rather carried an even more ancient and purer form of void malice!

"Finally found you! The special seed..."

A chilling thought resonated directly in their minds, causing the mother fluid in their source eyes to boil wildly, transforming into a gigantic black hand that reached out to grab Sun Rong!

This attack was completely different—it seemed to have seen through Wang Ling's protection mode and instead of attacking directly, it attempted to drag Sun Rong directly into a spatial rift for transfer!

Sun Rong felt the space around her freeze instantly, and even though the Ao Hai Sword Qi exploded to its limit, she still couldn't move an inch!

A fleeting, extremely subtle hint of annoyance finally flashed in Wang Ling's dead fish eyes.

It's like seeing a fly constantly circling around your food, and no matter how many times you chase it away, it won't give up.

Instead of intercepting the giant black hand, he did something simpler—he snapped his fingers.

*Smack*

A soft sound, almost inaudible under the pressure of the boiling source eye space and the terrifying will.

However, with this snap of the fingers, the flow of time in the entire Source Eye space underwent an extremely subtle change—not a stop, but a forced acceleration of all ongoing processes by billions of times!

The moment the giant black hand touched Sun Rong, it was as if it had undergone the erosion of countless eras of time. From its energy structure to its will carrier, everything aged instantly and silently dissipated into the most primitive energy particles.

The ancient will that descended was also as if it had been forcibly stuffed into a time trap, letting out a half-scream of anger before abruptly ceasing.

The whole process was so fast that Sun Rong only felt the oppressive feeling suddenly appear and then suddenly disappear, as if nothing had happened.

Only Wang Ling's slightly narrowed eyes and the ripples on the surface of the Source Eye, which had not yet completely subsided, proved that what had just happened was not an illusion.

"It...it ran away?" Sun Rong asked, still shaken.

Wang Ling shook his head and pointed to the ground.

Sun Rong looked down and noticed that a small clump of dark red crystal sand had appeared on the ground sometime during the night.

It was the residue left behind after the crystal core aged rapidly over time.

Wang Ling bent down, picked up a small grain of crystal sand, rubbed it between his fingertips, and a knowing glint flashed in his dead fish eyes.

Through this fragment, he had thoroughly analyzed part of the essence of the Void Nightmare and Bai Zhe's plan.

"Wang Ling, what do we do next..." Before Sun Rong could finish speaking, the entire Source Eye space suddenly trembled violently!

It wasn't an attack, but rather some kind of... repulsive reaction?

It was as if the world was refusing to allow them to continue existing.

Wang Ling looked up in a certain direction, seemingly piercing through endless layers of rock, and saw the anomaly taking place in the Thousand Caves City labyrinth—all the subterranean creatures and remnants of the Glass Clan influenced by the will of the Outer God were frantically rushing towards the spatial nodes of various regions under the guidance of some force, attempting to completely detonate the energy balance of the subterranean world!

At the same time, Sun Rong received an urgent message from Jiu Gong Liangzi via the array plate:

"Sun Rong! Where are you? The maze is collapsing! All the monsters have gone mad! Senior Wang Zhen said this is a diversionary tactic; Bai Zhe's real target is your area!"

Wang Ling's expression finally changed slightly—not with worry, but with a hint of excitement that the game's difficulty had finally been raised from the tutorial to easy mode.

He took Sun Rong's hand, and with his other hand, he gently gestured towards the space in front of him—

Sizzle!

A stable spatial rift appeared out of thin air, and on the other side of the rift was the main passage of the Thousand Caves City maze!

Liu Qingyi was protecting Jiugong Liangzi as they fought and retreated, surrounded by a tidal wave of frenzied subterranean creatures!

"Hurry up," Wang Ling said, unusually.

Before Sun Rong could react, he pulled her into the crevice!

The moment they left the Source Eye space, the entire Source Eye suddenly collapsed inward, turning into a tiny point of light before disappearing completely.

In its final moments, the spirit of the ancient land transferred its core, severing its connection with the marked coordinates.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong appeared like divine soldiers descending from the heavens, at the very center of the battle in the Thousand Caves City maze!
