Deep within the Netherworld Stone Forest, a few wisps of cold, eerie blue light danced among the jagged black crystals, outlining the contours of several "familiar" faces.

"Time calibration".

A cold voice broke the deathly silence; the voice was clearly Sun Rong's, yet utterly devoid of warmth.

"There are still three standard geocentric times away from the target for dispersed movement."

"The stability rate of simulated fluctuations is 99.8%, and the simulation of the target spiritual energy fluctuation characteristics is 100% complete."

Another voice rang out, it was Zhang Linyan's voice, so stereotypical as if she were reciting instrument data.

"‘The clones Sun Rong, Liu Qingyi, and Jiugong Liangzi are about to enter the edge of the Crystal Forest, as expected.'"

"The energy readings of the internal defensive array of the cave have been analyzed."

The "Li Changzhe" interface, with a metallic scraping sound in its voice: "Constructed with the abilities of Kyuumei Ryoko, the core is the Aohai Sword Qi Sensing Network. Sun Rong's sword qi imprint is the only identification node."

The leader, "Sun Rong"—a humanoid figure modeled after the glass leader—smiled with a faint, identical curve to Sun Rong's lips, but a cold, glassy glint flashed deep within her eyes: "Sword Qi Mark? What a brilliant idea. Unfortunately, it can only identify whether something exists or not, but cannot discern its essence."

Her gaze swept over the group of incredibly lifelike Glass Clan members before her: "We don't need to imitate their essence; we only need to become what they perceive. When the Aohai Sword Qi senses us, it will only see the mark left by its master—its own aura. This is the biggest flaw!"

"Operation begins!"

Sun Rong waved her hand, her voice decisive: "According to your assigned mission numbers, take your positions! Remember, the primary objective is to detach from the main body! The secondary objective is to eliminate all threats! Replacement, begin!"

Inside the underground cave, artificial sunrise light shines through the dome simulation device, bathing the interior space in a light gold hue.

Outside the enormous floor-to-ceiling crystal window lies the eternal darkness of the Earth's core, dotted with the faint, cold light of distant mineral veins—a scene of utter stillness.

On the open ground of the platform, members of the resource exploration team are conducting a final check.

Zhang Linyan was checking her equipment bag, her expression focused; Li Changzhe was flexing his wrists, the iconic old-fashioned longbow on his back gleaming in the morning light.

Su Xingyue's face was still a little pale, but her eyes had regained their composure as she carefully adjusted the wristband of the image guide terminal on her wrist.

Sun Rong stood slightly forward, her tall and straight figure accentuated by her form-fitting adaptive psionic combat suit.

She closed her eyes slightly, her mind sinking deep into her dantian. The core of the Aohai Sword was quietly operating, like the most sophisticated radar core.

An invisible net, with her at its core, covers the entire cave area and extends outwards for several miles.

The isolation barrier set up by Ryoko Kugimiya is like a solid eggshell, while her Okai Sword Qi is like living water with the ability to identify things flowing inside the eggshell.

Every soul dot she marked was clearly visible in the net—Chen Chao and Guo Hao were in the rest area, one grimacing as he changed the dressing on his leg injury, and the other adjusting a small rune device.

Liu Qingyi stood at the warehouse entrance, hands on her hips, counting a pile of supplies, occasionally kicking the clumsy porter next to her who was helping out.

Ryoko Kugimiya sat cross-legged inside the cave entrance, with the array plate floating in front of her knees, maintaining the stable output of the barrier.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing lay in wait in the shadows of the passage near the entrance, while the rest of the logistics team members were scattered around, busy with their own tasks.

Everything was normal, so calm that it was as if the tension of last night was just an illusion.

"Captain Sun, you can prepare to depart." Zhang Linyan's calm voice came through.

Sun Rong opened her eyes, her clear pupils reflecting the golden light of the morning sun, and also reflecting the corner deep inside the cave—Wang Ling, still sitting in his reserved seat.

Holding a small bag of freshly opened "Hellfire" spicy instant noodles, she focused intently on...crushing the noodles.

His presence, steady and reassuring, resided at the very core of her senses, giving her an indescribable sense of security.

"Okay." Sun Rong nodded, her voice steady and strong.

She glanced one last time at the quiet corner, then turned and gestured to Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi: "Qingyi, Jiugong, let's go."

The three figures passed through the barrier gate opened by Ryoko Kugimiya and disappeared into the dark passage leading to the Crystal Forest.

...

The cave warehouse area is filled with a strange aroma of mixed minerals and spiritual plants.

Suddenly, a hurried and weak cry, filled with obvious pain and panting, came from the direction of the backup passage leading to the outer perimeter of the cave: "Help...help! Guo Hao! Wang Ling! Someone come quickly!"

It was Chen Chao's voice! And it was filled with the panic of someone on the verge of despair!

The warehouse fell silent instantly.

All the logistics team members stopped what they were doing and stared in astonishment at the entrance to the passageway from which the sound was coming from.

Guo Hao's expression suddenly changed: "Chao'er? Isn't he in the rest cabin?"

He instinctively wanted to rush over.

"Stop!" Fang Xing emerged from the shadows, shouted, and stepped forward to block Guo Hao's path.

His eyebrows furrowed tightly, his eyes sharp as knives, fixed intently on the dark entrance to the passage.

"Fang Xing! It's Chao'er! Something must have happened to him!" Guo Hao said anxiously.

"Something happened? If something happened, how could he have crawled back through the backup passage? That path is a dead end!"

Fang Xing's voice was as cold as ice: "Where is Sun Rong's sword energy sensing network? Did you sense the fluctuations when Chen Chao left the rest area?"

Guo Hao was taken aback and immediately calmed his mind to sense it.

The sensory net woven by the Aohai Sword Qi was still operating smoothly, and in the rest area, the soul light representing Chen Chao was still in its original position!

Although her breathing was somewhat weak, she definitely did not move!

Cold sweat instantly soaked Guo Hao's back.

However, in the face of a powerful being like Fang Xing, who specializes in the soul level, this imitation was like a crude counterfeit encountering a master appraiser, full of flaws!

The figure of "Chen Chao" who was crawling and screaming suddenly froze, and then let out a non-human, sharp and piercing screech!

Its body surface is like water with pebbles thrown in, ripples violently and then dissolves.
