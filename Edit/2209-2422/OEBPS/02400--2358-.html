Two figures walked in, guided by the staff.

Walking in front was a young man, who looked to be in his early twenties, about 1.85 meters tall, with a straight posture.

He had short, silver-gray hair with a few strands of icy blue highlights at the ends. His features were sharp and handsome, giving him a dashing and debonair air.

He wore a stylish black and white color-blocked e-sports jacket over a simple black T-shirt, and a uniquely designed silver pendant around his neck.

Following behind him was a girl, about the same age and about 1.7 meters tall.

She had long, chestnut-brown wavy hair that reached her waist, adorned with several small star and moon-shaped hair clips.

The girl had delicate features, fair skin, and large, bright almond-shaped eyes. She wore a light purple short dress and exuded a sweet and refreshing aura.

The atmosphere in the hall changed noticeably the moment the two appeared.

"Holy crap! It's the Star Gemini!" someone exclaimed.

"It really is Luo Xinghe and Xia Zhinuan!"

"How did they get here?"

"I heard they're both streamers with a 100-star ranking in the King of Glory game, their skills are amazing!"

"Xia Zhinuan mainly plays mid lane and support, has outstanding awareness, and is especially good at protecting heroes. Her live streaming style is gentle and sweet, and she has seven million fans.
