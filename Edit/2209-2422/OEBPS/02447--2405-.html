Chapter 2405 Luo Xinghe's Further Actions

Night deepened

The black van drove smoothly on the suburban highway.

Inside the carriage, Mo Xuan took off his gold-rimmed glasses and slowly wiped the lenses with a silk scarf.

His movements were light and slow, as if he were performing some kind of ritual.

In the back seat, the five members of the Dark Owl gang huddled together, the spirit locks on their wrists gleaming with a cold metallic sheen in the dim light.

Their faces were increasingly grim, not only from the weakness caused by their spiritual power being suppressed, but also from a deep-seated sense of defeat.

"Captain..." a team member began in a hoarse voice, "...How are we going to explain this when we get back?"

The owl kept his eyes closed and did not answer.

He was still replaying the scene in the square: the spirit lock inexplicably reversed, the talisman silently disintegrated, he fell flat on his face, and was even filmed on camera...

Every frame is like a knife, cutting into the dignity of a professional killer.

"I don't need your explanation." Mo Xuan put his glasses back on, his eyes behind the lenses calm and unwavering. "I will explain the situation to my superiors. The responsibility for this mission's failure lies with me."

The sly opened his eyes and looked at the back of the person in the driver's seat: "Mr. Mo, that Wang Ling..."

"This is not someone you can touch." Mo Xuan interrupted him, his voice very calm: "Or rather, not someone you can touch at this stage."

Silence fell inside the car.

Mo Xuan looked at the road ahead, quickly reviewing all the information he had gathered over the past two days.

Wang Ling, eighteen years old (by Chinese reckoning), appears to be at the Foundation Establishment stage of cultivation, but his actual cultivation level is unknown.

He likes to eat instant noodles, has a cold personality, and doesn't talk much.

People who frequently appear around him include classmates such as Sun Rong, Chen Chao, and Guo Hao, as well as... Zhuo Yi.

"Outstanding..." Mo Xuan murmured the name.

He investigated Zhuo Yi and discovered that this person actually had a close relationship with Wang Lingzhi, and that at such a young age, he had already become a city hero of Songhai and the head of the Hundred Schools Administration... His level was simply off the charts. Given his level of attainment, it was impossible for him to have repeatedly performed extraordinary feats. But if someone was helping him rise to power, then everything would make sense.

Mo Xuan recalled the words spoken in a deep voice on the phone: "Continue to observe, but do not take any action for the time being."

The people above clearly know something.

But with his license, he is not qualified to know everything.

"Interesting." Mo Xuan's lips curled into a slight smile: "A high school student, able to draw so much power... Wang Ling, just what are you?"

The commercial vehicle drove into the city and merged into the nighttime traffic.

Mo Xuan glanced in the rearview mirror; the five members of the Dark Owl gang had already fallen asleep, exhausted.

He looked away and tapped his fingers lightly on the steering wheel.

observe.

The next morning, at Disneyland.

The sunlight was wonderful, shining on the castle's spire and reflecting a dazzling golden light.

In the hotel lobby, students from Number 60 High School gathered together, waiting for the bus to pick them up.

"Lingzi, how did you sleep last night?" Chen Chao put his arm around Wang Ling's shoulder, his eyes were still a little swollen, but he was in high spirits: "I didn't sleep well at all, I was just thinking about our achievements these past two days!"

Wang Ling grunted in agreement, took out a packet of instant noodles from his pocket, and tore it open.

Chen Chao naturally took a slice and said while eating, "Let me tell you, Guo Hao analyzed the data all night last night and said that your tacit understanding with Sun Rong has broken historical records!"

"......"

Wang Ling opened his mouth, intending to say that he was only after the gift pack of instant noodles for one hundred people, but on the one hand, he felt that speaking up to refute was not in his nature, and on the other hand, given Chen Chao's loud and boisterous comprehension ability, he would probably only make things worse by explaining himself.

On the other hand, Wang Ling seemed to have discovered that Sun Rong was indeed an exception for him. It was a kind of emotion that Wang Ling could not yet understand at this stage.

In short, he could allow Sun Rong to do some special things for him and make some special agreements, but if this were done to other people, Wang Ling would be very disgusted.

At this moment, Sun Rong stood to the side, pretending to tidy her backpack, but her ears were perked up.

Wang Ling didn't say anything, focusing on eating his instant noodles.

Ryoko Kugimiya stepped out of the elevator, wearing a simple white dress and her hair tied in a high ponytail, looking fresh and capable.

"Everyone, the bus will arrive in ten minutes." She said with a smile, "Have you all had breakfast? The park has prepared bento boxes, you can eat them on the bus."

The data report from last night has been compiled.

All of Wang Ling's behavior during his time in the amusement park—his heart rate, emotional fluctuations, spiritual energy reactions, and the special energy feedback when interacting with Sun Rong—had already been recorded.

This data will be sent to Wang Ming today.

Ryoko Kumiya was very curious about what that genius scientist would deduce from this.

"Rongrong." She walked to Sun Rong's side and said softly, "Did you have fun this time?"

"Yes, I'm very happy."

"That's good." Ryoko Kumiya patted her shoulder: "Remember to let me know if anything happens after you go back to school."

Sun Rong blushed slightly: "Yes."

The bus arrived on time.

The students boarded the bus one after another. Wang Ling sat by the window, and Chen Chao "consciously" sat next to him, leaving the aisle seat for Sun Rong.

Sun Rong hesitated for a moment, but still sat down.

The car started and slowly drove out of the park.

Wang Ling looked out the window at the castle, roller coaster tracks, Ferris wheel, and other structures gradually receding into the distance, still holding half a bag of instant noodles in his hand.

The seal is very calm.

But yesterday on the Ferris wheel, when he handed the crystal token of his affection to Sun Rong, that unusually burning feeling...

Wang Ling frowned, then relaxed.

He closed his eyes, preparing to catch up on some sleep.

This is a state-classified scientific research facility.

In the center of the laboratory, a young man in a white lab coat is staring at the data stream scrolling on a holographic screen.

"Interesting..." Wang Ming pushed up his glasses, his fingers rapidly tapping on the virtual keyboard: "The frequency of the emotion module fluctuation increased by 37%, and the peak occurred at... 8:42 PM last night? Ferris wheel?"

The scene captured by the surveillance camera popped up on the screen—Wang Ling and Sun Rong were sitting in a Ferris wheel cabin, and the moment Wang Ling handed over the crystal of his feelings.

"Oh wow." Wang Ming's eyes lit up: "You're giving me a souvenir on your own initiative? Lingling, you've grown up."

He switched to another set of data.

"Spiritual energy feedback... almost zero, but the rules of the surrounding space are slightly distorted. Time: 7:35 pm yesterday, during the fireworks display in the central square."

Wang Ming zoomed in on the timeline and discovered that during that period, the square experienced a brief power outage, and... the energy signals of five unidentified individuals suddenly disappeared.

"Someone made a move." Wang Ming narrowed his eyes: "Infants, five of them. And then they all collapsed."

He doesn't even need to access the on-site video footage; he can deduce the general situation from the data alone.

"It seems to be a rule-based suppression. As long as you have malice towards Lingling, it will trigger a bad luck effect. The stronger the malice, the more outrageous the bad luck."

Wang Ming leaned back in his chair, grabbed an energy drink from the table, and took a big gulp.

"As expected of my brother, even his pranks are so creative."
