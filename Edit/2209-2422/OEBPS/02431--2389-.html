Chapter 2389 The Promise on the Ferris Wheel

Then, Wang Ling looked across.

The illusion on Sun Rong's side was also created.

It was a scene similar to a training camp battle hall, where virtual opponents were preparing their interfaces.

Her challenge is to win a solo match using a specific hero.

"It seems to be a challenge generated based on the memories we selected." Sun Rong looked at Wang Ling, where a virtual screen hanging above his head displayed the scenes Wang Ling encountered.

Sun Rong couldn't help but laugh: "Wang Ling, you have... a lot of instant noodles here."

Indeed, there are many.

Looking around, the entire factory was filled with instant noodles, in various packaging and flavors, some of which were even limited editions that were no longer available on the market.

Wang Ling's eyes lit up.

This challenge... seems pretty good?

He walked to the assembly line and picked up a bag at random.

I opened it and tasted a piece.

"Spicy crayfish flavor, spiciness 7, saltiness 5, umami 8," Wang Lingping calmly reported the data.

A checkmark immediately appeared on a display screen next to the production line.

He picked up the next package.

"Honey butter flavor, sweetness 6, oiliness 4, crispness 9."

Another checkmark.

Wang Ling's movements were neither hurried nor slow, but extremely efficient.

He only tastes one piece of each pack of instant noodles, yet he can accurately recite all the parameters and even deduce subtle differences in the production process.

In the monitoring room, the staff were dumbfounded.

"This person's sense of taste is just too outrageous!"

"He's tried thirty packets already, and they're all correct!"

"And look at his expression... he seems to be enjoying it?"

Wang Ling was indeed enjoying himself.

Although these instant noodles are virtual data generated by the illusion, their taste is very realistic.

He hadn't been able to try some of the limited-edition flavors for a long time.

So, while Sun Rong was seriously controlling the virtual character to play solo, Wang Ling was leisurely enjoying various kinds of instant noodles and passing the level.

Seven minutes later.

The words "Passed the test" popped up in large letters on Wang Ling's screen.

The production line stopped, all the crispy noodles disappeared, and in their place was a golden stamp phantom that slowly fell into Wang Ling's bracelet.

【Instant Noodle Master】Achievement Completed.

Wang Ling nodded in satisfaction.

At this time, Sun Rong's battle also ended.

Her virtual character made a comeback with a sliver of health, and the word "Victory" popped up on the screen.

The pink stamp's illusory image fell into her bracelet.

The two illusions vanished simultaneously, and they returned to the circular space.

The two spheres of light suspended in the center merged into one, transforming into an upward-reaching staircase of light.

"Congratulations on passing the Illusion of Choice," the system voice said. "Please proceed to the Corridor of Mental Images."

Sun Rong walked up to Wang Ling and asked curiously, "Wang Ling, was your challenge difficult?"

"It's not difficult." Wang Ling thought for a moment, then added, "It's delicious."

Sun Rong laughed out loud: "That's good."

The two stepped onto the light staircase.

...

"I give up." Chen Chao squatted in the observation area outside the illusion.

This was a hidden observation point specially set up by the staff for the "family and friends group".

Chen Chao was speechless: "Your son actually turned the challenge into a noodle tasting contest..."

Guo Hao was also dumbfounded: "Wang Ling's ability to distinguish tastes is far beyond that of ordinary people... It could just be that he's eaten too many instant noodles..."

"Is that the point?!" Chen Chao was exasperated: "The point is this romantic double fantasy challenge, but all he cares about is instant noodles!"

Li Changzhe said weakly, "But Sister Sun Rong seems quite happy..."

Indeed.

Sun Rong found it particularly interesting to see the rare look of satisfaction on Wang Ling's face when he talked about how "delicious" it was.

This person always manages to make her feel cute in the most unexpected ways.

...

The light staircase leads to the final area of the maze—the Corridor of the Mind.

The style here has changed again.

The entire space feels like it's submerged underwater, with pale blue light shimmering around you. Under your feet is a transparent glass floor, where you can see schools of glowing fish swimming below.

Many crystal-like mirrors floated all around, each reflecting the image of the person who entered.

But those reflections were clearly different.

In some mirrors, Wang Ling's reflection is eating instant noodles.

In some mirrors, Sun Rong's reflection is smiling.

The other mirror doesn't reflect them as they are now, but rather a kind of "possibility".

For example, two people wearing matching outfits, or a scene of them standing side by side on the stage to receive an award.

"These mirrors..." Sun Rong blushed again.

"An illusion," Wang Ling commented.

He walked up to a mirror.

In the mirror, his reflection was handing a packet of instant noodles to Sun Rong's reflection beside him.

Wang Ling stared at it for two seconds, then took out a real packet of instant noodles from his pocket and handed it to Sun Rong beside him.

Sun Rong paused for a moment, then took it: "...Huh?"

"Give it a try," Wang Ling said.

Sun Rong opened the package, tasted a piece, and exclaimed, "Hmm, a new flavor? Seaweed wasabi?"

"Yes." Wang Ling nodded: "Limited edition."

The two stood there in the shimmering blue light, sharing a bag of seaweed and wasabi-flavored instant noodles.

The reflection in the mirror overlaps with the real scene, making it impossible to tell which is more real.

"The rules of the Heart Corridor are..." the system voice sounded at the right time: "Find the mirror that reflects your true feelings, touch it to pass the level."

"Your true feelings?" Sun Rong asked, puzzled.

She looked around; there were so many mirrors, at least a hundred.

The images in each mirror are constantly changing: some are beautiful fantasies, some are exaggerated distortions, and some are mundane daily scenes.

Which side represents the "true intention"?

Wang Ling has already set his sights on his target.

In his perception, the energy flow throughout the corridor has a core node.

That floor-length mirror, located at the deepest part of the corridor, with its frame bearing fine cracks.

The scene reflected in the mirror was simple: he and Sun Rong stood side by side, about twenty centimeters apart. Both of them were holding instant noodles, with the rooftop of Number 60 High School in the background and the sunset over Songhai City in the distance.

It's very ordinary.

But Wang Ling knew that was the correct answer.

Because this mirror has no added illusions or embellishments, it truly reflects their current state.

They were shoulder to shoulder, but still some distance apart.

We share snacks, but we're not quite that close yet.

The background is a scene they are all familiar with.

However, he didn't walk over directly.

Instead, he looked at Sun Rong and said, "Try looking for it."

He wanted to see which mirror Sun Rong would choose.

"Hmm." Sun Rong carefully moved between the mirrors.

She looked very carefully.

Some of the images in the mirror made her blush and her heart race, such as the images of the two holding hands, or of Wang Ling smiling at her, even though Wang Ling almost never smiled in real life...

Some things she found funny, like the scene of Wang Ling being submerged in instant noodles.

Finally, she stopped in front of a mirror.

The mirror shows a scene from the school opening ceremony.
