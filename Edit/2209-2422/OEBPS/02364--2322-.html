Chapter 2322 Crack

Taking advantage of the chaos at the scene, Wang Ling began to decipher the memories of the "Shadowman" soul that had been captured.

He silently memorized some key information.

"So that's how it is... There are such survival rules in the Abyss Gate." Wang Ling frowned slightly.

He gleaned from the soul's memories that when carrying out missions inside the gate, the last survivor would be forced to pass through.

In other words, as long as someone cleverly uses the rules to kill their own family members and all other family members involved in the mission, that person can successfully survive.

At this moment, the courtyard was filled with a mingled stench of blood and the smell of stale medicine.

The dark red "Secret Manual of Resurrection" was held in Black 112's hand like a red-hot branding iron, so hot that he almost wanted to throw it away immediately.

"Hidden ancient books...taken out of the library..." a girl in gray muttered to herself, her face looking no better than a dead man.

She subconsciously took two steps back, away from the ominous book, but her gaze involuntarily drifted toward Lin Susu's tightly closed door.

The intention is all too obvious.

"It's her! It must be Lin Susu!" Another man in black robes, number 201, suddenly exclaimed.

He was thin and wiry, with shifty eyes. He roared in the courtyard, "She acted alone yesterday and came back the latest! And she said before that all her family members were dead! Who knows if she used some kind of method?"

Panic spread like a plague. Of the remaining people, except for Wang Ling, all eyes were fixed on that door.

Suspicion and fear continued to spread, along with a distorted sense of relief as if the "culprit" had been found.

However, Wang Ling did not believe that Lin Susu was the real killer who used the rules to commit murder.

Admittedly, her family members did not survive her previous missions within the sect, but if she really was the murderer, she had no reason to tell the others this information.

"Bang!"

The door was suddenly pulled open from the inside.

Lin Susu stood at the door, still with that cold demeanor, but deep in her eyes was suppressed anger and resentment at being framed.

"Bullshit!" Her voice was hoarse, filled with the exhaustion of not having slept all night. "I went to the back mountain yesterday to look for wild medicinal herbs or other clues! I didn't go into the library, and I didn't touch this tattered book!"

"Who can prove it?" Black 201 persisted, stepping forward: "You acted alone, so you can say whatever you want! I think you found this book, brought it back, and then hid it under someone's bed, trying to use them to kill them!"

"Right! The rules only say you can't take it out without permission, they don't say you can't frame someone!" someone whispered in agreement.

Lin Susu's chest heaved with anger, her hands clenched into fists beneath her black robe, her knuckles turning white.

She knew she was in danger; these terrified people would do anything for their so-called safety.

"What do you want?" Her fierce gaze swept over the crowd, finally landing on Black 112, her demeanor completely defiant.

Black 112's face was solemn. As the temporary leader, he had to make a decision.

He weighed the "Secret Manual of Resurrection" in his hand, then looked at the indignant crowd, and said in a deep voice, "Lin Susu, you are the prime suspect. For everyone's safety, until the truth is revealed, you... cannot move freely."

He phrased it euphemistically, but the implication of house arrest was clear.

Zhou Ping, terrified, gripped Wang Ling's sleeve tightly and whispered, "Brother 999, what do we do? Sister Lin, she..."

Although he was afraid of Lin Susu, he was even more afraid of the atmosphere of internal strife.

Wang Ling remained silent, calmly analyzing the current situation.

The spirit had already enveloped everyone present invisibly.

He heard Black 112's accelerated heartbeat, saw the fleeting calculation in Black 201's eyes, and sensed the fear of the others blindly following his lead.

The real "wolf" is likely among them, enjoying the outcome, and may even be the one who instigated the siege of Lin Susu.

Just as Black 112 was about to lead his men to forcibly control Lin Susu, Wang Ling suddenly moved.

Wang Ling raised his hand, not pointing at anyone, but at the dark red fragments on the ground near where the "Secret Manual of Resurrection" had been discovered, which had not yet been noticed by anyone.

"What is this?" Wang Ling's voice was flat and flat, intentionally drawing everyone's attention.

Everyone was taken aback and followed his gaze.

Black 112 crouched down, picked up a small piece of debris with his fingers, sniffed it, and frowned.

"It's... metal scraps? And it smells strongly of smoke and has an indescribable fishy odor."

"It feels like fragments of the alchemy furnace," Wang Ling added, his tone still calm.

This was one of the pieces of information he had just obtained from the fragments of the deceased's soul.

Before his death, the deceased seemed to be paying attention to things related to alchemy furnaces.

"The alchemy furnace?" Everyone's attention was temporarily diverted.

"In the Sutra Repository, there are ancient books that state that the refining of the Soul-Returning Pill requires a special Soul-Guiding Furnace." Wang Ling continued to play the role of a taciturn newcomer who accidentally discovered the clue.

He combined the fragments of information about the alchemy furnace from the deceased's memory with his own observations and said: "But strangely, all the alchemy furnaces here are broken."

"Yes! We found that yesterday too, none of them are usable!" a man in gray quickly said.

"This book."

Wang Ling turned his gaze to the "Secret Manual of Resurrection" in Hei 112's hand: "It may not just be the pill formula. Perhaps there are other clues as well."

His words did not directly exonerate Lin Susu, but they cleverly provided a new direction.

Instead of getting caught up in who framed whom, we should focus on completing our mission and surviving.

The key to completing the mission may lie in this book, "The Secret of Resurrection," and the damaged alchemical furnace.

Black 112's eyes flickered.

As a martial arts coach, he is more accustomed to straightforward confrontations, but this kind of life-or-death mystery is a challenge for him.

Wang Ling's words reminded him that internal strife could not solve the fundamental problem.

"You mean..." Black 112 looked at Wang Ling.

"Let's all read the book together. Let's find the refining method or other solutions as soon as possible," Wang Ling said succinctly.

"Trap her." He pointed at Lin Susu: "It will reduce the number of people searching for clues."

Lin Susu glanced at Wang Ling with some surprise. This gray-clad man, who had been rather unremarkable, had actually uttered a "human word" at this moment.

She snorted coldly, which was tantamount to acknowledging Wang Ling's statement.

Black201 wanted to say something, but Black112 stopped him with a look.

"He makes a good point. Now is not the time for infighting."

Black 112 brandished the "Secret Manual of Resurrection" in his hand: "Let's study this book first. Lin Susu, you are free to move around for now, but if there is any suspicious behavior..."

He didn't finish his sentence, but it was full of threat.

Lin Susu snorted coldly, ignored him, turned around and went back to her room, slamming the door shut, but without locking it.

A potential conflict was temporarily averted by Wang Ling's small discovery.

But the tension in the air did not dissipate, and suspicion began to spread like poison ivy in everyone's hearts.

The crowd gathered around, and Black 112 carefully opened "The Secret of Resurrection".

The pages were made of some kind of animal hide, and the writing was dark red, as if it were written in blood.

The group began to disperse their spiritual consciousness to cover the entire book, each person carefully searching for key clues between the lines.
