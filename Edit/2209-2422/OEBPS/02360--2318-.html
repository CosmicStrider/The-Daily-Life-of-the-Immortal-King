Chapter 2318 A Great Victory

Just as that secret realm origin mark, symbolizing victory, was about to fly towards the sixty people.

"Make your move!"

Zhao Qian's face contorted, and he let out a roar filled with resentment and venom.

Almost as soon as he finished speaking, the short, sinister-looking teammate who had been lurking beside him suddenly raised his head!

His eyes no longer held any human emotion, only a deep, abyss-like darkness!

"Boom!"

A malevolent spiritual power, far exceeding the Foundation Establishment stage and even faintly touching the threshold of infancy, suddenly erupted!

This power was not something he cultivated himself, but rather it was composed of a powerful evil force.

Streams of sinister power pierced through layers of spatial barriers, transforming into a filthy spring that forcibly poured into his body, driving him completely insane. The chaotic will caused by the invasion of evil power made everyone around him feel nauseous.

The short team member formed a strange hand seal, and the dark aura around him writhed like a living thing, instantly condensing into a pitch-black arrow wrapped with ominous runes in front of him.

"Soul-Eating Demon Arrow!" he muttered under his breath.

The arrow was aimed at Li Jun!

This attack was extremely vicious!

If Li Jun could be killed on the spot, the creator of the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" would perish, fundamentally striking at this spell that counters their "shadow" technique!

Even if you can't kill them, severely damaging their soul is enough to have a huge impact.

"Watch out for Li Jun!"

"Damn it, you can't win the competition, so you resort to dirty tricks, huh?"

Chen Chao and Guo Hao reacted extremely quickly, filled with shock and anger, and tried to rush over to stop them, but the demonic arrow was too fast and had a strange property of locking onto the soul, making their Golden Core stage spiritual energy barrier as easy to penetrate as paper!

Sun Rong's pupils contracted sharply. The Ao Hai Sword emitted a rapid hum, and a curtain of azure water instantly condensed in front of her. Although she was on guard, she did not expect that the people from Qingyun High School would make a move in the secret realm of the competition, in full view of everyone.

At this moment, Li Jun faced the demonic arrow that exuded an aura of death. His mind went blank, and the intense fear made him forget to even recite the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra".

A hair's breadth away!

Outside the venue, the observation group gasped in astonishment!

"mean!"

"Qingyun High School dares to commit violence in front of Jingling!"

"Stop him!"

No one expected that Zhao Qian and this hidden "Shadowman" would dare to be so crazy, launching an attack just as the spirit of the secret realm was about to bestow the core mark!

Wang Muyu suddenly stood up from the sidelines, his small face tense, his pure dragon energy almost overflowing uncontrollably.

At this extremely critical moment—

"Buzz!"

A slight ripple, accompanied by a supreme will, silently unfolded.

The Soul-Eating Demon Arrow, powerful enough to severely injure or even destroy the soul of a Golden Core cultivator, came to an abrupt halt less than three inches from Li Jun's brow, as if it had struck an invisible yet indestructible barrier!

At this moment, time seemed to have been paused.

The dark runes that were spinning wildly at the tip of the magic arrow instantly froze.

Then, under the gaze of countless astonished eyes, the sound of shattering rang out in front of everyone.

The demonic arrow, imbued with evil power, was petrified from the tip onwards, then shattered into dust, ultimately vanishing into nothingness.

Not even a ripple was stirred, as if it had never existed.

An eerie, deathly silence fell over the entire shore of Liuli Lake; you could hear a pin drop.

The short "shadow hunter's" smirk froze, replaced by astonishment and a hint of instinctive fear from the depths of his soul.

Zhao Qian stared wide-eyed, unable to comprehend what was happening before his eyes.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao remained frozen in their forward-leaning positions.

Sun Rong's heart, which had been hanging in suspense, instantly settled down. The curtain of water in the Aohai Sea slowly receded, and she subconsciously looked to her side.

Wang Ling had already withdrawn the finger that had been raised unintentionally, and was now patting non-existent dust off the cuffs of his school uniform.

As expected of you, Wang Ling...

Sun Rong immediately understood, and an indescribable sense of security welled up in her heart.

Li Jun's legs went weak, and he almost collapsed to the ground. Guo Hao, who was next to him, caught him. He was panting heavily, his forehead covered in cold sweat, and he was still very scared.

"presumptuous!!!"

After a brief silence, the spirit of the secret realm, Liuli, roared with earth-shattering fury!

Her small body burst forth with dazzling seven-colored light due to her anger, causing the entire Azure Netherworld Secret Realm to tremble violently and towering waves to rise on the surface of the Crystal Lake!

The terrifying pressure solidified into a tangible form, slamming down on the five students from Qingyun High School, especially the short "Shadowman" who made the move!

"How dare you commit murder before the spirits of this realm! You have violated the rules of the trial and desecrated this secret realm! Your crimes are unforgivable!"

Liuli was truly enraged.

Not only because they made a move on her turf, but also because... they almost disturbed that being!

What if she angers the big shot? Will she still be able to keep this secret realm?

"Please calm your anger, Spirit Realm Spirit!"

Zhao Qian was so overwhelmed by the oppressive aura that he could barely breathe. His face turned ashen, and he hurriedly tried to explain, "It was him! It was his own doing! It has nothing to do with Qingyun High School! We don't know why he suddenly..."

He tried to distance himself from the situation and pin the blame on the shorter teammate.

"Shut up!"

Liuli ignored his explanation and waved her small hand. The seven-colored light transformed into countless chains, instantly binding the short "Soul Dweller" tightly. The evil power within his body was sealed away as if it had encountered its nemesis.

"Your team's trial qualification is revoked! You are immediately expelled from the secret realm! This person, stay and be interrogated!"

As Liuli pronounced her verdict, a blinding teleportation light shone beneath Zhao Qian and the other three Qingyun High School team members.

"No! Lord Jingling, give us another chance!" Zhao Qian roared unwillingly, but he could not resist the power of the secret realm's rules.

With a flash of light, the four figures vanished, having been humiliated and kicked out of the Azure Netherworld Secret Realm.

Only the "Soul Dweller," bound by the seven-colored chains, lay paralyzed on the ground.

He couldn't understand what had happened.

Everything happened in the blink of an eye; his attack was fast enough, so fast that even the spirit of the secret realm didn't have time to react, but in the end, he failed...

...

Outside the venue, in the observation area.

The astonishing change that occurred on the screen left all the spectators speechless.

After a brief silence, an explosion of discussion erupted.
