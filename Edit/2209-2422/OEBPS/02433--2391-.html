Chapter 2391 An Uninvited Guest in the Paradise

The staff member dressed as a ghost had clearly never encountered this kind of situation before, and stood there, unsure whether to accept the answer or not.

When Wang Ling saw that he didn't take it, he stuffed the instant noodles back into his pocket and calmly said, "Thank you for your hard work."

After saying that, he pulled Sun Rong, who was still in a daze, and continued walking forward.

After walking several meters, Sun Rong burst out laughing: "Wang Ling, you just... handed a ghost some instant noodles?"

"He's been standing there for a long time," Wang Ling said. "He's quite pitiful."

He could indeed sense that the staff member had been staking out the cell for at least fifteen minutes, during which time he had remained curled up, expending a considerable amount of energy, so he took pity on him.

Unfortunately, the staff member didn't seize the opportunity. Otherwise, even just eating a few crumbs from that packet of instant noodles would have been enough to propel the staff member to a whole new level.

Sun Rong laughed so hard her shoulders shook: "Who feeds snacks to the staff at a haunted house...?"

She laughed until tears streamed down her face, all the tension from before completely gone.

Wang Ling looked at her and smiled, and the seal rippled slightly again.

It seems that making her laugh is quite interesting.

...

In the monitoring room, Ryoko Kugimiya sighed, "I'm truly impressed by Ryou's actions."

Wang Zhen's laughter came through the communicator: "Hahaha, did you guys see that NPC's expression just now? He was totally dumbfounded!"

"The plan has been disrupted," Liu Qingyi said calmly. "That dungeon NPC was supposed to rush to the railing and roar loudly, scaring Sun Rong into hiding with Wang Ling."

"It's alright." Ryoko Kugimiya quickly adjusted her mindset: "There will be plenty of opportunities later. And look, Rongrong is completely relaxed now, she's smiling so happily."

In the video, Sun Rong is indeed smiling with her eyes crinkled, and the hand that was tightly gripping Wang Ling's sleeve has loosened, and her steps are much lighter.

"Sometimes, breaking the atmosphere of fear can actually create a more natural sense of closeness."

Zhuo Yi's voice came through: "However, they probably can't avoid that 'two-person collaborative mechanism' that's coming next."

...

At the end of the dungeon was a torture chamber.

In the center of the room stood a huge iron instrument of torture for virgins.

It's one of those human-shaped iron coffins, the kind with iron spikes inside.

However, this is clearly a replica; the coffin lid is open, and the inside is empty.

The room has walls on three sides and only one exit from which it was brought in.

"A dead end?" Sun Rong frowned.

Wang Ling walked up to the Iron Maiden and looked inside.

There is a clearly visible pedal mechanism at the bottom of the coffin. Someone would need to stand inside and step on the pedal to trigger the next mechanism.

On the wall directly opposite the coffin, there was a mirror covered with cracks.

Wang Ling reached out and touched the mirror; it felt cool to the touch and contained complex psionic circuits.

"We need two people," he said.

"How do I do it?" Sun Rong asked.

Wang Ling pointed to the Iron Maiden: "One person goes in and steps on the pedal. The other person stands in front of the mirror and, when the mirror lights up, puts their hand on it."

"And then?"

"The door will open."

Sun Rong glanced at the sinister Iron Maiden, then at Wang Ling: "Then... who's going in?"

She actually didn't really want to go into that iron coffin, even though she knew it was fake.

Wang Ling didn't say anything, and stepped directly into the Iron Virgin's interior.

"Wang Ling!" Sun Rong hurriedly followed.

The interior of the coffin was cramped; once Wang Ling stepped inside, there was almost no extra space. He looked down at the footplate and stepped down.

"Click".

The sound of a mechanism being triggered.

Almost simultaneously, the mirror on the other side lit up!

Starting from the center of the mirror, a faint blue light emanated from the cracks, and soon the entire mirror turned into a glowing blue screen.

A line of text appeared on the screen:

[Please have another person place their hand on the mirror and hold it for ten seconds.]

"Hurry!" Wang Ling's voice came from the Iron Maiden, sounding somewhat muffled.

Sun Rong quickly ran to the mirror and placed her hand on it.

The mirror surface was cool, but the light was soft.

She counted the seconds: "One, two, three..."

When the count reached the fifth second, a sudden change occurred!

Suddenly, a loud mechanical roar came from inside the Iron Maiden, and then the coffin lid began to slowly close!

"Wang Ling!" Sun Rong exclaimed in surprise, instinctively trying to remove her hand from the mirror.

"Don't move." Wang Ling's voice remained calm: "Continue."

"But the lid—"

"Fake."

The coffin lid was indeed closing, but very slowly. Moreover, Wang Ling could clearly sense that the lid would stop when it was still twenty centimeters away from the top of his head—this was clearly a frightening design to create a sense of tension that "his companion was about to be locked in the torture device."

But Sun Rong didn't know that.

She watched as the coffin lid closed little by little, gradually obscuring Wang Ling's figure, and her heart nearly leaped out of her chest.

"Seven, eight, nine..."

By the ninth second, the coffin lid was mostly closed, and only Wang Ling's chest and below could be seen.

Sun Rong gritted her teeth and pressed her hands firmly against the mirror.

Ten seconds!

The mirror emitted a bright blue light, which then instantly went out.

At the same time, the Iron Maiden's coffin lid stopped, about ten centimeters away from being completely closed.

"Boom!"

The side wall suddenly slid open to one side, revealing a new passage.

The coffin lid was slowly reopened.

Wang Ling stepped out and patted off imaginary dust from his clothes: "All done."

Sun Rong rushed over, grabbed his arm, and looked him up and down: "Are you alright?"

"It's nothing," Wang Ling shook his head.

He then pointed to the newly opened passage: "Let's go."

The two walked into the passageway.

No one noticed that there was a small camera above the footplate inside the Iron Maiden, which had just recorded Sun Rong's anxious and worried expression throughout the entire process.

In the monitoring room, Ryoko Kugimiya nodded with satisfaction: "This device is quite effective. Although it didn't scare Rongrong as originally planned, her concern for Wang Ling is genuine."

"Feelings are two-way," Zhuo Yi said with a smile. "Master can feel Junior Sister Sun's trust and care."

...

A new passageway leads to the upper levels of the castle.

The stairs were made of wood, and they creaked as you stepped on them. Combined with the dripping sound coming from somewhere, the atmosphere was just right.

When she reached the middle of the stairs, Sun Rong suddenly stopped.

"What's wrong?" Wang Ling turned around.

"Listen..." Sun Rong whispered, "Is there a child crying?"

Wang Ling listened intently.

There was indeed a faint crying sound coming from upstairs; it was the voice of a little girl, crying very pitifully.

However, the energy fluctuations that Wang Ling sensed indicated that it was just a recording playing on a loop, accompanied by a simple phantom projection.

"Although it's fake..." Sun Rong frowned, "it's still so painful to hear."
