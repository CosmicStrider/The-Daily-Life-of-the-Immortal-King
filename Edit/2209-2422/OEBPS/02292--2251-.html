Chapter 2251 The Future Jealous King

The lingering core consciousness, carrying the fury and obsession of Hades [Wan Shi], fled frantically. Having just pierced through the deep rock layers, it thought it was far away from the threat of Wang Zhen and Fang Xing, unaware that a pair of dead fish eyes were watching it.

Inside the underground cave, Wang Ling was picking up a fresh crumb of instant noodles, about to put it in his mouth.

This decaying yet exceptionally powerful aura of Hades, like a poisonous miasma, precisely pierced his tranquil sensory realm.

The feeling of annoyance was like a pebble thrown into a lake, creating a barely perceptible ripple in the still depths of his heart.

His gaze never left the bag of instant noodles in his hand; a thought merely crossed his mind.

An invisible force transcended space and dimensions, instantly locking onto the fleeing, fading consciousness.

This force did not have a vast, undulating energy, but it carried a cold, obliterative will originating from the very source of the laws.

Tens or even hundreds of miles away, the core consciousness projection of Wan Shi, which embodied countless resources and ambitions, was like a blazing fire thrown into the absolute zero of the universe.

His will to escape came to an abrupt halt, as if he had been gripped by an invisible giant hand. Before he could even let out a complete desperate roar, the very structure of his existence in the world crumbled in an instant.

It's not about crushing or annihilating, but about completely "erasing"—like the chalk writing on a blackboard being wiped clean with a blackboard brush, leaving no trace of its existence.

Hades' struggles and backup plans were reduced to nothingness, not even worthy of being called stardust, in the blink of an eye, by Wang Ling's single thought.

Meanwhile, the scene in front of the gentle light gate of the Earth's core cave was somewhat chaotic.

The students of the Holy Science Academy had just been rescued from the deadly peril of the Netherworld Stone Forest, and most of them were still in shock and covered in wounds.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing delivered them safely.

However, when the group of still-shaken students looked up and saw the scene before them, they were collectively stunned into silence.

What kind of shelter is this?

This is simply a dream fortress!

The magnificent building, blending cutting-edge technology with pure cultivation essence, features a streamlined exterior that radiates a warm, spiritual light. Inside, one can vaguely see comfortable rest areas, strange light screens, and instruments that emit a precious glow... It's like the difference between hell and heaven compared to the dark and bloody bone fortress they just escaped!

Holy crap! The shock of the students at Shengke was no less than that of Zhang Linyan, Li Changzhe, Chen Chao, and Guo Hao.

This doesn't look like a temporary base at all; it's more like a palace or villa deep in the earth's core belonging to some being beyond comprehension!

"My goodness...where am I?"

A student from the Holy Science Academy stammered, rubbing his eyes in disbelief.

"My God! This architectural style, this psionic energy... Is there such a secret base in the Earth's core?" Another student's voice trembled.

Amidst their shock, and even with a hint of fear of the unknown, they all turned their gazes toward the group from Number 60 High School.

Company Commander Su Xingyue, supported by Kyuu Miyako, looked at the scene before her with astonishment on her pale face.

Just then, a gentle yet capable voice rang out, accompanied by a convincing smile:

"Friends of St. Science Academy, I'm sorry to have surprised you. This is an ancient site that we at Number 60 High School discovered by accident. It was first discovered by Wang Ling, and I, Liu Qingyi, and Jiugong Liangzi are all witnesses."

Sun Rong walked forward with a smile on her face, her tone natural and fluent, without any trace of fabrication.

She glanced discreetly at Wang Ling, who sat quietly in the corner like a lucky charm, and continued to explain to the group: "We only found this place recently. We were just lucky enough to temporarily stay here thanks to the blessings of our ancestors. It was a mess when we came in, and we just cleaned it up."

Standing next to Sun Rong, Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi instantly understood and nodded in agreement: "Yes, yes, yes! It's all dusty! It's so hard to clean!"

Liu Qingyi had an expression that said, "You have no idea how hard we work."

Ryoko Kumiya, with her superb acting skills, pointed to the clean corner of her clothes: "Look, my clothes got dirty while cleaning some corners."

"..." The people of Saint Cole fell silent.

This mascot of Number 60 High School truly lives up to its name...

Sun Rong didn't give the members of Shengke much time to digest or question her. She quickly took out several exquisite jade bottles from her brocade pouch: "Your souls and bodies were corroded by the foul air in the dungeon. This is the [Soul Cleansing and Vitality Strengthening Pill], made with the precious Jade Heart Fruit we collected here as the main ingredient, combined with other spiritual herbs. It has miraculous effects on clearing away residual nether energy, stabilizing the soul, and treating internal and external injuries. Everyone should take it first to heal your injuries and restore your vitality."

This was developed on-site by Sun Rong based on the essence of the juice that Wang Ling had developed, relying on the perfect experimental equipment in the cave. Her talent for pill formulas was so strong that it almost reached the level of a natural pill formula saint, which made Wang Ling feel somewhat suppressed.

It can only be said that they are worthy of being the successors of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group.

As she spoke, she gestured for Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi to come forward and distribute the pills.

Once these jade bottles were opened, they immediately released a refreshing and invigorating fragrance, carrying the unique pure energy fluctuations of the Jade Heart Fruit.

The students of St. Cole have just experienced a great calamity and are in dire need of healing medicine.

At this moment, Sun Rong not only provided a perfect explanation to alleviate their doubts about the origin of the cave, but also brought out such a precious Earth Core Pill, the meaning of which was self-evident—to shut everyone up with a practical benefit.

As the team leader, Su Xingyue felt it most deeply. The aura of the elixir brought a touch of comfort to her injured soul.

She looked at Sun Rong distributing the pills with a complex expression, and finally sighed softly. On behalf of the team, she accepted the pills and said sincerely, "Captain Sun, and everyone from Number 60 High School, we will always remember your life-saving grace and the kindness of healing us. We will never speak of what happened here to outsiders."

The implication was that they accepted Sun Rong's explanation of the secret of the cave and acknowledged her kindness.

"Captain Su, you flatter me. As fellow members of the Huaxiu Kingdom's exploration team, helping each other is only natural." Sun Rong smiled gently and gracefully, secretly letting out a sigh of relief. This attempt to smooth things over was flawless.

Deep within the cave, Wang Ling remained seated at his workbench, quietly placing down an empty instant noodle bag, as if all the clamor outside, including the storm that obliterated the remnant of Hades, were nothing more than a gentle breeze blowing across his fingertips.

It was only at the moment that Sun Rong successfully got the people of Shengke to "accept" the cave's explanation.

Deep within his calm, still eyes, a flicker of emotion seemed to pass through him, even more clearly than when he had instantly killed Hades... almost imperceptible to anyone.

It seemed like praise, but also like a tiny bit of reassurance.

The alternation of day and night in the Earth's core is not obvious, but according to the simulated time scale displayed on the handheld device, the group ushered in their first "night" after entering the Earth's core.

On the wide platform in front of Wang Lingna's cave dwelling, which blends the charm of cultivation with futuristic technology, a small bonfire party is underway.

The venue cleverly utilizes fluorescein with its own faint glow to adorn the surroundings, and several pieces of lava core ore have been slightly processed by Wang Ling to emit a stable and warm red light, replacing the traditional campfire.

The air was filled with an enticing aroma, the result of Guo Hao and Chen Chao grilling several kinds of subterranean plant roots and oily "jade mushrooms" that they had discovered, under Liu Qingyi's "coercion".

Sun Rong entrusted the Mountain-Moving Beast with the important task of being a "transporter." Its huge head was topped with several plates of cleaned Jade Heart Fruit and a kind of juicy Night-Shining Fruit.

It was carefully placed next to the soft cushions where everyone sat, huddled in the shadows of a corner, with only one eye peeking out, secretly observing everyone, especially the unassuming boy carrying the juice.
