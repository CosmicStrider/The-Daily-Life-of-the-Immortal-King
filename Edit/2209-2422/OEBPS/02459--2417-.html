Chapter 2417 A Gathering of Big Shots

At the VIP table, Zhao Tieshan's face darkened slightly, but he quickly regained his composure.

He picked up his teacup, took a slow sip, and said to the middle-aged man who looked like a school principal next to him, "Little Li, look at the young people nowadays. They become arrogant as soon as they achieve a little success. Back when we were following our old squad leader Jiang Lei on missions, we didn't have so many tricks up our sleeves."

"Yes, yes, Elder Zhao is right."

The principal, known as Little Li, quickly chimed in, "When the Martial Saint led you on missions back then, you made great contributions. These kids don't know anything about real combat."

Several school leaders nearby listened intently to what was said.

Seeing everyone's attention, Zhao Tieshan felt smug and deliberately raised his voice: "Speaking of which, that was four hundred years ago. Back then, our old squad leader led twelve of us deep into the heart of the Western Desert to annihilate that group of cross-border evil cultivators. The other side had three late-stage Golden Core cultivators, while on our side, apart from our old squad leader who was an incarnation, the rest were all early-stage Golden Core cultivators."

He paused, gazing into the distance as if lost in memories: "That battle was brutal. The old squad leader fought two late-stage Nascent Soul cultivators single-handedly, while I led the remaining men to deal with the others. I had only recently broken through to the Golden Core stage, and I managed to withstand more than thirty heavy blows from them by relying on the second level of the Vajra Subduing Demon Body."

"And then what?" someone couldn't help but ask.

Zhao Tieshan smiled slightly: "In the end, of course we won. The old sergeant killed those two evil cultivators, and I also risked serious injury to work with my comrades to take down the third one. After that battle, the old sergeant was promoted to major general, and I was also awarded a second-class merit."

He put down his teacup, his tone tinged with emotion: "True battles are forged on the brink of life and death. They can't be compared to these childish games."

There was a chorus of admiration all around.

"Old Zhao was truly brave back then!"

"No wonder the Martial Saint values you so much."

"With a grandfather like this, Zhao Wuji's future is limitless..."

Zhao Tieshan felt much better after hearing these flattering remarks.

He glanced at the few young elites on the B section of the stands and thought: They're just a few juniors. No matter how good they are, they're still just students. Can they compare to my friendship with the Martial Saint?

However, at this moment—

A commotion suddenly broke out at the entrance of the stadium.

Everyone turned to look and saw a little boy, about six years old, wearing a white kendo uniform, holding the hand of an old man in gray, skipping and hopping into the room.

The little boy's features were incredibly delicate, especially his eyes, which held an indescribable depth within their calmness.

He was wearing a panda-shaped hat on his head, and if you looked closely, there seemed to be two small bulges underneath the hat.

The old man he was leading was dressed in a simple gray cloth robe, with a long sword hanging at his waist. His hair and beard were all white, but his eyes were clear as a mirror, and his steps exuded a calm and dignified air.

"That's... Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan?!" A judge in the VIP section exclaimed in surprise.

The entire room fell silent for half a second.

Immediately afterwards, a tidal wave of exclamations and discussions arose.

"The Sword Saint? One of the Ten Generals, the Sword Saint?"

Why would he come to this kind of competition?

"Wait, who is that child? Why is he holding the Sword Saint's hand?"

Under the watchful eyes of countless people, Wang Muyu pulled Yi Jianchuan and headed straight for the B section of the stands—the area where Number 60 High School was located.

Sun Rong was the first to react. She looked at the familiar little figure and her eyes widened: "Mu Yu?"

Upon hearing the voice, Wang Muyu immediately released Yi Jianchuan's hand and ran towards Sun Rong, shouting, "Sister Sun Rong!"

The cry of "Sister Sun Rong" was clear and loud, and everyone around heard it.

Sun Rong's face flushed instantly, but she still naturally hugged Wang Muyu: "Why are you here?"

Yi Jianchuan walked over slowly and nodded slightly to Sun Rong: "Mu Yu wanted to watch the match, so I brought him along."

Chen Chao, who was listening nearby, was taken aback.

He never expected that Wang Muyu would have such a relationship with the Sword Saint.

Wang Muyu lifted his head from Sun Rong's arms, suppressing the urge to call Wang Ling "Dad," and pointed towards the waiting area: "I'm here to cheer on Brother Wang Ling!"

Meanwhile, at the VIP table, Zhao Tieshan's teacup wobbled, spilling a few drops of tea onto his hand.

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan?

One of the Ten Generals, the number one swordsman in Huaxiu Kingdom, whose status is in no way inferior to that of Martial Saint Jiang Lei, Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan.

How did he end up here? And why was he dragged here by a child?

Zhao Tieshan was a little confused.

He was just boasting about his friendship with the Martial Saint, and in the blink of an eye, a Sword Saint of equal status appeared, clearly there to support Wang Ling.

This is such a quick slap in the face!

Several judges on the panel were already getting restless.

The white-haired elder was the first to stand up and cupped his hands towards Yi Jianchuan: "Sword Saint, your presence is an honor! I have failed to greet you properly!"

Yi Jianchuan waved his hand: "No need for formalities. I'm just here to watch a match with my child. Please continue."

He spoke casually, but he had already walked to the third row of section B and sat down in the empty seat next to Sun Rong.

Wang Muyu squeezed between Sun Rong and Jiu Gong Liangzi, swinging his short legs, and pulled out a bag of potato chips from his small backpack, crunching away as he ate them.

All eyes were on this spot.

The flags that were waving so enthusiastically at Ti Xiu High School are now almost too heavy to wave.

Zhao Wuji watched this scene from the waiting area, his face turning ashen.

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan...

His grandfather did know the Martial Saint, but their relationship was more of that of "old comrades-in-arms," and they hadn't had much contact over the years.

But the Sword Saint himself came today and sat in the opponent's support area, which makes all the difference.

This is a public platform!

Those who had been fawning over Zhao Tieshan in the VIP section now had somewhat subtle expressions.

Some even quietly moved to a different spot, further away from Zhao Tieshan.

Zhao Tieshan was seething with anger, but dared not let it out.

When facing the Sword Saint, his title of "comrade-in-arms of the Martial Saint" is no match for him.

After all, the Martial Saint himself didn't come; only his comrades did. The Sword Saint, on the other hand, was present in person.

He forced a smile and said to the people around him, "I didn't expect that even the Sword Saint would be interested in this kind of competition. It seems that young people these days are really something."

These words were spoken reluctantly, and anyone could hear the bitterness in them.

However, just when everyone thought the scene was shocking enough—

Another burst of hearty laughter came from the entrance.

"Oh dear, it seems I'm late! Did I miss some good show?"

A handsome young man dressed in ancient costume with a head of black hair walked in with light steps.

He was followed by two imposing attendants in black uniforms.

"Thunderbolt True Monarch?!" This time, it was a reporter in the media area who exclaimed in surprise: "The War Sect Master, Thunderbolt True Monarch!"

The entire audience erupted in cheers once again.

If Sword Saint is a legendary figure, then Thunder Lord is the most popular star sect leader in the cultivation world right now.

The founder of the world's number one sect, the largest investment target of the Huaxiu Alliance, and a well-known "self-destructive senior" in the cultivation circle.

Of course, no one dares to call him that to his face.

Lord Diu Lei pushed up his monocle, his gaze sweeping across the audience before quickly locking onto his target.

He walked straight to section B of the stands, waving as he went: "Miss Sun Rong! And little Mu Yu! Long time no see!"

The familiar tone of his voice made it sound as if he were attending a gathering of old friends.

Yi Jianchuan nodded slightly when he saw Diule Zhenjun: "Young friend Diule has also arrived."

"Sword Saint Senior!" Lord Thunderclap hurriedly bowed and said, "I heard that your brother has a competition today, so I came to show my support. I didn't expect to find you here as well. What a coincidence!"

As he spoke, he naturally sat down in the empty seat next to Zhuo Yi.

The two attendants stood quietly in the back aisle, their postures ramrod straight.

At this point, the third row of Section B and the surrounding area were completely filled with big shots:

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan, War Sect Master Diu Lei Zhenjun, Honorary Alumna of Number 60 High School Jiu Gong Liangzi, and the Chief Administrator of Songhai City's Hundred Schools Administration.
