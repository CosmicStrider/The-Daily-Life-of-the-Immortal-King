Chapter 2264 The Undercurrents of Thousand Caves City

The moment they rushed into the cave, the darkness and dust hit them, blocking out most of the deafening roar and horn sounds behind them.

Sun Rong was pulled along by Wang Ling, and they went deeper into the unknown tunnel with their feet barely touching the ground.

The light from the ocean and the red lotus petals intertwined, creating a soft field of vision in front of them.

The passage was initially narrow, allowing only two people to walk side by side.

But it soon opened up, and the four walls were no longer rough rock layers, but had a smooth feel as if they had been artificially polished.

The ancient murals depicted on it, now blurred and indistinct, tell the story of the sealed history of the Earth's inner world.

Wang Ling's steps were unwavering, as if he had already known everything about this path.

His palm was cool and steady, holding Sun Rong's wrist firmly with just the right amount of strength, enough to propel her forward quickly without making her feel uncomfortable.

After the initial panic, Sun Rong's heartbeat gradually calmed down, replaced by an indescribable sense of relief.

As long as he was in front of her, she had no fear, no matter how dangerous the maze was.

She could sense that he wasn't blindly rushing around, but rather following a certain pattern.

Sometimes you turn left or right without hesitation at a fork in the road, and sometimes you stop and lightly tap a spot on the wall with your fingertip, and the seemingly dead-end stone wall will silently slide open to reveal a new passage.

"Wang Ling, do you... know the way?" Sun Rong finally couldn't help but ask in a low voice.

Wang Ling didn't turn around, but shook his head very slightly.

He didn't know the way; rather, his Wang Tong was guiding him to the place he should be at this moment.

Or rather, he was "listening" to the faint guidance of the ancient spirits of this land, who were crying out in anguish deep within it.

They are searching for a node that can temporarily isolate them from external prying eyes and allow them to observe deeper changes.

This basic operation was too simple for Wang Ling.

To be honest, if it weren't for this collective action, Wang Ling could have easily sorted out all these messy troubles by going deep into the Earth's core on his own.

However, Wang Ling couldn't go too far.

If the entire inner world is destroyed, the cultivation world on the surface will likely suffer annihilation as well.

Meanwhile, the other members who had scattered into different caves found themselves in a real maze.

"Ryoko! Can you find the way?" Liu Qingyi asked Ryoko Kunomiya, who was working hard to operate the array plate, after she shattered several Earth Core Bats that swarmed out from the shadows.

In fact, with Liu Qingyi's supernatural abilities, finding the direction was not difficult. However, although Wang Ling did not explicitly state their purpose for venturing into the Earth's core together, everyone knew it perfectly well.

Based on the relationship between Zhuo Yi and Wang Ling, plus Zhuo Yi's own emotional connection with Jiu Gong Liangzi, Liu Qingyi was quite clear that Wang Ling was intentionally cultivating his own confidants.

The addition of her, Wang Zhen, and Fang Xing was ultimately for the purpose of secretly protecting and escorting the people around Wang Ling during this trip to the inner world, and incidentally, to cultivate the people around Wang Ling.

As for Sun Rong, Liu Qingyi has nothing to worry about. With Wang Ling personally guiding her, she is much better off than those on their side.

At this moment, fine beads of sweat appeared on Ryoko Kugimiya's forehead: "No! The psionic field here is too chaotic! The spatial structure is still constantly adjusting! It's like a living maze! My array can only detect a hundred meters around us; beyond that, it's all interference!"

The passage they were on was winding and twisting, with frequent forks in the road, and sometimes they would find themselves back where they started.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao, following Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe's group, were even more confused.

"Sister Yan! Where are we going? I feel like we're going in circles." Chen Chao asked breathlessly, leaning on a stone stick he had found on the spot.

Zhang Linyan's expression was grave, her longbow poised at all times: "Stay alert! This Thousand Caves City lives up to its name; it's a giant natural maze in itself. Stay close, don't fall behind!"

Li Changzhe's armor would occasionally light up, deflecting some of the sneak arrows or poisoned stings that were shot from the shadows.

That was an ambush launched by the Glass Clan who had previously infiltrated, or by some intelligent subterranean creatures.

"Damn it, if you're so tough, come out and face me head-on! What kind of hero hides like that!" Li Changzhe cursed angrily.

The group from the Holy Science Academy led by Su Xingyue was in slightly better shape. They used some portable detectors to try to analyze the rock composition and energy flow to determine the direction, but progress was slow.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing chose a passage, and the two moved extremely fast.

Fang Xing's skull scythe is like the most precise probe. It can often lightly slash at what he considers to be "spatial nodes" and briefly break through the illusion to find the correct direction.

"It's the power of the Outer Gods influencing the labyrinth," Fang Xing said in a deep voice, his eyes sharp beneath his hood. "It's trying to trap us here or lead us into some kind of trap."

Wang Zhen's usual smile vanished, replaced by a cold gaze: "It seems our move really messed with them. That Bai Zhe guy, hiding deep underground, has found quite a powerful backer."

"It's not necessarily a powerful backer; it seems more like Bai Zhe is orchestrating things." Fang Xing had actually heard those ancient beings mention many things about the Dragonborn and Bai Zhe. This immortal cockroach, every time he revives, triggers a great catastrophe.

Fang Xing knew that Bai Zhe's current strength was incredibly powerful compared to the past. If they were to meet in the Earth's core world, even if he, Wang Zhen, and Liu Qingyi joined forces, they might not be a match for him.

At the same time, both he and Wang Zhen could feel the omnipresent malice seeping in through the labyrinth's rock walls, attempting to erode their minds.

...

At this moment, deep within the Earth's core, in that distorted space that defies description.

The Outer God's enormous will, composed of countless tentacles, was emitting an impatient roar.

The resistance of the ancient spirit was even stronger than it had expected. The unexpectedly blooming red lotus from the earth's core and the strange power attached to it seemed to sense the aura of the king's decree, and briefly strengthened the ancient spirit's fortune, hindering its process of devouring and assimilating it.

What angered it even more was that the "little insects" that had invaded the earth's core did not collapse in despair under its drive and siege, but instead escaped into the troublesome labyrinth of Thousand Caves City!

In particular, a faint yet extremely powerful force repeatedly thwarted its efforts!

"Find them...devour! Assimilate!"

A chilling will echoed in the abyss.

The malevolent threads that had seeped into the labyrinth of the Thousand Caves City suddenly thickened and became more active.

The walls inside the maze began to undergo strange changes. Some of the originally hard rock walls became as soft as viscous fluid, and tentacles made of shadows and negative energy reached out from them, silently grabbing at the lost students.

At the end of some passages, illusions begin to appear, which may be the things they most desire or the images they fear most, enticing or forcing them to go in the wrong direction.

Several lone students from the Academy of Sciences were dragged into the rock wall by shadowy tentacles the moment they were bewitched by the illusion. Their screams were cut short, leaving only a wisp of black smoke that quickly dissipated.

The crisis escalates silently within the labyrinth.

...

Wang Ling led Sun Rong to a relatively spacious cave.

This appears to be the site of an ancient sacrificial site, with a dried-up pool in the center and several broken stone pillars standing around it, carved with patterns similar to the Earth Core Red Lotus.

Wang Ling released his grip, walked to a stone pillar, gently placed his palm on it, and closed his eyes to sense it.
