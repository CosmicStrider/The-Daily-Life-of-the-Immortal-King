Chapter 2341 Your Name

The golden "Gate of the Abyss" slowly closed behind him and vanished into the void.

The scene before everyone's eyes quickly changed, replaced by a cold sea breeze carrying the fresh salty and rusty smell of Xi'an, and the deafening roar of the waves in the distance.

Zhou Ziwei, possessed by Wang Ling, appeared along this winding coastline with thousands of lurkers.

Behind them was one of the most famous scenic spots in Songhai: Tianzhiya·Zhangxindian. At this moment, everyone was on the coastline in front of them, looking at the surging dark blue sea.

The atmosphere of war made everyone somewhat uneasy. The low-hanging, lead-gray clouds in the distance seemed within reach, and a strong sense of oppression permeated the air.

Modern cultivators, having lived in a long period of peace, had not felt the atmosphere of a battlefield for a long time. Now, experiencing it firsthand, they couldn't help but feel a surge of emotion.

[Scene: Weeping Cliff Defense Line. Mission: Hold out until dawn and repel all invading enemies! Hua Xiu is immortal!]

At that moment, a brand new message popped up on the bracelet.

The last four words carry a strange power, warming the hearts of many.

Almost the instant the group landed, countless black dots appeared on the distant horizon.

The psionic ship from Sun Island, with a dazzling sun flag flying from its bow, was cutting through the waves and rushing in with overwhelming force.

"Form ranks! Body cultivators at the front! Magic swords in the center! Talisman cultivators, set up defenses! Quickly!"

A deep voice boomed out; it was the physical training instructor, numbered Black 112. He was among the first to react, rushing to the front, waving his arms and shouting at the top of his lungs.

Surprisingly, the shadowy figures, who were previously scheming against each other and refusing to yield to one another, now had no objections.

With the nation and its people facing a formidable enemy, the suspicions that once existed within the Shadow Stalkers' House, based on its rules for raising Gu, have clearly vanished.

Everyone shares the same goal: to protect Hua Xiu!

People subconsciously begin to move, searching for their place.

A young man in gray, belonging to the B-07 family, moved a little slower and blocked the path of a fa Xiu from the A-13 family.

The monk initially intended to reprimand him as usual, but upon seeing the same resolute expression on the other's face, he swallowed his words and silently turned away.

Wang Ling noticed these subtle changes and needed to use this fictional battle to guide these shadowy figures back to the right path.

Wang Ling stood silently at the back of the crowd, his gaze as calm as ever.

He knew that these shadow warriors were generally weak in cultivation and had not experienced a major battle among cultivators for a long time, so they did not yet possess the potential to fully face the enemy. Therefore, Wang Lingxin set up a rule—

In this simulated battlefield, the purer and firmer the Shadow Warriors' belief in "protecting Hua Xiu," the higher the upper limit of spiritual power they can mobilize. At the same time, every time an enemy unit is killed, a trace of pure primordial spiritual power will feed back to them, promoting their cultivation growth.

This generous gift, specially prepared by the king for these shadow agents, is also their means of survival on the battlefield.

However, this advancement in cultivation level is limited to the mission at the Gate of the Abyss. Once they return to the real world, their cultivation level will be reset to zero.

However, it's not entirely without its advantages, because the feeling of their cultivation improving is real. Once they return to the real world, as long as they find the right feeling of improving their cultivation, their future cultivation will be twice as effective.

"Whoosh whoosh-!"

Before the ships from Sun Island had even fully approached, countless orbs enveloped in intense psionic energy rained down like a storm!

"Defense!"

Black 112 roared, its body radiating an unprecedented thick yellow light. It unleashed a punch that blasted into the sky, the force of which was several times stronger than usual, shattering a large area of magic orbs!

He was slightly taken aback, feeling the spiritual energy surging within his body, as if he were about to break through a bottleneck!

"Boom! Boom! Boom!"

Explosions rang out one after another, and the shockwaves from the collision of psionic energy sent sand and gravel flying everywhere.

The coastal defense line is under tremendous pressure, with people constantly being injured and forced to retreat.

"Damn it! Let's fight them!" The lean man with the code Black 201 no longer had any of his previous scheming on his face, only murderous intent!

The poisoned dagger in his hand drew an eerie arc, precisely severing the throat of a Sun Island assault cultivator from a distance.

Just as the Sun Island cultivator collapsed, a warm current carrying pure primordial spiritual power flowed into Hei 201's dantian.

His spiritual energy was not only instantly restored, but it even subtly improved!

"!?!" Black 201's pupils contracted as he looked at his hand in disbelief.

This is clearly not a hallucination!

Similar feelings began to appear in other struggling lurkers.

"Killing enemies...can improve cultivation?!" A young girl in gray had just managed to kill an enemy with a weak fireball spell. She also felt the buff feedback from the kill, a blush returning to her pale face, and a new hope suddenly ignited in her previously fearful eyes.

In the real world, she was just an ordinary person with little presence. Every time she saw the Sun Island Master causing trouble, she wished she could personally go to Sun Island and announce to the world that she had disappeared on Sun Island.

Now that such a great opportunity is before her, and killing enemies can help her improve her cultivation, her fighting spirit is even stronger!

"Those who offend our Chinese people, though far away, shall be punished!"

It's unclear who was the first to shout out this familiar slogan.

The next moment, a thunderous response resounded throughout the entire Bloodshed Cliff!

"Those who offend our Chinese cultivators, though far away, shall be punished! Kill them—!"

At this moment, belief began to be directly linked with power.

The Shadows, who had initially been passively defending, began to show a fierce, proactive glint in their eyes.

They no longer held back their spiritual power, nor did they keep any tricks up their sleeves. Every spell and every swing of their sword carried the determination to completely annihilate any invading enemy!

Those personal grudges and rivalries that had been brewing from the backstabbing and infighting in order to survive the mission behind the door were completely forgotten at this moment.

A burly man in black robes shielded his companion, with whom he had recently clashed over resource allocation, from a deadly blade strike.

He took the hit with his left arm, and his arm bone shattered instantly.

However, he only grunted and smashed the head of the Sun Island cultivator who had attacked him to pieces with a backhand punch!

"Brother...you..." The rescued companion's eyes instantly reddened.

"Enough with the nonsense! My name is Shi Tie! If I make it back alive, I'll treat you to drinks!" The burly man grinned, revealing his blood-stained teeth, and boldly announced his long-forgotten real name from the Shadowman's House.

"Shi Tie... Alright! I've got it! My name is Zhao Chuang!" the companion roared, turning around to unleash his fury on the enemy.

The exchange of real names is happening in every corner of the battlefield at this moment.

These divers all remembered their original names, and they began to realize that their names should not just be cold numbers in the divers' home.

Now, having fought together, they have restored their tacit understanding of shared hatred and rediscovered their original identity and dignity as "Chinese cultivators".

Wang Ling continued to drift along with the crowd, symbolically releasing beams of spiritual energy.

But his consciousness was secretly manipulating the battle.

Time and again, he redirected the deadly attacks to open areas, trying his best to appease the crowd.

At this moment, he was more like a battlefield commander hidden among the crowd, constantly using his own power to guide these shadowy figures in their struggle.

However, the real test has only just begun.
