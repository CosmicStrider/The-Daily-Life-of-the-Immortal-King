Chapter 2349 The Love Strategist Alliance is established!

The game ended, and Wang Ling calmly exited the game cabin.

Almost the instant his consciousness returned to reality, the portable communication device on his wrist vibrated, projecting Chen Chao's large face covered with the word "gossip".

"Your son! Quickly! Confess and you'll be treated leniently; resist and you'll be punished severely!"

Even through the projector, Chen Chao's voice was filled with excitement: "You're acting really strange today! Tell me, are you... having a reaction to Sun Rong?"

Wang Ling: "..."

Wang Ling was speechless at Chen Chao's smirking expression.

It will react? What kind of reaction will it trigger?

But Wang Ling's face remained expressionless; he merely tilted his head, seemingly still processing the issue.

What about Sun Rong?

That feeling was strange.

Seeing her playing games with that "Whirlwind Qingqingzi" made me feel a slight tightness in my chest, as if something had gently twisted it.

Then, his fingers started moving uncontrollably, chopping and slashing at Liu Kaichuan.

Wang Ling felt that this was not the kind of goodwill Chen Chao had described.

He paused for a few seconds, organized his thoughts, and explained in what he considered a very logical tone: "He has many flaws in his gameplay. He's suitable as a target for practicing heroes."

"......"

Chen Chao's smile froze. He picked at his ear and said, "No, Lingzi, look me in the eye and say it again! Look my eyes! Tell my why? You call that practicing heroes? You've almost given President Liu a psychological trauma! He's a 20-star King of Glory player! And you treated him like a bot!"

"Hmm." Wang Ling nodded and added, "Even among kings, there are differences in strength."

Chen Chao almost spat out a mouthful of blood: "Then tell me, why do you always let Sun Rong go? Don't tell me you didn't see it! She's been hovering over your face several times!"

"..." Wang Ling paused, then found what he thought was an impeccable reason: "The screen will go gray after a kill, which is bad for your eyes."

Chen Chao: "???"

He took several deep breaths in front of the projection, looking at Wang Ling's still expressionless face, and suddenly felt a sense of powerlessness, like punching cotton.

He had known Wang Ling for so long and knew that this guy could be a bit clueless at times, but today's reaction was beyond clueless; it was practically an emotional cognitive disorder!

But who is Chen Chao? He personally witnessed Wang Ling venture into the Earth's core for Sun Rong, and he could vaguely sense Wang Ling's unique concern for Sun Rong.

He was certain that Wang Ling had definitely become somewhat enlightened, but he himself was either unaware of it or... just being stubborn?

"Okay, okay, you're not jealous. You just want to practice Hero, and you just think President Liu is bad."

Chen Chao gave up on reasoning with Wang Ling and chuckled, "But Lingzi, as your brother, I have to say, well done!"

Wang Ling: "?"

"It's nothing!" Chen Chao laughed. "You should get some rest! The city-level competition is starting soon, we still need to catch up on training! Bye!"

The communication was cut off, and Chen Chao's projection disappeared.

Wang Ling frowned slightly as he looked at the empty room.

Chen Chao's reaction made him feel that his explanation was not accepted.

He looked down at his hands, and the image of Lulu, controlled by Sun Rong, standing in front of him with a sliver of blood flashed through his mind.

Why... didn't you kill her?

This emotion was unfamiliar, causing an invisible shackle within him to tremble slightly. Then, the strange feeling was quietly suppressed by the sealing talisman and returned to calm.

Meanwhile, Chen Chao was sprawled out on his bed in his bedroom, staring at the ceiling.

The smile on his face gradually faded, replaced by a complex emotion.

"This piece of wood..." he muttered under his breath.

Once upon a time, he was one of Sun Rong's many suitors.

That bright and cheerful girl from a prominent family, yet hardworking and earnest, was the white moonlight in the hearts of almost all the boys at Number 60 High School.

He had also mustered the courage to send gifts and make appointments for reconciliation, but Sun Rong always politely declined.

He could tell that Sun Rong's gaze remained fixed on his best brother, Wang Ling.

After returning from the inner Earth, he understood everything more completely.

Some things cannot be forced.

Although I felt a pang of sadness, I felt mostly relieved.

"Forget it, forget it." Chen Chao rolled over and slapped his face hard. "A brother's wife is not to be trifled with! Wait... they're not even together yet! Then I should give them a hand!"

Chen Chao is someone who can take things in stride!

Since Sun Rong's heart belongs to Wang Ling, and Wang Ling, that blockhead, has finally shown some signs of understanding today, then as her brother, he must help her achieve her goal!

But how can we provide the assist?

Wang Ling is impervious to reason, and I'm sure my skills are no match for him.

Chen Chao's eyes darted around, and he suddenly sat up in bed.

He thought of two people—Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi.

These transfer students were unusual from the moment they appeared.

In the Earth's core, their performance far surpasses that of ordinary high school students, and... Wang Zhen and Wang Ling both share the surname Wang?

Chen Chao's intuition told him that Wang Zhen was definitely not simple, and his strength was probably unfathomable.

He even vaguely suspected that Wang Zhen and Wang Ling might be related, and that Wang Zhen's purpose in coming to Number 60 High School was to protect Wang Ling from being bullied.

This idea excited him.

If Wang Zhen is that capable and also one of our own, wouldn't it be much more efficient to ask him for help and support?

Without hesitation, Chen Chao immediately picked up the communication device, found Wang Zhen's contact number, took a deep breath, and dialed it.

...

Inside the family apartment in Matsumi City.

Wang Zhen was pinned down on the sofa and ravaged by Liu Qingyi.

Because he accidentally spilled ice cream on Liu Qingyi's newly bought limited edition robe.

The playful banter between the young couple has become commonplace, and Wei Zhi, who lives with them, is used to it and pretends not to see it even when he does.

At this moment, Chen Chao's call saved Wang Zhen.

"Huh? Why did he call me?" Wang Zhen answered the call with some surprise.

He and Chen Chao had fought side by side in the inner world, but they didn't have much contact in private. It wasn't because he felt that his status as a scion of a divine family was vastly different from that of a mortal on Earth, and he looked down on Chen Chao, but rather because he felt that if he had too much contact with Chen Chao and Guo Hao, it would be difficult to conceal himself, and might even cause trouble for Wang Ling.

"Haha, Wang Zhen, did I disturb you?" Chen Chao's cheerful voice rang out.

"No, is there anything wrong?" Wang Zhen asked.

Liu Qingyi pouted, released her grip on his ear, but moved closer to him, gesturing for him to turn on speakerphone.

"Well, it's like this." Chen Chao lowered his voice a little: "Wang Zhen, I'm a pretty straightforward person, so I'll get straight to the point. I always felt... that you and Liu Qingyi weren't ordinary high school students, were you?"

Wang Zhen's heart skipped a beat, but his face remained calm: "Why do you say that, Chen Chao?"
