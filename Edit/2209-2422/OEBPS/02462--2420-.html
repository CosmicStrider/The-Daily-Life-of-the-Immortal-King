Chapter 2420 Some people are destined not to be kept.

The gymnasium's basement level has a storage room that has been converted into a temporary meeting room.

Lord Diu Lei sat on the only chair that wasn't dusty, crossed his legs, leaned against the Heavenly Dao Broccoli doll he had brought, and fiddled with a mint candy he had just pulled out of his sleeve.

The door was open.

Luo Xinghe stood at the doorway but did not go inside.

It wasn't that I didn't want to go in, it was that the two disciples of the Battle Sect at the entrance stood there like two guardian deities.

He didn't receive a clear instruction to "come in," and his legs felt like they were filled with lead.

"Mr. Luo." Diu Lei Zhenjun popped a mint into his mouth, his voice languid: "Standing at the door enjoying the breeze? Come in and sit down."

Luo Xinghe took a deep breath and stepped over the threshold.

The door closed silently behind him.

There were only two people left in the room.

Luo Xinghe stood still, without sitting down.

It's not that I don't want to sit, it's that I'm afraid to.

"That match today," Lord Thunderclap began, his tone as casual as if he were talking about the weather, "You watched it?"

Luo Xinghe's throat tightened: "I saw it."

"Is it pretty?"

"......Wonderful."

Lord Diu Lei smiled and raised his head.

Those eyes held no murderous intent, even a hint of gentleness, yet they still sent a chill down Luo Xinghe's spine.

Luo Xinghe dared not say a word.

Lord Diu Lei continued, "I've known student Wang Ling for a long time. He's very outstanding, you know? Strictly speaking, he's like my younger brother."

After saying this, Lord Diu Lei felt a little embarrassed, as if he had taken advantage of Wang Ling. However, at this critical moment, he was only pretending to say that in order to protect Wang Ling. He felt that Wang Ling would not mind the brotherhood between him and Wang Ling.

Luo Xinghe's pupils contracted slightly.

He knew that Diu Lei Zhenjun and Wang Ling were acquaintances.

But he didn't know it was this level of relationship.

"So?" Lord Thunderclap looked at him, his tone still gentle. "Now you know what I'm going to say?"

Luo Xinghe remained silent for three seconds.

"True Lord," he began, his voice low, "I did not know that Wang Ling and you had this connection. My previous offense was my fault."

"Oh?" Lord Die Lei raised an eyebrow.

"I will stop the investigation into him." Luo Xinghe lowered his eyes: "All the arrangements made before... will be stopped. As for today's interference device incident, I will also go to the judging panel to explain the situation and take full responsibility."

Lord Diu Lei didn't speak, he just looked at him.

That gaze was not oppressive; in fact, it could be described as peaceful.

But Luo Xinghe felt as if he had been stripped naked and was standing in the snow.

"Mr. Luo," Lord Diulei finally spoke, his tone tinged with regret, "when you said those words, you were thinking that we should get through this immediate problem first and then make further plans later, right?"

Luo Xinghe's expression changed slightly.

"No need to deny it." Lord Thunder Displacement waved his hand: "I've lived a long time and seen too many people like you. Young, talented, unwilling to accept defeat, thinking that you're just unlucky, the timing is wrong, or the opponent is too strong. You always think that you can turn the tables next time, that you can win back the game with the next move."

He stood up and said slowly, "I'm not here to threaten you."

With his back to him, Lord Diu Lei spoke softly: "I just want to tell you one thing: you can't touch him."

Luo Xinghe's face turned deathly pale.

It's not fear.

It is hatred.

On what grounds?

Why is it that Wang Ling, an ordinary high school student, could have the Sword Saint personally come and the War Sect Master speak for him?

Why is it that my hard work since childhood, my foundation building at sixteen, my golden core at eighteen, and the connections and resources I've accumulated over five years in the professional world are no match for theirs?

On what grounds?

He didn't speak, but a deep-seated resentment seeped from every pore of his body.

Lord Diu Lei watched and suddenly sighed.

It's so good to be young.

They can still get angry over such trivial matters.

He raised his hand and made a gesture in mid-air.

Luo Xinghe could not see any spiritual energy fluctuations.

But in that instant, he felt something sink above his head.

It is a... very subtle sign of fate.

A faint gray mist was reflected deep within the pupils of Lord Diu Lei.

The gray mist emanated from between Luo Xinghe's eyebrows, meandering upwards and condensing into a faint, indistinct phantom three feet above his head.

This is a sign of impending death.

Lord Diu Lei remained silent for two seconds.

With his level of skill, it's not impossible for him to forcibly reverse this deathly state, but the cost would be extremely high.

The question is, is it worth it?

He recalled Wang Ling's perpetually calm face.

Your brother never specifically instructed anyone to be "taken care of," nor did he ever say he would "let go" of anyone.

Your brother is just living his own life.

Those who stand in the way of life will naturally crash into themselves.

"That's it."

Lord Diu Lei withdrew his gaze, his tone becoming indifferent: "I have said my piece. You'd better take care of yourself."

Luo Xinghe bowed deeply, took three steps back, turned around and pushed open the door.

His back was ramrod straight, and his steps were steady; there was not a trace of embarrassment about him.

The door closed behind him again.

Lord Diu Lei stared at the door and sighed.

He saw that the character "危" (danger) above Luo Xinghe's head was almost dripping blood, but he couldn't see it himself.

...

When Luo Xinghe came up from the basement, there were hardly any people left in the corridor.

The quarterfinals have all ended, and the audience is leaving one after another, leaving only the cleaning staff pushing cleaning carts back and forth.

He stood at the top of the stairs and took out his phone.

The screen lit up; Mo Xuan's message had arrived a minute earlier:

The surveillance data has been saved, but the venue just notified us that all of today's footage has been lost due to a system malfunction.

The jammer was remotely formatted three minutes after the match; the source of the operation could not be found.

We've been wiped out.

Luo Xinghe stared at the screen, his thumb hovering over the input box for a long time.

In the end, he only replied with three words:

【knew.】

He put away his phone and walked towards the exit.

As he passed by the B section of the stands, he subconsciously stopped for a moment.

It's empty now.

Sword Saint is gone, Thunder Lord is gone, and the group of students from Number 60 High School are gone too.

The cleaning lady is bending down to pick up the potato chip crumbs under the third row of seats.

Luo Xinghe stood there and suddenly felt that the scene was a bit absurd.

An hour ago, the Ten Generals, the Sect Master of the War Sect, and elites from various schools were sitting here.

Now, only one auntie is left sweeping up potato chip crumbs.

He turned around and went into the restroom.

He splashed cold water into his palms, looked up, and stared at the face in the mirror.

With regular features, sharp eyes and brows, and a cultivation level of mid-stage Golden Core at the age of twenty-six, he would be a key figure to be cultivated in any sect.

He did nothing wrong.

He just wanted to win.

He has been a winner since childhood.

He reached the Foundation Establishment stage at sixteen, the Golden Core stage at eighteen, made it to the professional league finals at twenty, and became a top streamer on the platform at twenty-three.

He was used to winning.

So when Wang Ling appeared, and when that person crushed him into the ground with such a nonchalant attitude, he was indignant.

It's not that I can't accept defeat.

That person didn't consider him an opponent at all.

During that simulated battle in the illusion, Wang Ling didn't look at him once from beginning to end.

That kind of disregard is more hurtful than any mockery.

Luo Xinghe turned off the tap and watched the water droplets drip down his wrist into the sink.

"The True Lord said he can't touch you."

He spoke softly to himself in the mirror.

"What about Sun Rong?"

The reflection in the mirror did not answer.

Luo Xinghe dried his hands and walked out of the restroom.

The evening breeze blew into the corridor, carrying the coolness of early autumn.

He took out his phone and found a number he hadn't contacted in a long time.

The note contains only two characters: 【江流】

The phone rang three times before being answered.

"Luo Xinghe?" The voice was hoarse, like sandpaper rubbing against a wooden board: "A rare guest."

"Senior Jiang," Luo Xinghe said calmly, "I need to speak with you."

There was a few seconds of silence on the other end.
