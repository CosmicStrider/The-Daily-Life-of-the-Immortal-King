Chapter 2379 Public Opinion Vortex

Luo Xinghe practically rushed back to his room.

He leaned against the door, the laughter piercing his ears like needles.

He was sure everyone was laughing at him.

They laughed at him for jumping around like a clown in the training camp, eventually falling and getting bruised and battered.

"Wang Ling...Sun Rong..."

Luo Xinghe slid down to the floor with his back against the door, his hands plunging into his hair, his nails digging fiercely into his scalp.

When has Luo Xinghe ever suffered such humiliation?

In every match against Wang Ling, the opponent completely crushed him, repeatedly humiliating him in public.

What's even more hateful is that Xia Zhinuan, that bitch, says nice things but is actually two-faced, and has been helping outsiders to deal with her all along!

"They all betrayed me...they all looked down on me..."

Luo Xinghe raised his head, his eyes bloodshot.

He can't just let this go!

If you can't win in the game, try another way.

Luo Xinghe was, after all, a big streamer with millions of fans. In the public sphere, he had plenty of ways to ruin a high school student's reputation.

And then there's Sun Rong... that pretentious woman!

Since she has chosen to side with Wang Ling, then she shouldn't blame him for dragging her down with him.

Luo Xinghe supported himself on the ground to stand up, staggered to the coffee table, and grabbed his phone.

The screen lit up, and the lock screen wallpaper was still a fashion photo he had taken some time ago.

In the photo, his silver-gray hair gleams in the sunlight, and his smile is confident and composed.

But now, in the mirror, his face is pale and his eyes are gloomy, like a gambler who has lost everything.

"It's okay..." Luo Xinghe forced a twisted smile at the mirror: "The game isn't over yet."

He unlocked his phone, opened WeChat, and found a contact whose contact was labeled "AAA Water Army Leader - Ah Long".

This was one of the connections he had built up over three years in the industry, specializing in taking on jobs in the gray areas.

It's expensive, but the results are absolutely top-notch.

Luo Xinghe's fast typing:

"Brother Long, I've got a big job. Target: a high school student, from Number 60 High School, named Wang Ling. I want him to become famous online."

A few seconds later, the other party replied: "Detailed requirements?"

"First, create rumors that he cheated in the training matches. Say that he used cultivation techniques to interfere with the game data, but that he has a strong background and that the training camp is protecting him."

"Secondly, it implies that he has a special relationship with Sun Rong, the daughter of the Huaguo Shuilian Group, and that he got in through her family background."

"Third, portray him as someone who bullies others and suppresses other athletes."

"We'll be as ruthless as we can; the budget isn't an issue."

Ah Long paused for a moment before sending a quote: "This kind of comprehensive smear campaign, including fabricating evidence, starts at 300,000. If we want it to spread within the gaming community, we'll need to pay extra to hire a few influential figures to sway the narrative. Also... the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group is not easy to deal with. If the budget isn't enough, those influential figures might not be willing to take action."

"Five million." Luo Xinghe immediately raised the price: "I want to see results within three days. Pay a deposit of five hundred thousand first, and settle the rest after the deal is done."

"Deal made. Send me the details."

Luo Xinghe found photos of Wang Ling that he had secretly taken in the training camp's internal group chat.

The boy, dressed in the uniform of Number 60 High School, stood in front of the snack area, holding a pack of instant noodles in his hand, his expression so calm it was almost blank.

With that face, he looks like an ordinary high school student.

But the more ordinary someone is, the easier it is to create the image of a "scheming man who pretends to be weak while actually being strong."

He then searched for information about Sun Rong online.

The identity of the young lady of the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group is public information, which can be used to make up a story.

Is the esports star actually the gigolo of a chaebol heiress?

The Inside Story of Training Camps: How Did an F-Level Qualified Trainee Crush a Professional Broadcaster?

Let's take a closer look at that "genius" athlete who rose to fame through women.

Luo Xinghe had already come up with more than a dozen sensational headlines.

He packaged the information and sent it to Ah Long, then transferred a deposit of 500,000.

After doing all that, he walked to the window and looked out at the Starry Night eSports Center in the night sky.

Several lights were still on in the training area of Building A, presumably indicating that some people were still practicing.

"Wang Ling, what are you doing right now?" Luo Xinghe sneered, "Still munching on your instant noodles?"

He imagined Wang Ling's expression when he saw those smear articles.

Will that face, which never shows any expression, finally crack?

And Sun Rong... that self-righteous woman, when she sees her "scandal" with Wang Ling being spread all over the place, will she be so angry that she jumps up and down?

"This is just the beginning," Luo Xinghe said in a low voice. "The real show is yet to come."

He has another card to play.

At the same time, in the monitoring room on the third floor of Building A.

Zhuo Yi sat in front of the control panel, looking at the thermal image of Luo Xinghe's room on the screen.

"He's been standing there for ten minutes." Wang Zhen came over to look: "Is he thinking of doing something rash?"

"Not at all." Ryoko Kugimiya tapped her palm lightly with her folding fan. "This kind of person is arrogant and proud. The more he loses, the more fiercely he bounces back. I bet he's plotting his revenge right now."

Liu Qingyi crossed her arms: "Should we just get rid of him? Let him get injured in the training camp and withdraw from the competition early?"

"There's no need." Zhuo Yi shook his head. "My master said there's no need to bother with such a clown. Besides... it's fine to keep him around."

"Okay?" Wang Zhen raised an eyebrow: "Brother Zhuo, you don't actually want to watch a show, do you?"

Zhuo Yi smiled, switched screens, and brought up the communication monitoring records.

The above displays the metadata of Luo Xinghe's encrypted call just now.

"He contacted a middleman named 'A Long' and transferred a deposit of 500,000 yuan," Zhuo Yi said. "This A Long has a record on the dark web and specializes in taking on jobs involving smear campaigns and cyberbullying. It seems that our great anchor Luo is planning to get revenge in the public opinion arena."

Ryoko Kugimiya's eyes lit up: "He's trying to smear Wang Ling?"

"And Sun Rong." Zhuo Yi enlarged the transfer note.

Wang Zhen clicked his tongue: "That's really despicable. If you can't win a game, you resort to dirty tricks."

"Isn't this perfect?" Ryoko Kumiya closed her folding fan, a sly smile spreading across her face. "External pressure is the best catalyst for feelings. Especially when two people face a storm of public opinion together..."

Liu Qingyi understood: "You mean, we want to use this opportunity to advance to the third stage?"

"That's right." Ryoko Kugimiya nodded. "Although Luo Xinghe's actions were disgusting, they objectively created excellent conditions. Imagine: Wang Ling and Rongrong are pushed into the spotlight because of a baseless rumor, and the two have to deal with it together. In this process, Wang Ling will instinctively protect Rongrong, and Rongrong will become more dependent on him..."

Zhuo Yi added, "Moreover, these experiences of sharing hardships are often more memorable than ordinary daily life. As long as we guide them properly from behind the scenes and ensure that public opinion does not really harm them, this wave can actually be an assist."

Wang Zhen thought for a moment: "Then what should we do?"

"Three things." Zhuo Yi held up one finger: "First, monitor online public opinion. We can't let Master and Junior Sister Sun's reputations be damaged. If necessary, guide the direction of public opinion. I will contact Mr. Wang Ming and Zhan Zong to be on guard at all times and be prepared for a reshuffling of public opinion. If necessary, the public's memory of this story will be directly distorted and then modified."

"This works?" Wang Zhen was taken aback.

"Mr. Wang Ming's brain radiation range, combined with specialized equipment, can already cover the human world; it's not a difficult task."

Zhuo Yi nodded and continued, "Secondly, create some scenarios within the training camp where the two of you have to work together. For example... for tomorrow's internal finals, could we adjust the group assignments?"

Ryoko Kugimiya immediately pulled up the schedule: "Tomorrow morning is the final round of the Hextech Brawl points tournament, and the afternoon is the draw for the exhibition match. If we team up Wang Ling and Rongrong in the finals, it will be very effective."

"One last thing," Zhuo Yi looked at everyone, "Give Luo Xinghe a little taste of success, so he thinks his plan is going smoothly. When he reaches his peak of agitation..."
