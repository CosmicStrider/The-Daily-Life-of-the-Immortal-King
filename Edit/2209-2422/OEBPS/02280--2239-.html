Chapter 2239 Solitude in the Medical Clinic

Inside the central hall, the atmosphere was as oppressive as the sea before a storm.

Teng Luchen stared at Wang Ling, who was being carefully carried into the medical lounge on the screen. His old face, covered with shrewd lines, was so gloomy that it looked like it could be squeezed out of water.

The monk Jin Deng's "righteous indignation" accusations were still ringing in his ears, and the medical team leader's report just now felt like a blow to the heart.

"Commander Fujii..."

The head of the medical team was a meticulous middle-aged female cultivator. She pushed up her glasses and spoke with a professional and steady tone.

"We used the highest precision spiritual vein resonance imaging instrument and consciousness wave detector. The results showed that Wang Ling's spiritual energy was severely depleted, with multiple overload spasms and micro-cracks in his meridians, and weak and chaotic fluctuations in his sea of consciousness. Based on the comprehensive assessment, it was determined that he suffered a spiritual pressure impact far exceeding his own tolerance limit, resulting in a deep spiritual energy backlash and a self-protective mental dormancy. In short, the symptoms of severe injury, overexertion, and unstable foundation are completely consistent."

"What? No... traces of disguise?" Teng Luchen's voice was tinged with disbelief.

"Disguise?"

The head of the medical team frowned slightly, seemingly feeling that the questioning was somewhat offensive to her professionalism.

"Commander Teng, the instruments don't lie. This level of fake injury requires mimicking real spiritual energy depletion, physical damage to the meridians, and the stress response of the sea of consciousness. Its complexity and risk far exceed withstanding a spiritual pressure impact. With his Foundation Establishment cultivation, it's absolutely impossible for him to achieve such a flawless result."

"..." Teng Luchen fell silent.

That's obvious. Doesn't he suspect that Wang Ling isn't at the Foundation Establishment stage, which is why he was able to disguise himself so perfectly?

He stared at Wang Ling's pale, peaceful sleeping face on the screen. Reason told him that the medical team's judgment was probably correct, but the thorn in his heart called "intuition" pierced even deeper.

Could it be that his meticulously prepared barrage of artillery fire and that final burst of spiritual pressure, by sheer chance, completely rendered this "mascot" he so skeptical of utterly useless?

This result... is truly bizarre...

"Elder Teng, what about the subsequent tests..." Jing Heqiu asked tentatively, secretly lamenting that Elder Teng seemed to have gone too far this time.

Teng Luchen took a deep breath, forcibly suppressing his turbulent thoughts, a shrewd calculation flashing in his eyes.

Since the test failed, why not play along?

A cold smile curled at the corner of his mouth as a plan formed in his mind.

"The stress test is crucial to the team's survival rate after entering the Earth's core. How can it be neglected because of one person?"

Teng Luchen's voice regained its usual authority, but carried an undeniable coldness: "Student Wang Ling... since the medical team has confirmed that his condition prevents him from participating, there's no need to force him. However..."

He changed the subject, his gaze falling on Sun Rong, who was standing close to the stretcher on the screen, her face full of worry.

"Sun Rong, classmate."

Teng Luchen's voice, amplified through the sound array, clearly reached the ears of everyone who had just entered the medical lounge: "Wang Ling was injured on duty and needs to rest and be observed. You are the future leader of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, an expert in pharmacology, and a close friend of Wang Ling from the same school. This responsibility of taking care of him falls squarely on your shoulders. You also do not need to participate in the stress test."

Sun Rong tightened her grip on the stretcher, looked up in surprise, seemingly completely unprepared for this turn of events.

Let her take care of Wang Ling? She doesn't need to take the test?

This...this is practically a pie falling from the sky!

The overwhelming joy instantly dispelled her worries, causing two red clouds to rise on her fair cheeks, and her heart began to pound uncontrollably.

But she immediately realized the situation, forcibly suppressed her excitement, put on a reluctant yet obliging expression, and bowed slightly to the air: "Yes, Commander Teng. I will take good care of Wang Ling."

The monk Jin Deng stood to the side with his hands clasped together, looking as if he had been knelt down: "I hope Commander Teng can restrain the intensity of subsequent tests and prevent such accidents from happening again."

His words still carried a hint of criticism.

Teng Luchen snorted and did not respond.

He achieved his goal—to tie Sun Rong, a "variable" who also needed close observation, together with Wang Ling, a "useless person," keeping them under his nose, while depriving them of the opportunity to participate in the testing and reveal more details.

Moreover, having Sun Rong look after him might allow her to observe some clues about Wang Ling's unconscious state.

The medical rest area is located in a relatively secluded corner of the entire experimental base, and is a semi-open space.

Soft light streamed down from the dome, and the air was filled with a faint scent of calming herbs.

The room is simply furnished with several comfortable treatment beds, several bookshelves displaying various materials, illustrations, and ancient books about the inner Earth, and a large alloy table.

Wang Ling was placed on the innermost bed, his breath still faint.

Sun Rong sat on a chair by the bed, looking at him with a complicated expression. The medical team's equipment had been removed, leaving only a basic vital signs monitoring rune floating above Wang Ling's bedside, emitting a soft green light.

"Wang...Ling?" Sun Rong murmured in a voice only she could hear, her fingertips gently brushing against Wang Ling's cold hand, feeling both heartache and amusement.

"You're really good at pretending..."

She was 100% certain that Wang Ling was faking it...

The talisman's effect could fool the instruments, but it couldn't fool her.

Teng Luchen's self-righteous scheming only served to benefit her.

Not long after, a staff member brought over a tray with several pills for nourishing the meridians and a pot of spiritual tea. He told Sun Rong that she and Wang Ling had been mainly staying in this medical hall for the past few days, and that the base would deliver meals on time.

At the same time, she thoughtfully cleared out the large table and placed more data from the Earth's core on it, making it easier for her to "take care of things and study at the same time".

After the staff left, only two people remained in the large medical hall.

Sun Rong's heart started racing again, much to her dismay.

She carefully tucked the blanket around Wang Ling, her movements so gentle as if she were afraid of disturbing something.

Then, I walked to the bookshelf, ran my finger along the spines of the books, and finally pulled out a thick book, "Creatures from the Earth's Core (Revised Edition)" and another book, "Tales of the Earth's Core".

She carried the books and sat down at the large alloy table, placing one in front of her and the other gently on the empty seat next to her—as if Wang Ling had only left temporarily and would return at any time to sit down and read together.

After doing all this, Sun Rong's cheeks felt hot again.

She composed herself, opened the book in front of her, and forced herself to focus on the descriptions of strange creatures and harsh environments from the Earth's core.

However, her thoughts involuntarily drifted to the empty chair beside her, and to the quietly "sleeping" boy.

His fingertips unconsciously rotated the scabbard of the Ouhai sword on his wrist, but he couldn't help but smile slightly.

Nothing could be more peaceful and serene than this.

Meanwhile, inside the "Vientiane Stress Test Site" in the core area of the base, a completely different and intense scene unfolded.

The enormous annular space was divided into three independent areas, corresponding to tests of gravity collapse, spiritual pressure permeation, and geomagnetic maze, respectively.

Elite representatives from each school entered different testing areas in randomly assigned groups.

In the gravity collapse zone, three times the gravity of Earth grips everyone like an invisible giant hand.
