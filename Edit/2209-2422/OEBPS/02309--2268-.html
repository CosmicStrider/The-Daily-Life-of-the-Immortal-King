Chapter 2268 Bai Zhe's Horror

The narrow passage came to an end.

The view suddenly opened up, but was instantly filled with boundless ferocity.

This is a space that is slightly smaller than the previous planetary heart cavity, but even more suffocating.

At the center of the space is not a physical entity, but a spring that constantly gushes forth a chaotic color—the [Earth Vein Source Eye].

Within the source eye, it seems to contain the vitality and power of the entire planet, and each surge carries a profound sense of creation.

It is the core of the ancient spirit's power and the ultimate source of the pulse of the inner world.

However, this place, which should be sacred, is now shrouded in tyranny and madness.

Around the source eye stood four enormous figures.

They are not made of flesh and blood, but are composed of the purest dark golden earth crystal cores and violent energy, with a blurred and ferocious form.

"Isn't it just a stone man?" Wang Ling was silent for a moment.

He seems even smarter than the clever Murphy.

Their eyes burned with murky, earthy-yellow flames. What should have been a steadfast will to protect the Earth's source eye was now nothing but a frenzied desire for destruction and an extreme hatred for outsiders.

At this moment, black, viscous threads, which originated from the same source as the previous shadows but were thicker and more solid, were wrapped around the stone's body. They were deeply embedded in the energy core like parasitic vines, twisting its will.

Wisps of impure energy are attempting to pollute the pure source below through these threads.

The anxieties of the ancient land have become a reality.

These strongest guardians have been corrupted and transformed into the greatest threat to the Source Eye.

The moment Wang Ling and Sun Rong stepped into this place, the four corrupted guardians simultaneously let out deafening roars.

The roar was a mixture of pain and utter madness.

Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!

Four terrifying spiritual pressures, enough to instantly crush the peak of a Dao God, crashed down on the two of them like physical mountains! The space itself trembled, and the primordial energy surging from the source eye became chaotic.

Sun Rong's expression changed drastically. The Ao Hai Sword was instantly drawn half an inch from its sheath, and the azure sword aura automatically protected its master, colliding fiercely with the overwhelming spiritual pressure!

Bang--!

Sun Rong let out a muffled groan, her body trembled violently, and her protective sword aura flickered erratically as she was forced to retreat step by step.

Even with just the clash of spiritual pressure, this power was already unstoppable!

The pinnacle of Dao God! A major realm second only to Dao Ancestor! It far surpasses the previous Dao Venerable-level elemental guardians!

Sun Rong sensed that as they went deeper, the level of danger emerging from the Earth's core far exceeded what was known on the surface. These beings, who transcended the realm of the mortal world, possessed terrifying power—even the aftershocks of battle were enough to destroy an entire continent in an instant!

"Wang Ling, be careful!" Sun Rong shouted urgently, activating her Ao Hai with all her might. The red lotus petals shone brightly, resisting the all-pervasive and frenzied spiritual pressure.

However, Wang Ling stood still, not even the hem of his clothes was stirred by the wind.

The four spiritual pressures, powerful enough to shatter mountains and split seas, vanished silently as soon as they approached within three feet of his body, as if they had hit an insurmountable invisible wall. They couldn't even make a hair on his head flutter.

He even had time to finish the last of his snacks, then carefully folded the packaging bag and put it back in his pocket.

The four guardians seemed to be stunned for a moment, and the madness in their murky eyes intensified.

They clearly couldn't understand why their pressure was ineffective against that tiny human.

"The invaders... must be destroyed..."

"Eye of Source Protection... Eliminate everything..."

Intermittent, malevolent thoughts emanated from them, already resembling the tone of an Outer God's will.

One of the guardians launched the first attack, raising its giant fist, made of crystal cores, and carrying the terrifying power of the law of collapse, smashing it down on Wang Ling in a simple and brutal manner!

Wherever the fist passed, the space seemed to groan in agony, unable to bear the weight!

This punch is enough to shatter a small star!

Sun Rong's heart was in her throat!

Wang Ling finally raised his eyelids and glanced at the enormous fist that blotted out the sky.

Just as the giant fist was about to land, Wang Ling made a very simple move.

He extended his right index finger and gently poked upwards at the giant rock fist, which was countless times larger than his entire body.

The movement was so gentle, as if he were about to pop a soap bubble.

Time seemed to stretch out indefinitely at that moment.

Before Sun Rong's incredulous gaze, the gigantic fist, imbued with the power of destruction, touched Wang Ling's fingertips—

From the point of contact, it instantly turned into sand and dust shimmering with dark golden light, collapsing silently!

And this "desertification" trend is spreading upwards at an incredible speed, from the arms to the shoulders, and eventually extending to half the torso...

The guardian couldn't even utter a scream. In the blink of an eye, its enormous body completely disintegrated into a pool of lifeless, dark golden sand, piled up on the spot like a small mountain.

The black threads wrapped around it twisted wildly, trying to escape, but at the same time they turned to ashes.

Sold out in seconds!

A true instant kill!

It wasn't about repelling or shattering, but about transforming its form directly from "Guardian" back into "a puddle of sand"!

The roars of the remaining three guardians abruptly ceased, and a rare, primal fear appeared in their murky, mad eyes!

What is this tiny creature before me?!

Wang Ling withdrew his finger, blew away the non-existent dust, and glanced at the remaining three statues.

The three guardians' enormous bodies all shrank back slightly!

But the Outer God's will that was eroding them was clearly unwilling to fail, and forcibly suppressed that fear, manipulating them to launch an attack at the same time!

A giant mouth opened, spewing out a murky yellow breath powerful enough to corrode everything!

With a fist slammed into the ground, countless sharp spikes burst forth from beneath Wang Ling and Sun Rong's feet!

The last one clasped his hands together, drawing upon the power of the Source Eye to unleash a massive gravitational field, intending to crush the two into a pulp!

Faced with this overwhelming attack, Wang Ling's expression remained calm and composed.

He stretched out his right hand, spread his five fingers, and gently pressed down into the void in front of him.

There was no sound, no light effects.

But the entire source space seemed to have been paused.

The surging, yellowish breath froze in mid-air, then vanished without a trace, like pencil marks erased by an eraser.

The sharp spikes that burst forth turned to dust and fell back to the ground when they were only 0.01 millimeters away from Wang Ling's feet.

The immense gravitational field disintegrated and vanished like an avalanche the moment it touched Wang Ling's surrounding force field.

Does a word spoken carry the weight of law?

No, this is a matter of a single thought, and the law arises spontaneously!

Immediately afterwards, Wang Ling slowly closed his outstretched five fingers.

As his fingers closed, the three enormous guardians seemed to be squeezed by the entire world, unable to even struggle, their massive bodies began to collapse rapidly from the outside in!

Snap... Bang!

Like clay sculptures crushed by an invisible giant hand, the three guardians were compressed into three extremely solid, dark golden spheres, each the size of a fist, almost simultaneously.

Then with a soft "snap," it vanished completely, disappearing without a trace.

Along with the evil black threads within them, they were completely wiped out of this world.

Silence returned to the Source Eye Space.

All that remained was the gurgling sound of the earth's vein source and Sun Rong's somewhat rapid breathing.

She looked at the empty space in front of her, and then at Wang Ling beside her who hadn't moved an inch, and felt her mind go completely blank.
