Chapter 2375 It's all about being big

In an instant, the entire room fell silent.

"Holy crap! This is possible?!"

"Flash to devour! Forced one-for-one trade!"

"No, it was a one-for-zero trade! Tahm Kench isn't dead!"

"This move...this audacity..."

Luo Xinghe stared at his black-and-white screen, his face ashen.

He had planned everything perfectly, but he hadn't anticipated that Wang Ling would flash forward and forcibly replace him with his remaining health.

What no one expected was that Wang Ling's understanding and application of Tahm Kench's skills were terrifyingly precise.

"I was careless." Luo Xinghe gritted his teeth: "Next time, I won't give him another chance."

The match lasts five minutes.

A system notification sounded:

[Hextech Rune Draw Time's Over]

A new roulette wheel appears in front of each player, covered with densely packed rune icons.

Rotate the pointer.

Stop.

Wang Ling looked at the rune he had drawn and remained silent for two seconds.

[Tank Engine: Gain 1 stack of effect upon defeating an enemy. Each stack increases your size by 5% and your maximum health by 5%. Upon death, lose 65% of the stack count.]

At the same time, Luo Xinghe drew the [Lightning Chain]: skills and basic attacks have a chance to trigger a chain of lightning, bouncing off nearby enemies.

Xia Zhinuan drew the [Glacier Amplification]: Control skills will create a slowing field.

Guo Hao drew [Aftershock]: After immobilizing the enemy, he gains a bonus to both physical and magical resistance and deals area damage.

Lee Chang-cheol drew the [Guardian]: Gain a shield when protecting teammates.

Zhao Ming drew the [Fountain of Life]: Mark enemies when they suffer movement damage, and teammates will recover health when attacking marked targets.

"A tank engine..." Chen Chao came over to look, his eyes lighting up: "Lingzi! This rune suits you! You are the tank!"

Wang Ling didn't say anything, but returned to the city and got his first piece of equipment.

Heart of Steel

The effect of this equipment is: when you hit a hero with a basic attack or skill, you will store a certain percentage of damage (half for ranged attacks). When the percentage is full, the next basic attack on a hero will consume all the stored damage, deal additional physical damage, and permanently increase your maximum health.

Combined with the [Tank Engine]'s kill stacking effect...

Wang Ling glanced at his Tamah.

It seems like we can do something interesting.

Back online.

Luo Xinghe had already adjusted his mindset and commanded, "Don't rush, take it slow. We have Lightning Chains and Glacial Augment, so we have strong attrition capabilities. Wait for Garen and Luo to find an opportunity to engage."

He looked at Tam of the King's Order.

Tamm was still so fat and clumsy.

But for some reason, Luo Xinghe felt a vague unease in his heart.

This Wang Ling always manages to do something unexpected.

He can no longer afford to be careless.

However, during the laning phase, Wang Ling's Tahm Kench played surprisingly timidly.

Stand behind the minion wave, use your Q skill to lick the minions, and occasionally lick the enemy hero, but never chase them too far.

If you get drained, use your E skill to heal.

If you get kicked, swallow your teammates for protection.

Like a conscientious nanny.

"He's scared." Luo Xinghe sneered inwardly. "That makes sense. Anyone would be scared if they were targeted like that from the very beginning."

He lowered his guard and began to focus on finishing off enemies and wearing them down.

But Xia Zhinuan felt something was wrong.

Based on her understanding of Wang Ling over this period of time, she discovered that this person was never afraid.

There must be another reason why you're behaving so timidly now.

The match lasts eight minutes.

The first large-scale team battle broke out.

Luo Xinghe seized the opportunity when Sun Rong's Jinx positioned herself forward, and Kai'Sa used her ultimate [Hunter's Instinct] to directly close the gap!

At the same time, Guo Hao's Garen flashed and used his Q to silence!

Zhao Ming's grand entrance was raised!

Jinx was instantly controlled to death, without even handing over her treatment.

"Kill AD! Kill AD!" Luo Xinghe shouted excitedly.

But just as Jinx fell, Tahm Kench, under the command of the King, moved.

Tahm Kench, having activated his ultimate ability [Abyssal Dive], charged to the center of the battlefield at an astonishing speed, and the moment he landed, he swallowed Kai'Sa, who was unleashing a barrage of attacks!

"Swallow me again?!" Luo Xinghe was going crazy.

But this time, Tam didn't turn back.

He stood in the middle of the crowd and vomited Kai'Sa in his own camp.

Kai'Sa was surrounded by Wang Ling and the rest of her teammates and vanished again.

Tahm Kench himself took all the skills from LeBlanc, Garen, Rakan, and Zilean, his health dropped to half, but he just wouldn't die!

Even more outrageous, at the very moment Kai'Sa died—

A faint light shone from Tam's body.

[Tank Engine] Triggered!

My body size has increased by 5%!

Tahm Kench, who was already overweight, now looks even fatter.

"What the hell?" Luo Xinghe looked at his black and white screen, then glanced at Tam, who was clearly much bigger, and the unease in his heart grew stronger.

But he was not yet aware of the seriousness of the problem.

The match lasts ten minutes.

Second wave of Hextech Rune draws.

Wang Ling drew the second rune:

【Scorching】: When a skill hits an enemy, it will deal additional magic damage and apply a continuous burning effect.

Luo Xinghe draws the [Malicious Injury] skill: deals additional true damage to enemies whose movement is impaired.

Xia Zhinuan drew the [Ultimate Hunter]: The cooldown time of the ultimate skill is greatly reduced.

...

"Anxious..." Wang Ling looked at the rune and then at his Q skill.

Tahm Kench's Q deals magic damage and can trigger Scorch.

Combined with Heartsteel's basic attack damage increase...

This seems interesting.

Twelve minutes.

In the center of the Howling Abyss map, the two sides clashed head-on around the new wave of Hextech Runefruit spawn points.

The fruit provides a short but powerful team attribute bonus, often serving as the trigger for team battles.

Luo Xinghe saw that Sun Rong's Jinx was positioned too far forward and wanted to get some fruit, so Kai'Sa used her ultimate [Hunter's Instinct] to rush in!

Then, at that moment, Wang Ling's Tahm Kench dashed to the scene and devoured Kai'Sa in one gulp!

"Are you fucking done yet?!" Luo Xinghe was going crazy, roaring internally.

But this time, Tam didn't turn back.

He stood in the middle of the crowd, waiting for his teammates to come and support him.

As expected, Kai'Sa has vanished again.

...

The match lasts fifteen minutes.

The third wave of Hextech Rune draws.

Wang Ling stared at the position where the roulette wheel pointer had stopped and remained silent for three seconds.

[Gigantify]: Your size increases by 25%, your health increases by 15%, but your movement speed decreases by 10%.

Luo Xinghe drew the [Reaper]: Deals additional damage to enemies with low health.

Xia Zhinuan drew the [Aura Cloak]: Gain a burst of movement speed after using a Summoner Spell.

Wang Ling clicked "confirm".

Then, everyone witnessed a scene they would never forget.

In the spring, Tahm Kench's size... began to swell wildly.

His body, which was already a size larger than that of an ordinary hero, suddenly grew bigger like an inflated balloon!

A 10% increase in tank engine performance, plus a 25% increase in size.

A total of 35% increase in body size!

Tahm Kench is no longer a hero.

He is a wall.

A moving, obese, and long-tongued wall of flesh.

His hero model took up almost half the screen width.

As I walked, I felt the ground of the entire Howling Abyss shaking.

"Holy...holy crap?!" Chen Chao stammered in his voice, "Reiko! How...how did you get so big?!"
