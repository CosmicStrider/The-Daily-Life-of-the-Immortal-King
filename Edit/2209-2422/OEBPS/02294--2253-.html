Chapter 2253 Glazed Shadow Dance

After the bonfire party on the platform in front of the underground cave ended, not everyone was able to fall asleep immediately.

Sun Rong sat cross-legged in her makeshift meditation area, her thoughts still lingering on the warm touch of Wang Ling's fingertips that had inadvertently brushed against her, and the strange vision that allowed her to glimpse the "countdown to destiny".

Suddenly, a very faint thought touched the edge of her consciousness.

She looked up and saw that Wang Ling had quietly appeared not far from her.

The soft light of the moonstone shone on the boy, whose usually expressionless face now held a pair of deep eyes that gazed at her with a serene calm that seemed to see through everything.

He slightly raised his hand, extended his index finger, and gently touched Sun Rong's brow from a distance.

The movements appear extremely casual, yet they carry an irresistible force.

In an instant, it was as if an invisible bridge had been erected.

A massive and somewhat ironic flood of memories rushed directly into Sun Rong's mind!

She "saw" it!

—At the bottom of the Dark Crystal Abyss, a dark and deep place in the Earth's core, covered with strange black crystals, a group of indistinct creatures with a texture like flowing mercury are writhing and deforming.

Like Mystique from the movies, they accurately simulated Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe's faces, figures, and even subtle facial features!

Following that, Su Xingyue, several classmates from the St. Science Academy, and even her own appearance were all "copied" one by one!

What shocked her even more was the cold dialogue between "herself" and "Zhang Linyan" in the video.

"Sufficient information has been collected..."

"Primary objective: Capture Sun Rong or strip her of her power source... Avoid that boy..."

"Replacement..."

As the memory sharing ended, Sun Rong gasped sharply, her body swaying almost imperceptibly.

"The Glass Clan..."

Sun Rong murmured softly, her pupils slightly constricted in shock.

She was well-read, but she had never heard of such a strange creature in the inner Earth, with such a powerful ability to mimic!

And their goal is to silently replace them and then return to the surface.

Shocked, a scorching heat uncontrollably climbed up her cheeks and ears.

Wang Ling's fingers did not actually touch her skin, but the intimacy of the connection of consciousness, and the trust she felt in being chosen by him to share such private and crucial information, made the girl's heart beat erratically for a moment.

The lingering sensation on her fingertips, as if still burning between her brows, subtly overlapped with the feeling of exchanging meat skewers by the campfire earlier, filling her with countless thoughts in this tranquil night deep within the earth.

She subconsciously looked up at Wang Ling, meeting his eyes, which were as calm and still as a clear pool.

Take a deep breath! Inhale again!

Sun Rong forcibly suppressed the turmoil in her heart and that damn blushing, pounding heart—now was not the time to let her mind wander!

A crisis is imminent!

The goal of the Glass Clan is all of them!

A resolute glint flashed in the girl's eyes. The composure and wisdom befitting an elite leader of Number 60 High School quickly suppressed her shyness and regained control.

A powerful will instantly connected with the Aohai Sword Core deep within his dantian.

A spark of wisdom ignites in calmness.

At the heart of it all is her most powerful partner—Ouhai!

She couldn't directly reveal the existence of the "Liuli Clan," as that would cause panic and might alert potential spies. She had to deploy defenses with a reasonable and universally acceptable justification, while concealing her true killing move within it.

Sun Rong stood up and walked to the center of the platform. Her voice, though not loud, clearly reached the ears of the group who were not yet fully rested: "Everyone, please wait a moment. While meditating just now, Ao Hai seemed to have detected some unusual psionic fluctuations."

She deliberately made her voice sound serious, but not overly tense.

"Unusual fluctuations?"

Zhang Linyan immediately became alert. As an experienced elite, she was very sensitive to any abnormalities in the environment.

Li Changzhe also dropped his joking expression and looked at Sun Rong.

"Hmm," Sun Rong nodded, her gaze sweeping over everyone. "Ao Hai is very sensitive to energy, especially to... disguise and mimicry-type fluctuations. I suspect that there may be some kind of creature deep within the earth that can mimic our form. Although it's just a guess, I want to make some preparations in advance, just to be on the safe side."

She raised a reasonable concern based on facts, but did not reveal specific information about the enemy.

Liu Qingyi raised an eyebrow: "Imitate forms? Tch, this godforsaken place really has all sorts of weirdos. What do you want to do, Rongrong?"

Ryoko Kumiya immediately expressed her support: "Do you need my array?"

Seeing that everyone was attracted by her reasons and was willing to cooperate, Sun Rong felt a little more at ease.

She immediately spoke in a low voice, her words fast but clear: "Thank you all for your trust. We have the advantage of being able to sense the Olympus first, which is crucial."

"If the enemy exists, the greatest threat is a surprise infiltration. We cannot passively wait or search the cave, as this could easily cause confusion and friendly fire." She succinctly pointed out the core of the potential threat.

Then he began to explain the core framework of the plan: "Therefore, we need two layers of protection, inside and out, and to proactively create opportunities to identify them."

"For the first level, we need Jiugong's help to immediately set up a strong isolation and warning barrier around the cave. The focus is not on defense and attack, but on sensing entry and exit and abnormal spiritual energy." She looked at Jiugong Liangzi, her eyes conveying a deeper meaning.

Ryoko Kugimiya understood immediately. Although she didn't understand why Sun Rong was so certain, based on her trust in Ouhai and Sun Rong, she nodded immediately: "Understood. Set up trigger alarms, focus on monitoring entrances and exits and locking down energy anomalies. Leave it to me!"

She quickly took out the array and began to operate it.

"The second level."

Sun Rong looked at everyone, especially the core combatants Zhang Linyan, Li Changzhe, Liu Qingyi, Wang Zhen, and Fang Xing: "We need to rely on everyone's strength. I plan to use the Ao Hai to leave a very subtle sword aura mark on each person. This mark itself is harmless; it's just a mark of the Ao Hai, like... an identity tag that only the Ao Hai can recognize. In this way, no matter what happens, the Ao Hai can immediately distinguish who is the real person and who is the possible imitator."

"Great idea!"
