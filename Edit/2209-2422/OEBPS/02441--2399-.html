Chapter 2399 Tacit Understanding Challenge

The air was still for a few seconds.

Wang Ling looked at Sun Rong's slightly red ear tips, and then at the bright "Double Teamwork Challenge" event announcement on the bracelet.

The temptation of five seals is indeed very strong.

Adding the existing ten, that makes fifteen.

After completing a few more projects, you'll have a gift pack of instant noodles for 100 people.

Efficiency is paramount.

"Um."

In the end, Wang Ling nodded.

Chen Chao immediately cheered, "Great! Your son is awesome! Let's go, the registration area is over there!"

The registration point for the two-person teamwork challenge was set up in the central square of the park. A small stage was temporarily set up, and the rules of the event were written on the backdrop with glowing runes.

The event host was a cartoon fox dressed as a park staff member, enthusiastically promoting the event while holding a megaphone.

"If the two of you can complete all the challenges within three minutes, you'll get five stamps at once! This is a rare opportunity!"

Many couples and groups of friends had already gathered around the stage, eager to try their luck.

Wang Ling and the other two squeezed to the registration desk.

The staff member, a girl with pigtails, lit up when she saw Wang Ling, but quickly regained her professional smile: "Would you two like to sign up for the teamwork challenge?"

"Yes!" Chen Chao answered quickly, "They both signed up!"

Sun Rong added softly, "...Is it okay for three people?"

"I'm sorry, this challenge requires two people to team up." The staff member smiled apologetically, "However, this student can watch and cheer from the sidelines."

Chen Chao waved his hand dismissively: "No problem! I'll be a cheerleader!"

Registration is very simple; just scan the wristband to register.

After registration, the staff handed Wang Ling and Sun Rong each a specially made wristband: "These are compatibility test wristbands, which will monitor your heart rate, emotional fluctuations, and body coordination in real time. Please be sure to wear them during the challenge."

Wang Ling took the wristband and put it on.

The wristband is made of silicone and has tiny psionic runes on the inside that automatically adhere to the skin upon contact.

Sun Rong finished putting on her gear, looked up at Wang Ling, her eyes showing a little nervousness, but even more so, eagerness to try.

"Please wait in the preparation area, the next group will be you." The staff pointed to the side of the stage.

There were already four couples waiting in the waiting area; they were all young people, some were lovers and some were friends.

Chen Chao followed Wang Ling and Sun Rong, whispering his advice: "Lingzi, when you're doing the challenge later, remember to interact with Sun Rong a lot! Make eye contact! Make physical contact! This wristband is testing your teamwork; the more synchronized you are, the higher your score will be!"

Wang Ling grunted in agreement, took out two pieces of instant noodles from his pocket, ate one himself, and handed the other to Sun Rong.

Sun Rong took it and ate it in small bites, which relieved her tension considerably.

At this moment, the pair that was challenging on stage failed.

The challenge is "synchronous mirroring".

Two people stand facing each other, separated by a one-way transparent glass. One person makes a movement, and the other person has to imitate it by looking at the reflection in the glass. The movements must be completely synchronized.

The two boys on stage were in a panic, and the girl stomped her foot in frustration: "I've had my hand raised for three seconds! How come you still haven't reacted!"

"I...I can't see clearly..." the boy said, sounding aggrieved.

The countdown ended, and the system determined that the teamwork level was only 42%, which did not meet the target.

"Challenge failed!" the cartoon fox host announced regretfully. "Next group, please prepare!"

It was Wang Ling and Sun Rong's turn.

Chen Chao clenched his fist from below the stage: "Lingzi, Sun Rong, go for it!"

The two walked onto the stage.

The spotlight shone down, and the tourists watching from below the stage all looked over.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong's appearances are simply too outstanding.

One is aloof and handsome, the other sweet and charming; standing together, they look like figures from a magazine.

"Wow, this couple is so good-looking..."

"Is she an internet celebrity?"

"Why is the boy holding instant noodles in his hand...?"

Whispers broke out among the audience.

Wang Ling completely ignored it, focused on finishing the last bit of instant noodles, folded the packaging bag and stuffed it back into his pocket.

Sun Rong took a deep breath, looked at Wang Ling, and her eyes were firm.

"Are you two ready?" The cartoon fox host hopped over: "First challenge: Understanding Each Other!"

A ring of runes lit up the stage floor, enveloping the two of them. Inside, there was a device to block sound transmission in order to prevent cheating, but it was useless to Wang Ling.

The rules are simple: the system will randomly display a word, one person describes it with gestures, and the other person guesses. There is a one-minute time limit; guessing five words correctly counts as passing. If you feel that you cannot describe a word, you can skip it.

"Who describes and who guesses?" the host asked.

Sun Rong looked at Wang Ling: "Wang Ling, guess what I'm going to say?"

Wang Ling nodded: "Okay."

His ability to guess words is ridiculously strong, but having Sun Rong describe them can increase interaction.

The challenge begins.

The first word that appears is very simple, just a trial run: 【Instant Noodles】

Sun Rong paused for a moment, then smiled: "Wang Ling, your favorite snack."

Wang Ling: "Instant noodles."

"Correct!" the system notification sounded.

Sun Rong explained and gestured throughout, and the difficulty of the words gradually increased.

The third term is "Yanran Angel Hospital," which is a popular vocabulary term.

Then, for the fourth and fifth words, which were too abstract, Sun Rong chose to skip them.

However, even Sun Rong herself did not expect that the following words were very abstract, but fortunately Wang Ling guessed them accurately.

By the ninth word, Sun Rong was stunned, and even the audience below the stage were at a loss. The difficulty was hellish!

Wang Ling watched Sun Rong gesture each word carefully, and finally blurted out: "Hakimi, North and South Green Beans".

"correct!"

Chen Chao and the other audience members were stunned.

How the hell did they figure that out?!

Next, there's only one word left. If you guess it correctly, you'll pass.

This time, the lyrics are a bit special: 【Heartbeat】

Sun Rong looked at the word, opened her mouth, and her ears instantly turned red.

How can I describe this...?

Time passed second by second.

Fifteen seconds remain.

Sun Rong bit her lip, looked at Wang Ling, and said in a voice as soft as a feather, "It's just... how I feel right now."

Wang Ling looked at her.

The spotlight above the stage reflected a shimmering light in Sun Rong's eyes, her cheeks flushed a faint pink, and her eyelashes trembled slightly.

The two stared at each other for three seconds.

Wang Ling spoke up: "My heart is fluttering."

"Correct!!!" The system voice rang out loudly: "Teamwork Rating: S!"

Applause erupted from the audience.

Chen Chao was so excited he almost jumped up: "Awesome!"

Sun Rong breathed a sigh of relief, stole a glance at Wang Ling, and found that Wang Ling was also looking at her, so she quickly looked away.

My heart is beating so fast.

The heart rate monitor on the wristband is surging.

"Congratulations to both of you for passing the first challenge!" the cartoon fox host hopped around. "Next up is the second challenge: Synchronous Collaboration!"

Two control panels rose from the stage floor, each with a pile of scattered rune blocks on it.

Rules: Each player assembles the building blocks individually, but the resulting pattern must be exactly the same, and players cannot communicate. Time limit: two minutes.

"This is too difficult!" someone in the audience exclaimed. "How can we know what the other person is spelling if we can't speak?"

Wang Ling and Sun Rong exchanged a glance and walked to the control panel.

The countdown has begun.

Sun Rong looked at the colorful rune blocks on the table, feeling a little troubled.

What are we competing for?

She secretly glanced at Wang Ling's direction.

Wang Ling has already taken action.

He picked up the building blocks casually, but very quickly, almost without thinking, piecing them together one after another.

Sun Rong quickly observed his gestures and the color and shape of the building blocks he chose.
