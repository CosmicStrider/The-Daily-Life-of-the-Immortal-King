Chapter 2332 The Victory of the Laying-Down Winners!

Inside the purple barrier, Principal Lu Qiu's pig-like screams continued for an unknown period of time.

It was clear that Lin Hai had vented all his anger on him.

Wang Ling was not particularly disturbed, because the NPCs that appeared behind the Abyss Gate were not real people at all, and even the scenes were fictional.

These characters and scenes were all constructed by that group of primordial evil cultivators using illusory magic, but these shadow practitioners generally had a low level of cultivation and could not perceive this at all. Instead, they felt an unprecedented sense of realism.

Outside the barrier, all was deathly silent.

Zhang Yu, along with the members of his B-07 family and the black-robed members of the A-13 family on Wang Ling's side, all froze in place, not daring to breathe.

They looked at Zhou Ziwei, who had become "ordinary" again, with disbelief and a deep sense of awe in their eyes.

That... solves the problem?

They are all people who have survived the trials of many trials.

Knowing just how difficult the quests are after each gate, and moreover... the difficulty of the dungeon quests after the Abyss Gate increases with each entry.

The danger level of the Sacred Bell High School instance is self-evident.

This is undoubtedly a death trap, with a four-day survival period and various rules and restrictions. Generally, a large number of casualties will occur on the second day after entering.

As a result, this "Zhou Ziwei" used an unreasonable method to directly expose the mastermind behind the scenes and hand him over to the victim, thus completing a precise demolition.

Upon realizing this, everyone couldn't help but gasp.

On the second day of entering the dungeon, they directly pulled out the BOSS and wiped him out, is that even possible...?

Zhang Yu, in particular, felt a burning pain on his face.

He had previously suspected Zhou Ziwei of plotting behind his back and even considered teaming up with others to take him down first.

Looking back now, I realize how incredibly stupid I was... This was undoubtedly a lifeline! Clinging to him meant survival!

A few minutes later, the commotion within the barrier gradually subsided.

The purplish-black barrier receded like the tide, revealing the scene inside.

Principal Lu Qiu's obese body slumped to the ground, his eyes wide open and his pupils dilated.

He had no wounds, but his life force had completely vanished, leaving only an expression of dying with his eyes wide open in intense fear.

The tall figure, shrouded in a swirling aura of bloodlust, was now rapidly losing its resentment. Its crimson eyes regained their clarity, and it transformed back into a resolute-looking middle-aged man.

Beside him, Lin Wanwan's spirit had solidified considerably, her face still bearing tear stains, yet also conveying a sense of complete liberation.

Lin Hai's gaze swept over everyone present, finally landing on Wang Ling.

He took his daughter's hand and bowed deeply to Wang Ling.

"Thank you... Your Excellency."

Lin Hai sincerely thanked him: "If it weren't for Your Excellency's enlightenment, which allowed me to take revenge myself, my daughter and I would probably be condemned to eternal damnation, never to be reborn."

Lin Wanwan also bowed: "Thank you, teaching assistant teacher... thank you everyone."

The surviving shadows had mixed feelings as they watched the father and daughter finally free.

Wang Ling knew that although the people and scenes behind the door were fictional, the events themselves might have actually happened somewhere in the real world.

Those primordial evil cultivators collected the negative emotions of countless cultivators, and naturally derived many tragic stories from these negative emotions.

The inspiration for the stories behind each abyss gate likely comes from this.

"It was no trouble at all," Wang Ling replied calmly, his tone flat, as he leaned against Zhou Ziwei's body.

Lin Hai straightened up, looked at his daughter with affection, and then said to Wang Ling, "Now that the resentment has dissipated and the rules have collapsed, fellow Daoists may leave. I cannot express my gratitude, and I wish you all a safe journey and prosperous immortality."

After he finished speaking, he took Lin Wanwan's hand, and the figures of the father and daughter began to emit a soft white light, which drifted away like stars and eventually disappeared into the air.

And just as they disappeared.

"Ding-a-ling!"

A melodious and clear school bell, no longer carrying any eerie atmosphere, echoed throughout St. Bell High School.

The lead gray in the sky quickly faded, revealing the long-awaited normal daylight.

The once desolate campus seemed to have been infused with life. Although it remained dilapidated, the suffocating sense of oppression had vanished.

The space began to ripple slightly, and a familiar sense of transmission washed over me.

[Mission World: St. Bell High School.]

[Task Status: Completed.]

[Number of survivors: 8/8]

[Mission Evaluation: SSS+]

[Family Points: +15 (Basic Completion) + 30 (SSS+ Evaluation Bonus) + 10 (Hidden Story "Father-Daughter Redemption" Triggered)]

[Rewards Settlement: Points have been credited to the family account. Special Reward: Blessing of Purification (grants each player one chance to resist a fatal attack the next time they enter the Abyss Gate dungeon) has been issued.]

The information that refreshed on the bracelet once again left everyone speechless.

SSS+ rating! Up to 55 points! There are even special rewards!

This is legendary in the history of missions at the House of Stalkers!

And they not only suffered zero casualties, but they also won the whole game without any effort!

A flash of light, and everyone was teleported out of the Holy Bell High School instance and back to the Shadowman's House Square where a blood moon hung.

When their feet touched the cold, black ground, Zhang Yu and the others felt as if they had been transported to another world.

Zhang Yu took a deep breath, suppressing the shock in his heart, and quickly walked up to Wang Ling. The arrogance and scheming on his face had long since disappeared, replaced by an extremely solemn expression.

"Brother Zhou!"

He clasped his hands in a respectful bow, his tone filled with unprecedented sincerity: "I, Zhang Yu, was blind before and offended you greatly! If it weren't for Brother Zhou's intervention today, we would all probably be dead. After this incident, I, Zhang Yu, am completely convinced!"

Wang Ling simply nodded slightly in response.

He's not very good at handling these kinds of situations.

Zhang Yu, however, did not take it to heart.

It's normal for a master to have a temper.

He stepped closer and lowered his voice, saying, "Brother Zhou, if there are similar missions in the future, I hope to have the opportunity to cooperate with you. I, Zhang Yu, have witnessed your abilities. Following you is a sure thing!"

He paused, seemingly making up his mind, and then recited an address and the contact number of a small communication device: "This is my contact information and usual address in the real world. If you need me to run errands or inquire about any information, please feel free to ask. I'm an intern in the Comprehensive Management Department of the Hundred Schools Administration. Although my position isn't high, I still have access to some internal information."

Interns at the General Administration of Education, Hundred Schools?

Wang Ling's heart stirred.

As expected, the infiltration of the Shadowers has reached the core management level of the cultivation education system.

Even places like the National Centre for the Promotion of Education have been infiltrated, and the level of threat and scope of coverage far exceed previous estimates.
