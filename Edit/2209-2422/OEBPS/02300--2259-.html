Chapter 2259 Bai Zhe's Henchmen

The earth shook violently!

The entire cave seemed to be gripped by an invisible giant hand, shaking wildly and groaning under its unbearable weight.

The crystal chandeliers in the dome, mimicking sunlight, flickered on and off, casting chaotic and intertwined light and shadow that turned the panicked faces of the crowd deathly pale.

"Stabilize! Inject spiritual energy into the barrier base!" Sun Rong's clear shout stood out amidst the chaos.

She formed a hand seal, and the azure azure sword energy flowed like a surging river, continuously pouring into the defensive array set up by Kyuunomiya Ryoko.

The light curtain flickered violently, struggling to support the main structure of the cave, but the violent energy originating from the deepest part of the earth was like billions of tons of lava roaring and surging underfoot, bringing not only physical vibrations, but also a kind of terror that struck the depths of the soul!

"Damn it... what the hell is this?!" Chen Chao clung tightly to a sturdy crystal pillar, his leg bone, which had just healed, seemed to ache again.

Guo Hao tried to draw stable runes, but his trembling fingers made the rune lines twist and turn: "The spiritual pressure... is too chaotic! I can't concentrate at all!"

Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe stood back to back, their longbows and magical artifacts gleaming, yet they didn't know which enemy to point them at.

Su Xingyue's face was pale. She stuck her "Dawn" sword into the ground and barely managed to stand up by using the sword for support.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing stood guard on either side of the cave entrance, their expressions more solemn than ever before.

Liu Qingyi's body surged with purple light, and the power of her divine domain transformed into a tangible halo to protect the people around her, but her gaze anxiously turned towards the core of the cave.

That will, filled with absolute malice, like an ancient behemoth awakening, is surging wildly along the ancient passage that was accidentally opened! Its target is clear—all living beings, especially Sun Rong, whose spiritual power is the most dazzling!

Moreover, this power was quite familiar to several hidden experts in the Number 60 High School.

Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, and Liu Qingyi could almost tell that this power originated from... some kind of external divine power!

Even before entering the inner world, intelligence gathered by the War Sect indicated that Bai Zhe, the Moon Dragon form who had become the leader of the Dragonborn, already had intricate connections with the Outer Gods. Furthermore, this condensed evil power would reach its peak before the official arrival of the War of Destiny!

If it weren't for this exploration of the Earth's core, probably no one would have imagined that the minions of the Outer Gods had already invaded the Earth's core.

Boom! At this moment, the dark and viscous malevolent energy surged towards Sun Rong from behind like a tidal wave, about to engulf her!

At this critical moment—

Wang Ling, who had been sitting still, finally moved.

He didn't unleash any earth-shattering momentum, nor was he protected by any dazzling divine light.

He simply put down the half-eaten "Hellfire" instant noodles.

The bag landed lightly on the recliner, making almost no sound.

Then, he raised his eyes.

Those lifeless fish eyes, unchanged for millennia, were no longer still as calm as an ancient well, but reflected a deep and boundless starry sky, as if billions of stars were born and died within them.

Deep within the pupil, the dormant seven-colored light suddenly shone, no longer a faint flicker, but transformed into a cold vertical line piercing through the pupil!

Time stretched infinitely in his field of vision.

The black, malevolent tide that rushed toward Sun Rong turned into a slowly wriggling, viscous substance in his eyes.

The terrified expressions of his companions, the bursts of spiritual power, and their defensive actions all seemed to be slowed down a thousand times, like a frozen frame.

At this moment, Wang Ling's thoughts transcended the constraints of matter and surged forward at a speed exceeding a billion times the speed of light, following the path of the surging malice!

It's not a collision of energies, nor a clash of laws.

Instead, it's more direct and domineering—the arrival of consciousness!

...

Sun Rong was doing her best to maintain the barrier, and the core of the Aohai Sword was spinning at an unprecedented speed, almost bursting into flames.

She could clearly feel the cold, malicious touch, as if countless slippery tentacles had already touched her back, the chill of death freezing her soul.

She could even smell a stench that was a mixture of decay and absolute emptiness.

Is it... over?

The thought had just arisen.

Suddenly, all the pressure, all the malice, all the cold touch...

It vanished in an instant.

It wasn't that it was blocked, but rather that it was as if a much larger, more invisible hand was gently wiping it away from a higher dimension.

The aura of light surrounding her expanded outwards suddenly because it had lost its target, then quickly receded.

Sun Rong stumbled and almost fell over due to the excessive force.

Just then, a slightly cool hand naturally supported her arm, its strength steady and just right to help her maintain her balance.

Sun Rong suddenly turned around.

It is the king's decree.

He had somehow appeared behind her, his expression still blank, his gaze not even fully focused on her, but rather casually sweeping over the empty space behind her, as if he had simply helped a classmate who was about to fall.

His fingers touched and then parted, so quickly that Sun Rong almost thought it was an illusion. Only the slight coolness lingering on her arm proved that it was not a dream.

"Wang Ling? You..." Sun Rong's heart was still pounding, her breathing was rapid, and she looked at him so close to her, not knowing what to say for a moment.

Is this a thank you?

Or should we inquire about that strange disappearance just now?

Wang Ling did not respond to her gaze, nor did he offer any explanation.

He even slightly turned his body to the side, using his not-so-broad, but unusually reliable back to subtly block Sun Rong from direct eye contact with the dark passage.

Then, as if nothing had happened, he turned his gaze back to the violent tremors and energy turbulence outside, leaving Sun Rong with only a silent and quiet profile.

It was as if the averting of that earth-shattering crisis and this subtle protective gesture were merely incidental and insignificant.

Sun Rong watched his retreating figure, but swallowed the words that were on the tip of her tongue.

A complex mix of emotions welled up in my chest: the lingering fear of surviving a close call, the peace of mind of being protected by him, and a hint of elusive loss.

He always did this, resolving everything silently, yet it was as if there was an invisible wall between them.

The cave was still trembling violently, but the suffocating malice had truly disappeared.

On Ryoko Kugimiya's array plate, the alarm representing an extremely powerful external malevolent energy source miraculously went out, leaving only the basic reading of the earth's ley line vibrations.

"The...the source of the evil signal...has disappeared?!"

Yoshiko Kunomiya exclaimed in disbelief, her voice trembling, "Just now, suddenly... gone?!"

Everyone was taken aback upon hearing this.

"Disappeared?" Li Changzhe was stunned. "Shattered by the shockwave?"

"Impossible!" Zhang Linyan categorically denied. "That level of will cannot be dispersed by ordinary ground energy!"

She just felt herself about to suffocate!

And it only takes a moment!

It was as if I were nothing more than an insignificant ant in the face of such terrifying will.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao looked at each other, seeing the same bewilderment in each other's eyes.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing exchanged a glance, both seeing deep shock and a hint of understanding in each other's eyes.

Their perception far surpassed that of others. Just a moment ago, they clearly captured a fleeting will that transcended everything, "devouring" the malice in a way they could not comprehend at all.

The skull sickle in Fang Xing's hand even emitted a faint whimper, a manifestation of the weapon spirit's instinctive awe in the face of an absolutely superior being.

Wang Zhen stroked his chin, looked at Wang Ling's excessively calm back, and a faint smile appeared on his lips.

Clearly... only Wang Ling could have cleaned it so thoroughly.

The will of the Outer Gods...

That is an existence powerful enough to annihilate all cultivators below the Dao Ancestor level on Earth!
