Chapter 2231 The Test Has Begun

In the core hall of the Palm Cliff Earth Core Project Experimental Base, dozens of figures dressed in white are observing the "Welcome Project Preliminary Test Project" that will soon take place outside the base in front of a huge crystal screen.

Four people sat in the chair in the very center: Jing Heqiu, the dean of the Nine Heavens Refined Academy; Teng Luchen, the manager of the Nine Heavens Refined Academy and the chief commander of the Earth's Core Project; Zhuo Yi; and Wang Ming.

They watched helplessly as Teng Luchen gave the operator in front of them the order to fire at the immortal ship, and he was using Wang Mingxin's newly developed "small Eastern Armor Cannon".

Zhuo Yi and Wang Ming exchanged a glance, and a look of disbelief flashed across their faces.

Wang Ming couldn't help but break out in a cold sweat. This small Eastern Dunjia cannon incorporated the hexagram runes of "Qimen Dunjia". Even though it was a small cannonball, its caliber was ridiculously large. Because it belonged to the level of a strategic weapon, its kill radius could easily destroy a small town. Cultivators below the Nascent Soul stage would have no chance to resist. Once hit and exploded, they would at least become disabled.

"Elder Teng, isn't this a bit too much?" Dean Jing Heqiu couldn't help but twitch his lips. He knew about the plan, but he didn't expect that the shells used for the artillery bombardment were strategic shells made by Wang Ming.

Using these shells to bombard these elite high school students is like using a sledgehammer to crack a nut; it seems a bit too much.

As for Zhuo Yi and Wang Ming, the most shocking thing for them was that they had no idea that Teng Luchen had made such an arrangement.

"Yes, Elder Teng, I had no idea you had such a plan," Zhuo Yi asked, feigning composure.

"This is a secret plan, and it has been approved by Lord Yuanzun. Only Lord Yuanzun and I know about it." Teng Luchen tugged at his shrewd little mustache and said with ease and self-satisfaction, "Don't worry, although this is a small shell, its power has been concentrated. It only has 30% of the original version's power."

"The inner world is extremely dangerous. Before the project began, I knew that all the core members involved in this project had undergone special training. This is the first step in testing the results of their training."

"What are the evaluation criteria?" Wang Ming frowned, his eyes fixed on the screen in front of him, his words still tinged with annoyance: "And Elder Teng, although you are the commander-in-chief, as the inventor of the Dongfeng Dunjia Cannon, I seem to have an obligation to assess the safety risks of the test."

Upon hearing this, Teng Luchen scratched his head sheepishly and gave Wang Ming a cute look: "Sorry, young friend Wang Ming. I should have informed you, but I was too busy and forgot."

"......"

One sentence left Zhuo Yi and Wang Ming speechless.

They knew perfectly well that Old Deng's suspicions about Wang Ling had never been completely dispelled; this was clearly a test.

Before the two could ask any further questions, Teng Luchen didn't give them a chance to respond and said to himself, "As for the evaluation criteria, you'll find out later. I'm not just targeting Number 60 High School. All the high schools participating in this project will be subject to this evaluation."

"As for how to block the cannonballs, any method currently available in the cultivation world can be used. Even having the accompanying teacher protect the calf is no problem. It will just result in a deduction of points."

To be honest, Teng Luchen didn't take the high school teachers who accompanied him seriously at all. After all, they were all in high school, and even if these high school teachers were excellent, they were only at the Golden Core stage.

Of course, for these elite universities, the accompanying teachers can be up to the vice-principal level to escort the students. However, the rules of Project Center of the Earth were clearly stated when the call for participation was issued: the accompanying teachers are only responsible for taking care of the students' daily lives and are not allowed to intervene unless absolutely necessary.

"......"

What a cunning and shrewd thief! He managed to leave no trace of his scheme.

Upon hearing this, Zhuo Yi and Wang Ming thought to themselves in unison.

...

On the celestial ship, everyone's expressions varied as they faced the sudden artillery barrage from the base.

Aside from the monk Jin Deng, who is disguised as the vice principal of Number 60 High School, the other core members of the plan on the Immortal Ship are Sun Rong, Jiu Gong Liangzi, Chen Chao, Guo Hao, Fang Xing, Wang Zhen, Liu Qingyi, and Wang Ling.

The cannonball fired straight at the Immortal Ship was less a test of Wang Ling and more a test of Chen Chao and Guo Hao's cultivation progress on Mount Rewind.

After all, apart from these two, everyone else here has the ability to easily neutralize artillery shells.

Wang Ling and Jin Deng need no introduction. Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi are both scions of powerful families in the Divine Realm, and their cultivation levels are already far higher than those of Earth cultivators. Fang Xing is now the hidden Grand Elder of the Battle Sect. As for Sun Rong, with Wang Ling's repeated assistance, she has become incredibly cunning. Not to mention that her cultivation level has been raised to the peak of the late Golden Core stage during this special training at the Mountain of Regression, even before that, the eight-core Ao Hai sword in her hand was enough to help Sun Rong resolve 99% of her troubles.

As for Ryoko Kugimiya, having already thoroughly understood the script, she was even more fearless. Although her level was not as high as the others, only about the same as Chen Chao and Guo Hao, she knew the whole story because of Zhuo Yi and Sun Rong, so she was not afraid at all.

Therefore, for Number 60 High School, the key point now is not how to defend, but how to play along with Chen Chao and Guo Hao, those two simpletons.

Although I am calm inside, I cannot appear too calm on the surface.

Just as Sun Rong was pondering how to react, Liu Qingyi suddenly exclaimed and quickly snuggled into Wang Zhen's arms: "Oh my god, that cannonball was terrifying! Does anyone have any ideas?"

Seeing this, everyone except Chen Chao and Guo Hao almost choked on their own saliva.

"Don't worry, everyone! Leave this shell to me and Brother Guo!" Chen Chao immediately took off his coat and stylishly tossed it aside.

Liu Qingyi's cry for help acted as a catalyst for Chen Chao, immediately stirring up his righteous and passionate heart.

Under his lead, Guo Hao quickly opened the cabin door of the fairy boat, and the two of them were ready to go: "Chao'er! Let's go together!"

Now that he and Guo Hao have both become stronger, it's time to test the results.

The moment Chen Chao and Guo Hao opened the cabin door, they leaped out in the direction of the artillery barrage, using magic to freeze themselves in mid-air.

Guo Hao instantly formed hand seals, without using any array flags or crystals. He relied solely on his spiritual power to coordinate the hand seals and the runes drawn on his fingertips to quickly weave a spider web-like defensive barrier in front of the immortal ship.

"Well done, Guo Hao! My turn!" Facing the cannonball, Chen Chao's muscles bulged instantly, tearing his shirt apart. A phantom image of a dragon's head and an elephant's body appeared behind him, its meridians connected to Chen Chao, pulsating along the muscles that had instantly bulged to the point of being grotesque, causing Chen Chao's body to swell to four or five times its original size.

Without dodging or avoiding, he rushed forward and grabbed the small Eastern-style cannon. His chest collided with the cannon head, producing a metallic clang.

This scene caused everyone on the immortal ship to raise their eyebrows, and even Wang Ling secretly marveled at the rapid progress of Chen Chao and Guo Hao.

I definitely found the right teacher; he is truly extraordinary.

The improvement in their cultivation level is secondary; what is most valuable is the practical experience they gained during their special training at Mount Rewind, following the cultivation of their two masters.

Furthermore, Chen Chao and Guo Hao already had a tacit understanding like comrades-in-arms. They had participated in many competitions related to Number 60 High School together. This tacit understanding was far beyond what other colleges and universities, especially elite high school students who liked to be unconventional, could match.

Therefore, even though Guo Hao was capable of withstanding artillery fire with his own body, the two men, upon leaping out of the cabin, quickly understood their roles and swiftly implemented them with just a glance.

"A Golden Core cultivator actually possesses a Dharma Aspect?" In the central hall of the base, Jing Heqiu stared at the screen in astonishment and stood up. He then corrected himself, "No, it should be something like a Martial Aspect."

His gaze darkened. This was a being that could only be born when one's physical strength was developed to its extreme!

Even Teng Luchen, with his shrewd and calculating eyes, showed a slight surprise.

He had seen Chen Chao and Guo Hao's information a long time ago, but he never expected that the two of them would improve so quickly after the special training.

But the person he really wanted to target was Wang Ling...

Seeing his shells blocked, he felt a surge of admiration for Guo Hao and Chen Chao, and immediately gave a new order to the operator in front: "Someone, fire three more!"

These two are very strong now, but Teng Luchen doesn't believe that Wang Ling can keep his true colors hidden even when he's under pressure.
