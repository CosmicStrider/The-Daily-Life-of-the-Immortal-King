Chapter 2398 The Unlucky Killer and the Heart-Pounding Drop Tower

The queue for the drop tower was shorter than that for the roller coaster, but the atmosphere was noticeably more somber.

A towering steel pillar, eighty meters high, stands before you, while the seats at the top appear to be only the size of a matchbox.

Every few minutes, a heart-wrenching scream would fall from the sky, accompanied by the whooshing sound of the seats plummeting.

Chen Chao looked up, his throat dry: "Lingzi... how about we go ride the carousel first?"

"Since we're already here," Wang Ling said calmly.

Sun Rong also stared at the drop tower: "Actually... I'd like to try it."

"Sun Rong, since you've said so, we have to go!" Chen Chao immediately changed his mind, patting his chest: "What storms haven't I, Chen Chao, weathered? It's just a drop tower!"

Wang Ling glanced at him but didn't say anything.

Chen Chao's legs were shaking, and his voice was eight octaves higher than usual, clearly a bluff.

But it doesn't matter.

Wang Ling's attention had already turned towards the control room of the drop tower.

There, the assassin codenamed "Falcon" is making his final preparations.

The Fallen Eagle is the most powerful of the four, at the peak of the Golden Core stage, specializing in gravity manipulation techniques.

His plan was simple: at the moment the drop tower reached its lowest point and was about to bounce back, he would secretly apply extra gravitational acceleration to make the seat vibrate violently.

Of course, the order given to the fallen eagle was to "avoid killing it as much as possible," but taking a blood sample from a minor scratch was acceptable.

"This time it will definitely succeed." The Fallen Eagle stared at Wang Ling and the other two who were queuing up on the surveillance footage, his eyes cold.

He had already received the failure reports from his first two companions, things like stepping on banana peels, equipment malfunctions, getting sprayed with water... In his view, these were all excuses.

Those two guys must be incompetent and making excuses.

His fall was different.

With twelve years of experience, I have a 97% task completion rate and have never made a mistake.

Today will be no exception.

The Falcon checked the backup devices on the control panel.

Those are three gravity rune stones; crushing them can instantly alter the local gravity field.

He also prepared an emergency plan: if the runestone failed, he would directly use his own spiritual power to forcefully cast the spell.

Double insurance, ensuring complete protection.

"It's their turn." Falcon stared at the screen.

At the front of the group, staff members were fastening seat belts for Wang Ling and the other two.

The drop tower seats are arranged side by side, with three seats in each row.

Wang Ling sat in the middle, Sun Rong on the left, and Chen Chao on the right.

The safety lever slowly descended and locked.

"Attention everyone, please hold on tight to the handrails, the equipment is about to start." The staff member's voice came through the loudspeaker, carrying a professional yet relaxed tone: "Enjoy the feeling of flying!"

Chen Chao gripped the handrail tightly, his knuckles turning white.

The park's facilities are all custom-made for cultivators. Even though cultivators are used to flying on swords in the sky, they will still feel the thrill of this exclusive customization.

Sun Rong took a deep breath and glanced at Wang Ling.

Wang Lingzheng calmly looked ahead, still holding the unfinished bag of wasabi-flavored instant noodles in his hand.

It was a miracle that the staff allowed him to bring snacks up.

"Wang Ling, aren't you afraid?" Sun Rong asked softly.

Wang Ling thought for a moment and said, "It's alright."

He was actually thinking that, if one were to free-fall from a height of eighty meters, disregarding air resistance, it would take about four seconds to reach the ground, and the speed would be...

Before he could finish calculating, the seat jolted violently and began to rise.

"It's starting! It's starting!" Chen Chao's voice trembled.

The seat slowly ascends along the steel pillar, gradually opening up the field of vision.

The entire Disneyland unfolds beneath your feet, the roller coaster tracks resemble silver ribbons, the Ferris wheel slowly rotates, and the distant castle spires glitter in the sunlight.

"So beautiful..." Sun Rong looked at the scenery below and suddenly felt less scared.

Wang Ling nodded.

It is indeed quite beautiful.

However, what concerned him more was that the Falcon in the control room had already begun its operation.

...

Inside the control room.

The eagle stared at the surveillance footage, clutching a gravity rune stone in its hand.

The drop tower has already risen to a height of sixty meters and is still climbing.

Seventy meters.

Seventy-five meters.

Reach the summit!

The seat paused for three seconds at its highest point.

This is designed to give visitors time to experience both the fear and the beautiful scenery from above.

Right now!

The eagle's eyes sharpened, and it tightened its grip on the rune stone, preparing to crush it!

But at the very moment he exerted his strength—

"Click".

It wasn't the sound of runestones shattering.

The chair he was sitting on had a broken leg.

The eagle fell backward, chair and all, and the back of its head hit the edge of the control panel with a thud!

"Ugh!" His vision went black, and the rune stone in his hand flew out, tracing an arc in the air before landing precisely in the hot tea that his colleague had just brewed.

"Pfft..."

When the runestone came into contact with water, its spiritual energy became disordered, and a wisp of blue smoke rose up, rendering it useless.

"My tea!" a colleague exclaimed.

The eagle scrambled to its feet, clutching the back of its head, its mind buzzing.

What happened?!

He'd been sitting in that control room chair for three years without a single problem! How could his leg suddenly break?!

He looked at the chair on the ground.

The break was clean, as if it had been cut by something sharp, but there were no tool marks around it.

Strange and bizarre.

That's too bizarre.

But time waits for no one!

The drop tower paused for two seconds at its highest point, and it's about to plummet!

The eagle gritted its teeth and decided to use the backup plan—direct spellcasting!

He formed hand seals and circulated his spiritual energy, preparing to condense a gravity field beneath Wang Ling's row of seats.

However, only half of the spiritual energy had been mobilized...

Achoo!

He sneezed loudly without warning.

The hand seals were interrupted, the flow of spiritual energy became disordered, and the spell backfired!

"Pfft!" The fallen eagle spat a mouthful of blood onto the control panel.

"Team leader! What's wrong?" The colleague was terrified.

"N-nothing...it's nothing..." The eagle wiped the blood from the corner of its mouth, its eyes red.

He doesn't believe in superstitions!

Third attempt!

This time he learned his lesson and checked his surroundings first: the chairs were new, the hot tea was taken away, and the doors and windows were closed to prevent drafts from causing him to sneeze.

Absolutely foolproof!

The fallen eagle re-formed its hand seals, and spiritual energy slowly gathered.

The drop tower had paused for four seconds at its highest point, and was about to—

"Boom!"

A loud noise suddenly came from outside.

It wasn't the sound of a drop tower; it was the "Flame Theater" next door testing the explosion effects for tonight's performance.

The moment the loud bang came, all the windows in the control room shattered with a crash!

It's just like the coincidence in the movie "Final Destination," but a non-fatal version.

Suddenly, the screws on the window frame came loose all at once, and the entire window fell down.

A strong wind rushed into the control room, scattering all the documents everywhere.

The Fallen Eagle's spellcasting was interrupted once again.

He stared blankly at the A4 papers fluttering all over the room and the window lying on the lawn outside.

What on earth happened today?!

At that moment, the drop tower had already started to fall.

...

"Aaaaahh ...

Chen Chao's scream pierced the sky.

The seat was freefalling from a height of eighty meters, and the feeling of weightlessness made my heart jump into my throat.

Sun Rong couldn't help but cry out, gripping the handrail tightly.

Wang Ling remained calm.
