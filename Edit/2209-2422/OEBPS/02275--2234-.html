Chapter 2234 Pressure Suddenly Increases! The Old Fox's Barrage of Attacks

Three more shots? Is this for real?

A brief, deathly silence fell over the central hall of the base.

On the crystal screen, the phantom of the dragon head and elephant body that appeared on Chen Chao's bulging muscles, and Guo Hao's instantaneous and exquisite defensive runes, seemed to still be flashing before everyone's eyes.

The strength and tacit understanding they displayed far exceeded what ordinary high school students and monks should possess, causing even the researchers in white robes walking through the hall to hold their breath.

Dean Jing Heqiu adjusted his glasses, his pupils behind the lenses still showing shock: "To withstand a small Eastern Armor Cannon with one's bare body... even if it's only at 30% of its power, that's still the foundation of a strategic-grade weapon!"

How did Chen Chao... Guo Hao... improve so quickly? Is Number 60 High School really full of hidden talents?

Wang Ming looked at the screen and saw Chen Chao completely unharmed (not counting the ripped clothes), only slightly out of breath. He secretly breathed a sigh of relief, but then his brows furrowed again.

That old fellow Teng Luchen is clearly determined to achieve his goal!

Zhuo Yi's mind raced. He knew this old man Teng too well. Seeing Chen Chao and the other two's amazing performance, the doubts in old man Teng's heart not only did not disappear, but turned into an even stronger desire to test them—the stronger they were, the more it showed that the "trump card" of Number 60 High School might be even deeper.

The suspect was always Wang Ling.

"Good! Very good!" Teng Luchen's mustache curled up even higher. Instead of the annoyance of being successfully defended, his eyes gleamed with the sharp light of a hunter who had discovered the prey's tracks.

"Are you all deaf?"

He swung his hand sharply, his voice decisive and authoritative, shattering the silence of the central hall: "Operators, listen up! Lock onto the Sixty Immortal Ships, 'Miniature Eastern Armor Cannons'—fire all three simultaneously, countdown, three, two, one! Fire!"

"Are you serious?" Wang Ming almost jumped up. "Elder Teng... this is beyond the scope of the preliminary test! Even a late-stage Golden Core cultivator couldn't withstand three shots fired at once!"

"Commander Teng, this..." Dean Jing Heqiu also felt his scalp tingle.

Zhuo Yi pressed down on Wang Ming's shoulder, signaling him to calm down, but his eyes also darkened, and his voice carried a hint of coldness that was hard to detect: "Elder Teng, turning a test into a saturation attack is a bit too hasty and violates safety regulations."

Teng Luchen seemed not to hear their questions, instead watching the screen with great interest, stroking his beard and slowly saying, "As I said, the evaluation criteria... will be revealed later. For now, the pressure is on! I also want to see where the upper limit of these kids from Number 60 High School is. Don't worry, the base's medical team is ready at any time."

As his voice fell, three blinding streaks of fire suddenly lit up on the huge crystal screen in the central hall, forming a triangular shape. They tore through the dark space of the earth's core, hurtling towards the seemingly insignificant immortal ship of the Number 60 High School with a more swift and cunning angle than the previous one, accompanied by a destructive shriek!

The oppressive force of the artillery fire seemed to permeate the entire hall even through the screen.

On the celestial ship, the atmosphere instantly shifted from the relaxed feeling of "first victory" to an extremely tense one.

"Again?!" Guo Hao had just removed his defensive runes, and his spiritual energy was still somewhat stagnant when he saw the three rapidly enlarging flames in his field of vision. His face turned pale instantly. "And... three shots!"

The afterimage around Chen Chao had barely faded, his muscles still bulging, but as he watched the devastating triangular attack array hurtling towards him, sweat beaded on his forehead. Taking just one hit had already made his blood boil, and he'd only managed to hold on thanks to an explosive burst of ferocity. Three hits at once... even if he risked his life, he could never manage to "hug" them from one direction like he had before.

"Damn it! Is this old man serious?!" Liu Qingyi lifted her head from Wang Zhen's arms. Her "performative fear" had vanished completely. Her aura churned, and her eyes were sharp as knives.

"Heh, it really is aimed at Wang Ling..." Sun Rong sneered inwardly, unconsciously summoning Ao Hai with her fingertips, the scabbard humming slightly.

She knew that Teng Luchen's target was ultimately Wang Ling.

Ryoko Kugimiya's face was tense. She quickly looked in the direction of Wang Ling, and then glanced at the kind-looking "vice principal" Monk Jin Deng, who was still standing in the same place.

At this moment, Wang Ling's eyes were slightly closed, as if he were in meditation. But just as the three cannon shots were about to hit him, his seemingly calm eyes suddenly opened a crack!

That tiny, open gap seemed to contain a pure and flawless universe.

Without uttering a word, Wang Ling, who had always seemed indifferent, made an extremely subtle, almost imperceptible gesture with his fingertips hanging at his side just as the cannonball was about to hit his good brother.

Two invisible and intangible energy streams, containing traces of the primordial power of the universe, precisely entered the bodies of Chen Chao and Guo Hao, who were preparing for a desperate fight, like the most exquisite silk threads!

Right now!

"Damn it, I'll fight!" Chen Chao roared, mustering all his strength once again, trying to forcefully block the shells directly in front of him.

Guo Hao gritted his teeth and instantly formed hand seals again.

However, just as their momentum began to rise, an indescribable, vast power, as boundless as the starry sea, suddenly surged forth from the depths of their bodies!

"Huh?!" Chen Chao was surprised to find that the dragon-headed elephant-bodied phantom on his body, which had been somewhat dim, suddenly swelled and solidified as if it had taken a powerful tonic! Golden light flowed out, and a terrifying force far surpassing the previous one, as if it could lift mountains, filled every muscle of his body!
