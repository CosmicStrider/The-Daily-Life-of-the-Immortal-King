Chapter 2296 The Prisoner of Taste

Songhai Number 60 High School, Grade 11 Regular Class

During the last self-study period in the afternoon, sunlight slanted into the classroom, and tiny dust particles floated in the air.

Most students were engrossed in reviewing various magical formulas and then discussing them quietly. The final exams at Number 60 High School were just around the corner, and everyone was taking them very seriously.

For students in regular classes, every major school exam is a rare opportunity for promotion.

Although they are currently in regular classes, as long as their ranking in regular classes is high enough, they will still have the opportunity to be promoted to the elite class of Number 60 High School in the future and enjoy the various training resources of the elite class.

Therefore, the school's major exam is a rare opportunity.

In today's scientific and cultivation-oriented era, this is the only path for children from poor families to climb higher in their lives.

At this moment, Li Jun, sitting in the back corner, unconsciously picked at the curled edge of his old exercise book with his fingers, his eyes somewhat unfocused, his mind blank.

At a parent-teacher conference after the entrance exam, his homeroom teacher, Hao Ren, told his parents, "Li Jun is actually quite smart. He just needs to work a little harder..."

As we all know, such polite remarks essentially negate a teacher's affirmation of a student's talent in cultivating their abilities.

Because truly intelligent and gifted children don't need to say much; often, the more something is emphasized, the less it is actually achieved.

Li Jun sighed silently. He didn't dislike his homeroom teacher because the other party had only said a polite truth.

However, Li Jun still felt a sense of unease, as if he had been pricked by a needle.

A penchant for internal conflict and overthinking is indeed a common ailment among those who lack ability.

"If only... if only I could be a little stronger..." Li Jun cried out countless times in his heart.

Sometimes even Li Jun himself doesn't know what kind of person he is.

His school uniform was faded from washing, and his schoolbag was a style that was popular a few years ago, with worn-out corners.

In this class full of elites, including students from wealthy families or with exceptional talents like Sun Rong and Chen Chao, Li Jun's presence was almost comparable to Wang Ling's.

His cultivation has been stuck at the early stage of Foundation Establishment for almost a year, while among his peers, reaching the Golden Core stage is no longer news.

Every time he took a spiritual power test, seeing his name steadily declining on the leaderboard and the slightly sympathetic and pitying looks around him, Li Jun's sensitive heart was always filled with frustration.

Even though no one blamed him, Li Jun still felt a strong sense of resentment.

Sometimes, she even blames her parents.

It's their fault for not working hard enough to become a second-generation immortal.

Although his family wasn't exactly poor, Li Jun always felt a huge gap compared to his peers.

His parents were ordinary working-class people, and it was already a huge effort for them to send him to Number 60 High School. They couldn't even dream of expensive training resources or guidance from famous teachers.

All Li Jun could do was spend more time meditating than others and practicing the most basic techniques.

Despite trying my best to learn and improve each time, the results were still minimal.

Recently, a group of boys from well-off families, led by Zhang Hao, have started to pick on him, whether intentionally or unintentionally, mocking his old schoolbag and imitating his slightly clumsy posture when meditating because he was anxious.

They were all in regular classes, and their cultivation levels weren't particularly high, but their rankings were consistently quite high. This was because before each major exam, their parents would feed them many spirit-gathering pills and other similar medicines to help them improve their rankings through the power of money.

In the cultivation world, this is not considered cheating.

So, Li Jun silently endured this bullying every time, his fists clenching under the table and then helplessly relaxing.

The school bell rang, and the students filed out of the school.

Li Jun habitually waited until almost everyone had left before slowly packing his schoolbag.

He didn't want to stand out in the crowd, and he was even more afraid of running into Zhang Hao and his group.

"Hey, Li Jun!"

Just as he feared, Zhang Hao, accompanied by two followers, blocked the classroom door, grinning at him: "Why are you walking so slowly? Are you hiding in the classroom studying again? Studying? Study my ass! Your spiritual roots are too mixed. If you want to make progress, you'll have to put in a hundred years more time than others!"

Li Jun remained silent, his head down, trying to walk around him.

Zhang Hao tripped him, and Li Jun stumbled, almost falling. The talisman box and other spiritual materials in his schoolbag scattered all over the ground.

"Hahaha!"

Zhang Hao and his followers burst into laughter: "You can't even walk steadily? Your family's broke, what kind of immortality are you trying to cultivate?"

Li Jun squatted down and silently picked up the books, his fingernails almost digging into his palms.

Humiliation and helplessness overwhelmed him like a tide.

He hated his own weakness and the injustice of the world.

Just then, the homeroom teacher, Mr. Hao, walked in. Seeing this scene, he frowned and said, "Zhang Hao, what are you doing? Go home right now!"

Zhang Hao curled his lip and led his men away.

Teacher Hao looked at Li Jun squatting on the ground, sighed, and softened his tone: "Li Jun, don't be discouraged. In the path of cultivation, perseverance is the most important thing. By the way, I have some good news. The school has secured a batch of 'Taste Master' experience slots from the Xibei Cultivation Food Group, and based on your performance in the last social practice, you're on the list. I've heard that this stuff is very helpful for stabilizing one's state of mind and improving spiritual power. Go to the academic affairs office to pick it up later."

Li Jun suddenly raised his head, a look of disbelief flashing in his eyes.

Xi Pei Cultivation Food Group?

That magical device that's been advertised everywhere lately, claiming to revolutionize taste and aid in cultivation?

How could a poor student like him get a spot?

"Thank you... thank you, teacher!" Li Jun's voice trembled slightly with excitement.

A moment later, Li Jun stood in a corner of the campus, holding the small silver wristband device, his heart pounding.

The device has a cool touch and a streamlined design that is full of technological flair, making it seem out of place with everything else.

Following the instructions, he carefully put it on his wrist.

"Buzz!"

The device vibrated gently, a soft light flashed, and a slight suction felt where it touched the skin of the wrist.

Immediately afterwards, a minimalist interface was projected onto his retina, prompting him to [connect to the "Spirit Network"].

Li Jun chose to connect.

In an instant, it was as if a door to a new world had been opened.

An irresistible feeling of comfort flowed through my body, and the fatigue and depression of the past few days seemed to have lessened considerably.

He subconsciously licked his slightly chapped lips, and the image of the Spirit Fruit Cake he had seen when passing by the dessert shop yesterday involuntarily surfaced in his mind.

Because it was very expensive, Li Jun had been reluctant to buy it.

As if possessed, he took out the dry, hard steamed buns he hadn't finished eating that morning from his schoolbag.

Take a bite.

Li Jun's eyes widened instantly!

The dry and rough texture I had imagined didn't appear; instead, it was incredibly soft and sweet!
