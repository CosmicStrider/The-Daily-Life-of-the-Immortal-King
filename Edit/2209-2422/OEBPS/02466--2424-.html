Chapter 2424 Lin Jingzhe

Chen Chao lowered his voice and moved his phone closer to Wang Ling.

The screen displays a screenshot of a battle.

Lin Jingzhe won all three preliminary matches, with an average time of 47 seconds per match.

In the quarterfinals, the opponent forfeited.

"Forty-seven seconds?" Little Peanut, who was collecting the talisman practice paper, overheard the discussion and leaned over, half a spicy snack still dangling from his mouth. "Forty-seven seconds on average? That means the game is over before the warm-up is even over?"

"This result is indeed a bit outrageous," Guo Hao said solemnly. "Moreover, two of his opponents in the preliminaries were in the late Foundation Establishment stage. One of them was even in the top 16 of last year's city competition."

Chen Chao remained silent for two seconds.

In the later stages of Foundation Building, he reached the top 16 in the city competition, but couldn't last more than 50 seconds against his opponents.

What the hell is that monster?

"There's something even more shocking." Guo Hao pulled up another file on his phone: "He broke through to the Golden Core stage three months ago. When he was in the Foundation Establishment stage, he won the runner-up title in the National High School Kendo Competition. He lost the final match because his opponent was an old monster in the Nascent Soul stage who was disguised as someone else. Later, it was discovered that he had been disqualified."

Chen Chao: "..."

He has just broken through to the Golden Core stage.

National runner-up.

Wang Ling wants to fight this kind of person?

He subconsciously looked to the side.

Wang Lingzheng kept his head down, intently unpacking a new flavor of instant noodles, his expression unchanged.

"Your Highness," Chen Chao couldn't help but speak up, "Did you hear what Guo Hao said? Golden Core stage!"

"Mm." Wang Ling nodded and broke off another piece.

"That's it?" Chen Chao exclaimed anxiously, "That's the Golden Core stage!"

Wang Ling thought for a moment, then swallowed the instant noodles in his mouth: "And then?"

Chen Chao choked.

then?

Then you should be a little nervous!

Why don't you frown a little?

Guo Hao wasn't in a hurry; instead, he glanced at Wang Ling thoughtfully.

Having known Wang Ling for so long, he had figured out a pattern: the calmer Wang Ling was, the more confident he was in things.

Although he still can't figure out what kind of confidence a Foundation Establishment cultivator can have when facing a Golden Core cultivator.

But Wang Ling has this ability to make people believe him for no apparent reason.

"I've compiled the data." Guo Hao stuffed the stack of papers into Wang Ling's drawer. "His swordsmanship, habitual movements, spiritual attribute preferences, analysis notes from match videos... everything I could find is in there."

Wang Ling glanced down at the stack of papers and nodded: "Thank you."

Although he wouldn't care about Lin Jingzhe at all, he still had to thank his brothers for their kindness.

In addition, this information is not entirely useless. At least it allows Wang Ling to have a basic understanding of Lin Jingzhe's fighting habits, which will make it easier for Wang Ling to perform during the competition and avoid accidentally killing Lin Jingzhe.

The semifinals are almost over.

Further ahead is the battle for the championship and runner-up, which is too flashy.

Wang Ling glanced at the photo at the top of Guo Hao's stack of documents.

He had delicate features and sharp eyes.

A swordsmanship prodigy in the early stages of the Golden Core realm.

Such people are generally proud and arrogant, and easily become depressed when they lose.

We need to find a way to make him win with dignity, so that we don't lose in a bad way.

Wang Ling broke off a piece of instant noodles and put it in his mouth, chewing it slowly.

This is much more troublesome than winning.

At the same time, at St. Science Academy, in the gravity training room.

The indoor gravity was adjusted to twelve times.

A boy stood shirtless in the center of the training room, holding an unsharpened iron sword in his hand.

The sword was ordinary, just a basic teaching sword, but in his hands, every thrust left a faint white mark in the air.

That was water vapor formed by the compressed air due to the sword's high speed.

Half an hour later, the gravity slowly returned to normal.

The boy put down his sword and picked up a towel to wipe the sweat from his face.

The door opened.

Su Xingyue walked in, followed by two students in white lab coats carrying various monitoring devices.

"Junior Brother Lin," Su Xingyue said in a businesslike tone, "the opponents for the semi-finals have been announced."

Lin Jingzhe didn't turn around and continued wiping his sweat.

Su Xingyue gestured to the two people behind her to set up the equipment, then walked to his side and pressed the projector button in her hand.

The light screen unfolded in the air, displaying clips of Wang Ling's first three matches.

"It's Wang Ling. You should have heard of him. He also participated in the Earth's Core Project with me before." Su Xingyue's voice was calm, as if she were giving a research report: "Three wins in the preliminaries, the longest match lasting two minutes and fourteen seconds. In the quarterfinals, he defeated Zhao Wuji. At that time, Zhao Wuji had already broken through the third level of the Vajra Subduing Demon Body and burned his essence blood to reach the pseudo-Golden Core level of combat power in a short time, but he was defeated by Wang Ling with one punch. You can't underestimate him."

She paused for a moment, then pressed the pause button.

The image freezes on the moment Wang Ling throws his punch.

"You need to take a look at this punch."

Lin Jingzhe finally turned around.

He glanced at the light screen, then looked at Su Xingyue.

His eyes were indifferent, showing little emotion.

"I've finished reading it," he said.

Su Xingyue frowned.

Lin Jingzhe walked to the side, picked up the water bottle, and took a sip.

"Early Foundation Establishment stage, defeated a pseudo-Golden Core cultivator with a single punch." He put down the water jug: "You believe me?"

Su Xingyue was silent for a second.

"The judges concluded that it was a victory of skill. His punch created a high-frequency vibration the moment it touched Zhao Wuji's forehead, shattering the spiritual energy structure formed by the burning essence of his blood."

"Isn't that just technique?" Lin Jingzhe interrupted her.

Su Xingyue stared at him.

Lin Jingzhe picked up her sword and casually twirled it.

"Senior Sister Su, I know what you're trying to say. This person is very skilled, has excellent timing, and a sharp eye." He looked at the light screen, a slight smile playing on his lips: "But so what?"

He put the sword back on the shelf.

"Early Foundation Establishment is just that. No matter how good your skills are, can they make up for the difference in spiritual power? If I stand still and let him hit me, how many high-frequency tremors can his spiritual power withstand?"

Su Xingyue took a deep breath.

"Junior Brother Lin, I didn't tell you to treat him as an opponent of the same level. But at the very least, you should watch his match videos and learn about his habits."

"There's no need."

Lin Jingzhe picked up her clothes and walked towards the door.

Su Xingyue stepped aside to block him.

What do you mean?

Lin Jingzhe stopped and looked down at her.

Standing at over 1.8 meters tall, Su Xingyue, who is only 1.6 meters tall, definitely needs to lower his head.

"Senior Sister Su," he began, his tone still calm, "I respect that you are the student council president, and I respect that you led the team to risk your life in the Project: The Center of the Earth. But there's really no need to waste time on this competition."

He walked around Su Xingyue and pushed open the door.

"You'll understand why I say this once I face him."

The door closed.

Su Xingyue stood there, staring at the door for a few seconds.

The two students in research suits behind him dared not utter a sound.

"President..." one of them cautiously began, "Is the data still being analyzed?"

"Never mind, if he doesn't want to listen, there's nothing I can do." Su Xingyue closed her eyes and turned to look out the window.

Outside the training room, Lin Jingzhe's figure walked through the corridor, getting further and further away.

She recalled how she felt when she first saw Wang Ling's competition video.

That bizarre movement, that unbelievable timing...

What ordinary people see is "good skills".

She sensed something was wrong.

Lin Jingzhe possesses high talent and strong comprehension of swordsmanship.

But he has a fatal problem.

He has never lost.

From small sects to Saint Ke, they've crushed their way through, never encountering a truly tough opponent.

These kinds of people are most likely to capsize in the gutter.

Su Xingyue withdrew her gaze.

never mind.

Everything that needed to be said has been said.

He'll understand once he hits a wall himself.

I just hope this wall isn't too hard.
