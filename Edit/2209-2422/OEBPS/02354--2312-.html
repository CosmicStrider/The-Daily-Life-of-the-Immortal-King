Chapter 2312 Wooden Fish's Campus Radar

Wang Muyu never imagined that he would actually be able to experience daily life on campus at Xiuzhen High School with the two people he respected most in his life.

His "mother" was right beside him, and his "father" was sitting behind him.

This gave Wang Muyu a strong sense of security, and even his mood improved inexplicably.

As the bell rang to signal the end of class, Wang Muyu carefully organized his newly issued textbooks, his clear, bright eyes filled with utmost seriousness.

Although the content of the last lesson was so ordinary to him, strangely enough, he actually gained some insights.

"How's Little Wooden Fish doing? Did you understand everything?" Sun Rong asked with a smile.

Wang Muyu subconsciously wanted to call for his mother, but the school uniform he was wearing quickly made him realize that he absolutely could not cause trouble for Sun Rong and Wang Ling in this situation. He immediately stopped the car and just nodded obediently.

"Little wooden fish, come on, let me take you to try the spiritual energy-themed kitchen at our Number 60 High School cafeteria!"

Chen Chao enthusiastically hooked his arm around Wang Muyu's shoulder: "I guarantee it will taste even better than what you eat at home!"

Guo Hao added from the side, "Don't listen to Chao'er's hype, the taste is just so-so... but the effect of replenishing spiritual energy is indeed good, and it's cost-effective. Little Wooden Fish, remember this: if you encounter a chef named Juan Ma, be careful and eat less..."

Wang Muyu subconsciously looked at Wang Ling by the window first.

Wang Ling had already stood up, still with that indifferent and unassuming demeanor, and simply nodded slightly to him.

The dishes that Juan's mother makes are so incredibly potent that they're almost unbearable for most people.

"Okay! Thank you, Brother Chen Chao and Brother Guo Hao." Wang Muyu gave a well-behaved smile.

Sun Rong walked naturally alongside Wang Ling and chuckled softly, "Mu Yu adapted quite quickly."

Wang Ling: "Hmm."

His gaze swept indifferently over the people coming and going in the corridor, his consciousness silently scanning every teacher and student who passed by.

Most of the atmosphere was normal, a mix of youthful energy and the excitement every high school student feels for lunch.

They had barely stepped out of the classroom when a number of seasoned foodies, like wild dogs running free, rushed toward the cafeteria down the corridor, some even already armed with metal spoons.

However, just as they were about to descend the stairs, Wang Muyu paused slightly.

Deep within his pure dragon bloodline, a faint tremor emanated, and an undeniable, strange presence suddenly appeared.

"What's wrong, Mu Yu?" Sun Rong noticed his sudden change in expression and bent down to ask softly.

Wang Muyu looked up, a hint of confusion on his face. He tugged at Sun Rong's sleeve and then quietly pulled Wang Ling's finger, cautiously transmitting his voice: "Just now... a teacher who walked by over there had a strange feeling about him."

He pointed to the corner of the stairs, where the back of a maintenance worker wearing gray overalls and carrying a psionic toolbox could be seen.

Wang Ling's gaze remained unchanged, but a spiritual mark had silently descended upon the logistics personnel, directly marking him.

"uncomfortable?"

Sun Rong was extremely gentle: "Is the cafeteria smell too strong? Today is Thursday, it's probably a stinky tofu special!"

Wang Ling looked at Wang Muyu, his expression becoming more serious, and asked via telepathy, "Muyu, what exactly does it feel like?"

Wang Muyu struggled to organize his thoughts: "It's... a bit cold, and a bit... messy. Unlike other people."

Sun Rong and Wang Ling exchanged a glance.

The fact that O'Hay did not issue a strong warning suggests that the erosion was very subtle, or that it was not specifically targeting them.

However, Wang Muyu, relying on the dragon race's instinctive perception of negative auras, managed to detect something unusual.

"Maybe I'm mistaken," Wang Muyu said quickly when he saw the two men looking serious, not wanting to cause trouble so soon after arriving.

"It's alright," Wang Ling said, unusually calmly. "Observe more, listen more."

This statement is both a reassurance and an instruction.

Wang Muyu nodded vigorously: "Yes!"

The group walked into the cafeteria.

The canteen at Number 60 High School is quite large, and at this moment it was bustling with people, the aroma of various delicacies mingling together, creating a lively atmosphere.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao went to the window to queue up and grab vegetables without any trouble.

Sun Rong led Wang Muyu to choose food, while Wang Ling found a relatively quiet spot by the window to sit down.

Just as Wang Muyu was staring at the plate of glistening braised pork hock in the shop window and swallowing hard, that "uncomfortable" feeling returned, and it was much clearer than before!

This time, the source was a woman with a blank expression serving food at the food serving window.

She looked no different from the other busy cafeteria staff around her, and even seemed more skilled, and... her hands were shaking! A full spoonful of chicken nuggets would be shaken so much that only a third of it remained!

But Wang Muyu clearly saw that beneath the faint spiritual light belonging to human cultivators surrounding her, there was a faint gray aura, filled with a sense of frenzy.

This aura clashed with the pure dragon energy in his blood, causing him to instinctively feel repulsed.

Wang Muyu frowned and subconsciously moved closer to Sun Rong.

"You can feel it again?" Sun Rong asked in a low voice, shifting her body slightly to the side to block Wang Muyu's view in that direction.

"Hmm." Wang Muyu responded softly, "That auntie... there seems to be something inside."

"I understand." Sun Rong's expression remained unchanged as she gently patted his head. "What do you want to eat? I'll order for you."

She acted like an ordinary older sister taking care of her younger brother, but alarm bells were already ringing in her heart.

Logistics staff, canteen workers...

The infiltration of the "Shadows" is indeed ubiquitous, and has already penetrated into the daily life of the campus.

If they can tamper with food...

Thinking of this, Sun Rong felt a chill run down her spine.

After getting their food and returning to their seats, Chen Chao began wolfing it down, while Guo Hao was more refined.

Following everyone else's example, Wang Muyu used chopsticks to pick up a piece of broccoli brimming with spiritual energy, put it in his mouth and chewed, his eyes lighting up slightly.

The food in the high school cafeteria was better than he had imagined!

"How about that? I didn't lie to you, did I?" Chen Chao said vaguely.

"Delicious." Wang Muyu nodded, but his eating speed slowed down significantly, and he focused most of his attention on perceiving his surroundings.

He was like a human radar, silently scanning the entire cafeteria.

In Wang Ling's perception, Wang Muyu's pure dragon power rippled outwards from him like waves on water.

Although it is so faint as to be almost imperceptible, it has a peculiar effect on revealing the "impurities" hidden in the crowd.

During that short meal, Wang Muyu pointed out three more people who seemed unusual to him.

One was a boy sitting diagonally across from me, quickly eating his meal with his head down;

One was a cleaning lady clearing away the dishes from the next table;

There was another one, a senior student who had just entered the cafeteria wearing a school uniform, but whose eyes were somewhat vacant, and went straight to the energy drink vending machine.

Wang Ling remained silent as always. Of course, he could also sense the abnormality, but he used Wang Muyu's ability to double-confirm it.

At the same time, the corresponding spiritual imprints have been precisely attached to each target.

A list of "sneakers" hidden within the campus of Number 60 High School is quietly forming in Wang Ling's mind.

"Reiko, why are you eating so slowly today?"

Guo Hao finally realized that Wang Ling had barely touched his chopsticks.

Wang Ling raised his dead fish eyes and slowly added a piece of fried stinky tofu: "Contemplating life."
