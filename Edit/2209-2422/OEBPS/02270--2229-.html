Chapter 2229 Chen Chao and Guo Hao Emerge from Seclusion

Because of this mysterious embroidered pouch from the Immortal Mountain, Sun Rong gained a strange ability, somewhat similar to the system abilities in those online novels—she suddenly could see the countdown above other people's heads, and had confirmed that it was an emotional countdown.

Through Sun Rong's exploration, she learned that the countdown has three states.

Red status: The person involved is highly likely to be experiencing an emotional crisis, and once the countdown ends, the relationship will break down.

Golden state: It most likely symbolizes emotional stability, and once the countdown ends, the two people may directly become a couple or a higher level of relationship.

Mysterious state: It is not yet clear what this means, because Sun Rong has only seen it on herself when looking in the mirror.

To be honest, the appearance of this repair bag made Sun Rong very anxious. On the way back, she was quite absent-minded because the more she analyzed it, the more she felt that it was like some kind of supernatural rule.

But does this sudden ability mean she'll have a more accurate assessment of her feelings for Wang Ling...?

Inside the luxury car, Sun Rong leaned back in the back seat, deep in thought. The driver, Lao Lin, keenly noticed the unusual expression on his young mistress's face: "Miss, is something troubling you? Is it something related to your relationship?"

After being interrupted by Butler Lin's words, Sun Rong's fair face immediately flushed red: "No, Uncle Lin..." Then, she immediately looked out the window, pretending to be nonchalant.

Old Lin simply smiled faintly, not revealing the girl's hidden shyness. He had worked in the Huaguo Water Curtain Group for many years and was a veteran in the industry who had watched Sun Rong grow up. Moreover, due to his position, he probably spent more time with Sun Rong and her grandson Sun Yiyuan than with his own family. Of course, he could tell what Sun Rong was thinking from the subtle changes in her expression.

Most of the wild thoughts of teenage girls are due to emotions, and as someone who has been through it, Lao Lin understands this very well.

"The person Miss likes shouldn't be the proactive type, right? If it's not due to his personality, then it's most likely due to other external factors that have caused his lack of emotional perception." After a long silence, Butler Lin's deep gaze revealed the truth.

Sun Rong pretended to be calm, but her heart was in turmoil. Her slightly constricted pupils showed her shock. She never expected that the old driver from the Sun family who was so close to her was actually a hidden master of emotions.

She was quite certain that this was a guess made by Uncle Lin based on his rich experience and professional sensitivity, and that he was not trying to obtain any information from any source. He was not a meddlesome person.

However, Sun Rong also knew that Uncle Lin had probably heard some rumors about Wang Ling... After all, her husband, Sun Yiyuan, was extremely satisfied with Wang Ling.

But it was impossible for Steward Lin to know Wang Ling's background.

Therefore, what truly shocked Sun Rong was the second half of Butler Lin's statement: Wang Ling's lack of emotional perception.

Ever since Sun Rong learned of Wang Ling's true strength, she knew very well that the lack of this ability was mostly due to the sealing talisman, but there was no way that Butler Lin could know such a secret.

Seeing that Sun Rong remained silent, Butler Lin did not press further. Sometimes, it's enough to leave it at that.

Although his skill level wasn't particularly high, his professional habit made him far more adept at reading people than ordinary people. It was precisely because of this that he was able to survive in the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group until today.

...

On Monday, January 26, the day before the countdown to Project Center of the Earth, Chen Chao and Guo Hao, who had been undergoing arduous training in the Regression Mountain, finally emerged from their seclusion under the guidance of their respective masters.

The two of them were much more solid than before. In just a few days, they successfully broke through to the peak of the late Golden Core stage. Their original goal was to break through to the early Golden Core stage. Even Chen Chao and Guo Hao themselves did not expect that they could exceed the target, and by such a large margin!

This breakthrough speed is nothing compared to Wang Ling's, but it is already quite exaggerated compared to many of their peers. With Wang Ling's help, their current level is more than enough to get into a Heaven-level sect. In fact, it can be said that they are almost all guaranteed admission.

The Mountain of Regression was originally a cultivation secret realm specially created by Wang Ling for the two clams. Its spiritual energy concentration is high, and the flow of time is slower than in the outside world. It can also automatically assess the overall condition of the cultivators who enter and differentiate different illusions for targeted training.

Of course, the most important thing is that all the spiritual plants that appeared in the Mountain of Regression were real, including some that had already become extinct. This greatly surprised Master Canglong and Master Liu Yong, who were Chen Chao and Guo Hao respectively.

The main reason they accepted Chen Chao and Guo Hao as their disciples was because of the introduction by True Lord Lei. They couldn't possibly refuse such a huge favor.

Of course, after they secretly investigated Chen Chao and Guo Hao, they found that their personalities were very compatible with theirs, making them truly chosen disciples... as if they were tailor-made for them.

As the two followed the excited Chen Chao and Guo Hao out of the forest of Mount Rewind, they both gazed intently at the golden, formless sculpture in the open space before them. (For a recap of the sculpture, see Chapter 2008: The Immortal King's Chess Game)

They were both seasoned veterans of the cultivation world. Although they hadn't seen through the truth immediately, during their time cultivating on Mount Huisu, they gradually came to understand it better.

The appearance of Chen Chao and Guo Hao was definitely not a coincidence, but rather arranged by someone behind the scenes.

On the surface, it was Diu Lei Zhenjun and Zhuo Yi who came forward, but in fact, the two of them, who were as clear-headed as mirrors, knew that there were other behind-the-scenes manipulators.

"Canglong, what do you think?" Looking at the formless golden sculpture, Liu Yong felt a surge of excitement.

Is it really possible that Zhan Zong could rise to his current position so quickly in such a short time solely through the efforts of True Monarch Diu Lei? Although the current Zhan Zong leader's strength is unfathomable, Liu Yong always felt that there was a secret actual controller behind Zhan Zong.

As for Lord Diu Lei, he is merely a figurehead who is presented in the open.

"It cannot be spoken, it cannot be spoken." Master Canglong smiled, his massive, muscular body appearing exceptionally small in front of the statue.

At this moment, even though they had seen through everything, they also knew very well that pretending to be confused was the only capital they could rely on to survive in the cultivation world.
