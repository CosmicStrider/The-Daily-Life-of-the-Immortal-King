Chapter 2273 The strike covers the entire globe!

In the inner world, within a relatively stable main passage of the Thousand Caves City.

The intermittent communication signal, like a candle flickering in the wind, finally managed to stabilize to a point where it could transmit basic information.

Ryoko Kunomiya almost held her breath, her hands flying across the nearly broken array, not even bothering to wipe the fine sweat beading on her forehead.

"Buzz... This is... the core center of the Palm Cliff base... Please respond if you hear me!" The sound coming from the array plate was mixed with strong interference noise, intermittent, yet it entered everyone's ears like heavenly music.

"Received! Received! This is Sun Rong and Jiu Gong Liangzi from Number 60 High School! We heard you!" Sun Rong immediately moved closer, her voice filled with barely suppressed excitement, and responded first.

She subconsciously glanced at Wang Ling beside her and saw that he still had that indifferent expression, seemingly unsurprised that the communication had been restored, but Sun Rong's heart was at ease as a result.

"Sun Rong! Jiu Gong! Are you alright? Where are the others? Report the situation!" The voice on the other end of the call became noticeably urgent; it seemed to be Dean Jing Heqiu.

"We...we're still alive! Most of us are together! There are some injured, but none of them are in life-threatening condition! Everyone from Number 60 High School! Senior Li Changzhe, Senior Zhang Linyan, Senior Su Xingyue...everyone is here!" Sun Rong quickly rattled off a string of names, her voice unconsciously rising with excitement as she announced the team's safety.

"That's great...that's great..."

A distinct sigh of relief came from the other end of the communicator, followed by Teng Luchen's calm yet urgent voice cutting in: "Sun Rong, listen up! The energy fluctuations in the Earth's core are extremely abnormal, with many indicators far exceeding the safety threshold, triggering the highest emergency plan! The base has detected your approximate area. Immediately assemble at coordinates [Y-77-X-09]! I repeat, immediately assemble at coordinates [Y-77-X-09]! There are relatively stable spatial folds there, and the base will forcibly open a temporary evacuation passage! You only have a maximum of one core time!"

"Understood! We'll act immediately!" Sun Rong agreed without hesitation.

"Wait!" Teng Luchen's voice rang out again, filled with unprecedented solemnity: "Tell me, what exactly happened deep within the Earth's core? The energy peak detected by the base... even briefly surpassed the highest known level of Earth's response! There were also multiple large-scale energy annihilation disturbances!"

This question instantly silenced the passageway.

Everyone remembered the beast horde that swept through the labyrinth, the terrifying glass arena, the desperate guardian, the evil face that peeked out from the source eye, and the inexplicable strokes of luck that kept them from danger...

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and the others looked terrified and bewildered. They only knew it was very dangerous and terrifying, but they didn't know exactly how terrifying it was or why they survived. They attributed it mostly to the collective effort and a little bit of incredible luck.

Elites such as Zhang Linyan, Li Changzhe, and Su Xingyue frowned deeply.

They sensed more, their doubts deepened, and their gazes involuntarily drifted towards Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, and Liu Qingyi, before finally sweeping over Wang Ling, who had been quietly... Hmm? This time, instead of eating snacks, he was unconsciously tracing the ancient runes on the wall with his fingers.

Wang Zhen laughed heartily and spoke to the array plate before Sun Rong could, saying, "General Teng, this place at the Earth's core is incredibly strange! There are ancient beasts, traitors from the Liuli Clan, and an evil will from who-knows-where causing trouble. How can the energy not be chaotic? We barely escaped with our lives, almost being wiped out! It's all thanks to everyone's concerted efforts and a tiny bit of luck from our ancestral graves!"

His words were half true and half false, cleverly obscuring the truly astonishing parts and attributing them to the mystery of the Earth's core and the team's luck.

Liu Qingyi added in unison, "That's right, the energy fluctuations might be due to the loosening of some ancient seals or an anomaly in the earth's veins themselves, right? We've exhausted all our strength just trying to escape, how could we have time to investigate the source?"

Fang Xing succinctly summarized: "Crisis lurked everywhere; I was lucky to survive."

Looking at the terrifying energy curves and afterimages recorded on the array plate that remain unanalyzed to this day, Ryoko Kugimiya opened her mouth, but ultimately chose to remain silent, only stealing a glance at Wang Ling.

She knew more than Chen Chao and the others, but it was still just the tip of the iceberg.

Sun Rong understood immediately and summarized, "Commander Teng, the specific situation is complicated. We will provide a detailed report after we evacuate. The most urgent task is to head to the evacuation point immediately!"

Teng Luchen remained silent for a few seconds on the other end. He clearly did not believe this explanation, especially the point that the energy peak far exceeded the current highest level on Earth, which could not be explained by "geological vein anomalies".

But he also understood that now was not the time to delve into it.

"Alright... then let's act immediately! Keep communications open and report the situation at any time! The base will guide you in every way!" Teng Luchen finally suppressed his doubts and gave the order.

"yes!"

After the communication ended, no one dared to delay. They immediately formed a team and set off, based on the coordinates and faint guidance received from the Nine Palaces Liangzi array.

Wang Ling also withdrew his fingers from studying the runes and silently followed the group.

He walked casually in the middle of the group, but with each step he took, a subtle, almost imperceptible thought swept across the passage ahead.

They silently smoothed out the unstable psionic turbulence, dispersed the small subterranean creatures that might have been affected by the remnants of the Outer God's will, and even subtly adjusted the spatial structure of some side paths, ensuring that the team always traveled on the smoothest and shortest route.

Everything was done so flawlessly that even Wang Zhen and Fang Xing, with their level of cultivation, could only vaguely sense that their progress was unusually smooth, but could not detect any trace of human intervention.

To others, it was simply good luck; they had chosen the right path.

Sun Rong followed closely beside Wang Ling. The peaceful vibrations emanating from the Ao Hai sword sheath made her realize that this "success" was no accident.

Looking at Wang Lingping's calm profile, a sense of dependence and admiration quietly grew in her heart. Subconsciously, she adjusted her pace and kept an eye out for some inconspicuous pebbles under his feet.

...

Meanwhile, at the Palm Cliff base on the surface, in the core central hall.

The atmosphere isn't much better than in the inner Earth.

On the main screen, the graph representing the energy environment of the Earth's core was a mess, with countless glaring red peaks and black turbulent areas that were alarming. In particular, the few peaks that briefly appeared earlier that exceeded the limits of the scale made all the technicians' scalps tingle.

Teng Luchen's face was ashen, his withered fingers pressed tightly on the control panel, his knuckles turning white.

He stared intently at the cluster of lights on the screen representing the life signals of Wang Ling and the others, which were slowly moving towards the evacuation point, his heart filled with turbulent emotions.

"An energy reaction that surpasses the highest level currently on Earth... multiple disturbances at the rule level... and that crushing annihilation phenomenon..." He murmured, his gaze sharp as a knife: "What exactly happened?"

He was almost certain that the root of the Earth's core anomalies, and even those incredible reversals, must be manipulated by some unknown, mysterious force.

Just then, the heavy alloy doors of the central hall slid open, and the heads of foreign teams, led by representatives from major cultivation powers such as the United States, the Maoist Kingdom, and Sun Island, walked in with varying expressions, accompanied by a group of base officials.

They originally came to observe the initial results of the Huaxiu Kingdom's Earth Core Project and to conduct preliminary exchanges. They even brought a group of elite students with them, planning to take the Huaxiu team and explore the Earth's core in batches.

But now, the crisis in the inner world no longer supports their subsequent actions.

However, as soon as they entered the central hall, everyone's attention was drawn to the hellish geocentric energy monitoring map on the main screen.

"Oh, my God..." A blonde, blue-eyed female cultivator covered her mouth, her eyes filled with horror.

"What...what level of energy disaster is this?! Commander Teng, has your country's inner world...always been like this...active?" An old man dressed in a white robe and wearing a white headscarf asked in a trembling voice, his forehead instantly covered with cold sweat.

"That peak value...exceeded the maximum monitoring limit of our country's energy tower!" A tall, burly man from the Mishu Kingdom pointed to the remnants of one of the off-peak points on the screen, his voice hoarse. "This is impossible! What kind of being could survive in this environment?!"

The representatives from various countries paled and whispered among themselves. The relaxed demeanor of the observation and exchange was gone, replaced by disbelief, shock, and a barely perceptible fear.
