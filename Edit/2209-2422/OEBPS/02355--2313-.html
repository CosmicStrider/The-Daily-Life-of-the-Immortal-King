Chapter 2313 The Mantra for Clarifying the Spiritual Platform

The next morning, when Wang Muyu followed Wang Ling and Sun Rong into the classroom, he clearly sensed something different from the day before.

The classroom was still noisy. Chen Chao and Guo Hao were arguing about last night's League of Legends World Championship live stream. The Huaxiu AI team had successfully advanced to the quarterfinals, which had both of them incredibly excited. On the other side, several girls were gathered together discussing the newly released plant-based skincare products.

But amidst the daily hustle and bustle of the campus, Wang Muyu keenly caught several extremely obscure, even chaotic, gazes that lingered briefly on him, Wang Ling, and Sun Rong before quickly retreating.

"Father and Mother, they sense us," Wang Muyu whispered, using the gesture of putting down his schoolbag.

Wang Ling seemed not to hear, and as always, he slumped in his seat, took out a new flavor of "crab roe and pork floss" instant noodles from the drawer, and slowly opened it. The slight sound was almost negligible in the surrounding noise.

Sun Rong gave Wang Muyu a reassuring smile and transmitted her voice, "Don't be afraid, Muyu. Just feel normal, just like yesterday."

As she spoke, she looked at Wang Ling and said, "You must trust him!"

Wang Muyu nodded vigorously, his little face full of seriousness.

He felt that he had an important mission on his shoulders.

The first lesson was an old-fashioned book called "An Outline of Modern Cultivation History".

To cater to the recent topic of "the outbreak of inner demons," the old antique recounted a history of heretical cultivators. He said that there was once a famous heretical cultivator who disguised himself as a mental patient and could travel to parallel cultivation worlds full of evil through a jade pendant. He gained great power by constantly sacrificing his body parts.

This is the old-fashioned teaching method: combining recent social topics with some anecdotes about cultivation history to present the teaching content in a humorous way.

It sounds like a big juicy story to everyone, but in reality, the knowledge they should have learned has already entered their brains in a despicable way.

Wang Muyu listened with great interest; this teaching style was completely different from the rigid teaching methods he had used at the Wuji Sword Sect.

It was something he couldn't access within the Wuji Sword Sect.

However, just as he was listening with great interest, Wang Muyu suddenly felt a faint tremor in the pure dragon blood within his body.

The source was a boy wearing glasses, two rows away on the right front.

The boy was taking notes with his head down, looking extremely serious, but the aura of a human cultivator surrounding him was being entangled by a wisp of dark, parasitic energy.

Moreover, the aura seemed slightly more vibrant than it had been yesterday.

Wang Muyu quietly memorized the boy's appearance and seat.

During the break, Chen Chao and Guo Hao came over again.

"Little wooden fish, come on, I'll take you to the convenience store! I heard they have a new limited edition spirit fruit flavored sparkling water today!" Chen Chao was as enthusiastic as ever.

"Thank you, Brother Chen Chao," Wang Muyu replied obediently, but her gaze unconsciously swept over the boy with glasses.

He sat alone in his seat, staring out the window with a blank look in his eyes, completely out of place with the noisy environment around him.

"What are you looking at?" Guo Hao followed Wang Muyu's gaze and said, "Oh, Zhao Liang. He's usually like that, not very sociable, but he studies very hard."

Wang Muyu hummed in response and didn't say anything more.

He cannot startle the snake by striking the grass.

On his way to the convenience store, Wang Muyu sensed three more students with unusual auras.

One of them was even playing basketball on the playground, looking every bit the sunny and cheerful sports student. But a hint of gloom was hidden beneath his vigorous energy, almost imperceptible to Wang Muyu unless he had special perception.

Just then, the campus radio suddenly came on.

Principal Chen Tianxiang's voice was broadcast directly through the broadcasting system.

"Dear teachers and students, please pay attention. An important announcement is about to be made. In response to the call from the School Board of Education to strengthen the psychological development of young monks, our school will, starting today, pilot a group recitation activity of the *Lingtai Qingming Mantra* during lunch breaks in all teaching buildings and meditation halls. Class leaders are requested to organize their students to participate voluntarily..."

The broadcast echoed in every classroom, playground, and corridor.

Almost all the students stopped what they were doing and pricked up their ears.

Auxiliary mental techniques?

Why would the school suddenly force this to happen?

Perhaps sensing the doubts of the crowd, Principal Chen added, "This method was conceived independently by our students and perfected with technical support from the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group. It has significant effects on stabilizing the mind, enlightening wisdom, improving concentration in cultivation, and preventing the growth of inner demons..."

"Wow! Independent concept? Who's so amazing?"

"Technical support from Huaguo Water Curtain Group? That's a big investment!"

"Defeating inner demons? That sounds impressive..."

Discussions immediately erupted in every corner.

Inside the elite class, Chen Chao nudged Guo Hao next to him: "Hey Guo, did you hear that? Our school has such a talent hidden away? How come I haven't heard a thing about it?"

Guo Hao seemed thoughtful: "Considering what happened a few days ago between Director Ma and that aunt at the school gate... the school's actions probably have a deeper meaning."

Sun Rong sat in the front row with a slight smile on her lips, glancing out of the corner of her eye at Wang Ling by the window. Wang Ling was still looking out the window, seemingly indifferent, but Sun Rong knew that this was all within his expectations.

The school radio broadcast continued: "The Lingtai Qingming Mantra will be included in the daily compulsory course, to be practiced once in the morning and once in the evening. The relevant incantations and spiritual energy circulation routes have been sent to each student's campus jade slip. Please check them in time and start practicing. The school will conduct a preliminary effect check in one week."

really......

What the school calls "voluntary" is actually "required"...

Everyone who heard this couldn't help but complain.

As soon as the broadcast ended, the entire Number 60 High School erupted into chaos.

The students took out their school jade slips and probed them with their spiritual senses, and sure enough, they found a new mantra called "Lingtai Qingming Mantra".

The incantation text is concise and accompanied by several detailed diagrams illustrating the flow of spiritual energy, making it easy for even lower-grade students to understand.

"Focus your mind on your dantian, clear your mind, external evils will not invade, and your original heart will be firmly established... It sounds pretty convincing." A student recited softly.

"It feels like an upgraded version of the basic meditation mantra? But the circulation path is more subtle."

"Who cares? If the school tells us to practice, then we'll practice. It won't hurt."

Most students began trying it out of curiosity and a sense of obedience.

However, for a minority of people, the situation is quite different.

In the cafeteria, the cafeteria lady marked by Wang Muyu felt the students around her begin to try to circulate their faint Clarity Mantra. Her hand holding the large ladle trembled violently, and the ladle of spirit rice she scooped up spilled out.

The dormant, gray aura within her seemed to be scorched by the sunlight, giving rise to a subtle restlessness.

In the logistics department, the maintenance worker in the tool room suddenly turned pale and instinctively clutched his chest.

In the back row of the elite class, the boy with his head down had his hands clenched tightly under the desk, his nails almost digging into his flesh.

When the students around them curiously began to practice drawing spiritual energy according to the instructions on the jade slip.

He felt a disgusting "clean" force permeating the air, making the foreign sensation in his body clearer and more painful than ever before!

Wang Muyu sat next to Sun Rong, his face full of focus.

He could clearly see that as more and more students began to try the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra", the entire classroom, and even the entire campus, seemed to be shrouded in an incomparably pure "halo".

The glow made him feel very comfortable, as if he were soaking in a hot spring filled with medicinal fragrance.

But at the same time, he also pinpointed the sources of those anomalies once again.

In this gradually becoming purer environment, their "stains" appear even more glaring, almost completely exposed.

"Mother!"

Wang Muyu whispered, "Those bad guys don't seem to like this spell."

Sun Rong felt reassured and replied, "Yes, this means that Li Jun's spell is effective."
