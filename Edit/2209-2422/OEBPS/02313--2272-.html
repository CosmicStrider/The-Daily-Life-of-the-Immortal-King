Chapter 2272 Silent Annihilation

The dazzling light emitted by Bai Zhe's glass clone expanded rapidly, and the terrifying energy fluctuations resembled a volcano about to erupt.

This aura was suppressed, yet it clearly foreshadowed a cataclysmic self-destruction in the next second!

The cracks on the glass shell had spread to its entire body, exuding unstable and intense energy. Under this pressure, the entire glass martial arts arena groaned uncontrollably, the space twisted violently, and fine, dark cracks began to appear.

"Oh no! He's going to self-destruct!" Liu Qingyi's expression changed drastically, and she shouted sharply.

Her body was enveloped in a frenzied surge of purple light as she attempted to build a stronger defense, but faced with the resolute self-destruction of a being comparable to a Dao God-level avatar, even she felt her heart pounding and her strength waning.

Fang Xing remained silent, the skull scythe stuck in front of him, its dark aura expanding, attempting to absorb some of the impact.

Wang Zhen remained calm as he unleashed the power of his divine domain without reservation, transforming it into a thick shield of light to protect the others.

Sun Rong almost instinctively shifted half a step to the side, her Ao Hai Sword Qi rippling like water. It wasn't entirely for self-protection, but also subconsciously to protect Wang Ling, who seemed to be in a daze, even more.

Her heart was in her throat, not because of her own safety, but because of a deep-seated concern for Wang Ling, even though reason told her that he didn't need it at all.

"It's over! It's really over this time!" Chen Chao was pale and hugged Guo Hao, almost bursting into tears.

Li Changzhe's armor, the Ten Thousand Scales Armor, dimmed to its lowest point, and he closed his eyes in despair.

Zhang Linyan gripped the empty quiver tightly, Su Xingyue's sword tip dipped to the ground, and her face showed bitterness.

An atmosphere of despair, as real as the air itself, gripped the throat of every unsuspecting person.

However, in that critical moment, when even time seemed to freeze—

Wang Ling, the figure who was being protected by everyone, whether consciously or unconsciously, frowned very slightly.

The expression wasn't one of fear, but rather an impatience at being disturbed and cleaned up.

His right hand hung at his side, and his index finger twitched almost imperceptibly.

There was no inspiration, no fluctuation, not even a ripple in the air.

The movement was so small it was as if he had simply flicked away a non-existent speck of dust from his fingertips.

However, this extremely subtle movement seemed to pluck an invisible string in the dark.

The dazzling light of Bai Zhe's clone, which was expanding wildly and about to reach its limit, suddenly froze!

It's like someone whose heart is suddenly gripped by an invisible hand; all the momentum is forcibly choked in an instant!

Then, something happened that stunned everyone, including those in the know.

The cracked glass shell of Bai Zhe's clone did not explode as expected, but instead began to melt in a strange way, like an ice sculpture under the scorching sun.

There was no explosion, nor any shattering; instead, it silently dissolved and collapsed from the core.

The brilliant light quickly dimmed, as if it had been frantically swallowed up by some greedy black hole.

The terrifying energy fluctuations did not erupt outwards, but collapsed rapidly inwards, dragging the surrounding distorted space and those tiny black cracks together towards that invisible annihilation singularity.

This process was faster than I could have imagined!

In the blink of an eye!

The terrifying energy source that was about to destroy everything vanished into thin air right before everyone's eyes.

There was no loud noise, no shockwave, and no energy storm.

Only absolute silence remained, along with the lingering, scorching aura of spatial laws in the air.

It was as if the cataclysmic crisis just now was merely a collective illusion.

The Glazed Martial Arts Arena has returned to stability and is even cleaner than before, with even the lingering filth of the Outer God's will being wiped away.

Everyone: "??????"

Chen Chao remained in a defensive crouching position with his hands behind his head, his eyes wide open: "Explosion...it's over? Why was there no sound? It was a dud after all?"

Guo Hao released Chen Chao in a daze, touching his entire body: "I...I'm not missing any parts, am I? I feel like I've been tricked?"

Li Changzhe cautiously opened his eyes, looked at the empty center of the field, then at his perfectly intact Wanlin Armor, and murmured, "All thunder and no rain? No, not a single drop?"

Zhang Linyan and Su Xingyue looked at each other, seeing the same disbelief and deep confusion in each other's eyes.

This completely violates the common sense of cultivation!

I don't understand why such a horrific self-destruction could disappear so completely and so mysteriously...

"Is there something strange about this arena? Does it have its own energy absorption function?" a student from the Academy of Holy Science wondered tremulously, trying to explain this incomprehensible scene using scientific cultivation techniques.

"Possibly! After all, it's his own territory. Maybe there's a bug in the self-destruct program?" Another student immediately chimed in, as if grasping at a straw.

Those who were unaware of the situation began to find reasonable explanations for themselves, and all sorts of speculations emerged, filled with the bewilderment and absurdity of surviving a catastrophe.

However, for those in the know, the situation is quite different.

Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, and Liu Qingyi almost simultaneously glanced at Wang Ling with extremely subtle expressions.

Wang Zhen's lips twitched, forcibly suppressing the "Holy shit!" that was about to escape his lips, and transmitted his voice to Fang Xing and Liu Qingyi: "Did you see that? A weakened version of 'Words become Law'—'Intention cancels Law'! This doesn't even mention the conservation of energy anymore!"

Fang Xing remained silent for a moment, then replied telepathically, "To be more precise, it means reducing its existence to zero. It's not about absorption or transfer, but about direct erasure."

His skull sickle trembled slightly, the spirit of the weapon conveying an even deeper sense of awe.

At the same time, there was a deep sense of despair. He felt that the gap between himself and Wang Ling was getting bigger and bigger. How long would it take for him to cultivate before he could barely catch up with Wang Ling?

Liu Qingyi rolled her eyes and transmitted her voice, "Well, Bai Zhe is probably so angry that he's vomiting blood. His carefully prepared self-destruction was gone without even a sound."

The look in her eyes when she looked at Wang Ling held a mixture of helplessness, as if he were a monster, and a hint of relief.

Ryoko Kugimiya's face was deathly pale as she clutched the nearly destroyed array plate, her fingers still trembling slightly.

Although her cultivation level was insufficient to fully comprehend what had happened, she knew that the array had just detected a fleeting, inexplicable fluctuation of rules at an alarmingly high level.

The source... she dared not think too deeply about it, but subconsciously moved closer to Liu Qingyi.

Sun Rong breathed a sigh of relief, her heart finally settling down.

She glanced at Wang Ling and noticed that he had lowered his head again and was taking out a small bottle of what looked like a condensed liquid extracted from some kind of plant from the Earth's core.

He uncorked the bottle, took a small sip, seemingly savoring the taste, his brow slightly relaxed, as if he was quite satisfied with the new flavor.

His completely indifferent demeanor, lost in his own world, both amused and reassured Sun Rong.

At the same time, Sun Rong also noticed his right hand hanging down, his index finger was bent very naturally, just like before.

...

Deep within the universe, the Dark Palace, an ancient ruin of the Dragon Clan.

"Pfft—whoa!"
