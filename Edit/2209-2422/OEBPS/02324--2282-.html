Chapter 2282 Wang Muyu Awakens

The Silence of the End in Wuji Jueqi's hand, carrying a deathly gray light that could annihilate all things, had already pierced the air and was aimed directly at Wang Muyu's heart, whose consciousness was blurred due to exhaustion.

However, in that critical moment, when the line between life and death was about to be drawn—

"Wooden fish, stand up..."

"Wooden fish, stand up..."

A call, calm to the point of indifference, yet seemingly originating from the very source of the universe, resonated deep within the soul, like a resounding bell from the depths of the cosmos, dispelling the darkness that was about to engulf Wang Muyu's consciousness.

Accompanied by Wang Ling's continuous shouts.

Wang Muyu suddenly opened his eyes!

Those golden dragon eyes, which had been dimmed by exhaustion, now shone like two stars suddenly ignited, bursting forth with an unprecedentedly brilliant light!

An indescribable, terrifying pressure, like the sudden awakening of an ancient star core that had been dormant for billions of years, erupted with a thunderous roar centered on his young body!

"Boom—!!!"

The invisible shockwave exploded in a ring shape!

Jueji's deadly shot was abruptly halted in mid-air by the impact of pure momentum, the tip of the spear trembling violently and emitting a mournful cry of unbearable strain!

"Warning! Warning! The target's energy level is abnormally high! It has broken through the threshold of Dao Ancestor! It is continuing to rise!"

"Unknown force field generated! Law structure cannot be resolved! Priority exceeds the highest database permission!"

"Threat level redefined: Cataclysmic! Evade immediately! Evade immediately!"

For the first time, the eternally cold and ruthless mechanical mind was freed from its grip, experiencing a kind of terrifying emotion.

He tried to force down the bone spear to complete the assassination, but found that the laws of space and time around him had changed!

It was no longer the Spirit Garden of the Wuji Sword Sect, but a completely unfamiliar strange space composed of Wang Muyu's pure dragon will and golden dragon might!

This is Wang Muyu's exclusive spiritual domain space—the Myriad Dragon Domain!

It was at the critical moment of life and death that he was awakened by Wang Ling's words, and after the supreme dragon blood in his body resonated with that barely perceptible but supremely powerful Immortal King's will, he instinctively unleashed his Absolute Domain!

Here, he is the ruler!

Wang Muyu floated in the center of the domain, his small body enveloped in an intense, almost overwhelming golden light. Beneath his tattered clothes, fine, seven-colored dragon scales covered his entire body, and two tender yet budding dragon horns protruded from his forehead.

He slowly raised his hand, looking at his small palm wreathed in golden arcs of electricity. A hint of confusion flashed in his eyes, which was then replaced by a profound understanding that came from the depths of his blood.

He felt his body filled with boundless power, stronger than ever before!

The feeling of being suppressed by the Annihilation Domain has completely disappeared!

"You bad guy!"

No rules, no techniques, just pure, unrestrained power!

Boom!
