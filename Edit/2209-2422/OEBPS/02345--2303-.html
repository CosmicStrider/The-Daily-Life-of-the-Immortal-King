Chapter 2303 Death Dream

Whether it's the "Taste Master" or the "Sleep Treasure," they are essentially targeted scientific cultivation products developed by that group of ancient evil cultivators to address the common cultivation pain points of lower-level cultivators.

Their infiltration plan was swift and purposeful: to quickly infiltrate most of the lower-level cultivators, turning them into believers of the Primordial World, and enabling them to play a crucial role in the upcoming War of Destiny.

The promotion of 【Sleep Treasure】is far more extensive than imagined.

Following the precise marketing of "improving sleep quality" and "cultivating in dreams," coupled with the market's somewhat distorted desire for new cultivation technology products after the "Taste Master" incident, the market has been driven to embrace these products.

This eye mask-like device has become a "necessity" for many low-level monks and stressed-out urban white-collar workers.

Even knowing about the mass incident that occurred during the Sixty High School entrance exams, many people were still willing to give it a try.

They were too eager to "progress".

Their desire for advancement, their yearning to live the life of a superior cultivator after improving their cultivation level, coupled with the increasingly competitive cultivation society, led them to selectively ignore the potential dangers.

These past few days, the nights in Songhai have fallen silent earlier than usual.

Songhai, known as the "city that never sleeps," almost comes to a standstill after nightfall.

An invisible sense of weariness enveloped the city.

In the once brightly lit office buildings, white-collar workers who used to work overtime to cultivate their spirituality no longer rely on elixirs and meditation to stay awake; instead, they have all started wearing "Sleep Treasures."

In the residential area, many families turned off their lights early and entered the dream world of "Sleep Treasure" together.

Even the night market vendors packed up early, eager to go home and participate in this beautiful dream meticulously crafted by the primordial evil cultivators.

In the short term, the user's appearance did seem to improve; dark circles disappeared and complexion became rosy.

The price they pay is that their interest in the real world drops sharply when they are awake, making them lazy and passive, as if the whole meaning of life is just to prepare for the next time they put on the [Sleep Treasure].

Their spiritual cultivation seemed to be steadily improving, but in reality, their foundation was like a dam that had been hollowed out by insects; their spiritual energy was weak and vulnerable.

The following day, Wang ordered Wang to return to Number 60 High School.

Sure enough, it was found that many students, including some from the elite classes, appeared somewhat listless.

It wasn't the fatigue after the exam, but a feeling of emptiness, as if my body had been hollowed out after staying up all night watching dramas or playing games.

The dark circles under my eyes are gone, but my body has been further depleted.

"That's strange. I slept really well last night wearing the Sleep Aid, and I even dreamed that I was a sword immortal sweeping away thousands of troops. Why do I feel more tired than after running 10,000 meters when I woke up?" Chen Chao yawned, rubbing his swollen temples, and said to Guo Hao in a muffled voice.

Guo Hao's gaze was scrutinizing: "I've heard several classmates say the same thing. Chao'er, are you sure that thing is okay? Xibei's experience is still fresh in our minds."

As one of the few clear-headed people, Guo Hao has always maintained a cautious attitude towards these kinds of scientific cultivation products that clearly have a "shortcut" style.

"No way? [Sleep Treasure] and [Taste Master] aren't from the same company. Besides, the advertisement says it's deep psionic sleep, and it's normal to feel tired and uncomfortable at first, it's a detoxification process..." Chen Chao said, but he himself became a little unsure of his words.

Sun Rong sat in the front row, listening to the conversation between the two, while also sensing the warnings coming from Ao Hai.

The fluctuations emanating from the sword spirit were clearer than yesterday; it was a feeling of powerlessness, like being trapped in a quagmire, as if a pair of vampire fangs were viciously gnawing at the flesh and blood of the young cultivators, draining their life force.

She turned around, her gaze passing over the chattering classmates, and landing on the figure by the window.

Wang Ling remained gazing out the window, as if everything outside was irrelevant to him.

But Sun Rong noticed that what he was holding in his hand today was not instant noodles, but a seemingly ordinary white pebble.

That was one of the original stones of the earth given by the spirit of the ancient land.

His fingertips were stroking the surface of the pebble very lightly, as if sensing something.

Sun Rong immediately understood.

Wang Ling is investigating.

Just then, the homeroom teacher, Mr. Pan, walked in with a stack of documents, a hint of worry on his face.

"Students, please be quiet." Teacher Pan clapped his hands. "The school has noticed that some students may have been using some sleep aids due to fatigue from exam preparation. I want to emphasize again that in the path of cultivation, the foundation is paramount. Any external objects are merely aids. Do not rely on them excessively, especially products of unknown origin."

He paused, his tone becoming more serious: "The school clinic reports a significant increase in the number of students coming in recently due to mental fatigue and spiritual weakness. The school has launched an investigation, and we urge students to be vigilant and report any discomfort immediately."

A murmur arose from the audience.

Even the school has officially issued a warning?

Sun Rong noticed that Wang Ling's fingers, which were caressing the pebble, paused slightly before he subtly put the pebble away.

He turned his head, his gaze meeting Sun Rong's, and blinked very slightly.

"Did Wang Ling make it?"

Sun Rong was certain that Wang Ling must have somehow made the school notice the anomaly.

After school, Sun Rong quickly caught up with Wang Ling, who was preparing to leave alone.

"Royal Decree~"

She spoke softly, making sure only the two of them could hear: "Is it that [Sleep Treasure]? The same as last time?"

Wang Ling didn't stop walking, but nodded: "More or less, but this time the feeling is not exactly the same as the [Taste Master]."

He unusually spoke a few more words.

"What do you need me to do?"

Sun Rong asked directly, "Can the media and research institutes under the Huaguo Water Curtain Group cooperate?"

Wang Ling remained silent for a few seconds, seemingly deep in thought.

Just when Sun Rong thought he wouldn't answer, he spoke. His voice was still faint, but it had a gentler quality than before.

"Public opinion and popular science, as well as real dream cases," he succinctly summarized.

Sun Rong instantly understood: "I understand. I will have the group's media department take action, inviting authoritative sleep and psionic experts to produce a special feature exposing the dangers of excessive reliance on dream devices and collecting real negative cases. At the same time, the research institute will analyze the 'Sleep Treasure' devices circulating in the market."

"Hmm," Wang Ling responded, acknowledging the response.

He paused, then added two words: "Be careful."

Sun Rong felt a warmth in her heart and nodded vigorously: "You too."

She knew that Wang Ling was facing more than just the battle of public opinion.

Meanwhile, the magnet, Wang Ling's spiritual consciousness, had long since become like an invisible net, enveloping the entire Songhai City.

He saw countless energy threads, imbued with a dreamlike quality, rising from every household and converging into the core of the "Dream Web" beneath the city.

This core now beats even more powerfully, like a greedy heart, not only drawing on the users' spiritual energy and consciousness, but also beginning to conduct a deeper level of selection.

It analyzes the user's gene sequence and soul fluctuations.

Just as Wang Ling thought, this plan for the Primordial World was far more than just absorbing energy.
