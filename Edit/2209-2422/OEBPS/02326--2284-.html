Chapter 2284 Wang Ming and Qin Zong's Investigation

The next day, it was time for Number 60 High School to let out.

The setting sun cast long shadows of the teaching buildings as students, laughing and chatting, poured out of the school gates, discussing the newly distributed exam papers or the new "spiritual food" set meals in the cafeteria, creating an atmosphere that was both ordinary and vibrant.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong walked among the crowd, appearing inconspicuous.

"There's a mini-test in Plant Science tomorrow. I've highlighted the key points for you." Sun Rong took out a notebook from her exquisite storage bracelet and handed it to Wang Ling, her tone natural: "The symbiotic law in Chapter 3 and the catalysis techniques in Chapter 5 are the key points. If you want to lower your score, it's best to forget these two key points; that way, you'll just barely pass."

"......"

Looking at Sun Rong, who seemed to understand him better and better, Wang Ling remained silent for a moment, then nodded and accepted the gift: "Thank you."

His gaze swept past the notebook, looking at the bustling streets in the distance. His consciousness, like an invisible spider web, had already silently spread out, instantly traversing thousands of miles of mountains and rivers, landing on the southwestern mountain range of Huaxiu, marked as the "Deadly Forbidden Zone"—the Abyss of Demise.

The energy flow there was unusually subtle, as if deliberately integrated into the violent natural spiritual pressure, yet it remained imperceptible to his perception. Several remnants of annihilating energy, like streams flowing into the sea, converged towards a point at the bottom of the abyss that was obscured by a powerful spatial barrier.

Bai Zhe's hiding place is indeed quite intriguing.

"Wang Ling?" Sun Rong noticed his momentary lapse in concentration and asked softly, "Did something happen? Do you need help?"

Wang Ling withdrew his gaze from the distance and shook his head: "No need."

He paused, seemingly feeling that two words were too brief, and added, "It's nothing."

It wasn't that he intentionally rejected Sun Rong's kindness; it was just that he felt the current situation was too dangerous.

Looking at his calm profile, Sun Rong felt her slight worry ease.

She believed in his abilities, but couldn't help feeling concerned.

This conflicting feeling is probably what liking someone is all about.

She smiled and changed the subject: "The star projector that Nuannuan said she liked last time, they released a new model that can simulate nebula collapse and rebirth. I ordered one, I'll bring it over for her to play with this weekend?"

Wang Ling imagined his younger sister babbling and her shadow dancing wildly as she hugged her new toy, and a barely perceptible smile softened at the corner of his mouth: "Mmm."

The two walked to the fork in the road, where Sun Rong's luxurious flying car was already waiting.

"See you tomorrow." Sun Rong waved and got into the car.

Wang Ling nodded, watched the flying car silently glide into the traffic, then turned around and slowly walked towards home.

His shadow was elongated in the setting sun, seemingly leisurely, but the analysis of the Abyss of Fallen Stars in his mind never stopped.

...

Meanwhile, on the southwestern border of Huaxiu Kingdom, outside the Abyss of Fallen Stars.

The scene here is perfectly aptly named—the earth is shattered, riddled with bottomless cracks, and distorted spatial forces form grayish-white turbulent currents.

The surrounding spiritual energy was thin and violent, with occasional flashes of light from ancient restrictions posing a deadly danger.

Ordinary cultivators would likely find it difficult to even maintain their flight upon reaching this point.

However, on the very edge of this desolate place, an inconspicuous ancient teleportation array, half-buried in jagged rocks, suddenly lit up with faint spatial fluctuations.

With a flash of light, two figures appeared.

The man, dressed in a casual jacket, wearing smart glasses, and holding a tablet that was constantly flashing complex data, was Wang Ming.

He rubbed his temples with his other hand, his face flushed, as if he had a slight fever.

The other person, tall and wearing combat uniforms that allowed for easy movement, carried a long metal box on his back, but had a somewhat lazy and relaxed expression. This was Qin Zong.

"Tch, this place... the spiritual energy environment is even worse than the report said." Wang Ming pushed up his glasses, and environmental analysis data flashed across the lenses.

"The spatial turbulence index exceeds the standard by 370%, there are abnormal spiritual pressure points everywhere, and there are also strong interference fields... No wonder it has been designated as a forbidden zone."

He had only mentally simulated a few infiltration plans when his brain started to heat up.

"It's alright, the wind feels quite cool." Qin Zong took a deep breath of the air filled with the aura of destruction, and surprisingly revealed a hint of contentment.

He looked around and casually pointed to a huge, rickety rock next to him: "Watch out for that rock, I think it might fall."

As soon as he finished speaking, a tiny whirlwind swept by, and the boulder made a teeth-grinding creaking sound, then... it swayed twice steadily and got stuck in a more stable position.

Wang Ming: "..."

Well, as expected of a koi fish. Its word is law; that's its basic operation.

"The target location is on the southeast side of the abyss, a straight-line distance of 35 kilometers, but it requires navigating around 17 large spatial rifts and three unstable ancient killing array remnant areas."

Wang Ming quickly reported the data: "The probability of triggering the alarm by directly forcing our way in is 98.7%. We need a more covert infiltration method."

He crouched down, his fingers touching the scorched and broken earth beneath his feet. He then picked up several fragments of strange minerals that emitted faint energy radiation and a decaying bone of an unknown behemoth. His eyes behind his glasses flashed rapidly.

"Ming-ge, what are you doing?" Qin Zong asked curiously.

"Use whatever you have on hand and assemble a means of transportation that also serves as a reconnaissance device." Without looking up, Wang Ming's hands moved like phantoms.

He guided, disassembled, and reassembled the ores, animal bones, and even the surrounding spatial turbulence in an incredible way.

The core energy source is fragments of abandoned teleportation arrays, the framework is made of hard animal bones, and strange minerals are inlaid on it to form runes. Occasionally leaking spatial power is cleverly restrained and becomes part of the power source and assembly components.

Within minutes, a strangely shaped, gray-black stealth vehicle that blended seamlessly into its surroundings, resembling a large lizard, appeared before the two.

It has no wheels and relies on anti-gravity arrays and simulated spatial fluctuations to glide along the ground. Its surface is covered with a camouflage layer that can absorb spiritual detection and optical observation.

"I call it the Abyssal Lizard."

Wang Ming patted the creation and nodded in satisfaction: "Go up, it should be able to block most conventional detection."

Qin Zongrao jumped up with great interest: "Amazing, Ming-ge! With this skill, you'll never starve anywhere."

Wang Ming ignored his teasing, got into the driver's seat, and connected his glasses to the vehicle's control system: "Hold on tight, I've already planned the route. I hope your good luck will help us avoid those unexpected 'surprises'."

"No problem," Qin Zong said with a relaxed laugh, patting the metal box behind him. "If we really run into any fools, I'll treat them to a peanut."

The "Abyssal Lizard" silently activated, like a creature that blends into the shadows, skimming along the ferocious ground and gliding into the depths of the Fallen Abyss.

The trip went more smoothly than expected.

Several times the hidden restrictions were almost triggered, but Qin Zong's "unintentional" actions averted the danger.

Either he happened to adjust his posture and knocked off a control button, causing the vehicle to subtly change direction, or a small energy burst happened in the distance, attracting the attention of any potential surveillance cameras.

Wang Ming even suspected that even if they swaggered in, they might be overlooked due to various coincidences.

Is this the power of having maximum luck?

It's like using a game cheat code.

The deeper one goes, the worse the environment becomes, but the concealed flow of annihilating energy also becomes clearer.

Finally, the abyssal lizard stopped in front of a huge crack in the mountain wall.

"The destination of the energy flow is right here."

Wang Ming looked at the detection data with a solemn expression: "Very strong spatial shielding, the internal structure cannot be scanned, the energy reaction is deliberately dispersed and disguised... but there is definitely a problem."

Deep within the crack, the light was dim, with only the howling wind and the hissing of turbulent air currents.

Qin Zong untied the metal box from his back, opened it, and inside lay a sniper rifle with a design that combined science fiction and antiquity—the Nine Yang Divine Sword, a science-upgraded version of the cultivation technique and a version enlightened by Wang Ling.

He skillfully began assembling the device and loaded a specially made bullet engraved with mysterious runes.

"Should we launch a full-scale attack?" Wang Ming asked.

"Courtesy first, then force," Qin Zong grinned.
