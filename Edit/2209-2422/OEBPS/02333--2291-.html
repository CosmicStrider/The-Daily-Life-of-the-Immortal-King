Chapter 2291 Pursuit

Deep in the universe, above the ruins of the ancient palace of the Dragon Clan, the chaotic flow of energy has not yet completely subsided.

Along with the shattered runes following the collapse of the magic circle, broken dragon bones floated haphazardly all around, making the scene extremely chaotic.

Wang Muyu was surrounded by a dazzling array of seven-colored dragon lights.

At that moment... he became light!

Like an immortal lamp lit in this desolate void.

He watched the four figures flee in panic, his small face burning with fighting spirit.

"You think you can just hit me and run away? There's no such thing as a free lunch! You even wanted to melt me down and feed me to that demon child, you scoundrels! Don't you dare leave!"

With a clear shout, his small figure transformed into a seven-colored rainbow that pierced through the sea of stars, instantly catching up to the nearest Huang Yan.

The surge in spiritual energy after devouring the Dragon God Demon Child allowed Wang Muyu to reach speeds up to thirty times the speed of light, while his perception of space was also greatly enhanced!

"You little mutated kid, don't push your luck!"

Huang Yan felt the terrifying pressure approaching from behind, and was filled with shock and anger. He immediately unleashed a World-Burning Black Flame Fist in an attempt to block it.

In the past, Wang Muyu would have been much more careful in dealing with this.

But at this moment, he simply extended one claw.

Buzz!

The claw mark formed by the condensed power of the seven-colored dragon seemed simple and unadorned, but it contained a trace of the concept of all things returning to nothingness. It directly grabbed the raging black flames in its palm and squeezed them hard!

The black flames, powerful enough to incinerate galaxies, were extinguished in an instant, like a candle flickering in the wind, vanishing completely.

The scattered energy was swept up and completely absorbed by the seven-colored dragon light surrounding Wang Muyu.

"What?!" Huang Yan's pupils constricted in disbelief.

The opponent's power not only underwent a qualitative change, but their use of psionic energy also seemed to have been unlocked, carrying a natural and domineering aura.

This little monster's growth rate is truly astonishing!

"Take this punch from me too!"

Wang Muyu gave a childish hum and, taking advantage of Huang Yan's astonishment, punched him straight in the back.

This punch had no fancy light effects, only the immense power of the seven-colored celestial ancestor-level dragon force. Wherever the fist passed, the space was directly torn apart, collapsing into a crack that kept collapsing inward!

Huang Yan was terrified and had no choice but to urgently mobilize the remaining spiritual energy in his body that had not yet recovered, pouring all his energy into the dragon scales on his back to withstand the attack!

"Boom!"

A dull, deafening roar exploded in the depths of space, transforming into ripples of psionic energy.

As if struck by a comet, Huang Yan's massive body trembled violently, and he spat out a mouthful of dragon blood. His body was thrown forward like a kite with a broken string, and his aura instantly weakened by more than half.

Fine cracks appeared on the dragon scales on his body, and eventually they fell off and were rapidly frozen in the cosmic environment, becoming space debris.

Wang Muyu's attack severely injured him!

"Radiant Flame!" Shenming cried out anxiously.

Seeing this, she and Xunfeng had no choice but to stop their escape and turn back to help.

Shenming formed hand seals, and the power of ice transformed into countless crystal-clear ice mirrors, layer upon layer, blocking Wang Muyu's pursuit route in an attempt to interfere with his perception.

At the same time, a chill that could freeze one's soul spread out.

"Trying to slow me down? Is that all?" Wang Muyu chuckled and, without further ado, shattered the barrier with a single punch.

Xunfeng moved with lightning speed, circling around to Wang Muyu's flank, his fingertips flicking.

Countless sharp spatial wind blades silently sliced through the air, their target aimed directly at Wang Muyu's vital joints.

If it were the Wang Muyu of the past, he might have been flustered in the face of such tacit cooperation.

But at this moment, he no longer relied solely on brute force. Instead, he spread spiritual energy mixed with dragon breath throughout his body, automatically forming a defensive force field with no blind spots.

Ding ding ding!

The spatial wind blades collided with the seven-colored force field, but failed to harm Wang Muyu in the slightest.

Wang Muyu pressed his advantage, his eyes blazing with golden light. He used his powerful dragon soul will to discern truth from falsehood, see through all illusions, and firmly pinpoint the location of the Deep Sea's true form. He then unleashed a condensed dragon breath directly at it.

Shenming hurriedly dodged, the dragon's breath grazing her icy blue scales, leaving a charred mark that made her face turn pale with fright.

The last Juexi who evacuated did not directly participate in the siege.

He hovered outside the battlefield, holding the Endless Silence Spear and pointing it at Wang Muyu. Data streams flashed wildly in his molten gold vertical pupils, as if he were calculating Wang Muyu's weaknesses.

"Load the cargo!" Upon seeing this, Wang Muyu scoffed inwardly.

"Jueqi! Don't just stand there! Take him down together!" Huangyan roared, barely managing to stabilize his injuries.

His eyes were filled with humiliation; he couldn't accept being driven to such a state by a child.

Jueqi shook her head, her voice as cold as ever: "The calculation failed... I suggest a tactical retreat and a longer-term plan."

"Retreat? Where to retreat! Today it's either him or me!" Huang Yan was blinded by rage. Ignoring his injuries, he gathered black flames again and was about to charge forward.

...

Meanwhile, in Songhai City, Number 60 High School.

Sun Rong walked over with her neatly organized notes and gently placed them on Wang Ling's desk: "I've highlighted the key points in red for tomorrow's quiz on the principles of alchemy. Go back and study them? I've even prepared a list of the key points for you to control your score!"

She noticed that Wang Ling seemed unusually quiet today, and even ate less instant noodles than usual.

Wang Ling nodded and took the notebook: "Thank you."

In fact, the moment he received the notebook, Wang Ling had already finished reading it and memorized all the marked content.

But he couldn't say that directly to Sun Rong, as that would make him seem too Versailles, so he accepted the notes and planned to return them the next day.

"The king's decree..."

Sun Rong hesitated for a moment, then asked in a low voice, "Are you alright? You seem to be thinking about a lot of things today."

Wang Ling paused slightly, then looked up at Sun Rong.

The girl's clear eyes held undisguised concern.

He paused for a second, shook his head, and unusually said a few more words: "It's nothing, I'm just a little sleepy."

That's not entirely a lie.

Remotely and precisely controlling a cosmic-level battle while maintaining the multiple safeguards on Wang Muyu's body is not a burden for Wang Ling, but it does require some distraction, so it's reasonable for him to show some drowsiness.

Upon hearing this, Sun Rong visibly relaxed, a smile spreading across her face: "It must be because we haven't fully recovered since returning from the inner world!"

She automatically filled in the blanks and attributed Wang Ling's abnormality to his previous adventure.

Wang Ling: "..."

He didn't refute Sun Rong, but simply nodded slightly and left with her.

The two walked out of the teaching building with the flow of people.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao had already run off arm in arm, discussing the new dungeon.

The campus under the setting sun is filled with a tranquil, everyday atmosphere.

However, the vast majority of Wang Ling's mind remained on that distant cosmic battlefield.

He watched Wang Muyu's struggle with the four dragon descendants like a top Go player scrutinizing every move on the board.

"If we pursue too deeply, we'll easily fall into a trap," Wang Ling thought to himself.

He sensed that deep within the universe, an even more malevolent will was awakening, targeting Wang Muyu, who was engaged in fierce combat.

That was the true will of the Outer God Void Nightmare, which seemed to have been stimulated by the fact that the Dragon God Demon Child had been devoured.

At the same time, he also sensed that Bai Zhe's Moonlight Dragon Power was transmitting new instructions to Jueqi through some mysterious channel.
