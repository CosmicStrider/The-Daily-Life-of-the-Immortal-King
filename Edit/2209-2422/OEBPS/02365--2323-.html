Chapter 2323 Ghost Hand

After Wang Ling's group of three left the dilapidated house that exuded an ominous, dark aura, the atmosphere became increasingly tense.

Zhou Ping stayed right behind Wang Ling, as if only in this way could he draw a tiny bit of security.

Lin Susu maintained a cold expression, but her sharp eyes constantly scanned the dilapidated courtyard and corridors around her, alert to any disturbance.

"What exactly is that black soil?"

Zhou Ping couldn't help but speak again, his voice filled with barely suppressed fear: "I always feel like my head is spinning after smelling that smell for too long."

Wang Ling did not answer.

His spiritual consciousness had already enveloped the entire Danyuan East District.

That black soil was no ordinary thing; the bone powder and pure resentment mixed in it were more like a "culture medium".

It should be used to cultivate a medicinal herb that requires an extremely shady environment to grow.

Or perhaps it was a kind of offering.

His thoughts were connected to the spiritual imprint left in the courtyard.

Just now, he saw someone return and swiftly and discreetly pick up the fragment of the alchemy furnace that he had deliberately left behind.

It's Black 201.

The man in the black robe had a lean face and gleaming eyes.

After obtaining the fragment, he did not hesitate at all, quickly hiding it in his bosom, and then left as if nothing had happened, stealthily heading towards the western area opposite to the Sutra Repository.

"What exactly is he going to do?" Wang Ling wondered to himself.

Black 201's unusual focus on the fragments of the alchemy furnace confirmed his suspicions.

Sure enough, there were people in this team who possessed information unknown to others, and they might even be the "users" of the rules.

"Let's go take a look to the west."

Wang Ling suddenly spoke, intentionally guiding the two of them.

"West?" Zhou Ping was taken aback. "Isn't that the area Black 112 is responsible for searching? If we go there, will we...?"

"The rules only say that you are not allowed to go out after sunset, but they don't say that you are not allowed to cross districts."

Lin Susu interrupted coldly, seemingly sensing something as well, her eyes sharply scanning the west direction: "Follow him." She gestured towards Wang Ling, though displeased, her intuition telling her that this silent gray-clad 999 might be more reliable than those loud and boisterous black-robed figures.

The three changed direction and headed west along the overgrown stone path.

The further west you go, the stronger and more pungent the smell of medicine becomes, even mixed with a faint, almost imperceptible, burnt smell.

...

The western area was even more dilapidated than the eastern area, with large swathes of buildings completely collapsed, leaving only ruins.

In the center of a relatively open area of ruins, they saw the members of Black Group 112, who were standing there blankly, seemingly discussing something.

Upon closer inspection, Zhou Ping saw that Hei 112, along with his two gray-clad team members, were agonizing over the remains of a massive alchemy furnace that was half-buried underground.

The cauldron was about the height of a person, made of bronze, and its surface was covered with green rust and spiderweb-like cracks. There was also a huge hole in the belly of the cauldron, which meant it had long been abandoned.

"No, I can't sense any spirituality at all; it's just a lifeless object."

Black 112 sighed and punched the furnace wall with his fist, making a dull sound.

"Brother 112, we haven't found anything here either." One of his gray-clad team members reported dejectedly, "There are only weeds and stones, not even a decent spiritual plant."

When Black 112 saw Wang Ling and the other two, he frowned slightly: "Why did you come here? Have you finished searching the east side?"

"I've made a discovery and wanted to come and verify it," Wang Ling said succinctly, his gaze falling on the damaged alchemy furnace.

Lin Susu crossed her arms and chuckled, "Looks like things are going pretty well here too."

Black 112's expression turned somewhat unpleasant. Ignoring her sarcasm, he instead asked, "What have you discovered?"

Wang Ling did not answer directly, but walked to the alchemy furnace, squatted down, and ran his fingers along the cracks on the furnace wall.

Under his spiritual perception, there was a trace of extremely weak spiritual energy fluctuation inside the alchemy furnace, which was somewhat similar to the trace of "soul fire nurturing" recorded in the book "Secrets of Resurrection", but obviously more violent.

"This furnace didn't break naturally," Wang Ling suddenly said.

Everyone was taken aback.

"What do you mean?" Black112 pressed.

"It was forcibly ruptured from the inside."

Wang Ling pointed to the edge of the largest hole in the furnace belly: "Traces, curled outwards."

Zhou Ping went over for a closer look and exclaimed, "It really is! It looks like something exploded out of there!"

This discovery sent chills down everyone's spine.

It was forcibly ruptured and exploded from the inside of the alchemy furnace? What kind of pill was being refined? The marks from an ordinary furnace explosion shouldn't be like this, with obvious outward-curving explosion marks.

Recalling the record of "living beings as a guide" in "The Secret of Resurrection", a terrible conjecture arose in everyone's mind.

"Could it be... that it's..." A gray-clad man's voice trembled, and he dared not continue.

"Shut up!" Black 112 shouted sternly, but his eyes were also filled with surprise and uncertainty.

Just then, Wang Ling suddenly sensed a wave coming from deeper within the ruins.

The fluctuation carried a familiar chill, subtly resonating with the black soil in his sleeve and the fragments of the alchemy furnace in Black 201's arms.

"Over there." Wang Ling stood up and pointed in the direction from which the ripples came.

It was a shadowy area shrouded by huge, strange rocks and withered ancient trees.

Black112 hesitated for a moment, but with few clues at the moment, any anomaly was worth investigating.

He gritted his teeth: "Let's go check it out! Everyone be careful!"

The group carefully traversed the ruins, parting the tangled withered vines, and the sight before them made everyone gasp in shock.

This is a secluded mountain valley. The ground is not soil, but a strange black soil that exudes a fishy smell.

The area was not large, but dozens of fragments of alchemy furnaces of varying sizes were scattered on the black soil.

They are not scattered randomly, but rather subtly form an incomplete circular pattern.

In the center of the design, Black 201 is crouching on the ground with his back to them, holding a piece of metal he picked up from the yard, seemingly comparing something.

Beside him was a small cloth bag, from which clumps of equally dark earth could be seen.

Hearing the noise behind him, Black 201 turned around abruptly, a hint of panic flashing across his face, but it was quickly replaced by ferocity: "What are you doing here?"

"That's what we should be asking you!" Black 112 strode forward, his sharp eyes fixed on the shards in his hand and the cloth bag on the ground: "Black 201, what are you doing sneaking around here? What's with these shards and the black soil?"

Black 201 stood up, his eyes sweeping over the crowd with a sinister gaze, pausing for a moment on Wang Ling's face: "I've found some clues and am studying them. What, is that not allowed?"

"Research? You secretly picked up the fragments and came here alone to research them?" Lin Susu sneered, "I think you have something to hide! Was the death last night related to you?"

"Bullshit!" Black 201 retorted sharply. "What evidence do you have?"

"Evidence?" Black 112 pointed to the fragmented patterns and black soil on the ground: "How do you explain these things? Are the etiquette mentioned in 'The Secret of Resurrection' related to these?"

The atmosphere instantly became tense, and suspicious gazes refocused.

This time, however, the target has changed to Black 201.
