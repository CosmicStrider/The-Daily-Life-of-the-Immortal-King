Chapter 2262 Migration

Inside the underground cave, the atmosphere was solemn like never before.

The heavy pressure from the previous mournful cries of the earth veins had not yet completely dissipated, and Wang Ling's words, "It hurts a lot," weighed heavily on the hearts of those who knew the truth.

Without needing further prompting, everyone sprang into action as quickly as possible.

With the help of Ryoko Kugimiya and several students from the St. Science Academy, the logistics team efficiently organized the supplies that had accumulated in the cave over the past period.

The Jade Heart Fruit was carefully picked and packaged, and various minerals and spiritual herbs collected from the earth's core were categorized.

Even Wang Ling's specially made mineral recliner and workbench were carefully stored away by Banshan, as Sun Rong had indicated—she knew Wang Ling was used to such things.

Although Chen Chao and Guo Hao were still recovering from their injuries, they did their best to help with packing.

The usual playful smiles were gone from their faces, replaced by tension about the unknown road ahead and trust in their companion.

Li Changzhe and Zhang Linyan volunteered to take on the task of perimeter security. The two of them, one at a distance and the other at a distance, spread out their spiritual senses to the maximum extent, alerting themselves to any possible unusual activity.

The malicious attack and the abnormal movement of the earth's veins just now made them realize that this place had become a place of trouble.

After taking the pill and finishing her meditation, Su Xingyue led the students from the Holy Science Academy to check their respective psionic equipment to ensure they were ready for battle at any time.

Their gazes toward the students of Number 60 High School were complex, a mixture of gratitude, awe, and an indescribable curiosity.

Among all their elite high schools, No. 60 High School, although a newly emerging school in recent years, is indeed the one with the lowest overall ranking.

However, they never expected that this group of people could lead them through the crisis.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing stood at the entrance of the abandoned passageway leading to Thousand Caves City, exchanging opinions in hushed tones.

The skull sickle in Fang Xing's hand emitted a faint hum from time to time, sensing the more subtle energy flow deep within the passage.

"The psionic turbulence in the passage is more active than before," Fang Xing said in a deep voice. The shadow under his hood obscured his expression, but his tone was grave. "It's like it's been stirred up by something."

Wang Zhen stroked his chin, the nonchalant smile on his face fading slightly: "It seems our move might have disturbed some neighbors. Hopefully, it's just the aftershocks of the earth's veins."

Sun Rong put the last batch of sorted supplies into her storage magic item and walked to Wang Ling's side.

He still stood there, his gaze seemingly fixed on a point in the void, his fingers unconsciously twirling an unopened packet of instant noodles.

"Wang Ling, we are all ready," Sun Rong said softly, her voice carrying a hint of barely perceptible dependence.

Wang Ling focused his gaze on her face and nodded very slightly.

He glanced at the now-empty cave dwelling, his gaze lingering for a moment on the empty space where the recliner had once been placed, before he took the lead and headed towards the entrance of the passageway.

His steps remained steady, yet they subtly gave everyone a boost of confidence.

"Let's go!" Wang Zhen shouted in a low voice, and the other party gave him a wink.

Fang Xing understood, pointed the skull sickle forward, and a ghostly light flowed along the blade. An invisible force field spread out, temporarily stabilizing the restless psionic turbulence at the entrance.

He was the first to step into the dark passage.

Wang Zhen followed closely behind, followed by Sun Rong, Liu Qingyi, Jiu Gong Liangzi and other members of the exploration team. Then Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe were responsible for support, with the logistics team and the wounded in the middle, and Su Xingyue leading the people from the Holy Science Academy to cover the rear.

Wang Ling's figure quietly blended into the middle of the team, inconspicuous yet seemingly a silent anchor for the entire group.

The Mountain-Moving Beast shrank in size, following Sun Rong's feet like a large pet. Its huge single eye nervously looked around, its instinctive perception of danger being more acute than that of many people.

The passage was rugged and difficult to traverse, and the temperature was significantly higher than inside the cave. The air was filled with the smell of sulfur and some kind of burnt mineral.

The illumination runes cast by Ryoko Kugimiya could only illuminate a limited area; beyond the light lay an impenetrable darkness that seemed poised to engulf everything at any moment.

Footsteps, breathing, and the sound of rubble rolling echoed in the narrow space, further emphasizing the deathly silence around them.

Everyone held their breath and focused intently.

After walking for about the time it takes for an incense stick to burn, apart from the occasional jolts caused by the psionic turbulence, they did not encounter any real danger.

Just as Chen Chao was slightly relaxing his tense nerves and was about to whisper something to Guo Hao next to him,

Waaah—!

A deep, resonant horn sound, seemingly from the farthest reaches of the earth, pierced through the rock strata without warning and entered everyone's minds!

This sound did not travel through the air, but acted directly on the soul, carrying an ancient sense of sorrow and... a sense of summoning.

"What was that sound?!" Guo Hao was horrified and almost crushed the illumination talisman in his hand.

Everyone was startled and stopped in their tracks, looking warily at the dark rock walls around them.

"Is it an echo from the earth's veins?" Zhang Linyan said uncertainly, gripping her longbow tighter.

"It doesn't seem like..." Li Changzhe said gravely, "This voice... has directionality!"

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing exchanged a glance, their expressions changing slightly.

"It's the Horn of the Earth's Core!" Wang Zhen's gaze darkened, and he lowered his voice to a whisper as he said, "I've read about it in ancient books. Legend has it that deep within the Earth's core, some ancient guardian tribes or lost civilizations would sound this horn in times of great upheaval or invasion by foreign enemies, to warn or summon people!"

"Summon? Summon what?" Liu Qingyi pressed, her body shimmering with purple light.

No one can answer.

But the answer soon became clear.

Click... Click...

From all directions, the rock walls of the passage began to emit a teeth-grinding cracking sound!

Immediately afterward, at the edge of the light from the illumination runes, pairs of crimson, murky eyes lit up like will-o'-the-wisps!

Densely packed, countless!

The owners of these eyes slowly crawled out from cracks in the rock walls, from shadowy corners, and even from caves overhead.

They came in various shapes and sizes, and most of them were subterranean creatures that none of the people present had ever seen before!

There were lava borers like the ones we'd encountered before, but they were larger, their shells gleaming with a dark, blood-red light.

There were also faceless ghosts, like shadows coalescing, emanating a chilling aura that sucked away spiritual energy.

And a monster covered in hard stone spikes, resembling a pangolin but a hundred times more ferocious.

Their races and habits seem completely different, but at this moment, their eyes are shining with the same light.

This is a kind of utterly enraged, relentless madness.

It was as if all the creatures in the Earth's core had united because of that horn, determined to tear these intruding "outsiders" to shreds!

"Holy crap..."

Chen Chao stared at the massive army of subterranean creatures surging towards him like a tidal wave, his scalp tingling and his legs trembling: "This...this is disturbing a hornet's nest! No! It's disturbing the hornet's lair! My trypophobia is acting up!"

"Prepare for battle!" Zhang Linyan shouted sharply, and his longbow was instantly drawn to its full extent!

"Form a formation! Defensive formation!" Ryoko Kugimiya reacted extremely quickly. The array plate shone brightly, and a pale golden light screen instantly opened up, protecting the team in the center.

Bang bang bang!

Countless subterranean creatures fearlessly crashed into the light barrier, producing a dull, deafening roar that caused the barrier to shake violently!

The battle erupts instantly!
