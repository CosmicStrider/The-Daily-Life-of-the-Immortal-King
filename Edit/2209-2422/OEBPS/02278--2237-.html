Chapter 2237 The Spirit Hub Matrix and the Noise Outside the Wall

Fujiro's meaningful gaze, like a lingering ailment, remained in the hearts of the students at Number 60 High School even after they left the oppressive central hall.

Zhuo Yi led the way, guiding everyone through corridors constructed of flowing runic pipes and sturdy alloys, until they reached the rest area named "Spirit Pivot Matrix".

In stark contrast to the rugged and austere style of the main base, this place reveals a unique artistic conception where cultivation civilization and future technology intertwine.

The enormous dome mimics a starry dreamscape, with specks of light flickering like stars. The ground is not made of cold alloy, but is paved with a special material that is warm and smooth like jade and can deform slightly, making it feel like stepping on a resilient and warm jade.

The clearly defined areas extend around the central hub like flower petals. Each university's private rest area is separated by a semi-transparent energy membrane, ensuring privacy while allowing a glimpse of what's happening in the neighboring areas.

The area is lined with streamlined, independent rest cabins, all made of matte silver-gray metal and warm, spiritual wood. The cabin walls are engraved with intricate spirit-gathering patterns, harmoniously blending a sense of technology with the charm of cultivation.

"Wow, this place is nice!" Chen Chao whistled, rubbing his sore shoulders. Although the impact of Teng Luchen's spiritual pressure had been partially neutralized by the monk Jin Deng, the residual power was still there.

Guo Hao curiously reached out and touched the energy membrane that isolated his area, feeling a slight elasticity and a flow of spiritual energy through his fingers.

Zhuo Yi stopped, a jade talisman in his hand lighting up and projecting a holographic image: "Everyone, this is your rest area for the next few days of training. [Spirit Hub Matrix Xuan Character Area], each person has an independent rest cabin, equipped with a basic spirit gathering array, information access port, small psionic food printer, and emergency treatment unit. The cabin walls are soundproof, spyproof, and protective, and theoretically, once the internal protection is activated, it provides protection equivalent to the strength of a late-stage Core Formation cultivator. The numbers above the cabin entrance correspond to your information bracelets."

His gaze swept over the crowd. "Starting tomorrow, we will conduct three pressure tests: gravity collapse, spiritual pressure penetration, and the geomagnetic maze. Get yourself ready tonight. Vice Principal Huo Ding..."

He looked at the monk Jin Deng: "Please come with me, I need to take you to the area where the accompanying teachers will be arranged."

"Alright." Monk Jin Deng smiled and nodded. Before leaving, he said to everyone, "Everyone, get some rest and conserve your energy."

Wang Ling's face remained expressionless, only nodding almost imperceptibly. Sun Rong's gaze, however, secretly followed his retreating figure, her heart pounding restlessly in her chest.

When Zhuo Yi shared the image of the rest cabin to everyone's wristbands, Sun Rong immediately noticed an arrangement that made her heart race – her "Xuan-07" cabin was right next to Wang Ling's "Xuan-08" cabin!

An indescribable joy welled up in my heart, and a blush rose to my ears.

She tried hard to control her facial expressions, pretending to casually check her position with Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi next to her, but her heart was pounding so hard it felt like it was going to burst out of her chest.

"It's right next door...it's actually right next door!" This realization filled her with an indescribable mix of anticipation and nervousness for the night to come.

Everyone went to their respective cabins.

The cabin door slid open silently, revealing a simple yet comfortable interior space. Soft, adaptive lighting illuminated the space, filling the air with a subtle, refreshing atmosphere.

Sun Rong stepped into her "Xuan-07" cabin.

When the cabin door closed silently and the inner light-protecting membrane activated, the excitement of knowing there was someone in the next cabin subsided slightly.

The unusual scene I had observed in the central hall before resurfaced in my mind.

"The fog above Teng Luchen's head...why is it also fog?" Sun Rong leaned against the cabin wall, her fingertips unconsciously twisting the hem of her clothes.

She clearly remembered the gray mist above her head when she looked in the mirror, and Wang Ling had the same thing above his head. She had secretly observed it before they were on the Immortal Boat.

Now, this old man Teng, who is full of suspicion towards them, especially Wang Ling, is also shrouded in a similar, incomprehensible fog!

What does this mean?

Is the state of being shrouded in mist exclusive to her and Wang Ling?

What does this fog represent?

The relationship hasn't even started yet?

Does Teng Luchen, as a veteran cultivator, really have no past romantic or marital relationships?

Could the fog above his head also signify some kind of "gap period"?

But Sun Rong always felt that this explanation was too far-fetched when applied to Teng Luchen.

Those who are unable to establish deep emotional connections? In other words, "loveless" or "emotionally challenged"? That seems closer.

Teng Luchen's dedication to his career, his obsession with Wang Ling, and his willingness to do anything to achieve his goals make it seem as if he is unable to form truly close emotional bonds with others. He is an extremely rational pragmatist.

Is it some kind of special contract or oath? Or perhaps it's related to some kind of being?

For example, a higher power, a mysterious organization, or even the mysterious artifact or plan itself has achieved a bond that goes far beyond personal emotions.

This conjecture sent chills down Sun Rong's spine.

She had a vague feeling that the third possibility might point to a deeper truth.

Teng Luchen's obsession with Wang Ling is probably not just out of curiosity or a sense of duty; it may be entangled with a huge secret and constraints.

The mist above his head seemed to be a deeper shade of gray than his own... Could this subtle difference also represent a different state?

Just as Sun Rong was deep in thought, feeling increasingly that there were many hidden secrets behind the fog, Wang Ling was also quietly thinking in the next cabin.

Wang Ling sat cross-legged on a specially made meditation cushion in the rest area, seemingly resting with his eyes closed, but his brain was actually working at high speed.

Teng Luchen's direct probing today, especially that final, ruthless attack that disregarded his status, has clearly made the words "imminent" blatantly obvious.

The old man's suspicions have reached their peak, and he will not give up.

The stress test was clearly a pretext; its true purpose was to force him to reveal his weaknesses.

Using your own body to withstand the test? The risk is too great, almost equivalent to self-destruction.
