Chapter 2401 The Date is Underway

"......"

Inside the Time Capsule Room, Sun Rong stared at Wang Ling for several seconds before swallowing back her complaint, "What kind of agreement is this?"

She suddenly realized that this was very much in line with Wang Ling's style.

Take her to try a new flavor of instant noodles.

For Wang Ling, this was probably the highest level of invitation he could receive.

After all, "trying new flavors of instant noodles" would definitely be in the top three of this man's list of life's pleasures.

"Well... it's a deal." Sun Rong couldn't help but smile. "One year from now, you have to find the newest and best-tasting instant noodles."

Wang Ling nodded: "Mm."

The kind that takes it very seriously.

After recording, the two imported the audio file into a specially made time capsule.

It was a transparent crystal the size of a walnut, with fine, flowing patterns of light inside.

Then an NPC staff member pushed open the door, took the two crystals, placed them in an old-fashioned wooden box, and handed each of them a voucher card: "It is set to open in one year. At that time, you can use this card to collect it at the amusement park, or choose the mail service."

Sun Rong carefully put the card away.

Wang Ling also put the card into his pocket and placed it in the bag with the crispy bread.

Bracelet vibration:

Complete the Time Capsule House Challenge

[Obtained 1 stamp]

Current total number of seals: 18/20

Only two stamps are needed.

"Two more are needed!" Sun Rong's eyes lit up. "Wang Ling, can we collect them all today?"

Wang Ling glanced at the time: 3:10 PM.

"Can."

His voice was calm, but it carried a certain certainty.

The two walked out of the small house.

Chen Chao patiently squatted at the doorway, and as soon as they came out, he jumped up and asked, "How was it? What heartfelt confession did you record?"

Sun Rong blushed: "It's... just a regular agreement."

"What agreement? Tell me!" Chen Chao's gossip instincts were bursting.

Wang Ling took two pieces of instant noodles out of his pocket, stuffed one into Chen Chao's mouth: "Eat."

Chen Chao's mouth was gagged, and he could only chew with "mmm" sounds, temporarily stopping.

Sun Rong tactfully seized the opportunity to change the subject: "We still need two more stamps, what are we going to play next?"

Chen Chao swallowed a mouthful of instant noodles and immediately switched into strategist mode: "Let me think... We've already played on the maze, roller coaster, drop tower, and glass bridge... Of the remaining rides, the 'carousel' is too childish, the 'bumper cars' are too crowded, and we went to the 'haunted house' yesterday..."

He flipped through the amusement park map, then his eyes lit up: "I've got it! 'Psychic Shooting Range' and 'Rune Puzzle House'! Neither of these activities is tiring, and you can get stamps from both! The shooting range tests your eyesight and hand-eye coordination, while the puzzle house tests your brainpower—Reiko, these are both your strengths!"

Wang Ling glanced at the locations of the two projects on the map; both were in the western district and not far apart.

"Let's go to the shooting range first," he said.

"Let's go, let's go!"

...

The psionic shooting range is built on the edge of the park, modeled after an ancient hunting ground. The area is spacious and features moving targets, fixed targets, and various flying and running psionic projections.

A leaderboard stands at the entrance, displaying the highest score of the day.

The current first place score is 987 rings (out of 1000).

"Wow, not bad." Chen Chao glanced at the rules: "Each person gets ten psionic bullets to shoot at a moving target. A score of 900 or above gets a stamp, and a score of 950 or above gets an extra hidden stamp."

Sun Rong glanced at the detailed rules on the flyer and asked with some concern, "Will it work? The speed of these moving targets is set to the difficulty level of the Golden Core stage..."

After all, Ao Hai has chosen to remain silent in order to "assist," and without Ao Hai's assistance, Sun Rong is really unsure of herself.

"It's alright!" Chen Chao patted his chest. "With Lingzi here! Lingzi, you should guide Sun Rong a bit later! Teach her step by step!"

He made faces as he spoke.

Wang Ling ignored his hints and walked straight to the registration desk.

The staff member was a capable-looking woman with short hair. She paused for a moment when she saw Wang Ling.

This morning, the supervisor specifically instructed that if Wang Ling were to come and play, all equipment should be set to the highest precision mode, but the score calculation should be performed according to the normal standard.

Although she didn't understand why, she did as instructed.

"Would the three of you like to play together?" the staff member asked.

"Me and her." Wang Ling pointed to himself and Sun Rong.

Chen Chao readily agreed: "I'll just watch! I'll cheer you on!"

After registration, the two were taken to the firing range.

The firing platform is semi-open, with a wide simulated forest in front of it. Targets will suddenly appear from all directions and stay for only three to five seconds.

"Please wear psychic goggles." The staff handed over two pairs of glasses: "After wearing them, you can see the weak areas of the target, and you will score higher if you hit the weak points."

Wang Ling put on his glasses.

The scene before my eyes changed immediately.

Many pale red dots appeared in the forest; those were the possible locations where the targets might appear.

At the same time, the edge of the field of view displays information such as the scoreboard and remaining ammunition.

These are very common augmented reality technologies, but in Wang Ling's eyes, they are as rudimentary as children's toys.

He can even see at a glance the order in which all the targets appear, their movement trajectories, and the precise coordinates of their weak points.

However, he still listened patiently to the staff's explanation.

"Are you ready?" the staff member asked.

Sun Rong took a deep breath and picked up the psionic gun.

The psionic gun resembles a retro flintlock pistol, but it is lighter and the recoil has been specially treated, making it suitable for beginners.

"Ready," she said.

Wang Ling nodded as well.

"Start!"

Countdown: 3 seconds.

Deep in the forest, the first target is launched!

It was a projection of a phantom deer, leaping and weaving among the trees.

Sun Rong quickly aimed, but the deer was too fast; the target disappeared as soon as she locked on.

"Missed it..." she lamented.

Wang Ling did not fire.

He was waiting for Sun Rong to try first.

The second target appears: a flying phantom bird, its trajectory elusive.

Sun Rong aimed carefully and pulled the trigger.

The psionic bullets were launched, leaving pale blue trails in the air.

Hit!

But it only hit the bird's wing, scoring 72 points.

"Not bad!" Chen Chao shouted from the observation area, "Keep going!"

The third target, the fourth...

Sun Rong gradually found her rhythm and fired all ten bullets, ultimately scoring 876 rings.

"Almost 900..." she said with some regret.

The staff member smiled and said, "That's already very good. Many veterans are only at this level."

Sun Rong put down her gun and looked at Wang Ling: "Wang Ling, it's your turn."

Wang Ling raised his gun.

The movements were very casual, without even a standard shooting posture; he just held it casually.

The countdown has begun.

The first target bounces out.

Wang Ling didn't even look at the scope before firing a shot.

"Bang!"

The bullet struck the Phantom Deer's forehead precisely in a vulnerable area—a red dot less than two centimeters in diameter.

Scoreboard jumps: 100 rings.

"Wow!" The staff member's eyes widened.

The second target, the flying mirage bird.

Wang Ling still fired a shot casually.

Another weakness.

Another 100 rings.

The third one, the fourth one...

Wang Ling fired faster and faster, pulling the trigger almost the instant the target appeared, without any aiming process.

But every shot hit the weak point precisely.

"100, 100, 100..."

The numbers on the scoreboard were jumping wildly.

Chen Chao, watching from the observation area, was dumbfounded: "Lingzi...when did you learn marksmanship?"

The ninth target – this time three popped out at the same time, moving rapidly from different directions.

Wang Ling fired three shots in quick succession.

"Bang bang bang!"

The three bullets were fired almost simultaneously, leaving three intersecting blue streaks in the air.

All weaknesses were hit.

Three 100-rings.

The last target is the BOSS-level Phantom Giant Bear, which not only moves quickly, but also has its weak points that randomly flash in different parts of its body.

Players typically need to predict where the weak points will appear, or cover them with concentrated firepower.

Wang Ling glanced at the giant bear for a second.

Then he fired.
