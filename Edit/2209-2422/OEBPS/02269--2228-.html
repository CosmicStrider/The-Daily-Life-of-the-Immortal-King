Chapter 2228 Sun Rong's Nervousness

Liu Qingyi is a well-known female cultivator who is outspoken and righteous.

Although she has always been kind and helpful, she also has her own principles and rarely interferes in other people's affairs.

However, the situation is different in coffee shops today.

Liu Qingyi noticed the uneasy male cultivator at a glance. In his youthful face and his eyes full of confusion and helplessness, she saw her own past self.

In an instant, memories of the past flooded my mind like a tide.

By the time she came to her senses, she had already successfully broken up this fake couple, whose relationship revolved around a love-struck mind, thanks to her keen insight and eloquent words.

"Do you think this Li Yuanyuan ever liked that boy?" At this moment, Sun Rong, who was watching the show in Jiugong on the second floor of the coffee shop, couldn't help but murmur softly, her fair hand gently supporting her chin, her eyes full of doubt.

She and Ryoko Kugimiya watched helplessly as Li Yuanyuan, whose thoughts had been completely exposed, frantically picked up all sorts of couple rings from the floor and table. After finishing, she fled the café like a startled fawn, leaving only a trail of hurried footsteps behind.

"I don't think so." Ryoko Kugimiya shook her head slightly. She was straightforward and spoke her mind to Sun Rong without reservation.

Just as Liu Qingyi said, Li Yuanyuan's purpose in love was only for material things and for her own cultivation resources.

Whenever she interacted with others, her eyes always gleamed with a longing for wealth and cultivation resources.

But how long can this kind of relationship, which is based on material things, last?

For Li Yuanyuan herself, the value of these boyfriends was only whether they could continue to provide her and her family with a stable supply of cultivation resources. It was nothing more than squeezing one dry and waiting for the next one to appear.

As she spoke, Ryoko Kugimiya picked up the spiritual tea on the table, took a sip, and the aroma of the tea spread in her mouth, but it couldn't hide the disdain in her words.

The couple's rings scattered all over the table and floor shimmered in the sunlight streaming through the window. To Li Yuanyuan, they were nothing more than spoils of war obtained after toying with other people's feelings.

Upon hearing this, a hint of surprise flashed across Sun Rong's fair face.

Then, she curled her lips and couldn't help but laugh: "I didn't expect you to see things so clearly, Liangzi. It's true that things change after you have a partner. It seems that Senior Brother Zhuoyi has helped you grow a lot."

"Hmph, I figured it all out myself." Kugimiya Ryoko turned her head away haughtily, her cheeks slightly flushed. Although Sun Rong's words felt like a tease, Ryoko didn't seem to mind. She lightly flicked her sleeve, instead looking at Sun Rong with a mischievous grin: "As for you, how far have things progressed between you and Wang Ling?"

These words struck a nerve with Sun Rong, instantly turning the relaxed atmosphere somewhat gloomy.

Sun Rong's smile froze on her face, and a hint of loneliness flashed in her eyes.

Ryoko Kugimiya panicked slightly, realizing her behavior was a bit like "a well-fed man not knowing a hungry man's hunger." She quickly coughed lightly, trying to smooth things over, "This young lady... didn't mean to mock you, this is just necessary concern, you understand?"

"It's alright, Liangzi." Sun Rong steadied herself, took a deep breath, and reorganized her thoughts.

At least now she knows what the countdowns above the heads of all the different kinds of monks she can see are all about. However, the distinction between the gold and red colors of the countdown still needs further verification.

At this moment, Liu Qingyi also went upstairs again and came to Sun Rong's side. Her steps were light, bringing a breeze, and her hair fluttered gently: "By the way, Miss Sun Rong, I'm quite curious about this countdown."

"What are your thoughts, Sister Qingyi?" Sun Rong looked up, her eyes full of anticipation.

"Can you see your own countdown?" Liu Qingyi tilted her head slightly, her eyes sparkling with inquiry. "Since there's a difference between gold and red countdowns, I think a red countdown definitely indicates an emotional crisis and instability between the couple, so once the countdown reaches zero, the relationship will end. But if we think about it the other way around, does a gold countdown mean that if the countdown ends, they'll...get married? Like, tie the knot..."

"Married!?" When Ryoko Kugimiya heard Liu Qingyi's guess, her mouth dropped open and her eyes widened like bronze bells. She was even more shocked than Sun Rong.

So far, although her relationship with Zhuo Yi has been quite good and they've entered a stable romantic relationship, she hasn't fully considered marriage yet. Kugimiya Ryoko looked shy, tightly gripping an exquisite folding fan in both hands, trying to cool her flushed face quickly: "Who wants to marry that workaholic?! I don't want to!"

As soon as she finished speaking, Sun Rong noticed that the countdown timer above Ryoko Kugimiya's head had shrunk a little more...

Um......

Just as Liu Qingyi had guessed, this golden countdown was actually a countdown to entering the marriage hall.

Although Ryoko Kumiya said no, she actually had a strong desire to get married... As expected of her, she was as proud and aloof as ever.

Faced with Liu Qingyi's question, Sun Rong shook her head: "If I want to see a countdown, I'd at least have to be in a confirmed romantic relationship. The three male cultivators in this cafe who aren't showing a countdown are definitely still single. I looked in the mirror while washing up before I came to see you, and there was no countdown. It's just a mystery."

"A cloud of mist?" Liu Qingyi raised her eyebrows slightly, like willow leaves swaying in the spring breeze, feeling that things were becoming increasingly interesting. "Since you can't see the countdown when you're single, then why is there a cloud of mist above your head?" She looked at Sun Rong with a sly expression: "Why don't you check above Ling Zhenren's head in a couple of days? Is there a countdown? Is it golden, red, or... a cloud of mist too?"

"Ah...this, isn't that inappropriate?" Sun Rong hesitated, biting her lower lip, her eyes full of conflict.

"Aren't you curious?" Liu Qingyi grinned mischievously, a hint of slyness in her smile. "Everyone says that Ling Zhenren is a blockhead, but maybe he's just pretending? If there's a countdown timer above his head, it means he already has a girl he's dating... If he's a complete mystery like you, then I think we should continue to investigate what this mystery really means."

"With other... girls?" Sun Rong frowned, her heart leaping into her throat. She became nervous and unconsciously gripped the hem of her skirt.
