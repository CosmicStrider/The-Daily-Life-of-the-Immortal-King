Chapter 2248 He's just a mascot!

As soon as Guo Hao finished speaking, both of their hearts sank. Guo Hao's words struck at Chen Chao's biggest worry.

Wang Ling!

That little mascot who was barely breathing in the medical hall, severely injured and exhausted by Teng Luchen's spiritual pressure!

That little mascot they carefully helped into the rest area and eventually exited the stress test!

"Old Guo..." Chen Chao's voice was dry and hoarse, "Wang Ling... he was so badly injured before, and he landed in an unknown area... he probably doesn't even have a protective magic item. In this godforsaken place, even a random mole could kill someone!"

His analytical abilities were working at this moment, frantically deducing the worst possible outcome, and the more he thought about it, the more terrified he became.

Guo Hao's face was so dark it could drip water: "No! We can't wait any longer! We have to go find him! He absolutely can't handle this alone!"

As soon as he finished speaking, Chen Chao struggled to stand up, but the intense pain from his wound made his vision go black, and he almost fell over again.

"Are you crazy?! You're like this now?!"

Guo Hao quickly helped him up: "Running around in the lava zone with an injured leg is no different from suicide! Besides, we don't even know which way to go!"

"Then what do you suggest we do? Just wait to collect his corpse?!"

Chen Chao roared, his eyes reddening, "Wang Ling is my brother! Not one of us from Number 60 High School can be missing!"

The sincere friendship among classmates became even more intense after the life-or-death crisis.

Just then—

"Oh? Little brother from Number 60 High School? What happened to you and you look so disheveled?" A slightly familiar voice with a hint of Beijing accent came from the rocky ridge above.

The two looked up abruptly and saw Li Changzhe from Jingmen Number 8 High School leaning out halfway, staring at them in surprise.

Standing beside him was Zhang Linyan, a soldier from the Di Shitian Squadron, carrying an ancient longbow and with sharp eyes.

"Brother Li? Senior Sister Zhang?" Chen Chao and Guo Hao instantly saw hope.

Li Changzhe flipped and leaped down with agility, while Zhang Linyan followed closely behind, her steps as light as a cat's.

The two looked to be in much better condition than Chen Chao and the others, though their combat suits bore some marks from fighting and traversing complex terrain.

"Wow! That leg injury is pretty bad! And that face too!" Li Changzhe squatted down to examine Chen Chao's calf, gasped, and immediately took out a few fern-like plants that emitted a cool scent from his spiritual pouch. He used his spiritual power to extract the juice and applied it to the wound.

"These are freshly picked herbs, I don't know their name yet, they're kind of a local specialty, they have hemostatic properties, and the effect is immediate! We've all tried them! Use them with confidence!"

The cooling medicinal properties quickly penetrated, and Chen Chao immediately felt the burning pain lessen considerably.

The powder from a jade bottle handed to Guo Hao by Zhang Linyan quickly stopped the bleeding and scabbed over the scratches on his face.

"Thank you, Brother Li! Thank you, Senior Zhang!"

The two thanked each other profusely, feeling incredibly fortunate.

"Don't mention it! It's fate that we bumped into each other in this godforsaken place!"

Li Changzhe waved his hand heartily, then asked curiously, "How did you end up in this lava hell? And how did you get so injured?"

Chen Chao and Guo Hao exchanged a glance and briefly recounted how they were lured by the fragrance, fell into the rift and encountered the guardian giant lizard, and how the eerie power that threatened to kill them terrified the monster at the brink of death.

"A late-stage lava lizard? It was scared away by a powerful aura?"

After listening, Zhang Linyan frowned slightly, her sharp eyes scanning the surroundings with vigilance. "This place is indeed a hundred times more dangerous than the intelligence report. I also vaguely sensed the fluctuations you mentioned. They are very terrifying, and the source seems to be very far away... You are lucky."

"Yes!" Chen Chao quickly chimed in, "It's precisely because this place is too dangerous that we're so worried about Wang Ling! He's seriously injured! Brother Li, Senior Zhang, have you seen any of the other students from Number 60 High School?"

"Wang Ling, classmate?"

Upon hearing this, Li Changzhe's face also showed worry: "I haven't seen her. We just met up with Lin Yan not long ago, and we're planning to find other teams to join us. There's strength in numbers, right?"

He patted Chen Chao on the shoulder. "I understand you're worried about your brother, but now is not the time to be reckless. You can't go far right now. Here's what we'll do: you come with us first, find a relatively safe place to rest, and we'll treat your wounds. Once you're feeling better, we'll discuss how to find someone. Sun Rong and the others from Number 60 High School are quite capable, and Wang Ling... good fortune will surely come!"

Zhang Linyan nodded in agreement: "We shouldn't stay here any longer. Although the pressure we felt earlier was strong, the direction of its fluctuations was unclear, making it difficult to pinpoint its source. The lava lizard might return, or it might attract something else. Come with us."

Looking at his limping leg, Chen Chao was anxious, but he also knew that Li Changzhe and Zhang Linyan were right.

Under Guo Hao's worried gaze, he sighed heavily and said in a hoarse voice, "Then... I'll have to trouble Brother Li and Senior Sister Zhang!"

Guo Hao breathed a sigh of relief and quickly thanked him.

At the moment, Li Changzhe was helping Chen Chao, and Zhang Linyan was exploring the way. The four of them quickly left the lava ridge filled with the aura of death and headed towards a small cave that Li Changzhe and the others had discovered earlier that had a stable supply source and was relatively hidden.

Meanwhile, at the Palm Cliff Earth Core Project Base, the core central hall.

The glaring red alarm finally went out, replaced by a monitor screen and various data streams that were still flashing and working, albeit with some static.

"Report! The deep passive energy spectrum network has recovered 67% of its operation! The information filtering model reconstruction is complete!"

"The damaged sensor array has activated its backup unit..."

"Communication relay node...partially restored! Attempting to capture signal source!"

The technicians' tired but excited voices rose and fell.

Teng Luchen stood in front of the control panel, his hands gripping the alloy edge, his bloodshot eyes fixed on the screen, and the veins on his forehead throbbing slightly from the anxiety and pressure of repairing the system over the past few days.

Jing Heqiu stood aside and reported the latest progress: "Elder Teng, the system has been initially repaired, and we have recaptured the peak value of the abnormal spiritual energy fluctuations that erupted about three minutes ago! The intensity far exceeds that of the channel anomaly, and even surpasses the two previous high-energy annihilation fluctuations! The location... is roughly deep in the air above the vast boundary between the [Illusory Crystal Forest] and the [Lava Ridge]! The area is extremely large!"

Wang Ming was rapidly operating an independent terminal embedded in the main control panel. On the several small split screens in front of him, deep blue data streams were flowing like a waterfall, and countless complex waveforms were being analyzed and compared.

His expression was unusually serious. He pushed up his glasses and said in a deep voice, "Elder Teng, Dean Jing, the preliminary data analysis is in! The energy spectrum characteristics of this fluctuation... match the frequency spectrum of the residual reaction from the underworld passage we captured earlier with a 98% accuracy rate..."

Teng Luchen suddenly stood up straight, his thin fingers turning slightly white from pressing hard on the edge of the control panel.

His eyes flashed with a sharp light!

The underworld?

So this is the underworld...

In an instant, all the inexplicable anomalies that had previously pointed to Wang Ling were instantly linked together by the powerful logical chain in Teng Luchen's brain!

This is not about Wang Ling hiding his true abilities at all, but rather... a silent contest taking place in the shadows between the cultivation world of Huaxiu Kingdom and the forces of the underworld in the Earth's core!

All the previous guesses were wrong! Utterly wrong!

Wang Ling... that "seriously wounded" person whom he bombarded with artillery, tested with spiritual pressure, and even believed immediately when he was framed... how could he be some special being who needed to be closely monitored?

He was clearly a scapegoat who was innocently dragged into this earth-shattering secret battle and suffered an undeserved calamity!

The medical team's sophisticated equipment confirmed that the serious injury was real!

The damage to the meridians is real!

The weakness of the sea of consciousness is also true!

He had actually... believed his intuition was correct because the medical report showed "no signs of camouflage"!

He actually blamed all of this on a pitiful Foundation Establishment stage mascot who was caught up in the struggle between high-level cultivators or factions!

"Boom!"

An immense sense of guilt crashed down on Teng Luchen's heart like a massive boulder.

For the first time, a pale complexion, a mixture of immense shock and self-loathing, appeared on his usually shrewd and even slightly mean old face.

He could almost see Wang Ling's calm yet pale face lying in the medical pod, his weak breath...

This is not an act; it is a real and serious injury caused by the impact!

In order to test this "variable", he even tacitly allowed the stress test to continue, leaving Sun Rong behind to take care of him, thus depriving them of the opportunity to participate in the test and safely explore the Earth's core!

A bitter taste welled up in his throat, and Teng Luchen's lips moved, but no sound came out.

He slowly loosened his fingers, which were almost embedded in the alloy tabletop, his hands trembling slightly.

He turned to the screen, his gaze becoming extremely complex.

The mysterious organization of the Underworld has always been a highly secretive entity. In the past, only a very few psionic signals had been detected. Teng Luchen never expected that this mysterious organization with such a powerful background would have its base most likely located in the Earth's core.

Teng Luchen took a deep breath, forcibly suppressing his turbulent emotions. His voice was low and hoarse, carrying an undeniable weight:

"Immediately mobilize all monitoring resources! Lock onto any signs of activity in the underworld!"

"Highest alert! Project Core enters Phase Two: Combat Response!"

"At the same time..." Teng Luchen's voice paused, unusually tinged with a hint of bitterness, "Activate the highest priority mission—to search for and guide all the lost student teams with all efforts, providing them with the highest level of security! Especially... especially the students who have been caught up in the vicinity of the abnormal energy zone!"

His gaze fell heavily on the red area that symbolized the fluctuations of the underworld, as if piercing through the earth's crust to see the pale-faced boy's figure.

The price of misjudgment can sometimes be so heavy that it's hard to breathe.

Little mascot...

You absolutely must live!

At this moment, Wang Ling, who was enjoying his retirement vacation in his cave, couldn't help but sneeze.

At the end of the cave passage, adorned with a cold, blue light, lies the entrance, which the massive body of the Mountain Moving Monster respectfully makes way for.

The scene that greeted Sun Rong, Liu Qingyi, and Jiu Gong Liangzi instantly dispelled their tension and lingering fear from the attack they had encountered in the Illusionary Crystal Forest.

This place... is like a science fiction research institute moved into a fairy cave!

The smooth, mirror-like alloy floor reflected the soft yet clear light from the dome. The huge, inverted crystal clusters, seemingly tamed by some unknown force, emitted a warm and comfortable light after being harmonized.
