Chapter 2343 Worshiping ghosts? Worshiping my ass!

Wang Ying's shout of "Stand at attention and take the beating!" still echoed on the sea.

Che Li Jushi's body began to tremble violently and uncontrollably.

Her face was filled with extreme terror, and a final flash of madness suddenly appeared in her harsh eyes.

"You forced me to do this! You forced me to do this!" she screamed shrilly, her voice harsh and unpleasant, completely devoid of any of the island lord's dignity.

She suddenly plunged her hands into her wide Japanese-style robe, and when she pulled them out, she was holding a strange altar that was no bigger than the palm of her hand and completely black!

The surface of the altar was covered with twisted runes, emanating an ominous aura that made people want to vomit.

"May the Demon God appear!"

At that moment, with boundless piety on his face, Layman Che Li suddenly knelt down in front of the small altar with a "plop," his forehead hitting the cold deck heavily with a dull thud.

One, two, three!

With each kowtow, her spiritual energy was frantically drained by the altar, and her face grew increasingly pale.

"What is she doing?" On the coastline, a Chinese cultivator asked in surprise.

"I don't know, it feels... really weird!"

Just as Layman Che Li finished his third prostration—

"Buzz!!!"

The pitch-black altar suddenly trembled violently, unleashing a jet-black beam of light that stretched from the sky to the earth, shooting straight into the lead-gray clouds!

Within the pillar of light, it seemed as if countless vengeful spirits were struggling and wailing. The thick stench of blood and malevolent energy, even from a great distance, made everyone's hearts sink.

Boom!

The sky was pierced by this black light, and amidst a muffled thunderous roar, a colossal creature slowly descended from the black light!

That was a pitch-black demon god with three heads and six arms!

It stands tall and upright, its body pieced together from countless bones, its muscles bulging, its three heads displaying different expressions, and each of its six arms wielding a different weapon.

The two largest hands in the very center were tightly gripping a huge samurai sword shrouded in black mist!

Upon closer inspection, the blade was clearly covered with writhing black skulls!

They were densely packed, like demons crawling out of hell, forcibly bound to the blade.

The mere presence of the demon god caused the seawater that had just parted to boil violently, making the Chinese cultivators on the coastline behind him stop breathing, and those with weaker cultivation levels collapsed to the ground!

"What level is this..." Zhang Potian's face turned ashen.

The aura of this demon god far surpassed that of the three lord-level sea beasts from before. He could no longer specifically sense the opponent's strength; the gap was simply too great.

"Ha ha ha ha!"

Che Li Jushi struggled to his feet, his face ashen, his eyes filled with smugness: "See that? This is the Yamata no Orochi demon god that my Sun Island has worshipped for generations! You lowly Hua Xiu insects, tremble before the true god!"

She pointed at Wang Ying, her voice shrill: "Kill him! Crush them all!"

As if it had received a command, the Yamata no Orochi demon god turned its three heads toward Wang Ying at the same time, and its six eyes shot out a bloodthirsty red light.

It let out a deafening roar, the sound waves of which shook the hundred-meter-long water walls on both sides.

Then, it raised the giant samurai sword, which was wrapped with countless black skulls, high!

The moment the blade fell, countless black skulls, like freed prisoners, let out shrill screams and transformed into streaks of black light, surging towards the coastline in an overwhelming rush!

After landing, these skeletons quickly solidified, each wielding a samurai sword, with soul fire flickering in their eye sockets. Their auras were at least at the late Golden Core stage, and there were even quite a few Nascent Soul stage skeleton generals among them!

They were fearless and ferocious, and launched an attack on the coastal defense line in an instant!

"Form ranks! Hold on!" Shi Tie roared, brandishing his one arm, as he charged at a Nascent Soul-level skeleton general. His fist clashed with the bone blade, producing a metallic clang.

He let out a muffled groan, jolted backward by the shock.

The defensive line was instantly on the verge of collapse!

There are too many of them! And their cultivation levels are extremely high!

This is not even in the same league!

However, faced with this earth-shattering scene, Wang Ying merely picked at his ear, his sinister smile growing even more pronounced.

"Tch." He clicked his tongue in disdain, his purple eyes sweeping over the towering demon god, then glancing at the surging sea of skulls below, before finally settling on the smug-looking Che Li Jushi.

"I said old hag?"

Wang Ying's voice was filled with sarcasm: "Are you Sun Island people out of your minds? You can't win, so you kneel down and worship ghosts? You've come up with this thing, it looks impressive, but in reality..."

He drew out the word, then spat out, "Utterly useless!"

Che Li Jushi's smug expression froze, and he shrieked, "Still dare to be stubborn even when death is imminent! Demon God, please swiftly slay this scoundrel!"

The eight-headed serpent demon seemed to be enraged by Wang Ying's contempt, its three heads roaring in unison, and its massive katana was raised once more.

This time, the target is Wang Ying herself!

But Wang Ying moved even faster.

"Playing with skeletons? Using human wave tactics?" He scoffed. "You guys are still too green to play this game with me."

He casually raised his hand and grabbed at the void.

A surge of shadow power rippled outwards.

The next moment, an object appeared out of thin air in his hand.

Those were Wang Ling's school trousers from Number 60 High School...

Wang Ying began to search the pockets of her school trousers, hoping to find some useful magic items from the "King's Treasure Trousers" inside.

Soon, he summoned a large black banner that fluttered in the wind!

The banner surface was as deep as a black hole, the banner pole was pitch black, and a ferocious demon head was carved at the top, exuding a terrifying aura that was even more ancient than the Yamata no Orochi demon god!

[Ten Thousand Souls Banner]!

This is a version perfectly replicated from Wang Ling's "King's Treasure Pants" using the power of Wang Yingying's Dao. The power of the Great Dao is formidable! It is far superior to ordinary Myriad Soul Banners!

"What...is that?" Che Li Jushi's pupils contracted sharply, and an ominous premonition rose in his heart.

Wang Ying didn't give her any time to think. With a flick of his wrist, he threw the Ten Thousand Souls Banner into the air.

"go!"

A soft shout.

The Banner of Ten Thousand Souls grew longer in the wind, instantly transforming into a hundred-zhang-long cloud, like a giant dark cloud, suspended above the army of skeletons.

The banner moved on its own without any wind, and the vortex on it suddenly accelerated its rotation, with an indescribable and terrifying suction force descending like a black hole!

"Waaaah—!"

A mournful cry of ghosts and wolves emanated from the banner!

Below, the black skeletons that were frantically charging at the defensive line seemed to have encountered their nemesis!

Their charge suddenly halted, and the fire of their souls flickered violently in their eye sockets, revealing an instinctive fear.

The next second, the dozens of Golden Core stage skeletons closest to the source were uncontrollably uprooted by the suction force.

They brandished their bone knives in vain, uttering silent cries, while their bodies rapidly disintegrated in mid-air, turning into streams of pure black energy that were greedily swallowed by the vortex on the Ten Thousand Souls Banner!

And this is just the beginning.

The Banner of Ten Thousand Souls is like a bottomless pit, accepting all who come, and the aura emanating from its surface is becoming increasingly terrifying.

The army of skeletons, which was originally like a black tide, evaporated rapidly at a speed visible to the naked eye!

The coastal defense line, which was in dire straits just moments before, suddenly felt much less pressured.

The Hua Xiu monks stared in disbelief at everything, almost forgetting to breathe.

"This...that's it?" Shi Tie's mouth gaped open, big enough to fit an egg.

Zhang Potian's Adam's apple bobbed as he murmured, "This senior... just who is he...?"

The smugness on Layman Che Li's face had vanished, replaced by boundless fear and disbelief.

"No! Impossible! My skeleton army! Lord Yamata no Makoto! Kill him!" she screamed hysterically at the demon in the air.

The Yamata no Orochi demon seemed to sense the threat and abandoned its plan to attack the coastline. Its three heads locked onto Wang Ying, and its six arms swung, bringing down a massive samurai sword with earth-shattering power!

Wherever the blade passed, ripples spread through the space, as if it were about to be cleaved in two by this single strike!

Faced with this attack that could split mountains and shatter peaks, Wang Ying merely raised her eyelids lazily.

"Big guy, it's your turn."
