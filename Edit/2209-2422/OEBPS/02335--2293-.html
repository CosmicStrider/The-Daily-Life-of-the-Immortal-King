Chapter 2293 Rescue Plan

"Whoosh..."

The murky wastewater, gleaming with an odd oily sheen, was scooped up with a specially made food-grade metal strainer.

Water droplets flowed back into the ditch through the holes of the strainer, making a dull, oily sound as they dripped.

The sound was almost completely drowned out by the low hum of the large processing equipment around it.

The research and development manager, who wore wire-rimmed glasses and appeared to be a refined gentleman, was kneeling beside a drainage ditch that emitted a strong odor, his posture carrying a focus that seemed incongruous with his status.

Wang Ling's gaze pierced through layers of obstacles, precisely locking onto the strainer.

After the wastewater was filtered out, what remained at the bottom of the strainer was not solid waste, but a small clump of viscous, dark red substance that slowly wriggled like a living thing.

This substance contains more than ten times the primordial evil energy that Wang Ling had sensed in any product before!

It resembled a constantly beating heart, its surface shimmering with an ominous, malevolent light, and faint sounds of wailing could be heard.

"It's a crystallization of evil thoughts created using negative emotions as material through some kind of technology..." Wang Ling instantly deciphered the essence of this thing.

This stuff is clearly a new food additive developed by the Xibei Cultivation Food Group, and it is the "mother" of all additives.

It was incorporated into food and served to monks to stimulate the dark side of their hearts.

Just as Wang Ling analyzed before, short-term consumption will not affect the body, and you can even feel the improvement in cultivation. However, once you take it for a long time, your temperament will gradually become more restless, and you will become more susceptible to extreme ideas.

Now Wang Ling has discovered something else: this evil thought crystal is just like gutter oil. He guesses that the person in charge will dredge it up again and re-condense it using technical means, and its concentration will become higher and higher.

What was previously added to food was merely a dilution of it by billions of times.

If this small mass is not handled properly, it could pollute the entire underground water system of Songhai City in a short period of time, subtly affecting the minds of millions of low-level cultivators and ordinary people.

At this moment, the person in charge carefully used a pair of jade tweezers to pick up the dark red substance and put it into a metal box engraved with sealing runes.

"Hmph... Foolish mortal cultivators, only chasing after their appetites and meager spiritual power. Little do they know that what they are swallowing are the seeds of future submission to our Lord." The person in charge muttered to himself, a barely perceptible smirk curling at the corner of his mouth, completely different from his usual gentle demeanor.

He closed the box, carefully checked the seal, then stood up and straightened his white coat.

Then, as if nothing had happened, I walked towards a passageway deeper into the treatment area that was marked with the words "High-risk waste incinerator".

Wang Ling's lifeless eyes remained calm and undisturbed.

He unconsciously twirled his fingertips in his pocket.

A thought containing supreme purification power swept across the entire waste material processing area in an instant.

This intention precisely descended upon the evil crystal within the metal box, not destroying it immediately, but silently planting another mark.

Without hesitation, Wang Ling locked it directly, just in case.

After doing all this, Wang Ling refocused his gaze on Sun Rong, who was enjoying the new spiritual energy pudding.

Sun Rong ate the pudding in small bites, but her brows were slightly furrowed.

"What's wrong, Sun Rong? Is it not to your liking?"

Chen Chao and Zhu Bajie, who were standing to the side, swallowed their portions in a few bites like they were eating ginseng fruit. They smacked their lips and said, "I think it's alright. It's cool and refreshing, and the spiritual energy is quite gentle."

This left Wang Ling speechless.

Because he could figure out that this thing had a shelf life of 1000 years.

It can remain completely uncorroded even at room temperature.

If too much of this stuff is ingested, even tens of thousands of years after death, the body could be dug up looking brand new.

Guo Hao also noticed Sun Rong's unusual expression: "Is it too sweet? This stuff uses artificial sweeteners and is completely zero calories. Don't worry about gaining weight!"

Sun Rong shook her head, put down her small spoon, and said with a hint of uncertainty, "It's not a problem with the taste. It's just that after eating it, I feel a little... depressed? Like a little irritable? And this stuff feels like it has a lot of sweeteners in it."

Especially when she was tasting the food, a wisp of psionic energy wave with a warning connotation came from Ouhai.

If she and O'Hay hadn't been so in sync, she would have been almost completely unaware.

This fluctuation was fleeting, but it instantly put Sun Rong on alert.

She subconsciously looked at Wang Ling.

Wang Lingzheng looked down at the untouched spiritual energy juice in his hand, and upon seeing the ingredient list, which was longer than the dictionary, he fell into deep thought once again.

She remembered Wang Ling's earlier reminder to "eat less".

Sure enough... he noticed something was wrong with the food processing plant!

Sun Rong's cheeks flushed slightly, and the slight annoyance caused by the food was dispelled by a small happiness of being cared for by Wang Ling.

She composed herself and whispered to Chen Chao and Guo Hao, "Don't you two feel uncomfortable in any way? Like emotionally?"

Chen Chao scratched his head: "No, I just felt that the spiritual energy was quite strong. I felt great after eating it and wanted to find someone to fight."

"..." This time, it was Sun Rong's turn to fall silent.

This is practically evolving into "hyperandrogen syndrome," and you still say there's no problem?!

Guo Hao carefully assessed the situation: "Sun Rong, now that you mention it, it does seem a little bit! But it's very mild, maybe it's because I didn't sleep well last night?"

The difference in the three people's reactions clearly reflected the differences in their cultivation level and spiritual sensitivity.

Sun Rong had the strongest cultivation and possessed the Aohai warning, so she felt it the most deeply.

Guo Hao was observant and perceptive, so he noticed something amiss.

As for Chen Chao, he's oblivious and practically insensitive, and his "combative" tendencies have even been amplified. Without intervention, he's probably beyond saving...

Wang Ling took in their reactions and understood what was going on.

This kind of penetration has little effect on high-level cultivators; its main target is the vast majority of low- and mid-level cultivators and ordinary people.

The purpose is to directly control these cultivators at the bottom of their cultivation levels so that they can play a role in the future War of Destiny.

Perhaps they were being used as hostages by high-ranking monks? Or perhaps, for some other purpose...

"Dear students, the next area is the last stop of this tour, and it's also the core technology demonstration area of our HiPai Group: the Psychic Harmony Center!" The guide's sweet voice rang out again, drawing everyone's attention.

She led everyone toward the large door that Wang Ling had noticed earlier, which was protected by a powerful barrier.

"This is our core research and development area, which is not usually open to the public. We have specially applied for a short visit permission for everyone today. Please abide by the rules, do not take photos or videos, and do not touch any equipment at will."

The door slid open slowly, and a carefully blended aroma of spices wafted out.

The interior space is extremely large, filled with various sophisticated analytical instruments and extraction equipment, and many staff members are busy working in sterile suits.

However, Wang Ling's consciousness instantly locked onto the person in charge.

He was walking toward an inconspicuous corner where there was a device with an antique design that looked out of place in the surrounding high-tech environment. It resembled an alchemy furnace but was connected to countless pipes.

The person in charge quietly embedded the metal box into a hidden compartment at the bottom of the device.

The moment the hidden compartment closed, Wang Ling clearly sensed that a powerful energy was activated, slowly drawing power from the crystalline mass of evil thoughts and channeling it through a complex network of pipes into a huge liquid pool in the center that shone with seven-colored light.

That pool of liquid is what the Xibei Group calls "basic spiritual energy essence," the core of its many high-end products.

It's actually some kind of high-tech additive...

It's just a name change, which makes it sound much more upscale.

"So that's how it is. They want to dilute the reclaimed evil thoughts crystals a billion times and mix them into this pool of original liquid, then incorporate them into countless products as an additive..." Wang Ling immediately understood the other party's entire process.

This method is extremely covert, and conventional testing methods simply cannot detect it.
