Chapter 2266 Wang Ling's Decision

The deep passage twisted and extended downwards like the esophagus of a giant beast, and the fluorescent moss on the rock walls flickered weakly in the absolute darkness.

With each step taken, the sticky darkness beneath one's feet would swarm back like maggots clinging to bones, as if to drag the intruder into the eternal purgatory at the earth's core.

Wang Ling's figure was squeezed into a sharp silhouette by the rock wall, like a blade cutting through chaos, and even darkness itself trembled and retreated wherever it passed.

The air was filled with the pungent smell of sulfur and metal corrosion.

Each breath felt like swallowing scorching iron filings, while from deeper within, a pulse like the heartbeat of an ancient Titan slowly eroded the very marrow of every person who ventured deep into the heartland.

Sun Rong followed closely behind, the scabbard of the Ao Hai sword and the red lotus petals in her arms reflecting each other, becoming the only warm and bright light source in this absolute darkness.

The air became increasingly viscous, and each breath carried a heavy pressure, not just spiritual pressure, but more like the weight of life itself.

The pulse originating from the oldest core of the earth traveled through the rock wall, along the soles of their feet, striking the depths of their souls like a drumbeat.

The lamentations of the ancient spirits become even clearer here.

No longer a distant echo, it transformed into a terrifying, mournful wind, wailing as it passed through the passage, bringing with it a bone-chilling cold.

Sun Rong couldn't help but shiver, not because of the temperature, but because of the despair and pain contained in the mournful cry, which made her feel the same way.

Ao Hai emitted a soft hum, spreading out a circle of azure protective sword light that shielded her and slightly dispelled her discomfort.

Wang Ling paused almost imperceptibly, glancing at her sideways.

"I'm fine."

Sun Rong immediately shook her head, trying to put on a reassuring smile: "It's just...it seems to be in a lot of pain."

"I'm here, everything will be alright."

Wang Ling nodded, his gaze returning to the unfathomable darkness ahead.

He reached out and seemingly casually pressed his hand against the rough rock face beside him.

Buzz—!

An invisible yet vast and boundless thought, centered on his palm, quietly spread out like ripples on water, instantly calming the restless earth vein energy around him. Even the omnipresent mournful wind seemed to be forcibly suppressed for a moment, becoming gentle.

Sun Rong felt a sudden lightness all over her body, and the suffocating pressure vanished abruptly.

She looked at Wang Ling in surprise, but he had already withdrawn his hand, as if he had done nothing, and continued walking.

It's the same again.

He always shielded her from all the storms in the most unintentional way.

A warm feeling welled up in Sun Rong's heart. She quickened her pace, almost walking alongside him, and asked softly, "Wang Ling, we're going to help it, right? That... Ancient Spirit?"

Wang Ling nodded again.

This time, he unusually gave an extra half-word in response: "Mm."

Although it was just a syllable, it instantly put Sun Rong at ease, and her eyes became even more determined.

If he believes something should be done, then it must be correct.

The passageway began to change.

The rock walls on both sides were gradually replaced by a strange crystal with a dark golden texture, as if it were flowing with life.

Inside the crystal, countless fine filaments of light can be faintly seen swirling around, constantly pulsating like the nerve network of the human body.

The resonance between Ao Hai and Hong Lian grew stronger and stronger. Sun Rong could even feel the sword core in her dantian greedily absorbing the pure and ancient earth mother energy in the air, and the golden-red flame patterns shone brightly.

Suddenly, Wang Ling stopped in his tracks.

Three paths appeared ahead.

Each one exudes a completely different aura.

The leftmost one surges with scorching and violent lava energy, with faint traces of sulfur and beastly roars.

The middle path was dark and desolate, exuding a chilling emptiness that sent shivers down one's spine, as if leading to the underworld.

The rightmost line is relatively peaceful, with the dark gold crystals being the most concentrated. The lamentations of the ancient spirits seem to mainly emanate from there, but mixed in is a strange, sticky feeling that is extremely hidden and attempts to disguise assimilation.

This is a trap set by the will of the Outer Gods!

Wang Ling noticed it immediately.

"Three roads...which one should we take?" Sun Rong focused her senses, her delicate brows furrowing slightly.

Both Okumi and Kurenai pointed to the right, but her intuition and embroidery abilities sent a faint warning, indicating danger on the right.

Wang Ling's gaze swept over the three entrances, his dead fish eyes showing no emotion.

Without the slightest hesitation, he raised his hand and pointed to... the leftmost passage where lava was boiling and beasts were roaring.

"Huh?" Sun Rong was taken aback.

The one on the left looks the most dangerous!

The energy is raging, clearly indicating that a large number of powerful subterranean creatures reside there.

Wang Ling didn't explain, but simply took the lead and walked in.

Although Sun Rong was puzzled, her trust in Wang Ling overwhelmed everything, and she immediately followed.

The moment the two stepped into the left passage, something unexpected happened!

The scene in the passage suddenly distorted and changed!

The illusion of lava and beastly roars shattered and vanished like glass, revealing the true scene behind it!

It was a wide passageway constructed of pure dark gold crystals, leading to an incredibly vast underground space!

The passageways in the middle and on the right silently closed behind them, transforming into solid rock walls.

"An illusion? A trap?" Sun Rong realized instantly.

The Outer God's will distorted the entrance, disguising the most dangerous path as the safest, and the real path as a Jedi!

If Wang Ling hadn't seen through their deception, they would probably have already fallen into a deadly trap.

Wang Ling's expression remained calm, as if he had known this would happen all along.

In his eyes, this level of illusion was no different from a child's doodles.

"roar--!"

However, the real test was not over.

As if the illusion was broken, triggering some kind of defense mechanism, the dark golden crystals on both sides of the wide crystal passage ahead suddenly lit up!

One after another, figures formed from pure earth energy mixed with a certain violent fighting spirit stepped out from the crystal wall!

They are not physical entities, but rather similar to elemental guardians. They are tall and wield heavy weapons such as giant axes and war hammers, emanating a powerful aura comparable to that of a Dao Venerable cultivator!

Their eyes are a pure earthy yellow, driven only by the instinct to fight, devoid of any rationality.

This is a manifestation of the ancient spirits' innate defense system!

To protect its core, it will attack anything that gets close indiscriminately!

A full twelve Dao Venerable-level Earth Guardians!

They came rolling in like a wall, their steps shaking the passageway!

Sun Rong's face turned pale, and she instantly gripped Ao Hai tightly.

This number of Dao Venerable level guards is far beyond what she can handle alone!
