Chapter 2245 The Immortal King's Daily Life of Retirement

The mountain-moving creature huddled in the shadows at the warehouse entrance, its massive body straining to shrink into a ball, almost blending into the rock face.

Its enormous scarlet eye was reduced to a thin slit, and its crystal bone spikes lay obediently on its body surface, even its breathing was suppressed into a barely audible stream.

The scene just now—Wang Ling didn't even cast a glance at it, and the messenger from the underworld was directly erased—had exceeded the limits of its limited understanding.

That was a complete and utter destruction, leaving no trace.

It felt a deep-seated fear for having been tasked with probing such beings.

The private paradise created by Wang Ling was eerily silent.

Only the rhythmic crunching sound of Wang Ling chewing the instant noodles echoed, crisp and somewhat ethereal.

He stared intently at the packaging bag in his hand. The faint light unique to the Earth's core shone down from the dome, illuminating his expressionless profile. In this space he had created himself, a strangely harmonious blend of technology and fantasy, he resembled an ordinary teenager researching new snack flavors in his own study.

I wonder how everyone is doing?

The thought flashed through my mind and disappeared in an instant.

Wang Ling's consciousness, like a god's-eye view that doesn't need to activate radar, naturally swept across a radius of a hundred miles.

Chen Chao's loud shouts, Guo Hao's muttered analysis of the cause of the passage's collapse, Sun Rong's gentle tone as she soothed everyone, and Teng Luchen's outburst of rage and roaring in the central hall of the Palm Cliff base as if his tail had been stepped on...

Countless pieces of noisy information flooded in, but he filtered out the irrelevant bits even faster.

As long as it's safe.

After confirming that no one in the sixty small groups was injured, Wang Lingchang breathed a sigh of relief.

The chaotic spatial currents completely scattered the team, and now they are each taking root in different corners of the earth, either in small teams or individually.

Su Xingyue is organizing the elites of Shengke to establish a temporary base, while Zhang Linyan is leading the people of Di Shitian to hide near a crystal vein... In general, they are all carrying out the initial "intelligent image collection" task, but the process is full of stumbles.

Wang Ling's consciousness paused slightly in one direction—towards Sun Rong. She, along with Jiu Gong Liangzi and Liu Qingyi, had landed on the edge of a huge, dark blue mushroom forest. The three of them were relatively calm, with Jiu Gong Liangzi fiddling with the array plate that Zhuo Yi had given her, attempting to establish the most basic directional coordinates.

It is very safe.

A subtle sense of "reassurance" rippled through the depths of his consciousness.

His gaze returned to the neatly stacked instant noodles in the warehouse... they were empty.

The journey to the Earth's core was indeed rushed, and the supplies were still a bit insufficient.

Just then, a huge head, yet one that was extremely cautious and obsequious, slowly emerged from the shadows at the warehouse entrance.

Moving mountains.

It had a large handful of strangely shaped subterranean plants in its mouth.

Dark purple vines coiled around several fist-sized, translucent fruits, their leaves like jade, with fine, icy velvet along the edges.

This handful of things exuded a pure and understated fragrance of herbs and trees, instantly dispelling the lingering sulfur smell in the air.

"Waaah... Immortal... Immortal..." Moving Mountain carefully placed the handful of spiritual plants on the alloy floor not far from Wang Ling's feet. Its huge single eye struggled to turn upwards, conveying a humble and fearful thought.

"By the Earth Vein Spring... the sweetest Jade Heart Fruit... nearby there are... many more... smaller ones... offered to you to try..." Its crystal claws nervously scratched the ground, making soft humming sounds.

That shows some thought.

Wang Ling glanced at the things on the ground. Jade Heart Fruit... It looked quite refreshing, seemed suitable as an ingredient in herbal tea or juice? He hadn't seen a similar variety on the surface map. He casually picked up a topaz-like fruit and weighed it in his hand.

Suddenly, a faint coolness spread smoothly and quietly from the fingertips holding the fruit, instantly brushing through the meridians of his body, bringing a pure sense of comfort from the material itself.

The subtle stagnation caused by long-term self-isolation seemed to be gently smoothed out by this cool breeze.

Although this little bit of comfort is like a drop in the ocean to him right now, the key is that it doesn't hinder him at all!

I never imagined there were such wonderful things in the inner Earth!

A rare glimmer of light flashed in Wang Ling's usually stagnant eyes.

This could be considered... an unexpected bonus?

The taste is unknown, but it should taste good if made into juice? He looked at the pile of spiritual plants on the ground.

The Mountain-Moving Beast had been intently watching Wang Ling's impassive expression. The moment it caught sight of that faint light, its enormous single eye instantly shone with an overwhelming ecstatic joy, as if it had survived a catastrophe.

"I...I know the way! I know a big forest full of these kinds of fruits and vines!"

The voice in its mind was so excited that it changed tone, "If the immortal has any free time, I will go and pick more. The spiritual energy there is especially good, and the fruits are bigger and sweeter!" Its huge body straightened up a bit, and the crystal bone spurs shone with luster.

Wang Ling didn't speak, but simply nodded slightly.

"Yes, sir!" The Mountain-Moving Demon seemed to obey a divine decree. Its massive body instantly unleashed a level of agility disproportionate to its size, and it practically tumbled and crawled out of the cave entrance.

The cave dwelling, which could be considered a retirement home, has once again returned to tranquility.

Wang Ling held the Jade Heart Fruit in his hand, feeling the gentle coolness flowing through his fingertips.

This journey to the center of the earth... seems to be a little bit more interesting than I imagined.

At least, he discovered a new material that might be used to make sealing talismans.

He walked towards the fusion creation in the corner that looked like a molecular gastronomy machine or an alchemy furnace—a workbench he had built in his ideal form while constructing the base. Just like the workbenches in those early-game construction games.

With a slight thought, the Jade Heart Fruit floated up, and the ice crystal vines beside it disintegrated...

Meanwhile, in the remote Palm Cliff base's core central hall, a suffocatingly low pressure still lingered.

"Still no results..." Teng Luchen paced anxiously in front of the main screen, which was mostly black, his eyes bloodshot as he stared at the few remaining surveillance images that were still flickering and filled with static.

"When will the core system be restored? Can the backup power supply hold up? I need to see the spatial anchor point distribution map when the Earth's core tunnel finally stabilizes! Where did the Number 60 High School team land?" He bombarded him with questions.

"Teng...Teng Lao..."

A technician's voice trembled with tears, "The core processor is smoking, it's burned out all the circuits... We're organizing people to analyze it manually... but the interference source is off the charts, the data is all gibberish... It's very difficult to accurately determine the location of the elite members at the moment..."

"Oh, how could this be..." Teng Luchen almost crushed the handrail.

The target is lost! Especially... Wang Ling!

His intuition had never been so sharp—the anomalies in the passage, the instantaneous annihilation of the detection system, were definitely related to that kid.

But now he can't find the person, and he doesn't even know if the other person is alive or dead, or where they are in some remote corner.

...

Deep within the earth, in that secluded and luxurious cave, a fragrance mingled with the refreshing aroma of cool fruit juice and the essence of some peculiar herbs and plants was gently spreading.

Wang Ling held a half-full glass of juice that he had just gotten from the multi-purpose workbench, took a small sip, and comfortably squinted his dead fish eyes.

That feels good...

Outside the base, the Mountain-Moving Beast was huffing and puffing, engrossed in its newly discovered vast grove of Jade Heart Fruits. Its enormous single eye shone with an astonishing light, and it was full of energy. The fruits it held in its paws were piled up almost like a small mountain.

Life in the underground cave seemed to be moving into the peaceful path that Wang Ling had never anticipated, in a way that was completely unexpected.

The life of Hades, whose beloved general was brutally murdered, Teng Luchen, who was anxiously trying to restore communication with the Earth's core, and other elite high school members who were in a state of refugee mode, stood in stark contrast to Wang Ling's life, which was practically a retirement home.

At this moment, a familiar voice, transmitted through a certain frequency of spiritual wave medium, reached Wang Ling's mind.

It was Wang Ming's voice.

His good older brother had indeed prepared a backup plan and concealed the truth from Teng Luchen.
