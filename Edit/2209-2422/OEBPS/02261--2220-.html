Chapter 2220 Huh?

A deafening boom!

Under Meng Nian's heavy blow, the entire giant egg caved in with an extremely exaggerated force.

In an instant, it was as if a powerful bomb had been dropped into the laboratory. The violent tremors caused the smoke and dust to surge and swirl wildly in all directions like a raging black wave.

A fierce wind howled, its sound like the roar of a ferocious beast, and its powerful force shattered the glass jars placed around it.

The green slime inside the jar, mixed with some limbs that had not yet been completely decomposed by the liquid, slowly slid down the broken jar wall, pooling on the ground into a gruesome mixture that flowed everywhere.

The nauseating scene and the pungent smell of chemicals made one's stomach churn.

The laboratory lights flickered erratically in the smoke and dust, making the terrifying scene even more sinister and eerie.

This scene caused the sour and nauseating feeling that Sun Rong had managed to suppress to surge back up.

She felt a tightness in her throat, a tightness in her chest, and an invisible hand churning inside her stomach, making her feel like she was about to vomit.

But she kept her lips tightly closed, enduring the discomfort, took a deep breath to calm herself, and turned her gaze back to the boy in front of her with his hands in his pockets and an invincible look on his face, trying to divert her attention.

Sun Rong was already used to Wang Ling's deadly attacks.

Now that I've seen it so many times, I'm not surprised anymore.

Ever since she learned of Wang Ling's true strength, no one who has faced this young man has been able to withstand even a single move from him.

Even if some managed to hold out, it wasn't because Wang Ling was really serious; he was deliberately holding back.

Even Meng Nian, who was at the Ancestral Realm, never expected that he, a master of the Human Ancestor Realm, couldn't even withstand a single finger from Wang Ling.

Moreover, she had also unleashed a variety of magical weapons to protect her attack, and was full of confidence, certain of victory.

Just as he was about to make a move, the boy in front of him transformed into a golden flash and appeared instantly before him, lightly tapping the top of his head.

This seemingly insignificant detail contained an overwhelming force, completely overwhelming her and leaving her paralyzed on the ground, unable to get up again.

At this moment, Meng Nian lay dying in front of the giant egg, too badly injured to utter a complete sentence.

The dark energy surrounding her was originally meant to protect her, but unexpectedly, the golden light emanating from Wang Ling had a natural restraining effect on her, instantly rendering all her defenses and resistance ineffective and plunging her into such a miserable situation.

Wang Ling slowly stepped forward and carefully examined Meng Nian's injuries. In fact, he hadn't originally intended to knock Meng Nian out of the game with a single blow; it was just that Meng Nian's physique was too fragile.

"Although his cultivation level has risen to the Human Ancestor Realm by relying on a third-party power source, his physical constitution is still far too weak." Wang Ling sighed inwardly and came to this conclusion.

Lying on the ground, Meng Nian's body was still twitching slightly. She saw this incredibly powerful young man squatting down beside her and taking her pulse. Then, a surge of powerful spiritual energy rushed into her body through her meridians.

In an instant, her body, which had been shattered and in a terrible state, miraculously returned to normal.

Meng Nian sat up in astonishment, as if she had just experienced an extremely terrifying nightmare. She stared in disbelief at her body, which had been restored in an instant, her eyes filled with disbelief.

"It's recovered already?" Sun Rong's eyes widened, her face full of surprise.

"Temporary."

Facing Meng Nian, Wang Ling calmly stood up.

Just then, Jing Ke peeked out from Wang Ling's shoulder and said, "The Sword Master's spiritual power can only temporarily repair your body, because it will continue to deplete within you. Once it's exhausted, you will be back to your original state."

"Your methods are simply astonishing." Meng Nian couldn't help but exclaim in amazement. "In order to successfully enter the inner world of the earth and find my brother's soul, I tricked many cultivators into coming to this illusion laboratory. I have seen countless experts, some from outer space and some from our own earth, but I have never encountered a person as powerful as you."

"Now you see it."

Wang Ling looked at Meng Nian, feeling utterly speechless: "You went to all this trouble just to find your brother's soul?"

"Otherwise what?"

When she mentioned her younger brother, Meng Nian became agitated and almost lost control: "It's all that heartless liar's fault! She drove Meng Heng to his death! Everything I did was for him... I even made a deal with the underworld and became their messenger."

"The underworld really exists?" Sun Rong asked eagerly, her eyes sparkling with curiosity.

"It probably exists, but I've never been there."

Facing Wang Ling, Meng Nian knew that it would be futile to conceal the truth any longer, so she spoke frankly: "After my brother passed away, I was in a state of utter despair, plagued by nightmares all day long... I was listless and on the verge of collapse. I don't know how much time had passed since my brother's death, but one day, I suddenly heard a mysterious voice claiming that it could give me the power to bring my brother back to life, and made a deal with me."

"A transaction in a dream?" Wang Ling pressed.

"That's right."

Meng Nian nodded vigorously: "At first, I also thought it was just an illusion, until I truly felt that I had actually obtained that kind of powerful strength that ordinary cultivators could hardly achieve even after cultivating for thousands of years!"

"Besides that, an old storage bag has inexplicably appeared next to my pillow. Many of the magical weapons I used when I fought you before also came from this bag."

"I have never set foot in the Underworld, and my rank as an envoy is not high; I am merely an external envoy. Envoys in the Underworld are divided into eight realms. The first realm is the lowest, and the eighth realm is the highest. An external envoy like me only possesses the strength of the first realm."

Wang Ling was secretly surprised.

The power granted by a third party was able to directly transform an ordinary cultivator into a master at the level of the Human Ancestor, and the key point is that Meng Nian was just an envoy.

In the underworld, just how powerful must a higher-ranking messenger be?

Is there something that can rival oneself?

Upon hearing this, Wang Ling's curiosity was truly piqued.

"So, going to the inner world to find your brother's soul, is that also the Underworld's idea?" Wang Ling continued to ask.

"No, this is just something I heard while browsing ancient books. Once a cultivator dies, unless aided by magical treasures and elixirs, their three souls and seven spirits will quickly dissipate without a trace. According to the ancient books, these wandering souls will eventually reach the Earth's core," Meng Nian answered truthfully.

"There's no need to go through all that trouble."

At this moment, Jing Ke said, "You've harmed so many living beings just by searching for a single soul."

Meng Nian sneered repeatedly: "You little sword spirit, you make it sound so easy... Every day, countless new souls of dead cultivators are added to the world. Every day I delay, the probability of finding my brother's soul becomes increasingly slim, so I..."

Before Meng Nian could finish speaking, Wang Ling took out Jing Ke and drew a mysterious rift in the void, then forcibly dragged Meng Heng's soul out of the rift on the spot...

Meng Nian: "Huh?"
