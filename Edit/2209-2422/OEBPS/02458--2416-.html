Chapter 2416 Supporters

There are three days left until the quarterfinals.

In the standalone training hall of Ti-Shu High School, the muffled thud of fists striking metal continued from early morning until late at night.

Zhao Wuji was shirtless, white steam rising from his bronze skin, sweat dripping down his muscles and leaving wet marks on the ground.

The specially made force measuring target in front of him had already jumped to "2115kg".

"The third step is complete."

Standing to the side, Zhao Tieshan slowly exhaled, looking at his grandson with a complicated expression.

In just one week, he went from breaking through the second level to reaching the third level of perfection. This speed of cultivation is terrifying.

But the cost is also obvious.

Zhao Wuji's eyes were sunken, his lips were chapped, and his arms and back were covered with fine blood vessels, the marks of excessive muscle tearing and forced healing.

"Is it enough?" Zhao Wuji asked, panting heavily, his voice hoarse.

Zhao Tieshan didn't answer directly, but instead asked, "How much force do you think Wang Ling used when he caught your kick last week?"

"Thirty percent." Zhao Wuji gritted his teeth: "At most thirty percent."

"And now?" Zhao Tieshan asked again, "You've broken through to the third level, your punching power has increased by 40%, and your defense has doubled. How much of his strength do you think he can withstand your current full-force attack?"

Zhao Wuji clenched his fist.

He didn't know.

All he knew was that he had to win.

"Grandpa." He looked up, his eyes bloodshot. "Can you go to the competition that day?"

Zhao Tieshan was stunned.

"I want everyone to see," Zhao Wuji said, enunciating each word clearly, "I am your grandson. Our Zhao family will not lose to an ordinary high school student."

Looking at his grandson's stubborn face, Zhao Tieshan's hesitation dissipated.

He nodded: "Okay, I'll go."

...

Wednesday evening, in the elite class classroom of Number 60 High School.

Twenty minutes after the school bell rang, only Wang Ling, Chen Chao, and Guo Hao remained in the classroom.

Guo Hao pushed the tablet to the center of the table, and the screen displayed densely packed data charts.

"Zhao Wuji's physical test data, latest version."

Chen Chao went over to look, his eyes widening: "Two thousand one hundred kilograms of punching power? Wasn't it only one thousand eight hundred last week?"

"He has broken through," Guo Hao said. "He has reached the third level of the Vajra Subduing Demon Body. His defense is expected to increase by 120%, his strength by 40%, and his speed by 15%. His weaknesses are still there, but they are not as obvious anymore."

Chen Chao's face turned pale: "Then Wang Ling isn't..."

"Theoretically, it's very difficult," Guo Hao said. "If Wang Ling really used his full strength last week, then there's almost no chance of winning this time. But if..."

He didn't finish speaking, but Chen Chao understood what he meant.

What if Wang Ling didn't use his full strength at all last week?

"And this too." Guo Hao pulled up another document: "Zhao Wuji's grandfather, Zhao Tieshan, served in the army when he was young. It is said that he was a comrade-in-arms with Jiang Lei, the Martial Saint among the Ten Generals. Although they haven't had much contact in recent years, his reputation still stands. Zhao Tieshan will personally attend this event to give Zhao Wuji a boost."

Chen Chao frowned: "Is he trying to intimidate people with his connections?"

"Most likely." Guo Hao turned off the tablet: "Many people on the judging panel are within the system. When they hear the name of the Martial Saint, they will give him some face. Even if they don't show favoritism, they will still be inclined to support Zhao Wuji."

The classroom fell silent.

Wang Ling sat beside him, intently unpacking a new flavor of instant noodles.

Looking at him like this, Chen Chao suddenly felt that his worries were a bit unnecessary.

"Reiko," he couldn't help but ask, "Aren't you nervous at all?"

Wang Ling thought for a moment and shook his head.

"Why?" Chen Chao asked.

Wang Ling swallowed the instant noodles and said seriously, "Nervousness affects appetite."

Chen Chao: "..."

Guo Hao laughed and said, "Alright, as long as you know what I mean. In short, we'll be keeping a close eye on things on the day of the competition, and we'll be prepared if the Zhao family tries anything underhanded."

"What preparations?" Chen Chao asked.

Guo Hao took out a palm-sized metal device from his backpack, its surface engraved with fine runes.

"It was given to me by my uncle. It's a portable spiritual energy fluctuation recorder." He explained, "It can record all spiritual energy fluctuations within three meters of the battle arena. If Zhao Wuji uses any illegal methods, or if someone secretly helps him, this thing can catch him red-handed."

Chen Chao's eyes lit up: "Great stuff! How do I use it?"

"Don't do it on me," Guo Hao said. "I'll sit in the front row of the audience, facing E-03. But this thing is very sensitive. If Wang Ling uses spiritual power beyond the early stage of Foundation Establishment, it will be recorded."

After he finished speaking, he glanced at Wang Ling.

Wang Ling nodded, indicating that he understood.

Actually, what Wang Ling wanted to say was that this thing was useless to him.

His sealing talisman was made by Wang Ming himself. Not to mention portable recorders, even if you brought the most advanced testing equipment in Huaxiu Kingdom, it would not be able to detect anything abnormal.

But I can't say that.

"Oh, right, there's one more thing." Guo Hao put away his device: "Li Changzhe said he'll send some people over that day to support Wang Ling."

Chen Chao was taken aback: "Shaking people? Getting into a group fight?"

"No." Guo Hao chuckled. "It's about showing support. Didn't the Zhao family want to use their connections to intimidate us? Then we'll back down with our own momentum. Li Changzhe contacted a few friends who participated in Project Center of the Earth with us last time."

Chen Chaoguang was excited just thinking about it: "Wow! If those people could come, it would be absolutely amazing!"

Not to mention that these people all come from prestigious universities in Songhai and are elite representatives among the younger generation of cultivators, whose backgrounds are not stronger than Zhao Wuji's.

Guo Hao was also very excited: "We went through life and death with them in the Project Core, so we have a good relationship. Moreover, Li Changzhe said that many of those people dislike Zhao Wuji's bullying behavior. They all said they wanted to help Wang Ling!"

Wang Ling: "..."

...

Friday, 1 p.m.

Outside the Songhai City Gymnasium, there was a great deal of noise.

The quarterfinals garnered significantly more attention than the qualifiers, with students, parents, and media reporters from various schools crowding the square, and school flags fluttering in the wind.

The bus from Number 60 High School slowly drove into the parking lot.

The car door opened, and Lao Pan was the first to jump out. He turned around and shouted to the students inside, "Stay close! Don't run around! The competition starts at 2 PM, let's go to the waiting area first!"

The students got off the bus one after another. Chen Chao and Guo Hao naturally surrounded Wang Ling, while Li Changzhe got off from another bus at just the right moment and naturally mingled with Chen Chao and Guo Hao.

Wang Ling was wearing that dark blue tracksuit today, zipped up to his chest, with a white short-sleeved shirt underneath.

The dark-colored seal mark patch on her neck peeked out from her collar, and at first glance, it looked like a band-aid.

He was holding a half-eaten bag of instant noodles and putting pieces into his mouth one by one.

At the same time, a commotion suddenly broke out on the other side of the square.

The crowd automatically parted to form a passage.

Zhao Wuji stepped out of a black, extended psionic sedan.

Today he wore a black battle robe specially made by Physical Education High School, with dragon patterns embroidered in gold thread on the lapels and cuffs, which shimmered in the sunlight.

His bronze skin, muscular build, and angular face certainly gave him an imposing presence.

But what's even more eye-catching is the old man following behind him.

Zhao Tieshan.

He was dressed in a dark blue Zhongshan suit, his hair was neatly combed, and he was holding a cane carved with dragons.

Although he is old, he stands very straight, has sharp eyes, and walks with a brisk pace.

"Who is that?"

"Zhao Wuji's grandfather, I heard, was a big shot in the army."

"Jiang Lei's old comrade-in-arms! He's incredible!"

Discussions came from all directions.

Zhao Tieshan enjoyed the attention. He nodded slightly and greeted the familiar faces around him.

Those were all school principals, members of the judging panel, and some city leaders.

"Mr. Zhao, you've come too?" A middle-aged man wearing glasses came up and shook hands warmly.

"I came to watch my grandson's competition," Zhao Tieshan said with a smile. "I also took the opportunity to see some old friends. Sergeant Jiang Lei was also supposed to come, but something came up, so he asked me to give him his regards."

His voice wasn't loud, but it was loud enough for everyone around him to hear.

Jiang Lei, the Martial Saint.

These four words seemed to have a magical power, instantly changing the atmosphere around Zhao Tieshan.

Some people who were initially observing immediately joined in, exchanging pleasantries and business cards.

Zhao Tieshan accepted all requests and responded to each one, his smile growing increasingly composed.

Chen Chao watched this scene from afar, his teeth practically grinding to powder.

"Damn it, what a show-off." He muttered under his breath, "Using the title of Martial Saint to scare people."
