Chapter 2223 Mysterious Embroidered Pouch

January 25th, a quiet Sunday, the winter sun lazily shone on the streets and alleys of Songhai, and a faint chill permeated the air.

With only two days left until the highly anticipated destination's grand plan, the entire city is bustling with preparations for the upcoming momentous occasion.

On their winding journey to find Lao Huang, Wang Ling and Sun Rong had just experienced a minor unexpected incident, and only then did the tense atmosphere gradually ease.

Wang Ling, who was initially on edge, thought that this was some earth-shattering conspiracy that could overturn the entire cultivation world. Unexpectedly, when the truth came out, the source of the matter was just a ridiculous emotional scam.

In that instant, Wang Ling's slightly furrowed brows slowly relaxed, and the doubts in his heart dissipated.

As they were about to leave the somewhat eerie mental hospital, Sun Rong stopped, her eyes filled with concern, and softly asked Meng Nian if she still needed help.

After all, a mental hospital is like an invisible cage; it's easy to get in, but getting out is not so easy.

Moreover, this is in the modern cultivation world. If a cultivator with a high level of cultivation has a mental illness, the destructive power is many times greater than that of an ordinary person having a mental illness.

Those who suffer from cultivation-related illnesses have even acquired a special name – "cultivation-related mental illness".

Next, Meng Nian needs to complete a series of complicated mental assessments. Only after her condition is confirmed to be stable can she go through the discharge procedures.

Sun Rong's purpose in helping Meng Nian was actually very simple; she just hoped that Meng Nian could complete her redemption plan as soon as possible.

It's important to understand that Meng Nian harmed approximately a thousand lives, yet she needed to redeem a million times that number. How could that be easy? It was an almost unattainable goal, like climbing a towering mountain, where every step was fraught with hardship.

However, Meng Nian shook her head slightly in response to Sun Rong's well-intentioned help, her eyes revealing a hint of stubbornness and confidence.

Since she was able to choose to use a mental hospital as a cover, she must have had some connections. Her voice was calm and firm, as if she were telling Sun Rong about her abilities and determination.

Later, after a thorough investigation, Sun Rong learned that Meng Nian was actually a descendant of the Meng family, a prominent family of bannermen. However, because she and Meng Heng were illegitimate children, the Meng family did not publicly acknowledge their sibling relationship.

In today's cultivation industry, magical artifacts, elixirs, talismans, and array flags used to set up formations are all essential products. The Meng family's array flag manufacturing is second to none, both in terms of the completeness of the industrial chain and the quality of production.

It's no wonder Meng Nian has the ability to create such a large magic array, intending to drain the spiritual veins of the entire Songhai. With such a powerful family background, even the way she obtains resources is much easier than for ordinary people.

On their way back, Sun Rong and Wang Ling took a special subway train invested and built by the Huaguo Water Curtain Group.

This brand-new track, like a giant silver dragon, lies quietly beneath the pine forest. It is only open to the public on a trial basis at specific times, and is full of mystery and nobility.

As the eldest daughter of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, Sun Rong only needed to scan her face to slowly open the door that symbolized privilege, and she gracefully led Wang Ling inside.

She dismissed the group of bodyguards who had come uninvited, like leeches stuck to her skin. Although the bodyguards were somewhat unwilling, they had no choice but to leave.

Sun Rong and Wang Ling sat alone in the private room, soft lighting casting a warm glow on them. Sun Rong's cheeks were slightly flushed, and her eyes held a hint of shyness.

She lowered her head, looking shyly at the embroidered pouch that Meng Nian had given her. The pouch was small and exquisite, embroidered with beautiful patterns, and exuded a faint spiritual aura.

Sun Rong had long heard that the embroidered pouches from Mount Immortality were extremely effective, but Mount Immortality was not in Songhai. Due to her heavy studies and the various training sessions for family leaders, Sun Rong had never had the opportunity to go there. Unexpectedly, she actually managed to obtain an embroidered pouch from Mount Immortality from Meng Nian during this opportunity.

Her heart was filled with surprise and anticipation, as if she had grasped a precious treasure.

"I hope... Sister Meng Nian can embark on her redemption plan soon..." Sun Rong said softly, her lips slightly moving as she held the embroidered pouch. She glanced cautiously at Wang Ling beside her, only to find this straight-laced man deep in thought, his chin resting on his hand.

Wang Ling gave a soft "hmm," his voice low and calm, as if he were deep in thought.

At this moment, Wang Ling was pondering the reason why Meng Nian was chosen by the underworld.

To be honest, if he hadn't witnessed the power emanating from the underworld in Meng Nian this time, Wang Ling would have thought that the existence of the underworld was just a legend of the past before exploring the inner world.

The fact that an envoy from the underworld could possess combat power at the level of a human ancestor was terrifying, exceeding Wang Ling's expectations.

Since the underworld exists, there must also be a Hades King, but whether he is good or bad is unknown.

Based on Meng Nian's information, Wang Ling can roughly guess some of the selection criteria for people in the underworld.

For example, a cultivator who experiences intense emotional fluctuations in a short period of time and falls into a state of mental abnormality is very likely to become a target of the underworld and be identified as a messenger.

His eyes were deep and focused, as if he wanted to see through the fog before him to the mysterious underworld.

Seeing the boy deep in thought, Sun Rong suddenly seemed to remember something and couldn't help but ask, "Wang Ling... are you also thinking about what Sister Meng Nian said?"

Just as Meng Nian gave herself the embroidered pouch, she told Sun Rong to "cherish the people in front of you" and then said to Wang Ling, "Immortal, so are you."

Sun Rong felt that this could not be called a hint at all, but a blatant declaration. So, along the way, Sun Rong was actually reflecting on herself. She felt that her behavior was not so obvious.

Even a woman I've only known for a short time could tell at a glance that I like Wang Ling...

Faced with Sun Rong's question, Wang Ling simply blinked, revealing a big question mark.

His mind was filled with thoughts about Meng Nian and the underworld.

Whether the underworld exists or not is actually irrelevant to Wang Ling, but the fear is that the underworld might be used by people with ill intentions. At this time, Bai Zhe is preparing for the War of Destiny. If he gets the help of the underworld, Bai Zhe's Dragonborn Legion will be even more powerful with the help of the underworld.

At that time, all human cultivators will only fall into an even greater crisis.

Therefore, Wang Ling did not consider so many personal matters related to children at all.

His heart...

It exists solely for the sake of the cultivation world and the stability of human cultivators...
