Chapter 2286 Wang Muyu's Acting Skills Explode

The back mountain of the Wuji Sword Sect.

Wang Muyu sat cross-legged on the bluestone, his small face wrinkled in distress, his golden dragon eyes filled with focus.

He stretched out his fair little hand, palm up, trying to bind the vast, star-like power within his body into a single golden thread thinner than a hair.

"Buzz..." A wisp of savage dragon power overflowed, and with a "pop," a small ball of air exploded at his fingertips, producing a soft bang.

"It really is difficult..." Wang Muyu was startled and quickly withdrew his hand.

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan wasn't present; he had been invited by Thunder Lord to discuss important matters, naturally concerning that audacious "counter-strategy" plan. Only Wang Muyu remained here for special training.

"Controlling power... Even Dad can't do it, how could I possibly do it!" the little guy muttered, kicking the pebbles under his feet in frustration.

The stone flew out and directly blasted flat a large mountain in the distance within the territory of the Wuji Sword Sect.

"..." Wang Muyu, who had caused the trouble, was quite helpless and had no choice but to release the dragon's breath, which had restorative power, to restore the mess to its original state.

His little brain couldn't understand why Wang Ling wanted him to learn how to control his power.

All he knew was that if he couldn't learn to "play weak," his plan would fall through, and he would only cause trouble for his father and teacher.

My father rarely asks anything of me, so I can't let him down!

With this in mind, he mustered his courage again and stretched out his little hand, trying even more carefully to restrain the surging power within his body.

This time, he tried hard to recall the feeling of stagnation he had experienced when he was suppressed by the Extinction Domain.

Gradually, the brilliant golden light emanating from his body began to subside, and the soft, bulging dragon horns on his forehead seemed to dim further.

Although it's far from perfect control, at least it looks... less dazzling.

"Yes! That's it!" Wang Muyu's eyes lit up slightly, feeling that he had grasped a bit of the knack.

Just then, he suddenly felt four extremely terrifying auras pierce through the sword intent barrier surrounding the Wuji Sword Sect and lock onto him with unparalleled precision!

They're here!

Wang Muyu's heart skipped a beat, and he almost lost control of the force he was trying to control.

Almost simultaneously, Wang Lingping's calm and undisturbed voice echoed in his mind.

It was just three simple words: "According to the plan".

As if he had taken the most effective reassurance, Wang Muyu instantly felt at ease.

The initial panic of being discovered quickly faded, replaced by a sense of tension and excitement, like playing a role.

He pretended to jump down from the bluestone in a panic, trying to make his breathing appear even weaker and more unstable, and squeezed out just the right amount of fear on his small face as he looked up at the sky.

Boom!

Four streaks of light, carrying an aura of earth-shattering power, ignored the numerous restrictions within the Sword Sect and descended brazenly over the sky above the back mountain!

Blazing flames, deep sea, southeast wind, imprisonment!

The four Heavenly Ancestor-level Dragon Descendants hovered in mid-air, each with a different form, their cold gazes pressing down on Wang Muyu as if they were real.

The terrifying dragon's might intertwined, causing the entire space behind the mountain to begin to distort.

The land within the burial grounds is cracking, scorching, or freezing at a visible rate!

If it weren't for True Monarch Thunder's prior communication with Sword Saint, which led to the evacuation of core disciples and the reinforcement of certain areas, the mere confrontation of their auras would have been enough to cause cultivators below the Golden Core stage to explode and die!

"Tch, so this is the Son of Ten Thousand Dragons? Doesn't look that impressive." Huang Yan crossed his arms, his dark red scales shimmering with a magma-like sheen. His tone was arrogant, tinged with disappointment: "His aura is unstable and inconsistent, quite different from what's described in the database. Jueqin, could your data be wrong?"

Deep Sea's cold, vertical pupils swept over Wang Muyu, and he frowned slightly: "Indeed... very weak. It doesn't feel as solid as the core of the Death Star that I froze and shattered."

Xunfeng's figure left trails of afterimages in the air, his voice drifting and teasing: "This little guy will be easy to catch."

Only Jueqi, with his molten gold-filled vertical pupils fixed on Wang Muyu, his internal analysis system frantically operating, yet producing a series of contradictory data.

[Energy Reaction Level: Violent fluctuations, between the peak of Dao Ancestor and the early stage of Heavenly Ancestor]

[Condition Simulation: Suspected serious injury or loss of control of strength]

[Threat Assessment: Extremely high potential, but a 75% capture probability]

"Go ahead and do it," Jueqi said coldly.

Without any hesitation, he instantly pointed the Endless Silence Spear at Wang Muyu, and the Annihilation Domain was activated first, with the power of deathly desolation surging downwards like a tide!

Almost simultaneously, Huangyan's World-Burning Black Flame, Shenming's Eternal Silent Ice Breath, and Xunfeng's Dimensional Wind Blade blocked all of Wang Muyu's escape routes from three other directions!

The Four Heavenly Ancestors joined forces for one strike!

A star powerful enough to evaporate instantly!

Wang Muyu's face turned deathly pale instantly. This was not an act; it was a genuine instinctive reaction to an extreme threat to his life!

The supreme dragon power within him was almost roaring, ready to erupt and retaliate on its own!

At this critical moment!

"Buzz—!"

An invisible yet indescribably vast will descended from beyond the heavens, gently enveloping the entire back mountain of the Wuji Sword Sect.

At this moment, time and space underwent an extremely subtle change.

Wang Muyu felt a sudden lightness around him. The four attacks that were enough to crush him, as well as the terrifying pressure brought by the four dragon descendants, seemed to be separated by an invisible absolute barrier.

Immediately afterwards, the ground beneath his feet and the surrounding scenery... shattered into pieces like a broken mirror!

It's not about breaking down, it's about transformation!

The rules of space have been forcibly rewritten!

The four dragon descendants only saw a blur before their eyes, and their attacks missed!

The next second, they found themselves no longer on that blue planet!

We've actually arrived in a vast and boundless deep space of space!

In the distance, the Milky Way shines brightly, and nebulae swirl.

Nearby, only the floating meteorite belt and a red giant star about to burn out in the distance emitted a dim light.

"Outer space?" Huang Yan was taken aback, then grinned savagely. "Which idiot did this space teleportation? Didn't they know this place is more suitable for us to make our mark?!"

Feeling the absolute zero vacuum environment, Deep Sea stretched its ice-crystal-like scales comfortably: "It saves us the effort of breaking through the star barrier."

Xunfeng's figure moved faster and more strangely in the vacuum, emitting a silent shriek: "This is even more fun!"

Jueqi's molten gold-like vertical pupils suddenly scanned the surroundings, and the analysis system frantically issued an alarm: "High-dimensional space swap! This is not ordinary teleportation! A supreme being is interfering! Be vigilant!"

Directly below them, the familiar blue planet was slowly rotating, and the continental plate where the Wuji Sword Sect was located was clearly visible.

Wang Muyu was suspended on a huge meteorite fragment, looking weak and helpless.

"Playing tricks!" Huang Yan couldn't contain himself any longer: "Let's catch this little bug first!"

He roared, his dragon body swelled, and he directly revealed part of the true form of the Hellfire Black Flame Dragon. A giant claw covered in black flames tore through the vacuum, carrying terrifying heat capable of burning stars, and grabbed directly at Wang Muyu!

This time, he held nothing back!

At the same time, the Deep Sea opened its mouth, and its extremely cold breath spread silently. Wherever it passed, even the space itself seemed to be frozen, forming an absolute frozen domain, which was suppressed in conjunction with the Blazing Flame.

Xunfeng's figure vanished completely, like wind merging into the void, waiting for an opportunity to launch a deadly sneak attack and interference.

Absolute was extremely cautious. The Final Silence Spear trembled, and the Annihilation Domain expanded to its fullest extent. It was not aimed at Wang Muyu, but rather vigilantly scanned the surrounding universe, guarding against the possible "Supreme Intervener".

Faced with this even more terrifying combined attack than before, Wang Muyu's heart pounded wildly.

According to the plan, he should struggle and resist for a while, and then pretend to be defeated and captured.

He struggled to muster his strength, attempting to portray an outburst of panic.

Golden dragon power surged from his body, transforming into a slightly diffused pillar of light that met the blazing giant claw.

At the same time, he waved his little hands haphazardly, unleashing several dragon-shaped bursts of energy that seemed quite powerful but completely missed their mark. These bursts flew into the distant deep space, blasting several passing small meteorites into dust.

"Boom!!!"

The giant claw of the blazing flames collided with Wang Muyu's golden beam of light!

The result was beyond Huang Yan's expectations!

The scattered beam of light contained extremely strong resilience and a kind of hierarchical suppression.

Although it was retreating steadily, it did not collapse instantly! In fact, the golden power turned around and burned his black flame dragon claws, bringing a stinging pain!

"Huh?!" Huang Yan was taken aback, increasing the force of his dragon claw: "You do have some skill! But you're still too green!"

The black flames surged, finally crushing the golden pillar of light, and continued to claw downwards!

Wang Mu crossed his arms in front of him, his internal energy protecting him, and a solid, seven-colored dragon scale phantom appeared!

"Bang!"

The dragon claw slammed into the phantom of the dragon scales, producing a dull, deafening roar!

Wang Muyu was sent flying backward like a meteor, crashing hard into the dense meteorite field in the distance. He smashed through dozens of meteorites of varying sizes before finally coming to a stop, his face pale and a trickle of golden blood even spilling from the corner of his mouth.
