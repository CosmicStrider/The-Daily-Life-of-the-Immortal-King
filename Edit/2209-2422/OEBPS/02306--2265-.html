Chapter 2265 Each Person's Adventure

Deep within the Thousand Caves City, darkness swallows all light and sound, with only the faint fluorescent glow emanating from the rock walls themselves outlining the eerie contours of this enormous underground labyrinth.

The air was heavy, with dust that had accumulated for thousands of years spreading out, mixed with a suffocating pressure that weighed on everyone's nerves.

Liu Qingyi was engulfed in almost tangible purple divine flames. She threw a punch, and a section of rock wall in front of her instantly shattered, scattering rocks everywhere.

"Ryoko! Kunomiya Ryoko! Answer me if you hear me!" Her voice, filled with barely suppressed anxiety and anger, echoed through the intricate passages, only attracting the rustling movements of some subterranean creatures further away.

She had been searching in what she believed to be the right direction for a long time, but still couldn't find any trace of Ryoko Kumiya.

The connection between the arrays was completely severed by the chaotic psionic field here, and the feeling of powerlessness from losing her companions made her extremely anxious.

"Damn glass monsters! Don't let me catch you!" Liu Qingyi gritted her teeth, and another beam of divine light swept away several shadow hounds that pounced from her side.

She worried that with her limited cultivation, Kyuumiya Ryoko wouldn't be able to hold out for long in this perilous maze if she encountered an enemy.

...

Meanwhile, in another, completely different area of the maze.

Ryoko Kugimiya leaned against a stone wall covered with unknown runes, breathing heavily.

The array plate in her hand flashed intensely, and countless fine cracks spread across the light screen like a spider web, clearly indicating that it was already overwhelmed.

The passage she was in was not naturally formed; its walls were smooth as a mirror and stretched into endless darkness.

It was eerily quiet here; there were no shouts of battle or roars of beasts outside, only a persistent, almost ear-piercing, psychic buzzing.

"The Kan position has shifted, the Li palace has reversed... the Dui lake has collapsed... This is not a natural maze at all! This is... a pre-heavenly Qimen formation that is constantly evolving on its own!"

Ryoko Kunomiya's face was pale, her fingertips flying across the nearly broken array board, beads of sweat rolling down her forehead.

She tried to find a pattern, but the array changed far faster than her calculations could keep up.

Each simulation brought back increasingly chaotic and complex data, the backlash causing blood to seep from her tiger's mouth.

"No... the computing power is insufficient... if this continues, we will be completely trapped or assimilated and absorbed by this formation..."

A feeling of despair crept into my heart.

Just then, a series of ripples spread out on the smooth wall in front of them.

Three formed from flowing glass.

A figure, identical in appearance to her, Liu Qingyi, and Sun Rong, slowly materialized.

Their faces bore the anxious and concerned expressions of their original owners, but their eyes were cold and empty, like marionettes.

"Liangzi! We've finally found you! Come with us, it's safe here!" The fake Liu Qingyi stretched out her hand, mimicking the tone perfectly.

"Sun Rong and Wang Ling are waiting for us up ahead, come quickly!" The fake Sun Rong's voice was soft and coaxing.

The fake Kyuumei Ryoko even picked up a fake array plate that was also covered in cracks and said anxiously, "My array plate can't hold up any longer, we must leave this energy node immediately!"

They approached step by step, the malevolent threads on their bodies, almost blending into the darkness, trembled slightly, manipulating this perfect performance, attempting to break down Ryoko Kumiya's last psychological defenses.

Ryoko Kunomiya's heart pounded, and she gasped for breath.

Even though she knew it was fake, the extreme imitation and psychological attack still caused her to feel momentarily dazed and unsettled.

She subconsciously took a half step back, her back pressed tightly against the cold stone wall, with nowhere left to retreat.

The alarm from the array became sharp and piercing, as if mourning her impending doom.

...

"Click".

The crisp chewing sounds were particularly clear in the quiet passageway.

Wang Ling stopped and swallowed the last thin slice of mineral salt.

His calm gaze swept over Sun Rong's slightly nervous face beside him, then fell on a seemingly ordinary dead end to the left.

The passage was blocked by huge, strange stone shoots, exuding a decaying aura, as if no one had set foot there for thousands of years.

Sun Rong followed his gaze and saw a slight tremor emanating from the scabbard of the Ao Hai sword, indicating an abnormal energy accumulation there, but she did not sense any direct threat.

"Wang Ling, over there..."

Before she could finish speaking, Wang Ling had already stretched out his finger and lightly tapped the strange stone shoots blocking the passage in mid-air.

There were no earth-shattering sounds, no dazzling special effects.

The massive clump of stone shoots, enough to take a Golden Core cultivator half a day to bombard, melted away silently and rapidly from the spot where Wang Ling pointed, as if ice and snow had been thrown into strong acid!

It didn't shatter, but was completely reduced to the finest dust, falling softly as if it had never existed.

A brand new, deep, and narrow passage appeared before the two of them.

The inner walls of the passage were smooth, shimmering with a faint echo of spatial ripples.

Sun Rong opened her mouth slightly. Although she was already used to Wang Ling's unfathomable power, she was still shocked every time she witnessed such a "words become law" approach.

Wang Ling's expression remained unchanged, as if he had merely brushed away a speck of dust.

He turned to the side, gesturing for Sun Rong to follow, and then stepped into the new passageway first.

The passageway was very narrow, allowing only one person to squeeze through. Sun Rong followed closely behind Wang Ling, walking for about ten steps.

They somehow escaped the intricate main passage of the maze and arrived at an incredible place.

This is a huge natural grotto with a high dome and countless stalactites emitting a soft white light hanging upside down.

In the center of the grotto, there is a calm, dark lake that exudes an astonishingly pure and spiritual aura, yet its depths are unfathomable.

The most peculiar thing is that the walls of the grotto are not cold rock, but a crystal-like transparent material.

Through the crystal wall, one can vaguely see the scenes inside countless maze passages, like a huge monitoring center!

Inside a passageway, Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe were locked in a fierce battle back to back, their armor glowing and shimmering as arrows whistled through the air.

They also saw Chen Chao and Guo Hao awkwardly dodging the spike traps, encouraging each other.

Further away, Wang Zhen and Fang Xing moved swiftly like ghosts, seemingly clearing out something.

On the clearest crystal wall, Liu Qingyi's anxious and fierce attack was clearly reflected, as well as the scene of Kugimiya Ryoko being trapped in a desperate situation and cornered by three glass imitations!

"This is... one of the core nodes of Thousand Caves City?" Sun Rong suddenly realized why Wang Ling had brought her here directly.

This place not only offers a panoramic view, but may also hold the key to leaving or even controlling parts of the maze.

Her gaze was immediately drawn to the perilous situation facing Ryoko Kunomiya, and her heart leaped into her throat: "Ryoko is in danger! And what about Senior Liu...?"

Wang Ling's gaze also fell on the crystal wall, his eyes lingering for a moment on the three glass imitations, a very subtle hint of impatience flashing deep in his dead fish eyes.

These insects just never seem to stop.

He didn't intervene immediately. Instead, he glanced at the anxious Sun Rong beside him, then raised his hand and pointed to the dark lake.

Sun Rong didn't understand his meaning, but out of absolute trust, she still followed his guidance and looked at the lake.

The calm lake surface, like a mirror, began to ripple, revealing a more detailed maze of energy flow patterns!

Countless threads of light intertwined and flowed across the lake's surface, representing the core of an array that was in operation!

"This is... the energy center of the maze?" Sun Rong exclaimed.

The power of the Ocean Sword Core and the Earth Core Crimson Lotus resonated simultaneously, making her perception of this energy flow exceptionally clear.

Wang Ling nodded slightly.

He walked to the lakeside, squatted down, and gently touched the surface of the lake with his index finger.

Thump.

A soft sound, like a heartbeat.

Centered on his fingertips, a gentle yet irresistible thought spread across the entire lake like ripples, and through some mysterious connection, rapidly extended into the entire Thousand Caves City labyrinth!

In an instant, all the shadowy tentacles and malevolent illusions that were raging within the maze and were directly controlled by the Outer God's will suddenly came to a halt, as if a pause button had been pressed!

The shadowy figures surrounding Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe became sluggish and stiff in their movements.

The terrifying hallucinations that plagued Chen Chao and Guo Hao began to fade like a screen with a poor signal.

The remaining members of the Glazed Clan in front of Wang Zhen and Fang Xing let out a miserable scream. The illusion on the surface of their bodies collapsed instantly, revealing their true Glazed nature, and their movements became chaotic and disordered!

Wang Ling's move was not a direct attack, but rather a brief overlay of the Outer God's control over the labyrinth's energy with a will of higher authority!
