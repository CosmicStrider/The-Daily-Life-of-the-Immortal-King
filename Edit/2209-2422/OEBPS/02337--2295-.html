Chapter 2295 The Quietly Spreading Temptation

The secret door to the back room of the experience store quietly closed, shutting out the eerie dark red light and the whispers of the evil cultivators.

Wang Ling's consciousness silently withdrew from this area.

His eyes showed no emotion; he simply memorized the aura of those evil cultivators and the serial numbers of those specially "treated" [Taste Master] products.

This time, the "remedial plan" of the evil cultivators of the Primordial Era is simple and effective: taking advantage of young people's curiosity about new things and their desire to quickly improve their cultivation level, they directly present temptations to them.

In this era where everything strives for high efficiency, such temptation is undoubtedly deadly.

Especially Number 60 High School, which is clearly under special attention, and Wang Ling guessed that this time they should be much smarter and would definitely avoid giving priority to students from the elite class...

Instead, the focus is on students in regular classes who have average grades and are looking to quickly catch up.

The effects of this item are enough to drive any ordinary student with average talent to complete madness!

...

The campus of Number 60 High School was a little more agitated by the news that the Hip Hop Group had donated "Taste Master".

During breaks, this amazing device was being discussed in almost every corner.

"I heard that Li Hao from Class 3, his cousin works at XiBai and got one early with the employee discount! He said he ate his mom's ordinary spirit energy steamed bun with this thing last night and it felt like eating high-level spirit beast meat! It's incredibly powerful for cultivation!" A boy described vividly.

"Really? That's unbelievable!"

"Why would I lie to you! Didn't you watch the forum live stream? The results are amazing! It's said that long-term use can really improve your physical attributes!"

"And then there's Wang Jiahao, an ordinary student who has repeated a grade for ten years straight! He's broken the school's all-time record for repeating a grade!"

The student continued to talk incessantly, "But Jiahao received the equipment from Xibei as soon as he got home from school yesterday. In just one night, his cultivation level improved a little just from drinking cola. This morning, he went to the Spirit Gathering Test Platform for evaluation, and his overall quality has improved by leaps and bounds! He probably won't be repeating a grade this year!"

Similar conversations are frequently heard on campus.

A feeling called longing quietly grew in the hearts of every young monk on the campus of Number 60 High School.

For most students who are still struggling to build a foundation, this seemingly side-effect-free shortcut is too tempting.

Especially after experiencing the "Ground Center Project" at Number 60 High School, although Number 60 High School has a strong sense of collective honor, such obvious resource bias towards the elite class still makes people somewhat concerned.

This planted a deep thorn in the hearts of students who were already less gifted...

Moreover, after hearing that Chen Chao and Guo Hao had returned from the Inner Earth World Project and broken through to the Foundation Establishment stage in one fell swoop, the sense of disparity, like the widening gap between the rich and the poor, was like a heavy blow, severely impacting the dormant minds of many fellow students.

That's why they have to put in more effort and be more disciplined than the students in the elite classes!

If they don't work harder, they will still be among the group that will be eliminated in the future cultivation society.

Under such a trend, the impact is naturally enormous, which means that equipment like the [Taste Master], which clearly contains trap elements, will become a source of ecstasy for many lower-level cultivators.

Especially after using it for a period of time and experiencing this rapid improvement comparable to "heretical cultivation," they could no longer stop...

When their inner desires are fully aroused, these people's mentality will gradually become insane.

...

At this moment, the atmosphere in the elite classroom where Wang Ling was located was somewhat subtle.

The bell rang, and the homeroom teacher, Ms. Pan, walked into the classroom with her usual warm smile, but there seemed to be a hint of helplessness hidden deep in her eyes.

"Class, please be quiet. I'm sure you've all heard about the donation of the 'Taste Master' experience equipment from the HiPai Group. The school has received the specific allocation plan."

The classroom fell silent instantly, and all eyes were on Teacher Pan.

Chen Chao, in particular, stared wide-eyed, full of anticipation.

Teacher Pan cleared his throat and said, "Well... it's like this, based on the suggestion from the HiPai Group and the school's overall consideration, this batch of experiential equipment will be mainly allocated to other students in the regular classes who are not particularly outstanding in talent."

As soon as he finished speaking, a low murmur rippled through the classroom.

"Why, Teacher Pan?" Chen Chao was the first to exclaim, "Don't we, the elite class, need to experience new technologies? Maybe they'll be of great help to us!"

"That's right, Teacher Pan, this isn't fair!" The other students chimed in.

The arrogance of the elite class made them feel ignored.

Teacher Pan gestured with his hands to quiet everyone and explained, "Everyone, please be patient and listen to me. The XiBai Group's intention is that the product 'Taste Master' aims to help cultivators better discover the spiritual energy potential in their daily diet. It is especially beneficial for students with shallow cultivation foundations who are still in the early stages of their training, as the effects may be more significant and better reflect the product's universality."

She paused, her gaze sweeping over the "elites" below the stage who were already far beyond their peers, her tone reassuring: "The school also believes that the students in the elite class are exceptionally gifted, and their path of cultivation should focus more on their own insights."

"This kind of external auxiliary equipment is not recommended as a priority at the moment. Of course, this is just the first batch of experience. If the effect is indeed good, the school will try to equip everyone with it."

This was said in a tactful way, but the meaning was very clear: You are too excellent. This new product should be prioritized for ordinary students who need it more.

This actually aligns with China's consistent policy of allowing the wealthy to help the less fortunate, and the level of cultivation is, in a sense, a reflection of the wealth gap.

As a century-old school, Number 60 High School already devotes enough resources to its elite classes, so there is no need to show favoritism to these students on this new free trial product.

This involves considerations such as precise targeting according to the requirements of the Hip Hop Group, as well as the school's protection of the elite class, fearing that they might become too dependent on external resources.

Chen Chao opened his mouth, as if he wanted to say something, but was pulled back by Guo Hao next to him.

Guo Hao said in a low voice, "Teacher Pan has a point. We really don't need to rely on this. Besides, I always feel that this thing is a bit weird."

Sun Rong, sitting in the front row, nodded slightly upon hearing this, and subconsciously looked at Wang Ling by the window.

Wang Ling remained as still as a meditating monk, seemingly oblivious to the surrounding controversy.

But Sun Rong noticed that his eyes seemed to glance at several students from other classes in the corridor outside the classroom, who were excitedly discussing the equipment they were about to receive.

"He really does know something..." Sun Rong's heart sank even further.

This feeling of being excluded from welfare did not make her feel disappointed; instead, it aroused her vigilance.

Wang Ling's mind was as clear as a mirror.

The HiPai Group, or rather the powerful forces behind it, played this move very cleverly.
