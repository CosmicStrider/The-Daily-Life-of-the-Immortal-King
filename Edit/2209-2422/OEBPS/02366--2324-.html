Chapter 2324 The Principal's Etiquette

The valley was deathly silent, except for the heavy breathing of Black 201, which was particularly jarring.

He sat slumped on the cold, eerie black earth, his face ashen, cold sweat soaking the back of his black robe.

Although the ghostly hand, formed from resentment and evil energy, dissipated, its lingering chill still lingered in everyone's hearts.

Black 112 took a step forward, releasing its spiritual pressure of Foundation Establishment Great Perfection without any concealment, pressing down on Black 201.

"Are you mute? Be honest and tell me!" His voice was deep and menacing: "Fragments! Black soil! And everything you know about the 'Secret Manual of Resurrection'! Don't try anything funny, or don't blame me for being impolite..."

Lin Susu toyed with the poisoned dagger in her hand, its green light shimmering eerily in the dim light. She scoffed, "Looks like someone tried to hog all the credit, but they messed up. Black 201, what benefits did the people behind you promise you? That you'd even betray your own people?"

The other men in gray also gathered around, their eyes filled with anger and lingering fear.

The ghostly hand was clearly aimed at everyone; if it weren't for Wang Ling's unexpected kick, the consequences would have been unimaginable.

At this moment, their gazes toward Black 201 held no trace of camaraderie, only intense hostility.

Zhou Ping hid behind Wang Ling, his small hands tightly gripping the hem of Wang Ling's clothes. His body was still trembling slightly, but his gaze toward Black 201 was filled with indignation.

Wang Ling remained silent, standing a short distance away, as if he were an indifferent bystander.

But deep within his lowered eyes, his spiritual awareness was quietly observing every emotional fluctuation and soul tremor of Black 201.

Wang Ling knew very well that this Black 201 was definitely the player who opened the eyes to the "Gate of the Abyss" mission.

Those higher-level "shadowers" likely leaked the questions to him in advance through some means, letting him know the mission rules and related background stories of the world behind the door.

This phenomenon, though rare in the homes of lurkers, does exist.

As for how the higher-level shadow agents obtained the contents behind the door, it is currently unknown.

Under the intense gaze of everyone, Black 201's lips trembled, and his eyes frantically scanned his surroundings, as if searching for a non-existent escape route.

He knew he was finished.

When his scheme is exposed, he will face a terrible fate in this cruelly rule-bound home of lurkers.

He was especially struck by the thought of the punishment he would face if the mission failed, a punishment far more terrifying than death.

"I...I'll tell you..."

He finally broke down, his voice dry and hoarse, "It was...it was Lord Dark Star...giving me instructions..."

"Dark Star?" Black 112's pupils contracted. That was a true high-ranking member of the Shadow Walkers, with the authority to mobilize resources and issue orders, far superior to them, these black-robed and gray-clad individuals.

"Yes..." Black201 swallowed hard and said with difficulty, "Lord Dark Star said that the key to this mission of ‘Deep Mountain Pill Garden' lies in... Pill Induction."

"Dan Yin?" Lin Susu frowned. "The 'Secret Manual of Resurrection' mentions living beings as 'Dan Yin'?"

"Not entirely..." Black 201 shook his head, a smile more painful than a grimace appearing on his face: "Ordinary beings... are not qualified. This Soul-Returning Pill requires... the headmaster's own soul to be refined!"

His words caused a huge uproar!

Everyone was stunned by these words.

How can the head of the academy refine his own soul?

So, who exactly is this Rejuvenation Pill for?

Is it the headmaster himself?

What exactly is something that needs to refine its own soul?

"Continue!" Black 112 suppressed his shock and shouted sharply.

"Master Yuan... he's no longer alive." Black 201's voice was filled with fear: "He is the aggregate of countless resentments and the remnant souls of failed alchemists in this alchemy garden! He needs a perfect alchemy guide to carry the Resurrection Pill he is about to successfully refine, so that he can truly be resurrected, or become some kind of even more terrifying being!"

He pointed to the black soil and fragments on the ground: "This black soil is key to cultivating the Yin Soul Grass, and the Yin Soul Grass is a crucial auxiliary ingredient for stabilizing the properties of the Resurrection Pill, growing only on this extremely Yin and resentful soil. As for the fragments of the alchemy furnace... they retain the soul power imprints of those who failed in the past. Lord Dark Star gave me a secret method to combine the fragments in a specific way and guide the Yin energy in the black soil, which can temporarily activate the residual power in the fragments, forming a special kind of 'feed' marker..."

"Feed markings?"

Lin Susu astutely grasped the key point: "What's attracting this bait label? The headmaster? Or something else?"

Black 201 stammered, "It's...it's the Cleanser that attracts! The thing that killed someone last night! It's a byproduct of the Priest's power, responsible for eliminating any living creature that doesn't follow the rules, or...is unsuitable to be a Dan Guide!"

A chill instantly ran down everyone's spines.

It turns out that last night's death was not an accidental violation of the rules, but rather that it was cleaned up!

Was the deceased deemed an "unsuitable Dan-in"?

"What about etiquette?" Black 112 pressed. "The book says that one should prepare proper etiquette when meeting the headmaster!"

"Etiquette... is what makes a qualified Dan Yin!" Black 201's voice was filled with despair: "A... living person with a pure soul, vigorous life force, and extremely resentful spirit!"

He abruptly raised his head, his gaze sweeping over everyone present, his eyes turning somewhat sinister: "Each of us, from the moment we entered here, has been being selected! The fear and all the negative emotions caused by our mutual suspicion... will leave a mark of resentment on our souls. The greater the resentment, the more we meet the standards for a qualified pill extractor! Lord Dark Star had me activate the bait to accelerate this process, attracting the Cleaners to reap the rewards..."

A suffocating silence descended.

Everyone felt a stream of ice water being poured over their heads.

They tried their best to complete the mission and survive, but little did they know that they were fish on someone else's chopping board from the very beginning!

The so-called rules are nothing more than tools for screening and cultivating "Dan Yin"!

"So... you knew all along! You were helping that Dark Star choose a suitable Dan Yin for him!" a gray-clad girl shrieked, her voice distorted with fear.

Black201 didn't deny it, only gave a bitter smile: "I didn't want to either... but Lord Dark Star promised that if I helped him complete this mission, he would help me advance to the Bloodline level. I don't want to be just a black-robed figure forever..."

Greed, fear, and a thirst for power led him to betray and exploit his companions.

"Damn it!" Black 112 roared, grabbing Black 201 by the collar. "Tell me! How do I get rid of the bait mark? How do I get out of this hellhole!"

"It's useless..." Black 201's eyes were empty: "Once the bait mark is planted, unless it becomes a pill catalyst and is refined, or its soul is completely destroyed, it will not escape the cleaner's perception as long as it is still in this pill garden. And... time is running out."

He looked up at the gray sky, his voice trembling: "The bait marker has been activated once, and although it was interrupted, the aura has already dissipated. The Cleaners will return soon. And next time, they might be even more powerful... there might be more than one..."

Panic spread rapidly among the remaining people like a plague.

Zhou Ping was so frightened that he couldn't cry out, but he clung tightly to Wang Ling's arm.

The other men in gray were also pale-faced and almost unsteady on their feet.

Lin Susu's face was ashen, and her fingers gripping the dagger turned white from the force.

She finally understood why her "family" had all died before, but she was the only one who survived.

It wasn't due to luck, but rather because the rules of the Abyss Gate mission, while seemingly under strict regulations, actually allowed higher-level Shadows to manipulate things behind the scenes.

Her survival might simply be due to some special value she possesses, which allows her to survive time and again in the world behind the door...

Perhaps she had already been targeted by some dark star.

Black 112 released Black 201, staggered back two steps, and looked utterly dejected.

Knowing the truth only made people more desperate.
