Trouble always finds its way to you, like a shadow you can't shake off.

The remaining liquid at the bottom of the cup refracted a sliver of cold light.

Wang Ling felt like Iron Man now.

Wang Ling's fingers, which were holding the cup, paused almost imperceptibly.

It's Sun Rong.

His consciousness naturally "slid" toward where she was.

That direction... does seem to be not far away.

Deep within the earth, a "Crystal Forest of Illusory Light"

"The spiritual energy field here is incredibly strong, distorting all electromagnetic and spiritual signals! It's even more extreme than the simulated extreme environment in the testing ground!"

Liu Qingyi leaned against a smooth, cold crystal pillar, arms crossed, observing the distorted light and shadow around her with great interest: "The environment is indeed strange, but so far no dangerous creatures have been detected. I wonder where that guy Wang Zhen has gone."

"Number: DS-1221".

A three-dimensional model slowly appears on the screen.

"Okay, it's been recorded. Next one."

Sun Rong straightened up and looked at a huge cluster of mushrooms not far away, whose leaf edges shimmered with a faint golden light.

This would probably be classified as a fungus.

As soon as she took a step, a bright blue spirit mark on the inside of her wrist glowed slightly—it was the automatic warning from the Aohai Sword Sheath when it sensed a potential threat.

There was no strong sense of murderous intent or being locked on; it was more like... being spied on?

She instinctively tightened her grip on her wrist.

"What's wrong?" Liu Qingyi keenly noticed Sun Rong's slight pause.

"It's nothing."

Sun Rong shook her head, dispelling the inexplicable unease in her heart, and refocused the scanning beam on the target. "It might be spiritual pressure sensitivity caused by the disorder of the spiritual energy field."

She was manipulating the terminal interface with her fingers.

"Number DS-1038..."

Liu Qingyi and Sun Rong were both drawn to this change and looked at Jiugong Liangzi.

"Huh?! The signal's back?"

Kyuumiya Yoshiko raised the array plate in disbelief.

On the screen, the outline of the map around the green dot representing their location, which was originally blurry and distorted, became clearer!

More importantly, the yellow beacon indicating the base's location was flashing steadily, pointing southwest!

"A cave dwelling?" Liu Qingyi moved closer and looked at the white sign that had suddenly appeared. "Why is this place marked as a cave dwelling? Zhuo Yi's information didn't mention this place."

"This direction..."

Sun Rong followed the beacon's direction, looking southwest towards the area obscured by the giant crystal and distorted light: "It seems to match the general direction that Senior Wang Ming vaguely mentioned before, the approximate direction of Wang Ling...?"

Her heart skipped a beat unconsciously.

"Master Ling..." Liu Qingyi raised an eyebrow and smiled helplessly: "As expected of him... We've only been at the Earth's core for a short time, and he's already created a blessed land."

This is too much of a royal decree!

Moreover, now that the entire signal of the Palm Cliff subterranean base has disappeared, Wang Ling doesn't need to worry about whether he's being monitored; he can just enjoy himself first.

So, while they were all struggling to survive in the wilderness, Wang Ling was enjoying a comfortable vacation there?

"No!"

The red dot representing the dangerous creature is rapidly drawing a streak of light across the screen, aiming directly at the three people's location!

"A pincer attack?"
