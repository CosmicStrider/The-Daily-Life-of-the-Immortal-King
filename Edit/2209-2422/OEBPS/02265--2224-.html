Chapter 2224 Mysterious Countdown

The time spent with Wang Ling seemed to be accelerated by a spell, passing by exceptionally quickly.

The subway train slowly pulled up to the platform, making a soft whirring sound. Sun Rong tilted her head slightly, a hint of longing in her eyes, and formally said goodbye to Wang Ling.

The ups and downs of adventures I experienced after emerging from seclusion at Mount Regression, and the various challenges I faced with Wang Ling, all seem like they happened just yesterday.

Sun Rong hadn't been home for a while. Although she was reluctant to leave, she understood that all good things must come to an end, and she had no choice but to part ways with Wang Ling here.

"Project Center of the Earth is about to begin. I'm sure you have a lot of preparations to make, Wang Ling. See you in a couple of days then?" Sun Rong's lips curled up slightly, revealing a gentle smile. However, there was a hint of reluctance hidden in her eyes, as if she wanted to imprint Wang Ling's image deep in her mind.

"Hmm." Wang Ling nodded slightly, his eyes somewhat deep, seemingly still immersed in thoughts about Meng Nian and the Underworld. He was indeed worried that the journey to the Earth's core might bring unexpected dangers, and it was imperative to carefully plan ahead upon returning.

Especially before heading to the inner world, just in case, he needed Wang Ming to prepare some replacement, the latest version of the sealing talisman for him.

Before leaving, Wang Ling's gaze lingered for a moment on the red embroidered pouch that Meng Nian had given to Sun Rong.

He narrowed his eyes slightly, carefully sensing the aura emanating from the embroidered pouch. He confirmed that it was not a product of the underworld and contained no curse power. Coupled with the faint, almost imperceptible spiritual energy emanating from the pouch, Wang Ling's slightly furrowed brows relaxed, and he felt relieved about the pouch's origin.

Night fell like a giant black curtain, slowly enveloping the entire city, deep and serene, utterly silent.

That evening, Sun Rong returned home and lay on the soft bed, where fatigue gradually overwhelmed her.

She slowly closed her eyes, her consciousness gradually blurred, and she drifted off to sleep.

When she regained consciousness, she found herself seemingly in a misty cloud, surrounded by a faint halo of light, like a dream.

This is Mount Bulao.

Sun Rong slowly raised her head, and the sight before her made her mouth drop open.

Everywhere she looked, there were exotic flowers and rare herbs, their vibrant colors like a dreamlike painting. The colorful plants swayed gently in the breeze, each leaf seemingly shimmering with an otherworldly light, emitting a refreshing fragrance that, like invisible threads, gently teased her sense of smell.

These spiritual plants were all different in shape and came in pairs. What surprised Sun Rong even more was that these spiritual plants were all varieties she had never seen before.

As the future leader of the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group, she had been deeply versed in alchemy and the study of spiritual plants since childhood, possessing a profound understanding of various spiritual plants. Yet, at this moment, she couldn't recall a single piece of knowledge that corresponded to the spiritual plants before her. She frowned slightly, her eyes filled with doubt and curiosity, pondering the origin and uses of these spiritual plants.

"Is this... a dream?" Sun Rong shook her head slightly, unable to believe what she was seeing. She realized that this was a dream, but it was too real. Every detail was clearly visible, making it difficult to distinguish between reality and illusion.

Sun Rong was somewhat puzzled. She could roughly guess that it was because of that mysterious embroidered pouch that she had entered this fantastical secret realm.

She subconsciously touched her chest, as if she could feel the embroidered pouch.

Not far away, a crystal-clear stream flows gently, its water leaping and shimmering among the stones like a string of flowing pearls. On the stones along the stream, moss of various shapes grows, so green it seems to drip with water, like a work of art meticulously sculpted by nature.

A white jade bridge spans the stream, its surface carved with exquisite patterns, each line seemingly imbued with a mysterious power. In the hazy light, the bridge radiates a soft glow, as if it were a passage connecting two worlds.

Sun Rong walked forward along the stone bridge, her steps as light as if floating on clouds, each step feeling like she was treading on soft clouds.

After crossing the stone bridge, a dense forest came into view. The trees were tall and straight, their branches and leaves intertwining to form a natural arched corridor. Sunlight filtered through the gaps in the leaves, creating patches of golden light, like stars scattered on the ground, adding a touch of mystery to the forest.

Just as the young woman was captivated by the picturesque scenery, an elderly man with an air of otherworldly grace, dressed in a long white robe with silk threads shimmering like starlight, slowly emerged from the woods. He held an ancient cane, the tip of which was carved with exquisite runes.

His hair and beard were as white as snow, fluttering gently in the breeze, while his face was rosy like a child's, and his eyes revealed wisdom and peace.

Sun Rong was extremely surprised. Her body reacted instinctively. She quickly assumed a fighting stance, clenched her fists, and stared at the old man warily: "Hey! You wicked peach, Boundless Immortal! Do you still want to do evil!" Her voice was clear and firm.

"Young lady, please don't get agitated..."

The old man was speechless. He slapped his forehead helplessly, a bitter smile appearing on his face. He slowly walked up to Sun Rong, a kind smile on his face, and said, "Miss, you've mistaken me for someone else. This is the secret realm of the Immortal Mountain. It's fate that we meet here today."

As she spoke, Sun Rong saw the mysterious red embroidered pouch that Meng Nian had given her earlier appear before her eyes. The embroidered pouch emitted an incredible seven-colored spiritual light, which spread outward like ripples, and then slowly disappeared, gradually merging into Sun Rong's body. She could feel a warm power flowing through her body.

"This embroidered pouch has extraordinary uses. If used properly, it can enhance the relationships between people destined to meet. I hope you will use it wisely and find your own happiness." The old man said with a smile and earnestness. His voice seemed to have a kind of magic, making people feel at ease.

Just as Sun Rong was about to ask a question, she saw the old man's figure gradually disappear, and the surrounding scenery began to blur, as if it were a painting that had been blurred by water.

She woke up with a start, finding herself lying in bed, a few beads of sweat on her forehead. The embroidered pouch that had been beside her pillow was gone, and her eyes were filled with shock and confusion.

Sun Rong stared at all of this in disbelief.

"Wang Ling inspected this embroidery pouch...it should be fine..." Sun Rong was somewhat surprised. She felt as if some inexplicable changes were occurring within her, but she couldn't quite put her finger on what they were. Her heart was filled with unease and curiosity, wondering what all of this meant.

The following day, the day before the project to reach the center of the earth was to begin, Sun Rong was still unable to shake off the strange dream she had the previous night.

Every detail of that dream was etched deeply into her mind, like a brand.

She met with Ryoko Kunomiya and Qingyi Liu. As one of the sixty elite representatives of the Earth's Core Project, Sun Rong felt that perhaps these two would understand her better in matters of fate.

The "Yi Ling Xuan" coffee shop on Xiuzhen Ancient Street has a signboard hanging at its entrance that is a piece of black jade that exudes a warm and lustrous sheen. The three characters "Yi Ling Xuan" are engraved on it, and the characters are simple and vigorous, as if there is spiritual energy flowing through them.

In the VIP room at the back of the cafe, Liu Qingyi and Kugimiya Ryoko arrived on time for their appointment. Although Kugimiya Ryoko still displayed the arrogant temperament of a spoiled young lady from Sun Rong when Sun Rong asked her to meet her, saying that she would only come depending on her mood, Sun Rong found that Liu Qingyi and Kugimiya Ryoko were already sitting inside waiting for her when she arrived.

"You made an appointment to meet someone else, but you only showed up right on time. I had nothing to do today, so I decided to kindly keep you company for coffee." Ryoko Kugimiya held a cup of coffee she had already ordered, gave a light snort, her eyes showing a hint of dissatisfaction, but the corners of her mouth were slightly upturned, as if to hide her concern.

"Please sit down, Miss Sun Rong. What is it? Please speak frankly."

Beside her, Liu Qingyi smiled to herself. This young lady from Sun Island hadn't changed a bit in her temperament. Her eyes were gentle and concerned as she looked at Sun Rong, as if she could see right through her.

"I'm sorry to have made you come all this way."

To Sun Rong, Jiu Gong and Liu Qingyi were already considered successful in their relationship, which she greatly envied. A hint of envy and anticipation flashed in her eyes as she hoped to find her own happiness in love.

Faced with Sun Rong's sudden low posture today, Ryoko Kugimiya, who was drinking coffee, also noticed something was wrong and almost choked on her coffee: "Strange, what's wrong with you?" Her voice was filled with surprise and worry. She put down her coffee cup and looked at Sun Rong seriously.

"That's how it is..."

Sun Rong recounted how she and Wang Ling had dealt with Meng Nian a few days ago, how she obtained the mysterious embroidered pouch from Mount Immortality, and what happened in her dream last night. Liu Qingyi and Jiu Gong Liangzi couldn't help but burst out laughing.

"It was just a dream, Miss Sun Rong, you took it too seriously." Liu Qingyi smiled, her smile gentle and kind, but her eyes held a hint of helplessness.

"Are you a child? It's just a dream." Ryoko Kugimiya said dismissively, her tone carrying a hint of contempt, but her eyes revealed a trace of concern.

Sun Rong bit her lip. She knew that what she was saying at this moment sounded absurd. She was about to explain when she froze.

Because at that moment, Sun Rong saw a countdown number flashing with golden light suddenly appear above Liu Qingyi and Jiugong Liangzi's heads, and it was constantly jumping...

The countdown numbers were ticking and decreasing, but Sun Rong had no idea what it meant.

At this moment, Sun Rong's eyes widened, her mouth slightly open, her face filled with shock and disbelief.
