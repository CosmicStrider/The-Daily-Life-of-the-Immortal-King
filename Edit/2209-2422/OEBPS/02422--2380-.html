Chapter 2380 The Clown's Killing Time

The moment the group results were announced, a series of meaningful murmurs rang out in the hall.

"No way... I'm on the same team as this guy?" Chen Chao's face immediately fell when he saw that he was on the same team as Luo Xinghe.

Guo Hao didn't say anything, but his eyes clearly turned colder.

"How about we pretend to have a stomachache and withdraw from the competition?" Li Changzhe scratched his head and muttered to himself.

Clearly, he, Chen Chao, and Guo Hao were quite bothered by Luo Xinghe's comments regarding the negative public opinion surrounding Wang Ling's outburst.

Although Li Changzhe didn't have as much time to interact with Wang Ling as Chen Chao and Guo Hao, as a sworn brother who had gone through life and death together in the inner world, Li Changzhe never questioned Wang Ling's character.

He has always stood by Wang Ling's side.

Only Zhang Yu from Titan High School remained relatively calm. He glanced at Luo Xinghe, then at Wang Ling's team across the street, his brows furrowing slightly.

His intuition told him that the atmosphere in this situation would be delicate.

Luo Xinghe stood in the second team area, a professional smile on his face, but inwardly he was sneering.

Heaven is on his side!

Although Chen Chao and the others are Wang Ling's brothers, it is still a training camp, and the rules must be followed.

If you simply show them a little kindness and offer them some leeway, they should know what to choose.

Should I follow a quiet guy who eats instant noodles all day, or a professional streamer with millions of followers?

He looked at his teammates with a gentle smile: "Everyone, let's cooperate well in this game. I'm a Kai'Sa specialist, and I also got Kai'Sa in this game. I've looked at the lineup, and I think it's very reasonable."

He spoke so naturally, as if the schemes against Wang Ling that had just taken place in the cafeteria had never existed.

Chen Chao crossed his arms and glanced at him sideways: "How are we going to fight?"

"Play normally." Luo Xinghe maintained his smile: "Wang Ling's team has two girls, Sun Rong and Xia Zhinuan, so their strength might not be as balanced as ours. We'll stick together in the early game and break down their core players."

Guo Hao suddenly spoke up: "Data shows that Wang Ling used heroes in seven different positions during this training camp with a 100% win rate. Defining him as a single core player may not be accurate enough."

These words were spoken calmly, but every word was a slap in the face.

Luo Xinghe's smile froze for a moment, then quickly recovered: "Guo is right, I didn't consider it carefully. But their lineup this round... Wang Ling drew the Joker, right?"

He looked across.

The hero Wang Ling drew was indeed [Demon Clown - Saco].

A typical assassin-type jungler, but in the ARAM map, they usually go for an AP poke build or an AD assassin build.

Sun Rong drew [Magic Cat Yumi].

A support hero who relies almost entirely on possessing teammates.

Xia Zhinuan is [Blade Dancer Irelia].

Kobayashi Yu is [Night Hunter Vayne].

Lin Feng is [Morpheus, the Lava Beast].

A clown with a cat? A stealth assassin with an accessory?

Luo Xinghe sneered inwardly.

It seems that Wang Ling is going to mess up this game on his own without him even having to lift a finger.

"Everyone, prepare to connect," a staff member reminded.

Ten people lay down one after another in the access cabin.

Consciousness connection.

The deep abyss wailed, the spring water.

Wang Ling looked at the two daggers in his hand, painted with strange colors, and then at the purple magical cat floating in the air beside him.

Yumi circled around Saco twice, making soft "meow" sounds.

Sun Rong's voice rang out in the team voice chat, tinged with embarrassment: "Wang Ling... it seems my hero isn't very useful..."

The hero Magic Cat can possess teammates in normal mode, providing healing, shields, and speed boosts.

But in ARAM, possessing someone means you are almost entirely dependent on your teammates.

"Hmm." Wang Ling responded, then manipulated the clown to buy the [Sawtooth Dagger] and [Longsword] before heading out.

Sun Rong's cat quickly followed, using its W skill, "Yuumi, move out!", to possess the clown.

A purple kitten perched on the clown's shoulder, its tail swaying gently.

"I'll make sure you recover your health properly," Sun Rong whispered.

"Okay." Wang Ling responded softly, then controlled the clown to walk towards the center of the map.

Xia Zhinuan, playing Irelia, whispered a reminder: "Wang Ling, Luo Xinghe might target you. He drew Kai'Sa this game, which, combined with Zhang Yu's Thornstone, is quite strong."

"Hmm," Wang Ling replied calmly.

He walked up to a tower and stopped.

The five people opposite have already come into view.

Luo Xinghe's Kai'Sa stood at the back of the team, her gaze immediately fixed on Wang Ling's Joker.

When he saw the cat on the clown's shoulder, a mocking smile curled at the corner of his mouth.

A pendant paired with an assassin? How ridiculous!

When the minion waves converged, Wang Ling's Shaco didn't go forward to last-hit minions. Instead, he stood on the side of the minion wave and occasionally used his E skill, [Double-edged Poison Blade], to last-hit minions from a distance.

Luo Xinghe seized the opportunity, and Kai'Sa unleashed her W [Void Seeker]!

But just as the energy pulse was about to strike the Joker—

The Joker narrowly avoided the skill by shifting to the side with a very slight movement.

At the same time, he used his E skill, Poison Blade, to accurately hit Thornstone, who was last-hitting a minion.

"Tch." Luo Xinghe frowned.

That movement was still so tricky.

But it's okay, this is just the beginning.

He controlled Kai'Sa to move forward, intending to use basic attacks to whittle down her health.

But the Joker simply retreated, giving him no chance at all.

It's so stable it's frustrating.

...

The match lasts five minutes.

A system notification sounded:

[Hextech Rune Draw Time's Over]

A rune-inscribed wheel appeared in front of each person.

Wang Ling watched the pointer spin, finally stopping on a rune symbol he had never seen before.

He drew a Hextech rune that was almost exclusively for the Joker: [Joker Academy]:

This rune has roughly three effects. First, it deals additional physical damage when attacking from behind.

Second: Gain the new Summoner Spell "Deception". After use, teleport to the target location and enter a 3-second stealth state. The next attack during stealth deals additional physical damage. If attacking from behind, critical hit damage is 155% (including additional critical hit damage). Cooldown: 90 seconds.

Third: When you die, you deal real damage to enemies around you equal to 25% of your maximum health and inflict fear on them for 1 second.

Wang Ling remained silent for two seconds.

Then I clicked "confirm".

Almost simultaneously, Luo Xinghe drew the [Overheal]: Excess healing effects are converted into shields.

Xia Zhinuan drew the [Conqueror] card.

Yu Kobayashi drew [Fatal Rhythm].

Lin Feng drew the [Undying Grip].

Sun Rong's cat was drawn as the [Guardian].

"Exclusive runes?" Xia Zhinuan was taken aback when she saw the description of Wang Ling's runes. "There are such things?"

Wang Ling did not explain.

He simply returned to town and re-equipped the [Spirit of the Dream] component.

He's already decided to play a one-shot AD assassin, Shaco.

When Luo Xinghe returned to the online chat, he noticed the clown's equipment bar and sneered inwardly.

He opened the scoreboard and glanced at it.

The Joker deals 32 damage with a last hit, while Kai'Sa deals 35 damage with a last hit; they're about the same.

But the Joker got two kill assists? When?

Luo Xinghe was taken aback and quickly replayed the video.

It turns out that in those ten or so seconds when the rune was being drawn, Wang Ling's Shaco, along with Yuumi and Irelia, managed to kill the enemy's lone Thorn.

The process was simple: Shaco stealthily approached, initiated with a backstab, and with the help of Irelia's crowd control and Yuumi's speed boost, Thresh was gone before he could even use Flash.

The Joker only got hit by one E from Thor, and his health was almost full.

"Zhang Yu, be careful," Luo Xinghe said in the voice chat. "The clown can turn invisible, don't go alone."

Zhang Yu grunted in response, his voice sounding somewhat muffled.

He was actually quite frustrated.

Throughout the match, Luo Xinghe kept ordering him around, and the condescending tone in his voice made him very uncomfortable.

We're all training camp members, what are you pretending for, you're just a streamer?

But he didn't say it.

...

The match lasts seven minutes.

The first large-scale conflict broke out at the Hextech Runefruit spawn point.

Chen Chao's Lee Sin crouched in the bushes, marking Wang Ling's Shaco: "I can kick it back, focus fire and kill him instantly."

Luo Xinghe perked up: "Alright! Kick it back and kill it instantly! Cats can't dodge!"

However, just as the blind monk was about to dodge the eye and execute his roundhouse kick—
