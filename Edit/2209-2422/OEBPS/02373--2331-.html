Chapter 2331 Father's Love is as Solid as a Mountain

The commotion in the faculty and staff apartment corridor was forced to be suppressed by the sudden disciplinary notice from Principal Lu Qiu.

Suddenly, a sense of suspicion filled the air.

Zhang Yu looked at Wang Ling, a slight smile curving his lips, revealing an undisguised schadenfreude.

The other shadow agents, including the three black-robed members of the A-13 family, began to look at Wang Ling with increasingly complex expressions.

Liu Meng's gratitude for escaping death seems so fragile in the face of the official characterization.

"Inappropriate contact?" Zhang Yu sneered, his voice particularly grating in the corridor: "Zhou Ziwei, who exactly did you see yesterday? What shady things did you ask? Could it be that those deadly bamboo skewers were something you made to silence him?"

Faced with these inexplicable questions, Wang Ling didn't even bother to lift his eyelids.

He completely ignored Zhang Yu, his gaze sweeping over the crowd before finally settling on Liu Meng, who was still in shock.

"What exactly did the male voice you heard say?" Wang Ling asked, his voice calm and devoid of any emotion.

This made Zhang Yu even more dissatisfied. He didn't know why, but he always felt that Zhou Ziwei in front of him seemed even more pretentious than when he had seen him before!

But right now, seeing that Wang Ling had asked a crucial question, he suppressed his displeasure and decided to listen to Liu Meng's speech first.

Liu Meng tried hard to recall, fear reappearing on his face: "It was very blurry, it seemed like... they were saying something about why they didn't help... their daughter... who harmed Lin Wanwan, and that they all deserved to die!"

"Daughter? Who harmed Lin Wanwan?" Zhang Yu frowned.

This is the point of contradiction in all the known information.

The rules warned to avoid Lin Wanwan, but Liu Meng heard an angry voice belonging to a middle-aged man.

Inside Wang Ling's body, Wang Ying's impatient voice rang out: "Isn't it obvious? The one who killed her wasn't that little brat! It was her father from the Seven Star Team!"

Wang Ling remained silent.

The fragments provided by Liu Meng, combined with all the information he had previously learned about Lin Wanwan from Li Xiaoming, formed a clear logical chain in his mind.

The real killer was Lin Wanwan's father—the Seven Stars Special Forces monk who died in the line of duty.

After his death in the line of duty, his intense longing for his daughter prevented his spirit from dissipating. He went to the school to visit her, only to witness her being driven to her death.

Overwhelming resentment transformed him into a vengeful ghost, indiscriminately unleashing his hatred for his daughter's death upon everyone he considered an "accomplice" or "disobedient."

The sausages and sausage skewers, which originally symbolized the warm bond between father and daughter, became the trigger that drove him mad.

And Lin Wanwan...

Her weak soul was not a true vengeful spirit; perhaps she was trying to warn everyone in her own way to avoid her enraged father.

The faint aroma of grilled sausages is not a death warrant, but a warning.

"Let's go." Wang Ling suddenly turned around and walked towards the stairwell.

"Where to?" the black-robed teammate in the same room asked instinctively.

"Find Li Xiaoming."

Wang Ling didn't even turn his head: "He knows more than that."

Zhang Yu's eyes flashed, he snorted coldly, and followed with the B-07 team.

He wanted to see what tricks Zhou Ziwei, who had been disciplined, could pull next.

...

The teaching building remained lifeless during the day.

The students moved mechanically, like puppets on strings, under the command of the bell.

But when Wang Ling and his group appeared at the entrance of Class 3, Grade 11, that terrifying silence returned.

Li Xiaoming was not in his seat.

"Where is Li Xiaoming?" Wang Ling asked the teacher on the podium, a middle-aged male cultivator with a stern face.

The teacher pushed up his glasses and said in a cold tone, "Li Xiaoming is not feeling well and is resting in the infirmary."

Wang Ling said no more, and his spiritual consciousness instantly covered the entire teaching building.

He saw Li Xiaoming lying on the bed in the infirmary, trembling with fear. He also saw a tiny spirit quietly guarding Li Xiaoming in the corner of the infirmary. However, Li Xiaoming could not see the spirit. Instead, the aroma of grilled sausages in the air filled him with even deeper fear.

This wisp of soul belonged to none other than Lin Wanwan.

She was indeed trying to protect her classmate who had once been kind to her.

"Let's go." After saying that, Wang Ling turned around and left, his destination clear.

Zhang Yu and others followed closely behind.

The infirmary is located in a corner on the first floor of the teaching building, where the smell of disinfectant is particularly strong.

When Li Xiaoming saw so many people rushing in, especially Wang Ling and Zhang Yu, he was so frightened that he rolled off the bed and huddled in a corner.

"No...don't come any closer! I don't know anything!" He screamed, clutching his head.

Wang Ling ignored him and turned his gaze directly to that corner.

In his vision, a spirit girl wearing the uniform of St. Bell High School, with delicate features but an endless sorrow, stood there, looking at Li Xiaoming with worry.

"Lin Wanwan," Wang Lingping said calmly, calling out the name.

In an instant, the temperature in the infirmary plummeted!

The spirit in the corner began to tremble violently, and anxiety and fear appeared on the girl's face.

She shook her head desperately, her lips opening and closing, but no sound came out.

But a thought carrying a strong warning directly crashed into Wang Ling's sea of consciousness:

"Run! Don't say that name! He'll come! Run!"

Almost at the same time that Wang Ling received this thought.

"Waaaah!!!"

A deep, violent horn sound seemed to come from the depths of the earth, shaking the entire campus!

The sky suddenly darkened, and lead-gray clouds surged wildly.

A powerful resentment, carrying a terrifying spiritual pressure, surged in from all directions and locked onto the infirmary!

"He's here..." Wang Lingjing said calmly, her face expressionless, completely unaffected by the spiritual pressure.

On the contrary, Zhang Yu and the other shadow agents' expressions changed drastically. They felt a killing intent that was far more terrifying and pure than the one they felt the night before!

This is an aura that no ordinary vengeful spirit can possess!

"What's going on? The rules don't say we can't call people by their names!" a man in gray exclaimed in alarm.

"so it is."

After his test was confirmed, Wang Ling finally understood everything.

Wang Ling felt the sudden pressure of the vengeful spirit. In addition to the terrifying aura, he also sensed a trace of the spirit's unexpressed pain at being unable to protect its daughter.

He used Wang Tong to pinpoint the aura released by Lin Wanwan's father and then analyzed his memories in reverse.
