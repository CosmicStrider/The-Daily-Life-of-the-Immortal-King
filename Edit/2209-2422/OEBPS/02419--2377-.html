Chapter 2377 Tap Dance

The group list was displayed on the holographic screen, and the lobby erupted in a buzz.

"This grouping... is the system doing this on purpose?!"

Chen Chao's eyes nearly popped out of their sockets as he grabbed Wang Ling's shoulder and exclaimed, "Lingzi! This round is getting interesting! You're on Xia Zhinuan's team, and Sun Rong is on the other side! Shouldn't you give it your all?"

He spoke while making faces.

Wang Ling didn't reply, but simply looked up at the opposite preparation area.

Sun Rong was standing next to Luo Xinghe, and the two seemed to be discussing something in hushed tones.

Luo Xinghe wore his signature gentle smile, leaned slightly forward, and focused intently on the tactical tablet in Sun Rong's hand.

It's a bit close.

Wang Ling's brows furrowed slightly, but quickly returned to normal.

He lowered his head, took out a packet of new flavored instant noodles from his pocket, and calmly tore it open.

"Click".

The crisp chewing sounds were almost inaudible in the noisy hall, but Xia Zhinuan, standing next to him, could hear them clearly.

She turned her head to look at Wang Lingping's calm profile, her heart filled with mixed feelings.

Last night, Luo Xinghe showed her his signal operation, and now, she happens to be on the same team as Wang Ling.

Is this a coincidence?

Or did Luo Xinghe do something again?

"Xia Tongxue," Wang Ling suddenly spoke.

Xia Zhinuan was startled: "Huh? Wang Ling, what's wrong?"

"What position do you play?" Wang Ling asked in a calm tone.

"I...I can fill in any role." Xia Zhinuan composed herself: "However, in ARAM, the heroes are randomly selected, so we might not have a choice."

Wang Ling nodded and said nothing more.

He finished the last piece of instant noodles, folded the packaging bag into a neat little square, and stuffed it back into his school pants pocket.

Every movement was meticulous.

Looking at him like this, Xia Zhinuan suddenly remembered the game of Kieran from yesterday.

He was mentally disturbed and replaced as a hero; everyone thought he was going to collapse.

But he managed to torment the alligator to the point of withdrawal by using a support mage in the top lane.

This person always seems so calm, so calm that it's frightening.

...

"Round 2 Hextech Brawl, get ready to begin."

"Heroes and runes will be randomly drawn. Start now."

The system notification sound drew everyone's attention back.

The virtual spinning wheel in front of each team member spun wildly once again.

Wang Ling looked at the roulette wheel, feeling nothing in his heart.

For him, it doesn't matter which hero he draws; it's just a matter of how he wins.

The pointer slowly stopped, and the hero's portrait lit up.

【Merlin Gunner Tristana】

A standard ADC has a long attack range, mobility, and strong reaping ability in the late game, but is relatively weak in the early game and is very dependent on the development and output environment.

At the same time that Wang Ling drew the Tristana, the heroes of the other members of his team were also confirmed one after another:

Xia Zhinuan drew [Neeko, the Psychic of the Myriad Flowers]: a mage who can transform and control.

Chen Chao drew [Beast Walker Udir]: a melee fighter with four forms to switch between, commonly known as "the power of the bear".

Guo Hao drew [Ancient Witch Spirit Zelas]: an ultra-long-range turret mage.

Yu Kobayashi drew [Megami Girl - Riel]: Heavy Tank Support.

This lineup...

It looks weird no matter how you look at it.

Neeko and Zelas are considered standard damage dealers, Udyr is a melee fighter, Riven is an initiator tank, and Tristana is an ADC.

Wang Ling immediately recognized that this lineup lacked sustained control and hard engage, not only was the damage output scattered, but Udyr and Tristana also needed to be developed.

"It's over." Chen Chao looked at Udyr, the champion he had drawn, and his face fell. "I've only played this champion once!"

Kobayashi Yu said in broken Mandarin, "I, Rui'er, will protect the ADC. Wang Lingjun, please rest assured."

Xia Zhinuan didn't say anything, but she secretly glanced at Wang Ling.

Wang Lingzheng is adjusting the runes and summoner skills.

He brought Flash and Barrier.

Then comes Hextech Rune Extraction.

The first round of runes will be drawn in five minutes.

...

On the opposite side of the preparation area, Luo Xinghe looked at the hero he had drawn, and couldn't help but smile.

【Arrow of Punishment - Verus】

It's also one of his favorite characters.

Moreover, the teammates' heroes are also quite powerful:

Sun Rong drew [Sona, the Fairy of the Harp]: a group buff support.

Liu Kaichuan drew [Mountain of Flame - Ornn]: a top-tier tank capable of dealing damage with equipment.

Zhang Yu drew [Taliyah - Taliyah]: a roaming mage.

Lin Feng drew [Shadow Scythe - Kayn]: A flexible jungler who can transform into an assassin or warrior.

Compared to Wang Ling's team, their lineup not only has a front line, an initiator, and sustained damage, but also a reliable Sona for healing and protection.

From any perspective, it is much more reasonable than Wang Ling's team's bizarre lineup.

"Luck is on our side." Luo Xinghe smiled and said to Sun Rong, "Sun Rong, we can cooperate well this time. You play Sona, and I'll play Varus, a classic combination."

Sun Rong forced a smile: "Mm."

She felt a little confused.

Being assigned to the opposite side of Wang Ling feels... very strange.

And his teammate is Luo Xinghe.

This is the same person who was trying to humiliate Wang Ling yesterday.

"Listen to my commands, everyone." Luo Xinghe clapped his hands, drawing the team's attention: "We have a lineup advantage, so we can play more aggressively in the early game. Liu Kaichuan, you play Ornn top lane to withstand pressure, and look for an opportunity to initiate a team fight after reaching level six. Zhang Yu, you play Taliyah, roam more and coordinate with Lin Feng's Kayn to gank. Sun, you stick close to me and make sure to heal and speed up me."

He paused, his gaze sweeping over everyone before finally settling on Sun Rong, his tone gentle yet carrying an undeniable force.

"We're going to win this game. And we're going to win it beautifully."

"I want to let some people know that you can't go far by relying on luck and crooked methods."

The implication of this statement—a veiled criticism—is all too clear.

Sun Rong pursed her lips and remained silent.

Liu Kaichuan and Zhang Yu exchanged a glance, both seeing a hint of helplessness in each other's eyes.

Luo Xinghe really can't accept defeat.

...

The match begins.

The Howling Abyss map: ten people spawn at the springs on either side.

Five minutes later, the rune extraction countdown begins...

The system notification ended, and the first round of Hextech Rune extraction was completed.

Wang Ling looked at the rune he had drawn, his eyes flickering slightly.

[Tap Dance]: Your movement speed is increased by a percentage of your base movement speed, granting an attack speed bonus. Your attack speed is increased by a percentage of your base attack speed, granting a movement speed bonus.

A rune that creates a "movement speed to attack speed" cycle.

It's very suitable for ADCs who need to kite.

"Not bad," Wang Ling thought to himself.

At the same time, Luo Xinghe drew the [Scorching] skill: it causes additional burning damage when the skill hits, which, combined with Velus's skill consumption, is like adding wings to a tiger.

His smile deepened.

"AOE".
