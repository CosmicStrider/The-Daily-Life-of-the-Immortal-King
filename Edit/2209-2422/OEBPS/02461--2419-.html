Chapter 2419 The Protective Thunder Lord

Wang Ling threw a punch, but the speed was not fast; in fact, it was so slow that everyone in the audience could clearly see the trajectory of his fist.

Strangely, the golden light that Zhao Wuji transformed into seemed to deliberately collide with that fist.

Bang--

A dull thud rang out.

The golden light abruptly stopped.

Zhao Wuji's figure appeared, pressing against Wang Ling's fist.

Time seemed to stand still.

Then, in the next second, the golden patterns around Zhao Wuji shattered inch by inch, and the dark golden spiritual power receded like a tide.

His eyes rolled back, and he collapsed limply.

The referee paused for a second, then blew his whistle: "Match over! Wang Lingsheng!"

Medical staff rushed onto the stage.

The entire venue remained quiet.

Everyone was looking at the boy on the stage who had clenched his fist and stood up.

He wore a dark blue tracksuit, zipped up to his chest, and his bangs were slightly ruffled by the strong wind.

He looked down at his fist, seemingly realizing he had used too much force.

This was not Wang Ling's original intention, after all, Wang Ling did not want to win the championship or anything... However, thinking that Wang Ming would be able to clean up the mess later and that he would not expose too much, he felt much more at ease.

In the audience, Chen Chao's mouth was agape, and he couldn't close it for a long time.

The screen of the recorder in Guo Hao's hand was frozen on a strange image.

The moment Wang Ling threw his punch, the value of his spiritual energy fluctuations remained unchanged, still stable at the early stage of Foundation Establishment.

"How is this possible..." Guo Hao murmured.

Li Changzhe rubbed his eyes hard: "Did you guys see that punch just now?"

Su Xingyue remained silent, her fingers rapidly tapping on the virtual keyboard to bring up the slow-motion footage she had just captured.

In the scene, Wang Ling's fist made a slight vibration the moment it touched Zhao Wuji's forehead.

The frequency is extremely high, and the amplitude is extremely small.

But this tremor shattered all the power that Zhao Wuji had gathered from his burning blood essence.

"The shockwave absorbs the force." Gong Xuan suddenly spoke, tightening his grip on the sword. "But that frequency and precision..."

Zhang Linyan frowned: "To be able to do this much at the Foundation Establishment stage, it seems Wang Ling is quite capable."

The group looked at each other, and each saw confusion in the other's eyes.

At the VIP table, Zhao Tieshan rushed down to check on his grandson's condition.

Fortunately, it was just that his essence and blood were over-consumed, and he suffered from the backlash of the Dharma, so he needed to rest for a few months, but his foundation was not damaged.

He breathed a sigh of relief, but then anger surged within him.

Zhao Tieshan turned to the judges' panel, his voice filled with suppressed anger: "Judges, don't you think there was something wrong with that punch just now? How could someone in the early Foundation Establishment stage possibly defeat the Vajra Subduing Demon Body, which is burning with its essence blood?!"

The white-haired old man frowned: "Elder Zhao, the system detection shows that contestant Wang Ling's spiritual energy fluctuations remained stable at the early stage of Foundation Establishment throughout the entire process, without any violations."

"The system might be malfunctioning!" Zhao Tieshan gritted his teeth. "I demand a retest of his cultivation level!"

"This is against the rules." The bespectacled female judge shook her head: "Unless there is conclusive evidence that the system is malfunctioning, contestants cannot be subjected to a second test at will."

Before Zhao Tieshan could speak further, Yi Jianchuan's voice suddenly rang out:

"Zhao Tieshan".

The three calm words made Zhao Tieshan freeze.

He turned his head and saw that Yi Jianchuan had somehow walked to the vicinity of the judges' table.

The Sword Saint stood with his hands behind his back, his eyes indifferent: "Winning and losing are common occurrences in war. Your grandson used forbidden techniques, which was already against the rules. Now that you are outmatched, what else is there to say?"

Zhao Tieshan's face flushed red, but facing the Sword Saint, he dared not act rashly and could only lower his head: "What the Sword Saint said is true... I was out of line."

Yi Jianchuan ignored him and turned his gaze to the stage.

Wang Ling is eating his second piece of instant noodles.

Seemingly noticing the gaze, he turned his head and met Yi Jianchuan's eyes.

Then, he nodded naturally.

A hint of a smile flashed in Yi Jianchuan's eyes, and he nodded slightly.

This scene was captured by countless cameras.

In the VIP section, the school leaders whispered among themselves.

"Does the Sword Saint seem to know that Wang Ling?"

"It's more than just knowing him; you were clearly speaking up for him just now."

"Just what is the origin of this royal decree?"

Zhao Tieshan felt even more aggrieved as he listened to these comments.

He glared at Wang Ling on the stage, then, supporting the unconscious Zhao Wuji, turned and left.

His back view looked somewhat disheveled.

...

In the media area, reporters frantically typed on their keyboards.

"A stunning reversal! Wang Ling of the Number 60 High School once again defeats Zhao Wuji of the Body Cultivation High School!"

"Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan was present at the match, seemingly supporting Wang Ling!"

"A Foundation Establishment cultivator defeats a pseudo-Golden Core cultivator despite being at a higher level? Judges: A victory of skill!"

One by one, the titles were sent back to the editorial department.

But soon, they received a notification from their superiors:

"All reports concerning Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan and War Sect Master Diu Lei Zhenjun will be temporarily suspended pending review. In addition, all details regarding the competition will be blurred."

The reporters exchanged glances, but tacitly deleted the sensitive content.

...

Private rooms on the second floor of the stadium.

Luo Xinghe stared intently at the replay on the screen.

Wang Ling's nonchalant punch kept replaying in his mind.

"Mo Xuan," his voice hoarse, "Have you figured it out?"

Mo Xuan's voice came through the earpiece: "Preliminary analysis complete. The moment Wang Ling threw his punch, a high-frequency vibration was generated at the point of contact between his fist and Zhao Wuji's forehead."

"What level of spiritual power is required to control this kind of tremor?"

"At least late Golden Core stage." Mo Xuan paused, "And it requires microscopic control of power. Theoretically, it's impossible for someone at the Foundation Establishment stage to do that."

Luo Xinghe clenched his fist: "So, he definitely hid his strength!"

"But the problem is that the system detected no abnormalities, and the judging panel gave a reasonable explanation," Mo Xuan said calmly. "We currently lack direct evidence."

"Then let's find evidence!" Luo Xinghe gritted his teeth: "When will the jammer be activated?"

"According to the original plan, the match was to start after fifteen minutes." Mo Xuan glanced at the time: "But now that the match has ended early, the plan needs to be adjusted."

Luo Xinghe remained silent for a few seconds.

Looking at Wang Ling's calm face on the screen, a nameless anger surged within him.

"I can't wait any longer." He made the decision: "Activate the jammer now. Create a little surprise and test his reaction."

"Now?" Mo Xuan hesitated: "Sword Saint and Thunder Lord are both present, the risk is too great."

"That's why now!" Luo Xinghe sneered. "If he reveals his true strength in front of the two big shots, it will be even more convincing! Besides, with so many people present, he won't dare to go too far."

Mo Xuan thought for a moment, then finally nodded: "Understood. Activate in three minutes."

...

On stage, Wang Ling finished eating his instant noodles and was about to leave the stage.

Suddenly, he stopped in his tracks.

In my perception, the four hidden spiritual energy nodes below the battle arena simultaneously began to fluctuate abnormally.

It has a high frequency and is very covert.

But it couldn't escape his perception.

"Hmm?" Wang Ling blinked.

He glanced towards section B of the audience seating area.

Sun Rong was waving at him, smiling happily.

Wang Muyu was also waving vigorously, his little face full of admiration.

Yi Jianchuan and Diu Lei Zhenjun remained calm, as if the previous match was just an appetizer.

Wang Ling withdrew his gaze and continued walking down the stage.

But just as he reached the edge of the fighting arena—
