Chapter 2421 The Blind Spot of Brotherhood

Chen Chao was woken up by the urge to urinate.

At three in the morning, he stumbled into the toilet in a daze, and after relieving himself, he stood in front of the sink, lost in thought.

The face in the mirror looks a bit swollen. I ate too much salty barbecue last night, and I look like a steamed bun.

He turned on the tap and splashed a handful of cold water on his face.

The coolness jolted him back to his senses.

Images of Wang Ling's match today flashed through my mind: Zhao Wuji's final Blood Burning Vajra and Wang Ling's nonchalant punch.

And there's that row of big shots in section B of the stands.

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan, Thunder Lord, Nine Palaces Beauty, Outstanding...

Chen Chao sighed as he looked in the mirror.

He's not stupid.

The fact that so many big names showed up to support Wang Ling at the same time cannot be explained by the phrase "martial arts genius".

But he's not the kind of person who has to dig deep into things.

"That's settled then," Chen Chao said to himself in the mirror. The old Chen Chao's mindset was: he wasn't afraid of his brothers suffering, he was afraid of his brothers driving Land Rovers.

His mindset has changed now. If Wang Ling is really strong, his brother can benefit from it too!

...

At the same time, in a high-end apartment in Songhai City.

Luo Xinghe spent a sleepless night.

He stood in front of the floor-to-ceiling window, looking at the city still brightly lit at three in the morning, his phone in his hand.

The screen displayed the location sent by Jiang Liu.

Songhai Port, Warehouse No. 3.

Luo Xinghe ran his thumb over the line of text, then turned off the screen.

Mo Xuan emerged from the shadows and stood three steps behind him.

"Are you really decided?"

"Um."

"Over there with the War Sect..."

"Lord Diu Lei only warned me not to touch Wang Ling." Luo Xinghe turned around, a slight smile playing on his lips. "He didn't say I couldn't touch anyone else."

Mo Xuan remained silent for a few seconds.

Although he had long known that Luo Xinghe was stubborn, he did not expect that the man would be so fixated on a single point. However, the superiors' requirement for him was to fully assist Luo Xinghe and cooperate with whatever he wanted to do. The main purpose was to delve into Luo Xinghe's hatred for Wang Ling so that he could be used by the hidden big shot behind their Primordial Assets in the future... Other things were not Mo Xuan's concern.

"You still want to mess with Sun Rong?" Mo Xuan asked tentatively.

"Who said I wanted to do anything to her?" Luo Xinghe laughed. "I just wanted to invite her for a cup of tea and a chat. I'll also invite a few of her friends along."

Mo Xuan looked at him without saying a word.

Luo Xinghe's smile appeared somewhat sinister in the night.

"Go check Chen Chao's home address, as well as Guo Hao's and Li Changzhe's. The more detailed the better."

"Can."

Mo Xuan retreated into the shadows and disappeared at the end of the corridor.

Luo Xinghe turned back to look out the window.

His face was reflected in the glass, his eyes unusually bright.

"Wang Ling," he said softly, "don't you have the Sword Saint backing you up? Don't you have the War Sect Master as your older brother?"

"I'd like to see how many of your friends they can protect."

...

At 7 a.m. on Saturday, Chen Chao was woken up by his phone vibrating.

The screen displays a message from Guo Hao:

Meet at the usual place at 8 PM; we need to discuss something.

Chen Chao squinted and replied: 【What's the rush?】

Guo Hao: [Regarding Luo Xinghe.]

Chen Chao jolted awake and became completely sober.

He rolled out of bed, finished washing up in three minutes, put on his coat, and ran outside.

Mr. Chen was boiling eggs in the kitchen: "Aren't you going to eat breakfast?"

"I'm not eating, Dad! Something's wrong!"

The door slammed shut.

Mr. Chen shook his head, looked at the forty eggs in the pot, and sighed.

...

7:55, soy milk shop in the old town.

When Chen Chao arrived, Guo Hao was already sitting in a corner with three bowls of soy milk and a plate of fried dough sticks in front of him.

Two minutes later, Li Changzhe rushed in, plopped down, and grabbed a fried dough stick.

"What's going on?" His mouth was stuffed full. "What kind of trouble is Luo Xinghe causing now?"

Guo Hao and the other man were seated before they lowered their voices and spoke.

"I received an anonymous message last night."

He pushed his phone to the center of the table.

The screen displays a screenshot with very simple content:

Luo Xinghe is investigating the addresses and activity patterns of Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe. Be careful.

Chen Chao was stunned.

Li Changzhe was also stunned, forgetting to chew the fried dough stick that was still in his mouth.

"Who posted it?" Chen Chao asked.

"I don't know." Guo Hao shook his head: "The number is a virtual number, and we can't find its source. But I traced the transmission path, and the base station that sent the signal is near Songhai Port."

Chen Chao's mind went blank.

Luo Xinghe, can you find their address?

What does he want to do?

"Could it be a prank?" Li Changzhe finally swallowed the fried dough stick: "Luo Xinghe was just summoned by Lord Die Lei yesterday, and he dares to cause trouble today?"

"I also hoped it was a prank," Guo Hao said. "But I checked, and at 3 a.m. last night, someone used a fake identity to access the Songhai City household registration system's query interface. The query record included Chen Chao's address."

Chen Chao's expression changed.

"So what did you say?"

"I didn't say anything." Guo Hao looked at him. "I'm just telling you that someone is investigating us."

There was silence on the table for a few seconds.

Li Changzhe put down the fried dough stick and, unusually, became serious: "How about we hide, or call the police?"

"Why are you hiding?" Chen Chao frowned. "I haven't done anything wrong, why should I hide?"

"But that bastard Luo Xinghe isn't normal." Li Changzhe said anxiously, "He even dares to rig matches, who knows if he might..."

"What could it be?" Chen Chao interrupted him: "Hired someone to beat me? Kidnapped me? Has he gone mad?"

Guo Hao remained silent and did not speak.

He knew that Luo Xinghe might really have gone mad.

But he had no evidence.

Moreover, saying such things will only make Chen Chao more nervous.

"Okay," Guo Hao said, "Let's try to stay together these next few days and not be alone. I'll check who Luo Xinghe has been contacting lately and see if there are any clues."

"What about Lingzi?" Chen Chao asked. "Should we tell him?"

Guo Hao thought for a moment and shook his head.

"Wang Ling has a match next week, don't let him get distracted. Besides, Luo Xinghe is investigating us, not him."

Chen Chao opened his mouth as if to say something, but in the end he swallowed it back.

"Alright." Chen Chao grabbed a fried dough stick and took a bite. "Then let's stick together for the next few days. It's the weekend, let's go to the internet cafe and play games together, let's see what he can do."

Li Changzhe nodded: "I agree."

Guo Hao pushed up his glasses but didn't say anything.

He actually had a guess in his heart.

Who sent that anonymous message?

Why only tell those three and not Wang Ling?

...

Nine o'clock in the morning, Songhai Port, Warehouse No. 3.

The warehouse had been abandoned for many years. The iron gate was rusted, the windows were boarded up, and the surrounding area was overgrown with waist-high weeds.

A black van was parked at the back door of the warehouse.

Luo Xinghe got out of the car, with Mo Xuan following behind.

The warehouse door opened from the inside, and a man in gray overalls stood at the doorway.

He was in his forties, with a square face, dark skin, and a scar at the corner of his eye.

"Mr. Luo," he said, his voice hoarse, "Brother Jiang is waiting for you inside."

Luo Xinghe nodded and followed him inside.

The warehouse is much larger inside than it appears from the outside.

Rusty containers and abandoned machinery were piled up everywhere, and the air was filled with the smell of engine oil and rust.

After passing through a narrow passage, the view suddenly opened up before me.

A middle-aged man in his fifties was sitting on a container, holding a dagger in his hand, peeling an apple.

He was wearing a faded old military uniform, his hair was gray, but his back was straight and his eyes were sharp.

This person was none other than Jiang Liu.

"You're here?" Jiang Liu didn't even look up. He ran the knife along the fruit peel, slicing off a whole long strip.

Luo Xinghe stood three meters away and did not move forward.

"Jiang Qianbei".

"Sit down." Jiang Liu pointed with the tip of his knife at the empty box next to him.

Luo Xinghe sat down.

Mo Xuan stood behind him, maintaining a vigilant posture.

Jiang Liu finished peeling the apple, took a bite, chewed it a few times, and swallowed it.

"I've heard about your situation," he said slowly. "How did it go when the Sect Master of the War Sect wanted to talk to you?"

Luo Xinghe's expression remained unchanged: "That's good. Lord Diulei was very polite."

"You're too kind?" Jiang Liu laughed. "If Lord Thunder-Throwing were truly too kind, you wouldn't need to come looking for me."

Luo Xinghe did not respond.

Jiang Liu took another bite of the apple.

"I've looked into the children you're considering taking action against."

He put down the apple and took out several sheets of paper from his pocket, on which were written the information Jiang Liu had investigated: "Chen Chao, you know the basic information. His father runs a martial arts school, and his mother worked in the population census before she retired. Now she is a full-time housewife. Clean family background, no special relationship."

"Guo Hao has many uncles in his family. One of them works at the Psionic Technology Bureau in Songhai City, the one that manages the competition system. His family runs a spirit beast aquarium, and both his parents are in this line of work."
