Chapter 2285 A Counter-Strategy

War Sect Headquarters, Sect Master's Office.

The space rippled slightly, and an invisible barrier silently reinforced itself, completely isolating this place from the outside world.

Lord Diu Lei sat in the main seat, his usual playful smile gone, and he looked unusually serious.

Wang Ming sat to one side, his fingertips rapidly gliding across the virtual screen, displaying data and blurry images brought back from the Abyss of Demise.

Qin Zong leaned against the other side, wiping the Nine Yang Divine Sword. His posture seemed relaxed, but his eyes were sharp, scanning every detail in the image.

This test may seem easy, but it is actually extremely dangerous. The strength of the Primordial Chaos should not be underestimated.

That's the situation.

Wang Ming summarized, pushing up his slightly slipping smart glasses: "The main consciousness of Jueqi has most likely been recovered and is being strengthened at the Primordial Headquarters using the Dragon God Altar. Their purpose in capturing Brother Muyu's data is for targeted evolution, and they may even want to replicate or analyze his source of power. That Dragon God Altar has extremely abnormal energy reactions; their intentions are probably not small."

The atmosphere in the office was heavy.

Lord Diu Lei stroked his chin: "That old fox Bai Zhe is indeed not that simple. A puppet almost overturned the Wuji Sword Sect. If the main body were to complete its evolution, plus that altar..."

He looked at Wang Ming: "Brother Wang Ming, what are your initial thoughts?"

Just as Wang Ming was about to speak, he suddenly paused almost imperceptibly, as if he had received some information.

His eyes momentarily lost focus, then returned to normal, though a knowing smile played at the corners of his lips.

"That's not my idea."

Wang Ming smiled and pointed to an empty space in the office: "That's my dear younger brother. He already has a complete plan."

As soon as he finished speaking, a blurry, illusory figure slowly coalesced in the center of the office.

There was no earth-shattering momentum, nor any energy fluctuation; it appeared out of thin air like a mirage.

Wang Ling's projection stood there quietly, his dead fish eyes as expressionless as ever, as if he were just there to listen to the meeting.

"Brother!" Lord Diu Lei immediately sat up straighter.

Qin Zong also put away his laziness and nodded in acknowledgment.

Wang Ling's projection made no small talk; it simply turned its gaze to Wang Ming.

Wang Ming immediately understood and tacitly took on the role of "mouth substitute".

Clearly, in that instant, Wang Ling had used his supreme will to directly imprint the complete plan into his mind.

Wang Ming, on the other hand, used his super brain to perform detailed optimization and language organization.

"Here's the plan."

Wang Mingqing cleared his throat and quickened his pace: "Didn't Bai Zhe want the wooden fish? Didn't he get his combat data and think he found a way to deal with it or even a weakness? Then we'll... play along."

"A counter-strategy?" Lord Thunderclap wondered.

"That's right." Wang Ming tapped his finger, and Wang Muyu's data model appeared on the screen: "Based on Lingling's deduction and the information we brought back, the evolved abilities of the Absolute Imprisonment will most likely focus on imprisonment, suppressing energy bursts, and targeting the characteristics of the dragon bloodline. The goal is to capture it alive, not to kill it. Because what they want most is the Little Wooden Fish itself."

"So, we can have Muyu pretend to be arrested."

"What?!" Lord Thunderclap exclaimed in shock, "This is too dangerous!"

"Let me finish." Wang Ming waved his hand, clearly indicating that the plan had been meticulously calculated: "It's not about actually putting Little Wooden Fish in danger. The key is in the pretense."

He zoomed in on the structural projection diagram of the Primordial Headquarters: "Based on the architectural analysis, the Dragon God Altar is the core, and the Evolution Chamber and Gene Laboratory of the Absolute are located nearby."

"If we launch a strong attack, the other side is very likely to become desperate and resort to desperate measures, destroying data or even the altar. We may end up with nothing and alert the enemy, allowing Bai Zhe to hide even deeper."

"But if the captured target is actively sent into the core area..." Wang Ming's eyes flashed with a sharp light: "Then, as long as this target has the ability to create enough chaos inside in an instant, or... directly control key nodes."

He pointed to Wang Ling's projection: "Lingling will leave three layers of protection on the little wooden fish. First, it will secretly protect it, ensuring that it cannot be truly imprisoned or disintegrated by any means. Second, a hidden teleportation portal, so that Lingling can instantly reach its side when necessary. Third, and most importantly, the little wooden fish itself..."

Wang Ming looked at Wang Ling's projection, and Wang Ling nodded almost imperceptibly.

Wang Ming continued, "Little Wooden Fish needs to learn to control its power. When the other side is testing it, it should appear weak, consistent with their data model, to mislead them. Once in position, working together from the inside and outside, it can not only destroy the altar and capture the Extinction Master, but also seize all the data from the Primordial Headquarters!"

The office quieted down.

Qin Zong couldn't help but secretly criticize the plan in his heart.

Controlling power... even this strongest man hasn't found the best solution yet...

After a period of time, its aura became even stronger than before, indicating it was not human at all!

But he didn't dare to complain to the crickets in front of him, so he could only keep his complaints to himself and shut his mouth.

Lord Thunderclap digested this audacious plan, his eyes growing brighter and brighter: "Indeed brilliant! Infiltrating the tiger's den, striking at the heart of the enemy! And we can even extract their research data! Exciting!"

At this moment, Qin Zong smiled and said, "Sounds like you need a super bodyguard ready to cause trouble at any time? That's my specialty."

The key to the plan lies in Wang Muyu's acting skills and self-control, as well as Wang Ling's absolute strength and remote support.

Wang Ling's projection remained silent, as if this earth-shattering plan had nothing to do with him.

However, his gaze seemed to pierce through the void and land on the Wuji Sword Sect, where he began to silently communicate with Wang Muyu and have a necessary heart-to-heart talk with him.

Controlling power?

Feel sorry......

This might be the only thing Wang Ling isn't very good at.

Therefore, the wooden fish needs to comprehend it on its own...

Obviously, this is much more difficult than fighting openly.

Wang Muyu, who received the message, was also completely bewildered.

How could he know something that even "Dad" doesn't know?

At this moment, Wang Ming finished his "mouth stand-in" work and looked at Wang Ling's projection.

Wang Ling nodded slightly, and the projection faded away as if it had never appeared.

"Then it's settled!"

Lord Thunderclap slapped his thigh: "Start preparations immediately! Brother Wang Ming, we're counting on you for technical support! Brother Qin, the assault team will be led by you and the elite among the Eternal Ones! Zhang Zixiong and Li Xian are both excellent candidates!"

"I'll go and communicate with Senior Sword Saint... about some of the circumstances." The thought of persuading Sword Saint to cooperate with his disciple's capture made True Lord Thunder Drop feel a chill run down his spine; this task was no easier than fighting in the Primordial World.

...

Meanwhile, in the southwest of Huaxiu Kingdom, deep within the Abyss of Fallen Stars, lies the Dragon Remains Fortress.

Inside the core experimental area, dark red liquid boiled and surged in the huge culture tank.

Suspended within the azure dragon scale armor, the armor appeared even more profound, its intricate patterns seemingly containing cosmic dust.

His physique seemed to have swelled up as well, his muscle lines exuding a destructive sense of power.

At this moment, Jueqi kept his eyes tightly closed, but the destructive pressure emanating from his body made the black-robed evil cultivators working around him tremble and dare not approach.

Buzz—!

The energy liquid in the culture tank was instantly emptied!

Jueqi suddenly opened his eyes. His molten gold vertical pupils were no longer purely cold, but had become more profound and contained a trace of imperceptible fervor.

A powerful energy wave swept through the entire laboratory, and the instruments emitted an overloaded hum.

"Welcome to Lord Jueqi for completing your evolution!" The leading black-robed evil cultivator knelt on the ground, his voice trembling with excitement.

Jueqi landed slowly, moved his neck, and made a soft metallic scraping sound.
