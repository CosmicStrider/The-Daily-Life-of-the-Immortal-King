Chapter 2309 Wang Muyu Returns

In the past few days, several social news lists have dominated Huaxiu's top headlines, mainly focusing on the ten users of [Sleep Treasure] who were selected for the "death game".

Each news story, through the special processing of contemporary media, is given enough attention to generate explosive discussion:

A strange story from Songhai! A middle-aged man sat up in the middle of the night and frantically transferred millions of spirit stones: the anonymous remittance slips were filled with "Sorry, fellow miners"

Has the era of puppet-based zombie farming finally come to an end? An elderly woman in Songhai tearfully buries her AI robot son; residents of the neighborhood watch the psychic funeral, calling it outrageous.

"The King of Employees Awakens! A White-Collar Worker Sends a Letter of Repentance to the Industry Association at 4 AM: 'Boss, I Made Up the Data Back Then!'"

...

During a break at Number 60 High School, Wang Ling scrolled through his phone, finding the news headlines increasingly outrageous. But to be honest, even he, as someone in the know, was eager to click and see what was going on.

After all, being a gossipmonger is human nature, and this wouldn't seem strange in any world.

After these reports are published, public attention will be further focused on these individuals. If the Honghuang side wants to continue targeting them, they will probably have to consider the weight of public opinion.

Of course, choosing the path of honesty is not easy, and is even full of pain, but their souls will no longer be shrouded in gloom, and their Dao heart will become more resilient on their future path of cultivation.

"Lingzi! Have you seen the news? The social news these past few days has been so exciting! It's like the entertainment industry!" Chen Chao rushed into the classroom.

"These people have one thing in common: they are all users of [Hanmianbao]. And Lingqi, the company that produces [Hanmianbao], is under investigation. I think there's more to the story than meets the eye."

Guo Hao followed behind, calmly analyzing: "Two giants in the cultivation technology industry have run into trouble one after another. Although they are involved in completely different industries, it seems that the regulation of cultivation technology products will be tightened in the future."

"Well, no matter what happens in the future, it's best to avoid taking shortcuts and engaging in opportunistic behavior."

Chen Chao casually put his arm around Wang Ling and Guo Hao's shoulders: "Our biggest task right now is to help Lingzi break through to the Foundation Establishment stage as soon as possible!"

Wang Ling: "..." Thank you!

At that moment, Wang Ling was unpacking a bag of instant noodles when his movements suddenly froze.

A familiar and powerful dragon aura was penetrating the atmosphere at an astonishing speed, approaching Songhai City!

The unusual and turbulent psionic energy fluctuations made Wang Ling instantly realize who the newcomer was.

It was Wang Muyu!

The little guy is finally back.

Moreover, he did not seem to have perfectly contained the power that surged after devouring the son of the Dragon God. The seven-colored dragon light emanating from his body left a dazzling tail of light against the backdrop of the universe, like an out-of-control comet.

The case is solved! It turns out that the recently popular interstellar object 3IAtlas was actually Wang Muyu...

...

Wuji Sword Sect, forbidden area in the back mountain.

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan is teaching several newly accepted outer disciples basic sword techniques.

He was already at the age to retire and was used to a life of leisure, but Wang Muyu's appearance told him that this was the time to make a name for himself!

That's why they increased their efforts in the Wuji Sword Sect.

At that moment, Yi Jianchuan's heart suddenly stirred, and he looked up at the sky as if he had a premonition.

Although his cultivation level was far inferior to those of the Ancestral Realm cultivators, his sword heart was clear and his perception of aura was extremely sensitive.

"Master, what's wrong?" a disciple asked in confusion.

Yi Jianchuan shook his head, his brows furrowing slightly: "It's nothing, continue practicing your sword."

A turbulent feeling stirred within him.

Is it that kid, Muyu?

This aura is more than ten times stronger than what I sensed a while ago!

What exactly happened to him?

Meanwhile, at the War Sect headquarters.

"Warning! High-energy reaction detected approaching Earth! Psychic spectrum analysis matches Wang Muyu! The index has surpassed the threshold of the Celestial Ancestor level!" The monitor's voice was filled with disbelief and excitement.

Lord Diu Lei stood up abruptly and looked at the huge light screen in front of him, which displayed the trajectory of Wang Muyu's fall.

"Good heavens! Did Little Wooden Fish take some super-strength tonic while he was out there? The fluctuations in his spiritual energy are almost as strong as the traces your brother occasionally leaks out!" He clicked his tongue in amazement, and then ordered: "Activate the Level 3 concealment plan to help him cover his trajectory a little, and try not to let those space telescopes capture it."

"yes!"

At that moment, Wang Ming's virtual projection also appeared instantly next to Diu Lei Zhenjun.

Data streams flashed through his eyes: "Little Wooden Fish's spiritual energy structure is stable, its vital signs are vigorous, and there are no signs of it going out of control. It seems that Lingling's training has been very successful, and his return is just in time."

...

In the universe, Wang Muyu felt the boundless power surging around him, his little face full of excitement.

He gazed at the blue planet that was getting closer and closer, his heart yearning to return home.

"Dad! Mom Sun Rong! I'm home!"

He cheered and his speed increased again.

However, just as he was about to break through the atmosphere, Wang Ling's calm and unwavering will resounded in his mind:

"To withdraw."

Two simple words sent a jolt through Wang Muyu.

He then realized that he was so excited that his energy was leaking out a bit excessively.

"Oh! I'm sorry, Dad!" Wang Muyu quickly tried to control his strength.

However, the power he had just absorbed and fused was too enormous, and it was piling up inside his body, constantly expanding, like a nuclear reactor that never stops, making it difficult to control precisely for the time being.

The seven-colored dragon light flashed intensely on his skin, flickering unpredictably, and appeared even more dazzling under his fierce and powerful control.

Wang Ling sighed silently in the classroom.

He subtly hooked his fingertip under the table.

A thought containing the power of supreme laws traversed space and gently touched the outer edge of the restless dragon power surrounding Wang Muyu.

In an instant, all the radiant light that had been leaking out was drawn back in.

The violent energy fluctuations were forcibly suppressed, as if Wang Muyu had been given a layer of cloaking paint for a fighter jet.

Wang Muyu felt a sudden lightness all over his body, and the uncontrollable power instantly became obedient and easy to manage.

He looked at his small hands, which had returned to their original simple and unadorned state, in surprise, and his admiration for Wang Ling grew even stronger.

"Dad's awesome!"

He obediently concealed all his presence, silently passed through the atmosphere, and landed precisely in the direction of Wang Ling's villa in Songhai City.

......

Just as Wang Muyu returned safely.
