Chapter 2390 Night Visit to the Haunted House

The Ferris wheel cabin doors opened, and the cool night breeze, mixed with the sweet scent of cotton candy wafted in from the park.

Sun Rong stepped out of the cabin first, then turned around and reached out her hand to Wang Ling.

The action was almost subconscious; only after she finished did she realize it was a bit too natural. Her hand froze in mid-air, and her cheeks started to burn again.

Wang Ling glanced at her hand but didn't take it. However, fearing that Sun Rong would be embarrassed, he took out a White Rabbit milk candy from his pocket and placed it in her hand before walking out himself.

But after stepping out of the cabin, he naturally stood to Sun Rong's right.

"...What should we play next?" Sun Rong quickly put away the candy, pretending to fix her hair using her phone's selfie mode, to change the subject.

Wang Ling raised his wrist, and the screen of the smartwatch glowed with a soft blue light in the night.

He swiped twice, and several items on the map were flashing with the label "Recommended Nightlife Experience".

[The Haunted Castle: An immersive horror experience featuring live-action NPCs and challenging puzzles. Teams are recommended for the brave. Enhanced nighttime special effects are now available.]

【Starry Night Journey: Ride in a glowing boat through a dream tunnel and enjoy a holographic starry sky projection】

[Flame Theater: A large-scale live-action fantasy performance with special effects, starting at 8 PM sharp]

Wang Ling's gaze lingered on the "Terrifying Ancient Castle" for an extra half second.

Sun Rong leaned closer to look, and when she saw the eerie castle icon, her finger twitched slightly: "This... looks like a haunted house?"

"Mm." Wang Ling nodded.

"Do you want to go?" Sun Rong asked softly.

Wang Ling didn't really care.

For him, haunted houses were like strolling through his own backyard.

Even the most terrifying illusions and traps are nothing more than child's play to him.

But he noticed that when Sun Rong asked this question, there was a flicker of both eagerness and fear in her eyes.

"I'm fine with anything," Wang Ling said.

"I..." Sun Rong bit her lip, "I want to take on a challenge."

Seeing her expression of "clearly a little scared but also wanting to challenge," Wang Ling felt the seal ripple slightly again.

He pulled a packet of new flavored instant noodles from his pocket, tore open the packaging, and handed it over.

Sun Rong paused for a moment, then took it: "This is..."

"Calm down," Wang Ling said succinctly.

Sun Rong couldn't help but laugh: "You're already calming down before you've even gone inside?"

But she still ate the packet of crispy noodles in small bites; it was salty, peppery, and seaweed flavored, salty, fragrant, and crispy.

"Then...shall we go take a look?" She finished her instant noodles, as if she had regained her courage.

"good."

The two walked side by side towards the terrifying castle area.

...

Meanwhile, at the park's monitoring center.

Ryoko Kugimiya sat in the main seat of the circular control panel, with more than a dozen screens in front of her displaying real-time footage of various areas of the park.

One of the screens was locked onto Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

The high-definition camera can even capture the crumbs on Sun Rong's lips when she eats instant noodles.

"Target has headed to the haunted castle," Wang Zhen's voice came through the communicator.

He was currently disguised as a vendor selling glowing headbands, loitering near the castle entrance: "Need to adjust the difficulty?"

Ryoko Kugimiya tapped her chin lightly with her folding fan, pondering for a moment: "Proceed as planned, activate 'Special Assist Mode.' Adjust the timing of those frightening mechanisms and NPCs to the point where Rongrong might be startled and approach Ling Zhenren. Timing! We must control it perfectly!"

"Understood." Wang Zhen paused, then added, "But what if Master Ling gets bored?"

"Don't worry." Ryoko Kumiya smiled: "I had someone add some little surprises to the castle. There are a few puzzles that require two people to solve, and some places... will be very dark."

She switched screens, brought up a diagram of the castle's interior, and pointed to several areas marked with red circles: "In these places, I'll have the staff temporarily turn off the lights for three to five seconds. In the dark, a person's instinct is to want to get closer to their companions."

Liu Qingyi's voice came from another channel, tinged with helplessness: "You guys are really... unscrupulous."

"This is called creating an opportunity," Ryoko Kugimiya said confidently. "Besides, we have no ill intentions. Rin and Ryo already care about each other; they just need a catalyst."

Zhuo Yi's voice joined the discussion: "Be careful. Don't really scare Junior Sister Sun. Also, Chen Chao and the others seem to be moving towards the castle as well."

The camera switched to another surveillance camera, where Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe were seen sneaking around about fifty meters behind Wang Ling and Sun Rong, hiding and acting so badly that it was unbearable to watch.

Chen Chao even bought three glowing demon horn headdresses to wear on his head, thinking that he was very well disguised. Little did he know that the three bright red horns were as conspicuous as lighthouses in the night.

"Let them follow along." Ryoko Kugimiya shook her head helplessly. "It's good that we need the audience to record the exciting moments. Wang Zhen, make sure they don't get too close, so as not to spoil the atmosphere."

"receive."

...

The entrance to the haunted castle is designed as a dilapidated European manor gate.

The iron fence was covered with simulated vines and spider webs, and two grotesque stone gargoyles stood on either side of the door, their eyes flashing with an eerie red light.

There were more people in line than I expected. Most of them looked like couples, but actually 70% were staff members sent by the Kyuhō (Nine Palaces) to liven up the atmosphere. Only 30% were specially invited to participate in the park's internal testing activities. Many of these people were couple bloggers from short video platforms.

The nine palaces weren't hired for nothing; after the experience, these bloggers were required to publish videos recommending the park.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong were at the back of the line, with seven or eight groups ahead of them.

While waiting, Sun Rong couldn't help but look around at her surroundings.

The lights at the entrance were dimly lit, with only a few antique-style kerosene lamps emitting a yellowish glow.

The stereo played faint, whistling wind sounds and strange noises, and a faint scent of sandalwood lingered in the air.

"It's...quite realistic," Sun Rong said softly, her fingers unconsciously gripping the strap of her handbag.

Wang Ling grunted in response, his gaze sweeping over the "terrifying" decorations.

The gargoyle's eyes are ordinary LED lights, the vines are plastic, and the spiderweb is made of spray-coated filaments.

The craftsmanship was good, but he felt no fear whatsoever.

What bothered him more was that the procession was moving a bit slowly.

Five minutes have passed before they let two groups of people in.

At this rate, it will be at least another twenty minutes before it's their turn.

Wang Ling took out a packet of instant noodles from his pocket and tore open another one.

This time it's barbecue flavor.

"How many bags did you bring?" Sun Rong couldn't help but ask as she watched him magically produce snacks one after another.

Wang Ling thought for a moment and replied succinctly, "Enough to eat."

He had already set up a built-in storage space in his school uniform. It looked ordinary on the outside, but inside it was connected to a small storage space.

For example, right now, he has at least fifty different flavors of instant noodles, twenty bags of potato chips, and some jelly and chocolate in his pocket.

Sun Rong, of course, knew nothing of this. She simply assumed that Wang Ling really loved snacks, even bringing so many with him when he came out to play.

"Give me one." She held out her hand.

Wang Ling handed it over.

The two of them leisurely shared barbecue-flavored instant noodles at the entrance of the haunted house, a scene that contrasted sharply with the tense couples around them.

The girl in the couple in the front row glanced back at them and whispered to her boyfriend, "Look how calm they are, still eating..."

The boy stubbornly insisted, "I... I'm perfectly calm!"

No sooner had the words left his mouth than a piercing scream suddenly rang out from inside the castle.

The boy jumped in fright and almost spilled the cola in his hand.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong looked up at the same time.

The scream lasted for about three seconds, then turned into crying, and finally faded away.

It must be that some mechanism inside was triggered, or an NPC suddenly jumped out and scared people.

Sun Rong swallowed hard, slowing down her eating of the instant noodles.

Wang Ling glanced at her, then took a small item out of his pocket and handed it to her.

It was a white jade pendant about the size of a thumb, carved into the shape of a small rabbit, and it gleamed with a warm light in the dim light.

"What is this?" Sun Rong took it, and it felt warm to the touch.

"Amulet," Wang Ling said calmly.
