Chapter 2325 The Self-Cultivation of Elixir Materials

The passageway, parted by black mist, resembled the esophagus of a giant beast, exuding a nauseating, putrid stench.

Yuan Chang's question just now pierced through the last psychological defense of everyone except Wang Ling.

Zhou Ping's legs went weak, and he only managed to stay upright by clinging tightly to Wang Ling's arm.

The other two men in gray were also pale and trembling violently. This fear of facing death can only be understood by experiencing it firsthand.

Black 112's forehead veins bulged, and the spiritual power of the Foundation Establishment Great Perfection stage was uncertain in his body.

He knew this was a final, desperate gamble, and he was circulating his spiritual energy, preparing to find an opportunity to deliver his final blow.

Everyone was on edge.

Only Wang Ling maintained that bewildered expression.

Beneath his lowered eyelids, his consciousness had already silently permeated the entire area of darkness.

"Go inside!"

Black 112 squeezed out two words from between his teeth and stepped into the narrow passage first.

He knew there was no way out now; staying meant certain death, while going inside might offer a sliver of a chance for negotiation.

The crowd followed closely behind.

Wang Ling tugged at Zhou Ping, who was practically clinging to him, and stepped inside.

He never imagined that while he had Wang Muyu and Wang Nuan as his accessories at home, he would also grow new accessories on himself outside.

Black 201 and the remaining two dared not fall behind, and scrambled to catch up.

The passage wasn't long, but every step felt like walking on soft swampy soil, as if it might collapse at any moment.

Invisible pressure squeezed in from all directions, trying to crush the intruder's will.

At the end of the passage, the view suddenly opened up, but it was even more unsettling.

This is a huge circular hall, without any lights inside. The light comes from the dark green runes that flow on the walls and floor.

These runes, like living things, twisted and writh, emitting an eerie, malevolent light.

In the center of the hall was an enormous pit.

Inside the pit, countless twisted, tormented faces wailed in resentment, yet no sound could be uttered; only the piercing screams of the soul assaulted everyone's consciousness.

This is the source of all the resentment in the entire Deep Mountain Dan Garden, and also the true form of that garden master!

Above the pool of resentment, a twisted "throne" is suspended, pieced together from countless fragments of alchemy furnaces.

A blurry figure was lounging lazily on the throne.

He was draped in a wide robe that seemed to be woven from shadows, and his face had no clear features, only a constantly shifting mass of chaotic mist.

Only one pair of eyes, like two will-o'-the-wisps piercing through the fog, coldly scrutinized the "elixirs" that had entered.

"Hmm... this batch of goods is more interesting than the previous ones."

The headmaster's voice resonated directly in everyone's souls, carrying a playful tone, as if watching an ant.

His gaze first swept over Hei 112 and Lin Susu.

"One has a decent foundation and vigorous blood and energy; the other has a fierce aura and a resilient soul... both are excellent primary medicine ingredients."

Hei 112 and Lin Susu immediately felt a chilling power sweep over their entire bodies, as if even their souls had been seen through.

Then, the head of the garden looked at Wang Ling and Zhou Ping.

"This one... cowardly and timid, with weak soul power, can only be an extra to add a bit of resentment." He commented to Zhou Ping, who was so frightened that he closed his eyes and wished he could faint.

Finally, that gaze settled on Wang Ling.

"Huh?"

Yuan Chang let out a soft exclamation, and a cloud of mist swirled across his muddled face.

"This person is strange. His soul appears unremarkable. But why can't we see through him? It's like there's a layer of mist surrounding him?"

Wang Ling's heart skipped a beat.

It's not that I'm afraid, but I think it's troublesome.

He was already trying his best to disguise himself, suppressing his presence to the extreme.

However, as an aggregate of resentment, the head of the academy was indeed much more sensitive to souls than ordinary cultivators, and was able to detect even the slightest incoordination.

However, that's all.

Given Wang Ling's status, even if he stood still and let the other party observe him for ten thousand years, the head of the academy would never be able to figure out his true nature.

Sure enough, after examining him for a while, the head of the academy showed a hint of impatience on his face, which was covered in mist: "Fine, maybe he's just a useless person with an abnormal soul. When refining him, just throw him into the soul pool, and we'll be able to squeeze something out."

Wang Ling: "..."

He felt offended.

Just then, something unexpected happened!

Yuan Chang seemed to have lost patience for careful observation, and his dark green gaze suddenly locked onto Hei 112 and Lin Susu.

"Alright, the time has come. You two are of the best quality, so you will be the main ingredients and placed in the alchemy furnace for nurturing!"

Before the words were finished, two enormous ghostly hands, formed from pure resentment, suddenly emerged from the pool of resentment and grabbed at Hei 112 and Lin Susu with lightning speed!

The speed was so fast that it exceeded the reaction limit of a Foundation Establishment cultivator!

"careful!"

Black 112 had only a moment to let out a roar, unleashing all its spiritual power without reservation. Its fists glowed with scorching golden light as it fiercely slammed into the ghostly hand that was grabbing at it!

"Boom!"

The fist and the ghost hand collided, producing a dull, loud sound.

Black 112 groaned, a trickle of blood spilling from the corner of its mouth, and staggered backward. The ghostly hand only paused slightly before grabbing again!

On the other hand, Lin Susu reacted even faster.

Instead of taking the hit head-on, she retreated rapidly, while simultaneously drawing a green arc with her poisoned dagger and stabbing towards the ghost hand's wrist!

"laugh!"

A green light flashed, and the ghostly hand was actually stopped for a moment, its movement momentarily halted!

"Huh? You actually know how to dispel evil spirits?"

Yuan Chang let out a slightly surprised snort: "Unfortunately, your cultivation is too weak!"

The blocked ghostly hand suddenly erupted with even stronger resentment.

With its five fingers spread wide, accompanied by a soul-tearing shriek, it accelerated its grab towards Lin Susu once more!

Just as the two were about to be captured by the ghostly hand and thrown into that terrifying pool of resentment!

At this critical moment—

"Thump!"

A muffled thud, neither too loud nor too soft.

It was Zhou Ping, who had been hiding behind Wang Ling.

Overwhelmed by fear, his legs went weak, and he stumbled, crashing heavily into Wang Ling's back.

Wang Ling, on the other hand, was "just" knocked forward by this sudden collision.

The direction he lunged at was precisely the side where Black 112 was confronting the ghostly hand.

Just as he lost his balance and his arms were flailing wildly in search of a foothold,

His fingertips inadvertently brushed against a seemingly decorative jade pendant hanging from Black 112's waist.

That jade pendant is a protective artifact passed down through the Black 112 family, capable of resisting mental attacks to a certain extent.

However, under the powerful suppression of resentment in this place, the spiritual light had long since dimmed.

The moment Wang Ling's fingertips touched the jade pendant, a powerful transformation technique was instantly activated!

"Buzz!"

The jade pendant suddenly burst forth with an unprecedentedly dazzling golden light!

This golden light was not an attack, but rather carried a strange power that soothed the mind, instantly purifying the surrounding raging resentment!

The ghostly hand that reached for Black 112 was suddenly illuminated by the golden light, and the arm made of resentment instantly melted away by more than half, painfully shrinking back into the pool of resentment!

"what?!"

The head of the garden let out a fierce roar filled with shock and anger, and the figure on the throne sat up abruptly.

Even the ghostly hand that was grabbing at Lin Susu on the other side was slowed down for a moment because of this change, allowing Lin Susu to avoid a fatal blow, although her sleeve was torn open.

Everyone was stunned, including Black112 himself.

He stared in disbelief at the jade pendant at his waist, its light gradually dimming.

Is this thing... really that powerful?

It wasn't mentioned in the family heirloom's instruction manual!

Only Wang Ling knew the truth.

Just how powerful is his Great Transformation Technique?

Even a jade pendant that could be considered worthless has been transformed into a treasure under this enlightenment, instantly activating and amplifying its "calming and soul-soothing" properties, causing it to burst forth with holy light that wards off evil in a short period of time.
