Chapter 2305 Strange Questions and Answers: Is it Turtle Soup?

The enormous natural grotto has a high dome, with countless shimmering stalactites hanging upside down.

The air was cold and damp, filled with a strange sandalwood scent.

The interior space is spacious, and every scene detail is incredibly realistic.

The enormous statue, over twenty meters tall, stood majestically.

The statue is made of neither gold nor stone, and its surface is smooth, yet it is covered with countless fine dark red lines.

Its face was indistinct, except for a pair of huge eyes that radiated a scarlet light, looking down at the tiny masses with a kind of malice that disregarded all living beings.

What's even stranger is that the statue has a smile on its face, and just one glance is enough to send chills down your spine.

"What... what kind of place is this?!" The fat man's voice trembled, his previous arrogance vanishing completely in this bizarre environment.

"Are we in the second round of the game?" Li Jing, a female office worker, took a deep breath.

She forced herself to calm down and scanned her surroundings.

Finally, my gaze settled on the scarlet lettering that appeared in front of the statue's base.

[Second Round Game: Bizarre Quiz]

The rules are explained below:

[One: The statue will depict a dish of "turtle soup noodles".]

[Second: Each participant has one opportunity to ask a question to the statue. The question must be in the form of a "yes" or "no" question.]

[Part Three: In this round of the game, the statue will also answer with "yes" or "no." There is an 80% probability of it being the truth and a 20% probability of it being a lie. The distribution of truth and lies is determined by the statue itself.]

[Part 4: Participants must ask questions and make judgments to collectively reconstruct the complete story behind the "noodle soup".]

[Five: There is no time limit for the game, which continues until the truth is revealed to be more than 85% or everyone gives up. If the truth is not revealed in the first round, one person will be randomly eliminated. If the game proceeds to the second round of questions and answers, both people will be eliminated if they fail.]

[Now, the game begins.]

The cold, hard words of the rules are etched into everyone's vision.

"Annihilate...one person?!"

The high school student was deathly pale and almost couldn't stand steadily.

The construction worker, Xiao Li, and the man in glasses also looked pale and subconsciously huddled together.

Grandma Zhang held tightly to the thin, cold hand of the girl beside her.

Lin Feng's forehead was covered in cold sweat, and his gaze involuntarily drifted to Yan Ting beside him.

Wang Ling, possessing Yan Ting's body, maintained a calm demeanor that stood out from the crowd, quietly observing everyone's performance on the spot.

"Buzz!"

The blood-moon-like pupils of the statue shone brightly with a deep red light.

A deep, eerie sound echoed through the empty cave, reaching everyone's minds:

[Tangmian: A young cultivator hailed as a 'rising star of swordsmanship' secluded himself in his cave to break through to the Golden Core stage. Three days later, his master broke through and entered, only to find his disciple sitting cross-legged on the ground, surrounded by sword energy, his Golden Core formed, a smile on his lips, but he was already dead. Overwhelmed with grief, the master burst into laughter, calling out three times: "Good disciple!"]

The bizarre story of the noodle soup instantly captivated everyone's attention.

A rising star in swordsmanship, successfully achieving the Golden Core stage, simultaneously perishing, and the master laughing heartily...

Everyone listened to the perplexing riddle, their brows furrowed in confusion.

"What...what does this mean? Did the master kill his disciple?"

The fat man blurted out, but immediately realized that this did not comply with the rules of asking questions.

"Calm down, everyone!"

Li Jing stepped forward again. As expected of a former research elite, she quickly analyzed, "Due to the rules, each of us only has one chance to ask a question, and the statue will only answer ten times in total, and the answers are unpredictable! We must plan our questions carefully and not waste them!"

"How do we plan this? Who knows if it will tell the truth or lie!" Lin Feng scratched his hair in frustration.

He felt his memory was becoming clearer, as if he remembered indirectly causing the death of a colleague in his pursuit of promotion...

This made him even more anxious.

"It is precisely because the truth or falsehood is uncertain that cross-validation and logical reasoning are needed."

Li Jing's gaze swept over everyone: "I suggest we first raise some basic questions that can build a logical framework. For example, let's first confirm whether the disciple's death is directly related to the master."

"I agree."

Possessing Yan Ting's body, Wang Ling chimed in at the opportune moment, guiding the rhythm, "Could we first ask, was the disciple's death directly caused by the master?"

This question gets to the heart of the matter and can lead to a variety of possibilities.

"Okay! Who wants to go first?" Li Jing looked at everyone.

The group exchanged glances, none of them willing to waste their precious opportunity to ask questions. The atmosphere became tense for a moment.

"Damn it, what are you afraid of! I'll go first!"

The fat man seemed to want to regain his lost face. He stepped forward, looked up at the eerie statue, and shouted, "Hey! Was that disciple's death caused by his master?!"

The statue's crimson pupils stared at the fat man, and after a moment, the eerie voice rang out again:

【no】

That concludes my first answer.

The statue answered "no".

"Master isn't the murderer?"

The fat man paused for a moment, then looked at everyone with a smug look: "See, it's very simple!"

"Don't be too happy yet," Li Jing calmly reminded her. "It might be lying."

"The next question is important."

The bespectacled man pushed up his glasses and tentatively suggested, "I think we could ask... whether the disciple truly died of natural causes, or whether he suffered a qi deviation?"

"This question is too broad to be answered simply by yes or no."

Li Jing shook her head: "We should ask more precise questions. For example, did the disciple suffer any external attacks during the Core Formation process?"

This time, the usually taciturn, dark-skinned, muscular man stepped forward and repeated Li Jing's question in a rough voice.

The statue remained silent for a moment, then answered:

【yes】

"There is an external attack!"

Construction worker Xiao Li exclaimed in alarm, "Could it be that the enemy has infiltrated?"

"Not necessarily." Lin Feng interjected, feeling his thoughts clearer. "It could be... a problem with the technique itself? Or perhaps there's a trap in the cave? I ask: Did the external attack that caused the disciple's death originate from inside the cave?"

This was the problem he came up with in a moment of inspiration, trying to narrow down the scope.

The idol replied:

【yes】

The attack likely originated from inside the cave!

Everyone felt a surge of energy, sensing they were one step closer to the truth.

"But is there anyone else inside the cave besides my master? Is it possible that the formation or magical artifacts accidentally killed them?" the petite girl muttered to herself.

Grandma Zhang seemed to remember something, her eyes becoming somewhat unfocused. She murmured, "I...I think I made a similar puppet before. Its internal core was unstable, and it would self-destruct..."

Her words were somewhat disorganized, but they offered a possible line of thought.

Seizing the inspiration, Li Jing immediately whispered a few words to the bespectacled man who was about to ask the next question.

The bespectacled man nodded, then asked nervously, "Is there an offensive non-living device or formation inside the cave that caused your disciple's death?"

The idol replied:

【no】

"Not a device or array?"

Li Jing frowned: "What is that? Is it a living creature? A spirit beast? Or... a ghost?"

This "no" answer seems to have brought the reasoning to a standstill.

Inside the cave, if it wasn't the master who directly attacked, or the array device, where did the attack come from?

Just then, Wang Ling, who was possessing Yan Ting, felt the sealed memories in everyone's minds begin to emerge like shattered mirror shards.

He subtly released a soothing thought, not by giving a direct answer, but by silently helping everyone better piece together those fragments of memory.

"I...I think..."
