Chapter 2394 Wang Ling's Identity?

As night deepened, the lights in Disneyland were turned off one by one.

Wang Ling stood in front of the floor-to-ceiling window of his eighth-floor room, holding a freshly opened packet of honey butter-flavored instant noodles in his hand, his gaze calmly sweeping over the gradually falling silence of the amusement park below.

The direction in which those figures disappeared was the "terrifying castle" they had just experienced during the day.

Wang Ling put the instant noodles in his mouth and chewed slowly.

Almost simultaneously, extremely subtle changes in airflow were observed from the air conditioning vents in the room.

That's not normal airflow.

There are signs that someone sneaked in through the ventilation duct.

Wang Ling turned around and walked towards the minibar in the room.

He opened the small refrigerator, which was neatly filled with various drinks and snacks.

All of these were prepared by the hotel at Kunomiya Ryoko's request in advance, clearly tailored to his preferences.

Wang Ling took out a bottle of Lingquan soda water, unscrewed it, and took a sip.

Then he walked to the desk, opened the tactical tablet, and brought up the Paradise map.

The check-in progress displayed on the wristband is: 5/20.

Fifteen projects are still missing.

Which one should I do first tomorrow?

The "Mist Maze" requires a three-person team; you can team up with Chen Chao and Guo Hao.

The "Rapid Roller Coaster" stamp is said to be very difficult to obtain, requiring specific actions to be performed during the ride.

"Abyss Drop Tower"...

As Wang Ling was pondering his strategy for tomorrow, a soft "click" sound suddenly came from the grille of the ventilation duct.

It was so light that even a Foundation Establishment cultivator might not be able to detect it.

But Wang Ling heard it.

He put down his tablet, walked to the bedside and sat down, then pulled out another bag of snacks from his pocket—this time it was wasabi peas.

"Crunch."

The crisp sound of chewing echoed in the room.

Inside the ventilation shaft, the infiltrator codenamed "Shadow Blade" froze.

He held his breath, completely merging his body into the shadows, even reducing his heart rate to less than ten beats per minute.

As one of the top 20 professionals in the Primordial Assets Assassination Group, Shadowblade has carried out 43 infiltration missions without ever failing.

His specialty is "shadow magic," which allows him to transform himself into a true shadow, moving, lurking, and assassinating in corners where light cannot reach.

The mission was simple: infiltrate the target room and install listening devices and tracking talismans.

The goal is just to become a high school student, at most at the Foundation Establishment stage.

Logically speaking, Shadowblade could complete this task with his eyes closed.

Before setting off, Mo Xuan gave a special instruction: "Be careful of that guy named Wang Ling. If you sense anything unusual, retreat immediately."

Shadowblade didn't think much of it at the time.

How abnormal could a high school student be?

But now...

He felt a strange chill run down his spine as he listened to the rhythmic "crackling" sounds coming from the room.

The goal is to eat snacks.

I ate with particular focus and tranquility.

It was as if they had no idea there was someone else in the room.

Didn't you notice?

Or... do they simply not care?

Shadow Blade peered into the room through the gaps in the ventilation grille.

Wang Lingzheng sat on the edge of the bed, head down, intently eating wasabi peas.

He would occasionally glance up at the map on the tablet, doodle a couple of times with his finger, as if he were planning something.

It was exactly the state of an ordinary tourist relaxing before bed.

"They probably didn't notice," Shadowblade concluded.

He took a deep breath, and his body began to liquefy, seeping out like black ink from the gaps in the ventilation grille and dripping onto the carpet.

No sound was made.

The black liquid flowed across the carpet, silently sliding under the bed.

That was the darkest area in the room, and also his best hiding spot.

Once he enters the shadows under the bed, he can completely hide and then find an opportunity to install equipment.

The liquid flows slowly.

There was still one meter between the bed and the floor.

Half a meter.

Twenty centimeters...

Just as the shadow blade was about to touch the shadow on the edge of the bed—

"Smack."

The ceiling light in the room suddenly turned on.

It wasn't a normal turn-on; it suddenly lit up completely without warning!

The blinding white light filled the entire room, leaving no shadows unseen!

"!!!"

The black liquid that Shadow Blade had transformed into trembled violently, almost revealing its true form.

He forced himself to stay still, and continued to flow under the bed.

But the next second, he found that he couldn't move.

They were neither immobilized nor bound.

Instead... the shadow under the bed disappeared.

Under the all-around illumination of the overhead light, the area under the bed, which should have been the darkest, was now clearly illuminated, and even the texture of the carpet was clearly visible.

There is no shadow.

Not at all.

"How is this possible..." Shadowblade was filled with shock and disbelief.

He had studied the room's lighting design beforehand; the ceiling light was a soft, warm yellow, which couldn't possibly provide such a perfect, all-encompassing lighting effect!

Unless... someone changes the rules of light.

This thought sent a chill down Shadowblade's spine.

He suddenly realized that he might have made a fatal mistake.

The target wasn't that he hadn't been found.

The goal is to... play with him.

Like a cat catching a mouse, it doesn't rush to bite, but plays with it first.

Shadowblade made a decisive decision and abandoned the mission!

escape!

We must escape!

The Shadow Blade was no longer hidden, and the black liquid instantly condensed into a human shape, with a masked man wearing a black bodysuit appearing in the center of the room.

He held a teleportation talisman in his hand, about to activate it!

But the moment my finger touched the talisman—

"puff."

The talisman spontaneously combusted.

Without warning, as if ignited by an invisible flame, it was instantly reduced to ashes.

Shadow Blade's pupils suddenly contracted.

This is not an accident!

He suddenly looked up at Wang Ling.

The boy was still standing by the bar, holding a bottle of soda water, and was unscrewing the cap.

He didn't even glance at Shadowblade, as if the sudden appearance of another person in the room was perfectly normal.

"You..." Shadow Blade squeezed out a single word from between his teeth.

Wang Ling took a sip of soda water, and then finally turned around and glanced at him.

Those eyes were very calm, as calm as a deep pool.

But Shadowblade felt as if his soul had been frozen.

What kind of look was that...?

There was no murderous intent, no hostility, not even curiosity.

It's like looking at a piece of furniture, or... an insect that has wandered into the room.

It's insignificant; I can kill it with a flick of my wrist.

Shadowblade's back was instantly soaked with cold sweat.

He understood.

Why did the previous six people fail their mission?

Why did Mo Xuan specifically remind him?
