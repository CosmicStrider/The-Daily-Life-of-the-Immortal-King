Chapter 2218 Meng Nian's Mysterious Cave

Wang Ling was on high alert when suddenly, a powerful spiritual force, as strong as a physical entity, surged forth from Meng Nian's body with overwhelming force.

That power carried a chilling pressure, as if it could distort the surrounding air.

Wang Ling's pupils contracted slightly. His years of cultivation experience allowed him to quickly make a judgment: this was definitely not a power that Meng Nian could possess on her own; it must have been caused by a sudden external force.

After all, although Meng Nian's own realm was not to be underestimated, her strength was far from sufficient to release such terrifying mental pressure that could make one's soul tremble.

In his past experiences, ordinary mental power was nothing to Wang Ling.

Those faint mental fluctuations were like a tiny ant biting you; you felt almost nothing at the time, and only afterwards could you vaguely see some tiny marks on your skin.

But the power emanating from Meng Nian now is completely different, making Wang Ling secretly wary.

Sensing the powerful mental impact, Wang Ling subconsciously glanced quickly at Sun Rong beside him. He noticed that Sun Rong's expression was no longer as relaxed as his own; her brows furrowed slightly, and a hint of tension flashed in her eyes.

Fortunately, Sun Rong had the protection of the Ao Hai, a faint light that circulated around her, keeping the powerful mental pressure at bay. For the time being, it was not a problem for her.

In that instant, a powerful illusion, like a surging tide, swept over everything with immense spiritual power.

Accompanied by a sharp, howling gust of wind, which carried a strange power, the gauze covering Meng Nian's eyes was suddenly blown away with a "whoosh".

Meng Nian's purple-glowing eyes met Wang Ling's for a fleeting moment, her gaze filled with resentment and deep hatred, as if she wanted to see right through Wang Ling's soul.

At this point, Meng Nian probably already knew that her carefully planned scheme had been completely exposed.

Two major obsessions remain unfulfilled: first, the person who deceived her feelings has not yet received due punishment, rendering her revenge plan in vain; second, she has not yet completed her plan to enter the Earth's core to find her brother's spirit, a wish that has appeared countless times in her heart, now shattered.

She had planned and schemed endlessly, using every possible means, but she never expected that everything she had painstakingly planned and invested so much effort in would ultimately be interrupted by Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

Almost simultaneously, the atmosphere in the ward suddenly became eerie. The originally bright sunlight seemed to be frozen by some mysterious force, slowly condensing into the shape of liquid gold, radiating a strange luster, and then silently seeping into the void along the tiny cracks in the walls.

Wang Lingmin immediately detected a peculiar odor, emanating from a bronze vessel corroded for thousands of years. It carried a metallic, sweet smell unique to those from a spatial rift, sending chills down one's spine.

Immediately afterwards, the entire ward seemed to be gripped by an invisible giant hand, and twisted wantonly like crumpling a sheet of aluminum foil.

Large patches of plaster began to peel off, revealing dark red, flesh-like textures underneath, exuding an eerie aura.

Wang Ling knew that a heterogeneous space, somewhere between a biological cavity and a secret corridor, was rapidly forming, and an unknown crisis was about to descend.

As their consciousness was drawn into another space by that strange power, a dark green cave appeared before them. A thin layer of mist permeated the cave, making the entire space appear even more mysterious and eerie.

In the very center of the cave stood an enormous egg, five meters tall. This egg was connected to many tubes of varying thicknesses, which pulsated rhythmically like blood vessels.

The surface of the giant egg is not a smooth shell, but covered with scale-like hexagonal lattices. Each lattice unit is constantly playing apocalyptic scenes of different civilizations, including planetary explosions, city destruction, and the wails of living beings, which are chilling to behold.

On the scales at the center of the giant egg's shell, there are energy markings inscribed with ancient and mysterious symbols. These symbols emit a faint glow, as if telling ancient secrets.

Wang Ling could tell at a glance that this was clearly not a product of the ancient cultivation world. Compared with the ancient and strange style of the entire cave, it seemed out of place and even a bit abrupt.

Centered on this giant egg, the cave was filled with all sorts of glass jars, the kind of glass jars that can only be found in modern biochemical laboratories.

The sounds of viscous bubbles bursting echoed all around. The green liquid in the glass jars was not still; it continuously churned and swirled in tiny eddies. At the center of each eddy floated half-dissolved eyeballs and various limbs, the eyeballs still emitting an eerie light, as if retaining the fear and resentment of their former lives.

Each glass jar contains all sorts of strange creatures, which come from all corners of the universe and come in all shapes and sizes.

Some resemble winged humanoid creatures, while others look like giant octopus monsters.

Besides those strangely shaped creatures, there were also traces of human cultivators. Their clothes and accessories still showed some characteristics of the cultivation world, but now they were all tattered and soaked in green liquid, having long since lost their vitality.

Sun Rong immediately spotted a giant, disintegrating eyeball. The eyeball was the size of a basketball, with its pupils shrunk to the size of pinpoints, as if reflecting the flashes of light from the annihilation of different planets, sending chills down one's spine.

The terrifying sight also gave Sun Rong a bitter almond taste on her tongue, and her stomach began to churn.

Wang Ling's expression was solemn as he briefly inspected the contents of the glass jars.

He discovered that the creatures contained in the glass jars had long since died. Their bodies were being gradually broken down into energy particles by the green liquid inside the jars, which were then being fed into the giant egg in the center through the tubes, providing it with a continuous supply of energy.

"Strange, how could such an ancient evil cultivator's cave contain cutting-edge technology that only exists in modern times?" The incredible scene before her eyes left Sun Rong both shocked and puzzled.

Even though she had witnessed many grand scenes and experienced many strange things while following Wang Ling, the shocking scene before her still made her body tremble uncontrollably. A strong feeling of nausea surged up her throat, making the uncontrollable bitter almond taste even more intense. She barely managed to hold it in and not vomit.

At the railing surrounding the giant dome, Meng Nian, who had removed her blindfold, stood silently like a ghost, her back to Wang Ling and Sun Rong, wearing a pure white gauze robe.

Upon closer inspection, the materials used for these railings are also extraordinary. They are made from the corpses of ancient creatures, with each bone protrusion emitting a ghostly green light that flows slowly along the bone marrow cavity, as if telling the story of past killings and bloodshed.

Meng Nian turned her head to the side, staring coldly at the two of them. A beam of light shone down from the top of the cave, illuminating her stunning profile. The light filtered through the thin gauze robe, highlighting her perfect figure.

However, Meng Nian's cold face carried a hint of melancholy, but even more so, despair. The madness and determination revealed in her eyes were chilling.

Without a doubt, this gloomy and terrifying cave was Meng Nian's masterpiece.

"You are the first people to see this and still be standing." Meng Nian's voice was cold and deep, as if it came from the depths of hell, echoing in the cave.

She then slowly looked around at the jars containing the corpses of various creatures. Some of the bodies in the jars hadn't completely decomposed, leaving behind fragments of limbs that swayed slightly in the green liquid. Meng Nian's lips curled into a cold smile, a smile filled with disdain and cruelty: "Those who were able to find their way here in the past have all become the mediums to open the Earth's core..."
