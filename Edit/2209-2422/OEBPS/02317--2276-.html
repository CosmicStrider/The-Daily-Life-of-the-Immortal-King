Chapter 2276 Bai Zhe's Primordial Evil Cultivator Legion

On the first day back at Number 60 High School, the air was filled with a sense of laziness and relaxation.

During breaks, a corner of the elite class classroom was particularly lively.

Chen Chao was enthusiastically recounting his adventures in the "simplified youth version" of the inner world, focusing on how he bravely dealt with the lava lizards, while Guo Hao chimed in and debunked his claims.

The two of them recounted their experience in a dramatic, almost comedic way, manner, eliciting gasps of surprise from the surrounding classmates.

Of course, most importantly, after the special training at the Mountain of Regression, Chen Chao and Guo Hao have both entered the Golden Core realm, which is what the other elite students envy the most.

"As expected! The resources enjoyed by those selected for the Earth's Core Project are beyond imagination! Before entering the Earth's Core, there's intensive training, and now Chen Chao and Guo Haoshuang have already reached the Golden Core stage! They can choose any university they want!"

"Gentlemen! Don't forget me when you become rich and powerful! You two are now the face of Number 60 High School! Speaking of which, in all the years that Number 60 High School has been established, no one has ever directly entered the Golden Core stage in the second half of the first year of high school!"

In the elite class, voices of envy followed one after another.

There were feelings of regret and a little jealousy, but no one said anything sarcastic or unhelpful to the unity.

After all, Chen Chao and Guo Hao were both very popular, and their advancement to the Golden Core stage would benefit all the elite students in their class.

Having such a classmate by your side, who has already demonstrated the Golden Core formation process, can save you from many detours.

Almost no one noticed that Wang Ling also participated in this project to the center of the earth. He was pushed up as a "mascot," and everyone had become accustomed to Wang Ling's presence in every team's project.

Because as long as Wang Ling is present, things always go exceptionally smoothly at Number 60 High School...

Some people even started secretly worshipping Wang Ling because of this.

For example, they might stuff instant noodles and other snacks into his desk, then write notes with wishes on them... like wishing to become rich this year, to reach the Golden Core realm overnight, to pass every exam, and so on...

To be honest, these wishes are a bit too simple for Wang Ling.

Unfortunately, he needs to maintain his ordinary life.

Wang Ling would offer some discreet help to students who sent him instant noodles, but only if they put in some effort in line with their own wishes. If they just wanted to be idle, Wang Ling couldn't help them.

As for the classmates who didn't receive the right snacks, Wang Ling simply pretended not to have seen their wishes.

Yes, that makes sense.

At this moment, Wang Ling sat in his seat, as if he were in a transparent barrier, where the noise from the outside world was automatically reduced to background white noise.

He was looking down, intently staring at his phone screen.

On the screen is the snack shop owner's latest reply:

[Boss: "Wang! Your description is too abstract! What does 'mineral feel' mean? And how do you represent 'a flame that's been blown out'? I tried mixing black pepper with deep-sea salt and adding some squid ink. It turned out quite black, but my tongue felt numb for a long time after eating it. Is that what you call a 'burning sensation'?"]

[And then there's that "Hellfire" dish. I used ghost peppers and a fire-attribute spiritual plant powder. It's spicy enough, but the aftertaste is too strong; it doesn't "dissipate quickly" at all! [Attached: Several pictures of snacks that are abnormally charred or scarlet]]

Wang Ling frowned slightly.

Is this difficult?

He recalled the unique tactile sensation of the minerals deep within the earth and the spiciness that burst forth and quickly subsided, feeling that his description was accurate enough.

The human taste system is indeed too fragile.

He silently typed a reply: "[Mineral sensation, like licking a clean piece of jade. The spiciness should be short and sharp, like a needle prick.]"

Click to send.

He could almost picture the boss on the other end of the screen going crazy.

"Wang Ling, what are you looking at so intently?" Sun Rong's voice rang out beside him with a smile.

She naturally pulled over an empty chair next to her and sat down, placing a bottle of warm herbal yogurt on his table: "Try this? It helps restore your spiritual power."

Her movements were fluid and effortless, as if they were just incidental, but a barely perceptible blush at the base of her ears betrayed her.

The surrounding classmates were already used to this; it was perfectly normal for the young lady of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group to take care of a "weak" classmate.

Wang Ling temporarily pulled his attention away from the difficult "taste description project," glanced at the bottle of yogurt, and then looked at Sun Rong's bright, expectant eyes.

It was given to me by Sun Rong.

Although they are other "snacks".

But Wang Ling discovered that it wasn't so hard for him to accept.

During his travels in the inner Earth, he seemed to frequently accept food from Sun Rong, which had almost become a habit.

"...Thank you." He took the yogurt, inserted the straw, and drank it quietly.

It has a sweet taste with a subtle, ethereal quality, but it's definitely not as delicious as instant noodles.

Sun Rong watched him accept, and the curve of her lips became even more pronounced.

She didn't press him about what he was looking at. Instead, she took out a bottle of the same yogurt and drank it with him, her gaze seemingly casually sweeping across the bright sunlight outside the classroom window. She was in a good mood.

Wang Ling didn't dislike this quiet, companionship atmosphere; in fact, he found it somewhat comfortable.

He couldn't quite define the emotion, but he did sip his yogurt a little slower.

"Oh right!" Sun Rong seemed to suddenly remember something, lowering her voice to make sure only the two of them could hear: "How's Nuan Nuan doing lately? I haven't seen her since she came back from the Earth's core. I ordered a new star projector for her; I heard it can simulate the operation of a small star field, it should look pretty good at night."

When she mentioned Wang Nuan, her tone was exceptionally natural and gentle.

She knew that Wang Ling was special, and she genuinely loved the little one who could express her emotions by babbling and using her shadow.

Wang Ling paused in his yogurt-slurping motion, then nodded: "She's fine."

After thinking for a moment, I added two more words: "I miss you."

That's not a lie.

Wang Nuan is able to sense emotions, and she does show an extraordinary dependence and fondness for Sun Rong, who often brings her fun toys and has a gentle and kind demeanor.

Sun Rong's eyes instantly lit up like stars: "Really? Then I'll go see her this weekend... um, after I finish my report!" She tried to make her tone sound less impatient.

Wang Ling nodded, agreeing on behalf of his sister.

He thought it was good that Wang Nuan had someone to play with, which would save him some trouble... probably.

The school bell rang, rescuing Sun Rong, who seemed to be still pondering a topic.

Wang Ling precisely tossed the empty yogurt bottle into the distant trash can, slung his backpack over his shoulder, and was the first to leave the classroom—clocking out of get off work every day without ever delaying.

Chen Chao called out from behind, "Lingzi! It's time for a dungeon tonight! The new Netherworld Dungeon is supposed to drop rare materials!"

Wang Ling had his back to them, raised his hand and waved casually to indicate "I understand," but his steps did not stop at all.

He wasn't interested in the dungeon, but he was very interested in Chen Chao's promise to do his three days' worth of talisman homework after he finished the dungeon.

This is what makes daily life efficient and convenient.

Back home, it was still Wang's mother's concerned nagging and Wang's father's attempts to extract "novel material" from their son.
