Chapter 2221 Siblings Reunion

In fact, from the very beginning, Wang Ling never intended to have a major fight with Meng Nian. He knew that Meng Nian's obsession with her younger brother was deeply ingrained, and it was this excessive obsession that led her astray in life, causing her to make many heartbreaking mistakes.

However, deep down, Wang Ling always felt that Meng Nian was not inherently bad, but was blinded by her obsession and lost her way.

So, he and Sun Rong entered that eerie mental hospital to search for Meng Nian.

Their ultimate goal in this trip was to help Meng Nian untangle the knot in her heart and free her from the abyss of pain.

You should know that for other people, finding a person's soul is a thousand times more difficult than finding a needle in a haystack; it is almost an impossible task.

But for Wang Ling, this was not a difficult task; he only needed to use his miraculous "Great Blood Source Technique".

The "Great Blood Source Technique" is a powerful and versatile divine ability.

It can not only directly and deeply analyze the composition of bloodlines and accurately determine which race the other party belongs to, but also clearly know how many powerful bloodline powers the other party possesses.

Even more impressively, it also possesses the amazing ability to help with paternity testing and soul tracing.

Of course, there is an important prerequisite for tracing the origin of the soul: Meng Heng must be Meng Nian's biological younger brother. Only in this way can this supernatural power truly help Meng Nian.

Therefore, the moment Wang Ling saw Meng Nian, he made up his mind to do everything he could to help this woman who was suffering.

But who could have imagined that before Wang Ling could even utter a few words, Meng Nian suddenly launched an attack, unleashing a fierce assault on him. Left with no other choice, Wang Ling could only defend and counterattack, and after a few rounds, Meng Nian was severely injured.

Looking at Meng Nian lying on the ground, Wang Ling sighed inwardly. This conflict could have been completely avoided.

Wang Ling sighed softly, turning his gaze to a mysterious crack in the laboratory.

He reached out, and spiritual energy slowly condensed at his fingertips. Then, he carefully pulled Meng Heng's soul out of the crack.

The soul looked radiant, as if it contained endless mysterious power. Wang Ling grasped the soul at Meng Heng's head and slowly poured in a gentle spiritual energy.

In an instant, Meng Heng seemed to wake from a deep dream, and suddenly began to breathe heavily.

At this moment, the dim and flickering lights shone intermittently in the dilapidated biochemistry laboratory.

Broken glassware lay scattered on the floor, emitting a cold and eerie glow under the light. The air was thick with the pungent smell of disinfectant, mixed with a faint scent of death that sent chills down one's spine.

Meng Nian stared wide-eyed, hardly believing what she was seeing. After searching for so long and overcoming countless hardships, she finally saw that familiar yet unfamiliar figure in this place filled with terror and despair—her younger brother, Meng Heng.

"Ah Heng..." Meng Nian's voice trembled, filled with disbelief and endless longing, as if it were a call from the depths of her soul.

Meng Heng's spirit gradually regained consciousness. His faint soul, like a wisp of light swaying gently in the wind, appeared so fragile, as if it could be blown away by the wind at any moment.

His translucent body, reflected in the flickering lights of the laboratory, made him appear even more frail, as if a gentle breeze could blow him away.

Wang Ling had just successfully traced Meng Heng's soul back to its source, but Jing Ke had infused Meng Heng with a small amount of spiritual power to maintain the stability of his spirit. He dared not directly pour in excessive spiritual power, fearing that he might accidentally shatter Meng Heng's already fragile soul, causing him to disappear from this world forever.

Meng Heng weakly opened his eyes and looked at his sister Meng Nian. His heart was filled with shock and joy; he had never imagined that he would be able to see his sister again.

In the afterlife, there was a boundless void. He wandered aimlessly in that void, as if time had lost its meaning, and a moment felt like ten thousand years.

In that desolate, empty world, there was no sound, no light, only endless loneliness and darkness that followed like a shadow.

As time passed, his will was gradually eroded. Slowly, he even forgot who he was. All his past experiences and those beautiful memories vanished like smoke.

At that moment, a deep sense of regret flashed through Meng Heng's heart. He hated himself for so easily giving up his precious life for a scheming and manipulative woman.

"Sister..." Meng Heng's voice was weak and hollow, as if it came from another distant world, carrying a kind of coldness that did not belong to this world, making people feel a chill in their hearts.

Tears welled up in Meng Nian's eyes instantly. She rushed over impatiently, wanting to hug Meng Heng tightly, as if that would keep her younger brother by her side forever.

But the moment her body touched Meng Heng, she passed right through his spirit and couldn't grasp anything.

Her hand remained suspended in mid-air, her face filled with pain and despair.

"Ah Heng, I'm late... You're so foolish..." Meng Nian looked at Meng Heng, tears streaming down her face like a burst dam, sobbing uncontrollably, her voice filled with self-blame and regret.

A flicker of pain crossed Meng Heng's eyes as he looked at his sister's tear-streaked face, his heart filled with bitterness: "Sister, it's all my fault. I shouldn't have given up on myself so easily..."

Meng Heng's voice was filled with endless regret. He recalled the warm times he had spent with his sister, those little moments of happiness, which now felt like sharp needles piercing his heart.

At the same time, Meng Heng's body began to tremble slightly, his soul seemingly becoming increasingly unstable in this laboratory filled with evil energy. He suppressed the fading pain, using all his remaining strength to try and get closer to his sister, wanting to feel her presence one last time: "Sister, I'm already so happy to see you again... You shouldn't have harmed innocent lives for my sake, those beings were all innocent..."

Meng Nian reached out her hand, tears blurring her vision. She desperately tried to touch her younger brother: "Aheng, I don't want to lose you again. Let's find a way to live together, okay..."

Her voice was filled with helplessness and pleading.

A bitter smile appeared on Meng Heng's lips. He knew it was just an impossible hope: "Sister, I no longer belong to this world. You must live on, carrying my hope with you, and live on..."

Meng Nian shook her head desperately, her cries echoing in the empty and silent laboratory, as if trying to shatter the dark and oppressive space. Meanwhile, Meng Heng's body gradually became more and more transparent, his gaze remaining fixed on his sister, filled with longing and reluctance, as if he wanted to forever etch her image into the depths of his soul.

"Goodbye, sister..."

As Meng Heng's voice faded away, his soul coalesced in Wang Ling's hand into a blue gem, radiating a soft yet mysterious light. Meng Nian gazed at the gem, collapsed to the ground, tears streaming silently down her face, and the entire laboratory fell into a deathly silence.
