"He has now gone abroad. Note that this 'he' is a man."

"A man, him?"

Wang Ling and Zhi Jiunian's lips twitched simultaneously, feeling that the amount of information was a bit too much.

However, it makes sense. Changing one's name to conceal one's identity is too low-level. Changing one's gender directly might be more helpful in hiding one's identity.

For a cultivator, gender reassignment is quite easy to do, especially in the Mishu Kingdom... where there are as many as 112 genders to choose from.

Wang Ling was only slightly surprised, but his expression quickly returned to normal.

He suddenly thought of Fang Xing. The main reason was that Fang Xing could freely switch his gender, so there was nothing strange about what he did to Weng Jinxiang.

It's very convenient for monks to undergo gender transformation nowadays.

In fact, no surgery is even necessary.

With just one pill and one spell, one can completely change their gender.

But no matter how meticulously Weng Jinxiang planned, he never imagined that his seemingly flawless operation would be discovered.

Investigating Weng Jinxiang wouldn't even require involving the War Clan; the Huaguo Shuilian Group's current intelligence network is more than enough to thoroughly investigate him.

"Where is Meng Heng's sister?" Wang Ling asked Sun Rong.

"Found it."

Sun Rong looked at Wang Ling, opened the map on her phone, and marked a coordinate.

After reading it, Wang Ling frowned slightly.

Psychiatric hospital?

Could there be another patient named Li Huowang there?

Songhai, an inconspicuous mental hospital.

The hospital's name is "Unremarkable".

Upon seeing the plaque hanging high on the rooftop, Wang Ling even began to doubt whether the dean's mental state was still normal.

As Huaguo Water Curtain Group's main business is in the field of elixirs, and as a giant in the elixir industry, it has some supply relationship with hospitals of all sizes in Songhai.

However, according to Sun Rong's investigation, this inconspicuous mental hospital is not within the supply scope of Huaguo Water Curtain Group.

Based on this alone, Sun Rong guessed that the hospital might not be as inconspicuous as it seemed on the surface. In the domestic channels, Huaguo Shuilian Group's supply of pills could keep the price to the lowest level, focusing on small profits and quick turnover to benefit the people.

There are indeed companies competing with Huaguo Water Curtain Group in terms of import channels, but over the years, Grandpa Sun has never taken those companies seriously.

Wang Ling gazed at the mental hospital before him, lost in thought.

This wasn't his first time visiting a similar place.

A familiar feeling welled up inside me.

With the support of modern cultivation technology, mental illnesses have become quite easy to control. Therefore, most cultivators suffering from mental illnesses today have been tricked into practicing pirated or forbidden cultivation techniques, eventually leading to demonic possession.

Of course, there are also a small number of monks who voluntarily entered mental hospitals through connections.

This is out of concern for their own safety, especially for some monks who have gotten involved with too many worldly affairs; for them, mental hospitals are the perfect sanctuary.

The hospital will provide them with comprehensive protection, and even if someone comes to their door and they fight back directly inside the mental hospital, they will not break any laws.

It can be said that mental hospitals in the cultivation world are like arenas that have been specially carved out in the city, where all sorts of unexpected events happen almost every day.

Wang Ling and Sun Rong changed into casual clothes before arriving at the gate. If they had come here in their sect's uniforms, it would have been too conspicuous, regardless of their purpose, and they would have been easily remembered.

"Hello, we're here to visit relatives."

Sun Rong handed over two passes that Zhan Zong had just made, and said to the gatekeeper with a big smile.

Her expression was quite natural, showing no sign of anything amiss.

Although the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group has no connection with this mental hospital, it is not difficult for Zhan Zong to produce two freshly issued passes in a short period of time.

When the young boy and girl suddenly appeared at the door, the gatekeeper simply raised an eyebrow and said, "Your documents are fine, you can come in."

With all their credentials in order, the appearance of the two young monks did not seem to arouse much suspicion from the gatekeeper.

At such a young age, his cultivation level is at most at the Golden Core stage, while even the gatekeeper here is at the Nascent Soul stage.

Clang!

The gatekeeper pressed the remote control, and the iron gate opened directly, its sound echoing like a bell in the space.

After watching Wang Ling and Sun Rong walk into the inpatient area, the security guard slowly took out his walkie-talkie and said, "Two people have come looking for Meng Nian."

A deep, resonant middle-aged male voice quickly came from the other end of the walkie-talkie: "Looking for Meng Nian?"

"yes."

"Okay, I understand."

After the call ended, the gatekeeper lit a cigarette, crossed his legs, and turned on the old-fashioned stereo to listen to some music.

...

It can be said that Wang Ling and Sun Rong's entry into the mental hospital was quite smooth, with almost no obstacles.

However, this is precisely the abnormal manifestation.

Wang Ling knew that they were being watched, but while they were in the open, the people watching them were in the shadows.

Upon arriving at the inpatient department, Wang Ling found that the overall layout was quite cozy, without any oppressive atmosphere, and more like a family-style hotel apartment.

Each psychiatric patient in the suite has their detailed name and photo displayed on an electronic screen at the entrance. The name is encrypted, and visitors need to enter an access code, their full name, and the last four digits of their ID number. After system verification, the barrier at the entrance will be temporarily lifted, allowing visitors to pass.

If you leave the ward, you will need to repeat the above steps.

Wang Ling noticed that her eyes were wrapped with several layers of white cloth, which suggested that she had some vision problems.
