Chapter 2368 The Ghostly Hand of Drawing Lots

The lottery has begun.

On the holographic screen in the center of the hall, forty names began to scroll rapidly.

Chen Chao put his arm around Wang Ling's shoulder, his eyes wide open: "Lingzi, do you think we might be drawn into a group? If the two of us are in a group, and we bring three bronze-level fans along, wouldn't that be a free-for-all?"

Wang Ling remained silent, his gaze calmly fixed on the screen.

He doesn't really care who he's paired with.

Playing with bronze-level players and unfamiliar heroes might seem like an insurmountable challenge to the average person, but for him...

It's just a little troublesome.

That's all.

Sun Rong stood on the other side of Wang Ling, her fingers unconsciously twirling the ends of her hair.

She was also looking at the screen, but her peripheral vision kept falling on Wang Ling.

She watched the entire solo match where Wang Ling defeated Luo Xinghe 3-0, holding her breath the whole time.

Especially the last wave of explosive charges planted inside the wall, the millisecond-precise calculations, still make her heart pound.

"Wang Ling..." she began softly, "If, I mean if, we were drawn into the same group, which hero would you choose?"

Wang Ling turned his head and glanced at her.

Sun Rong blushed slightly under his gaze and quickly added, "I was just curious... you seem to be able to play all kinds of heroes."

"Look at the lineup," Wang Ling said in a very calm voice.

Then he pulled a packet of new flavored instant noodles out of his pocket.

Sun Rong couldn't help but laugh.

This person is always eating.

But for some reason, she felt that this version of Wang Ling was very real.

At least it's more real than Luo Xinghe, who always has a perfect smile.

Thinking of Luo Xinghe, Sun Rong's gaze unconsciously swept to the other side of the hall.

Luo Xinghe and Xia Zhinuan were standing together, whispering to each other.

Luo Xinghe wore his signature gentle smile and occasionally nodded to the teammates who looked over, as if the crushing defeat that afternoon had never happened.

But Sun Rong noticed that Luo Xinghe's hands were always in his pockets, clenched tightly.

"He's faking it," Sun Rong thought to herself.

And they put on a very strong front.

Just then—

"bite!"

The lottery system emitted a crisp beep.

The names on the screen stopped scrolling.

The grouping results are in.

Group 1: [Wang Ling (Number 60 High School)] + [Xia Zhinuan (Star Glory)] + three random viewers

Group 2: [Luo Xinghe (Xingyao)] + [Guo Hao (Number 60 High School)] + three random viewers

Group 3: [Sun Rong (Number 60 High School)] + [Chen Chao (Number 60 High School)] + three random viewers

Group 4: [Wang Muyu (Number 60 High School)] + [Zhou Rui (Xuanming)] + three random viewers

...

The group names are displayed in columns.

A chorus of voices rose and fell in the lobby.

"Wang Ling and Xia Zhinuan are paired together? Interesting!"

"Luo Xinghe and Guo Hao... Guo Hao's playing style is very stable, which should be able to make up for the shortcomings of the fans."

Chen Chao was overjoyed to see that he was paired with Sun Rong, and slapped his thigh: "Sun Rong! Let's go bot! You support me as AD! I guarantee we'll crush the enemy!"

Sun Rong smiled and nodded, but her gaze involuntarily drifted toward Wang Ling.

Wang Ling and Xia Zhinuan...

That sweet-looking female anchor looked at Wang Ling strangely that afternoon.

Now I've drawn another set.

Sun Rong pursed her lips.

She felt a little uncomfortable.

At this moment, Luo Xinghe looked at the pairing result of "Wang Ling + Xia Zhinuan" on the screen, and the corners of his mouth began to turn up wildly.

The first step of the plan has been successful.

He turned his head and gave Xia Zhinuan a look.

Xia Zhinuan received his gaze, her fingers trembled slightly, but she quickly lowered her head and pretended to straighten her clothes.

No one noticed that the small USB drive she was holding in her other hand had just been unplugged from the spare interface in meeting room number three of building B.

Thirty seconds.

The program ran for thirty seconds and then automatically destroyed itself.

Perfect.

"Zhi Nuan," Luo Xinghe said in a voice only the two of them could hear, "It's up to you now."

Xia Zhinuan gave a soft "hmm," her voice trembling.

...

After the groups are determined, each group will have ten minutes to discuss tactics.

Wang Ling and Xia Zhinuan were taken to the No. 3 preparation area by staff.

This is a semi-open cubicle with two tactical tables, a few chairs, and a holographic tactical board hanging on the wall.

Xia Zhinuan's heart was pounding when she walked in.

She glanced at Wang Ling secretly.

The boy was already sitting in a chair by the window, with another bag of instant noodles in his hand.

This time it's seaweed flavored.

He ate with great focus and slow, deliberate pace.

It felt less like a hellish friendly match and more like a leisurely afternoon snack.

"Wang Ling..." Xia Zhinuan began, her voice softer than usual, "Shall we discuss the lineup?"

Wang Ling looked up and glanced at her.

His eyes were very calm.

The calmness made Xia Zhinuan feel uneasy.

"What are you good at?" Wang Ling asked.

"I...I mainly play mid lane and support," Xia Zhinuan said. "But the rules restrict us to using heroes below Gold proficiency. My hero pool below Gold isn't actually that deep."

That's the truth.

As a streamer with a 100-star rating in King of Glory, she usually plays the strong heroes of the current version. She hasn't touched the simple heroes that are common in low-ranked games for a long time.

Wang Ling nodded without saying a word.

He swiped a few times on the tactical tablet and brought up the hero list.

Heroes below Gold Proficiency will have a faint gray border around their portrait.

Wang Ling's gaze swept across the list.

Suddenly, his fingers stopped.

I opened a hero's details page.

【Messenger of Sorrow - Vigus】

Xia Zhinuan was stunned when she saw the hero.

Vigus?

She had played this hero a few times, but she wasn't really familiar with him.

The skill mechanics are somewhat unique, requiring prediction and a sense of rhythm.

"You want to play this?" she asked.

Wang Ling shook his head.

He turned the tablet toward Xia Zhinuan and pointed to a detail in the hero's skill description.

【Passive - Twilight Aura】: Vigus's skills apply the 【Twilight Aura】 mark, which triggers a fear effect after stacking three layers.

"You play," Wang Ling said.

Xia Zhinu blinked: "I'll play mid lane? Then you..."

"I'll play jungle," Wang Ling said.

"Jungler?" Xia Zhinuan was even more surprised: "But the rules say we have to bring three Bronze players. If we give the jungler position to a player, the game might fall apart..."
