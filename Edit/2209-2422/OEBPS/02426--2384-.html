Chapter 2384 Performance Art

Youzhe's eyes narrowed, and he immediately activated a secret technique, trying to see through Wang Ling's true cultivation level.

But all he saw was a blurry fluctuation of spiritual energy.

Very weak, at most mid-Foundation Establishment stage.

But this "weak" spiritual energy formed a protective layer on Wang Ling's body that even he couldn't penetrate.

This kind of protection was something the phantom serpent had never seen before.

It's not protective aura, nor is it a magical artifact for defense; rather, it's a kind of repulsion at the level of rules.

That's an absolute difference in level.

A cold sweat instantly broke out on Youzhe's back.

He finally understood why the Funeral Pavilion had rated this "high school student" as a second-class Xuan-level student.

I also understand why the reward for this deal was so generous.

This is a complete trap!

The target is definitely not some Foundation Establishment stage high school student!

Youzhe wanted to retreat, but his professionalism told him: he couldn't escape.

The rules of the Funeral Parlor state that once a commission is accepted, it must be completed.

If it fails, not only will the commission be returned, but a huge amount of compensation will also have to be paid.

Moreover, his reputation will be damaged, making it difficult for him to accept high-paying commissions in the future.

"There's still a chance..." Youzhe forced herself to calm down.

Although the target is strange, it doesn't seem to have any intention of launching an attack.

Perhaps he didn't even realize it was an assassination attempt?

Youzhe looked at the Soul-Sealing Needle in the trash can, then at Wang Ling's indifferent expression, and a glimmer of hope rose in her heart.

If the target is unaware that this is an assassination attempt, then he still has the opportunity to use other means.

For example, the [Erosion Spirit Powder] should be taking effect soon.

Even if the target has antitoxic properties, it should have shown a reaction after such a long time.

The ghostly creature merged back into the shadows, waiting patiently.

Five minutes later...

Wang Ling sat at his desk and opened another packet of instant noodles.

This time it's spicy crayfish flavor.

He ate slowly, glancing occasionally at the time on his tablet.

1:10 PM.

The exhibition match starts at 2 PM, and he can rest for 40 minutes.

After finishing his instant noodles, Wang Ling stood up and walked towards the restroom.

Youzhe's spirits lifted.

The bathroom is small and not as well-ventilated as the master bedroom, so the concentration of corrosive substances will be higher!

As long as the target stays inside for a few minutes...

Wang Ling walked into the bathroom and closed the door.

Youzhe immediately adjusted the hysteroscope and switched to the bathroom view.

Then he saw a scene that made him question the meaning of life:

Wang Ling stood in front of the sink, turned on the tap, and washed his face.

Then he looked up at the mirror, stared at his reflection for three seconds.

Then, he stretched out his hand and drew a circle on the mirror.

As he moved his finger, faint white lines appeared on the mirror.

That was a purification array.

The lowest level one can be drawn by cultivators in the Qi Refining stage; its effect is to dispel negative energy and impurities.

The magic array was completed, and a flash of light appeared.

The toxins from the "Erosion Powder" that permeated the bathroom were instantly purified.

"......"

Youzhe opened his mouth, but couldn't speak.

After washing his face, Wang Ling dried it with a towel and then walked to the toilet.

He lifted the toilet lid and glanced at the water tank.

A yellow talisman was floating in the water tank.

【Gathering Yin Talisman】.

This is also one of the traps set by the Ghost Snake.

If the target goes to the toilet, the talisman will release yin energy that will invade the target's body, and when combined with the spirit-eating powder, it will accelerate the disorder of spiritual energy.

Wang Ling stared at the talisman for two seconds.

Then he reached out and scooped out the talisman.

The talisman paper was damp, and part of the cinnabar runes on it had already blurred.

Wang Ling spread the talisman on the sink and poked it with his finger.

The talisman did not react at all.

"Inferior product," Wang Ling commented, then crumpled the talisman into a ball and threw it into the trash can.

He flushed the toilet and walked out of the bathroom.

The whole process felt as natural as cleaning up clutter in your own bathroom.

Youzhe was completely dumbfounded.

The Soul-Eroding Powder has been purified.

The Yin-gathering talisman was thrown away as trash.

There was no response after the Soul-Sealing Needle was inserted.

All three insurance policies have failed.

Moreover, the target's every crack was done so casually, as if it were just a coincidence.

This is even more frustrating for Youzhe than a direct brute-force attack.

A brute-force attack at least indicates that the target recognized the danger and used force to crush it.

But this "effortless resolution" seems to mock his professional competence.

"I don't believe it!" Youzhe gritted her teeth.

He has one last trick up his sleeve.

It's a bit risky, but I have to try.

The ghost insect emerged from the shadows and re-integrated into the ventilation duct.

He took something out of the storage artifact.

It was a puppet the size of a palm.

[Substitute Doll].

The life-saving artifacts issued by the Funeral Pavilion can create a substitute at critical moments, while the main body can instantly teleport to a safe location.

Youzhe hadn't originally planned to use this, but now he's changed his mind.

He wanted to test the waters himself to see if the target was truly unfathomable or just a lucky break.

He bit his fingertip and dripped a drop of blood onto the doll.

The doll instantly came to life, its features contorted, transforming into the form of a ghost.

The stand-in doll jumped out of the ventilation duct and landed on the room floor.

It stretched its limbs and then swaggered toward Wang Ling.

Wang Lingzheng was sitting at his desk, looking down at his tablet, seemingly looking up some information.

The stand-in puppet walked behind him, raised its hand, and a ball of black energy condensed in its palm.

This is Youzhe's unique secret technique, "Soul Devouring Wave," which can directly attack the soul.

No matter how strong the target's physical body is, their soul is always fragile.

The stand-in doll pushed the energy ball toward the back of Wang Ling's head.

They are getting closer and closer.

Just as the energy ball was about to hit...

Wang Ling suddenly sneezed.

Achoo!

He leaned forward slightly and lowered his head.

The energy ball grazed his hair, flew past, hit the wall, and vanished without a trace.

Wang Ling rubbed his nose and muttered, "The air conditioning is too high."

Then he picked up the remote and turned up the air conditioning temperature by two degrees.

The stand-in doll stood frozen in place.

Youzhe, inside the ventilation duct, nearly coughed up a mouthful of blood.

Sneezing?!

This is fucking possible?!

Youzhe manipulated the substitute puppet to gather an energy ball once more.

This time, he targeted Wang Ling's side of the neck.

But just as the energy ball was about to be released—

"Knock knock knock".

A knock sounded on the door.

Wang Ling looked up: "Who?"

Chen Chao's loud voice came from outside the door: "Lingzi! Open the door! It's urgent!"

Wang Ling got up to open the door.

The stand-in doll quickly shrank behind the curtains.

The door opened, and Chen Chao rushed in: "Lingzi! Have you seen the forum? That bastard Luo Xinghe has hired trolls to smear you again!"

"I've seen it," Wang Ling said.

"Is this your reaction?" Chen Chao glared. "Those trolls say you got in through Sun Rong's family connections! They say you cheated in training matches! How can you tolerate that?"

Wang Ling walked to the desk, picked up a bottle of spiritual spring water, unscrewed it, and took a sip: "Let them be."

"What nonsense!" Chen Chao slammed his fist on the table in anger. "I'm ready to dox those influential bloggers who are stirring up trouble! Guo Hao said it's illegal, but I just can't swallow this!"

He grumbled and paced around the room, eventually ending up next to the curtains.

The stand-in doll held its breath.

Completely oblivious to the commotion behind the curtains, Chen Chao continued shouting, "In the exhibition match this afternoon, if we face Luo Xinghe again, we'll beat him to a pulp! Beat him until he quits the net!"

Wang Ling grunted in response.

Chen Chao continued to rant for a while, then suddenly remembered something: "By the way, Sun Rong just said in the group that her dad seems to have noticed the public opinion, and the public relations department of Huaguo Water Curtain Group may take action."

"oh."

"Couldn't you say just a couple more words?" Chen Chao said helplessly. "Fine, you've always been like this. I'm going back to prepare. Good luck this afternoon!"

After he finished speaking, he left in a hurry.

The door closed.

The room returned to silence.

The stand-in doll emerged from behind the curtains, ready to continue its attack.

But it had only taken one step—

Wang Ling suddenly turned his head and looked at it.

Their eyes met.

The stand-in doll froze.

Wang Ling stared at it for three seconds, then walked over, bent down, and picked it up.

"Who dropped this toy?" he muttered.

Then he walked to the trash can, preparing to throw the stand-in doll in.

But after a moment's hesitation, he took it back.

Wang Ling took the doll, walked to the desk, and took out a pen from the drawer.

He wrote one word on the back of the doll:

"vegetable."

The handwriting is neat and precise, with each stroke carefully executed.

After finishing writing, he placed the doll on the desk, next to the packets of instant noodles.
