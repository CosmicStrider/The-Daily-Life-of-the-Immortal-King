Chapter 2304 The Weight of Trust

The pure white space fell into a deathly silence.

The scorch mark left by that warning black thunderclap was deeply imprinted on everyone's heart, leaving only intense fear.

Elimination means the soul is removed.

This is not a game, but a raw survival challenge.

Wang Ling calmed himself down and examined the memories of the possessed middle-aged man.

Just as he suspected, in this almost realistic dream, a crucial part of the middle-aged man's memories was erased.

Moreover, Wang Ling deduced that it wasn't just this middle-aged man; probably everyone else was the same.

However, erasing memories is no challenge for Wang Ling. He can see through everything and fill in the missing memories.

Soon, the deliberately erased memory was restored, and Wang Ling learned all the information about the middle-aged man.

This person's name is Yan Ting. He is at the early stage of the Golden Core realm and has a good talent for cultivation. He runs a company dedicated to developing modern magical artifacts and spiritual energy charging devices.

As a business owner, Yan Ting originally had a smoother life experience than many ordinary cultivators. His company was doing well, he worked hard to marry his sweetheart, and they had two children. His family was happy and fulfilling.

But thirty years ago, an accident occurred that took everything from him.

Due to flaws in the materials supplied by upstream suppliers, coupled with some senior executives of the company embezzling from the material supply chain, their spiritual energy charging devices experienced product quality problems. Some cultivators experienced bulging and even explosions of the charging devices produced by Yan Ting's company after using them.

Yan Ting had no choice but to recall all the charging devices that had already been sold on the market, but the damage to the company's reputation and the huge compensation burdened him, ultimately taking away everything he owned.

After losing his wife and children and his family, Yan Ting, filled with intense resentment, continued searching for new evidence. He suspected that this was not an ordinary production accident, but that someone was framing him behind the scenes...

Because of this incident, he hasn't had a good night's sleep in decades. He wanted to use the "Sleep Treasure" to recharge himself, but he never expected to be dragged into this deadly game.

Just as Wang Ling was interpreting the life experiences of the man named Yan Ting, who was possessed by him.

A desperate roar suddenly rang out.

"Hurry! There's no time! Do as she says!"

Lin Feng pointed at the female office worker who called herself Li Jing: "We'll follow your orders!"

The instinct for survival temporarily overcame the fear.

Li Jing took a deep breath and quickly scanned the remaining nine people: "Now, as I instruct, split into two groups of five people each, and immediately stand on the platforms at both ends of the scale! Quickly!"

She walked to the left side of the platform first, calling out, "You! You! And you, come with me to the left!"

She pointed to Lin Feng, the frightened old lady, a muscular young man with a dark complexion, and a thin girl whose head was always down and whose face could not be seen.

Lin Feng immediately helped the old lady up and staggered onto the left platform.

The petite girl and the muscular man followed quietly.

On the right platform, the fat man with the shifty eyes took the lead. He grabbed the still-shaken high school student and pointed to two other men who looked bewildered.

A man wearing a construction site uniform had his hands covered in old cocoons.

The other one wore black-rimmed glasses and looked like an honest office worker.

The ten people quickly split into two groups and stepped onto the cold metal platform.

[Countdown: 8 minutes 31 seconds]

The massive balance scale remained perfectly level, its threads perfectly aligned.

Everyone breathed a sigh of relief.

Sudden!

Creak!

A series of harsh, scraping sounds rang out.

The scales suddenly tilted to the left!

The amplitude instantly exceeded the safety line, reaching nearly twenty degrees!

"What happened?!"

The fat man on the right platform exclaimed, "The one on the left is too heavy! Is he overweight?"

He glanced suspiciously at the group of people standing to his left. Apart from the dark-skinned, muscular man, everyone else was well-proportioned and didn't look overweight at all.

While she was still wondering, Li Jing retorted sharply: "You have five grown men over there! We have this muscular guy and this skinny guy, and the rest are all women! How could we be overweight!"

As Li Jing spoke, cold sweat began to bead on her forehead. She speculated, "The rule is to maintain balance, but maybe it's not based on physical weight at all!!"

"What are you measuring then?" The bespectacled man pushed up his glasses, his voice trembling with tears. "Brothers and sisters, I don't have time to mess around with you all here... My mother is seriously ill! She needs me to take care of her!"

At that moment, a thought flashed through Lin Feng's mind, and he blurted out, "It's trust! The game is called 'The Scales of Trust'! It measures the level of trust between us! But we don't know anything about each other's experiences! So there will definitely be doubts and distrust!"

"Trust?" The fat man scoffed, his chubby face jiggling. "Trust my ass! I don't trust anyone! The left side is too heavy now, either someone gets off, or..."

His sinister gaze swept over the five people on his left: "Push that old woman who looks the most useless down there!"

The old woman trembled and looked at Li Jing and Lin Feng in horror.

"How dare you!" Li Jing stepped forward and shielded the old lady behind her.

"What's there to be afraid of? Eliminating one of her will save nine of us! It's a great deal!" the fat man said confidently.

The high school student and the construction worker behind him exchanged glances, and although they didn't express their opinions, they seemed to agree with the fat man's actions in the face of human nature.

The bespectacled man whose mother was seriously ill remained silent. His eyes were obscured by the reflection in his glasses. He thought of the old woman and his mother, who looked to be about the same age and even had similar appearances. He felt a pang of pity.

[Countdown: 7 minutes 15 seconds]

The tilt of the balance is still slowly increasing!

Fine cracks have begun to appear at the edge of the platform!

Once doubt and suspicion arise, there is no unity whatsoever.

"He's right..." The construction worker on the right suddenly spoke up in a muffled voice, his eyes turning to the left with a complicated expression: "Grandma Zhang, I'm sorry. I have a wife and children to support at home, I can't die here. Maybe this is just a nightmare, and it won't endanger my life!"

Grandma Zhang looked at him in disbelief: "Little Li? You...you're someone I've watched grow up!"

Everyone was surprised; they never expected that the old lady and the construction site man actually knew each other!

The construction worker, Xiao Li, lowered his head in shame, but his feet seemed rooted to the spot and he didn't move.

"See? Reality is that cruel!" The fat man grinned smugly. "Hurry up and decide! Otherwise, we're all doomed!"

"No...no!" The thin girl on the left, who had been keeping her head down, suddenly raised her head, revealing a pale but pretty face. Her voice was trembling with tears: "The most useless person here is actually me...let me do it..."

She was about to jump as she spoke.

"Little girl! Don't do anything foolish!"

Grandma Zhang grabbed her arm and comforted her, saying, "No matter what has happened to you, you are still young... You have a long road ahead! Even if you encounter problems that you can't solve immediately, there's always a way out! You must never give up on yourself easily..."

"Keep acting! Keep acting!" the fat man interrupted impatiently. "You're making it sound so real! Who knows if you're all in cahoots, just putting on an act to gain sympathy?"

You bastard!
