Chapter 2279 The Descent of the New Dragonborn

Songhai City, Number 60 High School Campus.

In the afternoon, during the plant theory class, sunlight streamed through the back window, casting warm dappled patterns on the desks.

"The study of spiritual plants is the foundation of alchemy. Being able to remember more characteristics of spiritual plants means that you can develop newer types of elixirs. Therefore, cultivating new spiritual plants is also a key research topic in our country, just like materials science."

"Many technologies have been achieved in China, but what is often lacking is research on materials. This is similar to the relationship between alchemy and spiritual plants."

"This is the key point for the end of the semester, and it will be on the test! Everyone must remember this! As students in the elite class, you must memorize more than 3,000 kinds of spiritual plants by the end of this semester, and be able to recite the characteristics, growing environment, etc. of these spiritual plants from memory."

The ancient voice remained calm, explaining the symbiotic laws of low-level spiritual plants.

The students in the audience listened attentively, but Chen Chao and Guo Hao, who had returned from the inner world, were drowsy and enjoying the rare tranquility after their return.

The old man sighed and didn't bother with it much. After all, with Chen Chao and Guo Hao's current level of ability, they could choose any prestigious university and be admitted at will, so whether they attended classes or not was really not a big deal.

Moreover, after the Battle of the Center of the Earth, the textbook content is indeed of little help to the two who have experienced intense combat.

It seems that all schools have a similar situation, where good students are always given special treatment.

If the grades are good enough, man-made rules can always be broken.

Wang Ling sat by the window, a psionic pen between his fingers, unconsciously tapping the corner of his notebook.

His gaze seemed to fall on the cloudless sky outside the window.

In reality, Wang Tong had already pierced through layers of space, traversed endless distances, and landed outside that blessed land where sword energy soared into the sky—the Wuji Sword Sect.

The Zhanzong's interception network was operating efficiently, and Lord Diu Lei was in complete control, quickly eliminating or capturing several evil cultivator teams that attempted to infiltrate.

However, Wang Ling's fingertips, which were tapping the table, paused almost imperceptibly for a moment.

No.

Everything went so smoothly.

Bai Zhe's evil cultivator army was powerful, but its actions seemed somewhat childish.

These infiltration teams were more like bait, used to test the reaction speed of the War Master and the vigilance of the Sword Saint.

The real killer move is not here.

His consciousness, like an invisible radar, centered on the Wuji Sword Sect and swept outwards to the weaker secret realms that had sprung up around it.

Found it.

Almost the instant consciousness locked on.

Three small, hidden realms located in different directions simultaneously erupted with subtle yet unusually sharp spatial fluctuations!

That wasn't a sign of a forceful attack, but rather a precise entry! It was as if they had already locked onto that spot!

Three beams of dark red light, as thin as hair but containing the most filthy and destructive energy, precisely pierced the core of the laws of those three secret realms!

Buzz—!

The silent wails spread through the channels of the spiritual energy of heaven and earth.

Such a chilling sound cannot be perceived by those whose spiritual level is insufficient; they will only feel a sense of dread.

At the same time, the light halos around the three secret realms dimmed instantly, and the originally peaceful atmosphere was quickly polluted by an energy filled with destructive desire!

Before the creatures inside the secret realm could even scream, their life essence was forcibly twisted, turning them into deformed monsters that only knew how to kill and destroy, and began to frantically attack the secret realm's walls from the inside!

All of this happened extremely quickly and very discreetly.

The dark red energy attribute was extremely strange, and it could imitate and pollute the sword intent to a certain extent, so that when the "distress signal" they emitted was transmitted into the Wuji Sword Sect, it took on some characteristics of pretending to be possessed by evil spirits!

The Wuji Sword Sect, located on the back mountain of its main peak.

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan, who was instructing Wang Muyu in practicing basic sword techniques, suddenly trembled, his sharp, sword-like gaze piercing towards the outside of the cave, his expression instantly turning extremely ugly.

"How dare you!"

He clearly sensed the dramatic changes in those three subordinate secret realms!

What angered him even more was the strange, ambiguous sword-fighting aura mixed in with the distress signal!

This is the most vicious trap!

If left unattended, the three secret realms will be completely annihilated, countless lives will be lost, and the Sword Heart will be tarnished.

If you go to the rescue, you must use your supreme sword intent to forcibly purify the polluted core of the secret realm. The process must not be disturbed and will take time!

This was clearly a calculated move to force Yi Jianchuan to leave the core sword domain of the Wuji Sword Sect!

"Master?" Wang Muyu sensed the Sword Saint's sudden surge of anger and a hint of anxiety. He stopped playing with his little sword energy, looked up at him with a puzzled expression.

The Sword Saint took a deep breath and forcibly suppressed the surging sword intent.

He squatted down, looked at Wang Muyu, and said in an unprecedentedly serious tone: "Muyu, stay in the Spirit Garden and don't go anywhere. Your master has some things to take care of and will be back soon."

He raised his hand and unleashed a sword technique, instantly activating the sword intent throughout the entire spirit garden in the back mountain. It transformed into an incredibly solid, crystalline sword cocoon, firmly protecting Wang Muyu and the surrounding area.

This was an absolute defense he had laid out with his natal sword intent, which could withstand even a Dao Immortal's arrival for a short while.

"Oh." Wang Muyu nodded obediently and sat on the bluestone with his knees drawn up to his chest.

He watched as the Sword Saint's figure transformed into a streak of light, tearing through the cave and hurtling toward the nearest attacked secret realm!

With the departure of the Sword Saint, the vast domain of sword intent that enveloped the Wuji Sword Sect still existed, but that agile, dominating will temporarily left.

In the shadows, countless eyes gleamed with greed.

On the bluestone, Wang Muyu sighed silently. Although he was small, he could sense the malice that had been lurking around him.

His master told him that if he could win the fight, then he should kill the enemy!

As long as we can finish this quickly, that's fine.

These days he has been training with the Sword Saint. Strangely enough, although the old man's strength is far inferior to his own, Wang Muyu can still feel his progress in his mental state.

No wonder his "father" specially selected him as his master!

At this moment, Wang Muyu stared warily in all directions, his little face full of seriousness.

He loves his "dad" and also loves this adorable old man who protects his calves. He must not let them get hurt in any way!

The Wuji Sword Sect is an important training ground for Yi Jianchuan, and Wang Muyu has only one thought in his mind—that is, he must protect this place!

...

"Reporting to the Sect Master! The Sword Saint has left the Wuji Sword Sect! Heading to the Bibo Pool Secret Realm!" An urgent voice sounded from the communication channel inside the War Sect's meteorite war artifact.

Lord Diu Lei stared solemnly at the receding, dazzling sword light on the main screen, as well as the three mysterious realm lights that were rapidly emanating an evil aura.

"What a grand gesture! Directly polluting the core of the secret realm, forcing the Sword Saint to leave! Everyone, pay attention! The real attack is coming! Target: the interior of the Wuji Sword Sect! Shrink the defensive perimeter and move towards the sect's gate! We absolutely cannot let anything in!"

His order had just been issued.

The void outside the Wuji Sword Sect's cave was as if a giant, invisible hand had ripped open a massive rift a thousand miles long!

Beyond the rift lay not a dazzling starry sky, but a dark red, writhing lair of flesh and blood!

It's filled with an ominous aura!

Rumble—!

Countless grotesque monsters swarmed out from within like locusts!

Some of them retain a human form, but their bodies are covered with dragon scales, and bone spurs grow from their joints.

But even more numerous are various mutated spirit beasts, terrifying hybrids corrupted by dragon genes!

This is the true vanguard and main force of the Primordial Evil Cultivator Army!

They carried an overwhelming, suffocating aura of evil dragons, crashing fearlessly against the Wuji Sword Sect's natural sword intent barrier like a destructive tide!

Sizzle sizzle sizzle—!

The monsters at the very front crashed into the invisible barrier of sword intent, instantly being shredded into a bloody mist by the fierce sword energy.

But the monsters behind them didn't stop and continued their frenzied attack!

They consume the barrier's energy with their own flesh and blood and their lives!

Blood mist filled the air, staining the void a pale red.
