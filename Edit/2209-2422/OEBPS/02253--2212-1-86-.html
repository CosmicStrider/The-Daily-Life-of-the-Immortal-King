Chapter 2212 Quick Victory

Wang Ling rarely gets involved in conflicts within the martial arts world because he has always been most afraid of all kinds of trouble. His philosophy of "better to avoid trouble than to get involved" is a strategy that his parents formulated through countless family meetings in the past.

However, this strategy was disrupted after Wang Ling entered high school. The reason was that Wang Ling found that even if he didn't want to cause trouble, trouble would still come knocking on his door. Moreover, if left unchecked, this snowball of trouble would only grow bigger and bigger until it became unmanageable.

So what else can we do?

He had no choice but to take action.

For the sake of his ideal peaceful and tranquil life, and also for the sake of being able to peacefully enjoy the ultimate delicacy like instant noodles in a world of cultivation free from strife.

Therefore, protecting Pan Lei right now is not just because he is the son of my homeroom teacher. The main reason is that Pan Lei is connected with Zhi Jiunian. He works for Zhi Jiunian. Although he may not know that much information, he is still valuable.

In addition, Zhi Jiunian is related to the magic array module that Wang Ling previously found out. When Zhi Jiunian is brought to justice, Pan Lei will be an important witness to the case.

Regardless of the circumstances, hiring aliens who smuggled themselves to Earth to do things for him, and buying a piece of land in a cultivation metropolis like Songhai to set up a modular magic array, all these things undoubtedly reveal that Zhijiunian is no ordinary person.

This person is adept at navigating social situations, skilled in using strategies, and adept at deceiving others.

Before capturing Pan Lei, Sun Rong had never imagined that Zhi Jiunian was connected to the Infinite Universe Group. The group's latest recruitment mission was to have applicants capture aliens who had infiltrated Earth.

However, Zhijiu used these aliens who had smuggled themselves back to Earth to set up a modular magic array. The two sides seemed to be at odds, but in reality, they were in cahoots. Following this line of thought, Sun Rong suddenly felt that the chaotic thoughts in her mind were gradually becoming clearer.

"The mission to capture stowaway aliens is just a pretext; it appears to be an act of justice. But in the end, it always benefits this infinite cosmic group."

Sun Rong looked at Wang Ling and Pan Lei and gave her analysis: "As long as this mission is released, at least on the surface, this company can gain a good reputation and look like a legitimate cultivation hunting company."

"Whether we catch them or not is irrelevant. If we don't catch the aliens, at most we'll eliminate that batch of interviewees. But if we do catch them, these capable people with certain intelligence gathering abilities and skills might be targeted by this group... and they might take them away along with the captured stowaway aliens."

"Then offer high enough incentives to get these monks to work for the company, and have the stowaway aliens do the dirty work of assembling the block array."

Upon hearing this, Wang Ling sighed inwardly. Judging from the clues he had gathered so far, it was probably as Sun Rong had said. Moreover, the most ruthless thing about this group was that once these stowaway aliens who were working were discovered, they would wipe them off the earth without hesitation.

Even if they are eventually caught, there will be no cultivation methods applicable to smuggling aliens, which can be described as truly doing whatever they want.

After figuring out the ins and outs of the matter, Wang Ling felt that everything before him became much clearer. Next, he just needed to find out the secret of how the Cosmic Infinite Group used modular arrays to construct the "Spirit Gathering Integration Array".

Once this spirit-gathering array is completed, it can absorb all the spiritual energy from the entire underground veins of Songhai in a single night.

Their need for such a massive amount of psionic energy must be part of some grand plan, and Wang Ling needs to continue digging deeper.

To be honest, sometimes Wang Ling also felt quite helpless...

He has so much psychic energy that he can't even use it all. Even if the sun disappears, he can use his psychic energy to compress a new one to provide a continuous supply for trillions of years.

Some people will go to any lengths, even resorting to unscrupulous means, to collect enough psionic energy.

This is probably the difference between people.

"Go back now," Wang Ling said, looking at Pan Lei.

"Senior, you want to let me go back? You won't arrest me?" Pan Lei was stunned for a moment. He could see that Wang Ling possessed immense power, and even his voice trembled slightly as he spoke.

Wang Ling remained silent, but gave Pan Lei a wink, nodded, and drew a portal on the spot, directly taking Sun Rong away.

As for the other part of the portal, Zhuo Yi was also ready to make a move at lightning speed to clean up the mess. Just as Wang Ling was preparing to protect the group of smuggled aliens, Zhuo Yi used high-energy mud lotus roots to create many lifelike alien corpses according to the steps provided by Wang Ling.

These simulated corpses are constructed using real objects and powerful illusions; without Wang Tong, no one can decipher them.

All Wang Ling did was to get Pan Lei to go back and report to Zhi Jiunian, so that he could continue to lie in wait by Zhi Jiunian's side and not arouse the suspicion of this cunning old man.

This is not releasing a tiger back into the mountains; it's merely leaving a pawn for Zhijiu Nian and Pan Lei's other employers.

Therefore, when Pan Lei saw Zhuo Yi leading dozens of clones, carrying simulated alien corpses to replace and transfer the aliens who had fainted on the ferry, he understood Wang Ling's meaning almost instantly.

"Don't worry, senior, I will never tell you anything about your affairs. I will cooperate with whatever you ask me to do." Pan Lei knelt on one knee, and after saying this, he transformed into a phantom and left.

He knew that Wang Ling would definitely find a way to find him and assign him tasks.

And he knew very well that clinging tightly to the young man in front of him was probably his only way out of his current predicament...

He had inadvertently joined the ranks of the grey mercenary monks and was blackmailed into doing many wrong things, but it's never too late to make amends.

Wang Ling knew that Pan Lei was not a bad person.

Given Ms. Pan's character, how bad a child could she possibly raise?

Let him atone for his sins by doing good deeds.

Of course, what Wang Ling regrets most about the series of events that happened today is not Pan Lei, who went astray.

Wang Ling felt that after what he had done, the Cosmic Infinite Group was probably doomed, and Lao Huang's job search plan would likely fall through because of him.

...

Nanxian Village, on the southern outskirts of Songhai.

To Sun Rong's surprise, this time Wang Ling did not choose to wait and see, but instead planned to strike at the heart of the matter.

This is the small village that Zhijiu usually uses for live streaming. Wang Ling simply used a teleportation portal to bring Sun Rong here.

Wang Ling knew very well that time was of the essence, as there were less than three days left before the Earth's Core Project. He needed to clear up the troubles on the surface before heading to the Earth's Core World to complete the sect's trial.

Of course, troubles are never-ending, but who knew that this unlucky guy, Jiji, would run into him...
