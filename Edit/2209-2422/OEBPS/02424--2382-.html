Chapter 2382 Complete Darkening

After the Hextech Brawl points tournament, everyone went back to the StarCraft eSports Center for lunch break, and everything seemed to be muted.

Luo Xinghe sat alone in his suite on the ninth floor of Building B, the curtains drawn tightly, with only the light from the tactical tablet screen illuminating his pale face.

On the screen is a preview of the group stage of the exhibition match finals.

Although the final list will be drawn this afternoon, based on the points ranking, he and Wang Lingyou are very likely to face each other again in the final.

Xinghe stared at Wang Ling's name, the hidden killing intent in his eyes almost devouring everything he saw.

The scene of the Hextech brawl that morning was still replaying in his mind.

Wang Ling was too cruel, and each of his deaths felt like a slap in the face, severely damaging his dignity as a professional news anchor.

Although there was no video recording, dozens of eyes in the lobby saw it at the time.

Those whispers, and the expressions of wanting to laugh but holding it in...

He couldn't imagine what the consequences would be if the recording of the final exhibition match were released.

This is what he fears most.

No matter how humiliating the match was in the morning, it was only seen by people inside the training camp.

But the exhibition match in the afternoon was different.

The video will be made public, edited into a promotional video, and disseminated through partner universities and media channels.

If he gets beaten up in public by Wang Ling again...

Luo Xinghe couldn't help but shiver.

With trembling hands, he opened Weibo and saw the comments section under his latest post.

His fans are still comforting him:

"Don't be discouraged, Xinghe! It's just a training match!"

"They're definitely not in good form, but they'll bounce back in the exhibition match this afternoon!"

"We all believe in you!"

But the more this happened, the more panicked Luo Xinghe became.

This trust and support is like a beautiful wrapping paper.

Once the video of the afternoon exhibition match is released and the packaging is torn open, the ugly reality inside will completely ruin his reputation.

"This can't be..." Luo Xinghe closed his eyes and dug his fingers deep into his palms.

The fingernail pierced the skin, drawing blood.

The pain brought him to his senses a little more.

He opened his eyes, the last trace of hesitation in his gaze vanished, and then quickly unlocked his phone, opening an app he never opened easily.

The app icon is an upside-down black longsword with chains wrapped around its blade, the end of which connects to an empty skull eye socket.

This is the logo of [Burial Pavilion], a well-known gray platform on the dark web of the cultivation world.

I obtained the download link and invitation code for Luo Xinghe by chance in an encrypted chat group three years ago.

The group admin said it cryptically: "This thing can solve some problems that conventional methods can't solve. But remember, once you use it, there's no going back."

At the time, he scoffed at it.

He's Luo Xinghe, a positive and inspiring e-sports streamer with a bright future. How could he possibly be involved with such shady things?

But now...

Luo Xinghe's finger was already hovering over the icon.

Then, he pressed down hard.

The interface requires triple verification: fingerprint, pupil, and a special mental energy imprint.

After everything was approved, the screen went dark, and then flowing dark gold patterns appeared, outlining the phantom of a magnificent yet eerie palace.

The main doors of the hall slowly opened, revealing a deep darkness inside.

A line of blood-red ancient seal script characters emerged in the darkness:

"The Burial Pavilion, where all worries and sorrows are buried, what should be offered as a sacrifice?"

Luo Xinghe took a deep breath and typed on the virtual keyboard:

"Spirit stones, lots of spirit stones. I want to place an order."

Changes in the blood-red text:

"Very well. Customer service [Judge No. 17] will guide you."

The screen switches to a simple, dark gold dialogue interface, with faint background sounds of wailing and dragging chains, sending chills down your spine.

A cold, synthesized voice, indistinguishable between male and female, rang directly in Luo Xinghe's mind.

This is a unique direct mental communication system of the Burial Pavilion, ensuring absolute privacy:

"Client identity verified: Luo Xinghe, contracted anchor of Xingyao Media, early stage of Golden Core cultivation. Please state the details of the commission."

Luo Xinghe composed himself and conveyed the message through his mind:

"Target: Wang Ling, a 17-year-old first-year high school student at Songhai City Number 60 High School. Registered cultivation aptitude: F-level. It is confirmed that his current realm does not exceed the Foundation Establishment stage, but his reaction speed and combat intuition far exceed those of his peers, and he may have the ability to fight against opponents of higher levels."

"Deadline: Must be completed by 2 PM today. This is an urgent order."

"Location: Inside the Starry Sky Esports Center, preferably near the accommodation area on the seventh floor of Building B or the training area of Building A, during times when there are relatively few people."

Specific requirements:

First, non-lethal strikes. It must be ensured that the target loses the ability to move and compete within the next 24 hours, but must not endanger their life.

Secondly, a high-level magical artifact of the [Soul Capture] type must be used to completely separate and imprison its soul, and then deliver it to me.

Third, the scene must be staged to appear as if the target has suddenly suffered a spiritual energy disorder or gone mad, leaving no external injuries or any evidence pointing to homicide.

Once the message was transmitted, the chat window went silent.

Luo Xinghe could sense that the judge on the other end, codenamed "Seventeen," was analyzing data and assessing risks.

About a minute later, the synthesized voice sounded again, its tone flat and even, but its content made Luo Xinghe's heart clench:

"Based on the information provided by the client and cross-referencing with our limited database, the target Wang Ling's life trajectory in Songhai City is clear, but he is involved in multiple cases of the destruction of sect forces, such as the Shadow Stream destruction case. Therefore, we have upgraded his risk rating to: Level B of the Mysterious category."

"The basic price for the urgent order of the Class B Xuan-level is 28 million spirit stones."

Luo Xinghe gasped.

Twenty-eight million!

After more than three years of live streaming, he painstakingly saved up all his wealth through tips, signing fees, and advertising endorsements. The total value of his wealth, converted into spirit stones, is only about 35 million!

The judge's voice continued, carrying a cruel, businesslike tone:

"This quote already takes into account surcharges for urgent orders and risk premiums. If the target's actual combat strength or background exceeds expectations, resulting in casualties of the executor or mission failure, the client will need to pay an additional compensation fee of up to three times the quoted price. If you accept, you will pay 60% upfront, and the balance will be paid upon confirmation of mission completion. If you refuse, the conversation will end, and the consultation fee of 100,000 spirit stones will be deducted from your linked account."

Luo Xinghe's fingers gripped the tactical tablet tightly, countless images flashing through his mind: Wang Ling's indifferent face, Sun Rong's bright eyes when she looked at Wang Ling, and the public video that might be leaked in the future...

"I...accept!" Luo Xinghe made his final decision, going all in!

"The contract is established."

As the synthesized voice faded, a virtual contract gleaming with a dark gold light popped up in the chat interface.

The terms were densely packed, and the core content was exactly the same as what he had just agreed to.

At the very bottom, he needs to infuse a touch of his spirit as his signature.

Luo Xinghe closed his eyes, suppressing the waves of heart palpitations, and signed the document.

The contract transformed into a stream of light and disappeared into the void.

Almost simultaneously, a notification came from one of the encrypted digital accounts linked to his phone.

Sixteen million eight hundred thousand spirit stones have been automatically transferred.

Seeing his account balance shrink by nearly half in an instant, Luo Xinghe felt a suffocating pain in his heart.
