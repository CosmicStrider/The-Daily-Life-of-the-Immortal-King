Chapter 2261 Wang Zhen's Rant

Inside the Earth's core cavity, the golden-red light gradually receded, converging like a breath into the fully bloomed Earth Core Red Lotus.

The powerful and pure energy waves transformed into a warm current, caressing everyone's body and mind, dispelling the fatigue and chill brought on by the previous fierce battle. Even Chen Chao's throbbing leg felt a sense of comfort.

"Wow... this stuff is even more potent than the Ten-Ingredient Tonic!"

Chen Chao smacked his lips, gazing longingly at the sacred object.

If he hadn't known it was precious and potentially dangerous, he might have been tempted to take a bite.

Guo Hao was busy scanning the image with the image-encyclopedia terminal, muttering to himself, "Unknown energy spectrum! Surpassing the S-grade spiritual plant standard! This discovery is something to brag about for a lifetime... if we can make it back alive."

In his excitement, he did not forget the harsh reality.

As Ryoko Kugimiya focused her array on the Crimson Lotus and the Crystal Heart, a torrent of data streamed down her face. She frowned and said, "The energy level is still rising, but it's stabilizing."

"They are fully connected to the core pulsation of this subterranean region, and forcibly collecting them could have unpredictable consequences."

This means they cannot take the red lotus away.

Zhang Linyan cautiously surveyed the area around the cavity, fearing that some other monster might emerge.

The frenzied and insidious attack of the lava borer had left them with lingering fear.

Sun Rong slowly descended, and Ao Hai silently returned to its sheath.

She could clearly feel the new life force flowing within the sword core.

The golden-red flame patterns seemed to possess a life of their own, intimately connected to her soul.

But in fact, only Wang Ling knew the truth.

It appears that the Earth Core Red Lotus and Sun Rong are experiencing some kind of strange resonance, but in reality, the Earth Core Red Lotus is unable to resist the temptation of the nine Heavenly Dao Cubes within the body of the Ao Hai Sword.

This is like a "trap" for any sacred object, and it is hard to resist.

At this moment, Sun Rong looked down at her palm. The red embroidered pouch had returned to calm, as if the mysterious power that had just turned the tide was just an illusion.

She carefully put it back close to her body, her heart still pounding from the previous fright and the unusual movement of the embroidered pouch.

Subconsciously, she looked in the direction of Wang Ling. Despite the thick layers of rock between them, she seemed to be able to sense his calm gaze.

"Did he...see it?" Sun Rong's heart stirred slightly, and her cheeks flushed.

Wang Zhen walked to the vicinity of the Red Lotus, observed it carefully for a moment, and exclaimed in amazement: "The Earth Core Red Lotus is recorded in the ancient books of the Divine Realm. I never thought it would actually appear in the world. It's a pity that it's rooted here. If you disturb it, it might blow up this half of the Earth's core."

His tone was relaxed, but the content of his words left everyone speechless.

At the same time, Wang Zhen was hiding something, such as that this thing was a treasure for forging Chaos Artifacts...

Many representatives from elite universities present had no concept of the "Chaos Artifact," a top-tier existence that transcends the level of magic artifacts. It would be futile for them to say anything; it would be meaningless.

Fang Xing's skull scythe hummed slightly, seemingly both yearning for and wary of the red lotus's energy.

He said in a deep voice, "We shouldn't stay here any longer. The commotion we just made might attract more attention."

Liu Qingyi nodded in agreement, her gaze sweeping over Sun Rong: "Rongrong, are you alright? The power that Ao Hai and that Xiu... uh, and you unleashed just now was really impressive!"

She quickly stopped talking, not mentioning the embroidery pouch, and just winked at Sun Rong.

Sun Rong understood immediately and shook her head: "I'm fine, but Ao Hai seems to have become stronger because of it."

She kept the secret of the embroidered pouch to herself; it was a unique opportunity for her, and also a mystery left behind by her predecessor, Meng Nian.

"The exploration ends here."

Wang Zhen clapped his hands and made a decision: "Red Lotus can't be touched, and its heart is even more dangerous. Our mission is to survive and find a way back, not to demolish the Earth's core. Let's retreat!"

The team quickly returned the way they came. Compared to the tension on the way there, they returned with a sense of exhilaration at their gains and a deeper sense of bewilderment.

Although the evil of the Outer God has receded, it lingers like a shadow over the hearts of those who know the truth.

...

Inside the cave, those who remained breathed a sigh of relief upon seeing the exploration team return safely.

Li Changzhe hurriedly asked about the situation below, and Zhang Linyan briefly explained the matter of the Red Lotus, as well as the powerful Earth Core Protection Energy and the insect swarm.

Su Xingyue noticed that Sun Rong's aura seemed more refined, and the scabbard of the Ao Hai Sword shimmered with light. She couldn't help but praise, "Sun Rong's cultivation has improved again."

Sun Rong smiled slightly and subconsciously touched her pocket.

Besides the embroidery pouch, there was also the small piece of crispy noodle crumb that Wang Ling had given me.

His instructions still seemed to echo in her ears, filling her heart with a sweet feeling.

Wang Ling remained in his corner, seemingly uninterested in the news brought back by the team.

He was examining a new flavor of crispy bread, his brow furrowed almost imperceptibly, as if he was somewhat dissatisfied with the ingredient list.

Sun Rong walked over and reported softly, "Below is a very strong Earth Core Red Lotus, and a lot of insects, but we have already dealt with them."

She omitted the dangerous part, as if sharing something ordinary.

But in fact, the King had already seen everything that happened in the inner world.

Although Wang Ling himself did not go, they were a team after all. If they encountered any life-threatening danger, he would still take action without hesitation, even if it risked revealing his true strength.

All you need to do is erase the memory of the person who saw him make the move afterward.

Wang Ling looked up at her, his gaze lingering on her for a fraction of a second, confirming once again that she was unharmed and even had a slightly stronger aura, before nodding very slightly.

He casually pushed a glass of freshly squeezed Yuxin juice next to him.

This time, instead of pushing it on the table, it was handed directly to Sun Rong's hand.

Sun Rong's heart skipped a beat, and she took the cup with slightly trembling fingertips.

The cool touch traveled through the cup, yet it felt as if it had scalded her heart.

"Thank you... thank you, Wang Ling." Her voice was so soft it was barely audible, and two blushes rose on her cheeks. She quickly lowered her head and took a sip; it was sweet and refreshing, just like her mood at that moment.

Wang Ling withdrew his hand, his fingertips unconsciously rubbing against each other.

He picked up the instant noodles again, but unusually, he didn't start eating them right away. Instead, he watched Sun Rong's profile as she drank her juice, a glimmer of light flashing in his dead fish eyes that he himself didn't even notice.

This scene was witnessed by Liu Qingyi, who was not far away.

She nudged Wang Zhen beside her with her elbow and whispered, "Hey, look over there, it seems like that ice-cold face has suddenly become a little more intelligent?"

Wang Zhen glanced at it lazily, a playful smile playing on his lips: "The road ahead is long and arduous~ I think you're overthinking it. We can't judge this character's actions by common sense, but at least there's some progress. Some of the readers of Immortal King have already gone from elementary school to high school, and some have already entered the workforce, and this damn novel is still not finished! We have to go to the extra chapters to ship the characters! It's so frustrating!"

He appeared nonchalant, but a hint of satisfaction flickered in his eyes.

Wang Ling's romantic developments are more interesting to him than the battles he fought against several star systems back then.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao were completely oblivious to the subtle atmosphere, still enthusiastically discussing how wonderful it would be to bring back the Earth Core Red Lotus to make tea, when they were glared at by Kugimiya Ryoko with an annoyance.

After a brief respite, the real problems are back on the table.

"Communication is still completely interrupted."

Ryoko Kugimiya looked at the array plate with a worried expression: "The base's signal has completely disappeared. It's like we've been forgotten here."

"Although the ley line activity has stabilized somewhat, the overall spiritual pressure level is much higher than before, and the environment is much worse."
