Chapter 2227 Sunk Costs

The atmosphere of the coffee shop, which was filled with the rich aroma of coffee, suddenly plummeted to freezing point.

Liu Qingyi's sudden attack caught Li Yuanyuan, who was sitting at the table, completely off guard.

On Li Yuanyuan's face, which was already tense and uneasy due to her guilty conscience, a fleeting, extremely subtle ferocity flashed, like a wild beast whose scales had been touched. However, this expression disappeared in an instant, as fast as a shooting star streaking across the night sky. If it weren't for Liu Qingyi, who was keeping a close eye on her, no one else would have been able to catch it.

Everything was proceeding smoothly according to Li Yuanyuan's plan. She used her keen understanding of people's hearts to navigate the game of emotions with ease.

For many years, she has relied on this way of understanding her lover's psychology to continuously obtain cultivation resources for herself and her family.

In her mind, cultivators have long lifespans, and modern cultivators often live for hundreds of years. The time it takes for a love affair to last is indeed just a drop in the ocean in the long lifespan of a cultivator. Who can be sure that they can stay with the other person for a lifetime?

She felt that even if she had taken advantage of those "boyfriends," she had also given them so-called emotional value.

It was a completely fair trade, and I didn't do anything wrong.

At this moment, facing a table full of couple rings engraved with the names of different boys, Li Yuanyuan quickly adjusted her state after a brief moment of contemplation.

She quickly looked at the boy in front of her with pitiful eyes, her eyes slightly reddening, her voice trembling slightly as she said in a coquettish voice, "Baby, this storage bag really isn't mine. I don't know this female Daoist at all, and I don't know why she suddenly attacked me and slandered me like this. I guess she must have been sent by my ex to get revenge on me... If you don't believe me, I really have no way to defend myself."

As she spoke, a glistening tear welled up in the corner of her eye, the tear swirling in her eye socket as if it would roll down at any moment; her acting skills were undeniably superb.

The boy looked at the wedding rings on the table. Those rings engraved with unfamiliar names were like sharp knives, piercing his heart.

He glanced at Li Yuanyuan's extremely aggrieved expression, and a slight wavering appeared in his heart.

His fingers unconsciously rubbed the corner of the table, his eyes wandering, glancing at the ring one moment and at Li Yuanyuan the next, as if trying to convince himself to believe her.

"How pitiful are those love-struck fools." Upstairs, Sun Rong and Jiu Gong Liangzi were watching all this from their high vantage point, remarking as they watched the spectacle unfold.

They all understood that the boy subconsciously knew everything, but he was unwilling to admit that he had been deceived in this relationship. Deep down, he was still making excuses for Li Yuanyuan, trying to maintain this relationship that was already riddled with problems.

"This is probably what they call sunk costs in 'The Love Bible'," Ryoko Kumiya murmured in a heavy accent. She narrowed her eyes slightly, her gaze fixed on the hesitant boy downstairs.

"Sunk costs?" Sun Rong asked, puzzled. She tilted her head, her eyes filled with a thirst for knowledge.

"You don't even know this?" Kugimiya Ryoko held up her small folding fan to cover half of her face, pretending to look at Sun Rong with disdain, but in reality she was a little smug. She finally had a chance to show off her knowledge.

"Simply put, it refers to the time, energy, money, and emotional investment that has been made in a relationship and cannot be recovered. Even if problems arise in the relationship, these costs will still affect whether people continue to maintain the relationship." Ryoko Kumiya said, tapping her palm lightly with her fan, looking confident.

"I see." Sun Rong suddenly realized, then changed the subject: "I didn't expect you, Liangzi, to know so much about this, and you've even read a book like 'The Love Bible'?"

"Ahem! I was just bored, so I read some miscellaneous books."

Ryoko Kugimiya blushed and hurriedly explained, the blush spreading rapidly across her face, even her ears turning bright red, "I didn't watch it for that idiot Zhuo Yi!"

As she spoke, she waved her fan, seemingly trying to fan away the awkwardness.

Sun Rong smiled gently.

This explanation sounds rather weak and feeble, more like a case of trying to cover up a lie.

Downstairs, Liu Qingyi, who had already decided to be the first to speak out, was not accommodating to Li Yuanyuan's explanation at all.

She immediately rolled her eyes, a very vivid one, as if to say, "Your acting is terrible." She elegantly tucked her hair behind her ear, revealing an expression that said, "What haven't I seen?" and stared sharply at Li Yuanyuan: "You're right, I don't know you at all. I just couldn't stand it."

She stared at Li Yuanyuan, as if she had heard something amusing, and her jade lips moved slightly: "Do you think someone of my cultivation level needs to deliberately use such methods to retaliate against you?"

In the modern cultivation world, a cultivator's cultivation level is extremely important. Unless one's family is incredibly wealthy, every step a cultivator takes in their cultivation is enough to cross a different social class.

Liu Qingyi's subtext was that she and Li Yuanyuan were not on the same social class, separated by vast distances, and she had no reason to slander Li Yuanyuan in this way.

Liu Qingyi casually grabbed a handful of wedding rings from the table. The rings gleamed coldly in her hand. A confident smile played on her lips as she said, "All the couple's rings retain a portion of each partner's spirit when they were engraved. Although this spirit will gradually dissipate over time, with my cultivation level, tracing it back is not difficult. Do you need me to find these boys and confront them face-to-face?"

Upon hearing this, Li Yuanyuan's expression changed drastically. Her already fair face turned ashen, and beads of sweat from her panic slid down her cheeks and dripped onto the table.

"No...no..." She seemed to have gone mad, trying to snatch the ring from Liu Qingyi's hand. Her hands were flailing wildly in the air, her eyes filled with fear and despair.

Liu Qingyi feinted and easily dodged Li Yuanyuan's attempt to snatch it, as if she were toying with a trapped beast, her eyes filled with a hint of mockery.

She then looked at the boy next to her who was still hesitating, a look of disappointment on her face, and couldn't help but scold him: "You good-for-nothing, what are you pretending to be confused for? She's playing you!"

Liu Qingyi stared at him and bluntly said, "You! And the boys corresponding to these rings are just a means for her to exchange for cultivation resources."

As she spoke, Liu Qingyi casually scattered these worthless wedding rings on the ground. The rings scattered on the ground made a crisp sound, as if it were the sound of this false relationship breaking apart.

Then she looked at Li Yuanyuan, who was almost burying her head in the ground, and sneered: "Miss Li, I know what you're thinking. It's true that a romance takes up very little time in a cultivator's life, but that doesn't mean you can play with someone's feelings at will. What do you have? Looks? There are countless female cultivators in this world who are more beautiful than you. You're just taking advantage of these boys' affection for you."

Liu Qingyi's resounding question struck a chord with everyone present. Even the coffee shop staff, who were making coffee, momentarily forgot their work. While pouring coffee, they accidentally spilled it from their cups, splashing scalding hot coffee onto the counter. The staff, however, were completely oblivious, simply watching the spectacle unfold.

Amid Liu Qingyi's constant questioning, Sun Rong also noticed that the red countdown timer for love above the boy's head was dropping rapidly.

3......

2......

1......

Sun Rong mentally counted the countdown she saw, her eyes filled with pity as she looked at the boy who was about to wake up from his beautiful dream.

As the clock struck zero, the boy seemed to have suddenly made up his mind. He stood up, his eyes red, his hands clenched into fists, the veins on the back of his hands bulging. Looking at Li Yuanyuan, his voice was a little hoarse, yet full of determination, and his face was mixed with a hint of hatred: "I think, let's end it here!"
