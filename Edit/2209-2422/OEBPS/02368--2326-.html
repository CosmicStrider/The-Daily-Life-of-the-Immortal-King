Chapter 2326 Showdown

"Smack."

The crisp snap of the fingers sounded particularly abrupt in the deathly silence of the hall, where the shrieks of vengeful spirits had not yet completely subsided.

The giant hand formed by Yuan Chang's shadow was still frozen in mid-air, pointing directly at Wang Ling.

The fog on his muddled face froze, and his two ghostly green eyes flickered violently.

Black 112 and Lin Susu maintained a defensive or offensive stance, but their eyes became completely blank after Wang Ling's sudden revelation.

Zhou Ping even forgot to cry, his mouth agape, staring dumbfounded at the incredible scene before him.

On the ground, the gray-clad companion who had just been drained dry by the chains of resentment was sitting there unharmed.

The look of terror on his face hadn't faded yet, and he touched his face and body blankly with his hands, as if trying to confirm whether he really existed.

Not only that.

In the shadows at the entrance of the hall, the unlucky fellow who had been eliminated for hoarding the "Secret Manual of Resurrection" was now rubbing his head, swaying as he stood up, looking completely bewildered.

Dead...

But it survived again.

In the blink of an eye, with just a snap of the fingers.

This not only violates the basic principles of the cycle of life and death, but also directly subverts the rules of death in this place.

"......"

An even more eerie silence enveloped the entire hall than before.

In that brief moment, the pained faces in the pool of resentment seemed to have forgotten to wail, and the contortions of their expressions became much smaller.

"No...impossible..." The headmaster's voice trembled for the first time, a sound of shock at having his understanding utterly shattered.

"The laws of life and death... You... You've tampered with the laws of life and death here?! What exactly are you?!"

Wang Ling patted off the dust that had gotten on him from the "fall," maintaining his expressionless face.

He just felt that continuing to pretend would be a bit troublesome.

And with all his teammates dead, who will do his work? Who will provide him with the backdrop to continue his pretense?

He raised his eyes and calmly looked at the shadow that was pulsating violently with fear.

"......"

Wang Ling did not answer the director's question.

Because it's unnecessary.

It's pointless to explain who you are in front of a program that's about to be formatted.

He simply took one step forward.

Zhou Ping subconsciously tried to grab the hem of his clothes, but found that he had grasped nothing.

Black 112 and Lin Susu's pupils suddenly contracted.

They saw that as Wang Ling took that step, the unremarkable disguise he wore was quietly melting away.

An indescribable aura began to emanate from Wang Ling.

That wasn't spiritual pressure, killing intent, or any kind of imposing aura that any cultivator could comprehend.

That is the embodiment of the rules themselves...

It is the breath of the Heavenly Way.

Hei 112, Lin Susu, and the others felt an irresistible tremor emanating from the depths of their souls, as if ants were gazing up at the sky.

Their breathing became incredibly difficult, their knees buckled uncontrollably, and a primal awe gripped them, almost causing them to prostrate themselves on the ground.

For the first time, the distorted figure on the throne of the Garden Chief leaned back slightly.

That was a fear that stemmed from instinct.

He is formed from resentment, and his perception of danger and status far surpasses that of ordinary beings.

At this moment, he felt a sense of insignificance and despair from this "gray-clad 999," as if facing the vastness of the universe.

"Heaven... Dao...?" The headmaster's words became incoherent.

Wang Ling glanced at him again.

This time, his gaze fell upon the twisted throne pieced together from countless fragments of alchemy furnaces.

I didn't see him make any move.

"Whoosh..."

The throne, which embodied the resentment of countless losers, crumbled instantly, starting from the very top! Utterly annihilated!

The fragments that made up the throne turned into the finest dust in an instant, leaving not a trace, as if they had never existed in this world.

The chieftain let out a piercing scream. The collapse of the throne had severely damaged his body, and his shadowy form had become much thinner.

"No! My throne! My source of power!" He frantically urged the pool of resentment, attempting to mobilize the entire "Ten Thousand Resentments Devouring Spirit Array" to launch a counterattack.

However, the dark green runes on the ground that had been flickering were erased on the spot after Wang Ling's gaze swept over them, disappearing in large swaths.

The array that sustains his existence is being silently dismantled.

"Thump!"

Black 201 finally couldn't withstand this invisible pressure. His legs gave way, and he knelt on the ground, tears streaming down his face, kowtowing to Wang Ling: "My lord! Spare me! My lord! I was blind! I was forced to do this! It was Dark Star! Dark Star forced me to do this!"

Wang Ling ignored him.

His attention fell on the still churning pool of resentment.

The distorted faces inside sensed an indescribable pure power, and their wails gradually subsided.

Wang Ling stretched out his finger and gently flicked it upwards towards the pool of resentment.

The entire pool of resentment seemed to have been paused, instantly ceasing its boiling.

Immediately afterwards, the thick black resentment in the pool began to flow backward, converging towards the center.

Countless pained faces began to merge and be continuously purified, and those negative emotions that had accumulated for countless years were finally forcibly stripped away.

Within a few breaths, the enormous pool of resentment vanished.

Instead, a bead the size of a dragon's eye, radiating white light, floated on Wang Ling's fingertip.

This is the Soul Purifying Pearl.

It is the essence of life left behind by countless pure souls that have been tormented and polluted, after being tempered by the supreme law.

Having lost the support of the pool of resentment, the headmaster's shadowy body let out a final, desperate cry and quickly became transparent.

"No...this shouldn't be...I am the master...I am about to be resurrected..." His voice grew weaker and weaker, filled with resentment.

His meticulously cultivated alchemical garden in the deep mountains over countless years was ultimately utterly destroyed by a seemingly insignificant "alchemical ingredient," leaving him no room for resistance.

Wang Ling looked at the Soul-Purifying Pearl in his hand, and then at the soon-to-dissipate Yuan Chang.

He thought for a moment, then flicked his finger at the core of the shadow that was about to completely dissipate from the garden.

A wisp of consciousness, carrying all of the academy director's memories and information about the "Hidden Shadows' Home," was forcibly extracted and copied. It then transformed into a stream of light, piercing through layers of spatial barriers and directly reaching Diu Lei Zhenjun and Wang Ming, who were far away at the Battle Sect headquarters.

After doing all this, the last trace of the headmaster vanished without a trace.

The ultimate boss of the Deep Mountain Dan Garden has died.

...

A long silence fell over the hall once again.

Hei 112 and Lin Susu looked at Wang Ling with extremely complicated expressions.

Awe, fear, gratitude, disbelief... a mix of emotions.

They finally understood why this "Gray-Clad 999" always managed to coincidentally resolve crises.

That wasn't luck; that was a devastating blow.

Zhou Ping finally snapped out of his daze. He looked at Wang Ling's back, his face flushed with excitement. He wanted to say something, but he didn't dare to go forward. He could only pinch his thigh hard to make sure he wasn't dreaming.

Wang Ling turned around and looked at the survivors.

He thought for a moment, then walked up to Hei 112 and Lin Susu and handed them the Pure Soul Pearl.

"Using it properly will allow you to survive later."

His voice remained calm, yet carried an undeniable power.

Both Hei 112 and Lin Susu were taken aback.
