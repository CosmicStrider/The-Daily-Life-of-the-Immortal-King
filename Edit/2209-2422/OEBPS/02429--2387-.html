Chapter 2387 Paradise Overture

The silence in the carriage lasted for about three seconds.

All eyes were on Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

Chen Chao had a gossipy face that he was trying not to laugh, Guo Hao had a typical amused look on his face, Li Changzhe's eyes were wide open, and Zhou Rui had a "I don't know anything" look on his face as he teased Wang Muyu.

Sun Rong regretted asking that question as soon as she did.

Her cheeks were so hot they could fry an egg on them, and her fingers unconsciously twisted the hem of her skirt.

Even Sun Rong herself didn't know where she got the courage from. Maybe it was because of the article "Analysis of the Ten Opportunities for Taking the Initiative" that Jiugong Liangzi posted in the group last night?

Wang Ling's movements did indeed pause.

He held half a piece of instant noodles to his lips and turned his head to look at Sun Rong.

The sealing talisman on his chest felt slightly hot, as if a small flame was flickering inside.

This feeling was unfamiliar, but he didn't dislike it.

"...Hmm," Wang Ling said.

Just one word.

But Sun Rong's eyes lit up instantly.

"Really?" Her voice held a hint of excitement that she herself didn't realize.

Wang Ling nodded, put the last piece of crispy noodles into his mouth, and crunched it down.

Then he took out a new packet from his pocket and handed one to Sun Rong.

"..." Sun Rong took the bag of instant noodles, paused for two seconds, and then smiled.

The atmosphere in the carriage immediately relaxed.

"Oh my gosh!" Chen Chao drawled, "Lingzi, you're something else! You value your girlfriend over your friends! How many times have you ever treated me to instant noodles? You gave them to me the moment Sun Rong asked!"

Guo Hao calmly added, "According to statistics, when Wang Ling takes the initiative to share snacks, seven out of ten times the recipient is Sun Rong."

"Eat yours." Wang Ling took out several more packets of different flavors and threw them toward Chen Chao.

Chen Chao hurriedly caught it: "Alright! Long live your son!"

A small "crisis" was thus resolved by instant noodles.

Ryoko Kunomiya sat in the front row, half-covering her face with a folding fan, but the smile on her lips was impossible to hide.

She quickly sent a message in the Strategist Alliance encrypted group:

[Kumiya Ryoko: Phase 1 successful! Target has accepted Sun Rong's team invitation.]

[Wang Zhen: Received. I have arrived at the park ahead of schedule. All internal surveillance has been adjusted, and "unexpected" opportunities for solitude will be created in key areas.]

[Zhuo Yi: Be mindful of your limits, don't go too far. Master's perception is very sharp.]

[Liu Qingyi: Enough with the nonsense. If Master Ling really doesn't want to, what good will your little tricks do?]

[Kumiya Yoshiko: So we have to use overt methods. For example, the "Flying Swing" that requires two-person cooperation, and the "Light and Shadow Maze" that requires a certain level of teamwork... These are all legitimate attractions; they chose to play them themselves. What does it have to do with us? (smiles)]

...

The bus exited the highway and turned onto a scenic avenue lined with colorful, glowing plants on both sides.

In the distance, the outline of Disneyland gradually becomes clear.

It was a surreal architectural complex.

An upside-down castle suspended in the air, a Ferris wheel flowing with colorful waterfalls of light, a roller coaster track winding like a giant dragon through the clouds, and an area shrouded in pale purple mist where a spinning carousel and a giant teacup can be vaguely seen.

"Wow..." Wang Muyu pressed his face against the car window, his eyes wide open.

Chen Chao clicked his tongue in amazement: "Three thousand spirit stones per hour? How much money does this amusement park burn every day?"

Ryoko Kugimiya turned around and smiled, "So it's the closed beta period. A lot of the technology is still being tested, so we'd love to have everyone help us experience it and give us feedback."

As they were talking, the bus drove into the main gate of the park.

On the huge archway, the five characters "Disneyland" shimmered with dreamlike light.

Two rows of staff members dressed in cute mascot costumes stood on either side of the door. Upon seeing the bus, they bowed in unison and said, "Welcome, all experience officers!"

The car door opened, and fresh air mixed with a faint floral fragrance rushed in.

"Please go to the reception center to pick up your wristbands first." A middle-aged man in a suit, who looked like a manager, came up with a smile: "The wristbands are your passes to the park and also record your check-in challenge. If you lose one, you'll need to replace it, and your challenge progress will be reset."

Everyone got out of the car and followed the manager toward a crystal-clear building.

The reception center is clean and bright, with a dozen staff members busy behind the circular counter.

A huge holographic screen on the wall was playing promotional videos for the park and the rules for the check-in challenge.

As soon as Wang Ling entered, his gaze was fixed on the life-size holographic model of the "Spiritual Food Palace Supreme Collection Gift Box" on the display shelf behind the counter.

Serves 100, unique flavor, space compression technology.

His fingers twitched unconsciously.

"Want it?" Sun Rong asked softly.

Wang Ling nodded, very honestly: "Mm."

"Then... shall we work together to collect stamps?" Sun Rong suggested tentatively.

"Okay." Wang Ling paused.

Sun Rong felt a ticklish, sweet sensation in her heart, as if a tiny feather had brushed against her skin.

The process of receiving the wristband was quick. It was made of a semi-transparent silicone material that automatically adhered to the wrist, and the screen displayed personal information and the current stamp count: 0/20.

"The wristband has built-in navigation and reservation functions." The staff patiently explained: "Green means that the activity is currently available, yellow means that you need to wait in line or make a reservation, and red means that the equipment is under maintenance. In addition, some activities have height, age or health requirements, and the system will automatically filter them."

Chen Chao eagerly put on the bracelet and poked at the screen: "Let me see... 'Extreme Roller Coaster' is green! 'Abyss Drop Tower' is green! 'Mist Haunted House' is also green! Lingzi, which one should we go for first?"

Before Wang Ling could speak, Ryoko Kugimiya interjected with a smile: "I suggest starting with gentler rides, like the 'Rainbow Carousel' or the 'Dream Teacups,' to get used to the atmosphere of the amusement park."

Chen Chao pouted: "That's so boring..."

Guo Hao analyzed, "From an ergonomic perspective, suddenly engaging in high-intensity, stimulating activities may lead to dizziness or discomfort. A gradual approach is the reasonable choice."

"Alright, alright," Chen Chao conceded. "Shall we go sit at the teacup first?"

"Let's split into groups," Ryoko Kumiya suggested. "The park is so big, and everyone has different interests, so we can form groups freely. How about we meet here at 4 p.m. to share our experiences?"

This is part of the Advisors Alliance's plan—to create a "natural" opportunity for Wang Ling and Sun Rong to be alone.

"I'm fine with it." Zhou Rui took Wang Muyu's hand. "Muyu wants to go see the ‘Elf Garden' first."

He and Wang Muyu have become very close these days, and Wang Muyu also likes Zhou Rui, his older brother.

"I made a date with Kobayashi Yu to have a shooting game match." Liu Kaichuan shook his bracelet.

Chen Chao looked around, then suddenly grabbed Guo Hao and Li Changzhe by the neck: "Then let's form a group of three!"

The group walked away noisily.

In the blink of an eye, only Wang Ling, Sun Rong, and the smiling Ryoko Kugimiya remained in the reception center.

"Wang Ling, Rongrong, you two have fun." Kugimiya Ryoko gently tapped Sun Rong's shoulder with her folding fan and said meaningfully, "Some projects... require good cooperation."

After saying that, she turned and left, her back view as elegant as if she were just taking a casual stroll.

The air suddenly became quiet.

Sun Rong secretly glanced at Wang Ling.

The boy was looking down at the map on his wristband, his profile clearly visible in the soft park lights.

His eyelashes were long, casting faint shadows on his eyelids when they drooped down.

"Wang Ling..." Sun Rong asked softly, "What do you want to play first?"

Wang Ling raised his head, swiped his finger twice on the virtual screen, and then pointed to an icon on the map marked "Double Flight".

It was a project that required two people to share a cabin and spin and tumble in the air.

Next to the icon is a line of small print: [Recommended for a well-coordinated experience; you need to work together to maintain balance throughout the game].

Sun Rong blushed again.

"This... seems a little exciting," she said softly.

"Afraid?" Wang Ling asked.

"I'm not afraid!" Sun Rong immediately shook her head, then added, "It's just... I've never played this kind of game before."

"I don't have any either," Wang Ling said.

That's the truth. The Celestial King's life rarely includes "amusement parks."

"Then..." Sun Rong took a deep breath, raised her head, her eyes sparkling: "Shall we try it together?"

"Um."
