Chapter 2423 The Tipping Point

Monday afternoon at four o'clock, a high-end apartment in Songhai City.

Luo Xinghe stood in front of the floor-to-ceiling window, his brows furrowed, seemingly deep in thought.

"Mr. Luo."

Mo Xuan's voice came from behind him.

Luo Xinghe did not turn back.

Mo Xuan walked to his side and followed his gaze to the window.

The streets downstairs were bustling with traffic, and everything seemed so normal.

"You didn't go out today?"

"I don't want to pay." Luo Xinghe's voice was indifferent.

Mo Xuan glanced at him but didn't say anything.

Luo Xinghe has been unusually quiet these past few days.

I don't go out, I don't see anyone, and I rarely even look at my phone.

Every day, I would sit by the window daydreaming or watch Wang Ling's match recordings in the study.

Over and over again.

"Mo Xuan," Luo Xinghe suddenly spoke.

"exist."

"Tell me, how much hatred must a person have to feel to be unable to sleep?"

Mo Xuan remained silent for a few seconds.

"That depends on what he lost."

Luo Xinghe smiled.

He smiled calmly, but there was no hint of amusement in his eyes.

"What I lost was my dignity."

He turned around and looked at Mo Xuan.

"You know what? Although I come from a humble background, I'm very ambitious. I've never lost since I was a child. I reached Foundation Establishment at sixteen, Golden Core at eighteen, and made it into the Glory League Professional League Finals at twenty. I gained unprecedented wealth and cultivation resources compared to my peers. Everyone says I'm an inspirational role model and a rising star."

He paused for a moment.

"Until I met Wang Ling."

Mo Xuan remained silent.

Luo Xinghe walked back to the living room and plopped down on the sofa.

"In that simulated battle, he didn't treat me as an opponent at all. He didn't even glance at me once from beginning to end."

He grabbed a wine glass from the coffee table and downed the chilled red wine in one gulp.

"Later, I investigated him and hired people to deal with him, spending millions of spirit stones. In the end, I didn't even touch a hair on Wang Ling's head."

He put down his wine glass, a hint of self-mockery in his voice.

Mo Xuan stood in front of him, his expression calm.

"Mr. Luo, I advised you to stop."

"I know." Luo Xinghe looked up at him. "So I listened to you. I haven't done anything these past few days."

He stood up, walked up to Mo Xuan, and stared into his eyes.

"But Mo Xuan, do you know what I want to do most right now?"

Mo Xuan remained silent.

Luo Xinghe said, word by word, "I want to see someone."

"who?"

"Your young master of the Primordial Assets, Bai Zijin."

Mo Xuan's pupils contracted slightly.

He looked at Luo Xinghe with a complicated expression, and deliberately asked, "How do you know this name?"

Luo Xinghe smiled.

"You think I've been doing nothing but acting crazy these past few days?"

He walked back to the coffee table, took a document from the drawer, and tossed it to Mo Xuan.

Mo Xuan took it and opened it.

The document contained his own information.

When did you first contact Luo Xinghe? How many spirit stones did you provide? Through which channels...?

There are even several photos of him with other members of Honghuang Asset Management Company.

Mo Xuan closed the file, looked up, and a slight smile appeared on his lips.

In fact, this information was deliberately guided by Mo Xuan to Luo Xinghe in secret, and it was also part of the plan.

How much information could be revealed to Luo Xinghe was all within Honghuang's calculations. Of course... the name Bai Zijing was only revealed to Luo Xinghe after approval from higher-ups.

"You're investigating me?" Mo Xuan's acting was excellent; he feigned surprise.

"Yes." Luo Xinghe nodded. "I investigated you."

He sat back on the sofa and crossed his legs.

"Mo Xuan, you gave me so many spirit stones, helped me contact Jiang Liu, and accompanied me through all this trouble. What's in it for you?"

Mo Xuan remained silent.

Luo Xinghe continued, "Don't talk to me about investment potential. I may be crazy, but I'm not stupid. You definitely have someone backing you up."

The living room was quiet for a few seconds.

Mo Xuan suddenly smiled.

He put the documents back on the coffee table and sat down opposite Luo Xinghe.

"Mr. Luo, what would you like to know?"

"I want to know what you want." Luo Xinghe stared at him: "And, can you help me with Bai Zijing?"

Mo Xuan looked at him, a hint of appreciation flashing in his eyes.

"Mr. Luo, you are smarter than I thought."

"Don't flatter me." Luo Xinghe waved his hand. "Just tell me directly."

Mo Xuan was silent for a few seconds before speaking.

"What we want is related to Wang Ling."

Luo Xinghe raised an eyebrow.

Mo Xuan continued, "But it's not in the way you think. We don't intend to kill him, nor do we intend to hurt him. What we want is..."

He paused, seemingly considering his words.

"It's your hatred for Wang Ling."

Luo Xinghe was stunned.

Mo Xuan looked at his expression and a slight smile appeared on his lips.

"It's strange, isn't it? What good is it for one person to hate another?"

Luo Xinghe remained silent.

Mo Xuan stood up and walked to the window.

"Mr. Luo, do you know what the most powerful force in this world is?"

Luo Xinghe frowned.

Mo Xuan continued, "It's not spiritual power, it's not talent, it's obsession."

He turned around and looked at Luo Xinghe.

"When a person's obsession with something reaches a certain level, they can break through all limitations. This is something that countless sages have proven with their lives."

Luo Xinghe remained silent.

Mo Xuan walked back to him and looked down at him.

"And now, your hatred for Wang Ling is nearing its breaking point."

Luo Xinghe looked up at him.

"So what you want is my hatred?"

"Yes." Mo Xuan nodded. "But not ordinary hatred. What we want is hatred that can ignite your passion. Hatred that can make you disregard everything."

He squatted down to look Luo Xinghe in the eye.

"Mr. Luo, you want to see Boss Bai, I can arrange that. But I have a question for you first."

Luo Xinghe looked at him.

Mo Xuan said, word by word, "What price are you willing to pay to deal with Wang Ling?"

The living room was silent for a full five seconds.

Then Luo Xinghe smiled.

He laughed softly, but there was something chilling about his laughter.

"Mo Xuan, what do you think I can't give up now?"

Mo Xuan stared into his eyes for a long time.

Then he stood up and took out his phone.

"I'll contact them right away."

...

At 7 p.m., in an abandoned industrial park on the western outskirts of Songhai City.

Luo Xinghe sat in a black minivan, looking out the window.

Mo Xuan sat in the driver's seat, also waiting.

"When will he arrive?" Luo Xinghe asked.

"Almost there." Mo Xuan glanced at the time: "About ten minutes."

Luo Xinghe nodded and continued to stare out the window.

The setting sun cast long shadows of the abandoned factory buildings, and the withered grass swayed in the evening breeze.

This place is a perfect spot for murder and robbery.

He suddenly smiled.

"Mo Xuan, is Boss Bai really coming? Or is it just a stand-in?"

Mo Xuan glanced at him: "Mr. Luo, have you been watching too many TV dramas? You can rest assured that if you are useful to him, Boss Bai will naturally show his true self to demonstrate his sincerity."

"Useful?" Luo Xinghe raised an eyebrow: "Just because I hate Wang Ling?"

"Yes," Mo Xuan said, "because of this."

Luo Xinghe remained silent.

He recalled what Mo Xuan had just said.

It sounds pretty mysterious.

But he didn't mind.

I'll do anything as long as it helps me deal with Wang Ling.

At 7:10, a silver-white sedan came into view.

The car slowly approached and stopped ten meters away.

The car door opened, and a young man stepped out.

He had short black hair, pale skin, and a cold, stern expression. He wore a well-tailored black suit with dark gold dragon patterns embroidered on the cuffs.

This person is Bai Zhe's son, Bai Zijing.

Luo Xinghe pushed open the car door and got out.

The two looked at each other from a distance of ten meters.

Bai Zijing spoke first.

"Luo Xinghe?"

"It's me."
