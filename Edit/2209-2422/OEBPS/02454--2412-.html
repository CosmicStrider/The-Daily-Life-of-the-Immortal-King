Chapter 2412 Clash of Pure Martial Arts Cultivators

After leaving the audience, Luo Xinghe did not go far.

He went around to the media area on the second floor of the stadium, where there were private boxes facing VIPs, but they were empty at the moment.

Mo Xuan was already waiting inside. There was a psionic monitoring device on the table, with several images displayed on the screen.

The system includes real-time video feeds of each battle arena, the players' waiting areas, the referee's bench, and even the data streams from the system's backend.

"Have you retrieved Zhao Wuji's information?" Luo Xinghe closed the door of the private room, shutting out the noise from the outside world.

Mo Xuan tapped his fingers a few times on the virtual keyboard, and a detailed file popped up in the center of the screen.

"Zhao Wuji, eighteen years old, a senior high school student at Songhai City Sports Cultivation High School. He is majoring in the Vajra Subduing Demon Body, in the late stage of Foundation Establishment, with the spiritual root attribute being earth and a purity of 65%."

"Body cultivation?" Luo Xinghe frowned.

"Moreover, he is a pure martial arts practitioner and does not practice magic." Mo Xuan added, "His fighting style is very simple, which is close combat, relying on his strong physique and martial arts skills to suppress his opponents. Last year, he was the runner-up in the city's youth martial arts competition. In the final, he withstood thirteen lightning spells from his opponent before falling."

Luo Xinghe stared at the photo of Zhao Wuji on the screen.

He was a boy with a buzz cut, dark skin, and well-defined muscles. He wore the black sleeveless training uniform that was the signature of the Physical Training High School, revealing his strong arms with a few faint scars on them.

"Wang Ling won the first two matches by relying on his footwork and skills," Luo Xinghe analyzed. "When facing Li Haoran, he used footwork to dodge and finally used a clever force to knock the sword away. When facing Liu Zixuan, he seemed to be able to see through the flaws in the spell and used some kind of technique to interfere with the spiritual energy structure."

Mo Xuan nodded: "According to the monitoring data, Wang Ling's spiritual energy fluctuations remained stable at the early stage of Foundation Establishment throughout the entire process, without any abnormal outbursts. The judges' initial conclusion is that it was a skillful suppression, believing that his control over the rhythm and details of the battle has reached the pinnacle among those of the same level."

"The ultimate?" Luo Xinghe sneered, "Can someone in the early stages of Foundation Establishment have such insight and skill? Do you believe that?"

"I don't believe it." Mo Xuan pushed up his glasses. "But the system detected no abnormalities, and the judging panel gave a reasonable explanation. Unless we can get conclusive evidence, questioning is useless."

Luo Xinghe was silent for a few seconds, then suddenly asked, "What is Zhao Wuji's weakness?"

"Speed." Mo Xuan pulled up Zhao Wuji's match video: "His defense and strength are top-notch among his peers, but his movement speed is relatively slow and his explosive power is average. If his opponent is good at hit-and-run tactics, he will have a hard time fighting."

"Then the royal decree is just what he needs to counter." Luo Xinghe's expression darkened further.

"Theoretically, yes." Mo Xuan paused for a moment, "But Zhao Wuji has a special characteristic: he is extremely focused in battle. Once he enters a state, he ignores all interference from his opponent and executes his tactics like a machine. Moreover, he is extremely resistant to blows; even if he is hit multiple times, it will not affect his combat power."

Luo Xinghe stared at the screen, his eyes darting around.

When is the next match?

"3:40, E-03." Mo Xuan glanced at the time: "Twenty-five minutes left."

"Alright." Luo Xinghe sat down on the sofa. "I'll watch from here. If Wang Ling can even beat Zhao Wuji, and do so reasonably... then there's no need to test him anymore."

What do you mean?

"I'm going to get serious right now." Luo Xinghe's voice was icy: "No matter how well he's hiding, I'm going to dig him out."

Mo Xuan didn't say anything, but silently adjusted the parameters of the monitoring device.

Silence fell over the private room, broken only by the soft hum of the instruments.

...

Meanwhile, in Section B, third row of the audience seats.

Chen Chao plopped back into his seat and let out a long sigh.

"That scared me to death... When Liu Zixuan unleashed that powerful move just now, I thought someone was going to die."

Guo Hao remained relatively calm: "Wang Ling timed it perfectly, just when Liu Zixuan's spiritual power was at its peak, but the spell was not yet fully formed. Forcibly interrupting it at this moment minimizes the damage from the spell's backlash and avoids causing serious internal injuries."

"So he's helping Liu Zixuan?" Li Changzhe was stunned.

"That's one way to put it." Guo Hao nodded. "If we wait until the Fiery Storm is fully formed before interrupting it, the backlash will be several times stronger. Liu Zixuan will have to stay in bed for at least half a month."

Chen Chao was stunned: "Ling Zilian even foresaw this?"

"Probably." Guo Hao looked towards the waiting area, his eyes filled with complex emotions. "Wang Ling's understanding of combat is probably much deeper than we imagined."

Sun Rong listened from the side and breathed a sigh of relief.

She knew Wang Ling was sensible and wouldn't really hurt his classmates.

But what just happened was indeed dangerous. If Liu Zixuan's Blazing Storm had fully erupted, the entire protective shield of the battle arena might not have been able to withstand it.

"The next match is against Zhao Wuji."

Ryoko Kugimiya suddenly spoke up: "I've heard of that kid. He's a tough guy from the Physical Training High School. Last year, he broke three of his opponent's ribs in the martial arts competition."

Chen Chao's heart, which had just settled, tightened again: "So brutal?"

"Don't worry, there are rules in the competition, we can't go all out," Zhuo Yi said with a smile. "Besides, Zhao Wuji might not be able to touch Wang Ling's movements."

Sun Rong, however, was somewhat worried.

But what I'm worried about isn't Wang Ling, but Zhao Wuji.

She was a little worried that Wang Ling might accidentally kill Zhao Wuji.

"I'll go get him a bottle of water." She stood up.

Chen Chao immediately raised his hand: "I'll go too! Lingzi must be hungry after playing two games, and I have some snacks here!"

Ryoko Kugimiya and Zhuo Yi exchanged a glance, smiled, and didn't stop them.

...

In a corner of the waiting area, Wang Ling stood against the wall, holding a newly opened bag of seafood-flavored instant noodles.

The two matches just now were actually quite boring.

Li Haoran's swordsmanship had too many flaws, and Liu Zixuan's spell structure was not stable enough. He didn't even need to use his full strength; he could win with just a few pointers.

But this is for the best.

Discreet, safe, and won't attract too much attention.

I'm a little hungry.

He broke off a piece of bread and stuffed it into his mouth, crunching it loudly.

"Royal Decree!"

Sun Rong's voice came from the side.

Wang Ling turned his head and saw Sun Rong jogging over, holding an energy drink in her hand.

Chen Chao followed behind, carrying a plastic bag filled with various snacks.

"Here you go." Sun Rong handed over the drink, her cheeks slightly flushed: "To replenish your energy."

Wang Ling took it, unscrewed it, and took a sip.

It's sweet with a subtle fruity aroma; the taste is quite good.

"Lingzi, you're awesome! Two consecutive wins!!" Chen Chao put the plastic bag on the ground and excitedly patted him on the shoulder.

Wang Ling hummed in agreement, his gaze falling on the plastic bag.

Besides potato chips, chocolate, and beef jerky, there were even several packs of imported instant noodles that he had never tried before.

"Are you confident in your next match against Zhao Wuji?" Sun Rong asked softly.

Wang Ling thought for a moment and nodded.

Guo Hao and Li Changzhe also came over at this time.

"We just watched the video of Zhao Wuji's match."

Guo Hao said, "His fighting style is very direct. He just charges in and withstands the opponent's attacks, then finishes the fight with a heavy punch or a grappling move. His defense is ridiculously high. When a technique of the same level hits him, the effect is halved."

Li Changzhe added, "And he doesn't seem to be afraid of pain; being hit doesn't affect his movements."

After hearing this, Wang Ling didn't react much, but simply broke off another piece of instant noodles and put it in his mouth.

"Lingzi, do you have a plan?" Chen Chao couldn't help but ask.

Wang Ling swallowed the instant noodles and said, "Watch how you hit me."

It's that same phrase again.

Chen Chao was both amused and exasperated.

But then I thought about it again. Wang Ling had also been "playing it safe" in the first two matches, and he still won both of them, and won them very well.

Perhaps he really does have his own method?

"Alright then." Chen Chao patted him on the shoulder: "Just be careful. If you can't beat them, just admit defeat. It's not embarrassing."

Wang Ling nodded.

Actually, he had just been thinking about what method would be more appropriate to win.

Using movement techniques to dodge? That's too obvious; the judges might have doubts.

Can it be countered with skill? Zhao Wuji's martial arts are very simple; there's not much to counter.

Then...

Wang Ling looked at the bag of crispy bread in his hand and suddenly had an idea.

These recordings would eventually be destroyed for various mysterious reasons, and Wang Ming would also use scientific means to interfere with Wang Ling's memory of the day of the competition, thus removing him from the incident.

This is also the biggest reason why Wang Ling has been able to play with such ease in these few matches.
