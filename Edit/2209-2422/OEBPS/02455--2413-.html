Chapter 2413 Brotherhood and Trust

Zhao Wuji's fist grazed past Wang Ling's clothes, and the force of the wind even made the stray hairs on Wang Ling's forehead fly backward.

A collective gasp rippled through the audience.

"Almost!"

"You managed to dodge that?"

"Wang Ling's footwork is too strange!"

On the stage, Zhao Wuji's face was solemn.

He had been attacking continuously for more than two minutes, using all available methods of attack—fists, elbows, knees, and feet—but he hadn't even managed to touch the hem of Wang Ling's clothes.

This isn't right, this is very wrong.

Such movement techniques are impossible in the early stages of Foundation Building.

Zhao Wuji stopped, slightly out of breath, staring intently at Wang Ling, who was three meters away.

Wang Ling was also looking at him, his expression still calm, and his breathing was not even disordered.

"Who exactly are you?" Zhao Wuji asked in a deep voice.

After a moment of silence, Wang Ling said, "An ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivator."

Zhao Wuji gritted his teeth: "Nonsense! How can someone at the Foundation Establishment stage have such movement techniques? You must have practiced close combat skills for at least ten years!"

"Yes." Wang Ling nodded: "I've practiced."

This answer left Zhao Wuji speechless.

He had thought Wang Ling would deny it or make excuses, but to his surprise, the other party admitted it so readily.

"So you've been hiding your strength all this time?" Zhao Wuji continued to press.

Wang Ling shook his head: "No."

Before he could finish speaking, Zhao Wuji suddenly moved.

This time, instead of charging directly at Wang Ling, he stomped his right foot on the ground!

Boom!

A shallow pit was created on the specially designed ground of the battle arena, and gravel flew everywhere.

Using the recoil, Zhao Wuji shot into the air like a cannonball, twisting his body in mid-air and whipping his right leg like a steel whip toward Wang Ling's head!

Whip kick!

Moreover, it's a whip kick with downward momentum!

The speed and power of this strike have exceeded the scope of the late Foundation Establishment stage and are infinitely close to the early Golden Core stage!

Several judges in the VIP section stood up at the same time.

"Foul!" the bespectacled female judge shouted. "That could kill someone!"

The white-haired old man's expression changed as well: "Where's the referee? Stop it now!"

But it was too late.

Zhao Wuji's leg had already reached ten centimeters to the side of Wang Ling's head!

In the audience, Chen Chao's exclamation caught in his throat.

Sun Rong subconsciously began to pray for Zhao Wuji's legs.

If that blow had landed, it would have been a comminuted fracture.

However, the next second, Wang Ling moved.

He didn't dodge, but instead raised his left hand, fingers spread, to meet the whip kick.

"He's gone mad?!" someone in the VIP section exclaimed.

If you take that kick head-on, your arm will be crippled even if it's broken!

The next second.

Snap!

It wasn't the crisp sound of bones breaking, but the muffled sound of a hand colliding with a calf.

Wang Ling's left hand steadily caught Zhao Wuji's whip kick.

To be precise, it wasn't a direct hit, but rather, at the moment of contact, he flipped his wrist, his five fingers hooking around Zhao Wuji's calf, while simultaneously rotating his body to the side and rear using the force of the whip kick.

Use someone's strength against them!

Zhao Wuji felt a strange force travel from his legs through his entire body, and he was spun halfway through the air by Wang Ling.

Then, bang!

He was slammed hard to the ground!

Zhao Wuji groaned, a sharp pain shooting through his right shoulder, but he gritted his teeth and tried to get up by supporting himself with his left hand.

But Wang Ling's actions were even faster.

The moment Zhao Wuji landed, Wang Ling had already loosened his leg, stepped forward with his right foot, and delivered a sweeping kick!

The kick wasn't fast or powerful, but the timing and angle were extremely precise.

It happened precisely at the moment when Zhao Wuji braced himself with his left hand and lost his balance.

Bang!

Just as Zhao Wuji had raised half of his body, the kick struck the ankle of his supporting leg, causing him to lose his balance again and fall backward.

After sweeping, Wang Ling did not retract his left foot, but instead raised it upwards, with his heel hovering three centimeters above Zhao Wuji's throat.

It stopped.

The entire room fell silent.

The referee paused for two seconds before reacting and blowing his whistle: "Match over! Wang Lingsheng!"

Silent.

A few seconds later, the audience erupted in deafening applause and cheers.

"We won!!"

"Wang Ling is awesome!"

"That leg-catching takedown was so cool!"

"The timing for the final sweeping leg kick was perfect!"

In the third row of section B, Chen Chao plopped back into his seat, covered in cold sweat.

"Holy crap...that scared me to death..." he muttered, "I thought Lingzi was going to die from that kick just now..."

Guo Hao was still shaken, but his eyes were filled with more doubt: "Did you guys see clearly how Wang Ling caught that kick?"

Li Changzhe shook his head: "It was too fast, I didn't see it clearly. But it definitely wasn't a direct hit, it must have been a clever technique."

"Yes." Guo Hao nodded. "He absorbed most of the force at the moment of contact, and then used Zhao Wuji's own momentum to throw him away. This technique... requires a level of precision in controlling the force and angle."

Chen Chao didn't quite understand, but he roughly grasped the meaning: "So Lingzi still won through skill?"

"It must be technique." Guo Hao pushed up his glasses. "If it were a suppression of his cultivation level, he wouldn't have needed to go through all this trouble. He could have just used his spiritual power to blast Zhao Wuji away. But his spiritual power fluctuations remained stable at the early Foundation Establishment stage throughout, which means he is indeed only at the early Foundation Establishment stage. It's just that... his technique is ridiculously strong."

Chen Chao breathed a sigh of relief: "That's good."

Although I still have some doubts, can the skills in the early stages of Foundation Establishment really be this powerful?

But he chose not to think about it too deeply.

Who cares, as long as we win.

On stage, medical staff rushed up to check on Zhao Wuji's condition.

The minor fracture in my right shoulder and the sprained ankle are both superficial injuries that will heal in a few days.

Zhao Wuji was helped up and looked at Wang Ling with a complicated expression.

"That fall just now..." his voice was hoarse: "You held back?"

If Wang Ling had made him land on his back or the back of his head, he might be seriously injured by now.

Wang Ling nodded.

"Why?" Zhao Wuji asked.

"There's no need," Wang Ling said.

Zhao Wuji was silent for a few seconds, then smiled bitterly: "I accept my defeat wholeheartedly. Your martial arts skills... are far superior to mine."

He reached out his hand.

Wang Ling glanced at him, then extended his hand and shook hands with him.

"Let's fight again next time," Zhao Wuji said.

Wang Ling thought for a moment, then took out a packet of instant noodles from his pocket and handed it over.

Zhao Wuji was taken aback for a moment, then took it: "...Thank you."

...

In the VIP section, several judges were engaged in a heated discussion.

"That catch and throw were definitely at the level of a master!" The white-haired old man was so excited that his beard was trembling. "I dare say that even we old guys might not be able to do that with such precision without using our spiritual power!"

The bespectacled female judge nodded: "His grasp of timing, control of force, and choice of angle have all reached the level of 'skill approaching the Tao.' This is no longer just a simple skill, but... the art of combat."

The middle-aged judge frowned: "The problem is, how could a high school student at the early stage of Foundation Establishment have the time to practice such martial arts? Even if he started practicing from the womb, it's impossible."

"Talent," the white-haired old man said decisively. "Some people are born to fight."

"You mean..." The bespectacled female judge's eyes lit up.
