Chapter 2397 Chen Chao, the Light Bulb

Deep inside the maze, the third checkpoint.

This is a room with a pool of water, the water of which is deep and reflects a pale blue light.

In the middle of the pool is a small island, and on the island is a treasure chest.

Three small wooden boats were moored by the pond, each only able to seat one person.

"We're going to row across?" Chen Chao looked at the three dilapidated boats. "Won't these boats fall apart halfway there?"

Sun Rong observed carefully: "There is something moving in the pool."

Wang Ling looked at the water.

Several mechanical fish lurking underwater are likely an obstacle to the park's design.

If someone rows a boat across, the mechanical fish will create waves, causing the boat to rock or even capsize.

But now, all those mechanical fish are stuck.

Each fish was tightly bound by the seaweed and could not move.

Wang Ling knew without even thinking who did it.

The "assist" arranged by Ryoko Kumiya has arrived.

"I'll give it a try." Chen Chao jumped onto a boat and picked up the oar.

The boat was very stable and didn't rock at all.

He made two strokes and successfully reached the island, where he opened the treasure chest.

There are three tokens inside.

"Do we need to bring the token back?" Chen Chao picked up the token.

A line of text appears at the bottom of the treasure chest:

Each of the three men must hold a token and simultaneously insert it into the stone tablet by the pool to open the next passage.

Chen Chao brought the tokens back and distributed them to Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

The three walked to the stone tablet by the pool.

The stone tablet has three grooves, the shape of which matches the token perfectly.

"I'll count to three, then we'll insert them all at once," Chen Chao said.

"Okay." Sun Rong nodded.

Wang Ling also picked up the token.

"Three, two, one!"

Insert all three tokens at the same time!

The stone tablet lit up, and the side wall suddenly opened, revealing a new passage.

Bracelet vibration:

[Activate check-in points 3/7]

[Current Teamwork Rating: A]

"You've improved again!" Chen Chao exclaimed happily. "At this rate, I'll definitely get an S when we go out!"

Sun Rong was also very happy and looked at Wang Ling: "Wang Ling, what do you think?"

Wang Ling: "Hmm."

He actually felt the rating system was a bit slow.

By his standards, he should be given an S grade right now.

It doesn't matter, as long as I get the extra stamp in the end.

The three of them walked into the new passage.

This area is quite special; it's a long corridor with mirrors hanging on both sides of the walls.

It wasn't an ordinary mirror; it was one of those funhouse mirrors, which distorted and warped the image of the person reflected in it.

"A funhouse mirror corridor?" Chen Chao laughed. "What's so difficult about that?"

He walked up to a mirror, in which his elongated, bamboo-like body was reflected.

"Hahaha, Reiko, look at me!"

Wang Ling glanced at it.

His reflection in the mirror was normal, without any distortion.

Because the distorting effect of the funhouse mirror was automatically corrected the moment he approached.

It's not that the mirror is broken, it's that the mirror "dares not" distort him.

Sun Rong's mirror also reflected a normal image, but she didn't notice and thought all mirrors were like that.

"These mirrors... don't seem to have anything special about them?" she wondered.

"It's probably just an entertainment segment," Chen Chao said. "Let's get it approved quickly."

The three continued walking forward.

When they reached the middle of the corridor, something unexpected happened!

All the mirrors suddenly flipped over at the same time!

Behind the mirror was a holographic projection screen, which began playing rapidly flashing, chaotic images!

Colors, lines, and distorted geometric shapes switch at an extremely high frequency, accompanied by piercing sound effects!

"Ah!" Sun Rong subconsciously closed her eyes.

Chen Chao also felt a wave of dizziness: "What the hell?!"

This is a psychological distraction element in the maze design, using visual and auditory confusion to disrupt the sense of direction and cause people to lose their judgment.

But it had no effect on Wang Ling.

Those chaotic images appeared as clear as if in slow motion to him, without any jarring effect.

He could even discern patterns in image switching.

However, Sun Rong and Chen Chao obviously couldn't stand it.

Sun Rong closed her eyes, her body swayed, and she looked like she was about to fall.

Wang Ling reached out and put his hand on her shoulder.

Sun Rong felt that hand, and the panic in her heart instantly subsided a lot.

"Thank you..." she whispered, but still didn't dare to open her eyes.

Chen Chao squatted on the ground, clutching his head: "Holy crap... this is even more thrilling than a haunted house..."

Wang Ling glanced at the projection screens on both sides.

Then, he gently stepped on the ground.

Very light.

However, all projection screens went black at the same time.

The sound effects stopped too.

The corridor returned to silence, with only the light from the ordinary streetlights remaining.

"Is it over?" Chen Chao cautiously opened his eyes.

Sun Rong opened her eyes and saw that Wang Ling was still supporting her. Her face flushed, and she quickly stood up straight: "Thank you..."

Wang Ling released his hand: "It's alright."

Bracelet vibration:

[Activate check-in point 4/7]

[Passed the psychological interference test]

[Current Teamwork Rating: A+]

"A+!" Chen Chao exclaimed, recovering his composure. "Almost there, almost there!"

Sun Rong looked at the rating on the bracelet and felt a little excited.

It seems that her chemistry with Wang Ling is really improving.

Although Wang Ling speaks very little, he always makes a move at crucial moments.

This feels good.

Monitoring room outside the maze.

Ryoko Kugimiya looked at the image on the screen with a smile on her lips.

"Our teamwork rating is already A+. At this rate, we'll definitely reach S by the time we leave."

Wang Zhen's voice came through the communicator: "Of those four assassins, two are now crippled."

"How so?"

"The guy in the mist maze stabbed himself and is now limping away trying to retreat. The guy at the control panel of the extreme roller coaster is in even worse shape; he got an electric shock while adjusting the equipment, and now his hair is standing on end, and he's in the infirmary."

Ryoko Kugimiya couldn't help but laugh out loud: "Is Master Rin's magic array that powerful?"

"It's incredibly powerful. And I misjudged it; this isn't just a simple magic array, but some kind of domain." Wang Zhen sighed, his voice filled with awe. He was well-informed and once again marveled at Wang Ling's extraordinary abilities: "It's a suppression at the level of rules. From what I can see, the effect of this domain should be: anyone who harbors ill will towards Ling Zhenren and those around him will suffer misfortune. The stronger the ill will, the more outrageous the misfortune will be."

"What about the other two?"

"The one on the drop tower hasn't started yet, but the one on the Heart-to-Heart Bridge is setting up a trap. I think he'll be in trouble soon too."

Ryoko Kugimiya nodded: "Let them have fun. Our priority is to ensure the safety of other tourists, and... to give Rongrong and Renjin some time alone."

She switched screens to look at the next area of the maze.

There was a "specially designed" room there.

It was specially prepared by the Advisors Alliance for Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

"Fifth checkpoint, almost there." A sly glint flashed in Ryoko Kumiya's eyes.

Inside the maze, Wang Ling and his two companions arrived at a circular room.

There were no other exits from the room, only a stone table in the center.

There was a chessboard on the table.

The chessboard is a common "spiritual chess" in the cultivation world. The chess pieces are divided into black and white, and each piece is engraved with different runes.

There are three stone benches next to the table.

"Play chess?" Chen Chao was dumbfounded. "I don't know how!"

Sun Rong walked to the chessboard and looked at it: "This is... an endgame."

The chessboard was already filled with pieces, black and white, alternating, resulting in a stalemate.

The bracelet vibrates, displaying a notification:

Solve the remaining puzzle on the board to unlock the exit. Ten minutes time limit.

"Ten minutes?!" Chen Chao panicked. "I don't even know the rules!"

Sun Rong frowned and observed: "The rules of the Spirit Chess are... each piece's rune represents a different attribute, which are mutually reinforcing and mutually restraining. We need to find the move to break the deadlock."

Although she understands the rules, it is almost impossible to solve such a complex puzzle within ten minutes.

Chen Chao looked at Wang Ling: "Lingzi, do you know how to play chess?"

Wang Ling looked at the chessboard.

Three seconds later, he reached out and picked up a white piece.

Place the piece.

The position is ordinary; it's an empty space on the chessboard that seems insignificant.

But just as the chess piece was placed—

All the pieces on the chessboard light up at the same time!

Black and white light intertwined, then projected a star map into the sky!

The star chart slowly rotated, eventually pointing to the wall on one side of the room.

The wall suddenly opened.

"It's untied?" Chen Chao was dumbfounded.

Sun Rong was also stunned: "Wang Ling...you can play Spirit Chess?"

Wang Ling: "I've seen it."

He did see it.

In his dream, his consciousness traveled to the distant depths of the universe and witnessed two immortal emperors playing chess.

That game lasted three years and ended in a draw.

In comparison, the current mess seems like child's play.

Bracelet vibration:

[Activate check-in points 5/7]

[Solving the remaining puzzle in four seconds]

[Current Teamwork Rating: S-]

"S-!" Chen Chao exclaimed excitedly, "Just a hair's breadth away!"

Sun Rong looked at Wang Ling with a mixture of curiosity and admiration in her eyes.

How many more surprises is this man hiding?

The three of them walked into the newly opened passage.

The passage was short; after walking about ten meters, we arrived at the next room.

But this room... is very special.

The room is heart-shaped.

The walls are a soft pink, and the floor is covered with a soft carpet.

There was a double sofa in the center of the room, and snacks and drinks were placed on a small coffee table in front of the sofa.

The most unusual thing was that the room had only one door—the one they came in through.

There are no other exits.

"What...what is this?" Chen Chao was dumbfounded.

Bracelet vibration:

[Sixth Checkpoint: Rest Area]

Please rest here for five minutes to adjust your state. The exit will open automatically after five minutes.

[Note: During the break, you can enjoy tea and snacks and exchange ideas.]

Chen Chao looked at the "double sofa" sign, then at the "five-minute rest" sign on his wristband, and suddenly understood.

This is for couples!

He's superfluous here!

"Um..." Chen Chao immediately said, "I suddenly remembered that I ate too much for breakfast and need to use the restroom! You guys rest first, I'll go out and find where the restroom is!"

He started to walk back as he spoke.

But the door had already closed automatically when we arrived.

"..." Chen Chao's lips twitched.

Sun Rong realized what was happening and her cheeks instantly turned bright red.

The atmosphere in the room became subtle.

Double sofa.

Five minutes alone.

This is clearly... a deliberate design.

Wang Ling remained calm.

He walked to the coffee table and looked at the snacks.

It's a fruit pudding and a flower tea; the presentation is quite good.

He picked up a piece of pudding and took a bite.
