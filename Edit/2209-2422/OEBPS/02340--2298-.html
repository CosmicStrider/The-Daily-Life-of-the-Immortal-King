Chapter 2298 The Inner Demons in the Examination Hall

As night deepens, the nights in Matsumi City are destined to be anything but peaceful.

As the influence of "Taste Master" continues to ferment online, coupled with its extremely tempting marketing reports, purchasing "Taste Master" to aid in cultivation has become the most popular thing at present.

Many people online follow suit, especially online influencers and review bloggers. In order to grab the first bit of traffic, they buy equipment through their own channels and share their experiences.

Regardless of whether the reviews are good or bad, the marketing momentum immediately picked up...

Inside Wang Ling's room, Wang Ming's holographic projection had not completely dissipated, and the psionic energy fluctuations that the data stream seemed to linger in the air.

Erha lay on the carpet, half-closing his eyes, digesting the astonishing information he had just heard about the [Spirit Network] and the [Emotional Bomb].

Wang Ling stood by the window, his gaze calmly traversing the space, landing on the blurry outline of the sixty-something in the distance.

His fingertips tapped lightly on the window frame unconsciously, the rhythm steady, as if he were contemplating countless future possibilities.

Li Jun, Taste Club, Final Exam, Core Nodes of the Spiritual Network, Peak of Negative Emotions...

These keywords strung together in his mind, and he began to analyze them.

Bai Zhe's move was indeed more insidious and more difficult to deal with than the direct attack of the Dragonborn army.

It attacks not the physical body, but the human heart, the very foundation of the future of the cultivation world.

In other words, the Daoist aspirations of the younger generation.

Forcibly destroying all the equipment could trigger a backlash from students who are already deeply dependent on it, causing irreversible damage.

Clearly, Bai Zhe had anticipated this, and he was using those people's lives to coerce the king's decree.

However, Wang Ling was not in a hurry; he needed time and an opportunity.

...

The day of the final exams at the Number 60 High School arrived in a tense atmosphere.

The atmosphere on campus seemed a bit more somber than usual.

On the playground and between the teaching buildings, students could be seen everywhere making the most of their last moments, clutching stacks of review materials and muttering to themselves.

An invisible pressure permeated the air.

Most of the students in the elite class looked calm and composed, either discussing the difficult points in twos and threes or resting alone with their eyes closed. The confidence brought by their inner strength made them appear much more at ease.

In the regular classroom area, the atmosphere is much more complex.

The most striking feature of the "Taste Club" members, led by Li Jun, is their uniform silver wristwatches.

They gathered together and stopped looking at the traditional jade slips for review. Instead, they shared various "taste templates" from "Taste Master" that could help with exam preparation.

"I feel particularly clear-headed when I use this [Clear Mind and Wise Mind - Ice Heart Cake Template], and I understand the 'Basic Array Diagram' I read yesterday very easily!"

"Why not try my [Zhou Shuren Smoked Pork Template]? It's pretty amazing, the taste is quite unique, but after using this template, you can memorize almost anything you see!!"

Li Jun listened to the members' discussion, a faint smile playing on his lips.

He gently rubbed the device on his wrist; the cool touch made him feel at ease.

After several days of "special training" and the support of his fellow members, he felt that he had been reborn. Although his spiritual power was still somewhat weak, he had a good grasp of the feeling of controlling his power.

He looked toward the building housing the elite classes, a fleeting, almost imperceptible gloom passing through his eyes.

"Just wait and see, this big test will show everyone the power of the Taste Club."

...

The first exam was a written test on "A Comprehensive Overview of the Principles of Advanced Alchemy".

The huge lecture hall was shrouded in a powerful soundproofing array and anti-cheating barrier, with only the scratching sound of pens tracing through specially made talismans and the steady footsteps of the proctors remaining.

Wang Ling sat in his usual spot by the window, answering questions at a moderate pace, precisely controlling his score to ensure that he barely passed each question.

Most of his attention, like an invisible radar, enveloped the entire examination room.

The focus, of course, is on the members of the "Taste Club," especially Li Jun.

What Wang Ling really wanted to complain about was that, since the "Taste Master" was a new device, it wasn't yet included in the list of prohibited tools, so these members of the Taste Club brazenly brought it into the exam room!

At this moment, Li Jun was sitting in the middle to back of the examination room, and he wrote with great speed.

He was chewing an ordinary piece of gum.

The taste templates provided by the 【Taste Master】 give him a significant and continuous "thinking acceleration" and "memory enhancement" effect, enabling him to quickly find solutions to many difficult problems.

However, as the exam progressed, he got stuck on a comprehensive question involving an obscure medicinal herb.

This question is beyond the syllabus; it requires a deeper understanding and mastery of the medicinal properties, and the ability to list the final equation based on the chemical reaction formula.

It cannot be solved by rote memorization or short-term stimulation.

Fine beads of sweat began to appear on Li Jun's forehead.

He subconsciously increased the "assistance" intensity of the device on his wrist and frantically chewed the gum in his mouth, trying to find more potentially useful "taste templates".

Soon, an even stronger cooling sensation rushed into my throat and spread into my mind.

But the imagined solution did not materialize; instead, a sense of anxiety, difficult to describe, arose.

The device's "thinking acceleration" has started to go awry; it's no longer about clear ideas, but rather fragmented flashes of all sorts of chaotic information.

Zhang Hao's mocking face, his parents' disappointed eyes, the possible whispers of other classmates... all these negative emotions that he had suppressed were forcibly pulled out by his amplified perception.

"Calm down!" Li Jun roared inwardly, trying to dispel these distracting thoughts.

The more he resisted, the stronger the anxiety became.

A dark red light suddenly flashed on the device on my wrist.

It faithfully executes the program, attempting to "help" the user overcome obstacles, but its underlying logic is based on amplifying the user's intrinsic motivation.

At this moment, Li Jun's deepest driving force is the obsession with "never failing" and "must prove himself," as well as the fear of failure.

As a result, the device began to excessively drain Li Jun's own energy, and mixed with the similar anxieties of other users fed back by the "Spirit Network," it all rushed into his mental world.

"Buzz!"

Li Jun felt like his brain was about to explode.

The handwriting on the exam paper in front of me began to distort and blur.

He suddenly raised his head, his eyes bloodshot, and his breathing became heavy.

The student sitting next to him was startled and subconsciously moved a little distance away.

This subtle action, when seen by Li Jun at that moment, immediately triggered his highly sensitive nerves, making him believe that his classmate was blatantly mocking him.

"What are you looking at!" Li Jun said in a low voice, his tone filled with hostility.

The student was taken aback, then frowned, ignored him, and continued answering the questions.

The proctor noticed the commotion and swept his stern gaze over.

Li Jun felt that gaze, and abruptly lowered his head, his heart pounding wildly. Shame, anger, fear... a mix of emotions instantly overwhelmed him.

He gripped the pen tightly, his nails almost digging into his palm.

No... I can't fail here!

He grasped at a straw and frantically activated the [Taste Master] again, switching to an advanced taste template that could [unleash potential].

This is a powerful template that he redeemed using a lot of community points in the "Shared Taste Community".

......

At the other end of the classroom, Wang Ling's pen paused slightly.
