Chapter 2406 The Beginning of Sowing Discord

The courtyard gate closed behind him with a dull thud.

Chen Chao led Luo Xinghe through the courtyard paved with bluestone slabs.

At this moment, Chen's father was still practicing his Guan Dao in the training ground. When he saw his son bring in a stranger, he simply nodded slightly and continued with his own business.

He noticed the seriousness on Chen Chao's face and knew that this was no ordinary visitor.

In the living room, Chen Chao didn't let Luo Xinghe sit in the main seat, but pointed to the mahogany chair on the side.

"Speak," Chen Chao said curtly, sitting down opposite him. "Then leave."

Luo Xinghe didn't seem to mind, and gracefully sat down, straightening the cuffs of his suit jacket.

He looked around. The living room was decorated in a traditional style, with several landscape paintings hanging on the walls and some porcelain on the display shelves. There was a faint sandalwood scent in the air.

"Chen Chao's home is very tasteful," Luo Xinghe said with a smile.

"Forget the pleasantries." Chen Chao frowned. "Let's get straight to the point. What did you mean by 'infant killer'?"

Luo Xinghe suppressed his smile, leaned forward slightly, and lowered his voice: "The literal meaning. Yesterday, during the fireworks display at the Ditunni Amusement Park, I arranged for five Nascent Soul stage cultivators to test Wang Ling's strength."

Chen Chao's heart sank, but he didn't show it on his face: "Testing? I think they're planning to do something underhanded."

"Interpret it however you like." Luo Xinghe shrugged. "The important thing is the result. Those five Nascent Soul stage cultivators lost their fighting ability before they could even get close to Wang Ling. And throughout the whole process, Wang Ling didn't even lift a hand."

Chen Chao stared into Luo Xinghe's eyes, trying to determine if he was lying.

But Luo Xinghe's eyes remained calm: "I'm stating the facts, believe it or not."

"Where's the evidence?" Chen Chao asked.

Luo Xinghe took out an ultra-high-definition camera from the inner pocket of his suit, gently placed it on the coffee table, and gently pressed the play button.

The scene depicts a night sky ablaze with fireworks, a square suddenly plunged into darkness by a power outage, and a chaotic crowd. The camera then zooms in, focusing on five figures dressed in black. Each of them has a glowing spirit lock on their wrist, and they are desperately trying to escape, stumbling and bumping into things one after another, even getting tangled in a balloon...

The footage was very short, only a dozen seconds long, but it was enough to clearly show the terrible state of those people.

"I took this picture outside the square," Luo Xinghe said. "Although the details are not clear, you should be able to recognize the spirit locks in those people's hands. They are specially made high-level binding magic artifacts that cannot be broken by anyone below the Nascent Soul stage."

Chen Chao watched silently as the video cut off.

His heart was indeed stirred.

He was right next to Wang Ling when the square lost power yesterday. There was chaos all around, and he did sense something was wrong, but before he could think about it, the lights came back on, and everything was normal again.

Looking back now, Wang Ling was too calm at the time.

Normal people would be a little nervous if they encountered a sudden power outage, right? But what about Wang Ling? He ate instant noodles as usual and watched fireworks as usual, as if everything around him had nothing to do with him.

"Even if what you're saying is true... what does that prove?" Chen Chao took a deep breath: "Maybe the assassin you hired was too incompetent, or maybe there are other experts protecting Wang Ling in the dark. Don't forget, the security level for this event arranged by Jiugong is definitely not low."

Luo Xinghe laughed, his smile laced with sarcasm: "Chen Chao, you're too naive. These are just superficial cover-ups."

He paused for a moment, then continued, "And then there's Zhuo Yi. Have you noticed Zhuo Yi's attitude towards Wang Ling? That's not the attitude of a senior or leader towards a student; it's more like... a subordinate towards a superior."

Chen Chao opened his mouth, wanting to refute, but was speechless for a moment.

He had indeed noticed that Zhuo Yi's attitude towards Wang Ling was somewhat special. For example, at the e-sports camp, Zhuo Yi's gaze towards Wang Ling would occasionally flash with an almost respectful expression. But at the time, Chen Chao thought he was just seeing things, or that Zhuo Yi was simply the kind of teacher who would give special attention to good students.

Now, with Luo Xinghe pointing it out, those details suddenly became clear.

"Let me ask you again," Luo Xinghe pressed, "You've known Wang Ling for so long, have you ever seen him go all out? Have you ever seen him panic? Have you ever seen him do something he couldn't do?"

Chen Chao was stunned.

Thinking about it carefully, it seems... there really isn't.

Wang Ling always maintained that calm expression, no matter what the situation.

Although he was not particularly outstanding in exams and always remained in the middle range, he seemed to be present at every major and minor event that happened at Number 60 High School, and always managed to complete his tasks safely.

Chen Chao used to think that Wang Ling was just a mascot, but what if there are deeper reasons behind all of this?

"What exactly are you trying to say?" Chen Chao's voice deepened.

"I want to say that your good brother Wang Ling is not the ordinary high school student you know at all." Luo Xinghe said, word by word, "He has hidden his strength, hidden his identity, hidden everything. And you, one of his best friends, have been kept in the dark by him for so long. Don't you think that's pathetic?"

Chen Chao clenched his fists, his knuckles turning white.

"Don't even think about trying to sow discord," he said through gritted teeth.

"I'm not trying to sow discord, I'm just telling you the truth." Luo Xinghe stood up, walked to the window, and turned his back to Chen Chao: "Chen Chao, I know you value loyalty and treat Wang Ling like a brother. But the most important thing between brothers is honesty. If he can't even give you the most basic trust, is this friendship really worth your efforts to maintain?"

The living room fell silent.

Only the sound of Chen's father practicing his sword, the sound of the blade cutting through the air, could be heard from the courtyard.

After a long silence, Chen Chao spoke up: "Even if what you said is true, so what? Wang Ling must have his reasons for concealing his strength. He's not telling me because it's not the right time yet. I believe him."

He spoke with great conviction, but a slight wavering existed in his heart.

Really... you don't care at all?

Luo Xinghe turned around, looked into Chen Chao's eyes, and suddenly smiled: "Very good, you really are loyal. Then let's make a deal."

"What deal?"

"I'll give you a chance to verify what I'm saying." Luo Xinghe walked back to the coffee table and sat down again. "If the verification proves I'm talking nonsense, then I'll disappear from now on and never bother you again. If it proves I'm telling the truth..."

He paused for a moment: "Then at least you know the truth. As for what you do next, that's your own choice."

Chen Chao looked at him warily: "How do you verify it?"

"It's simple." Luo Xinghe took out a jade slip from his pocket. "Three days from now, there will be a new generation of cultivators' exchange competition at the Songhai City Gymnasium, open to all high school students in the city. I have already registered you and Wang Ling."

"What makes you think you can sign us up?" Chen Chao frowned.

"The registration channel was open; I just used a little trick." Luo Xinghe waved his hand. "That's not the point. The point is that the rules of this competition are very special; it uses a real-skill matching system."

Chen Chao had never heard of this rule.

"Simply put, the competition system scans the participants' true cultivation level and then matches them with opponents of similar strength," Luo Xinghe explained. "If Wang Ling is truly only at the Foundation Establishment stage, then all his opponents will also be at the Foundation Establishment stage. But if..."

He didn't finish speaking, but the meaning was clear.

If Wang Ling's true cultivation level is not just at the Foundation Establishment stage, then the system will match him with an opponent of a higher realm.

The match is public, and the entire audience will see the match results.

Chen Chao understood Luo Xinghe's intention.

He wanted to force Wang Ling to reveal his true strength in front of everyone.

"What if I refuse?" Chen Chao said coldly.

"Of course you can refuse," Luo Xinghe smiled. "But the registration is already in effect. If Wang Ling doesn't participate, he will be penalized and it will affect the school's rating. Besides... don't you really want to know the truth?"

He stood up and straightened his suit: "The details of the competition, including the time and location, are in the jade slip. Whether you go or not is up to you."

After saying that, he walked towards the door.

As he reached the door, Luo Xinghe stopped and turned to look at Chen Chao: "By the way, as a token of my appreciation for your cooperation, I will reward you regardless of the outcome. Five hundred thousand spirit stones, or equivalent resources. That's enough for your expenses during your university years."

Chen Chao didn't say anything.

Luo Xinghe smiled, pushed open the door, and left.

The courtyard gate closed again.

Chen Chao was left alone in the living room, along with the jade slip that was gleaming faintly on the coffee table.

Chen Chao sat in the living room for a full hour.

My mind is a mess, like a tangled ball of threads.

Luo Xinghe's words echoed in my ears again and again.

"Is he really hiding his true strength?"

"If that's really the case, why did you hide it from me?"
