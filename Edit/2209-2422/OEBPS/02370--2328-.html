Chapter 2328 Sausage Girl

The sky above St. Bell High School was a somber gray, and the air on campus was filled with an indescribable atmosphere.

Wang Ling took a closer look and realized that the air was filled with the smell of chalk dust flying everywhere and the saliva of teachers when they were lecturing, just like he remembered from his childhood when he was in school.

It is no exaggeration to say that this fear originates from the depths of the soul for every student.

Besides, what Wang Ling found strange was that there was a faint aroma of fried rice noodle rolls from his childhood outside the school gate in the air.

However, the scent is so faint that it's impossible to detect if your sense of smell isn't very sensitive.

At this moment, Wang Ling, who was now wearing the body of "Zhou Ziwei", stood in front of the rusty school gate.

His dark-colored teaching assistant uniform was poorly made and constantly rubbed against the skin, making it quite uncomfortable.

He was accompanied by seven other people.

Aside from the three members of the A-13 family in black robes, the atmosphere was slightly different among the four members of the B-07 family.

The leader was also a blood-patterned individual, a lean and short-haired young man, whose number seemed to be Blood 107.

According to Zhou Ziwei's fragmented memories, this person's real name is Zhang Yu. He is a guy known for his ruthless methods and high efficiency in completing missions. He has had several experiences with Zhou Ziwei entering the Gate of the Abyss together to carry out missions, so he is an old acquaintance of Zhou Ziwei.

At this moment, Zhang Yu glanced at Wang Ling with sharp eyes, carrying an undisguised competitive intent. The corners of his mouth curled up slightly, as if he were plotting something.

Wang Ling looked at him expressionlessly, his heart completely unmoved.

Because in his mind, he didn't need to bother with such people at all; his purpose in coming to the Abyss Gate was essentially to gather more information.

"Ding-a-ling!!"

Another sharp and urgent ringing came from the old-fashioned broadcasting room in the teaching building, and then radiated to every corner of the campus, with the ringing of bells echoing throughout almost the entire school.

Wang Ling sighed inwardly at the miserable school life of the students at Shengling High School. If this were the bell for the start and end of classes, the intervals between the bells were far too short.

From the first ring until now, less than five minutes have passed...

While it is important for students to attend classes, it is also important to maintain rest time. Wang Ling has always held a contemptuous attitude towards the behavior of many teachers who do not hesitate to encroach on the break time in order to complete their teaching tasks.

The ringing lasted for a full half minute before abruptly stopping, leaving everyone who heard it with a splitting headache.

"Is everyone here?"

A dry, emotionless voice suddenly rang out.

The crowd looked in the direction of the sound and saw a middle-aged man wearing a smart but outdated black suit, black-rimmed glasses, and a large belly standing inside the school gate.

His hair was meticulously combed, his face was sallow, his eyes were cloudy, and there were dark circles around his eyes that looked terrifying, as if he hadn't rested in a long time. Wang Ling's immediate impression was that this person might suddenly die on the spot at any moment.

Wang Ling noticed that he was wearing a nameplate on his chest: [Dean of Studies - Wu Yongren].

"I am Wu Yongren, the Dean of Students at St. Bell High School."

His voice was flat and emotionless: "Welcome to St. Bell High School, all intern teaching assistants. Over the next four days, I hope you will strictly abide by the school's rules and regulations, assist in managing students, and successfully complete your internship tasks."

He paused, his murky eyes slowly sweeping over everyone present, exuding an eerie feeling.

Even though the people standing here are elderly people who have survived several missions, they still can't help but feel a little scared.

Before they entered, they all knew they would have to survive for four days at St. Bell High School.

The shorter survival time means they can complete their mission and get out sooner, but it also indicates that the mission behind this door is not simple and is far more dangerous than before.

"Saint Bell High School is a prestigious school with an excellent tradition. Maintaining campus order is of paramount importance. Now, I will announce the seven rules for teaching assistants that you must keep in mind during your work."

As soon as the words were spoken, everyone's hearts were in their throats.

The rules of the Abyss Gate often mean the key to survival.

Director Wu took a yellowed piece of paper from the inside pocket of his suit jacket, cleared his throat, and slowly read aloud:

"Rule Number 1: The bell is the signal. When the bell rings for the start of class, you must be in the classroom or office within three minutes; when the bell rings for the end of class, you are free to move around, but you cannot go to the playground, as the synthetic track on the playground has just been completed and needs a period of settling in. Anyone who loiters in the teaching building without a reason will be considered a violation of the rules, whether they are a student or a teaching assistant."

"Article Two: You must respect your teachers. When you encounter any faculty or staff member wearing work clothes and name tags, you must greet them politely. You must not contradict or question any decision made by faculty or staff members."

"Article 3: During your teaching assistant period, you must remain quiet. Inside the teaching building, except for classroom discussions and teachers' questions, loud noises, chasing, and playing are prohibited. During the lunch break, which is from 12:30 pm to 1 pm, the entire school must remain quiet and no talking is allowed."

"Article 4: As teaching assistants, you must pay attention to your appearance. While on campus, you must wear the designated uniform at all times and keep it neat. You must not wear outlandish clothes or have your hair down."

Article 5: Diligence and Responsibility. Teaching assistants must arrive on time, diligently grade assignments, and answer students' questions. Before the end of each school day, they must submit a work log to the grade leader of their assigned grade. Failure to submit will be treated as absence.

"Article Six: You must always maintain a friendly attitude towards the students of this school. You must be patient and fair to every student. You must not use corporal punishment, insults, or have any form of conflict with students. In particular, you should not provoke a female student named [Lin Wanwan]. If you see her alone, please try to avoid her."

Lin Wanwan?

Wang Ling memorized the name, his intuition telling him that it might be a key clue to the mission behind the door.

"Article Seven: The principal dislikes incompetent people. If you cannot prove your value as teaching assistants and gain the principal's approval within four days, then... you will remain at St. Bell High School forever and become a part of the school."

"Article 8... is also the most important one."

Director Wu paused deliberately here. A strange glint seemed to flash in his eyes: "As teaching assistants, you must set an example and avoid junk food. Absolutely, absolutely, you are not allowed to eat or bring... grilled sausages anywhere on campus. If caught, you will be immediately expelled and held accountable."

Grilled...grilled sausage?

Apart from Wang Ling, who was mentally prepared, the other shadow agents all showed a bit of surprise on their faces.

The first few rules are fairly normal school regulations, but what the heck is this last rule?

Is it illegal to eat a grilled sausage at this lousy school?

In everyone's subconscious, only bad schools strictly investigate disciplinary issues.

The reason why schools forbid students from eating grilled sausages or bringing food to school is clearly because the school cafeteria owner is likely a relative of one of the school's leaders.

"Have you understood the rules?" Director Wu closed the paper, his tone leaving no room for argument.

"Yes, we understand," the group replied, forcing themselves to speak.

"Very good." Director Wu pushed up his glasses: "Your dormitories are on the third floor of the faculty and staff apartments. The rooms have been assigned, two people per room. Here are the keys and work schedule."

He handed each person an old, rusty key and a freshly printed worksheet.

"Now, follow me to familiarize yourself with the teaching building. Remember, stay close to me, don't wander off, and don't look around." With that, he turned and walked toward the tallest building, the main bishop's building.

The others quickly followed.

The campus was large, yet unusually quiet.

The playground was deserted, with only a few withered leaves swirling on the ground in the cold wind.

The exterior walls of the teaching building were covered with withered vines, most of the windows were closed, and some windows were covered with a thick layer of dust, making it impossible to see inside.

Occasionally, a blurry figure seems to flash past behind a few windows, but whenever the gaze moves over, it becomes empty.

The faint aroma of grilled sausage fat seemed to waft through the air again, making many people swallow hard.

It smells fucking good...

Many lurkers were thinking to themselves.

Director Wu led them through the empty corridor.

On the walls on both sides of the corridor, there are photos and introductions of outstanding students, but many of the glass frames are broken, and even the faces in the photos are somewhat blurry.

Some classroom doors were ajar, and the sound of teachers lecturing could be heard inside. Judging from the course content, it was almost no different from the courses at cultivation colleges outside, which were all the most basic cultivation knowledge at the high school level.

Wang Ling's consciousness quietly spread out.
