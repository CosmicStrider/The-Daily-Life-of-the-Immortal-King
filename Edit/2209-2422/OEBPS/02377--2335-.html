Chapter 2335 Summoning the Gods: The Arrival of Uz and Brother Li!

"Lingzi...you?" Chen Chao blinked, somewhat incredulous.

For them, Wang Ling's acceptance of the challenge was more astonishing than the sun rising in the west.

Guo Hao also came over and whispered, "Wang Ling, there's no need to take this kind of person seriously. Chao'er and I have already taught him a lesson."

Wang Ling offered no explanation, but simply walked straight to an empty gaming cabin.

Seeing this, Ma Yu's face lit up with a morbidly excited smile: "Haha! You've got some guts! I'll show you what a real ADC is! Someone like you who looks so clumsy is just a cash cow for the enemy if you play bot lane!"

His incessant sarcasm was an attempt to completely disrupt Wang Ling's mind before the war began.

However, the king's order was ignored.

He calmly lay down in the gaming cabin, and just before the door closed, he slowly closed his eyes.

At that moment, his aura underwent a subtle change.

"Huh?" Wang Muyu, who had been closely watching Wang Ling, suddenly showed a look of surprise on his face.

"Mu Yu, what's wrong?" Sun Rong keenly noticed the change in Wang Mu Yu's emotions and immediately asked via telepathy.

She knew that Wang Muyu was very perceptive, especially about Wang Ling's condition.

Wang Muyu stared at the now-closed gaming cabin, and a childish voice echoed in Sun Rong's mind: "Sister Sun Rong, Father... seems to be invoking a deity."

"Invite the gods?" Sun Rong was taken aback.

This term is not unfamiliar in the cultivation world; it usually refers to invoking the power of celestial beings or patriarchs from the upper realms to possess a person.

But... would you use it for playing games?

"Yes!" Wang Muyu nodded vigorously: "This is a very wonderful soul fluctuation frequency adjustment... It can quickly obtain the ultimate experience in a certain field! However, I know too little about this game and can't tell which god my father has invited."

Just as Sun Rong and Wang Muyu were communicating telepathically, Chen Chao and Guo Hao, who were standing next to the e-sports team, also sensed something was wrong.

They couldn't quite describe what it felt like.

"hiss......"

Chen Chao took a deep breath and nudged Guo Hao with his elbow: "Old Guo, don't you think your son seems like a completely different person all of a sudden?"

Guo Hao's eyes were equally filled with suspicion and uncertainty: "It's a bit eerie!"

At this moment, the hero selection screen has been displayed on the holographic projection.

To prove his absolute strength as an ADC, Ma Yu almost instantly locked in the current version's top laner hero - [Glory Executioner Drew].

This hero is known for his extremely high single-target burst damage and unique axe-catching mechanism. When played well, he can snowball very quickly, making him extremely visually impactful.

"See that? Delaiwu! My signature move! Tremble, kid!" Ma Yu's arrogant voice came through the audio channel.

It was Wang Ling's turn to choose.

His cursor moved slowly across the pool of heroes.

Finally, the image landed on the head of a female hero wearing a dark red cloak and holding a huge crossbow.

【Night Assassin - Vayne】!

"VN?" Chen Chao was taken aback. "This champion is pretty weak in the early laning phase. She has short range and no wave clear. Wouldn't she be in jail if she played against Draven?"

Guo Hao, however, seemed thoughtful: "VN... a synonym for flashy plays... picking this hero means you're either extremely confident in yourself!"

The moment Sun Rong saw the VN icon light up, a knowing glint flashed in her eyes.

As the eldest daughter of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, she naturally has some understanding of the e-sports field in which she invests, and she also has her own e-sports team.

She was especially familiar with the legendary player who had imprinted the Vayne champion on his own and made her a symbol of faith for countless ADC players.

"So... Wang Ling invited him!"

Sun Rong murmured softly, "...Uz!"

On the game loading screen, Ma Yu was still rambling on: "VN? Hahaha, you think you're not dying fast enough? I'll kill you in lane at level one!"

Wang Ling remained silent.

Inside the esports arena, his closed eyelids trembled slightly, and countless images of extreme kiting, low-health comebacks, and team fight cleanups flashed through his mind like a revolving lantern.

That was the instinct honed through countless trials in the ADC position by another soul.

"Welcome to the Glory Alliance!"

The system's female voice sounded, and the solo officially began!

The map remains the "Ancient Ruins" 1v1 map, where heroes from both sides appear at the intersection of the bottom lane minion waves.

Ma Yu's Delaiwu arrogantly wielded two throwing axes, attempting to use his long reach advantage to snipe Wang Ling's Vayne right from the start.

However, Wang Ling's Vayne showed a completely different style from the very first step of her positioning!

Draven's throwing axe barely grazed the edge of Vayne's cloak before missing its mark!

"Huh?" Ma Yu was taken aback, subconsciously thinking it was a coincidence.

He controlled Draven to step forward and launched another basic attack!

Vayne made another nimble turn, interrupting Draven's basic attack animation once again, preventing him from attacking!

Twice in a row!

Ma Yu frowned, a sense of foreboding rising in her heart.

This Vayne's positioning is a bit weird!

The minion wave has arrived, and the real battle begins.

Ma Yu wanted to use the hero's early-game strength to exert pressure, but he quickly realized that he couldn't even touch Vayne!

Wang Ling's Vayne seems to be able to predict the intention of every basic attack. She always makes the perfect move at the moment he attacks, either by getting behind minions or by using the maximum range to kite him.

What made Ma Yu's scalp tingle even more was Wang Ling's finishing blow!

Every low-health minion fell to Vayne's crossbow bolts, each strike landing flawlessly, as steady as a script!

Ma Yu himself, in his eagerness to exhaust his troops, inadvertently lost two soldiers.

"Damn it, all you can do is dodge? If you're so tough, go for it!" Ma Yu yelled angrily in the voice chat.

Wang Ling naturally ignored it. His Vayne weaved between the minion waves, each movement carrying a unique rhythm characteristic of a top-tier ADC.

Both parties reached Level 2.

Ma Yu learned the W skill [Blood Dash] and tried to accelerate forward to unleash a combo.

Just as he activated W and accelerated forward!

Wang Ling's Vayne has moved!

Instead of retreating, it moves to the side at a right angle while simultaneously learning the E skill [Demon Judgment]!

"Whoosh!"

The enormous crossbow bolt, imbued with the power of holy silver, roared forth!

Delewu was pinned to the wall to his side and behind by this incredibly accurate arrow! He was momentarily stunned!

"What?!" Ma Yu exclaimed in surprise. He had not expected Vayne to dare to fight back, and he had not expected that her E skill would be so tricky!

Taking advantage of his moment of dizziness, Wang Ling's Vayne smoothly moved forward, firing one arrow, then two!

Simultaneously ignite!

When the dizziness ended, Ma Yu's health had dropped significantly. He was so scared that he quickly used a double DF combo, followed by a healing spell and a blink to pull back.

Inside the venue, all was silent.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao stared wide-eyed.

"Holy crap!" Chen Chao rubbed his eyes. "Lingzi's Vayne... her positioning, her E skill!"

Guo Hao took a deep breath, his voice filled with shock: "This isn't just ordinary skill... This pulling, this sense of distance, this confidence to counterattack... It's absolutely professional level!"

Sun Rong watched the figure calmly controlling Vayne on the screen, and the corners of her mouth unconsciously turned up.

She was almost certain that what was dominating Wang Ling's consciousness at this moment was that legendary figure known for his lane dominance and extreme skills!

Although Wang Muyu couldn't understand the specific operations, he could feel the aura emanating from Wang Ling that was perfectly integrated with the "inviting the gods" state.

Ma Yu returned online, her face pale; she was now completely out of touch with the world in terms of both finances and experience.

He tried to develop indecently, but Wang Ling didn't give him the chance at all.

VN's equipment is gradually taking shape. With the Berserker's Legplates and Frenzy in hand, her mobility and damage are beginning to show.
