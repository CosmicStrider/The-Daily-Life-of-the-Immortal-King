Chapter 2355 The First Lesson of the Love Strategist Alliance

The Starry Sky Esports Center is located at the foot of Qingyun Mountain on the outskirts of Songhai City, covering an area of nearly 1,000 acres.

The silver-gray streamlined building complex gleams with a technologically advanced metallic sheen in the morning light.

This is a newly built comprehensive e-sports training base spearheaded by the Hundred Schools Administration and in conjunction with several government agencies. It is equipped with the most advanced spirit network equipment and holographic simulation battle system in China, and is specifically designed to cultivate the next generation of e-sports talents.

A luxurious psionic spaceship bearing the "Huaguo Water Curtain Group" logo slowly landed on the helipad in front of the main building.

The cabin door opened, and Sun Rong was the first to jump out, her black hair swaying gently in the morning breeze.

She wore a light blue tracksuit today, with her jacket casually draped over her shoulders, revealing a white T-shirt with cartoon prints underneath, looking refreshing and full of energy.

"Is this the Starry Sky Esports Center?"

Chen Chao was the second to emerge. Looking up at the magnificent building before him, he was dumbfounded: "Holy crap, this is even bigger than our school!"

Guo Hao, who was following behind him, shrugged: "After all, it's an official training base, so the resources invested are definitely different. I heard that the psionic e-sports cabins here are all the latest models, with latency controlled within 0.1 milliseconds."

"Isn't that the same as no delay?" Li Changzhe's eyes lit up.

Although he was just a student at Beijing Number 8 High School, Chen Chao managed to get him into the training team, and Zhuo Yi didn't have any objections.

After all, having such a lively and cheerful person to lighten the mood is crucial in a relationship development plan.

Soon, Wang Muyu also jumped off the fairy boat, his little face full of excitement.

Today, he specially wore a miniature version of the Number 60 High School uniform and carried a small backpack with a cartoon dragon pattern, which was cute and lively.

"Sister Sun Rong! This place is so big!" Wang Muyu ran to Sun Rong's side and took her hand.

Sun Rong smiled and ruffled his hair: "Well, we'll be training here for the next three days. Muyu, are you nervous?"

"Don't be nervous!" Wang Muyu clenched his little fist: "I will study hard to become a mid laner!"

Just then, another celestial ship landed.

The cabin door opened, and Wang Ling slowly walked out.

He was still wearing the same old Number 60 High School uniform, his hands in his pockets, still clutching a freshly opened packet of instant noodles.

"Lingzi! You've finally arrived!" Chen Chao immediately came over and hooked his arm around Wang Ling's shoulder: "Let me tell you, I just checked the information, and the canteen here is specially sponsored by Ling Shi Fu! They even have specialty dishes made primarily with instant noodles!"

Wang Ling paused for 0.5 seconds while chewing the instant noodles.

He looked up at the massive building in front of him, and a flicker of emotion finally appeared in his eyes.

Seeing this, Chen Chao smiled slightly, knowing that Wang Ling had definitely taken the bait.

In fact, this was his suggestion to Zhuo Yi. If he wanted Wang Ling to stay and train steadily, pleasing him was naturally essential.

"Is everyone here?"

A gentle voice came from the side.

Everyone turned around and saw Zhuo Yi, dressed in casual clothes, walking towards them with a smile on his face.

Behind him followed Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi, who were also dressed in casual clothes.

Zhuo Yi smiled and said, "This exchange camp is hosted by the General Administration of Hundred Schools, and as the summer camp director, I've come here personally to oversee it. Moreover..."

He paused, his gaze sweeping over Wang Ling and Sun Rong: "I heard that our Number 60 High School team is the most promising dark horse this time."

Wang Zhen interjected, "Yes, Qingyi and I even applied for the status of accompanying observers. We wanted to experience the facilities here."

As he spoke, he winked at Chen Chao.

Chen Chao immediately understood and chuckled.

All members of the Love Strategist Alliance are now in place.

"Let's go, I'll take you to your accommodations first." Zhuo Yi turned around and led everyone towards the main building.

The interior design of the Starry Sky Esports Center is extremely futuristic.

In the void, holographic signs float in mid-air, while intelligent cleaning robots silently move across the smooth ground.

"The accommodation area is in Building B, and the training area is in Building A, connected by a corridor."

As Zhuo Yi walked, he introduced, "This exchange camp has invited a total of eight teams. In addition to Number 60 High School, there are several well-known e-sports clubs from high schools, such as Chihuo High School, Xuanming High School, Titan High School, Shengke Academy, Dishitian High School, Jiudaohe High School exchange team, as well as a social youth training team."

"Jiudao and Gaozhong?" Sun Rong was taken aback: "They're here too?"

"Hmm." Zhuo Yi nodded: "After the last training match, Ryoko Kugimiya extended a formal invitation to the Kudo High School e-sports club. They sent a brand new team this time, saying they want to redeem themselves."

Upon hearing this, Chen Chao couldn't help but laugh out loud: "Again? Aren't you afraid of being mentally crushed by Lingzi again?"

Wang Ling munched on his instant noodles expressionlessly, as if he hadn't heard a thing.

"Oh, right." Zhuo Yi suddenly remembered something, took out an electronic card from his pocket and handed it to Sun Rong: "This is your team's room key. Accommodation is two people per room, you can pair up freely. During the training camp, all teams will live together for easy communication."

"Two people per room?" Sun Rong took the room key and subconsciously looked at Wang Ling.

Chen Chao immediately jumped in, feigning concern, and asked, "Then I'll share a room with your son! We're brothers, perfect!"

"No." Zhuo Yi shook his head, a knowing look on his face: "Your room has already been arranged to be with Junior Guo Hao."

"Oh? I see." Chen Chao chuckled sheepishly; he knew Zhuo Yi had come prepared!

Li Changzhe raised his hand: "And me? I'm from Jingmen Number 8 High School, but I'm actually here to experience life... Where will I stay?"

Zhuo Yi smiled: "Chang Zhe, you'll be sharing a room with Wang Muyu. Remember to take good care of him."

"No problem!" Li Changzhe patted his chest to assure him.

Wang Muyu was very excited: "Brother Changzhe, can we watch the game recording together tonight?"

"Of course!"

After assigning roles to Chen Chao, Guo Hao, Li Changzhe, and Wang Muyu, only Wang Ling, Sun Rong, and Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi, who served as "observers," remained on site.

The air suddenly became quiet.

Sun Rong felt her cheeks start to burn.

She glanced at Wang Ling furtively and found that he was still engrossed in eating his instant noodles, seemingly oblivious to what was about to happen.

Zhuo Yi cleared his throat and continued in that businesslike tone, "Observers have their own accommodations, so Wang Zhen and Liu Qingyi won't be assigned. So, the rest..."

He resisted the urge to forcefully call out to his master and mistress, and looked at Wang Ling and Sun Rong: "Wang Ling, Sun Rong, according to the training camp rules, men and women are separate and cannot live together. But..."

He deliberately dragged out his words: "In cases where there aren't enough beds... exceptions can be made. Currently, there's only one single room left in the girls' dormitory that isn't fully renovated yet, and one bed in a double room."

Sun Rong's heart raced.

"You've come at a bad time. The bed in the double room..." Zhuo Yi looked at Wang Ling, "has already been assigned to a female member of a youth social training team. So, Sun Rong, you can choose to stay in a single room, or..."

He paused, a troubled expression on his face: "Or, in accordance with the exchange camp's aim of promoting inter-school exchanges, you can choose to share a room with male team members from different schools. Of course, this must be with the consent of both parties and registered."

"......"

Sun Rong's face turned completely red.

What kind of ridiculous rule is this!

How could she possibly share a room with a strange guy?

Thinking of this, Sun Rong couldn't help but glance at Wang Ling again.

Wang Ling finally finished the packet of instant noodles, folded the bag and stuffed it back into his pocket, then looked up at Zhuo Yi.

"So?" he asked slowly, "she can only stay in a single room?"

Zhuo Yi nodded: "Theoretically, yes. Unless..."

"Unless what?" Chen Chao couldn't help but interject.

"Unless there are special circumstances," Zhuo Yi pushed up his non-existent glasses, "such as someone in the team voluntarily applying to change dormitories, or... a leader at the observer level granting special approval."

As he spoke, he pulled out another electronic card from his pocket: "Actually, as the summer camp leader, I also have the authority to reserve a few spare rooms. These rooms are originally reserved for emergencies, such as when a team member suddenly falls ill and needs to be isolated alone."

He looked at Wang Ling and Sun Rong, a sly smile on his face: "However, if you two really need it, I can make an exception and arrange a spare double room. Of course, this requires both of you to agree and sign a waiver of liability."

"A liability waiver?" Sun Rong was stunned.

"Hmm." Zhuo Yi explained seriously, "It means that you are living together voluntarily, and the organizers of the exchange camp will not be responsible for any problems that occur during the period. After all, there are some risks to avoid when men and women live together."

Wang Ling looked at Zhuo Yi in silence.

His gaze was calm, but Zhuo Yi felt a chill run down his spine.

Could it be that Master has seen through it?

This shouldn't be happening. This plan was meticulously designed by their strategist alliance; it's logically flawless...
