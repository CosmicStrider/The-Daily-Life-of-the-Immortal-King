Chapter 2311 Wooden Fish's First High School Experience

Wang Ling carried Wang Nuan and led Wang Muyu, beginning another day of taking care of the children.

Today, Mr. and Mrs. Wang went out to attend a community gathering for spiritual practitioners, ostensibly to seek creative inspiration, leaving the house unusually clean.

"Dad, what are we eating today?" Seeing that the couple wasn't there, Wang Muyu stopped pretending and looked up at them with bright, sparkling eyes.

Ever since experiencing Wang Ma's cooking, the little guy's longing for the simple pleasures of everyday life has grown stronger every day.

Wang Ling didn't speak, his gaze sweeping over the kitchen.

Wang Nuan babbled on his shoulder, waved her little hand toward the refrigerator, and her lips glistened.

Got it, instant noodles.

Wang Ling walked expressionlessly to the refrigerator and skillfully took out three packs of different flavored instant noodles.

After thinking for a moment, I gave Wang Muyu and Wang Nuan each a box of Lingguo yogurt.

Wang Ling carefully placed Wang Nuan on a soft baby crawling mat and then settled Wang Muyu next to the dining table. Then, Wang Ling began his daily instant noodle tasting session.

Wang Muyu followed suit, taking small bites of the instant noodles, his cheeks puffing out, then slurping up a mouthful of yogurt, his eyes squinting happily. He didn't forget to open another box and feed it to Wang Nuan.

Nuan Yatou's little feet kicked happily as she received care from her two older brothers.

For a moment, the room was strangely harmonious.

Wang Ling watched this scene, a hint of satisfaction flashing in his eyes.

This is the daily life he wants to protect.

However, this tranquility did not last long.

His consciousness, like an invisible spider web, constantly shrouded Songhai City.

Just now, he once again detected an abnormal mental fluctuation belonging to the "Shadowman," and it was right inside the Number 60 High School.

The target seems to be mixed in with the logistics staff?

Wang Ling paused slightly while chewing.

Bai Zhe's infiltration was indeed all-pervasive.

Without making a sound, he closed his eyes and immediately activated his spiritual consciousness.

A wisp of thought has clung to the past like a shadow, deeply marking and monitoring it.

"Dad..." Wang Muyu swallowed the instant noodles in his mouth and hesitated before speaking, "Can I...can I go to school? To your school?"

"?" Wang Ling looked at him.

Wang Muyu lowered his head, clutching the hem of his clothes with his small hands: "Master Yi said that cultivation also requires cultivating the mind, and it is very important to refine the mind in the mortal world. I think high school life seems very interesting. Moreover, I want to make more friends so that I can... help you."

His voice grew softer and softer, filled with longing, yet also with a fear of causing trouble for Wang Ling.

Wang Ling paused for a moment.

Sending Wang Muyu to Number 60 High School would undoubtedly allow him to integrate more deeply into human society, which would be beneficial for his control of power and cultivation of his mind.

Moreover, with his pure dragon bloodline, he has an instinctive sense for evil spirits in the vicinity, which may make him a mobile detector and stabilizer.

But risks also exist.

Wang Muyu has a special identity, and his power is strong and unstable. Although the frontoceratops scales have been temporarily hidden, it is hard to guarantee that there will be no accidents.

The environment at Number 60 High School is much more complicated now than it used to be.

Seeing that Wang Ling remained silent, a hint of disappointment flashed across Wang Muyu's face: "If it doesn't work, then forget it..."

Just then, Wang Ling's phone vibrated.

It was a message from Sun Rong.

[Sun Rong: Wang Ling, I have good news! The research institute has successfully verified Li Jun's improved version of the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" using high-performance computing! It has a significant effect on stabilizing the mind and resisting low-level mental interference! The General Administration of Education and the school attach great importance to this, and Principal Chen plans to implement it throughout the school as soon as possible!]

Wang Ling looked at the message, then at Wang Muyu, who was staring at him expectantly.

The time seems to have come.

He picked up his phone and replied briefly:

[Wang Ling: Okay, also, I'd like Muyu to attend Number 60 High School.]

[Sun Rong: Then the campus life that follows is sure to be exciting!]

[Wang Ling: ...]

The only reason Wang Ling could reply with so many words was that, besides having technical exchanges with Wang Ming, Sun Rong was the only one who received this special treatment.

Then, he looked at Wang Muyu and nodded.

Wang Muyu was taken aback at first, but his little face instantly lit up with excitement, and he jumped up from his chair: "Yay! Thank you, Dad!"

Wang Ling looked at the jubilant little dragon and the corners of his mouth curved slightly.

That's fine.

Let the arrival of the little wooden fish stir up some more ripples in this pool of water.

...

The next day, at Number 60 High School, in the principal's office.

Principal Chen Tianxiang looked at the imposing young man in front of him who bore a striking resemblance to Wang Ling, and then at Wang Ling and Sun Rong standing beside him, and began to have some doubts.

When did Wang Ling's family get such a big "younger brother"?

The information shows that his name is Wang Muyu and he is applying to transfer into the senior high school of Number 60 High School.

Although in the current cultivation world, schooling is indeed not based on age, but rather on cultivation level and talent, Xiao Muyu's delicate baby face entering school will surely attract a lot of attention.

"Wang Muyu, right?"

Principal Chen adjusted his glasses, trying to appear approachable: "According to procedure, transfer students need to undergo a simple test of their spiritual root aptitude and cultivation level so that we can tailor our teaching to their individual needs. You see..."

"Yes, Principal." Wang Muyu nodded obediently.

He was wearing a casual tracksuit that Sun Rong had prepared in advance, and he looked exceptionally refreshed.

Principal Chen signaled to the school staff to activate the latest model of the psionic comprehensive evaluation instrument in the corner of the office.

This equipment is quite valuable and can accurately test the cultivation strength and spiritual root attributes from the Foundation Establishment stage to the early Infant stage.

"Wang Muyu, relax and place your hand on the sensor area," Principal Chen instructed.

Wang Muyu followed the instructions and stepped forward, curiously examining the instrument that was flashing with spiritual light.

Then, I carefully placed my palm on it.

He remembered Wang Ling's instructions: Conceal your power, you must conceal most of it, only reveal a little bit...

The little wooden fish tried its best to control itself, attempting to separate only spiritual power equivalent to the peak of the Foundation Establishment stage.

However, he underestimated the immense power he would gain after devouring the son of the Dragon God, and overestimated the limits of this mortal instrument.

The instant his palm touched the sensory area.

"Buzz!!!"

The testing device suddenly emitted an unprecedentedly dazzling light.

The value on the display screen instantly exceeded the upper limit of the scale!

Immediately afterwards, a metallic boom, as if it were about to collapse, came from inside the instrument.

"Bang!!!"

With a muffled thud, the top of the testing instrument was blasted open by the out-of-control psionic energy inside. Several rune nodes flashed wildly, and finally a wisp of smoke appeared before the screen went black and the device completely crashed.

The office was deathly silent.

Principal Chen's mouth dropped open, and a fine layer of cold sweat instantly appeared on his forehead.
