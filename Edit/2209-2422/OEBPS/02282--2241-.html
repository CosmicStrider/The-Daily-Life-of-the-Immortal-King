Chapter 2241 A Shaken Heart

Fujiro Chen's furious roar, like a thunderclap, echoed in the cramped medical room, completely crushing the last shred of hope of the female researcher who had collapsed to the ground.

Her face was ashen, her body trembled like sieves, her lips moved but she could not utter any words of explanation, only deep regret and fear in her eyes.

Sun Rong stood beside Wang Ling, though the anger in her heart was still burning for this dirty conspiracy.

But soon, calmness took over. As the future successor of the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group, she still possessed such composure.

Now we should carefully consider who the real mastermind behind framing Wang Ling is, and what their purpose is.

Just as Lin Xuan's sharp and shrewd face flashed through her mind, she inadvertently caught a glimpse of the area above the female researcher's head out of the corner of her eye.

She was jolted awake.

The "countdown" scene, which had been blurred due to the intense emotional fluctuations, now appeared clearly—not a countdown, but a state of intertwining that she had never seen in anyone else before!

A blinding, burning crimson light emanated from above the female researcher's head, like filthy blood.

However, this red color is not her own emotional state; it is mixed with a thread that is in a state of "red crisis." This thread extends outward, penetrates the walls of the medical hall, and points directly to a certain direction inside the base.

At the edge of this chaotic red light, a wisp of gray mist was rapidly dissipating.

This is an emotional connection!

Moreover, it's an extremely negative connecting line!

Sun Rong instantly realized: this red connecting line meant that the female researcher's strong negative emotions were pointing to a specific target!

This form of "connection countdown" was something she had never observed before, yet it seemed perfectly reasonable in the current situation—it pointed to the person who was coercing or instigating her!

At the same time, her embroidery skills seemed to be temporarily enhanced due to the intense emotional impact and strong "desire for knowledge".

In the direction the red connecting line pierced, the outline of a familiar figure appeared faintly in her mind, and even the state of the top of his head was clearly "mapped" over in sync—that direction, that figure sneaking around the corner, panicked by Teng Luchen's rage, was none other than Lin Xuan!

His head was also covered in a blinding crimson, perfectly synchronized with the female researcher's state.

Lin Xuan, it really was him...

Sun Rong's eyes instantly sharpened like knives as she spoke in a deep voice to the jade slip, "Senior Zhuo Yi, please immediately retrieve the surveillance footage from the corner thirty meters away on the west side of the medical hall! Also, pinpoint the location of Lin Xuan, the substitute member of the Holy Science Academy, and his communication records!"

Her voice traveled clearly through the jade slip to the central hall.

Zhuo Yi reacted extremely quickly, immediately shouting, "Operations team! Follow Sun Rong's instructions and start operating immediately! Information team, investigate all of Lin Xuan's communications and movement patterns over the past hour!"

Several split-screen windows appeared instantly on the crystal screen.

Two scenes made everyone's pupils shrink: one scene showed Lin Xuan, his face pale and covered in cold sweat, hiding behind the corner wall that Sun Rong pointed to, peeking out towards the medical hall, with an anonymous communication jade talisman in his hand still glowing faintly.

Another scene flashed back quickly, clearly capturing how, shortly after Sun Rong and Wang Ling entered the medical hall, Lin Xuan sneakily approached and briefly spoke with the female researcher, handing her a large bag of spirit stones and a note. However, after reading the note, the female researcher immediately destroyed the evidence in a simple and brutal way, swallowing it directly.

Upon seeing this, Sun Rong immediately rubbed her delicate little fist in front of the female researcher.

The female researcher was even more terrified: "Sun...Sun Rong, what are you going to do?"

Sun Rong, without a smile, said, "Take evidence!" Then, boom! She punched the female researcher in the stomach, and the woman instantly felt dizzy and disoriented.

This scene also slightly startled Wang Ling, who was standing nearby...

Um......

It was indeed Sun Rong, a classmate she had never seen before...

The female researcher immediately vomited up a wad of paper covered in gastric juices. Fortunately, it hadn't been in her stomach for too long, and the paper was made of a special material that wasn't easily digested. Sun Rong scanned it with her spiritual sense and found that the writing on it was still clear, so she breathed a sigh of relief.

Now the chain of evidence is complete.

"Lin Xuan!" Dean Jing Heqiu's face was ashen, his voice filled with furious anger at being fooled. "Take him down!"

The base guards, who had been waiting in the passage, moved instantly and pounced on the enemy like wolves.

Lin Xuan tried to argue and struggle, but faced with irrefutable evidence, it was like a mantis trying to stop a chariot; he was subdued and taken away in an instant.

Under Sun Rong's watchful gaze and irrefutable evidence, the limp female researcher's psychological defenses completely collapsed. She tearfully confessed that Lin Xuan had coerced and lured her into falsely accusing Wang Ling by offering her extra spirit stones as a reward and promising her a formal position if she successfully reported him.

The goal was to kick Wang Ling out of the plan and severely damage the reputation of the Number 60 High School team, while also "stepping on the two 'pebbles' that are currently in the limelight, namely Number 8 High School and Number 60 High School".

Inside the central hall, Teng Luchen's anger was no longer directed at Wang Ling's "special" status, but rather at this behavior that disrupted the project's stability and attacked the participants with underhanded means.

"It's a disgrace to the entire cultivation world of China..."
