Chapter 2299 The Furnace of Emotions

Even during dinner that evening, the news was still filled with reports about user experiences with the "Taste Master".

This made Wang Ling feel slightly uncomfortable.

In this modern society where self-media is prevalent and everything emphasizes a fast pace, much content no longer adheres to the three elements of news. As long as it's trending and has an eye-catching headline, it can generate huge traffic, coupled with various AI voiceovers.

This was originally a common problem for self-media, but Wang Ling did not expect that even mainstream media would start to decline.

The efficacy of "Taste Master" has not been rigorously scientifically verified, and its side effects are not even mentioned. Under such circumstances, it still manages to attract a large number of reports.

In the end, it was a case of bad money driving out good, Wang Ling thought to himself, somewhat speechless.

Fortunately, some people could see it clearly, such as Wang's father and mother.

Wang's father shoveled a mouthful of rice into his mouth, then looked at the newspaper and questioned, "This thing looks like an 'IQ tax.' Although your mother and I are only at the Body Refining stage, there are no shortcuts on the path of cultivation. Of course, you and Xiao Nuannuan are an exception!"

Wang Ling: "..."

"You know, your dad is right."

Wang Ma also said, "The path of cultivation is already incredibly resource-intensive. If there really was such a thing that could transform the most ordinary food into ingredients that replenish spiritual energy in large quantities, and could even have its flavor customized, wouldn't a huge number of legitimate cultivation companies have to go bankrupt?"

Upon hearing this, Wang Ling gave the couple a thumbs up.

After dinner, he went back to his bedroom.

The hustle and bustle outside the window contrasted sharply with the tranquility within him.

He didn't put the sweet crystal berry candied fruit that Sun Rong had given him into his mouth, but instead gently placed it on the windowsill.

His gaze had already pierced through layers of barriers, locking onto a dark corner of Songhai City.

That's the iconic headquarters building of the Xibei Cultivation Food Group, which resembles a giant lunchbox.

Deep underground, inside a core chamber entirely constructed from an isolated spirit array.

Dr. Lynch shed all pretenses, and his former refined demeanor vanished.

He paced anxiously back and forth in front of the control panel covered with screens, his eyes behind his wire-rimmed glasses filled with astonishment.

"Have you found out? What exactly is going on with the abnormal spiritual energy fluctuations in the examination room? Why did Li Jun's emotional peak plummet?" He roared into the communication device, his voice hoarse.

From the other end of the magical artifact came a hesitant report: "The doctor... can't figure it out. There are no signs of any strong external interference; it's as if he suddenly figured it out himself..."

"fart!"

Lin Qi slammed his hand on the control panel, causing the screens to flicker: "The [Potential Burst] template, combined with the [Inner Demon Catalyst] we injected, is so powerful that even a Golden Core Peak cultivator might not be able to withstand the pressure of that kind of exam! How can a Foundation Establishment stage brat expect to have such a sudden enlightenment on his own?"

He was breathing heavily, as if a fire was churning inside his chest.

The "demonstration site" that Li Jun carefully selected not only did not explode, but seemed to have escaped control.

This was a heavy blow and a resounding slap in the face for him and for the entire plan.

"Backup plan! Activate the backup plan immediately!"

Lin Qi forced himself to calm down, a cold glint flashing in his eyes again: "Focus on monitoring the other core members of the [Number 60 High School Taste Club], especially that girl named Liu Yun. She's more sensitive and more dependent! In the upcoming practical assessment, give her a little extra challenge to ensure everything goes perfectly! I want to see results!"

"Yes, Doctor!"

"And..." Lin Qi added, his tone grim, "Report to the Primordial World that Plan A has been thwarted, with suspicions of intervention from a high-level force. Request authorization to use the [Deep Link] license. We need an even stronger emotional torrent to break through all obstacles; we absolutely cannot afford any more mistakes!"

"clear!"

Communication cut off.

Lin Qi stood alone in the cold, enclosed room.

He stared at the countless green dots representing users of the "Taste Master" app on the main screen, along with the scattered yellow dots that flickered, representing unstable emotions, and the very few dim dots like Li Jun's that briefly turned red before stabilizing. His face was extremely grim.

He had a strong sense of unease.

It was as if behind all of this, an invisible net was slowly tightening, yet he couldn't even touch the shadow of the net weaver.

...

Meanwhile, Wang Ling was at home.

Wang Ming's holographic projection lit up again, this time with a hint of excitement on his face.

"Lingling, we've made a major discovery!"

He moved his fingers rapidly through the air, conjuring up a complex psionic energy flow diagram.

Wang Ming: "Through cross-analysis of the data streams of the entire city's [Spirit Network] before and after Li Jun's equipment was interfered with, I captured a signal with extremely high intensity and traced its source! It only flashed for a moment! The other side is quite cautious, but unfortunately I still caught it by the tail."

At the center of the image, a core node hidden beneath countless disguised paths is highlighted.

"The location is confirmed! It's not overseas; they've hidden it in the dark! It's in the mezzanine space 300 meters underground at the headquarters of the Xibei Cultivation Food Group!"

Wang Ming spoke rapidly: "Moreover, this node is not just a simple data relay station; it's more like an emotional melting pot."

He zoomed in on the core of the image: "It is constantly absorbing the negative emotions of all users, especially those whose emotions are fluctuating wildly."

"These negative emotions are purified and compressed. I suspect that this emotional furnace is not only key to maintaining the operation of the Spirit Web, but may also be feeding something."

Wang Ling listened quietly, his gaze fixed on the marked node.

His deep eyes were completely still, as if he had already seen through everything.

"The difficulty lies in..."

Wang Ming used an ice pack to cool down his overheated head: "This node's defense mechanism is quite peculiar. It's deeply bound to the [Taste Master] of all online users. Any attempt to destroy the node will instantly trigger a mental backlash from all devices. At best, it will damage one's consciousness; at worst... it might directly detonate the [Emotional Bomb] we previously speculated, causing widespread mental chaos."

He looked at Wang Ling: "A direct approach won't work. We need a gentler approach, dismantling it from within without alerting the defense mechanism, or at least severing this fatal connection between it and the user."

Wang Ling's gaze slowly shifted from the psionic diagram shown by Wang Ming to the direction of the Xibei headquarters outside the window, as if lost in thought.

...

The headquarters of Xibei, the underground core node, the mother nest.

This is not a cold, mechanical machine room, but a space filled with bizarre life forms.

A huge, flesh-colored tissue, resembling a biological brain, pulsates slowly, its surface flowing with dark red energy streams. Countless fine energy channels extend out like human nerves, disappearing into the void and connecting thousands of users.

At the very center of this "brain" floats a dark red energy core that constantly changes shape.

This is a highly condensed collection of negative emotions, emanating a nauseatingly complex flavor.

It is a mixture of human greed, anxiety, and resentment.

Sudden!

The entire nest space trembled as if in a hallucination.

It seemed as if some high-dimensional force, transcending the biological level, had suddenly descended without warning, creating ripples here!
