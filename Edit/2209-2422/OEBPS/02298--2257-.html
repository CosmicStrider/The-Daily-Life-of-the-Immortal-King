Chapter 2257 Wang Tong's Devouring

Inside the cave dwelling that Wang Ling built, the air was frozen solid like a block of ice.

Chen Chao's altered "Holy crap!" was like a pebble thrown into stagnant water, creating ripples that made everyone's pupils shrink.

All eyes were fixed on Ryoko Kumiya's battle formation.

On that small screen, the shadow representing the "Eternal Ink Abyss" area was being devoured by a terrifying dark red madness!

The energy fluctuation curve is not rising, it's exploding!

Like an ancient beast utterly enraged, breaking free from the chains of the abyss, it seeks to tear the entire inner world apart along with them!

"What... what energy level is that?!" Li Changzhe's voice was dry, as if he were looking up at a collapsing sky.

Zhang Linyan's knuckles turned white as she gripped the longbow; even the usually composed woman felt a tremor emanating from the depths of her soul.

The "Dawn" sword in Su Xingyue's hand trembled slightly, emitting a mournful groan.

Everyone is afraid.

Guo Hao's face was deathly pale, his lips trembled, and he couldn't utter a single word. Chen Chao's legs went weak, and he almost collapsed to the ground if Guo Hao hadn't caught him in time.

Even Liu Qingyi's divine aura burst uncontrollably at this moment, her deep purple halo pulsating violently. Her eyes were sharp as knives, carrying an unprecedented solemnity since entering the Earth's core world.

The skull sickle in Fang Xing's hand hummed incessantly, the black gemstone spinning wildly, its greed mixed with extreme fear.

The playful smile on Wang Zhen's face vanished completely, replaced by a rare seriousness.

The entire cave felt as if it had been plunged into a dark, deep sea, the immense pressure making it hard to breathe. The shadow of death had never been so clear.

Only deep within the cave, the figure clutching crumbs of instant noodles remained calm.

The crumb of dough that Wang Ling was holding between his fingertips hovered in mid-air, only an inch away from his lips.

Deep within his eyes, a sudden, clear, iridescent light stared back at the terrifying presence that was about to materialize completely in the depths of the dark abyss.

His eyes, which showed no emotion whatsoever, held only a hint of displeasure at being disturbed.

Especially when the other party's will, a mixture of destruction and greed, surges forth like a filthy tide.

Wang Ling's eyelids lifted very slightly.

The movement was so subtle that it was almost negligible.

Buzz—!

Inside the cave, the extremely bright dark red warning light spot on the Nine Palaces Liangzi Formation Plate suddenly froze!

It was like a red-hot iron being plunged into an ice spring at absolute zero.

The energy curve representing the final roar of the Glass Queen was instantly forced back to its origin from the top of the chart by an invisible giant hand!

Reset to zero!

It was as if that world-destroying existence had never materialized!

The blinding red light on the array screen went out instantly, like a candle being extinguished, leaving only a faint green light representing safety and a calm energy countdown.

The heavy pressure inside the cave, like a punctured balloon, suddenly eased.

"Huh?" Chen Chao, still in a terrified state, blinked blankly. "Gone...gone?"

Guo Hao helped Chen Chao up, looking completely bewildered.

Zhang Linyan, Li Changzhe, Liu Qingyi, Fang Xing, Wang Zhen... everyone's expression froze instantly.

One second it was a catastrophe, the next second... it was calm and peaceful?

What happened?!

No one saw any light or shadow of the energy collision, nor heard any earth-shattering noise.

That terrifying pressure, enough to make even a true immortal tremble, just like that... disappeared?

Like a nightmare, waking up to find only a cold sweat on your back and a void.

Only Sun Rong's heart skipped a beat the instant the alarm disappeared.

His gaze, tinged with a hint of sudden realization, fixed on Wang Ling.

The moment the terrifying pressure disappeared, Wang Ling's finger, which had been hovering in mid-air, moved.

Still eating, instant noodles...

"Click".

The crisp chewing sound was particularly loud in the deathly silent cave.

He picked up the Yuxin juice next to him, took a small sip, and the movement was so smooth that there was not a trace of hesitation.

It was as if the destructive storm that had just swept across the entire Earth's core was merely a wisp of breeze brushing against the hem of his clothes.

But Sun Rong saw it clearly!

The moment he swallowed that mouthful of instant noodles, the suddenly clear, iridescent light deep in his eyes seemed to have completed some kind of mission, quietly disappearing and returning to a state of calm as still as a deep well.

Wang Ling...

Just how strong are you?

Sun Rong's heart pounded in her chest, not from fear, but from an indescribable shock and...

She finally realized with absolute certainty that her understanding of Wang Ling's strength was still only the tip of the iceberg.

At this moment, Wang Ling lowered his eyelids slightly, casually tossed the empty bag of instant noodles he had finished eating onto the table, and reached out to a bag of brand-new instant noodles next to him that was printed with the words "Hellfire".

The ripping sound of the packaging bag being torn open broke the silence of the cave once again.

All eyes were drawn to him.

Wang Ling seemed oblivious, slowly and methodically kneading the new dough, focusing intently on his "crushing" process.

In the corner, the Mountain Moving Beast, who had been trying to minimize its presence, stared intently at Wang Ling with its huge single eye, its whole body trembling like a leaf in the wind.

It let out a low, almost sobbing wail, filled with extreme fear and submission.

It sensed it!

It's a hundred times clearer than ever before!

That absolute will, powerful enough to wipe out the entire Glazed Continent, originated from that seemingly harmless boy who was kneading instant noodles!

monster!

This is the real monster!

The mountain-moving creature screamed wildly in its heart, wanting only to bury itself deep within the rocks and never be noticed by that being again.

Inside the cave, an eerie silence reigned. The only sound was the rhythmic "crack" of Wang Ling crushing the instant noodles.
