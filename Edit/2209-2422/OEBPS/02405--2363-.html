Chapter 2363 A Fake Becomes Real

After the training match, the air in the Starry Sky Esports Center still lingered with the lingering heat of the boys' secret competition.

Luo Xinghe stood at the exit of the A-building battle hall, his back to the gradually dispersing crowd, his body and face trembling.

0-10.

Super weird.

In his three years in the industry, even the worst time he was targeted in the thousand-point rankings was only 0-6.

Today, in front of dozens of peers, he was completely crushed by a high school student who eats instant noodles all day long, using Olaf, who can be described as a version of a sewer character.

What he found most unacceptable was that the guy named Wang Ling never even glanced at him from beginning to end.

Fortunately, this training match was not open to the public, and recording was prohibited internally. Otherwise, if this had been broadcast live, his reputation as an e-sports streamer would have been completely ruined.

"Xinghe, are you alright?"

Xia Zhinuan walked over from behind, her voice filled with worry.

She had just finished her own match and heard that Luo Xinghe had been badly beaten, so she came over to see what was going on.

"It's nothing." Luo Xinghe turned around, his face already showing his signature gentle smile: "It's just a training match, it's normal to win and lose."

He spoke casually, even shrugging.

But Xia Zhinuan knew him too well.

She saw Luo Xinghe's hand hanging by his side, his nails already digging into his palm.

"That Wang Ling... his fighting style is indeed a bit special."

Xia Zhinu carefully chose her words: "The fact that Olaf played like that shows he has a deep understanding of the game. We can focus on that when we review the game tonight..."

"No need." Luo Xinghe interrupted her, his smile unchanged: "He's just a random player, not worth spending too much time on."

After he finished speaking, he patted Xia Zhinuan on the shoulder: "You go back to your room and rest first. I'll go talk to Zhuo Shuzhang about tomorrow's arrangements."

The smile on Luo Xinghe's face vanished the instant he turned to leave.

Random player? Not worth mentioning?

No.

He is determined to get his revenge.

And it has to be done in front of everyone.

Especially... Sun Rong.

...

Building B, 7th floor, corridor.

Chen Chao, with his arm around Wang Ling's shoulder, shouted in a voice that could be heard throughout the corridor: "Lingzi! Awesome! Absolutely awesome! Did you see Luo Xinghe's expression at the end? Hahaha, 0-10, guys! We totally crushed that professional streamer!"

Wang Muyu jogged alongside him, excitedly saying, "This Olaf is amazing! Every time he uses his ultimate to charge in, Caitlyn dies!"

Zhou Rui pushed up his glasses and calmly analyzed: "From a data perspective, Wang Ling's KDA for Olaf in this game was 14-0-9, with a team fight participation rate of 87%, and an economic difference of 5,300 against his opponent. Such a crushing performance is extremely rare even in professional matches."

Yamaguchi Kenzou, who was at the very end, earnestly praised Wang Lingjun in broken Mandarin, saying, "Wang Lingjun's jungling rhythm was perfect. I learned a lot from him."

Wang Ling was being held by Chen Chao, his face expressionless, as he slowly pulled out his fifth pack of instant noodles for the day from his pocket.

Just then, another elevator opened with a "ding".

Sun Rong, Guo Hao, Li Changzhe, and Xiaolin You came out—the members of Team B.

The two groups collided head-on in the middle of the corridor.

The atmosphere instantly became subtle.

Chen Chao immediately released Wang Ling, scratched his head, and chuckled twice: "Ahem... well, Sun Rong, Guo Hao, and Chang Zhe, you played well!"

He sounded a little guilty about what he said. After all, Team D had completely crushed Team B, and it was his good friend who had utterly destroyed their ADC.

Guo Hao pushed up his glasses, his face showing the same surprise: "I never realized Lingzi had such talent in gaming. She can play ADC, and she's even so strong at jungling. Could this be the law of conservation of spiritual power? Because her spiritual power is low, she excels in other areas?"

Wang Ling: "..."

Actually, his purpose in participating in this training camp was nothing more than to get unlimited instant noodles. Moreover, since this training camp was not open to the public, it was quite reasonable for him to perform exceptionally well.

For Wang Ling, playing games was a relatively stress-relieving activity. As mentioned before, people in games can be resurrected after being killed... But in reality, if he took it even slightly seriously, the Earth might be destroyed.

Li Changzhe, who had been a mere bystander throughout the entire competition, was quite magnanimous, chuckling, "Losing is losing! Wang Ling is indeed fierce, and that Olaf is like a mad dog!"

Sun Rong remained silent.

She stood beside Guo Hao, holding her tactical tablet in her hand, but her gaze involuntarily drifted toward Wang Ling.

Wang Ling also looked over.

Their eyes met.

Sun Rong's heart skipped a beat. She subconsciously wanted to look away, but felt that would be too deliberate, so she had no choice but to brace herself and meet his gaze.

Wang Ling looked at her and paused for about two seconds.

Then he looked down, took out another unopened pack of barbecue-flavored instant noodles from his pocket, walked over, and handed it to Sun Rong.

"Here you go," he said.

Sun Rong was taken aback: "Huh? Why?"

Wang Ling said calmly, "Replenish some energy."

Is this someone who cares about themselves?

Sun Rong blushed slightly and took the instant noodles: "Thank you..."

Chen Chao immediately shouted from the side, "Lingzi! What about me? I solo-killed the enemy Orianna with my Irelia! I even split-pushed and destroyed two towers! Is there a reward?"

Wang Ling glanced at him, took out a small bag of mini shrimp chips from another pocket, and tossed it over.

Chen Chao chuckled and caught it: "Hehe, that's great!"

Guo Hao pushed up his glasses, but before he could even speak, a bag of original flavor potato chips flew over.

He caught it precisely and nodded: "Although my Sword Demon was targeted, his damage taken was the highest in the team—he definitely deserves a snack reward."

Li Changzhe also raised his hand: "I wound myself up really fast!"

Wang Ling tossed over a bag of pea crisps.

Lastly, it was Kobayashi Yu's turn. This exchange student from Sun Island stood somewhat reservedly. Wang Ling looked at him and handed him a bag of seaweed-flavored biscuits.

Overwhelmed with gratitude, Kobayashi Yu accepted the gift with both hands, bowing repeatedly and saying, "Arigato! Arigato!"

The four members of Team B looked at the snacks that had suddenly appeared in their hands, each with a different expression.

Sun Rong held the bag of barbecue-flavored instant noodles, feeling warm inside and wanting to smile.

Is this piece of wood a way of comforting the opponent who lost the game?

"Let's go." Wang Ling waved and led Team D back to their room.

As he turned around, his gaze swept over Sun Rong almost imperceptibly, then glanced at the empty corridor behind her.

Luo Xinghe did not return with Team B.

...

At 7 p.m. that evening, at the Starry Sky Esports Center cafeteria.

The atmosphere at dinner time was somewhat subtle.

The students from Number 60 High School still gathered at one table.

Luo Xinghe and Xia Zhinuan sat with the captains of Titan High School and Saint Science Academy, chatting and laughing.

"See that?" Chen Chao lowered his voice and pointed with his chopsticks at Luo Xinghe's side: "That guy, he can still laugh so happily even after losing the game, his mental fortitude is impressive."

Guo Hao chuckled: "From a behavioral perspective, excessively concealing one's true emotions is often a sign of intense inner turmoil. He was unilaterally crushed by Lingzi today and lost face in public, yet he appears so relaxed now, which is actually quite illogical."

"So it was all an act?" Li Changzhe stuffed a piece of braised pork into his mouth.

"Most likely." Guo Hao nodded. "And while he was talking, his eyes kept glancing in our direction... and he kept secretly looking at Lingzi and Sun Rong."

Sun Rong was taking small sips of porridge when she heard this and paused.

"Look at me?" she asked.

Guo Hao corrected him: "When you handed the green pepper to Lingzi just now, Luo Xinghe was watching the whole time..."

Sun Rong blushed: "I...I just don't like eating green peppers..."
