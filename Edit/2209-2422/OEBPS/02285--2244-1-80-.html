Chapter 2244 Annihilation of Divine Consciousness

The thought penetrated the thick rock layers, ignoring spatial barriers, and precisely locked onto the bottomless precipice dozens of miles away, like an invisible probe.

On the platform built of skeletons, the figure shrouded in a cloak suddenly trembled violently!

The miniature markers covering the mountain-moving formation were instantly captured by Wang Ling, who traced them back to their source. The entire process was faster than the transmission speed of divine consciousness fluctuations, and it was more like he directly "saw" the connection between cause and effect.

That cold and filthy aura of the underworld instantly froze and vanished!

Meanwhile, on the bone platform at the edge of the cliff.

"puff--!"

The cloaked figure suddenly spat out a mouthful of pitch-black blood, as if his soul had been struck by an invisible sledgehammer. His entire body staggered backward, and the hood exploded violently under the intense energy backlash!

It revealed a face that was definitely not that of a living being—the skin was as pale as death, covered with fine, cracked runes, and the eye sockets were not eyeballs, but two eerie green, flickering soul flames that were now flaring wildly and almost going out!

"Well......?!"

Its furious roar was not a real sound, but a spiritual wave that directly shook the space, carrying indescribable terror and disbelief! The dense death and soul energy around it surged and churned wildly and out of control, like boiling oil with a stone thrown in.

It saw it!

It "saw" a figure.

A being as insignificant as a mustard seed in its vast divine perception, yet emanating an aura that caused the entire abyss to freeze and tremble!

That will! Vast! Cold! Eternal!

Just as the laws of the universe themselves are watching over a speck of dust!

That was an absolute suppression that transcended the level of life!

It wasn't an attack; its sheer weight was enough to crush its very soul!

This is no ordinary surface cultivator!

What... what kind of deity has descended?!

An absurd yet chilling thought instantly froze its soul flame—the information was wrong! Terribly wrong! It had kicked a truly terrifying iron plate that even the deepest recesses of the underworld were shrouded in mystery!

Counterattack!

The threat must be eliminated! At all costs!

Driven by an instinctive fear of those in higher positions and a frenzy of feeling thwarted in his duty, reason instantly overwhelmed him.

It knew that such a failed reconnaissance would surely alert the enemy, and retreat was the best option.

But a stubbornness deeply rooted in its mission and a misjudgment of power led it to choose the most foolish form of resistance.

"Disrespect the Netherworld! Shattered Soul Purgatory!" The silent divine shriek transformed into a tangible dark purple shockwave. It was no longer a probe, but rather a condensation of all its original power as a high-level Netherworld Envoy, mixed with the deathly resentment accumulated over billions of years from the bottom of the abyss cliff. It transformed into an invisible yet sharp "soul spike" capable of cutting through space, ignoring physical distance, and relentlessly stabbed in the opposite direction of Wang Ling's location along the not-yet-completely-severed "connection" of perception!

This is no longer a test; it is a deadly technique to annihilate divine consciousness!

The target is the very source of that will that makes the very depths of its soul tremble!

It attempted to completely shatter that terrifying "presence" at the spiritual level!

Inside the Earth's core base.

Wang Linggang picked up a bag of new flavored instant noodles, tore a small hole in the packaging, and hadn't even had time to stick his finger inside.

The sharp, piercing intent, imbued with intense resentment and the destructive power of divine consciousness, instantly pierced through the rock barrier, ignoring distance, and viciously "crashed" into his consciousness.

Like a drop of water crashing into the ocean of the entire universe, it doesn't even amount to a ripple.

"......"

Wang Ling's fingertips didn't even pause for a moment, his eyes still fixed on the packaging of the instant noodles, as if he slightly frowned in dissatisfaction with a certain seafood flavor.

They are even too lazy to think about being "serious".

With just a slight thought, the ripples of everyday thoughts that had been attacked quietly disappeared, replaced by Wang Ling slightly raising his gaze from the packaging bag—and glancing "at" the direction far away on the cliff.

There was no burst of light, no dissipation of energy.

*Plop—Snap!*

On the bone platform dozens of miles away, the space seemed to be violently kneaded by an invisible giant hand.

The "Soul Spike," which contained the life's cultivation of the underworld messenger, shattered the moment Wang Ling's gaze fell upon it.

It was as if delicate glass had collided with an absolute rigid body, along with the primordial consciousness attached to it, all of which were utterly crushed into pure information dust by an incomprehensible, irresistible force, like the will of the universe itself.

"ah--!!!"

A mournful wail, audible only to the soul, exploded above the precipice!

The runes covering the Netherworld Envoy's body cracked and burst open, releasing a jet of black light. The dense aura of deathly resentment around him dissipated wildly like a punctured balloon. The two eerie green soul flames flickered and swayed violently before shrinking to a third of their original size!

Its aura, like a punctured ball, rapidly weakened, and the skeletal platform collapsed in large sections after losing its support.

Irreversible damage! Just from a single glance!

The instinct to survive overwhelmed everything.

"My...master...please...please..." An extremely faint, intermittent mental wave, filled with endless fear and pleading, crossed space and directly reached Wang Ling's consciousness, humbled to the dust.

At that moment, a will far more powerful, ancient, and majestic than the dying Netherworld Envoy forcefully tore apart the absolute protective barrier that Wang Ling's divine consciousness had placed over that area. It seemed to emanate from the depths of the Netherworld, carrying an undeniable tone, attempting to intervene:

"Young friend! This is our messenger from the underworld. He has misjudged the situation! Show mercy! Give me some face..." The voice was loud and desolate, carrying the irresistible power of the laws of the underworld, trying to save the wisp of soul that was about to be extinguished.

Wang Ling frowned, feeling a little annoyed by the sound that was interrupting his research on the new instant noodles.

face?

It is meaningless in the face of absolute power, especially when such a persistent ghost pleads for mercy, which only seems more noisy.

He was too lazy to use any "means".

Erase it.

This idea formed clearly and directly in Wang Ling's mind, like an eraser brushing across a drawing.

puff!

There was no energy fluctuation, not a single sign.

On the cliff platform, the already cracked and dilapidated body of the Netherworld Envoy, along with the precarious central part of the skeletal platform beneath it, was completely transformed into a pool of black powder, so fine it was invisible to the naked eye and devoid of any spiritual vitality.

It was as if it had never existed.

The ancient, majestic will that had just torn through space suddenly froze, conveying an unbelievable sense of horror.

The sound abruptly stopped, and the forcibly torn spatial rift closed instantly, leaving behind a deathly silence in which fragments of Hades's furious yet fearful thoughts seemed to still echo.

The dust has settled.

Wang Ling finally got his wish and picked up a fan-shaped piece of crispy noodles, put it in his mouth, and made a crunching sound.

The crisp sound echoed within the tranquil underground base he had personally built.

At the warehouse entrance, the massive body of the Mountain-Moving Beast stood frozen in place like a stone sculpture, its huge single eye tightly closed, its body trembling like chaff. Deep within its soul was an unforgettable and terrifying scene—its once unreachable and high-ranking "Envoy" of the Underworld was completely erased like an ant by a single "glance," unable to even gain the respect of the King of the Underworld!

The Mountain-Moving Stone felt that every single crystal molecule within it was howling in fear, wishing it could shrink itself into the cracks of the stone and disappear.

Wang Ling seemed to have merely swatted a disturbing flying insect.

He chewed on his instant noodles, his gaze calmly sweeping over the Mountain Moving Team: "Shh~"

The Mountain-Moving Beast immediately fell silent, even suppressing its breathing to the lowest possible frequency. Its massive body slowly moved to the most inconspicuous shadow in the corner of the base and began frantically licking the ore fragments it had smashed earlier, trying to conceal its presence with its busyness.
