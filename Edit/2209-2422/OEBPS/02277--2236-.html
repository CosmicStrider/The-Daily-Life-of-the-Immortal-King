Chapter 2236 Facing the Test

Inside the central hall, a huge crystal screen was displaying images of the Jingmen Number 8 High School's fairy boat entering its berth, but the hall was so quiet you could hear a pin drop.

Li Changzhe stared blankly at the group of students from Number 60 High School being led away. His usually warm and friendly face, which was about to greet them, suddenly turned cold, and he also sensed a bit of tension.

All the researchers' eyes were unconsciously drawn to the group from Number 60 High School who had just been led in by Zhuo Yi—especially Wang Ling, who was walking on the edge of the slightly "shrinking" line with a calm and even somewhat indifferent expression.

Teng Luchen stood with his back to the entrance, his hands behind his back, exuding a heavy and imposing aura like a mountain.

The anger ignited by the ten consecutive shells seemed to have been forcibly suppressed, transforming into a deeper calculation.

He slowly turned around, a seemingly gentle but actually cold smile appearing on his old face, which was covered with shrewd lines.

"Elite representatives of Number 60 High School, welcome to the core center of the experimental base. I am Teng Luchen."

His voice wasn't loud, but it carried clearly to everyone's ears, exuding an undeniable sense of authority: "I've gathered everyone here for two reasons: first, to commend Guo Hao and Chen Chao for their outstanding performance during the welcoming ceremony; and second,..."

His gaze, like a searchlight, landed precisely and penetratingly on Wang Ling, and the smile on his face faded slightly: "I am very curious about your school's student, Wang Ling."

The moment the word "curiosity" was uttered, the atmosphere in the central hall instantly plummeted to freezing point.

Jing Heqiu glanced at Teng Luchen with concern, while Wang Ming and Zhuo Yi were even more on edge.

Chen Chao was an impatient man. Seeing this, even if he were stupid, he would know that this was an action aimed at Wang Ling. He took a step forward and was in front of Wang Ling, his burly body like a wall. He spoke first in a deep, booming voice:

"Elder Teng, Wang Ling is my brother! He's usually quiet and his cultivation level seems average, but we'll stick together on this mission! If he needs any help, I, Chen Chao, will be the first to step up! Please don't look down on him because of his low cultivation level and send him away! Going to the Earth's core world for training is a rare opportunity!"

He patted his chest, his bulging muscles still trembling slightly, displaying the power he had just unleashed, trying to endorse Wang Ling with his "strength".

Everyone: "..."

Guo Hao also stood next to Chen Chao, not as excited as Chen Chao, but with a resolute attitude: "Commander Teng, Wang Ling is a member of our Number 60 High School, and we are responsible for his safety! Whatever tests you have, bring them on! We can handle it!" Although he did not think Wang Ling was that strong, his loyalty to his friends was ingrained in his bones.

On the surface, Ryoko Kugimiya, Zhen Wang, and Qingyi Liu appeared calm, but in reality, their spiritual energy had already been quietly circulating throughout their limbs and bones, ready to deal with any unexpected situation. Fang Xing frowned slightly and subtly adjusted his position, protecting Wang Ling's flank even more tightly.

Sun Rong, the most meticulous future leader of the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group, instantly recalled the "countdown" ability brought by the mysterious embroidery pouch, as well as her vigilance against Teng Luchen's continuous probing.

Unlike Chen Chao and Guo Hao, who immediately took physical protection, she stepped forward, bowed slightly, and spoke with poise and confidence, yet her words were sharp and pointed, revealing Teng Luchen's intentions: "Commander Teng, I am Sun Rong. My grandfather, Sun Yiyuan, often speaks of Elder Teng's contributions to the country and deeply admires him. As the elite representatives of the Sixty Sects in this project, our team's determination to cooperate and advance together is beyond doubt. If you have any doubts about the strength of our entire team or our tactical coordination, and wish to conduct some necessary tests to assess the risks after entering the Earth's core, we fully understand and will cooperate fully."

She paused slightly, her bright eyes meeting Teng Luchen's deep and sharp gaze. She emphasized her point, trying to smooth things over for Wang Ling as much as possible: "However, if the Commander-in-Chief simply wants to use a special method that far exceeds the intensity of conventional testing..."

She deliberately paused on the "special method".

Then he continued, "Is it too hasty to test one person in our team in this way? Does it violate the official principles of teamwork and student safety procedures? You should know that there are still formal gravity, spiritual pressure, and geomagnetic pressure tests waiting for all of us later. Is your action both public and private, not well thought out?"

Sun Rong's words were reasonable, well-founded, and measured. She pointed out the inappropriateness of Teng Luchen's behavior, while cleverly invoking official procedures and her grandfather's reputation. This not only expressed her determination to uphold the imperial decree but also gave Teng Luchen a way out.

Standing behind the "human wall" built by Chen Chao and Guo Hao, Wang Ling felt the unreserved protection of his friends and listened to Sun Rong's watertight yet sharp defense.

In those lifeless, unchanging eyes of a dead fish, a fleeting, almost imperceptible glimmer of emotion flashed—a rare occurrence.

Although it was fleeting, the touching feeling of being steadfastly supported by so many partners was clearly conveyed to the bottom of my heart.

However, who is Teng Luchen? He is a cunning old fox who has long been shrewd. He understood Sun Rong's warning and the steps she was taking, but he did not want to accept them!

He was eager to unveil the "too ordinary" veil surrounding Wang Ling, and became somewhat impatient due to his repeated failed attempts to probe her.

"Ha, Sun Rong is indeed quick-witted. You're right, safety procedures are very important."

Teng Luchen gave a forced smile, a glint of shrewdness in his eyes, "But what I'm more concerned about is the core stability of the sixty-member teams under extreme conditions! Especially their instinctive reactions when faced with sudden, extreme spiritual energy shocks, which precisely demonstrate their true teamwork and potential!"

Before he finished speaking, Teng Luchen's aura suddenly changed!

Without any warning, an incomparably terrifying spiritual pressure, like an invisible primordial wave, suddenly erupted from his withered body!

This spiritual pressure was not evenly distributed, but rather precise, domineering, and possessing a sharp will that could pierce through all disguises. It instantly locked onto Wang Ling at the back of the crowd and then crushed over him with overwhelming force!

The objective is clear—the king's command! The goal is brutal—to force them to reveal their true strength!

Jing Heqiu was shocked: "Old Teng! Stop!"

Zhuo Yi and Wang Ming shouted and tried to step forward: "Elder Teng! You're being too impulsive. This is really against the rules!"

They never expected that this time, old man Teng Luchen would not play by the rules at all...

But it was too late! He was the hidden eleventh general, an undeniable force! How swift was his domineering spiritual pressure!
