Chapter 2329 A Tragedy Triggered by a Grilled Sausage

Wang Ying's patience is always measured in seconds.

Li Xiaoming's pathetic, scrambling appearance did not evoke any sympathy from him; instead, it made his sneer intensify.

Run? Do you think you can escape from Wang Ying?

"Tch, trouble." He spat under his breath, a sound only Wang Ling inside his body could hear.

Unlike Wang Ling's pursuit of minimal interference, Wang Ying believes in maximum efficiency.

He was no delicate immortal king, and dealing with such a cowardly person required a firm approach.

He didn't chase after him immediately; that would be too undignified. Instead, he strolled leisurely to the classroom window, his gaze precisely locking onto the obese figure who was frantically rushing towards the corner toilet on the playground.

"See? You can't get anything out of this kind of coward by talking nicely; you have to scare them." Wang Ying imparted his experience to Wang Ling inside his body.

Wang Ling: "..."

He was too lazy to refute.

To some extent, Wang Ying's efficiency was indeed high. Although the method was crude, it was useful at this critical juncture.

Although the rules state that they cannot have conflicts with students, given Li Xiaoming's personality, this doesn't even count as a conflict. He won't go to the teacher to complain; he will just silently process the matter in his heart.

Therefore, it cannot be considered a violation of Rule 6.

The break between classes was brief, and the students in the teaching building were like puppets that had been wound up again, quickly returning to their respective cages with the sound of the school bell.

The corridor instantly became empty.

Li Xiaoming indeed didn't dare to go back to the classroom.

But the teacher in the classroom seemed completely unconcerned about the absence of this student and continued teaching as usual.

This caught Wang Ling's attention.

It seems that this student Li Xiaoming does indeed have a special identity, as he is able to circumvent some of the rules?

Based on Zhou Ziwei's experience of executing the Abyss Gate several times, Wang Ling knew that this was a privilege possessed only by special NPCs.

This proves that they have indeed found the right person.

This student, Li Xiaoming, is indeed a breakthrough point.

Wang Ying timed it perfectly, and about five minutes after the first class started, she used a "Great Clone Technique" to go out and find Li Xiaoming.

Although the rules state that "you are not allowed to move around during class", sending a clone out is theoretically not a violation of the rules. After all, the main body is indeed in the classroom and has not moved around, while the clone is just a psionic entity, which is not even a human in essence, but just a ball of energy.

This is purely a behavior that exploits a rule bug.

Following Li Xiaoming's psychic energy, Wang Ying's clone easily found the toilet where he was hiding.

The old wooden door was pushed open, revealing a dimly lit toilet filled with a pungent smell of disinfectant mixed with urine.

Only one of the cubicles had its door tightly closed.

Wang Ying didn't even need to use her spiritual sense to detect the faint sobbing coming from inside.

He walked to the sink and turned on the tap.

The icy water gushed out, but he didn't wash; he simply let the sound amplify in the silent space, pounding on the fragile nerves of the person in the cubicle.

"Li Xiaoming".

Wang Ying spoke, her voice not loud, but carrying a domineering air that seemed to penetrate the door: "Are you going to come out yourself, or wait for me to invite you out?"

The sobbing inside the cubicle stopped abruptly.

I will count to three.

Wang Ying had little patience and started counting down: "Three."

A muffled thud came from inside the cubicle as a body hit the partition.

"two."

"No...don't come in!" Li Xiaoming's scream, choked with sobs, finally rang out, filled with despair.

"one."

The moment she finished speaking, Wang Ying didn't even use any magic power; she simply tapped the lock on the cubicle door lightly with her fingertip.

"Click".

With a soft click, the simple latch inside snapped off.

The door slowly opened inward.

Li Xiaoming huddled in the corner, his face covered in snot and tears, his whole body trembling uncontrollably.

"Now, can you speak properly?" Wang Ying leaned against the door frame, looking down at him with a completely unwavering gaze.

"I don't know anything! Please, teaching assistant... let me go!" Li Xiaoming buried his face in his knees, trying to escape.

"have no idea?"

Wang Ying sneered: "Then why are you trembling? Does the name Lin Wanwan scare you?"

Upon hearing the name "Lin Wanwan," Li Xiaoming's body stiffened abruptly, and he trembled even more violently.

"It seems so." Wang Ying pressed on, "I don't have the patience to play guessing games with you. Tell me everything you know about Lin Wanwan. Otherwise... you know the consequences."

This is not an empty threat.

Li Xiaoming sensed a chilling murderous aura emanating from this "teaching assistant," an aura even more icy than that of Director Wu.

That wasn't the constraint of school rules, but a true indifference that treats life as worthless.

"I'll tell you... I'll tell you!" His psychological defenses completely collapsed, and Li Xiaoming burst into tears, beginning to recount his story in fits and starts.

"Wanwan...she wasn't like this before..."

...

Just as the fragments of information that Wang Ling had previously captured pointed to.

Lin Wanwan's story began with an ordinary grilled sausage.

Her father was a special cleric in the glorious Seven Stars Team and was a great hero in his daughter's eyes.

Every time he returned from a dangerous mission, no matter how late it was, he would always remember to bring his beloved daughter a starch sausage from a street vendor.

That sizzling, oily aroma was the warmest and safest smell in Lin Wanwan's childhood.

Later, her father died in the line of duty and never returned.

That grilled sausage became Lin Wanwan's only memory, an invisible bond between her and her father.

She started buying grilled sausages for herself, as if only in this way could her father still be by her side.

"At that time... although she was sad, she was still very cheerful..."

Li Xiaoming wiped away his tears: "We...we used to be deskmates. We were very close..."

Teenage feelings quietly sprouted amidst the monotonous and oppressive school life.

Li Xiaoming once secretly liked this girl whose smile carried a hint of stubbornness and sadness, and even mustered up the courage to bring her breakfast.

Although Lin Wanwan would only smile politely each time, she would then quietly put the steamed buns he gave her into the table.

The tragedy was indeed triggered by a grilled sausage.

That day, Lin Wanwan was buying grilled sausages at the school gate after school to eat something, but she was caught red-handed by the vice principal who was patrolling at the gate.

This vice principal, who took it upon himself to "rectify the school's atmosphere and eliminate junk food," disregarded a young girl's dignity and snatched the sausage away, slamming it to the ground.

Not only that, he also stole Lin Wanwan's electric scooter keys and house keys, threatening, "See if I don't give them back and that's it!"

He believed that Lin Wanwan's decision to park her electric scooter at the school gate to buy grilled sausages would affect other people's travel during peak hours.

However, the surveillance footage shows that there were only the vice principal, Lin Wanwan, and the aunt selling grilled sausages at the scene, and there was no peak in the flow of people at all.

Lin Wanwan pleaded desperately, but she could not awaken the vice principal's compassionate heart as a teacher.

"Wanwan... she cried then..." Li Xiaoming choked up as he continued.
