Chapter 2314 Dirty Water

The promotion of the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" has sparked a surge of spiritual practice at Number 60 High School.

But as the saying goes, "the taller the tree, the more the wind blows," and soon, voices of doubt began to emerge from various dark corners.

"What independent idea? I think it's plagiarism."

During lunch break in the cafeteria, several monks who looked like students from another school gathered at a table, their voices neither too loud nor too soft, just loud enough for those around them to hear.

"A student from a regular class could research something like this? You're kidding me!"

"Exactly, it might have been dug out of some ancient book, and then repackaged as its own."

Rumors spread rapidly through the internet like a virus.

Anonymous technical analysis posts began appearing on campus forums, comparing the similarities between the Lingtai Qingming Mantra and some obscure ancient books word by word.

The post was full of sharp and inflammatory language.

Meanwhile, an extremely excited sound came from the cafeteria of Number 60 High School.

"That's utter bullshit! What kind of crap is this? How can this be considered plagiarism? Are those people online brainless?" Chen Chao scrolled through his phone, angrily showing the screen to Guo Hao.

"There's no way around it. It's common for people to create idols online and then destroy them. Some of them are brainless, but most are just people who can't stand seeing others do well. They want to drown others in their spittle and enjoy the feeling of trampling everyone who is better than them under their feet." Guo Hao frowned. "Moreover, these posts are appearing too frequently, as if someone is deliberately setting the pace."

Sun Rong's face was frosty: "Guo Hao is right. These people are best at stigmatizing a normal thing."

She didn't need to guess to know that it must have been those shadowy figures and the people behind them who were behind it.

The appearance of the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" disrupted these people's plans.

Wang Ling chewed his rice slowly, seemingly detached from the situation, but his spiritual awareness had already formed an invisible net, locking onto those instigators in Number 60 High School and the surrounding high schools whose auras resonated subtly with those of other "shadows."

Wang Muyu sat next to Sun Rong, his little face also puffed up with anger.

His pure dragon blood was particularly sensitive to this kind of malice, and he wished he could point out those bad guys on the spot.

"Wooden Fish, eat." Wang Ling spoke softly, his voice low but carrying a strange soothing power.

Wang Muyu said "Oh" and obediently lowered his head to eat his rice, but his little ears were still perked up, like radars.

The real storm struck that night.

An anonymous account claiming to be a "genius student from a top high school in the neighboring city" published a long article on a well-known cultivation forum, accusing Li Jun of "plagiarizing" his unpublished family secret technique in the core logical framework of "Lingtai Qingming Mantra", and attached several photos of seemingly old notes as evidence.

The article was written with great emotion, vividly portraying the grievances and anger of a "plagiarized genius".

The mastermind behind the scenes is clearly well-versed in the art of manipulating public opinion.

Afterwards, online trolls quickly joined in, and the topic was hyped up wildly.

The hashtags #Lingtai Qingming Mantra plagiarism# and #Li Jun of Number 60 High School academic misconduct# quickly became trending topics.

"They've come as expected." Sun Rong looked at the explosive public opinion on her phone screen with a cold gaze.

She immediately contacted the public relations and legal departments of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group: "Activate the emergency plan, collect evidence, and prepare a countersuit! Contact the school where the poster works to verify the situation!"

However, the other side acted even faster.

The next morning, as soon as Li Jun stepped out of his house, he was surrounded by a group of reporters who seemed to appear out of nowhere, their cameras practically aimed at his face.

"Li Jun, what do you have to say about the plagiarism accusations?"

"Did the inspiration for your 'Lingtai Qingming Mantra' really come from yourself?"

Did Huaguo Water Curtain Group participate in data fabrication?

Sharp questions rained down like cannonballs.

Li Jun had never seen anything like this before. His face turned deathly pale, his lips trembled, and he couldn't utter a single word.

He was just an ordinary high school student, and the sudden malice and attention almost crushed him.

Just then, a figure blocked Li Jun's way.

It's Wang Ying.

This was specially sent by Wang Ling.

At this moment, Wang Ying was wearing a hooded sweatshirt, a mask, and carrying a backpack, looking like a righteous monk who just happened to be passing by.

Although only half of her face was visible, her mere presence instantly silenced the noisy group of reporters.

"You're blocking my way, get the hell out of here!" Wang Ying's voice was as domineering as ever, and his sharp gaze seemed capable of killing people instantly.

He completely ignored the reporters' cameras and started yelling, "You believe everything people say? Are you professional journalists? Do you even know what the three elements of news are? You're taking pictures before anything's been settled, what the hell are you doing?"

The reporters instinctively took a step back.

Wang Ying has such a strong presence that many reporters silently turned off their cameras because of her ability to be so sarcastic and offensive in front of the camera.

Then, Wang Ying said no more, directly pulled up the almost frozen Li Jun, and walked straight through the crowd.

Sun Rong and Wang Muyu arrived just in time and helped Li Jun leave quickly.

"Sun Rong, who was that hero just now?" Back on the relatively safe campus, Li Jun finally came to his senses. He felt that the figure of the person just now looked somewhat familiar.

He's somewhat like Wang Ling, but their personalities are completely different...

He knew that Wang Ling's personality was similar to his own; they were both the kind of people who would treat saying even half a word more as if it were a matter of life and death.

"They're talking nonsense!" Wang Muyu's face turned red with anger. "Brother Li Jun's magic is flawless!"

Sun Rong patted Li Jun on the shoulder: "Don't be afraid, the innocent will clear themselves. The group is already handling it."

But the matter was far from over.

That afternoon, the Songhai City School Administration intervened and announced that due to a "property rights dispute" over the Lingtai Qingming Mantra, its promotion throughout the city would be temporarily suspended, and the Number 60 High School was required to submit a detailed research process report.

This was a deliberate plan by Wang Ling, allowing Zhuo Yi to issue the announcement directly in the name of the Director of the Hundred Schools Administration.

This is a strategic retreat, allowing online public opinion to temporarily subside due to official intervention, and then waiting for Sun Rong to use her group's resources to gather complete evidence before delivering a resounding slap in the face.

But the other party was clearly on guard.

An even more startling piece of news arrived.

The "genius student from the neighboring city" who accused Li Jun online was on his way to Songhai City to provide evidence when he encountered an "accident" and fell into a coma!

His family members' angry accusations suggest that Li Jun or his supporters deliberately attacked and retaliated against him!

Public opinion was in an uproar!

Conspiracy theories are rampant.

Li Jun went from being a role model of a comeback to a negative example of plagiarism and ruthlessness in an instant.

Even the reputation of Number 60 High School was affected.

Wang Ling had not expected that the other party would have such rich experience in "public opinion battles".

"Damn it! This is outrageous!" Chen Chao slammed his fist on the table in the classroom, furious. "That kid was definitely faking it! He's trying to scam us!"

Guo Hao remained calm: "Their plan is very meticulous. They stirred up public opinion, then applied pressure, and now they're using a self-inflicted injury ploy. It's all linked together. They're trying to completely ruin Li Jun and the 'Lingtai Qingming Mantra'."

The pressure poured down on Li Jun like a mountain.

He became taciturn, his eyes dimmed, and he even dared not walk alone on campus, for fear of seeing pointing eyes and hearing whispers.

As Wang Ling watched all this, a flicker of emotion finally crossed his dead fish eyes.

The other party's goal was not only to destroy Li Jun, but also to stifle the purification effect of the "Lingtai Qingming Mantra" and create space for the survival and activities of the "shadowy".

We can't wait any longer.

......

Just as the public opinion storm reached its peak, the Songhai City School Administration, under Wang Ling's secret instruction, issued an unexpected announcement.
