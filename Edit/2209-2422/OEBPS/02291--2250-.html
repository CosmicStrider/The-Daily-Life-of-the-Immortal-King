Chapter 2250 Let's Push Through!

Deep within the Netherworld Stone Forest, at the Bone Fortress, lies the core dungeon of the Demon-Sealing Forbidden Zone Array.

A foul, viscous aura of death and resentment swirled around the cold, white figure in the center.

Su Xingyue knelt on one knee, her "Dawn" spirit sword piercing the ground. The sword hummed violently, creating a stubborn white halo that barely shielded her and several students from the Holy Science Academy who were huddled together in fear behind her.

Besides fear, disbelief was also visible on everyone's face.

They were just a group of students at the Golden Core stage, and they had never seen such a dangerous enemy before... Although they had prepared to face those ferocious Earth Spirit Beasts before they came, no one told them that there were other forces that had been entrenched in the Earth's core world for a long time!

"What exactly happened..." Su Xingyue's resolute eyes revealed a hint of weakness.

Saint Cole's entire team fell out of the safe zone, and she couldn't think of any other reason besides someone doing it on purpose.

But who exactly is it?

The power of the Ten Thousand Bones Sealing Demon Domain Formation was like a millstone, constantly grinding down this last barrier.

Su Xingyue's adaptive psionic combat suit was already damaged, and her fair skin was covered with strange black patterns, which were traces of the underworld's erosion.

This means that her soul is beginning to be corrupted and will soon be eroded without treatment.

In the shadows of the dungeon, the cold eyes of Wan Shi's projection are concentrated.

It did not act personally, but merely used its supreme underworld authority to activate the demonic array constructed from countless pale skeletons.

Each ripple of the array was accompanied by the shrieks of the dead, drawing upon the life essence and spiritual power of the creatures within the array and continuously channeling it towards his projection.

"Saint Science Academy... Sword Cultivator, your sword heart is pure and unyielding. Yet, before the eternal Netherworld, it will ultimately turn to ashes."

The hollow sound of Wan Shi's projection echoed in the dungeon, each syllable like an ice cone striking Su Xingyue's will.

The pervasive resentment assaulted her mind, tainting her past pride, the honor of the academy, and her responsibilities as a team leader. These thoughts were corrupted, tearing at her consciousness and attempting to assimilate her into this hell of despair.

The excruciating pain that felt like her soul was being torn apart was constantly pushing her to her limits.

The students sobbed softly behind her, their fear having numbed them.

Their armbands had long been torn off, their symbols of identity representing the civilized world carelessly discarded in dark corners, left to be trampled upon by the underworld's soldiers.

Su Xingyue's breathing grew weaker and weaker, her consciousness like a candle flickering in the wind.

She knew that once her sword heart was completely lost, not only her, but also the innocent students behind her would be instantly transformed into the "vessels" that the Underworld Lord's projection spoke of.

Is this the end...?

A thought flashed through Su Xingyue's hazy consciousness.

no!

Absolutely not!

The glory of the Academy of Sciences must not be tarnished by the hands of the underworld!

Even if my body perishes and my soul vanishes, I cannot sit idly by and await my death!

Just as her consciousness was about to completely sink into despair, an indomitable sword intent from the very depths of her soul suddenly erupted!

"Dawn..."

With her last ounce of strength, Su Xingyue whispered the name of her beloved sword.

With difficulty, she raised her blood-stained right hand and placed it on the trembling hilt of the sword.

In an instant, she stopped resisting the deathly resentment that was eroding her will. Instead, she actively guided it, drawing it all into her already shattered sword heart essence!

"Sword Heart... Burning Soul!" The silent cry exploded in her heart.

The pure core of the sword seed within her body suddenly erupted with an extremely dazzling and destructive brilliance!

That was a final, poignant act, a sacrifice of life for a single, decisive strike!

The "Dawn" spirit sword instantly transformed into a molten white sun!

The overwhelming sword intent was no longer merely defensive; it soared into the sky with a resolute will to destroy everything in its path!

This is not an attack on the projection of eternity.

She was well aware of the vast difference between the two.

Instead, it launched its final attack on a crack in the Great Array of the Sealed Demon Domain, which originated from the sickle's slash before Fang Xing's awakening!

she......

It burned out...

laugh--!

A pure, unadulterated sword aura, imbued with all of Su Xingyue's sword comprehension, shot out suddenly from the core of the dungeon at a speed that surpassed the limits of space!

The sword energy easily tore through the prison walls of the array, pierced through layers of thick resentment fog, and pointed with unparalleled precision at... the location where Wang Zhen and Fang Xing were located outside the Great Array of the Desolate Domain, dozens of miles away!

The sword energy flashed and disappeared in an instant, leaving a trail in the air!

At the same time, outside the Bone Fortress, at the edge of the Demon-Sealing Domain Array.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing are facing relentless attacks.

Although Wan Shi's projection focused most of its power on refining Su Xingyue, the power of the array he controlled was still astonishing.

The skull sickle in Fang Xing's hand seemed to possess a life of its own, greedily devouring every bit of deathly resentment it came into contact with. The ancient runes on the blade grew brighter and brighter, emanating an aura that instilled instinctive fear in the creatures of the underworld.

Wang Zhen's movements appeared leisurely, but with each dodge or wave of his hand, large shards of bone would silently turn to dust wherever his gaze fell.

"We need to finish this quickly; those students' life force is rapidly weakening." Fang Xing endured the impact of a bone spear, his eyes sharp as knives.

Wang Zhen frowned slightly, as he also sensed the extremely unusual aura fluctuation emanating from the core of the array.

That was more like... the final glory of a powerful being's life coming to an end!

Right now!

laugh--!

The sword energy, imbued with determination and guidance, pierced through the thick barrier of resentment and exploded less than three feet in front of Wang Zhen!

The sword energy did not attack, but instead transformed into specks of pure light, outlining a spiritual coordinate pointing to a specific point deep within the core dungeon of the Bone Fortress!

The familiar scent contained within made Wang Zhen instantly recognize it!

"Su Xingyue! Sword Intent Burning Soul?!"

Wang Zhen's usually calm expression suddenly changed. Even he was moved by this way of burning the very essence of life just to transmit information.

The coordinates were scorching hot, filled with a tragic sense of sacrifice!

Fang Xing also caught the information and coordinates of the remaining sword energy.

A hint of fierceness and respect flashed in his eyes: "I didn't expect there to be such a tough nut to crack as Su Xingyue in the Academy of St. Science!"

"Let's push straight through."

Wang Zhen's eyes dimmed, leaving only these three words.

Neither he nor Fang Xing used their full strength, simply because they were worried that their excessively strong spiritual energy fluctuations might attract Teng Luchen's attention afterward.

But now, with lives at stake, they can't care about any of that.

If they push forward smoothly, Su Xingyue can still be saved.

As long as Wang Zhen replenishes his soul with the Qi of Heaven in time, he will be safe and sound.

At that moment, the skull sickle in Fang Xing's hand suddenly erupted with a terrifying dark light, as if it had been completely ignited by Su Xingyue's indomitable sword intent. The sickle let out a deep roar, "Open for me!"

"You're all going to die!" Wang Zhen said, holding nothing back.

His gaze, sharp as lightning, was fixed on the core coordinates indicated by the sword energy.

Wherever the gaze fell, the Demon-Sealing Domain Formation, constructed of countless white bones, collapsed suddenly from its core, like ice and snow thrown into the scorching sun!

The massive bone spikes that formed the array broke and shattered one by one, and the viscous barrier of resentment was annihilated inch by inch!

Within Wang Zhen's field of vision, an absolutely straight and clean passage was forcibly "seen" out, leading directly to the deepest dungeon of the Bone Fortress!

There was no explosion, no roar, only a grand and ruthless force of "erasure" at work!

The core node of the great formation was completely negated under Wang Zhen's will!

"Go!" Fang Xing seized this fleeting opportunity, his body transforming into a streak of light, following the passage opened by Wang Zhen's gaze, and rushing straight to the core of the dungeon!

Wang Zhen followed closely behind, moving even faster, his eyes filled with a chilling coldness.

Rumble—!

The passage completely destroyed the foundation of the Absolute Array, causing the entire Bone Fortress to shake violently, and a large number of the outer structural skeletons began to peel off and collapse.

Deep inside the dungeon.

The projection of Wan Shi was floating in the air, ready to completely absorb Su Xingyue's final resistance of Sword Heart Burning Soul.

However, the sight of Su Xingyue's soul-burning sword strike and Wang Zhen's penetrating gaze caused this projection of the Lord of the Underworld to feel a tremor originating from the level of rules!

"Who is it? How could they destroy my Netherworld Array like this?!"
