Chapter 2354 The Strategist Alliance's Interim Victory

Inside the Dream Esports Arena, the holographic projections had been turned off, but the air still seemed to retain the lingering smoke of Ziggs' bomb and the poisonous mist of Teemo's mushroom.

Chen Chao, his arm around Li Changzhe's neck, was beaming with excitement, his voice so loud it could lift the roof off: "See that! Changzhe! This is my brother! The god of ADCs! No! The god of everything as an ADC! Ziggs! Teemo! I wouldn't be surprised if your son picked Garen to play bot lane next time!"

Although Li Changzhe was a little breathless from being choked, his face was filled with admiration: "Brother Chao... I'm impressed! Completely impressed! Wang Ling's understanding of games is beyond human comprehension! I feel like I've wasted the last few years playing games!"

Zhou Rui silently took off his glasses and wiped them with the corner of his clothes, trying to calm the shock in his heart.

For Wang Ling, a player who is a tactical analyst, these two matches were a revolutionary experience.

This is no longer a matter of operation and consciousness, but a redefinition of the game rules themselves.

He secretly decided that when he got back, he would write "ADC Ziggs" and "Four-protect-one Teemo" into the secret tactics manual of Xuanming High School, even though he knew that without a monster like Wang Ling in charge, this tactic would most likely become an easy question.

Liu Kaichuan crossed his arms and looked at the miserable scene in the blue electric gaming cabin opposite him. He felt extremely relieved. The frustration he had felt when Wang Ling used the weapon master to crush him in the top lane was completely swept away, and he even felt a sense of pride.

He leaned closer to Wang Ling, lowered his voice, and said with a hint of flattery, "Pro, next time... next time let's duo queue again? I'll definitely hold my own in the top lane and be your dog!"

Wang Ling glanced at him, said nothing, and silently took out another packet of instant noodles from his school trouser pocket.

On Sun Rong's team's side, the atmosphere was as cold as ice.

Fujiwara Ren, Suzuki Haruto, Takahashi Tsuyoshi, and Saeki Kenta practically had to support each other as they walked out of the gaming cabin.

Their faces were pale and their eyes were unfocused, as if they hadn't just played two games, but had been thrown into another world and endured countless hardships.

Fujiwara Ren's aloof and noble image had vanished completely. His hair was disheveled, his lips were chapped, and he kept muttering in Taiyo Island language, "Mushrooms... mushrooms everywhere..."

Haruto Suzuki's bright smile disappeared, and his little tiger teeth seemed to lose their luster.

He clutched his head, squatted on the ground, his body trembling slightly: "I can't see him... He's right there... Why can't I see him..."

Takahashi Tsuyoshi, the dark-skinned athlete, was trembling uncontrollably with his muscular arms uttering, "Baka... Baka yarou..."

Kenta Saeki was somewhat calmer, but his face was still ashen. His pride in his protection and field of vision was a joke in the face of the ubiquitous mushroom array.

He glanced at his absent-minded companion beside him and said bitterly to Kyuhyun Ryoko, "Miss...we...have let you down."

Ryoko Kugimiya pressed her folding fan tightly against her forehead, feeling her temples throbbing.

The four-person "handsome man scheme" she carefully selected has not only failed to make Wang Ling feel threatened, but now it seems that it's questionable whether they can even overcome their own psychological trauma.

She took a deep breath, suppressing the urge to pack up these four embarrassing guys and stuff them back to Sun Island, and squeezed out a sentence through gritted teeth: "That's enough! We'll do the debriefing when we get back! Now, all of you, get your act together!"

Sun Rong stood aside, watching the four students from other schools who had arrived with such elegance but were now acting like madmen, her feelings extremely complicated.

Sympathy? A little...

But more than anything, it was a sweet feeling that even she couldn't suppress.

Her gaze involuntarily drifted to the figure across from her who was quietly munching on instant noodles.

Is he really that angry because I played games with other people?

This realization caused Sun Rong's heart to race uncontrollably, and two blushes rose to her cheeks.

She quickly lowered her head, pretending to straighten the not-so-messy hem of her clothes, afraid that someone would notice something amiss.

...

The monitoring room is located on the second floor of the esports arena.

"Pfft, hahaha—!" Wang Zhen, seeing the four Sun Island boys downstairs looking so distracted, finally couldn't help but slap his thigh and burst into laughter: "I can't take it anymore! Look at that dark-skinned guy, his hand is still shaking! Ling Zhenren's hand is absolutely deadly!"

Although Liu Qingyi still crossed her arms, a faint smile appeared on her lips: "You've improved somewhat. You know how to declare sovereignty in this way, although the method is as childish as a primary school student's."

Zhuo Yi leisurely sipped his spiritual tea, his eyes gleaming: "The effect is outstanding! My master may not have a clear understanding of it himself, but his actions have explained everything. This is a milestone victory for our strategist alliance."

He turned to the team of Sun Island psychology experts who were still arguing heatedly: "Gentlemen, how are the data records?"

The chief analyst pushed up his glasses, his face filled with almost frenzied excitement: "Mr. Zhuo Yi! The data is astonishing! Throughout the match, especially in the second round, the target's special attention to those four men can almost be defined as precise elimination in behavioral terms! And his success rate in avoiding collateral damage to Ms. Sun Rong is one hundred percent!"

Another female researcher added, "That's right! This further confirms our hypothesis that protecting Miss Sun Rong and driving away those who approach her brings him pleasure!"

"To put it simply..." Zhuo Yi concluded, "He's very happy to be jealous, but he's not even aware that he's jealous."

"That's right!" the experts said in unison.

"In that case..." Zhuo Yi put down his teacup, a knowing smile spreading across his face. "Our first phase of stimulation therapy has been a complete success. Now, we can move on to the second phase: creating an opportunity."

...

Downstairs, Ryoko Kumiya struggled to clean up the mess.

She walked to Sun Rong's side, gently nudged her with her folding fan, and transmitted her voice: "Rongrong, you've seen the results. Although the process was a bit... brutal, the goal was achieved. Next, according to the plan, you need to take the initiative and go thank Wang Ling, and also test his reaction."

Sun Rong's heart skipped a beat. She looked up at Kugimiya Ryoko, a hint of panic in her eyes: "Huh? Now...now? I go and thank him? What should I say?"

"Just say thank you for going easy on him and not making your data look too bad."

Ryoko Kumiya looked at him with a disappointed expression: "Try to act more naturally! Don't forget, we're trying to help him unlock his potential!"

Encouraged by her best friend, Sun Rong took a deep breath, mustered her courage, and walked towards Wang Ling's direction.

Her movement instantly drew the attention of everyone present.

Chen Chao immediately stopped shouting, nudged Li Changzhe and Zhou Rui beside him with his elbow, and made faces.

Liu Kaichuan also subconsciously straightened up, his eyes scanning back and forth between Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

Even the four boys from Sun Island, who were completely lost in their own world, seemed to be drawn in by this subtle atmosphere, temporarily snapping out of their mushroom hell and staring blankly over.

Wang Ling seemed to sense something and looked at Sun Rong, who was walking towards him.

The venue was so quiet that only the faint sound of the central air conditioning could be heard.

Sun Rong walked up to Wang Ling and felt her cheeks burning.

She tried to make her voice sound calm and natural: "Wang Ling, thank you for what you just said."

Wang Ling: "?"

He looked at Sun Rong with a hint of confusion in his eyes.

Sun Rong felt even more embarrassed under his gaze, and her voice lowered slightly: "It's just... thank you for that final ultimate move... and also, for not killing me..."

After saying that, Sun Rong felt like she was about to experience social death.

What on earth is this all about?!

Wang Ling paused for two seconds, seemingly processing the meaning of those words.

Then, as if he had thought of something, he looked down at the instant noodles in his hand, then at Sun Rong, and suddenly reached into his pocket and rummaged around for a bit.

The next moment, to everyone's astonishment, Wang Ling pulled out a brand new bag of braised beef flavored instant noodles from his seemingly ordinary school trouser pocket and handed it to Sun Rong.

"Here you go." His voice remained flat, without any inflection.

"......"

An eerie silence fell over the entire Dream Esports Arena.

Chen Chao's jaw almost dropped to the ground.

Li Changzhe and Zhou Rui looked at each other.

Liu Kaichuan rubbed his eyes vigorously.

In the monitoring room on the second floor, Wang Zhen spat out a mouthful of spiritual tea and coughed incessantly.

Zhuo Yi was taken aback for a moment, then chuckled and shook his head: "As expected of Master... this logic is beyond the reach of ordinary people."
