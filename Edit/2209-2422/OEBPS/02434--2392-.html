Chapter 2392 The Theater of Flames

The Fire Theatre is located in the East Zone of the park. Its appearance is modeled after an ancient monk's arena, but it is entirely constructed of translucent psionic crystals.

When Wang Ling and Sun Rong arrived at the theater entrance, there were still five minutes before the performance was scheduled to begin.

There was a queue at the ticket gate.

Staff members, dressed in uniforms with flame patterns, scanned each visitor's wristband with smiles.

"This way, please." A female guide with a high ponytail saw Wang Ling and Sun Rong, her eyes lit up slightly, but she quickly regained her professional smile: "The performance is about to begin, please take your seats as soon as possible."

She directed us to the seating area near the central aisle, which is one of the areas with the best views.

Sun Rong was somewhat surprised: "These are our seats?"

"Yes, all the experience participants' seats are arranged in advance," the guide explained with a smile. "Please enjoy the performance."

Wang Ling didn't speak, but simply glanced at the circular structure above the theater.

In his perception, the entire theater was shrouded in a complex psionic shield, presumably for the sake of special effects and safety.

The energy flow of the shield is very stable.

But in several inconspicuous corners, there were a few abrupt and abnormal fluctuations.

Not a staff member.

Its psychic nature is cold and sinister, carrying a faint but palpable killing intent.

Here we go again...

It seems that every time he and Sun Rong go to amusement park-like places, there are always some clueless people who like to bother them.

Wang Ling thought to himself, but his face remained expressionless.

He reached into his pocket, felt for the few packets of instant noodles he had left, and then followed Sun Rong into the seating area.

Their seats were in the middle of the fifth row, with two seats next to each other and the armrests could be lowered.

After Sun Rong sat down, she looked around curiously.

The interior of the theater appears larger than the exterior, featuring a circular, tiered design that can accommodate at least a thousand people.

The venue was about 50% full, not particularly packed, but the atmosphere was already very lively. Most of the attendees were short video bloggers who had been invited, and these people naturally liked to stir things up.

The stage is sunken and is currently covered by a light blue holographic projection, simulating a deep-sea scene with schools of fish swimming around.

"So beautiful..." Sun Rong exclaimed softly.

Wang Ling grunted in agreement and pulled a packet of instant noodles from his pocket.

He tore open the package and handed one piece to Sun Rong.

Sun Rong took it, ate it in small bites, her eyes still fixed on the stage: "What do you think the performance will be like?"

"I don't know," Wang Ling replied honestly.

He had no concept of this kind of performance.

The life of an immortal king consists of either cultivation, fighting, or eating instant noodles.

Wang Ling had no interest in performing arts, but for some reason, he felt unusually patient when he was with Sun Rong.

The two quietly shared their instant noodles, waiting for the performance to begin.

...

About thirty meters away from them, in the seventh row, near the edge.

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and Li Changzhe huddled furtively in their seats.

They had taken off the three glowing devil horns on their heads, but Chen Chao had somehow gotten three glow stick hats printed with "I love Disney," which were now flashing green.

"Brother Chao, aren't we too conspicuous like this?" Li Changzhe lowered his voice and pointed to his hat.

"What's so eye-catching about it!" Chen Chao said confidently. "Look around, lots of people are wearing these kinds of hats! We call it blending in!"

Guo Hao said warily, "Our seats are quite close to Wang Ling and Sun Rong... They may have already spotted us."

"That's called 'blind spot under the lamp,' and besides, so what if they find out?" Chen Chao said nonchalantly, "We're here to cheer him on! Look, fireworks, performances, romantic atmosphere... what a great opportunity!"

As he spoke, he took out a mini telescope from his small bag, adjusted the focus, and aimed it at the middle of the fifth row.

In the video, Wang Ling is putting the last piece of instant noodles into his mouth. Sun Rong turns her head and says something to him, and Wang Ling nods.

"They're talking! They're talking!" Chen Chao exclaimed excitedly. "Guo Hao, can you read lips? What are they saying?"

Guo Hao came over and glanced at it: "Sun Rong said the performance was about to start, and Wang Ling replied: 'Hmm.'"

"It really suits Reiko's style. Couldn't you have something more nutritious?" Chen Chao said disappointedly.

Li Changzhe also came over to see: "Sun Rong is smiling so happily..."

Indeed.

The stage lighting shone on Sun Rong's profile, her eyes sparkling, and a faint smile playing on her lips.

When she occasionally turns to look at Wang Ling, her smile softens.

Chen Chao stroked his chin: "Lingzi, that blockhead, has finally started to see the light. But he's still not proactive enough... Look, Sun Rong is so close to him, and all he does is eat instant noodles."

"Perhaps he's expressing his concern in a way that he's good at," Guo Hao said. "Sharing snacks is also a way of showing care."

"How can that be the same!" Chen Chao said with exasperation, "At this point, you should be offering a drink, a coat, or... well, never mind, it's already quite good that Lingzi is doing this."

As the three were whispering to each other, the lights in the theater suddenly went out.

The noise gradually subsided.

The performance has begun.

...

Meanwhile, on the second floor of the theater, in the VIP monitoring room.

Ryoko Kugimiya stood in front of the circular control console, where a dozen screens displayed real-time footage from different angles within the theater.

One of the screens was locked onto Wang Ling and Sun Rong, and the high-definition camera could even capture the trembling of Sun Rong's eyelashes.

On another screen were several figures marked with red circles.

"Confirmed." Wang Zhen's voice came through the communicator. He was disguised as a popcorn vendor, loitering in the corridor outside the theater. "There are six people in total, divided into three groups. One group is near the control panel on the west side of the second floor, disguised as technicians. Another group is near the emergency exit on the east side of the first floor, pretending to be a couple. And there's another group..."

He paused for a moment: "At the entrance to the equipment room below the stage, there are staff members wearing uniforms."

Ryoko Kunomiya tapped her palm lightly with her folding fan: "Where's the energy fluctuation?"

"They're all between the mid and late stages of the Golden Core realm." Liu Qingyi's indifferent voice joined the communication: "They're not ordinary assassins. Their modus operandi is very professional, and they have some technical skills; they communicate with each other using encrypted communication."

Zhuo Yi's voice came from another line: "Is there any reaction from Master's side?"

Ryoko Kunomiya looked at the main screen.

In the video, Wang Lingzheng calmly looks at the stage, holding an empty bag of crispy bread in his hand, slowly folding it into a small square.

Sun Rong was completely captivated by the performance, leaning slightly forward with her eyes glued to the screen.

"Rin looked very relaxed," Ryoko Kumiya said. "But when he came in just now, he looked up at those seats on the second floor. He must have noticed."

"That's good." Zhuo Yi said calmly, "Have Wang Zhen and Miss Liu continue to keep watch, but don't alert them. I want to see what these people are up to."

"Won't this be bad for Rongrong?" Ryoko Kugimiya asked with some concern.

"It's highly likely that the target is Junior Sister Sun," Zhuo Yi analyzed. "Luo Xinghe suffered a great loss at the hands of Master, but given his personality, he won't let it go easily. He doesn't dare to confront Master directly, so he might start with Junior Sister Sun."

Ryoko Kunomiya's eyes turned cold: "He wouldn't dare!"

"It's just speculation," Zhuo Yi said, "but it's never wrong to be prepared. And..."

He paused for a moment, then continued, "I always felt that these people's methods didn't seem like something Luo Xinghe could mobilize. Where would a streamer like him get resources of this caliber?"

Ryoko Kunomiya frowned: "You mean..."

"There might be someone else behind this." Zhuo Yi's voice was deep. "Let's watch the show first. With Master here, nothing will happen."

...

Inside the theater, the performance had entered its first climax.

"Phoenix Rebirth" tells the story of a fire phoenix's rebirth after experiencing hardships, blending fantasy special effects with modern stage technology. Originally, the performance was titled "Hakimi Dance," but it was canceled due to poor test results.
