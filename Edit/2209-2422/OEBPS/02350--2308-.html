Chapter 2308 The Road to Redemption

Wang Ling and Wang Ying worked together seamlessly, with Wang Ying's sharp tongue waking everyone up.

He instantly read everyone's memories, combined with his understanding of each person's background, and guided them to confront their inner demons with sharp yet incisive words.

Under Wang Ying's reprimand, Lin Feng finally admitted his despicable behavior to the "victim's mirror" and swore to turn himself in after he got out.

Encouraged, the petite girl repeatedly apologized to the wronged souls and promised to take good care of the deceased's family.

The bespectacled man vowed to work hard to earn money to cure his mother's illness, the construction worker promised to support his coworker's family, and the high school student cried as he promised to do his best to make amends to the neighbor he had wronged...

As individuals begin to muster the courage to confront their inner selves.

Although their images did not immediately disappear, their aggressiveness was greatly reduced, and their forms began to become unstable.

The sense of oppression in the entire mirror field suddenly decreased.

Wang Ling nodded to himself.

This level of intervention gave them a way out without interfering too much, allowing them to sincerely repent for their past "inner demons."

At the same time, the efficiency of [DedeCMS] in absorbing negative emotions also drops significantly at this moment.

...

Lingqi Technology Headquarters, Underground Monitoring Center.

"Team leader! Something's wrong! The Mirror of Inner Demons has gone out of control! The emotional fluctuations of multiple targets are stabilizing..." the technician said in disbelief.

The elderly group leader stared at the precipitous drop in the chart on the main screen, his face turning deathly pale.

He felt ice-cold all over, and a sense of utter powerlessness gripped him.

The scales of trust have been balanced, the rules of the bizarre Q&A have been altered, and now even the invincible mirror of inner demons has been collectively breached...

This is no longer a coincidence, nor a bug!

This is crushing!

He was certain that some unknown entity was manipulating their meticulously designed death game in ways they couldn't comprehend!

"Who exactly is it..." the old man murmured to himself.

He appeared to be having a mental breakdown, but in reality, he had run out of options.

After a long pause, the old man sighed and said, "Let's call it a day! We'll set up a new system!"

"Yes!" Several technicians responded in unison and began to operate according to the instructions.

...

Within the Mirror of Inner Demons.

Just as everyone was gradually regaining their composure...

The entire mirror space suddenly began to shake violently, and countless cracks appeared on all the mirror surfaces at the same time!

"What...what's wrong now?" The fat man, who had just escaped the danger of suffocation, looked around in fear.

"The space seems to be collapsing?"

Li Jing leaned against the trembling mirror, her face grave.

Wang Ling raised his head and sensed that the Lingqi Technology headquarters outside had abandoned this batch of tests and planned to reset the system.

"It seems the game has ended ahead of schedule," he said softly.

As soon as the words were spoken, countless mirrors shattered with a deafening roar!

The blinding white light engulfed everything.

Li Jing, Lin Feng, Fatty, Granny Zhang... everyone's consciousness was instantly thrown out, returning to their respective real worlds.

...

Inside a room in a residential complex in Songhai City.

Lin Feng suddenly sat up in bed, ripped off the Sleepyhead Mask, and was drenched in sweat, his heart pounding wildly.

Outside the window, the sky was just beginning to lighten.

He stared blankly at his hands. The sinful memory that had become clear in his dream was still imprinted in his mind, bringing him waves of pain.

But the difference was that the feeling of being swallowed by fear in the dream disappeared, and in its place was an unprecedented sense of ease.

He picked up the laptop from his bedside table, his fingers trembling, and began typing an email, confessing his past mistakes and outlining his remedial plan. He sent it to the industry association from back then and to the family of the colleague he had indirectly caused to die.

The moment he pressed the send button, he felt a huge weight lifted off his shoulders.

He decided to turn himself in after daybreak and take responsibility.

The same situation occurs in every corner of the city.

The fat man, still shaken, got up and immediately pulled out his phone to try and find contact information for the families of the victims of the mining accident years ago.

After learning the detailed address and the repayment method of the Cultivation Bank, he transferred one by one considerable sum of spirit stones to the account of the family of the victim of the mining accident many years ago, along with an anonymous letter of repentance.

He didn't know how much he could make up for what he was doing.

But at least it can bring him some psychological comfort.

Grandma Zhang sat on the edge of the bed, tears streaming down her face, but her eyes were no longer a lifeless gray.

She has contacted a professional funeral home to prepare a real funeral for her "puppet son" so that he can be laid to rest.

Li Jing then picked up the communication device, preparing to contact the industry association from back then and confess everything.

A petite girl, a man with glasses, a construction worker, a high school student...

Everyone woke up, carrying the memories of their dreams and a promise they had to keep.

At the same time, Yan Ting, who was possessed by Wang Ling, was also redeemed.

Although he was constantly possessed, Wang Ling still retained all the memories he experienced in the dream, only the parts that were separated from himself and Wang Ying.

Afterwards, the other dream participants also gradually found Yan Ting's contact information.

To them, Yan Ting was the benefactor who helped them escape this "nightmare," so these elites from all walks of life were able to do their part in Yan Ting's search for evidence.

They may never be forgiven, but this seemingly terrifying "mirror of inner demons" experience inadvertently gave them a chance to start over.

...

The next day, early morning at Number 60 High School.

Li Jun, a student in the regular class of the first year of high school, arrived at the classroom early.

He didn't immediately start studying as usual, but sat in his seat with an unprecedented clarity in his eyes.

Li Jun's fingers unconsciously rubbed his empty wrist.

Thinking back to when I wore the "Taste Master".

Now, his wrists are empty.

He slept unusually soundly last night, without relying on any devices.

Although it's not like what other students said, that wearing the "Sleep Treasure" will give you all sorts of beautiful dreams that meet your expectations.

But when he woke up, his mind was clear and his spiritual energy was flowing smoothly.

This feeling of being grounded gave him a strange sense of peace.

"Good morning, Jun-ge!" A classmate who usually doesn't talk much actually greeted him, with a hint of curiosity in his eyes.

Li Jun was stunned for a moment before realizing that he was being called.

He nodded somewhat awkwardly: "Um... good morning to you too."
