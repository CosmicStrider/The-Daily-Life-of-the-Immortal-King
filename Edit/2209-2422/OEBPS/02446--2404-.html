Chapter 2404 Appointment on the Ferris Wheel

The Ferris wheel area was indeed not crowded.

Most of the people riding the Ferris wheel at night are couples; the queue isn't long, and it's their turn soon.

The staff opened the sedan chair door and smiled, "Please come in, gentlemen. One round trip takes about twenty minutes. The sedan chair has panoramic windows that offer a view of the entire park at night."

Chen Chao was the first to squeeze in, and then "consciously" sat down in a seat near the door: "I'll sit here! Lingzi, Sun Rong, you two sit across from me, the view is better from there!"

Wang Ling and Sun Rong sat down opposite each other.

The sedan chair doors closed, and it slowly ascended.

Soft lighting illuminated the spacious interior of the sedan chair, where the seats were upholstered in soft leather. There was also a small table in front of the chair with bottled water and tissues on it.

The park's night view gradually unfolds through the surrounding glass.

The roller coaster track was illuminated by blue light stripes, like a giant glowing dragon coiling in the air.

The castle's spires gleamed with golden light.

The spiritual plants on the small island in the middle of the lake sprinkled specks of light, which were reflected on the lake surface as a dreamlike sea of stars.

The city lights in the distance stretch out in a continuous line, like a scattered galaxy.

"So beautiful..." Sun Rong pressed her face against the glass, her eyes unblinking.

Wang Ling also looked out the window.

It is indeed quite beautiful.

He had actually seen even more magnificent scenery.

But those places don't have this kind of lively atmosphere.

"Wang Ling," Sun Rong suddenly turned her head, "Are you happy today?"

Wang Ling looked at her.

The girl's eyes shone brightly, like they were filled with stars, in the soft light of the sedan chair.

He thought for a moment and nodded: "Mm."

"That's good." Sun Rong smiled and turned back to look at the night view.

But her heart was beating very fast.

In the quiet sedan chair, it was almost audible.

Chen Chao sat opposite, pretending to admire the scenery, but actually eavesdropping.

However, after waiting for a long time, neither of them said anything.

Chen Chao is in a hurry.

What a wonderful atmosphere! The enclosed space is so inviting! The night view is so romantic!

Reiko, say something!

Even just asking Sun Rong if she's cold would be fine!

Just as Chen Chao was about to give his "helping hand," Wang Ling suddenly spoke up.

"Sun Rong".

"Hmm?" Sun Rong immediately turned her head.

Wang Ling took the crystal with heartfelt wishes out of his pocket.

It's the one I got yesterday at the "Heart Image Corridor," which contains a picture of the two of them standing in front of a mirror.

The crystal gleamed with a warm glow under the light.

"Here you go." Wang Ling handed it over.

Sun Rong was stunned.

She looked at the crystal, then at Wang Ling, and was momentarily speechless.

"This...this isn't yours?" she whispered.

Wang Ling shook his head: "You take it."

He didn't actually know why he wanted to give it to Sun Rong.

I just feel that this crystal is more suitable for her than for me.

Sun Rong reached out and carefully took the crystal.

When their fingertips touched Wang Ling's palm, both of their hands paused slightly.

The seal started getting hot again.

Sun Rong gripped the crystal tightly, feeling the slight warmth emanating from it, and blushed.

"Thank you... thank you," she said softly.

Chen Chao, watching from the opposite side, was so excited he almost jumped up.

A gift! A token of love!

Although it's just a souvenir crystal, it was given to me by Reiko on her own initiative!

Major breakthrough!

He quickly looked down at his phone, pretending he didn't exist.

The sedan chair continued to rise slowly until it reached its highest point.

From here, the entire Disneyland is spread out before you, a dazzling display of lights, like a dream.

The distant city lights and the nearby neon lights of the amusement park intertwine to create a vibrant yet warm scene.

Sun Rong held the crystal and said softly, "I will keep it safe."

Wang Ling grunted in response.

...

Twenty minutes passed quickly.

The Ferris wheel slowly descended, and the cabin doors opened.

It was 8:20 p.m. when the three of them stepped out of the sedan chair.

"What's next?" Chen Chao asked. "Go back to the hotel? Or do we look around some more?"

Wang Ling glanced at the bracelet.

The challenge is complete, the gift box is received, the fireworks are watched, and the Ferris wheel is ridden.

It seems... nothing's wrong anymore.

"Let's go back to the hotel," Sun Rong said. "I've walked a lot today and I'm a little tired."

Actually, she wasn't tired.

But she felt that today was beautiful enough.

If we become greedy, we might ruin this beautiful thing.

"Okay!" Chen Chao nodded. "Let's go back to the hotel! Get some rest, we can still have half a day of fun tomorrow!"

The three of them walked towards the hotel.

The park was much quieter at night than during the day, with most visitors having already left, and only a few couples still strolling around.

The streetlights cast long shadows.

Sun Rong and Wang Ling walked side by side, with about twenty centimeters between them.

Chen Chao tactfully walked ahead, humming an off-key song.

"Wang Ling," Sun Rong suddenly whispered.

"Um?"

"Are you going back tomorrow?"

"I'll be back this afternoon."

"Oh..." Sun Rong paused for a moment, "Then we can play a little longer this morning."

Wang Ling nodded: "Mm."

"Next time..." Sun Rong mustered her courage: "Next time, if there's a newly opened amusement park, let's go together again, okay?"

Wang Ling turned his head to look at her.

The girl lowered her head, her ears slightly red, and her fingers unconsciously twisted the hem of her clothes.

He thought for a moment and said, "Okay."

Sun Rong looked up, her eyes lighting up: "Really?"

"Um."

"It's a deal!" Sun Rong smiled, her eyes curving into crescents.

Chen Chao, who was walking ahead, was overjoyed when he heard this conversation.

Next time! See you next time!

This trend is stable!

...

It was already 9 p.m. when we got back to the hotel.

In the lobby, Xiao Linyou, Liu Kaichuan and others were playing cards together. When they saw Wang Ling and the other two return, they immediately surrounded them.

"Lingzi! Where's the gift box? Let us see it!" Liu Kaichuan's eyes lit up.

Wang Ling casually took the gift box out of his storage bag, placed it on the coffee table in the lobby, and opened it.

Five layers of crispy noodles were displayed in front of everyone.

"Wow..." Kobayashi Yu swallowed hard. "How long will this take to eat?"

Wang Ling generously took out more than a dozen packets and distributed them to the students present.

"Thank you, Brother Ling!"

"Your son is truly magnificent!"

The boys cheered and each chose their favorite flavor.

Sun Rong also grabbed a small cheese-flavored packet, smiling and saying, "I'll get some too."

Just then, Ryoko Kumiya stepped out of the elevator, a smile flashing in her eyes: "Thank you all for your hard work on this event. The park will be open for half a day tomorrow morning, so you can all do whatever you want. There will be a farewell banquet at the restaurant at noon, and after lunch, the bus will take you back to school."

"Great!" Chen Chao raised his hand: "Nine Palaces, remember to call us again next time there's an event like this!"

"Definitely." Ryoko Kugimiya smiled, her gaze sweeping over Wang Ling and Sun Rong, hinting, "Perhaps... there will be a next time soon."

Sun Rong blushed again.

Wang Ling remained calm and took out another packet of instant noodles, tearing it open.

For him, the trip to the amusement park was very successful.
