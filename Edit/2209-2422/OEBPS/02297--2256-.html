Chapter 2256 The Wrath of the Glass

Inside the underground cave, the artificial light of dawn tried to create tranquility, but could not dispel the lingering tension in the air.

The mess in the warehouse area has been quickly cleaned up by the logistics team, and the solidified colored glass shards have been carefully collected and sealed.

Guo Hao patted Chen Chao on the shoulder, spitting as he described how the "fake Chen Chao" screamed in agony before Fang Xing exposed and purified him, causing Chen Chao to grimace and curse the Liuli Clan for their lack of martial ethics.

"Damn it, these monsters from the Earth's core are even more hellish than Old Man Teng's stress test!" Chen Chao said indignantly, taking a glass of Jade Heart Juice from Guo Hao to calm his nerves.

"Exactly! It's all thanks to Sun Rong's sword aura mark and Fang Xing's sharp eyes!"

Guo Hao nodded with lingering fear, his gaze sweeping over Wang Ling, who was quietly eating noodles in the corner. His tone was filled with relief, "Lingzi, you're a lucky star too. Luckily you stayed here and didn't run around, otherwise those monsters might have turned into a fake version of you to fool us!"

Wang Ling's hand, which was holding a piece of "Hellfire" spicy instant noodles, paused almost imperceptibly. He glanced at Guo Hao with his dead fish eyes, then continued to focus intently on putting the crumbs into his mouth.

The sound of chewing, in the somewhat noisy cave, had an eerie sense of calm.

Sun Rong stood a little distance away, talking quietly with Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe.

She maintained a calm expression, but the corner of her eye never left the figure in the core area.

Only after confirming that he was safe and sound, without even a wrinkle on his clothes, did I finally feel completely relieved.

But... thinking about how he devoured those glass shards containing malevolent energy without batting an eye, a mix of worry and amusement welled up inside him again.

Is he really okay?

That rainbow-colored light...

A barely perceptible hum emanated from the Aohai Sword Spirit deep within his dantian. (Sun)

Rong's heart skipped a beat, and she immediately focused her mind and looked inward.

The core world of the Spirit Sword remained a brilliant azure, but deep within the core, a tiny, seven-colored thread seemed to flash by, instantly engulfed by the pure sword energy of the Ao Hai itself, and completely purified.

Is it an illusion?

Or perhaps after devouring the core energy of the Glazed Clan, Ao Hai also absorbed and transformed a trace of it?

Sun Rong couldn't be sure, but Ao Hai himself didn't give any unusual feedback, and even seemed to have a faint sense of satisfaction.

She could only suppress her doubts for the time being.

"Sun Rong!" Zhang Linyan's cold voice pulled her back to reality: "These Glass Clan members' ability to mimic is far beyond our expectations, and they are clearly targeting us. How should we defend ourselves? Will they come back again?"

As a seasoned elite, she knew all too well how terrifying an enemy who could perfectly mimic her state could be.

Sun Rong took a deep breath, her eyes regaining their sharpness: "Their goal is not just replacement, but more likely a deeper conspiracy. After the attack just now, they should know that we are on guard. But precisely because of this, the next attack may be even more fierce and direct."

She looked at Ryoko Kunomiya: "Ryoko, reinforce the barrier, expand the alert range outward by three miles, and set the energy threshold to the highest level! Any unmarked or abnormally energetic individual that approaches will immediately trigger the highest level alarm!"

"Understood!" Ryoko Kugimiya immediately operated the array plate. The pale golden light screen lit up slightly, and the invisible barrier extended outward, becoming more solid.

"Sister Qingyi, Brother Fangxing." Sun Rong turned to the core of the support team: "Please expand the patrol area, using the cave as the center, and explore outwards. Pay close attention to any abnormal spatial fluctuations."

Liu Qingyi, who was standing with her hands on her hips, staring at Su Xingyue who was whispering with Wang Zhen not far away, immediately snapped out of her daze upon hearing this and flicked her ponytail: "Fine! I'm good at catching bugs!"

A faint aura of divine power swirled around her, clearly indicating that she had entered battle mode.

Fang Xing nodded silently, gripping the skull sickle tightly, the black gem at the end of the hilt gleaming faintly.

He had just carefully examined the glass fragments. The thorough removal of the core, leaving not even a trace, made him more clearly aware of the power of the "mascot" deep within the cave.

"All logistics team members are not permitted to leave the cave's protective barrier unless absolutely necessary! Resource exploration is suspended!"

Sun Rong's voice carried an undeniable determination, clearly reaching everyone's ears.

A calm and composed aura belonging to a leader quietly spread out, instantly giving the somewhat bewildered crowd a sense of security.

"Yes!" everyone responded in unison, and the atmosphere inside the cave became tense.

Wang Zhen stretched lazily, walked over to Wang Ling, and leaned casually against the workbench: "Tsk, looks like the Earth Core vacation is going to end early? It's been quite a commotion."

His voice was not loud; only Wang Ling, Sun Rong, and Fang Xing, who were nearby, could hear him clearly.

Wang Ling's gaze finally shifted from the eternal, dim light of the mineral vein outside the window to Wang Zhen's face.

He said nothing, his eyes indifferent.

But Sun Rong keenly noticed that the moment Wang Ling looked away, the last trace of iridescent light deep in his eyes seemed to flicker slightly.

It wasn't a mistake after all! Sun Rong's heart jumped again.

Wang Zhen seemed to have received some kind of confirmation, a playful smile curving his lips, and said no more.

Just as the cave was being heavily fortified—

Within the Earth's core, a spatial passage corresponding to the "Ink Abyss·Glassy Continent" was opened.

The deathly silence and abyss of nothingness have been completely shattered.

On the surface of that continent, which is made up of solidified stardust, billions of deep holes spew forth torrents of colorful energy!

These torrents converged in the air and instantly transformed into legions made entirely of flowing glass!

They no longer need to imitate any form; their very essence is the weapon.

The sky was torn open by countless enormous spatial rifts.

Beyond the crack were countless pairs of cold, glassy eyes, densely packed above the abyss, all locked in the same direction!

Buzz—! !!! !

The indescribable, terrifying energy fluctuations were like the first explosion at the beginning of the universe, yet eerily silent.

A destructive torrent composed of pure seven-colored glazed energy instantly broke through the constraints of space!

It was neither light nor matter, but the manifestation of rules, carrying the absolute will to annihilate all non-glass existences. Ignoring the thick strata, the chaotic spiritual energy field, and even time, it descended with unparalleled precision toward the coordinates of Wang Ling's cave!

Inside the underground cave.

Warning! Warning! Warning!

Ryoko Kunomiya's array plate suddenly lit up with a dazzling scarlet light!

A piercing alarm, sharp enough to tear the soul apart, resounded throughout the entire cave!

On the array screen, the outer area representing the warning barrier was instantly completely covered by a massive amount of destructive energy!

The seven-colored light spot expanded rapidly like a tsunami, and was less than three breaths away from zero!

"Enemy attack!!" Ryoko Kumiya screamed, her voice distorted with extreme horror: "An incalculable level of energy! A comprehensive strike! The range... is too vast!!!"

Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe's expressions changed drastically, and they instantly unleashed their strongest protective spiritual light!

Su Xingyue gripped "Fuxiao" tightly, the sword humming as a warning.

"What?!" Liu Qingyi suddenly raised her head, her eyes blazing with purple light! The skull sickle in Fang Xing's hand let out a hungry and ferocious roar!

The smiles on Chen Chao and Guo Hao's faces froze instantly. A huge fear gripped their hearts like a cold, giant hand, and they couldn't even breathe!

They had never felt such terrifying and despairing energy pressure, as if the entire Earth's core was about to be turned to dust by this attack!

"It's over..." Guo Hao murmured, his face as white as paper.

Chen Chao opened his mouth, but could not make a sound; only instinctive trembling remained.

Sun Rong's pupils contracted sharply! The Ao Hai sword sheath vibrated wildly at her wrist, and a burst of azure light erupted in response, instantly forming a flowing, water-patterned circular shield in front of her!
