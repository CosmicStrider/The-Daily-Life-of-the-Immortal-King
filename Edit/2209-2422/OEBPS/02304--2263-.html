Chapter 2263 Thousand Caves City

The migrating caravan moved silently through the dark and winding passageways.

After enduring the frenzied attack of the subterranean creatures and the almost miraculous power of the red lotus petals in Sun Rong's hand, the atmosphere became as heavy as solidified magma.

Everyone gripped their magical implements tightly, projecting their divine senses to the maximum extent, vigilant against any danger that might lurk from the darkness.

Ryoko Kugimiya's array plate emitted a steady, faint light, providing illumination and constantly scanning the psionic environment and spatial structure in front of her.

"The passageway is becoming more complex, with more branching paths."

Ryoko Kugimiya's voice was deep as her fingertips moved rapidly across the array plate, outlining the maze-like energy diagram ahead: "The psychic energy turbulence index is rising, which is very disruptive to divine sense detection."

Wang Zhen leaned over and glanced at it, then clicked his tongue: "Tsk, this place is really winding and twisting, like entering an anthill. Fang Xing, can you still smell the scent of home?"

He was referring to the entrance to the Thousand Caves City that was mentioned earlier.

Fang Xing's hood twitched slightly, as if sensing something. After a moment, he replied succinctly: "The direction is right. Distance unknown. Something is interfering with my sense of smell."

His "sense of smell" is not literal, but rather an extraordinary perception of energy traces and spatial fluctuations.

Holding a red lotus petal that shone with a restrained yet gentle light, Sun Rong walked at the front of the group.

The Ohai hummed softly on her wrist, and azure light rippled outwards from her like waves, merging with the power of the petals to form a strange calming force field that seemed to soothe the restless psionic turbulence around her.

She could feel that both the sea and the petals longed to get closer to the pulse of the earth, an instinct of mutual attraction between those of the same origin.

Wang Ling remained in the middle of the line, walking steadily, looking out of place among the tense classmates around him.

He somehow pulled out a bag of snacks again. This time it wasn't instant noodles, but a kind of crispy mineral salt chip similar to potato chips, which was found in the Earth's core. He was putting one chip after another into his mouth, making a soft "crunch" sound.

Um......

It's still not as good as instant noodles.

Wang Ling's inner evaluation.

The sound was unusually clear in the silent corridor, prompting Chen Chao to turn around and glance back. He muttered to Guo Hao, "Lingzi's mental fortitude... I'm truly impressed. Do you think he doesn't even know the word 'fear'?"

Guo Hao rolled his eyes: "If he knew, would he still be called a mascot? But then again, with Lingzi snapping photos right there, I'm actually not so panicked anymore..."

This is probably the magical effect of Wang Ling as a mascot.

It always brings peace of mind.

Hearing the "click" sound behind her, and looking at the backs of Wang Zhen and Fang Xing walking side by side in front of her, Liu Qingyi couldn't help but send a telepathic message to Wang Zhen: "Hey, that Su Xingyue, is she no longer giving you the eye?"

Wang Zhen stumbled, nearly tripping over the loose stones beneath her feet, and replied irritably via telepathy, "Grandma, what time is it? Are we running for our lives? Can't you focus on something more important? Besides, Captain Su is very polite and courteous; he's not like what you're thinking!"

"Hmph! It'd better not be!" Liu Qingyi snorted, but her gaze still sharply swept over Su Xingyue, who was covering the rear.

Ever since she saw Wang Zhen being so affectionate with Su Xingyue that day, Liu Qingyi had become wary.

Su Xingyue seemed to sense something and returned a calm but slightly questioning look, and Liu Qingyi immediately looked away as if nothing had happened.

Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe flanked the team, one on each side. Li Changzhe's Wanlin Armor was ready to be unleashed at any moment, while Zhang Linyan's longbow was half-drawn, arrows barely nocked, ready to deliver a fatal blow at any time.

Just as the procession passed through a particularly narrow crevice and entered a slightly wider cave—

Waaaah!

That deep, mournful bugle call, seemingly emanating from the very heart of the earth, rang out once again without warning!

This time, the voice seemed closer, bringing not just a simple summons, but also a hint of anger and anxiety.

"Here we go again!" Chen Chao's face turned pale, and he almost covered his ears.

After the horn sounded, the anticipated surge of subterranean creatures did not immediately materialize.

However, an even more chilling feeling crept into everyone's minds.

In the darkness, it seemed as if countless eyes were peering in.

They are no longer as frenzied and aggressive as before, but have become more cunning and elusive, and full of malice.

"They're still following us."

Fang Xing suddenly stopped, pointing his skull sickle at a dense shadow behind him: "There are many of them. But they haven't gotten any closer."

Ryoko Kunomiya immediately aimed the array plate in that direction, but the screen only displayed a chaotic energy clutter, unable to lock onto a specific target.

"Something is helping them hide."

Ryoko Kugimiya's face darkened: "This kind of concealment... is very clever."

Wang Zhen's smile vanished completely, his eyes sharp as a hawk's: "It's not an ability that subterranean creatures can possess on their own. There must be a big guy directing them, or... giving them some kind of blessing."

The "big guy" he mentioned undoubtedly refers to the Outer God or the will of Bai Zhe.

Sun Rong felt a slight tremor emanating from Ao Hai.

It's not a warning of danger, but rather like analysis and identification?

She quietly channeled a wisp of her divine consciousness into the core of the Ocean Sword, using the golden-red flame patterns as a means of communication.

Her perception seemed to penetrate layers of concealment, vaguely glimpsing some scenes—

In the distant darkness behind the column, some subterranean creatures were entwined with extremely faint, black, viscous energy threads.

These threads extend into the endless darkness, their other end connected to a chilling presence.

These threads provided the subterranean creatures with concealment and a degree of intelligence.

What alarmed her even more was that among the subterranean creatures entangled in threads, there would occasionally be one or two eerie figures whose movements were slightly stiff, but whose eyes were more human-like than those of the other creatures!

The shapes of those figures vaguely resembled the distorted state of the Glass Clan after their simulation failed!

They did not attempt to attack, but simply mingled among the subterranean biota, coldly watching the migrating caravan.

Sun Rong abruptly withdrew her consciousness, a cold sweat breaking out on her back.

"What's up?"

Her subtle emotional fluctuations immediately caught the attention of Wang Ling, who was standing beside her.

He stopped eating his snack and looked at her.

"They...don't just follow."

Sun Rong's voice was dry and low, barely audible to those around her: "The Glass Clan... are mixed in there. And... those black threads are controlling them."

Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, and Liu Qingyi's expressions instantly darkened.

Upon hearing this, Wang Ling stared into the darkness behind him with dead fish eyes, and the faint, iridescent light in the depths of his eyes flickered for a moment.

Then, calm was restored.

He didn't say anything, but simply handed the bag of mineral salt flakes to Sun Rong.

Sun Rong was stunned for a moment, then subconsciously took the bag. When her fingertips touched the bag, a barely perceptible coolness flowed into her body, instantly calming the fear in her heart.

He found out.

Moreover, he didn't care.

Sun Rong took a deep breath and calmed herself down again.

As long as he's there, the sky won't fall.

"Keep moving forward."

Wang Zhen made a decisive move: "Speed up! Liangzi, find a stable path first, avoid dead ends! Fang Xing, if you've lost your sense of smell, rely on your intuition! Sun Rong, hold the petals steady! They're the pass to the center of the earth!"

The team started moving again, and their speed increased significantly.

The constant feeling of being watched from behind creates intense psychological pressure, as if a sharp blade could stab you from behind at any moment.

After struggling for about halfway to the center of the earth, they encountered several minor forks in the road and brief encounters.

The passage ahead suddenly began to slope downwards, and the smell of sulfur in the air was gradually replaced by a dry, dusty scent.

Fang Xing suddenly raised his head, two beams of light shooting out from under his hood: "I can sense it. There's a huge void ahead. Many entrances to passages. The energy is mixed, but... there's no crazy malice."
