Chapter 2321 The Death Rules of the Deep Mountain Pill Garden

Zhou Ping huddled in the corner of the hard bed, wrapped himself tightly in a tattered quilt he had found somewhere, his body still trembling slightly uncontrollably.

He almost habitually tucked the blanket tightly under his feet, as if afraid that some ghostly hand might reach out from under the bed and grab his ankles.

It was already night, but Zhou Ping dared not close his eyes at all.

The moment he closed his eyes, all he could see was Elder Xuanse's withered face hidden in the shadows. He even doubted whether Elder Xuanse was human at all.

"Brother 999... are you asleep?" Zhou Ping's voice trembled and trembled with sobs.

Wang Lingze sat against the foot of another bed, eyes closed, breathing steadily, as if he had truly fallen asleep.

But in reality, his consciousness had already searched the entire "Deep Mountain Elixir Garden" several times.

In his perception, a deep resentment and deathly aura lingered beneath the buildings.

Those abandoned medicine fields, buried beneath the soil, do not contain the roots and stems of spiritual plants, but rather skeletons that have long since lost their vitality, some of which are small, resembling children.

Inside the abandoned alchemy room, broken vessels were stained with dark red grime, and faint wails could be heard emanating from them.

"The Rejuvenation Pill..." Wang Ling murmured to himself.

He felt that the task itself was a blatant trap.

The so-called "assistance to the headmaster in training" is more like a lure to tempt them to break the rules.

His spiritual senses focused on the direction where Elder Xuanse had disappeared, and on the area deep within the Dan Garden that was shrouded in even denser darkness.

That should be where the "Headmaster" is located, and it is also the core where all the resentment in the entire Dan Garden gathers.

...

The next day, the sky was not yet fully bright, maintaining a depressing tone.

People from other rooms also came out one after another, all with pale faces and bloodshot eyes, clearly having not rested well.

A pervasive sense of fear grips everyone's heart.

"Are you all awake?" The burly man in black robes who had suggested sharing a room yesterday, numbered Black 112, spoke in a hoarse voice, trying to rally the group.

"We can't just sit here and wait to die. The mission deadline is only five days, and we must start today. I suggest we split up and explore, focusing on finding the recipe for the Rejuvenation Pill in the Scripture Pavilion, and also see if we can find any useful materials or clues. We must return here before sunset to gather and share information."

The proposal was agreed upon by the majority of people.

When faced with unknown dangers, sticking together and gathering information are instinctive behaviors.

Lin Susu stood with her arms crossed, watching coldly. After they had finished discussing, she scoffed, "Do as you please. I prefer to act alone."

After saying that, without waiting for anyone's reaction, she chose a path in the opposite direction from the Sutra Repository and left quickly, her figure soon disappearing into the depths of the overgrown courtyard.

"Damn it, what are you so arrogant about!"

Black 112 muttered a curse under his breath, but there was nothing he could do.

"Let's set off too," Black 112 said to the rest of the group, glancing at Wang Ling and Zhou Ping. "You two, follow our group toward the Scripture Pavilion. You can look out for each other."

Zhou Ping felt as if he had been granted a pardon, and nodded hurriedly, following closely beside Wang Ling.

Wang Ling remained silent, only nodding slightly, playing the role of a taciturn and dull newcomer.

The Dan Garden covers a vast area, with most of its pavilions and towers in ruins and its paths being complex.

The strange smell of medicine was everywhere in the air. Occasionally, rustling sounds or the shadow of something quickly passing by came from the depths of the ruins, making everyone nervous.

The Sutra Repository is a three-story wooden building, equally dilapidated, with its plaque crooked and covered in cobwebs.

The interior was dimly lit, with many bookshelves collapsed on the ground and no one taking care of them. Jade slips, bamboo slips, and paper books were scattered all over the floor, most of them covered with a thick layer of dust, and many were already rotten.

"How...how do we find this?" a girl in gray asked, her voice trembling with tears.

"Find the scriptures related to alchemy, especially those related to the soul and revival!" Black 112 said in a deep voice. "Be quick! Be careful not to make too much noise or damage anything here."

He remembered Elder Xuanse's rules; although he didn't know the specific consequences of breaking them, no one wanted to try.

Everyone began searching intently.

Wang Ling also pretended to pick up a few bamboo slips and look at them. They contained some basic identification diagrams of spiritual plants, nothing special.

His consciousness had already scanned the entire library.

There were indeed some ancient texts here that recorded unorthodox elixirs, but he didn't point them out; he simply noted their locations silently.

He noticed that although Lin Susu said she would act alone, the direction she left vaguely pointed to the back mountain of Danyuan, where there seemed to be an area hidden by an array, whose aura was both connected to and somewhat different from the main body of Danyuan.

During the exploration, Wang Ling noticed some details:

First, under some of the collapsed bookshelves lay not books, but the tiny finger bones of some kind of creature.

Secondly, on a mottled wall, there was a distorted pattern painted with dried dark red liquid, exuding an ominous aura.

Finally, there were signs that the dust in a corner had been disturbed, but no valuable items were found nearby.

Wang Ling guessed that someone must have already searched this place and secretly hidden it.

...

Time flew by, and the gray sky began to deepen, foreshadowing the approaching sunset.

"It's getting late, we have to go back!" Black 112 looked at the sky and gave the order decisively.

With some or more spoils, the group hurried back to their residential area.

Lin Susu returned almost at the last glimmer of dawn.

Her face remained cold, but a faint glint of something unusual seemed to flash deep in her eyes, and there was still a trace of fresh dirt on her cuffs.

But she didn't say anything and went straight back to her room.

Then, night fell quickly, and darkness engulfed the entire Dan Garden.

In the distance, the flickering lights of those few ghostly lanterns reappeared, swaying in the wind, adding to the eeriness.

The group gathered in the largest room.

This is the house occupied by Black 112 and another man in a black robe, along with their roommate.

The doors and windows were tightly shut, and they used everything they could find to plug the gaps, lighting only an old oil lamp that they had dug out of the house.

"Tell me, what did you discover today?" Black 112, as the temporary leader, spoke up.

He also briefly introduced himself, saying that he is a physical education coach at a chain gym and has reached the Foundation Establishment stage.

The others also introduced themselves one after another. Most of them were independent cultivators or disciples of small forces in society, with cultivation levels ranging from the mid to late stages of Foundation Establishment.

When it was Zhou Ping's turn, he stammered, "I... Brother 999 and I mainly searched the east side of the Sutra Repository, but we only found useless things. There, behind a bookshelf, there seemed to be a floorboard that was a different color, but I didn't dare touch it..."

The clues he provided were of little value; they were more of a way to create an atmosphere of fear.

Wang Ling remained silent. When Black 112 looked over, he simply shook his head, indicating that he had gained nothing.

His performance was typical of a mediocre newcomer who was terrified.

The clues shared by others were also fragmented.

Some people mentioned that they smelled a particularly strong medicinal smell near the abandoned pill room on the west side of Dan Garden, but they could not find the source.

Someone discovered a well, but the wellhead was blocked by a huge rock, which was impossible to move even with the cultivation level of the Foundation Establishment stage.

Some people also said that throughout the entire exploration process, it seemed as if there was a gaze secretly watching them from behind.

All the clues pointed to the strangeness and abnormality of this pill garden, but there was almost no useful information about the "Resurrection Pill" or how one could survive.

"Where's Lin Susu? She must have discovered something!" a man in gray couldn't help but say, his tone filled with dissatisfaction and anticipation.

Black 112 walked to Lin Susu's door and knocked, but there was no response from inside.

"Forget it, she doesn't want to talk about it, there's no point in forcing her."

Black 112 sighed, his face grave: "Everyone, remember Elder Xuanse's rules: no going out after sunset. Be alert tonight!"

Panic spread in the silence.

Unknown rules and a clueless task weighed heavily on everyone's mind.

......
