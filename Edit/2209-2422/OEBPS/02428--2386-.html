Chapter 2386 The Seed of Evil Cultivation

Luo Xinghe's cough echoed in the ward, like a broken bellows.

Xia Zhinu stood by the bed, her fingers tightly gripping the hem of her clothes, somewhat at a loss.

She looked at the once spirited anchorwoman, now lying pale-faced in a hospital bed, her eyes burning with twisted hatred, and a wave of distress washed over her.

"Xinghe, calm down..." she tried to step forward to help him catch his breath.

"Don't touch me!" Luo Xinghe suddenly waved his hand, almost knocking over the water glass on the bedside table.

The monitoring device's alarm blared sharply, and the sound of nurses' hurried footsteps immediately came from outside the door.

Xia Zhinu took a step back, tears finally falling from her eyes: "I just wanted to help you..."

"Help me?" Luo Xinghe laughed hoarsely, his smile full of sarcasm. "Xia Zhinuan, put your hand on your heart and tell me, from the training camp until now, what have you ever helped me with? I told you to act like Wang Ling in the competition, and you turned around and played support for him like crazy. I told you to interfere with the draw, and you immediately revealed the whole USB drive thing to Zhuo Yi. Now that you see me lying here, do you feel particularly relieved? Finally, you don't have to pretend to cooperate with me anymore?"

Every word is like a knife.

Xia Zhinu bit her lip, her voice trembling: "I admit, I listened to you at first. But later I saw it, Wang Ling... he clearly knew I was acting, but he still treated me as a teammate..."

"Shut up! Shut up!" Luo Xinghe pounded on the edge of the bed in agitation, the electrocardiogram on the monitoring screen fluctuating wildly: "So you've fallen for him now? You think he's better than me? More worthy of your following?"

"I didn't!" Xia Zhinu cried out, "I just feel... that what we did before was wrong."

"Wrong?" Luo Xinghe sneered, "Then what is right? Is it right to be a traitor like you? Is it right to pretend to be virtuous like Wang Ling? Xia Zhinuan, let me tell you, in this world only the winners have the right to talk about right and wrong. I lost, so everything I do is wrong. But if I had won, it would be Wang Ling lying in the hospital right now! It would be him being ridiculed by the entire internet!"

He became increasingly agitated as he spoke, his face shifting from pale to a sickly flush: "Do you know how much I prepared for this training camp? I turned down three commercial events and trained for twelve hours every day, all to prove to everyone that I was the strongest. And now? Now everyone thinks I'm a joke! A joke of being slaughtered like a pig by high school students!"

"That's because you care too much about winning and losing!" Xia Zhinu wiped away her tears, her voice suddenly turning cold: "Xinghe, you weren't like this before. When you first started streaming, you would be happy for half a day if a viewer praised your skills, and you would reply to every comment seriously. But now? All you see are data, popularity, and how to beat others down."

She took a deep breath, as if she had made a firm decision: "The Luo Xinghe I knew is long dead."

This sentence was like the last straw.

Luo Xinghe paused for two seconds, then burst into maniacal laughter, tears streaming down his face: "Yes, I'm dead. Killed by you, by Wang Ling, by everyone. Are you satisfied now? You can get lost. Go lick your boots at your classmate Wang Ling, maybe he'll take pity on you and give you a bite to eat."

Looking at his distorted face, Xia Zhinuan suddenly felt incredibly unfamiliar with him.

It's been three years.

From a smiling, sunny young man in the live stream to a man lying in a hospital bed with eyes full of hatred.

She once truly believed that she could accompany him to even greater heights.

"Take care," Xia Zhinuan said softly, then turned around, pushed open the door, and left.

The moment the door closed, Luo Xinghe grabbed a pillow and slammed it against the door.

The soft pillow fell to the floor.

Just like his current dignity, it's worthless.

...

The ward fell silent again.

Only the regular "beep" sound of the monitoring instrument.

Luo Xinghe leaned against the headboard, staring at the ceiling, his mind a jumbled mess.

Xia Zhinuan's last words echoed in her ears like a curse: "The Luo Xinghe I knew is long dead."

Is he dead?

Maybe.

It all started when I first lost to Wang Ling at the training camp, it all started when I first hired online trolls to smear him, it all started when I first contacted the Funeral Pavilion...

Luo Xinghe, who once dreamed of becoming a top anchor, had long been killed by his own hands.

All that's left is a gambler who's lost everything.

Luo Xinghe muttered to himself, his fingers unconsciously tugging at the bed sheet.

The spirit stones he used to hire the assassins represented almost all of his wealth.

Now it's all gone.

The compensation from the Funeral Pavilion must be paid within 24 hours, otherwise its credit rating will drop, making it difficult for it to operate on the dark web in the future.

But where is he going to get 14.6 million?

Borrow money? He had already borrowed from everyone he could.

A loan? Given his current situation, which institution would lend him money?

Luo Xinghe closed his eyes, and despair washed over his entire body like ice water.

Just then—

"Knock knock".

A soft knock on the door.

Luo Xinghe didn't open his eyes: "Get lost, I don't need to be visited."

The door opened by itself.

The footsteps were very light, like walking on cotton.

Luo Xinghe opened her eyes and saw a middle-aged man wearing a gray Zhongshan suit standing at the door.

The man looked to be around fifty years old, with his hair neatly combed, wearing gold-rimmed glasses, and carrying an ordinary briefcase.

He looks like an ordinary High School teacher or a junior official in a government agency.

But Luo Xinghe sensed something was wrong.

There was no spiritual energy fluctuation from this man; it was so subtle that even he couldn't detect it.

It should be noted that Luo Xinghe was at least at the early stage of the Golden Core realm, and those who could completely conceal their cultivation level in front of him were at least at the Nascent Soul stage or above.

"Who are you?" Luo Xinghe asked warily.

The middle-aged man smiled gently, closed the door behind him, and sat down in the chair next to the hospital bed.

He moved very naturally, as if this were his own office.

"Mr. Luo Xinghe, it's a pleasure to meet you." The man took out a business card from his briefcase and handed it to him.

The business card was black with dark gold patterns embossed along the edges. It contained only one line of text:

Mo Xuan, Senior Consultant, Honghuang Asset Management Co., Ltd.

There was no phone number, no address, not even a company logo.

"Primordial assets?" Luo Xinghe frowned. "I've never heard of them."

"That's normal." Mo Xuan placed his briefcase on his lap, crossed his arms, and relaxed his posture. "Our company mainly deals with cross-border investment and special asset disposal, and does not serve ordinary clients. We have come here today because we have noticed that Mr. Luo has recently... encountered some financial difficulties."

Luo Xinghe's heart tightened: "You're investigating me?"

"It's just necessary background information on the client." Mo Xuan's smile remained unchanged: "After all, we plan to provide Mr. Luo with an interest-free loan of 15 million spirit stones. Naturally, we need to know the borrower's situation."

Fifteen million!

That's just enough to pay the compensation to the Funeral Pavilion!

Luo Xinghe's breathing quickened, but reason told him that no pie would fall from the sky.

"What are the conditions?" He stared at Mo Xuan.

Mo Xuan pushed up his glasses: "The conditions are simple. We hope that Mr. Luo can continue to do what you want to do."

What I want to do?
