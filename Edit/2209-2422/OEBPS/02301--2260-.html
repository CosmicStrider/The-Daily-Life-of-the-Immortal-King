Chapter 2260 Echoes from the Earth's Core

The whispers of the earth veins gradually subsided, and the tremors within the cave also ceased, leaving only a mess on the ground and the still-shaken breathing of the group.

Chen Chao plopped down on the ground, grimacing as he rubbed his leg, which had almost split open again: "Damn it... what the hell is this subterranean world? One problem after another, is it even allowed to live?"

I thought it was a slightly dangerous adventure, but it turned out to be a life-threatening adventure.

This disparity made the elite university representatives, who were only at the Golden Core stage, feel a strong sense of inadequacy, even fear.

The loss of contact with the surface base has stirred a sense of despair in some people.

Chen Chao even saw several representatives start writing their wills, wondering if they would make it out alive.

Guo Hao, panting heavily, still clutching half a ruined talisman in his hand, said with a wry smile, "I'm starting to miss Old Man Teng's spiritual pressure test now; at least that thing was something you could see and touch."

Ryoko Kugimiya quickly manipulated the array plate, her expression still grave: "The earth vein spiritual pressure baseline has been permanently raised by 15%, and the deep spiritual energy flow is abnormally active. Although that malice from just now has disappeared... I still feel like it hasn't gone far."

Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe exchanged a glance, both seeing the heaviness in each other's eyes.

As experienced elites, they knew very well that such malice was extraordinary, more like a harbinger of the awakening of some ancient being.

Su Xingyue gently wiped the blade of the "Dawn" sword and whispered, "It's aimed at Sun Rong."

In an instant, everyone's attention was focused on Sun Rong.

Sun Rong was still standing there, slightly lost in thought, the cool touch of Wang Ling's touch still lingering on her arm.

She unconsciously touched that spot, her cheeks flushing slightly.

"Rongrong, are you alright?" Liu Qingyi walked over quickly and looked her over with concern.

"No...it's nothing." Sun Rong came back to her senses, forcibly suppressing the ripples in her heart, and tried to keep her voice steady: "It's probably because the energy in the Aohai is purer, attracting that malice."

The Nine-Core Ao Hai is a life-saving artifact that Wang Ling tailored specifically for her.

Now, not only is O'Hay being targeted, but he himself is also being affected.

It would be a lie to say there's no pressure.

But she knew that with Wang Ling around... this was Sun Rong's greatest comfort at the moment.

Wang Zhen stroked his chin, glanced thoughtfully at Wang Ling who had started a new round of "squeezing instant noodles" project, and smiled playfully: "It is attractive, but it has also been purified quite thoroughly."

He deliberately emphasized the word "purification".

Fang Xing nodded silently, the black gemstone on the skull sickle glowing subdued, as if it too agreed with this statement.

Sun Rong knew what they were implying, but she chose to avoid the topic.

She walked to Ryoko Kunomiya's side and looked at the array screen: "Ryoko, can you trace the remaining trail of that evil source?"

Ryoko Kumiya shook her head: "Completely disappeared, without a trace. It's like... being erased from a higher dimension by something."

As she spoke, her eyes involuntarily drifted toward Wang Ling.

Just then, Wang Ling, who had been quiet as if he were detached from the matter, suddenly stopped kneading the dough.

He raised his eyes, his gaze once again fixed on the abandoned tunnel leading deeper into the earth's core.

This time, his gaze was no longer simply listening, but carried a faint hint of inquiry.

Everyone fell silent instantly, even their breathing became soft.

Unbeknownst to them, Wang Ling's slightest actions could affect everyone's nerves.

Wang Ling's fingertips tapped unconsciously twice on the armrest of the recliner, and an extremely faint wave of thought spread out.

The Mountain-Moving Beast in the corner shuddered suddenly. Its huge, single eye looked at Wang Ling with fear, then lowered its head in apprehension. It used its claws to pull out a piece of silver brilliance that it treasured, which contained rich spatial elements, and carefully pushed it toward Wang Ling.

Wang Ling didn't look at the stone, but instead gave Wang Zhen a very slight nod of his chin.

As if receiving some kind of instruction, Wang Zhen's eyes flashed with a sharp light, and he smiled, "It seems that this place is not safe enough. We need to find another place."

Only a few of them, who were highly skilled and had fought against Bai Zhe, knew what they were really facing.

Clearly, this inhuman Moon Dragon is getting restless.

"Change locations?" Li Changzhe's eyes widened. "Isn't this cave dwelling quite nice? It's well-defended, and we have food and drink."

"That thing just now could directly penetrate the barrier and lock onto Sun Rong."

Zhang Linyan calmly analyzed, "This means we've been exposed. Staying here will likely make us a target."

After a moment's thought, Sun Rong decisively said, "Senior Wang Zhen is right. Although the malice has temporarily subsided, its source is still unknown, and we cannot wait passively. I suggest we actively explore that passage."

She pointed in the direction Wang Ling had previously indicated.

"explore?"

Chen Chao's voice trembled, "Sister, my dear sister! That terrifying thing that just crawled out from down there, and we just went and delivered ourselves to its doorstep?"

"It is precisely because it is dangerous that we need to find out what it is."

Sun Rong's eyes were firm: "Otherwise, we might not be so lucky next time it comes."

What she didn't say was that she believed in Wang Ling's guidance.

Since he pointed out that passage, there must be a deeper meaning behind it.

"I agree with Sun Rong's opinion."

Su Xingyue softly echoed, her face regaining some color after she finished regulating her breathing: "Passive defense is not the solution. The original intention of the Earth Core Project is also to explore the unknown."

Ryoko Kugimiya manipulated the array plate, attempting to scan the passage, but the signal was extremely chaotic: "The spatial structure within the passage is very unstable, filled with unknown psionic turbulence, making detection spells almost ineffective."

"Then we definitely need to go and see."

Wang Zhen laughed like a husky about to wreak havoc: "Maybe the ultimate treasure of the Earth's core is hidden down there?"

Liu Qingyi rolled her eyes at him, annoyed: "Treasure? I think it's more like a burial ground!"

However, she still walked up to Sun Rong and made her stance clear.

Fang Xing succinctly stated, "I'll clear the way."

Ultimately, after some discussion, it was decided that a select team would conduct the initial exploration.

Exploration Team Members: Wang Zhen (Leader), Fang Xing (Forward), Sun Rong (Ocean Sensing), Jiu Gong Liangzi (Array and Detection), Liu Qingyi (Support), Zhang Linyan (Remote Support).

Li Changzhe, Su Xingyue, and the others stayed behind in the cave to care for the wounded and maintain the barrier.

Wang Ling remained seated in his recliner, seemingly indifferent to everyone's decision, though the expression in his eyes seemed to have become subtly more serious.

Sun Rong walked up to him, took a deep breath, and mustered her courage to ask softly, "Wang Ling, we're going down... Is there anything you want to remind us of?"

She was hoping for even just a single word of guidance.

Wang Ling paused in his kneading of the dough, then looked up at her.

Those dead fish eyes remained calm, but Sun Rong seemed to catch a fleeting, unreadable emotion.

He paused for a few seconds, then reached out, picked up a small piece of crumb from the instant noodle bag, and handed it to Sun Rong.

Sun Rong: "???"

Everyone: "..."

Is this encouragement?

Or should we send it as a farewell gift?

Or maybe you just think she's hungry?

Sun Rong blushed slightly and blankly took the small piece of dough crumb, holding it in her palm. It felt a little hot.

Wang Ling had already looked away and continued to focus on his pancake, as if he had just casually handed him a tissue.

Sun Rong looked at the small, minty crumb in her palm and suddenly had a flash of inspiration. She carefully put it into her pocket.

"Thank you," she said softly, her tone extremely earnest.

Wang Ling did not respond.

Until the exploration team was fully organized, until it was time to depart.

Wang Ling's voice suddenly echoed in Sun Rong's mind in a faint and ethereal way.

"If you are in mortal danger, eat that piece of instant noodles."

This was Wang Ling's last request.

Sun Rong, who was standing in the middle of the line, was taken aback.

She smiled slightly.

Then, following the exploration team, they arrived at the entrance to the deep, dark, abandoned tunnel.

A wind carrying the scent of primal, unknown minerals blew out from the depths, sending chills down one's spine.

Ryoko Kugimiya was the first to throw out her array plate, and dozens of faintly glowing runes disappeared into the stone wall of the passage, barely illuminating an area of more than ten meters in front of her.

The road descends steeply, covered with jagged rocks and traces of dried moss, clearly abandoned for many years.

Fang Xing took the lead, holding the skull sickle in his hand, its blade gleaming with an eerie light as he vigilantly sensed what lay ahead.

Wang Zhen followed lazily behind, but his eyes were sharp.
