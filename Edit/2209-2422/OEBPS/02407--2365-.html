Chapter 2365 Mid-lane Sniping Match

"Rules of this game: Mid-lane duel, unlimited respawns, the player who kills the opponent three times first wins."

"Jungle resources are now available; killing jungle monsters will grant additional attribute bonuses."

"Special Note: The spiritual energy fluctuations within the illusion are monitored in real time. Unauthorized use of cultivation abilities will result in penalties."

As Wang Ling controlled Kuchi to slide towards the middle lane, the system's victory mechanism notification sound echoed in his mind three times.

The bottom of the Yodel aircraft emitted a pale blue tail flame, hovering about half a meter off the ground as it moved forward at a speed much faster than walking.

Middle lane.

When Wang Ling reached the first tower, the minion waves just happened to converge.

Meanwhile, Luo Xinghe, disguised as Kaisa, was already in place.

Luo Xinghe was standing behind the blue team's minion line. His purplish-black biological armor gleamed with a cold light, and the wing membranes on his back were slightly open, ready to enter overload mode at any moment.

"Wang Ling, classmate."

Luo Xinghe's voice came through the communication channel built into the illusion, still gentle, but Wang Ling sensed a hint of gloom in it.

"Please give me your guidance," Luo Xinghe said.

Wang Ling did not reply.

He simply raised Kuchi's modified rifle and aimed at the first low-health melee soldier.

"Bang!"

With a basic attack, the bullets hit their mark precisely, and the soldier fell.

Finishing blow +1.

"Not going to speak?" Luo Xinghe chuckled. "Fine, communicating with strength is more direct."

Before she finished speaking, Kai'Sa moved.

Q skill [Icacia Rainstorm]!

Dozens of micro-missiles were launched from the launcher on Kai'Sa's shoulder, targeting the entire minion wave!

"He's aiming for the second rank," Wang Ling instantly deduced.

While the Illusionary Simulation Battle retains the basic rules of Honor of Kings, the experience gain rate has been adjusted.

In a solo lane, you can reach level two by killing six minions.

Kai'Sa's Q skill is an AOE damage skill that clears minions extremely quickly.

If he levels up to level two first and learns the W skill [Void Seeker] to harass, Corki will have a very difficult laning phase in the early game.

Wang Ling reacted extremely quickly.

Corki raises his hand, unleashing his Q ability, Phosphorus Bomb!

A bomb emitting a pungent odor flew in an arc and landed precisely between three low-health melee soldiers.

"Boom!"

The explosion caused damage, and three soldiers fell to the ground simultaneously!

Almost simultaneously, Corki and Kai'Sa both glowed with the light of their upgrades!

Level 2!

Luo Xinghe's eyes narrowed.

He hadn't expected Wang Ling's troops to be able to keep up with his speed.

Corki's Q skill does have high damage, but the flight is delayed and requires prediction.

Wang Ling's phosphorescent bomb was released almost simultaneously with the launch of the Kasa missile.

Anticipation? Or reaction?

"Just a coincidence." Luo Xinghe suppressed the turmoil in his heart and instantly learned the W skill [Void Target].

A purplish-black energy pulse shot out from Kai'Sa's palm, heading straight for Corki!

At this distance, it's almost guaranteed to hit.

If it hits, it will stack two layers of the [Plasma] passive, greatly increasing the damage of subsequent normal attacks.

However, just as the energy pulse was about to hit, the thrusters on Kuchi's back suddenly erupted with an even more intense tail flame!

W skill [Valkyrie Dive], a small dash to the left and forward!

"laugh!"

The energy pulse grazed the edge of Kuchi's aircraft, but was perfectly neutralized by Wang Ling.

"What?!" Luo Xinghe's pupils constricted slightly.

The angle and timing of that displacement were outrageously precise, as if he knew in advance where his W would be fired.

"Another prediction?"

In the viewing hall, a holographic projection screen broadcasts everything in the illusion in real time.

The huge screen was divided into two halves, with Wang Ling's first-person perspective on the left and Luo Xinghe's on the right.

At that moment, everyone saw Corki's brilliant little movement.

"That positioning..." Chen Chao's mouth dropped open: "How did Lingzi know he was going to use W?"

"It's not knowing, it's predicting." Zhou Rui pushed up his glasses and calmly analyzed, "When Kai'Sa levels up to level two, there's an 80% or higher chance she'll learn W and try to harass. And Wang Ling had already prepared his movement ability the moment he leveled up to level two."

Guo Hao stared at the screen: "The key is the direction of movement. Moving to the left and forward allows you to dodge skills while maintaining distance for finishing off enemies... Holy crap, he's insane!"

Sun Rong did not speak.

She looked at Corki on the screen, who was calmly gazing at Kai'Sa in front of him while wearing goggles.

The viewpoint was stable, without the slightest shake, just as calm as Wang Ling himself.

...

Inside the illusion.

Luo Xinghe took a deep breath.

"Good luck," he said on the channel, his tone unreadable. "Then let's continue."

Kai'Sa started to steadily last-hit minions.

Since consumption has failed, let's focus on development first.

Kai'Sa is a late-game champion; once she gets her items, her 1v1 ability will crush Corki.

But Wang Ling did not intend to let him develop peacefully.

Corki kept using auto-attacks to hit Kai'Sa between last-hitting minions.

Every time she targeted someone, it was precisely at the moment Kai'Sa raised her hand to last-hit.

This is the most basic consumption technique.

When you attack while your opponent is finishing off a minion, they either have to give up and retreat, or take the damage and finish off the minion.

Luo Xinghe chose the latter.

He controlled Kai'Sa to tank two auto-attacks and last-hit the cannon minion, thinking to himself, "I lost the trade. But the cannon minion gold is more important. I'll go back to base and buy a Long Sword later, then the laning phase will be much more comfortable."

However, Wang Ling's suppression did not stop there.

When the third wave of minions arrived, Corki suddenly pushed forward, his W skill [Valkyrie Dive] was off cooldown, and he flew directly to Kai'Sa's side and front, blocking her retreat!

At the same time, a phosphorescent bomb was launched!

Luo Xinghe reacted extremely quickly, activating Kai'Sa's E skill [Extreme Overload], gaining a movement speed bonus, and pulling backward.

But Wang Ling's phosphorescent bombs predicted his retreat route.

The bomb exploded at Kaisa's feet!

"Bang!"

Kai'Sa's health dropped significantly.

Even more fatally, Corki used his W to dash, and his position was right behind and to the side of Kai'Sa.

Now, if Kai'Sa wants to return to the tower, she has to pass by Corki, which will force her to take damage.

Taking the long way will result in losing more minion experience.

"We've been blocked..." Luo Xinghe's face was grim.

He chose to force his way through.

Kai'Sa turned around and used her basic attack on Corki, attempting to trade blows.

However, Corki's slight sideways glide caused Kai'Sa's basic attack to miss, while his own basic attack landed precisely.

Once, twice.

The red buff effect hasn't been released yet, but Corki's starting Doran's Blade gives him higher attack damage than Kai'Sa in the early game.

After several rounds of trading, Kai'Sa's health had dropped to below half.

"Damn it..." Luo Xinghe gritted his teeth and knocked over the only bottle of blood medicine.

He can't have any more blood transfusions; he must return to the city.

However, the minion wave is pushing towards the red side, so returning to base now would result in losing at least two waves of minions.

In the instant he hesitated, Kuchi suddenly turned and flew towards the river.

"Going to farm in the jungle?" Luo Xinghe was taken aback, then breathed a sigh of relief.

It seems that Wang Ling is not in good condition and needs to replenish his jungle resources.

He quickly controlled Kai'Sa to clear the wave, preparing to push the minion wave into the tower before recalling.

However, ten seconds later.

When Kai'Sa pushed the minion wave to the red side tower, Corki flew back from the river.

Moreover, the health bar is full.

"He ate the candied fruit?" Luo Xinghe looked at the mini-map, and sure enough, the candied fruit in the river had been eaten.

Corki not only recovered his health, but also gained a newly refreshed blue buff.

"We've fallen into their trap..." Luo Xinghe's heart sank.

Wang Ling wasn't actually in bad shape; he deliberately let the lane go first so he could go and get the blue buff.

Kai'Sa is currently at half health and has no potion, with the minion wave in front of the red side tower, while Corki is at full health with a blue buff, making their combat power completely different.

"We must return to the city." Luo Xinghe decisively retreated.
