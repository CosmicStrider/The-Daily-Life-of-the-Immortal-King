Chapter 2333 Esports Arena Experience

The action-oriented Sun Rong used her privileges as the young lady of the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group on the afternoon of the day the team was formed.

In reality, Sun Rong made a phone call during the break between classes, and everything was already ready.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao were stunned. They never expected Sun Rong to be so serious. They once again realized how wonderful it was to have such a classmate with money-making abilities by their side!

After school, the group stood in front of a futuristic, streamlined building located in the core area of Songhai City.

The building's exterior walls are made of psionic glass, which is currently projecting dynamic images of the heroes from "Honor of Kings," creating a powerful aura.

This is the [Fantasy Esports Experience Hall], a subsidiary of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, which is known as the "Holy Land of Cultivation Esports".

The largest LCD screen on the front even features a very familiar figure: the 60-time champion of "Glory of Legends," nicknamed the "Great Demon King"!

This person's name is Li Xianghe. He started playing the game "Honor of Kings" when it was first developed, and participated in the World Championship from the very first season. He has won sixty championships in his gaming career, and even created a record of six consecutive World Championship titles, which remains unbroken to this day.

Every player who has played this game has been deeply moved by Brother Li's professionalism...

No one could have imagined that, more than sixty years later, he would still be in C!

【Fantasy Esports Experience Center】is usually bustling with activity, serving as the starting point for countless esports teenagers' dreams.

But today, Sun Rong has completely cleared this place out, preparing it as a training location for Number 60 High School.

The interior space is extremely large and divided into several areas.

Most notably, there are dozens of top-of-the-line psionic simulators arranged in a central, circular layout.

The walls are covered with huge real-time data screens, and there are also dedicated physical fitness monitoring areas and tactical analysis rooms in the corners.

An air purification array operates underground in the venue, bringing a fresh and pleasant atmosphere.

Meanwhile, the holographic projection screens around the venue are currently playing promotional CG for "Glory of the League," with the light effects of hero skills intertwined with exciting background music, creating a thrilling experience.

"Wow!"

Chen Chao let out a pathetic sigh and widened his eyes.

He touched the metal casing of a streamlined gaming cabin next to him, croaking repeatedly, "This is so cool! Sun Rong, how much does it usually cost to come to this experience center of yours?"

Sun Rong changed into a refreshing tracksuit, her ponytail swinging neatly behind her head.

She simply smiled calmly upon hearing this: "If we're operating it externally, a single session costs about 100 spirit stones per hour. Actually, we don't make any money at all; the main purpose is to support the dreams of domestic e-sports prodigies. However, today, only we can use it here, so this slight loss in revenue is nothing."

Chen Chao and Guo Hao: "..." Listen to this, is this even human language!

Guo Hao was also very excited: "With this setup, plus venue rental and spiritual energy consumption, the cost of closing it off for a day is probably close to six figures in spirit stones. Sun Rong, we really benefited from your help this time."

This was Wang Muyu's first time in such a place. Although he was old-fashioned in his understanding, he still had the nature of a child and was extremely excited, darting back and forth between the gaming cabins.

The excitement was indescribable, especially considering that he could actually fight alongside Wang Ling in the game.

Wang Lingze remained expressionless, his hands in his school trouser pockets, seemingly indifferent to everything around him.

He actually quite enjoys playing games. After all, the game world is virtual, the values are constant, and it all depends on skill; you can't just kill someone in a few moves.

But in order to avoid attracting too much attention, he rarely plays ranked matches.

At this moment, Sun Rong walked to the center of the venue, clapped her hands, and attracted everyone's attention.

"Alright, since we're aiming for the championship, we can't just train blindly. This place is not only a training ground, but also a selection base."

She pointed to the silver watches that everyone had just put on before entering the hall: "The group established this place with the initial intention of discovering talented young cultivators to devote themselves to the e-sports industry. Of course, it will also help some peers who are addicted to games and cannot recognize reality to get back on track."

She phrased it tactfully, but everyone understood.

This is also a deluxe version of an "internet addiction rehabilitation center".

"Our special training starts with the most basic talent test," Sun Rong explained, pointing to one side of the venue: "First, it's the dynamic vision test."

Suddenly, a thin, silver streak of light lit up in the sky above that area!

"call out--!"

A quaint and ancient-looking spirit sword swept across the venue at near bullet speed, so fast that only a fleeting afterimage could be seen.

"Look closely, there's a series of numbers engraved on the spirit sword."

Sun Rong's voice was clear: "It will randomly shuttle through the venue three times. Your task is to see and remember the number on the sword the moment it flies by, and input it through your wristwatch. Only if all three rounds of input are correct will it be considered excellent data!"

Chen Chao rubbed his hands together, eager to try: "I'm good at this! Those stone-armored leopards in the inner world are much faster than this!"

Guo Hao also focused his mind and prepared to give it his all.

Wang Muyu was eager to try.

But this test was rather boring for Wang Ling, since his Wang Tong could freeze time directly; whenever he wanted, all the dynamic movements in his eyes would slow down.

In the first round, the spirit sword swept past the crowd diagonally above them like a ghost.

Chen Chao held his breath and focused his gaze intently. The instant the spirit sword disappeared, he immediately entered the number he saw on his watch.

Guo Hao frowned slightly, trying hard to recall.

Wang Muyu's face was full of seriousness.

Wang Ling slowly raised his wrist, glanced at the watch screen, and leisurely entered the data.

The second and third rounds of flying swords came one after another, each one faster than the last. Everyone noticed that the numbers on the swords were even more numerous than in the first round.

The more numbers there are, the harder it is to remember them.

By the time the third flying sword swept past, Chen Chao and Guo Hao were clearly overwhelmed. Before they could even see the numbers clearly, the flying sword had already passed by.

After the test, the watch data is synchronized to the central screen, and the test results of several people are represented by √ or ×.

Chen Chao: √, ×, ×.

Guo Hao: √, √, ×.

Wang Muyu: √, √, √.

Sun Rong: √, √, √.

Wang Ling: √, √, √.

"Huh?" Chen Chao looked at Wang Ling in surprise: "Lingzi, you're amazing! You've been hiding your true abilities! You could see everything clearly even when the third round was going so fast?"

Wang Ling: "...luck."

Sun Rong and Wang Muyu: "..." I'd be a fool to believe you!

He originally wanted to put on an act, but the venue was closed and there were no outsiders. In addition, the test results would not be leaked to outsiders, so Wang Ling also wanted to occasionally show his true colors and be a little mischievous.

"Next is the stress test."

Sun Rong led the group to a green crystal pillar about half a person's height: "See the crystal? It will randomly change from green to red. The moment the color changes, immediately press the response button on your wristwatch. For ordinary people, a value below 200 milliseconds is considered excellent. We are all cultivators, so a value below 50 milliseconds is considered excellent. The threshold for professional players is around 80 milliseconds."

Even Wang Ling showed some interest this time.

Test begins.

Chen Chao held his breath and pressed the button the instant the crystal turned red.

The watch shows: 47ms.

"Haha! Excellent!" Chen Chao said proudly.

Guo Hao followed closely behind: 56ms.
