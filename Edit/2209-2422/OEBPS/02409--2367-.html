Chapter 2367 Luo Xinghe's Scumbag-Style Deception

Luo Xinghe leaned against the hospital bed, his pale face fixed on the call history on his phone screen that had just ended.

"The king's decree..."

He repeated Wang Ling's name in a low voice, a kind of almost morbid obsession surging in his eyes.

Three to zero.

"No normal person can make that kind of prediction," he told himself. "Unless..."

Unless there is external intervention.

Cultivation techniques? Special talent? Or some unknown cheating method?

Luo Xinghe didn't know.

But he needs to know.

So he made that call and contacted an "information consulting company" that had a good reputation on the dark web.

The price was high, but I didn't care.

His savings from three years of live streaming were enough to buy this level of service.

"Number 60 High School, Wang Ling, all the data, all the background, all the anomalies."

He told the person on the other end of the phone, "Money is not a problem, I need the most detailed report."

The other party agreed and promised to provide preliminary results within seventy-two hours.

Luo Xinghe put down his phone and closed his eyes.

The headache persists, and the aftereffects of the mental overload are more serious than I imagined.

The psionic soothing device in the medical room hummed by the bedside, releasing calming vibrations, but its effects were limited.

Sun Rong's face reappeared in his mind.

The girl's eyes flashed with a smile as she accepted the instant noodles from Wang Ling.

"Why should I..." Luo Xinghe gritted his teeth.

In terms of looks, fame, and emotional intelligence, in what way is he inferior to that wooden guy who eats instant noodles all day?

But Sun Rong's gaze remained fixed on Wang Ling from beginning to end.

That's not fair!

...

At the same time, in the central control room of the Starry Sky Esports Center.

Zhuo Yi stood in front of a screen of light covering an entire wall, holding a thermos.

The screen displays dozens of surveillance images: the training area, the accommodation area, the canteen, and even the corridor outside the medical room.

His gaze fell on the scene at the entrance to the infirmary.

The high-definition camera captured the exact time of Luo Xinghe's phone call, its duration, and the mixed expression of resentment on his face after the call ended.

"As expected." Zhuo Yi took a sip of tea, his tone calm.

Behind him, Wang Zhen frowned and told Zhuo Yi about the information he had obtained from Jiugong: "Zhuo Yi, according to the communication monitoring, Luo Xinghe contacted Dark Eye Consulting five minutes ago through an encrypted line. This is a gray organization that specializes in background investigations and information mining."

"Dark eyes?" Zhuo Yi raised an eyebrow: "Not high-end, but more than enough to investigate ordinary high school students."

"Should we intercept them?" Wang Zhen asked.

"No need." Zhuo Yi put down his cup, a playful smile playing on his lips. "Let them investigate. However..."

He turned and walked to the console, his fingers rapidly tapping on the virtual keyboard to bring up an encrypted file.

File number: Top Secret.

Name: Wang Ling.

License level: Top Secret.

"Mr. Wang Ming should have already given the instructions," Zhuo Yi said, while working on the file. "We just need to add a little something to the information."

He selected several key sections from the file: family background, cultivation aptitude, past experiences, and social relationships.

Then, all the information about Wang Ling was disguised as that of an ordinary high school student.

Finally, click "Confirm".

"Alright." Zhuo Yi clapped his hands. "From now on, any request to investigate Master will receive a flawless file of an ordinary high school student. Father is an ordinary civil servant, mother is an elementary school teacher, family lives in the old town of Songhai City, cultivation aptitude is F-level, grades are below average, introverted personality, hobbies are eating snacks and playing games."

Looking at the data stream flowing on the screen, Wang Zhen couldn't help but ask, "Isn't this too obvious? If the other party is a professional organization, they might notice that the information has been tampered with."

"That's exactly the effect I wanted." Zhuo Yi smiled. "To make them notice something unusual, but without finding any clues. The deeper they investigate, the more they'll find that all the clues point to this person being completely ordinary. This contradiction will confuse them even more..."

He paused, a sharp glint in his eyes.

"Then more clues will be revealed."

...

Six o'clock in the evening, at the Starry Sky Esports Center cafeteria.

Xia Zhinuan, carrying a plate, found Luo Xinghe in the corner.

He sat by the window, a bowl of almost untouched spiritual porridge in front of him, his gaze fixed on the darkening sky outside the window, his profile tightly drawn.

"Xinghe," Xia Zhinuan called softly, sitting down opposite him. "Are you feeling better?"

Luo Xinghe snapped out of his daze and forced a smile: "Much better. My head still hurts a little."

"The doctor said that you need to rest because of the mental overload." Xia Zhinu pushed a plate of sliced spiritual fruit over to her: "Don't participate in any more high-intensity training tonight. Ask Zhuo Shuchang for a break."

"Rest?" Luo Xinghe scoffed. "And then watch Wang Ling continue to steal the spotlight? Watch Sun Rong fawn over him?"

Xia Zhinuan paused for a moment while holding the spoon.

She looked up at Luo Xinghe: "Xinghe, are you... too concerned about the King's decree?"

"You care?" Luo Xinghe's eyes turned cold: "Zhi Nuan, I don't care about him, I care about fairness. You saw today's match, was his performance normal? That kind of prediction, that kind of awareness, is that what a high school student should have?"

"Perhaps he's just naturally gifted..."

"So talented that she can crush professional streamers?" Luo Xinghe interrupted her, his voice unconsciously rising: "Zhi Nuan, we've been playing professionally for three years, how many geniuses have we seen? Which genius could design such a series of traps in six minutes?"

People at the surrounding tables looked over.

Xia Zhinuan blushed and lowered her voice, "Xinghe, keep your voice down..."

Luo Xinghe took a deep breath, forcing himself to calm down.

He rubbed his temples, his tone softening slightly: "Sorry, I was a little agitated. But Zhi Nuan, believe me, Wang Ling definitely has a problem. I've already had someone investigate, and we'll have results soon."

"You're sending someone to investigate him?" Xia Zhinuan's eyes widened. "This...this isn't a good idea, is it? What if they find out..."

"We won't be found out," Luo Xinghe said confidently. "I hired a professional agency. And even if we are found out, so what? If he has nothing to hide, what is he afraid of in an investigation?"

Xia Zhinu bit her lip, unsure of what to say.

She has known Luo Xinghe for three years, ever since he was a small-time live streamer, and has been by his side ever since.

She had seen him in high spirits, seen him with ease during live streams, and seen him suffer from insomnia all night after losing an important match.

But never before has it been like this.

Paranoid, gloomy, even... a little scary.
