Chapter 2374 Hextech Mayhem!

Xia Zhinuan stood at the door, holding the button that had already turned off the recording function, her palms still damp with cold sweat.

She took a deep breath, exchanged a glance with Wang Zhen across the street, and turned to walk towards the Starry Sky Esports Center.

Luo Xinghe had already disappeared around the street corner, but she could sense that the suppressed anger in that figure was about to burst forth.

"He's going to make his move during tomorrow's chaotic brawl."

As Xia Zhinuan walked, she whispered a report through the Strategist Alliance's specially made encrypted communicator: "He wouldn't go into the details of the plan, only saying that he would give me a good show. But I could tell from his words that he had probably bribed a few people from other teams in tomorrow's free-for-all to focus fire on Wang Ling in the competition and crush him in the most humiliating way."

Ryoko Kunomiya's calm voice came through the earpiece: "Understood. You did a good job. After you return to the center, come directly to the meeting room on the third floor of Building A. We need to do a detailed review."

"clear."

Xia Zhinuan hung up the call and quickened her pace.

She didn't know exactly what Luo Xinghe was going to do, but she had a feeling.

Tomorrow's match is unlikely to be peaceful.

Meeting room on the third floor of Building A, Star Dome Esports Center.

When Xia Zhinu pushed open the door and went in, the core members of the Love Strategist Alliance had already arrived.

Zhuo Yi, Jiu Gong Liangzi, Wang Zhen, and Liu Qingyi sat around the conference table, where a holographic projection screen displayed the schedule and rules for tomorrow's free-for-all.

"Sit down." Ryoko Kunomiya gestured with her folding fan to the empty seat.

Xia Zhinuan sat down and immediately handed the recording button to Zhuo Yi.

Zhuo Yi inserted it into the reader, and Luo Xinghe's voice, filled with suppressed anger and calculation, rang out in the conference room.

After listening to the recording, Wang Zhen stroked his chin and sneered, "This guy's gotten desperate after losing. But he probably doesn't know that tomorrow's free-for-all isn't your average free-for-all."

"Hextech Brawl." Liu Qingyi crossed her arms and spoke up, unusually. "All participants are randomly assigned champions and simultaneously draw Hextech runes at fixed time points to enhance their champions. There are a lot of variables, and theoretically there are no absolute strengths or weaknesses."

Ryoko Kugimiya brought up the rules details page: "Match time: 9:00 AM tomorrow. Location: Battle Hall 1, Building A. Mode: Hextech Brawl Special. Participants: All forty team members will participate, randomly divided into eight five-player teams, and will compete in three rounds of points matches."

She paused, then pointed to a special description on the screen: "Here's the important part: At the 5th, 10th, and 15th minute of each game, the system will randomly assign each player a Hextech Rune Enhancement. The rune effect lasts until the end of the game and cannot be changed."

Zhuo Yi added, "The Rune Library has over 120 effects, ranging from simple attribute bonuses to special effects that alter skill mechanics, and some effects can even drastically impact hero gameplay. For example, this one..."

He zoomed in on a rune icon:

[Tank Engine: Gain 1 stack of effect upon defeating an enemy. Each stack increases your size by 5% and your maximum health by 5%. Upon death, lose 65% of the stack count.]

"Increased size?" Xia Zhinuan was taken aback.

"Yes." Ryoko Kumiya nodded: "It's not just about the visual increase in size; the collision volume and skill range will also increase accordingly. Theoretically, if enough are stacked, a single hero can take up almost half the screen."

Wang Zhen chuckled.

He thought to himself, if a real person could draw this rune, and then pair it with a hero who could stack health...

I don't even want to think about that scene.

"Luo Xinghe doesn't know the details of this rule." Zhuo Yi turned off the projector: "He thought it was just a regular brawl, and he thought he could humiliate Wang Ling by bribing a few people to focus fire on him. But the randomness of Hextech Brawl will make all his calculations a joke."

Ryoko Kugimiya closed her folding fan, her eyes sharp: "But that's not enough. We need to make sure that Master can draw the right runes and heroes—at least, we can't let him be affected by Luo Xinghe's little actions in the game."

"How can we ensure that?" Xia Zhinuan asked.

"The system is random, so of course we can't interfere directly." Zhuo Yi smiled. "But we can use some tricks to increase the probability of drawing certain runes and heroes. Mr. Wang Ming has already remotely accessed the training camp's system backend and made some...fine-tuning."

Liu Qingyi snorted coldly: "There's no need to follow the rules when dealing with such a despicable person."

"So what is my mission?" Xia Zhinuan asked.

Ryoko Kugimiya looked at her: "You will participate in the competition as scheduled tomorrow. If Luo Xinghe contacts you, or if you find that someone is obviously targeting Wang Ling, please let us know immediately. Also..."

She paused, then softened her tone: "Take care of yourself. Luo Xinghe is emotionally unstable right now, and there's no guarantee he won't do something rash."

"I will." Xia Zhinuan nodded emphatically.

That evening, at the Starry Sky Esports Center cafeteria.

Discussions about tomorrow's Hextech Smash Bros. have become the main topic of conversation at the dinner table.

Chen Chao, carrying his tray, plopped down opposite Wang Ling, his voice so loud it could be heard throughout half the cafeteria: "Lingzi! Did you hear? Tomorrow's ARAM (All Random All Mid) mode, random heroes! And there are Hextech runes too! How exciting!"

Wang Ling remained calm as always: "Hmm."

"You mean...?" Chen Chao glared. "What if I get a random champion I don't know how to play? Like, if I get Ivern, wouldn't I have to retire on the spot?"

Li Changzhe stuffed a piece of pork rib into his mouth and mumbled, "What's there to be afraid of! What hero can't Wang Ling play? Give him a healer and he can play like an assassin!"

Sun Rong sat next to Wang Ling, sipping her soup, and asked softly, "Wang Ling, do you like this random mode?"

Wang Ling put a piece of instant noodles in his mouth, chewed it a couple of times, and then slowly said, "It's alright."

"Isn't it... too reliant on luck?" Sun Rong asked with some concern.

"With sufficient strength, luck has little impact," Wang Ling said.

These words were spoken in a very plain tone, but Sun Rong could hear the absolute confidence in them.

She couldn't help but laugh.

Indeed, for Wang Ling, the only difference between randomly getting a hero and drawing runes might be the way he wins.

Not far away, Luo Xinghe and several other team members from other schools sat together, chatting and laughing.

He seemed to have fully recovered, even more composed than before, and was enthusiastically introducing several powerful combinations of Hextech runes to his teammates.

"That Lightning Chain rune, when paired with heroes who have AOE skills, results in explosive damage in team fights."

"There's also Glacier Amplification, which doubles the control effect; if you get it, you've basically won."

"But the most powerful one is the Omnistone. It randomly changes to a different keystone rune every now and then, making for maximum entertainment value..."

He spoke eloquently, displaying the demeanor of a seasoned analyst.

Several team members nodded repeatedly as they listened, their eyes filled with admiration as they looked at him.

But Sun Rong noticed that when Luo Xinghe spoke, his gaze would always drift towards Wang Ling's direction, seemingly unintentionally.

That look in his eyes was very cold.

"He's acting," Sun Rong thought to herself.

I put on an act for everyone, giving the impression that I have put aside my grudges and am ready to focus on studying the game.

But Sun Rong knew he wouldn't let go.
