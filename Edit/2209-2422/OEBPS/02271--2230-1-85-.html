Chapter 2230 Welcome Ceremony

Tomorrow is the recruitment day for Project Center of the Earth. The recruitment will last for four days, and selected project members from all over the world, including elite high school students, scientists, as well as the team led by Fujiwara no Teng, the director of the Nine Heavens Refining Institute and the chief commander of Project Center of the Earth, will all be present.

It can be said that Wang Ling arranged everything perfectly, and the timing was just right.

According to the official plan, he and a group of selected elite high school students will officially enter the inner world through the magic circle on January 31. The specific mission to be carried out will also be announced during the recruitment period.

Of course, in addition to gathering specific personnel and explaining the mission during the four days of the call-up, there is another important purpose: to simulate the environment and conduct adaptive training.

The gravity and climate of the inner Earth are completely different from those on the surface. Therefore, scientists from various cultivation countries have jointly developed a large-scale simulation chamber based on previously collected sample data, intending to provide environmental adaptation training for everyone before entering the inner Earth.

Those who fail the stress test within 72 hours will completely lose their chance to enter the project to the center of the earth.

Of course, this step is actually quite unnecessary and completely unnecessary for Wang Ling, since he can even adapt to the environment required by the universe.

"That's the situation, Master." Zhuo Yi called Wang Ling to report in advance the information he had gathered from the meeting: "There are roughly three stress tests. The first is gravity collapse training, where we will complete various hand seals and spell tests under three times the gravity of Earth."

"Secondly, there is the spiritual pressure penetration test. The authorities will send some powerful individuals to conduct spiritual pressure limit tests on the personnel entering the Earth's Core Project. If they can withstand the spiritual pressure, they will pass. The purpose of this test is to eliminate fear. After all, no one has faced the Earth's Core Beasts so far. They are generally huge and extremely ferocious. Without the ability to withstand pressure, it is definitely not possible."

"The last one is the geomagnetic maze, which requires you to accurately find the escape route using your own senses even when there is spiritual interference."

That's it?

After hearing the report, Wang Ling raised an eyebrow and asked.

"Hmm...that's all..." Zhuo Yi replied, "Master, I have my doubts..."

"I know."

Wang Ling's words were concise and to the point.

He knew what Zhuo Yi was going to say.

Adaptability training is a recent addition by the official team. Wang Ling had never heard of this before when the selection process for entering the Earth's Core Project was underway.

Clearly, the head of the Nine Heavens Refining Academy, the commander-in-chief of the Earth's Core Project, and the eleventh hidden general of the Huaxiu Kingdom, Teng Luchen, may still not have dispelled his doubts about himself.

From the very beginning at the Nine Heavens Teahouse, Wang Ling knew that the other party had begun to doubt him, and in order to verify his guess, he made many tests during the selection process.

Later, Wang Ling secretly supported Li Changzhe from Jingmen Number 8 High School and Zhang Linyan from Dishitian High School, causing the other party to shift their attention to these two young people who seemed to have outstanding talents and could almost be called the pride of heaven.

It's important to know that Wang Ling's support was so strong that the two of them once surpassed Qu Shuling, the top young cultivator in many people's eyes. After the house collapsed, Qu Shuling was locked up and then kidnapped by a force called Honghuang. Currently, his whereabouts are unknown.

That old guy is definitely not easy to deal with...

This was the first time Wang Ling had encountered such a difficult person.

"Master, do you think we should discuss the next steps in our plan?" Zhuo Yi asked over the phone.

After a moment, Wang Lingsi shook his head: "No need, let's take it one step at a time."

He immediately ended the call.

In such situations, the more you act, the more dangerous it becomes. Sometimes, the more you try to hide something, the more suspicious the other person becomes.

Before this Earth Core Project, Wang Ling had Zhuo Yi arrange special training for the Sixty Sects. In addition to helping Sun Rong, Chen Chao, and Guo Hao improve their combat power in a short period of time, there was another purpose: to provide cover for himself.

After all, they have undergone special training, and Chen Chao and Guo Hao have made such great progress in such a short time. So... as mascots who trained with them, it is reasonable for them to look a little stronger than before.

...

After the special training ended, the sixty students selected for the Earth's Core Project gathered at an adaptation training base specially prepared for the project.

This is a mountain-rock symbiotic base, inside which is a huge man-made cave, and outside is a technologically advanced semi-enclosed dome. It uses a sophisticated biomimetic rock layer pouring technique, and some fragments of sacred artifacts are mixed into the concrete to form a golden texture. Even at night, the entire mountain-rock symbiotic base emits a shimmering glow.

Looking at the architecture that combines the beauty of ancient cultivation with modern science and technology, anyone who sees it will be stunned and secretly marvel at the importance the authorities attach to this project at the center of the earth.

As Wang Ling and Sun Rong sat on the fairy boat, looking down at the entire building from afar, a strange expression appeared on both of their faces.

Of course, compared to the shock others felt at the base, what surprised the two was their familiarity with the place...

"I never expected it to be here..." On the special fairy boat of Number 60 High School, Sun Rong looked out the window at Palm Cliff, which was not far from the experimental base. She estimated that the distance between the two coordinates was less than five kilometers.

She knows this place very well...

That's the place where Wang Ling saved her back then...

Originally, Palm Cliff had become a tourist attraction in Songhai, but because the entrance to the Earth's core is located here, it has been temporarily taken over by the Chinese military, and no one is allowed to approach except for personnel related to the Earth's core project.

Wang Ling sighed inwardly.

Sure enough, I used too much force with that palm strike back then.

If his palm strike hadn't pierced through the Earth's core, creating a crack, cultivation nations around the world might still be unable to accurately locate the entrance to the Earth's core using modern cultivation technology.

At this moment, Chen Chao and Guo Hao looked at the magnificent base building in front of them, their faces filled with excitement and even more confidence.

They have now transformed and are no longer the same as they were in the past.

They were representatives of the elite class at Number 60 High School, but looking at all the elite high schools in the country, the other elite high school students on the list were all outstanding individuals, and were the envy of the world.

They never imagined that one day they could participate in missions alongside these elite high school students, let alone that their level could improve so much in such a short time.

To be honest, before being selected for the Earth's Core Project mission, Chen Chao and Guo Hao were even a little apprehensive. Even with Wang Ling as their "mascot," they were still afraid that they would be a burden.

But now, that fear has completely disappeared.

"I wonder how we compare to the elite high school students from the other colleges?" Chen Chao asked excitedly.

"There shouldn't be any problem," Guo Hao said confidently with a smile. "Didn't our master tell us to hold back a bit when we really have to make a move, so as not to hurt others?"

Both of them were excited and looking forward to competing with the elite high school students from other universities in the upcoming stress test.

"You should be able to assess your current strength soon. I heard there will be a welcoming ceremony," Sun Rong said with a smile.

"A welcome ceremony?" Chen Chao and Guo Hao were both taken aback.

Meanwhile, on the special fairy boat belonging to Number 60 High School, Huo Ding, the vice principal of Number 60 High School who was leading the team, also known as Monk Jin Deng, calmly surveyed everything below.

"Watch out, this is a show of force." His soft warning immediately dampened Chen Chao and Guo Hao's excitement, plunging them into a tense atmosphere.

Suddenly, the protective shield outside the experimental base opened, and a steel cannon with a diameter of five meters extended out from it, aiming at the special fairy boat of Number 60 High School, and letting out a loud roar!

"Damn! This is the welcoming ceremony... They're just shelling us?" Chen Chao and Guo Hao were speechless when they saw this.
