Chapter 2320 The Gate to the Abyss Opens!

Under the Blood Moon, the Shadow Walkers' Home was filled with a thick stench of blood and an overwhelming sense of tension. Clearly, most of the newcomers were unsure of what kind of place this was or how they would face the upcoming Abyss Gate mission.

The uncertainty and unknown of this world are too great, and a fear of death is ever-present.

Wang Ling silently followed behind the woman in black robes, Hei 079, all the way to the D-level villa No. 777 assigned to them by the World Central Government.

Another boy, numbered Gray 885, wore thick glasses and was extremely thin. He followed timidly beside them, his eyes showing a particularly obvious sense of fear.

He almost had to jog to keep up with Black 079's pace, glancing back nervously at Wang Ling every now and then, as if he could find a pitiful sense of security from this fellow "gray-clad" companion.

The so-called Class D Villa No. 777 is more of a gray box of unknown material than a villa.

The number "777" flashed bright red on the doorplate.

The interior space is cramped, with only the most basic living facilities. The style is minimalist to the point of being cold, with walls that are a cold, metallic gray. It has a kind of heavy industrial wasteland style, and living here for a long time will only make people feel infinitely oppressed.

"Bang!"

Black079 slammed the door shut, shutting out the unsettling crimson moonlight from outside.

She turned around, crossed her arms, leaned against the cold wall, and looked down at Wang Ling and Gray 885.

Those eyes, heavily made up with eyeshadow, revealed undisguised disgust.

"Listen, you two rookies."

Her voice was hoarse from long-term smoking, and her tone was filled with the arrogance of an elder of the Shadow Walkers' Home: "My name is Lin Susu. In reality, I am the regional manager of Baicaotang Pharmacy Chain, and I am in the late stage of Foundation Establishment. This is the fourth time I have been dragged into this hellhole to participate in this damned Abyss Gate mission."

Lin Susu paused, her gaze sweeping over the two unresponsive faces.

Seeing Wang Ling's calm and collected demeanor, Lin Susu felt inexplicably angry and raised her voice: "The first three times, all my family members died, leaving none behind, so I was forced to rebuild my family."

"What about points?" Wang Ling finally asked.

"If only one person survives in the villa, the points will be reset to zero, and we'll start from scratch. All the famous villas on the list are at least A grade; villas below A grade will only have their grade and number." Lin Susu replied impatiently, "So don't expect me to do anything extra for you. If you want to live, be smart and don't hold me back!"

Gray885 shuddered in fright, quickly lowered his head, and whispered, "Yes... Sister Lin. My name is Zhou Ping. I'm an intern at the Psionic Materials Analysis Institute, and I've only recently entered the late stage of Foundation Establishment..."

Wang Ling didn't speak, but simply nodded slightly.

He intercepted a crucial piece of information: if only one family member completes the Abyss Gate quest, their points will be reset to zero, and they will have to rebuild their family from scratch.

Lin Susu, who had participated in the Abyss Gate mission four times, survived alone each time.

Could this be a coincidence?

At the same time, Wang Ling also clearly sensed that the dark aura surrounding the woman in front of him, who called herself Lin Susu, was far more intense than that of Zhou Ping beside her. Her soul was imbued with a strong will to survive, a greed for power, and a hint of darkness buried deep within.

It seems that the longer one stays in this "House of Shadows" and the more missions one participates in, the more extreme the second personality becomes.

Although Zhou Ping was afraid, compared to these shadowy figures of the black robe level or higher, his soul was still relatively clean.

But Lin Susu had clearly already stepped one foot into the abyss.

"Hmph." Although Lin Susu was somewhat annoyed by Wang Ling's silence, she didn't really care about the thoughts of these two new family members: "The mission starts in about twenty hours. You two can familiarize yourselves with the environment and adjust your mindset."

After saying that, she ignored the two of them and went straight to the second floor, choosing the only room with a private bathroom and slamming the door shut.

The allocation of family members is mandatory. If there were no family member option, Lin Susu would definitely participate in the Abyss Gate mission alone.

In Lin Susu's view, these short-lived "family members" were nothing more than tools and teammates during missions, and there was no need to develop a deep relationship with them. After all, the world behind the door was extremely dangerous, with a frighteningly high mortality rate. Investing emotions and then watching teammates die was an unnecessary torment for Lin Susu.

Zhou Ping looked at the tightly closed door, then at the empty and cold living room.

Finally, he turned to Wang Ling for help: "Brother 999, what should we do next?"

Wang Ling ignored him, sat down on the only metal bench in the living room, and closed his eyes.

While seemingly resting, his spiritual awareness had already silently spread, beginning to explore the mysteries of the villa and the entire "Hidden Shadow House".

His perception penetrated the villa's simple partitioning array, reaching deep into the building materials themselves.

Wang Ling discovered that the gray material that made up the walls and floors was not ordinary reinforced concrete, but a living, wriggling biological tissue. Its surface was covered with a metallic sheen and could even simulate the texture of metal.

At this moment, they are absorbing and transforming the negative emotional energy permeating the air.

This absorption efficiency is significantly enhanced, especially when the emotions of the "shadowers" fluctuate dramatically.

These energies are gathered and continuously purified, and eventually flow towards the Blood Moon.

"A breeding ground for venomous insects, truly deserving of its name." Wang Ling understood.

This place is not only a place to select the strong, but it is also a huge energy collector, providing a continuous stream of evil energy to the evil cultivators and Bai Zhe.

Time passed in deathly silence.

Zhou Ping was restless, glancing at the eternal blood moon outside the window one moment, and then stealing a glance at Wang Ling, who seemed to be in deep meditation.

Finally, the 23-hour countdown ended.

The gray bracelets on the wrists of the three people simultaneously emitted a blinding red light and vibrated violently.

The Abyss Gate Challenge is about to begin!

[Mission World: Deep Mountain Garden]

[Team matching in progress... Matchmaking complete. This mission will be conducted in collaboration with two other families of the same level.]

[Transfer begins!]

A strong sense of spatial pull came over me, several times more intense than when I entered the Shroud of Shadows.

Zhou Ping let out a short exclamation, while Lin Susu gritted her teeth, her face grim.

Wang Ling's projection remained stable, silently recording the spatial coordinate fluctuations during the transmission process.

The scene before my eyes suddenly changed.

The nauseating stench and blood-red moonlight vanished, replaced by a damp, musty smell of decaying vegetation and stale medicinal dregs.

They appeared on a narrow stone path.
