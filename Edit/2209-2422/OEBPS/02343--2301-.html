Chapter 2301 A Simple and Unpretentious Business Battle

Everything was within Wang Ling's prediction. The moment Wang Ming received the order, he pressed the red button without hesitation. The tacit understanding between the two brothers was outrageous.

In an instant, a switch deep within the universe was directly triggered.

With a "whoosh!" sound!

Immediately, an invisible yet incredibly precise and powerful psychic interference ripple, traveling at speeds exceeding the speed of light, pierced straight down from the cultivation satellite suspended above the Number 60 High School!

The target of this interference wave is not the underground "emotional furnace".

Rather, it refers to all the "taste master" devices that are operating wildly.

Like a precise psionic scalpel, it instantly severed the last, potentially fatal, connection between the device and the user's mental core, following the route already laid out by Wang Ling.

"Crack..."

Almost at the same time, inside the Number 60 High School gymnasium.

On the wrists of all the students struggling in agony, the screens of the devices, which had been flashing with an ominous blood-red light, suddenly went out like light bulbs that had been cut off.

The high-frequency vibrations stopped abruptly.

The terrifying suction force that threatened to tear one's soul away vanished as quickly as the tide receded.

"Uh, I feel much better..."

"What on earth happened... I felt like I was about to see my great-grandmother just now!"

The students collapsed to the ground, panting heavily, their faces showing shock and bewilderment.

The intense headache and nausea subsided quickly, though mentally it felt like I had just endured a brutal torture.

But the terrifying experience of having those emotions forcibly removed and consciousness gradually being stripped away from the body finally stopped.

"Success?!" The Aohai water curtain surrounding Sun Rong rippled slightly.

She immediately sensed the disappearance of the evil suction force and her beautiful eyes turned to Wang Ling, who was pretending to be in shock.

Wang Ling then feigned lingering fear, even stumbling slightly before grabbing the railing beside him.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao rushed over: "Lingzi! Are you alright?"

"It's nothing..." Wang Ling shook his head, his voice "weak".

On the judges' panel, Principal Chen Tianxiang and Teacher Pan immediately directed the school doctor and teachers skilled in calming the mind to step forward to treat and examine the students.

Although the scene was chaotic, it had been pulled back from the brink of getting out of control.

"That familiar fluctuation from outer space just now...it seems that Brother Wang Ming's side coordinated their efforts!" Sun Rong said, supporting Wang Ling while glancing at the ceiling, making a quick judgment.

"Damn it, what kind of weird equipment is this? It's so creepy! Luckily we weren't wearing it!" Chen Chao slammed his hand on the railing and cursed, still shaken.

Looking at his classmates groaning all over the ground, especially the members of the "Taste Club," he felt a lingering fear in his heart.

Li Jun stood in the crowd, watching the scene unfold before him, his fists clenched tightly.

He was incredibly grateful that he had managed to break free from that thing's control beforehand.

Through this experience, he fully understood that there may indeed be shortcuts on the path of cultivation, but rather than relying on opportunistic methods to grow quickly, it is better to take solid steps forward one by one.

...

Meanwhile, in the secret underground chamber of the XiBai headquarters.

"What's going on! Why did the energy extraction stop? All user connections are down!!!" Dr. Lynch roared in despair as he watched the large number of user dots on the main screen go dark in an instant!

The sudden drop in the energy curve made him unable to believe what he was seeing!

"Doctor! High-intensity directional psionic interference detected! Source... from a near-Earth orbit satellite! The other party has severed the mental bond between the equipment and the user!" the subordinate reported in alarm.

"Impossible! Our encryption protocol..." Lynch's words were cut short.

In the center of the control panel, the screen representing the core status of the [Emotional Furnace] suddenly changed!

The dark red energy core, which should have been filled with violent negative emotions and was about to explode, not only did not become unstable, but instead emitted a pure and gentle light.

Within the light, faint golden markings, resembling celestial runes, could be seen flowing within.

"What...what is this?!" Lin Qi's pupils contracted sharply, a chill deeper than the failure of the plan rising from the soles of his feet to the top of his head.

He attempted to activate the nest self-destruct program.

[Command denied! Permission verification failed! Core rule sequence has been overridden by higher permission!]

A cold system notification sound rang out.

"No!!!" Lin Qi went completely mad, frantically pounding on the control panel with both hands, but received no response.

His last trump card for revenge not only failed to be used, but it was also taken over by someone else?

At that moment, the core of the purified emotional furnace, guided by Wang Ling's remote will, began its final work.

It no longer absorbs negative emotions, but instead gently sends back the pure spiritual energy that Wang Ling had previously purified of its malicious aspects, along the not-yet-completely-severed spiritual network channels, soothing the minds of all the victims.

This energy, like warm spring water, gently caressed all the users of "Taste Master" who had just experienced a mental storm.

"Ah! That feels so good!"

Inside the training ground, the students who were initially shaken suddenly felt an indescribable sense of peace rising from the bottom of their hearts.

They could feel a warm force comforting their wounded spirits.

It felt as if a ray of sunlight shone down from deep within, dispelling the lingering fear and anxiety.

Even the restlessness and irritability caused by prolonged use of the equipment were quietly smoothed out at this moment.

The pain on their faces gradually subsided, replaced by a tranquility born of exhaustion.

"It feels... so strange, I suddenly feel so calm..."

"It feels like I had a nightmare, and now I'm awake..."

The students looked at each other, and each saw the same confusion in the other's eyes.

The judges and teachers also keenly noticed the change in the students' aura, sensing that their aura had changed from chaotic and restless to peaceful and stable.

Although the spiritual energy was greatly depleted, it seems that the mental aspect has turned out to be a blessing in disguise?

"What... what's going on here?" The old man pushed up his glasses, looking utterly incredulous.

Teacher Pan seemed to be deep in thought, his gaze sweeping across the entire room once more, finally exchanging a knowing look with Principal Chen Tianxiang.

At their level, they can already vaguely sense some clues about certain things.

Sun Rong felt the lingering, pure ripples in the air, and then looked at Wang Ling beside her with his head down. She couldn't help but smile slightly.

Sure enough, it was him again...

He always does this, silently and in ways that no one can understand, defusing the most dangerous crises without anyone knowing.

And this time, they actually gave us some benefits instead?

...

Inside the underground chamber.

Dr. Lynch sat slumped in his chair, his face ashen.

It's over, it's all over.

The plan failed completely, the nest went out of control, and it didn't even have the chance to perish together in the end.

He could even imagine the fury of the Primordial Headquarters upon learning the news, and the fate of being "cleaned up" that awaited him.

"Hehe...hahaha..." He let out a desperate and manic laugh.

Just then, the heavy alloy door of the secret chamber, along with the powerful restrictions on it, turned to dust silently, like a piece of paper thrown into lava.

A pale blue figure, exuding an aura of cold desolation, appeared at the doorway like a ghost.
