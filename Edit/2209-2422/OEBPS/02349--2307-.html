Chapter 2307 The Mirror Realm of Inner Demons

The pure white space and the eerie cave scene vanished without a trace in an instant.

It was as if everyone had traveled from one dream to another.

The passage of time has lost all meaning in this space...

This kind of experience, which can be described as "infinite terror," could only be created by evil cultivators from the primordial era.

At this moment, everyone is facing an infinitely extending maze composed of countless smooth mirror walls.

The mirror was cold, clearly reflecting the figures of everyone present.

Li Jing, Lin Feng, the fat man, Granny Zhang, the skinny girl, the bespectacled man, Xiao Li from the construction site, the high school student, and Wang Ling, who is possessed by Yan Ting...

However, the image in the mirror was different from their true selves; it was the very inner demon they least wanted to face...

And these people in the mirror were giving them strange smiles.

The fat man was the first to let out a piercing scream, pointing at the mirror in front of him, his bulky body trembling violently.

The "he" in the mirror was no longer the greasy and cunning middle-aged man, but a young man wearing tattered miner's clothes, his face covered in coal dust, his eyes vacant, and clear marks on his neck.

Behind the young man, two other equally lifeless figures could be vaguely seen swaying.

"A ghost! There's a ghost!" The fat man was so frightened that he backed away and fell to the ground with a thud, his crotch instantly getting wet and emitting a foul odor.

He remembered everything.

That was one of the three young miners he caused to die in an accident in order to monopolize the vein!

It was the deepest nightmare he had experienced in the middle of the night in recent years!

Next up is Lin Feng.

The image of the programmer monk was no longer reflected in the mirror before him.

Instead, it was a man dressed in a suit, with a deathly pale face and a spiritual dagger stuck in his chest.

The man stared intently at Lin Feng, his lips moving silently, and a sharp whistle rang in Lin Feng's mind:

"Why...why did you steal my design...and push me off the building..."

Lin Feng was struck dumb, his face turned ashen, and he was almost suffocating.

This sin, which he had deliberately forgotten and numbed with alcohol and "Sleep Treasure," was now laid bare.

"No...it wasn't me...I didn't mean to..." He crouched down, clutching his head, his words incoherent.

He also remembered the colleague he had unintentionally killed.

After the incident that night, the colleague came to his workstation and argued with him.

Then the two got into a scuffle, and the colleague crashed hard into the floor-to-ceiling window behind him, falling straight to the ground from a height...

Moreover, that colleague's cultivation level was not high; he was only at the Body Refinement stage, and there was almost no chance of survival after falling from a height.

...

At this moment, Wang Ling continued to use his spiritual sense to scan the inner demons of the crowd.

He saw the image in Grandma Zhang's mirror: a broken puppet made of gears and pipes.

A dull soul crystal was embedded in the core of the puppet.

Wang Ling could tell that it was extracted from the body of her son, who had died many years ago, using some kind of forbidden magic.

At that moment, the puppet in the mirror cried out again and again in her son's voice, "Mom! Why...why did you turn me into a puppet..."

Tears welled up in the old woman's cloudy eyes instantly, making her look ten years older. She murmured, "Little Bao! It's Mommy's fault... Mommy just wanted to keep you here..."

She could not bear the pain of losing her beloved son.

Driven by intense selfishness, she chose this extreme method to keep her child.

But the price is that her son will be permanently imprisoned in this puppet, never to be reborn.

Next to Grandma Zhang, in the mirror of the petite girl, was a burning laboratory ruin.

Several charred human figures wailed in the fire.

A voice shrieked in her mind: "It's all your fault! You made a mistake! You killed us!"

The girl curled up, shivering, tears silently streaming down her face.

She hugged herself tightly, as if trying to hide herself away.

...

The next man with glasses saw his mother lying on the hospital bed, barely alive.

Unable to afford the expensive life-extending pills, she looked at him with disappointed and pained eyes.

What Xiao Li, a construction worker, saw was his coworker who had accidentally pushed down the scaffolding in an attempt to secure payment for the project.

These coworkers, like vengeful spirits, waved their hands and bared their teeth in the mirror, trying to kill him outright.

What the high school student finally saw was his genius neighbor, who had become mentally impaired due to the damage to his Dao heart after he destroyed the spiritual plant...

At this moment, everyone's deepest, most fearful, and most guilty images are mercilessly reflected and magnified by this inner demon mirror!

The sound of crying instantly filled the entire mirror field.

A fragile bond that had just been built up through the Q&A session.

Under this direct confrontation with the darkest corners of one's heart, everything crumbled instantly.

Apart from Wang Ling.

At this moment, he possessed Yan Ting's body.

The mirror in front of me also changed.

But what was reflected in the mirror was not the bankrupt, abandoned company that Yan Ting remembered, the one that had left him with no wife or children...

After a series of ripples, what appeared inside was actually the scene of the Devouring Toad descending and crushing the century-old flagship instant noodle store...

This is his deepest childhood memory, and it can indeed be called a psychological shadow.

Wang Ling: "..."

He was speechless.

However, the core of this "Weaving Web" is clearly limited to this.

Ultimately, they were unable to discern his true nature as a Celestial King.

And then, the next second.

"Click".

A soft sound.

The mirror in front of Wang Ling shattered instantly.

It didn't explode; rather, it vanished as if it had been erased.

This scene was caught by Li Jing, who was closest to him and on the verge of collapse, through her peripheral vision.

Li Jing's inner demon is a crucial instance of falsifying scientific research data.

That experience led to the failure of the entire project and brought shame upon the team.

She was painfully confronting the self in the mirror that was blaming her, when out of the corner of her eye she caught a glimpse of the mirror in front of Wang Ling... which had mysteriously disappeared?

Yan Ting himself remained standing there, his eyes seemingly filled with a sense of bewilderment, yet deep down he possessed a composure that stood out starkly against the despairing atmosphere around him.

"Mr. Yan...you?" Li Jing couldn't help but ask.

Wang Ling manipulated Yan Ting to turn his head and sighed, "This mirror... seems to have malfunctioned, and then it shattered by itself."

He must maintain Yan Ting's persona: an ordinary businessman who has experienced great ups and downs, whose will is slightly stronger than the average person, but who is by no means extraordinary.

"It's broken?" Li Jing was taken aback.

She looked towards where the mirror had originally been placed.

It was completely empty.
