Chapter 2418 The Competition Begins

In section B of the stands, Diu Lei Zhenjun and Zhuo Yi were exchanging telepathic messages.

"How is your brother doing lately?" asked Lord Diu Lei.

"It's great." Zhuo Yi said with a smile, "Master is adapting well to his current life."

"Your brother seems to be living a very comfortable life." Lord Diu Lei raised an eyebrow. "How about I arrange something interesting for you? A few secret realms have just been developed at the overseas branch of the War Sect recently, which could help your brother pass the time..."

"Let's not..." Zhuo Yi quickly waved his hand.

Lord Thunderclap shrugged regretfully: "Alright. But speaking of which, how does your brother plan to play in today's match?"

Zhuo Yi looked towards the waiting area: "Master said, play normally."

"Fight normally?" Lord Thunderclap laughed. "That Zhao kid is in for trouble. But if your brother reveals too much, will there be any trouble?"

"With Senior Wang Ming here, there shouldn't be any major problems," Zhuo Yi said. "Besides, Master knows what he's doing."

Lord Diu Lei nodded and asked no further questions.

He had absolute confidence in Wang Ling.

Just kidding, that's his elder brother, a being who can make the entire cultivation world tremble.

He came today mainly to support Wang Ling and also to watch the excitement.

After all, he earned the title of "Master of Courting Death" for a reason; wherever there's excitement, there he is, the Lord of Throwing Lightning.

...

On the other side of the audience seats, Mo Xuan put down his binoculars, his face solemn.

"Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan, War Sect Master Diu Lei Zhenjun..." He muttered to himself, "This royal decree is indeed not simple."

Luo Xinghe's voice came through the earpiece: "What do we do now? Should the plan continue?"

Mo Xuan remained silent for a few seconds.

According to the original plan, they were to activate the jammer fifteen minutes into the match to create chaos and force Wang Ling to take action.

But now that Sword Saint and Thunder Lord are present, either of these two big shots could easily crush them.

"Since things have come to this, we have no choice but to bite the bullet and go for it." Mo Xuan finally made his decision.

...

Time passed by minute by minute.

At 2:00 PM sharp, the host announced the start of the competition.

The first match of the quarterfinals was not between Wang Ling and Zhao Wuji, but between two other pairs of players.

However, the audience's attention remained mostly focused on the B section of the stands and the waiting area.

Every now and then, people would secretly take photos and whisper among themselves.

Chen Chao was restless. He looked at the competition on the stage and then at the big shots around him, feeling like he was dreaming.

Guo Hao gradually calmed down and began to focus on analyzing the match data.

His portable recorder was already activated, even though he knew it might not be very useful, but he still had to make the necessary preparations.

Li Changzhe looked excited and nudged Gao Tianming next to him: "Old Gao, do you think Zhao Wuji will be scared to death by today's scene?"

Gao Tianming's puppet turned its head and answered in a mechanical voice: "According to Zhao Wuji's past competition data, his mental quality is rated A, and the probability of him collapsing directly is less than 13.7%. However, under the current pressure, the probability of him underperforming increases to 68.2%."

"That means he'll most likely mess up!" Li Changzhe grinned. "Serves him right! He was so arrogant before."

Zhang Linyan crossed her arms and said calmly, "The competition hasn't even started yet, so don't rush to conclusions. Zhao Wuji is so high-profile, he must have some trump cards up his sleeve."

Gong Xuan closed his eyes to rest, only uttering two words: "Boring."

Su Xingyue, meanwhile, was observing Yi Jianchuan and Diu Lei Zhenjun, seemingly deep in thought.

Sun Rong's attention was entirely on Wang Ling. She would occasionally glance towards the waiting area, her eyes filled with worry.

Wang Muyu was very excited, swinging his little feet as he ate potato chips and asked Yi Jianchuan, "Master, Master, can Brother Wang Ling win?"

Yi Jianchuan patted his head: "Don't worry, of course we'll win."

His tone was firm, without the slightest doubt.

Upon hearing this, Lord Diu Lei smiled and approached, "Little Mu Yu, you have so much confidence in him?"

Wang Muyu nodded vigorously: "Of course! He's the best!"

That adoring look in his eyes made Lord Diu Lei laugh heartily.

...

It was 2:40.

After the first two matches ended, the host came back on stage.

"Next up is the third match of the quarterfinals, and also the most anticipated match of the day! It's Wang Ling from Number 60 High School versus Zhao Wuji from Tixiu High School! Please come to the stage!"

The audience erupted in applause.

Under the watchful eyes of countless people, Wang Ling and Zhao Wuji walked out of the waiting area at the same time and stepped onto the E-03 battle stage.

Wang Ling was still wearing that dark blue tracksuit, holding half a bag of instant noodles in his hand.

Zhao Wuji was wearing a black jersey, with tight muscles and sharp eyes.

The two stood in the center of the stage, ten meters apart.

The referee walked to the center and routinely read out the rules.

But this time, no one's attention was on the rules.

VIP seats, audience seats, media area... countless eyes were fixed on the stage.

Sword Saint Yi Jianchuan sat up straight.

A playful smile appeared on the corner of Lord Diu Lei's mouth.

Sun Rong clenched her fists.

Chen Chao held his breath.

Zhao Tieshan gripped the handrail tightly.

Luo Xinghe stared intently at the screen in the private room.

Zhuo Yi and Jiu Gong Liangzi exchanged a glance.

Wang Muyu stopped eating potato chips and stared intently at the stage.

In complete silence, the referee finally finished reading the rules and looked at the two players:

"Both sides, get ready—the match begins!"

The moment the referee finished speaking.

Zhao Wuji moved.

Without holding back, the full power of the third level of the Vajra Subduing Demon Body cultivation erupted!

Boom—

Earthy yellow spiritual energy surged from his body, forming a visible shockwave around him.

The specially designed ground of the battle arena was cracked like a spider web as it was stepped on, and gravel flew everywhere.

In the front row of the audience, several students with lower cultivation levels turned pale from the oppressive aura and subconsciously leaned back.

"Going all out right from the start?" The bespectacled female judge in the VIP section frowned. "That doesn't fit the fighting style of a body cultivator."

The white-haired old man squinted: "He's trying to seize the rhythm. He wants to use overwhelming force to suppress Wang Ling and not give the other party a chance to use his skills."

On the stage, Zhao Wuji's figure had already disappeared from the spot.

When it reappeared, it was already three meters directly in front of Wang Ling!

His right fist shot out like a cannonball, hurtling towards Wang Ling's face with a sharp, piercing sound.

The force of the punch compressed the air, forming a pale yellow cone of air, and the strong wind made Wang Ling's bangs fly wildly backward.

This punch was at least 30% faster than last week!

"So fast!" Chen Chao exclaimed from the audience.

The screen of the portable recorder in Guo Hao's hand was flashing wildly, and the value of the spiritual energy fluctuation instantly broke through the threshold of the late Foundation Establishment stage, approaching the early Golden Core stage.

In section B, Sun Rong frowned slightly, while Wang Muyu, standing next to her, kept the potato chips in his mouth, looking calm.

Yi Jianchuan remained calm, only lightly tapping his knee with his right index finger.

Lord Diu Lei pushed up his monocle, a curious smile curving his lips.

On stage.

Wang Ling looked at the fist that was rapidly enlarging in front of him, but did not dodge.

He raised his left hand, fingers spread, to meet the fist.

Just before the fist strikes the palm, the wrist rotates inward, and the palm traces a semi-circle.

Zhao Wuji felt as if his punch had hit cotton. The force that was powerful enough to shatter a steel plate was pulled away by a strange force and involuntarily changed direction.

The fist grazed past Wang Ling's left ear, the force of the blow making Wang Ling's cheek sting.

But that's all.

Wang Ling's left hand pressed down smoothly, his five fingers hooking Zhao Wuji's wrist.

One pull, one push.

The movements were so fluid, as if they had been rehearsed a thousand times.

Zhao Wuji felt a subtle force coming from his wrist, which caused him to stumble forward two steps.

He reacted extremely quickly, and his left fist slammed towards Wang Ling's ribs!

But Wang Ling seemed to have anticipated his move. The instant Zhao Wuji's left shoulder twitched, he released his right hand and slid half a step to the right.

His left fist missed again.

The two passed each other.

The whole process took less than two seconds.

The entire room fell silent.

Then, a deafening gasp erupted from the audience.

"You dodged it?!"
