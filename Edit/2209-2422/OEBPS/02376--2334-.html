Chapter 2334 Esports Father-Son Game

Because of Ma Yu's open provocation, the atmosphere inside the venue instantly became tense.

Chen Chao is a man who gets easily angered, and when Ma Yu pointed his finger at him and said they should have a father-son competition, his temper flared up instantly.

The key issue is that Ma Yu is quite sarcastic, constantly muttering, "Tch, the strictest father, huh? We'll find out who's the father after we've had the beating!"

"You're going to die!"

Chen Chao rolled up the sleeves of his school uniform, revealing his muscular arms: "Come on, today I'm going to teach you a lesson and show you what it means to be outmatched!"

Ma Yu scoffed, "Hmph, so stubborn! Wait till you get 0-10, then let's see if you're still laughing!"

Mr. Ma sighed and quickly apologized to the students from Number 60 High School: "I'm sorry, everyone, that's just how Xiao Ma is... It's all my fault. I'm usually too busy with work and haven't been able to discipline him properly."

"It's alright, Uncle. It's good to take this opportunity to deflate his arrogance." Sun Rong responded politely with a smile. As the future head of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group, she had her own vision.

Moreover, letting Chen Chao teach this arrogant guy a lesson will also allow everyone to gain a more intuitive understanding of the actual gameplay of "Honor of Kings," especially since Wang Muyu has never played this game before. This is an excellent opportunity.

She signaled to the staff to activate the two adjacent top-tier psionic simulators.

"The rules are simple. The map is the standard ‘Ancient Ruins' 1v1 duel map. First blood, one tower, and one hundred CS. You win if you achieve any one of these conditions. As for lane selection, everyone can choose the same hero to fight... Since you started this, let Chen Chao choose. You don't have any objections, right?" Sun Rong looked at Ma Yu, her tone calm.

Ma Yu wanted to retort, but he was too proud to admit it, so he could only bite the bullet and say, "Whatever! I, Hero Sea, can crush him in any position!"

Chen Chao chuckled, rubbing his hands together as he lay down in the gaming cabin, saying, "Kid, let's see how your grandpa Chen teaches you a lesson!"

The cabin door closed, and a ghostly blue light flickered on the surface of the cabin, instantly drawing the two people's consciousness into the game world.

A huge holographic projection screen lit up in the center of the venue, clearly displaying the game's visuals and audio commentary, making it convenient for outsiders to watch.

Guo Hao sidled up to Wang Ling and Sun Rong, crossed his arms, and laughed, "Chao'er, don't let his usual recklessness fool you, he's got a real knack for the top lane. When he duos with me, he goes solo eight out of ten times. He's got solid fundamentals in tanking, trading blows, and controlling the lane. He also has a deep champion pool, from heavy warriors to magic tanks, there's no champion he can't play."

Sun Rong nodded in surprise: "It seems that our team's top laner position is settled."

Wang Muyu excitedly waved his little fist: "Go, Brother Chen Chao!"

As Sun Rong said, he had never played this game before, so Wang Muyu watched intently.

With Wang Muyu's learning ability, after these few matches, he will know how to operate the hero. The rest is to familiarize himself with the hero and improve the team's synergy.

In the game, the hero selection screen.

Chen Chao didn't hesitate at all and immediately locked onto a colossal creature covered in dark red rock armor and wielding a huge lava pillar—[Lava Monster Moot].

This is a typical top lane tank hero. He is tough and durable, has a shield, long-range poke, and is not afraid of melee combat. His ultimate can instantly dash and knock up players, and also has a certain burst damage.

Once fully developed, unleash a barrage of skills followed by a few basic attacks, and you can easily take down your opponent in one combo.

"Tch, what a point-scoring beast!" Ma Yu sneered from the other side.

Seemingly to highlight his aggressiveness, he chose a wildly styled warrior wielding two axes—[Berserker Aura's Father].

This hero is known for his powerful sustained damage output and immunity to crowd control, and is theoretically extremely strong in early-game duels.

Upon entering the loading screen, Ma Yu's irritating voice blared through the audio channel, echoing throughout the entire venue.

"See that? I got the limited-edition 'Twilight of the Gods' skin! It cost me a full 10 spirit stones to pull it! You, you piece of trash, just have the base skin, right? You poor bastard!"

"Wait, I'll learn Q [Reverse Throw] at level one, and I'll throw an axe at you until you're dead!"

"Level 3 is a guaranteed solo kill! I'm telling you! Even Jesus couldn't save him!"

Chen Chao's microphone indicator light flashed, but he calmed down instead: "Oh. Does skin increase attack power? If not, what are you talking about?"

These words left Ma Yu speechless with anger, and she huffed and puffed without saying another word.

The competition has officially begun!

The two heroes appeared at the intersection of the top lane minion waves.

As expected, Ma Yu learned the Q skill [Reverse Throw] at level one and quickly stepped forward, attempting to use the throwing axe to whittle down Chen Chao's stone man's health.

However, Chen Chao's positioning was exceptionally cunning, as if he could predict Ma Yu's movements and always managed to dodge them smoothly, causing Ma Yu's axe to either hit the minions or miss completely. He even deliberately prevented Ma Yu from picking up the axe to reset the cooldown time.

If it hits once or twice, the damage is negligible compared to the passive shield that the stone golem has.

In contrast, Chen Chao, controlling the stone man, calmly and deliberately delivered the finishing blows.

Every melee soldier and every ranged soldier was turned into gold coins by his steady hand.

His finishing touch skills were on full display at this moment, almost flawless.

Chen Chao is currently on a hot streak. After returning home from the Underworld event, he had nothing to do but do basic character customization and play games all day, which has improved his skills even more than before.

Ma Yu initially tried to interfere, but he quickly realized something was wrong.

This stone golem not only finishes off enemies steadily, but also occasionally uses the skill [Barbarian Strike] to reset its basic attack and quickly clear out low-health minions.

What bothered him even more was that Chen Chao always managed to time his attacks perfectly precisely when he wanted to finish him off.

Either go up and use your E skill [Earthquake] to slam the ground and slow him down, causing him to miss his last hit; or rely on your passive rock shield to tank one of his basic attacks and finish off his siege minions instead.

This is a new mechanism introduced in "Honor of Kings" this year, which allows you to finish off enemy minions and gain economy.

"You son of a bitch... don't eat my soldiers!" Ma Yu shouted angrily into the voice chat.

Chen Chao laughed: "What, did you sign this minion wave with your name on it? Whoever gets it gets it, don't you know the rules? Or is it that our genius can't adapt to the new version? I've never seen a genius with such poor adaptability!"

Ma Yu was so angry he almost spat out a mouthful of blood.

He tried to force a trade, but without the support of equipment in the early game, he couldn't gain any advantage in the trade. Instead, due to his impatience, he missed several minions.

After the first wave of minions was cleared, Chen Chao almost completely farmed all the minions, while Ma Yu only farmed three.

A slight gap has already formed in terms of economics and experience.

Guo Hao, who was watching the game, pointed at the screen and laughed: "Look, Chao'er is starting to play dirty. He's controlling the lane and not letting the minions into the tower. Ma Yu is having a really tough time. If he dares to come up, he'll get beaten up."

As expected, Chen Chao perfectly controlled the minion wave, positioning it close to his own tower.

Ma Yu could only watch helplessly as the minions were on the verge of death from afar, but dared not approach. He could only cower at the back of the minion wave and rely on his skill to absorb the minions' health to recover some health.

Occasionally, when someone tries to throw an axe to make amends, Chen Chao often blocks their way.

"Damn it! Come out and fight if you dare!" Ma Yu roared in impotence.

Chen Chao ignored him completely and leisurely added, "Don't worry, Dad is saving up for your tuition."

Finally, the two sides made it back to the city for supplies for the first time.

With his perfect last-hit and the automatic payout from the system, Chen Chao immediately pulled out his first core item: [Steel Lungs]!

Upon seeing this equipment, the spectators outside the stadium all wore expressions of "I knew it!"

[The Steel of the Lungs]:

+1000 Health Points

Unique Passive - Molten Forge: Your next basic attack deals bonus physical damage equal to 8% of your maximum health (30-second cooldown). Each successful trigger of this effect adds 20 "Forge Stacks" to this item. Every 100 "Forge Stacks" permanently grants this item an additional 100 health.

This item is a powerful tool for top lane tank heroes to stay in lane in certain matchups. It provides a huge health boost and has extremely high scaling. Once stacked, it becomes an impenetrable steel fortress in the late game.

Ma Yu glanced at the shop and at her meager gold coins, only buying a small item that increased base health and attack power and a few bottles of red medicine, instantly widening the equipment gap between her and Chen Chao.

Back online, Ma Yu's eyelids twitched when he saw the [Steel Lung] item on the Stone Golem, but he still retorted defiantly, "Building tank items? Coward! Watch me shatter your turtle shell!"

He thought that with his hero's healing mechanics, he could gain an advantage in a fight.

However, reality is cruel.

Chen Chao didn't give him a chance at all, just standing right next to the minion wave.

If Ma Yu dared to come up, he would unleash a basic attack with the passive effect of "Steel Lungs" on him.

"Bang!"

With a muffled thud, his health bar visibly dropped a small portion!

This injury startled Ma Yu.

That wasn't the end of it. After Chen Chao finished his attack, he immediately followed up with his E skill, Earthquake, to reduce Ma Yu's attack speed. Then, while enduring Ma Yu's axe and auto-attacks, he slowly retreated.

Ma Yu's attack speed was reduced, significantly decreasing his damage, and the Stone Golem also had a passive shield.

After this round of personnel changes, Ma Yu has really lost out.

"Damn! What kind of damage is this?!" Ma Yu looked at his health bar, which had dropped by nearly a third, while the stone golem had hardly lost any health. He was starting to panic.

Chen Chao looked at the "20th floor" notification flashing on [Steel Lung], nodded in satisfaction, and continued to finish off the enemy.

The ensuing laning phase turned into a performance of the elderly Chen Chao teasing the mischievous boy.
