Chapter 2246 Ripples in the Underworld

Wang Ling's flat, almost monotonous "hmm" echoed clearly through the mysterious spiritual wave medium within this absolutely tranquil fortress deep in the earth's core.

The moment the words left his mouth, Wang Ming's barely suppressed anxiety surged forth like a barrage of bullets:

"Finally found your encrypted channel. You seem to be living a comfortable life..." Wang Ming said sarcastically.

"Get to the point..." Wang Ling's thoughts interrupted his rambling.

Wang Ming immediately stopped, his voice urgent and low: "That old fox Lao Teng is going crazy because he can't recover the key data. Right now, the communication in the Earth's core basically relies on the shortwave of the backup bracelet that I developed, which is relayed by the spiritual wave signal. The range is limited and the delay is huge, almost like that of primitive people."

He took a deep breath, his mind heavy with unprecedented seriousness: "Just now, the base forcibly restarted the most primitive deep psionic spectrum detection network, using passive receiving mode, for fear of another equipment failure. As a result, it detected two extremely faint anomalies!"

"One is an annihilation wave that erupted twice deep within the Earth's core... How should I put it... Its intensity was ridiculously high, yet its frequency spectrum was strangely stable, far exceeding the energy level of that monster in the passage just now! It highly matches the frequency of the abnormal interference remnants from when we were on the Immortal Ship! The direction is very close to the residual heat signal from when you disappeared... It's definitely you again..."

Wang Ling didn't speak, but simply hummed in agreement, tacitly acknowledging the annihilation of the underworld messenger.

"Hiss..." Wang Ming gasped, "I knew it was you, you monster, who made the move... But that's not the key! The key is the second anomaly!"

Wang Ming's thoughts suddenly surged: "The second faint fluctuation detected by the base... is the forced closure reaction of the Netherworld passage! It happened less than three breaths after the first annihilation wave erupted! Moreover, the closure point is extremely deep, deeper than all the fissures we have observed before!"

Wang Ling frowned almost imperceptibly, recalling the old will that had tried to intervene and plead for leniency but then retreated in panic.

"Do you know what this means, bro?"

Wang Ming's tone carried a hint of suspicion, "Previously, we all thought the Underworld was just infiltrating through the gaps in the rules of the Earth's core, engaging in small-scale skirmishes by scattered forces. But now it's different. This will that can forcibly open deep passages near the range of your power's influence is definitely not to be underestimated! It is very likely... one of the Domain Lords under the Underworld Lord [Wan Shi]! Or even... a projection of Wan Shi's own will!"

"You know this? The underworld?" Wang Ling was taken aback.

"Initially, this mysterious organization was discovered by one of the Ten Generals of Huaxiu Kingdom, and it is very likely related to the Earth's core. Your brother has a very high level of authorization, so naturally I know about it."

"......"

"Now, the true ruling class of the underworld has probably been alarmed because of your intervention."

Wang Ming's voice became exceptionally serious: "The resources they expended to temporarily open a passage at that depth and maintain its stability are unimaginable. Their efforts are so extensive that their goal cannot be as simple as a test or protecting an envoy! I highly suspect that their real goal is to find a vessel that can contain a massive amount of psionic energy."

Wang Ling: "..." Isn't this describing himself?

"These high-ranking beings in the Underworld are extremely arrogant and vengeful. By eliminating one of their high-ranking henchmen, you have slapped their entire class in the face. More importantly, the power you displayed... is both a threat and a huge key to them!"

Wang Ming spoke rapidly: "Deep within the Earth's core lies countless ancient secrets, even those from the beginning of the universe. Some forbidden areas are inaccessible even to the underworld, requiring specific conditions or powerful energy keys to open. In their eyes, your power might be the universal key to unlocking some ancient treasure or forbidden land! They might be testing you, or they might be scheming, trying to drag you into one of their plans, making you an aid in their acquisition of the Earth's core's secrets, or even... ultimately sacrificing you!"

"In short," Wang Ming took a deep breath, "the intervention of high-ranking beings in the Underworld has completely changed the nature of the game! This is no longer a simple survival competition or a record-keeping mission! Old Man Teng currently only has preliminary suspicions about the intervention of the Underworld's high-ranking beings, without direct evidence, but I feel that he will soon realize the serious escalation of the situation."

"knew."

Wang Ling's mind returned to calm. Keys, conspiracies, covetousness... these words did not stir much turmoil in his heart.

Power is a double-edged sword; it's normal for it to attract covetousness.

Moreover, no matter how he listened, it sounded like the underworld was coming to cause him trouble; it was clearly the underworld.

"Also..." Wang Ming's tone suddenly relaxed a bit, "Although the main force was scattered, I used the spare jade talisman to initially create a regional network map. Miss Sun Rong and the others are not too far from you... well, the straight-line distance isn't too far, in an area called [Illusory Crystal Forest]. They seem safe for the time being, and I'm trying to make a map record. Chen Chao and Guo Hao, those two rascals, probably fell and got all bruised and battered about fifty miles southwest of you in [Lava Ridge], but they're still full of energy, and you can hear them yelling from far away... You can decide what to do with them yourself."

"Hmm," Wang Ling responded, acknowledging the information.

His spiritual sense could indeed scan the general area, but Wang Ming's precise location information was still useful, especially for Sun Rong's side, which, although safe, was close to a crystal area with chaotic energy.

"Okay, that's all for now. I have to deal with Old Teng's endless questions, he looks so grumpy... I'll hang up the channel for now, I'll contact you if there's any new information." Wang Ming's words carried a hint of helplessness.

"That's right!" Wang Ming suddenly exclaimed loudly.

"?" Wang Ling asked, puzzled.

"If you see any nice jade or anything like that, get one for your sister-in-law, Zhai Yin." After saying that, Wang Ming proactively disconnected the encrypted connection.

"..." Wang Ling didn't expect that this man would be thinking about women while working. He had really changed.

Absolute tranquility returned to the cave, with only the distant sound of the Mountain Moving Team diligently rubbing ore.

Wang Ling, holding a half-full glass of juice, walked to the rock wall that he had sculpted into a huge floor-to-ceiling crystal window.

Outside the cave window, in the dark subterranean world, various glowing minerals, fungi, and crystals are like stars adorning black velvet.

The higher echelons of the underworld? Coveting?

His eyes were calm and undisturbed.

It's best not to come.

If you absolutely must come...

Wang Ling's gaze fell upon the deep, boundless darkness outside the window, and a glimmer of light, quieter than the abyss, flashed briefly in the depths of his pupils.

Disrupting his peaceful life or disturbing him, no matter what realm the ruler is, the final result will be complete and silent disappearance.
