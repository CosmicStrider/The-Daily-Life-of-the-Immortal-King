Chapter 2219 I don't care whether I live or die, I just want you to die!

"You've set up a giant array that's already absorbing the spiritual energy of the entire nation, yet you're also collecting the spiritual energy of all sorts of creatures in this cave. What is your intention?" Sun Rong suppressed the surging fear in her heart and the nausea rising in her stomach, her voice trembling as she asked loudly.

Her hands were clenched into fists, her nails almost digging into her palms, and her eyes revealed a hint of panic and vigilance.

"Anyway, you're already dead, so telling you won't hurt."

Meng Nian leaned against the railing, her posture languid yet exuding an arrogant air. She slowly took out an old-fashioned pipe, put it to her lips, took a light puff, and slowly exhaled a perfectly round smoke ring.

The smoke rings drifted slowly through the air, as if proclaiming her composure. She looked down at Wang Ling and Sun Rong with disdain, as if she were looking at two ants.

Then, she slowly said, "I like to plan ahead and have a backup plan; it's just my usual approach."

Wang Ling saw that she was releasing a ghostly aura, which permeated the air as if it were real, carrying a slight chill.

Unlike the overwhelming aura of the primordial heavens emanating from the Eternal One, which seemed to convey the vicissitudes of time and the majesty of heaven and earth;

Unlike the divinity of the gods, it is a noble and mysterious power that seems to be able to control everything;

Unlike the pure and holy aura of the Holy Race, it is a kind of purity that makes people feel peaceful and serene;

Unlike the aura of ancient cosmic overlords emanating from the descendants of the dragons, which is a powerful aura filled with oppression.

Meng Nian's aura was a chilling, sinister one that seemed to reach straight to the underworld, capable of absorbing one's soul completely, like a deep abyss of endless darkness, sending shivers down one's spine.

Similarly, this aura does not belong to any of the Great Daos that Wang Ling currently knows.

Earlier in the ward, Wang Ling had already sensed that the power in Meng Nian's body did not come from him, and now he had an even clearer answer in his heart.

Similar to selling her soul to a "demon," Meng Nian's power comes entirely from a third party, granting her strength close to the Ancestral Realm. Based solely on what Wang Ling has currently sensed, Meng Nian's strength has already reached the Human Ancestor level.

In the current human cultivation world on Earth, he is an existence that can walk sideways. Thinking of this, Wang Ling's eyes narrowed slightly, but he showed no fear whatsoever.

Wang Ling glanced around at the creatures sealed in glass jars. The laboratory was filled with a nauseating smell, and the glass jars shimmered with an eerie light.

He used Wang Tong's ability to rewind everything that had happened in the bio-illusion laboratory.

None of these beings were weaker than a Dao Venerable. They had all been tricked into this terrifying laboratory by Meng Nian through various means, and then sealed in these glass jars, becoming nourishment for the giant egg to replenish its spiritual energy. Their faces were contorted, as if they were filled with pain and resentment before their deaths.

"The Underworld." Suddenly, Wang Ling uttered two words. Although his voice was not loud, it was exceptionally clear in the silent laboratory, causing Meng Nian's pupils to dilate as she stood by the railing. Her body stiffened slightly, and a hint of barely perceptible panic flashed across her face.

Sun Rong, standing to the side, looked puzzled. Her brows furrowed slightly, and her eyes were filled with confusion: "Wang Ling, what is this?"

"She must be a messenger from the underworld."

Wang Tong's ability is all-encompassing, reaching through eternity. Even if there is knowledge that Wang Ling does not understand, Wang Tong can easily analyze it. However, using Wang Tong's divine power to analyze unknown information will consume Wang Ling's lifespan.

However, Wang Ling's lifespan is nearly unlimited, so no matter how it is used, it will not cause direct harm to Wang Ling.

For other cultivators, shortening their lifespan is the most damaging way to consume themselves, but for Wang Ling, this insignificant loss of lifespan is not so important.

"You actually know my identity?" Meng Nian was quite surprised. Among the many beings she had trapped in this laboratory, Wang Ling was the first to directly discern her identity. A hint of surprise appeared on her face, but she quickly regained her composure, a ruthless glint in her eyes.

The reason why the major cultivation nations formed their own cultivation forces to go to the Earth's core is not only to seize the various spiritual plants that have become extinct on the surface and the rich cultivation resources hidden in the Earth's core world, but also because there is an even more important purpose: that there may be a passage to the underworld in the Earth's core world.

Originally, this was a hypothesis that only existed in cultivation-themed academic papers and journals. If Wang Ling hadn't personally witnessed Meng Nian emanating a ghostly aura from the underworld, he wouldn't have been sure if the underworld truly existed.

To protect the lineage of the Human Ancestor cultivators, Wang Daozu sealed many exceptionally talented Eternal Beings within the Eternal Corpse Wrapping Scroll, awaiting their resurrection after thousands of years. However, a considerable number of Eternal Beings, not favored by Wang Daozu, did not have their souls dissipate after their physical demise, but instead went to the Underworld.

In the context of the modern cultivation world, the Mishu Kingdom, which has long been accustomed to being the hegemon, would certainly not accept the rise of the Huaxiu Kingdom in alliance with the surrounding cultivation kingdoms. Therefore, it is very likely that they will make a foolish move by going to the Earth's core world, intending to open the Netherworld passage and directly recruit the ancient beings from the Netherworld to serve them.

Wang Ling looked at Sun Rong and patiently explained.

Sun Rong's jaw dropped slightly, her beautiful face unable to hide her surprise. Her eyes were wide open, as if she couldn't believe what she was hearing.

She had assumed that the inner Earth was simply a matter of resource competition, but she hadn't expected there to be this hidden storyline.

Meanwhile, Meng Nian, whose identity had already been discovered by Wang Ling, could no longer remain calm.

Today, no matter what, she cannot let Wang Ling and Sun Rong go out.

In an instant, spiritual energy intertwined in the eerie green laboratory, forming a chaotic storm. The laboratory lights flickered and hissed under the impact of the spiritual energy. Meng Nian's face was contorted, filled with resentment and bitterness.

She tossed her hair, her long black hair dancing wildly like an ink snake, and then quickly reached down to her waist, where several magical artifacts exuding an aura of the underworld instantly shot out.

First came the soul-capturing black banner, its surface as deep as a black hole, seemingly capable of absorbing all souls. The runes drawn on it with blood shimmered with an eerie light, resembling grotesque ghost faces. Wisps of black energy seeped out from it, transforming into skeletal spirits that rushed towards Wang Ling and Sun Rong.

The skeletal spirits emitted sharp howls, as if they were about to pierce people's eardrums.

At the same time, she pointed to a bone-corroding pearl, which spun rapidly in the air, and a dark green poisonous mist quickly spread out from it, filling the entire laboratory.

Wherever the poisonous fog reached, it corroded the stone slabs on the ground, creating numerous pits and hissing sounds, and filling the air with a pungent odor.

The next second, Meng Nian shouted, her voice sharp and full of hatred.

Meng Nian: "Now that you know my identity, I don't care whether I live or die, I just want you to die!"

Wang Ling: "?"

Her hands moved swiftly, forming hand seals as she chanted incantations. Several black runes lit up on her arms, converging in the air to form a dark dragon that flew towards Wang Ling and Sun Rong. Wherever the dark dragon passed, space itself distorted, and the surrounding air seemed to be torn apart, emitting bursts of explosions.

"She has so many magical treasures on her?" Sun Rong was extremely astonished.

Her body trembled slightly, and a hint of tension was in her eyes.

This was clearly not the first time Meng Nian had done this; simultaneously sending out multiple magical treasures was the ultimate test of a cultivator's all-around control and the enormous consumption of spiritual energy. Under such a formation, any cultivator who faced it head-on would suffer.

Fortunately, Meng Nian's opponent was a max-level player.

Wang Ling's expression remained calm and serene, surrounded by a faint golden light, making him appear like an inviolable deity. He gently tapped Sun Rong's forehead, sharing his protective golden light with her. That golden light, like warm sunlight, instantly dispelled the fear in Sun Rong's heart.

When the skeletal spirits released by the Soul-Capturing Black Banner approached the golden light, they couldn't even get close and shattered instantly. Even the poisonous mist from the Corrosive Bone Pearl was purified without a trace. The descending Nether Dragon was like a clay ox entering the sea, directly shattered by the golden light, leaving only its stark white bones, which fell to the ground in mid-air. The bones scattered on the ground, making a crisp sound.

Meng Nian stared in disbelief at the scene before her.

Her eyes widened, her mouth slightly agape, her face filled with shock and disbelief. She had never imagined that her powerful attack would be so utterly futile in front of Wang Ling.

Before she could even see what the boy was doing, Wang Ling was already in front of her, flicking his finger and striking her on the top of her head.

"What are you looking at? I'm here to take you in."

Wang Ling's voice was calm yet authoritative, as if proclaiming Meng Nian's fate.

The next second, Meng Nian screamed and flew backward like a kite with a broken string, crashing heavily into the giant egg behind her, vomiting blood. Her body left a long trail of blood on the giant egg, which looked particularly shocking.
