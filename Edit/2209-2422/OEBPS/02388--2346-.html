Chapter 2346 About the incident where I kidnapped the wrong love rival and was forced to team up for esports

The air in Sun Yiyuan's chairman's office seemed to freeze.

Liu Kaichuan was wrapped in a golden binding rope like a human silkworm cocoon, with only his eyes showing, filled with bewilderment.

The coffin stood there like a cold sculpture, its face devoid of any superfluous expression.

He acted as if he had just gone downstairs to pick up a package.

Sun Yiyuan looked at the boy on the ground, who was wearing the Chihuo High School uniform, had a strong physique, and was completely different from the "Qingqingzi" image in his mind. He felt his old face burning, and his toes were digging out a three-room-one-living room from his leather shoes.

"Cough!" Finally, Old Master Sun broke the silence with a dry cough.

He tried to salvage some of his authority as chairman of the group, reiterating to his face, "Coffin... I asked you to invite him here as a courtesy! Not to tie him up with a magic rope! What kind of talk is that!"

The coffin tilted its head slightly and replied matter-of-factly, "Yes, Chairman, I will improve next time."

Sun Yiyuan: "..."

He felt his blood pressure was a little high.

Trying to reason with these elite agents from the former Seven Stars team about human relationships is like casting pearls before swine.

He waved his hand weakly: "Hurry up, untie them!"

"Yes," the coffin responded, pointing its finger in the air.

The golden binding rope then untied itself and shrunk back into the coffin's sleeve.

Upon regaining his freedom, Liu Kaichuan gasped for breath, his limbs still feeling weak.

He stared in disbelief at the lavish, almost blindingly luxurious decor of the office, then at the imposing old man before him and the chillingly cold man in a suit beside him. His mind raced, trying to comprehend the situation.

"You...you are Chairman Sun of the Huaguo Water Curtain Group?" Liu Kaichuan, being the head of a company, had a broader perspective than the average high school student and quickly recognized Sun Yiyuan.

"It is indeed I." Sun Yiyuan tried his best to remain calm and smooth over the misunderstanding: "Young man, your name is Liu Kaichuan, right? The president of the e-sports club at Chihuo High School?"

"It's...it's me." Liu Kaichuan nodded, his heart pounding.

Could it be that I angered this big shot by secretly duo-queuing with "L" using a female account?

But what does this have to do with the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group?

Sun Yiyuan walked behind the huge mahogany office desk, pulled out a drawer, took out a small jade box that exuded a strong spiritual energy, and placed it on the table with a "clatter".

The box was opened, and inside were high-quality spirit stones. At a glance, there were at least a hundred of them!

Its value is enough for an ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivator to cultivate without worries for several years.

Liu Kaichuan's eyes widened instantly.

"I heard you're pretty good at games."

Sun Yiyuan said in a calm tone: "This is just a small token of my appreciation, as compensation for what happened earlier... um, misunderstanding. Also, I have a favor to ask."

Liu Kaichuan looked at the box of spirit stones, swallowed hard, and forced himself to calm down: "Chairman Sun, please speak." He was indebted to the person he was dealing with, especially since the other party was so powerful; he had no room to refuse.

"I'll play a training match with my granddaughter, Sun Rong."

Sun Yiyuan revealed: "She is also preparing for the city-level selection competition recently, and needs to compete with different experts."

Liu Kaichuan was taken aback, then suddenly realized!

So that's where the root of the problem lies!

In order to successfully recruit Wang Ling as an external ally of Chi Huo, Liu Kaichuan had done some investigation into the situation of the students at Number 60 High School. He knew that there were quite a few rumors between Sun Rong and that classmate Wang Ling...

Now that Liu Kaichuan has calmed down, he almost immediately figured out the key point.

The reason he was kidnapped here is likely because he used the female character "Xuanfeng Qingqingzi" to duo queue with Wang Ling, which caused a misunderstanding with Sun Rong.

Sun Rong's original intention was to find herself, but the man in the suit misunderstood and ended up kidnapping her...

Considering the old man's "compensation" and the awkward atmosphere at the scene, Liu Kai felt that everything made sense!

Having figured out the key point, Liu Kaichuan suddenly felt a mix of amusement and exasperation.

What the hell is this?!

He quickly reassured everyone, "Chairman Sun, please rest assured, this is absolutely a misunderstanding! Wang Ling and I are just teammates in the game, and I only have admiration for Sun Rong! The training match is no problem, I will definitely do my best!"

Seeing his confident expression, Sun Yiyuan felt a little better.

This kid is actually pretty savvy.

...

Meanwhile, at the Sun Family Villa Restaurant.

Sun Rong was absentmindedly cutting the steak in front of her with a knife and fork.

The delicious meat juice tasted somewhat bland in Sun Rong's mouth, her thoughts already drifting to the ID "Whirlwind Qingqingzi".

Uncle Lin, the butler, stood to one side dressed in his impeccable butler's uniform, slowly and meticulously wiping a crystal-clear red wine glass with a clean white soft cloth.

His movements were elegant and precise, carrying a composure that came with the passage of time.

"Uncle Lin." Sun Rong put down her knife and fork and sighed softly, "Tell me... what does it mean if someone suddenly starts playing games with a girl you don't know?"

Uncle Lin paused slightly as he wiped the wine glass, a hint of a smile flashing in his eyes behind the mirror.

He put down his wine glass and looked at Sun Rong gently: "Miss, are you worried about Young Master Wang Ling?"

Sun Rong's cheeks flushed slightly, but she didn't deny it: "I'm just... a little curious."

"Relationships can't be rushed, Miss."

Uncle Lin comforted him, saying, "Like brewing a good cup of tea, the water temperature, the time, and the unfolding of the tea leaves all need to be just right. Being too hasty will only ruin the flavor. Let things take their natural course, and with careful management, what is meant for you will surely come to you."

Sun Rong nodded thoughtfully.

Uncle Lin had a rich emotional life when he was young, and his words always hit the nail on the head.

Just as she calmed down a bit and was preparing to continue her meal—

Ding-dong.

An inconspicuous watch-like magical artifact on Uncle Lin's wrist vibrated slightly, projecting a message.

He glanced at it, his eyebrows twitching almost imperceptibly.

"Young Miss." Uncle Lin's voice remained steady, but his pace quickened slightly: "I just received news. Mr. Coffin has... secured the game ID ‘Whirlwind Qingqingzi' and is currently at the group's headquarters, in the chairman's office."

"Pfft—cough cough cough!" Sun Rong almost choked on the steak she had just put in her mouth, coughing repeatedly.

Uncle Lin handed him a glass of water at the right moment.

Sun Rong gulped down several mouthfuls of water before she could catch her breath, her face flushed red with shock: "Grandpa... what is he doing!?"

She only asked the coffin maker to investigate, but her grandfather unexpectedly had his "love rival" kidnapped!

This is absolutely embarrassing!

"I...I'm not eating anymore!"

Sun Rong suddenly stood up, disregarding all etiquette, and immediately said, "Uncle Lin, quick! Take me to the group's headquarters!"

...

Huaguo Water Curtain Group Headquarters, Chairman's Office.

In an attempt to salvage his reputation and prove that he had indeed invited Liu Kaichuan for serious business, Sun Yiyuan instructed his staff to quickly set up two top-of-the-line smart gaming cabins in the office's break area.

Just then, the office door was pushed open, and Sun Rong, accompanied by Uncle Lin, hurriedly walked in.

"Grandpa!" Sun Rong spotted Liu Kaichuan standing next to the gaming booth, looking somewhat flustered.

She immediately understood what was going on, her face instantly turning bright red, so embarrassed she wanted to disappear into a crack in the ground.

Upon seeing his granddaughter, Sun Yiyuan's old face flushed slightly. He quickly sent a telepathic message: "Rongrong! It's a misunderstanding, a huge misunderstanding! Grandpa was just trying to help you identify your rival in love. Who knew that this ID 'Whirlwind Qingqingzi' was actually used by a young upstart!"
