Chapter 2345 The Kidnapped "Qingqingzi"

The night was deep and quiet.

In the luxurious bedroom of Sun Rong's family villa, the young girl was tossing and turning on the bed, hugging a soft pillow.

The moonlight streamed in through the window, outlining her slightly agitated features.

I can't sleep.

I can't sleep at all.

The moment I close my eyes, that glaring notification from my game friends list automatically plays in my mind:

【'L' is currently playing duo queue with his friend 'Whirlwind Qingqingzi'】.

"Whirlwind Qingqingzi..." she muttered softly, burying her face in the cushion and rubbing it against it.

This name sounds like it belongs to a girl!

Wang Ling... actually played games alone with a girl other than her? And so late at night!

Although reason told her that it was Wang Linghe's freedom to choose who to play games with, and that they were just ordinary classmates...

But that sour feeling in my heart clung to me like vines, tightening its grip.

"no!"

Sun Rong sat up abruptly, her curiosity ultimately overpowering her reason: "I need to know who this 'Whirlwind Qingqingzi' is!"

Should we go directly to Wang Ling? Given that blockhead's personality, he'll most likely just reply with an "um" or remain silent, and we won't get any information out of him.

Moreover, doing so would make it seem like I care too much, and it would be a bit embarrassing.

Sun Rong thought for a moment, then picked up a spare phone inlaid with spirit stones that was placed on the bedside table.

She quickly unlocked the app and found a code name in an encrypted contact list: "Coffin".

This is a private detective hired by the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group. He was originally an elite agent of the Seven Star Team and has top-notch professional skills. He specializes in handling investigative matters that cannot be made public.

Because the group does not directly invest in the operator of "Glory Alliance" and cannot directly access the backend data, using a coffin is currently the quickest and least noticeable method.

She edited a message and sent it: "Mr. Coffin, could you please help me investigate a game ID in Honor of Kings: 'Whirlwind Qingqingzi'? I need to know the real identity and information of this account user. This involves my personal privacy, please keep it confidential."

After sending the message, Sun Rong breathed a sigh of relief and lay back down.

Although the jealousy hadn't subsided, taking action finally put my mind at ease.

She stared at the ceiling, thinking dazedly, "As long as I know who it is... I'm just curious, yes, just curious!"

...

The headquarters of Huaguo Water Curtain Group, the chairman's office on the top floor.

Although it was late at night, Sun Yiyuan did not rest. He was sipping a cup of spiritual tea and listening to his assistant's report on the group's recent business.

At that moment, an inconspicuous jade bracelet on his wrist trembled slightly.

This was a special reminder he set up, related to certain security and contact channels for his beloved granddaughter, Sun Rong.

He subtly signaled his assistant to pause, then focused his attention on the jade bracelet, quickly scanning a message he had just intercepted—the investigation order that Sun Rong had sent to the coffin.

"oh?"

A hint of interest appeared on Sun Yiyuan's face: "Whirlwind Qingqingzi? Game ID? And it involves Rongrong's privacy?"

The shrewd old man immediately imagined a whole drama unfolding in his mind.

The fact that someone would keep their beloved granddaughter up all night and use their personal connections to investigate a game ID must be related to that kid named Wang Ling!

This "Whirlwind Qingqingzi" is most likely a suspicious person who appears around Wang Ling and makes his Rongrong feel a sense of crisis!

"Hey, this kid's pretty popular, isn't he?" Sun Yiyuan wasn't angry at all; on the contrary, he was rather pleased.

He thought highly of Wang Ling and couldn't wait for Sun Rong and Wang Ling's relationship to take another step forward.

He was interested in personally weighing any "stumbling block" that might hinder the development of their relationship.

He immediately contacted Coffin, who had just received instructions from Sun Rong and was preparing to begin the investigation, via internal communication.

"Coffin, didn't Rongrong just ask you to check a game account called ‘Whirlwind Qingqingzi'?" Sun Yiyuan's voice carried its usual authority.

"Yes, Chairman." The coffin's reply was as concise as ever.

"Okay, you can continue with this task. However, I'll give you an additional instruction."

Sun Yiyuan took a sip of tea and said slowly, "After finding this person, 'invite' her to my office. I want to talk to her..."

He deliberately emphasized the word "please" a little more.

In Sun Yiyuan's mind, this "please" was a polite invitation, at most carrying a hint of business negotiation intimidation. After all, he was a big shot in the cultivation world, so how could he really do anything to a little girl who might just be a high school student?

He just wanted to see what kind of girl could make his Rongrong care so much.

However, the coffin on the other end of the communication line began to interpret information in an abnormally formulaic way.

First, his mission target is a girl with the game ID "Whirlwind Sunny," who is suspected of causing emotional interference to someone important to Miss Sun Rong.

Secondly, the chairman personally ordered that the target be "invited" to his office for a talk.

The dual orders made Coffin take this mission even more seriously.

The fact that the chairman personally intervened, stemming from Ms. Sun Rong's personal feelings, demonstrates the high level of importance attached to the matter.

Conclusion: The "please" mentioned by Chairman Sun Yiyuan should be understood as "ensuring that the target arrives at the designated location unconditionally, without delay, and without resistance."

Therefore, the optimal implementation solution is: mandatory control.

"Understood, Chairman. Mission accomplished." The voice from the coffin remained completely flat and utterly misinterpreted Sun Yiyuan's true meaning.

After the communication ended, a cold glint flashed in the coffin's eyes.

He turned on the specially made laptop and began to work quickly on the keyboard.

The resources and methods of the former Seven Stars team agents were fully mobilized. Through IP tracking, online behavior analysis, and access to some less-than-legal databases, in less than half an hour, he accurately pinpointed the IP coordinates of the "Whirlwind Qingqingzi" account's recent frequent logins—Songhai City, Chihuo Senior High School area.

"Target locked."

The coffin closed its notebook, muttered to itself, and then its figure quickly blended into the shadows and disappeared.
