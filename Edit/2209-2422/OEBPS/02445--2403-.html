Chapter 2403 Flirting and Bantering?

Darkness spread across the square.

The commotion among the crowd grew louder and louder, with shouts and anxious attempts by staff to calm them down mingling together, and the scene gradually spiraled out of control.

"What's going on?!" Chen Chao subconsciously moved closer to Wang Ling and Sun Rong, lowering his voice: "Lingzi, Sun Rong, let's not get separated!"

Sun Rong was also a little nervous. She looked around and found that the visibility had dropped to less than five meters.

People were packed together, and the air they breathed smelled unfamiliar.

"Wang Ling..." She instinctively took half a step closer to Wang Ling.

Wang Ling remained silent.

His senses had already locked onto those approaching auras.

There were five people in total: four at the early Nascent Soul stage and one at the mid-Nascent Soul stage. Their cultivation was a whole realm higher than the assassins from earlier that day, and their actions were more professional and their teamwork was impeccable.

The five people were divided into two groups.

The three men slowly approached from the front, blending into the crowd, while the other two circled around to the side, blocking any escape route.

The target is clear: Sun Rong.

They all carried specially made binding artifacts, not the crude traps used during the day, but spirit locks engraved with high-level runes.

Once locked, even the peak of the Primordial Era would find it difficult to break free.

Their plan was clear: take advantage of the chaos to quickly seize control of the target, and then use some kind of spatial teleportation talisman to evacuate.

Clean and neat, leaving no trace.

Wang Ling could even hear their encrypted telepathic communication:

"Target confirmed: Sun Rong, the young lady of the Flower and Fruit Water Curtain Group, with Golden Core cultivation."

"Is that boy next to her Wang Ling?"

"Be careful, all four groups failed during the day, there must be something wrong."

"What problems could there be? Five of us are in the early stages of life, facing two students. How could we possibly mess things up?"

"Stop talking nonsense and act according to plan. Strike simultaneously in three seconds."

Wang Lingping stood calmly, sensing everything.

Those five infant killers made their move at the same time!

Three figures emerged from the crowd like ghosts, their spirit locks flicking like venomous snakes, lunging straight at Sun Rong!

The two people on the side each threw out two talismans—one was a soundproof barrier talisman, and the other was a visual interference talisman.

The double-layered enclosure ensures that the actions will not be noticed by ordinary tourists.

However.

Just as the lock was about to touch Sun Rong's wrist.

Wang Ling did three things.

The first thing he did was to lightly step on his left foot.

A faint vibration emanated from his toes and spread throughout the entire square.

Those five talismans—the soundproof barrier talisman, the visual interference talisman, and the three spatial teleportation talismans that the assassins had in their pockets—all became ineffective at the same time!

With just a slight movement, the psionic structure that constitutes the talisman begins to disintegrate from the most basic rune level, like an ice block encountering sunlight, instantly crumbling into the most primordial spiritual particles.

Secondly, Wang Ling casually drew a line in the air with his right index finger.

Then, the five spirit locks suddenly changed direction.

They spun sharply in the air as if they were alive, and the five spirit locks precisely locked onto the wrists of the five assassins.

Moreover, it's the highest level of confinement mode, with the locks automatically tightening and the runes fully activated.

The five Nascent Soul cultivators were instantly trapped by their own magical artifacts.

Thirdly, Wang Ling took out a packet of new flavored instant noodles from his pocket and calmly took a bite.

"Click".

The crisp chewing sounds were barely audible in the chaotic environment.

But that "click" sound froze all five assassins in place.

They looked down at the spirit locks on their wrists that had already been activated, then looked up at Wang Ling.

The boy was calmly eating his snacks, not even glancing at them.

It was as if everything that had just happened had nothing to do with him.

"How...how is this possible..." The assassin in the mid-infancy stage, codenamed "Dark Owl," suddenly narrowed his eyes.

He was the team leader for this operation. He had been in the industry for twenty years and had carried out hundreds of highly difficult missions without ever failing.

But in that brief moment, he didn't even see what happened.

Suddenly, I felt a lightness in my hand, and the spirit lock inexplicably locked onto my wrist.

And it's the strongest confinement mode!

Once this spiritual lock is activated, it will take at least three hours to be dispelled using a secret technique unless a Nascent Soul stage cultivator forcibly breaks it.

"Captain! The Spirit Lock is out of control!" a teammate whispered, his voice trembling.

"My spiritual power... is not flowing smoothly!" Another teammate's face turned pale.

"It's an inherent spiritual domain! There's a spiritual domain suppressing us here that's at the Nascent Soul stage or above!" Dark Owl finally realized, his back instantly soaked with cold sweat.

It can silently infect five infants at the same time, without giving them even a chance to resist...

This is at least the late stage of the Nascent Soul realm, or even higher!

However, the data shows that the target only has one high school student in the Qi Refining stage by his side!

Dark Owl suddenly looked at Wang Ling.

The boy was still eating instant noodles, his cheeks slightly puffed out, his eyes calmly watching the fireworks blooming in the sky.

It was as if all the chaos around him had nothing to do with him.

This person... might be a monster!

The Dark Lord panicked. Although he hadn't seen Wang Ling make a move, his intuition told him that Wang Ling was definitely in big trouble!

"Retreat! Retreat immediately!" Dark Owl gritted his teeth and transmitted his voice: "This is not a target we can handle! Report the situation to the higher-ups!"

Once they leave the crowded area, they can find a way to unlock the spirit lock, and then...
