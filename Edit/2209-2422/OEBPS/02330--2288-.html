Chapter 2288 Resonance Deep Within the Altar

Dragon Remains Fortress, at the bottom of the Dragon Transformation Pool.

The dark red energy liquid, like viscous blood plasma, enveloped Wang Muyu's small body.

Countless bizarre runic chains coiled around it like living things, constantly contracting and radiating a chilling, confinement power.

These binding forces attempted to completely lock up every ounce of his power and strip away the most primal imprint deep within his dragon soul.

Outwardly, Wang Muyu appeared to be on the verge of death, the seven-colored dragon scales around his body had dimmed to the extreme, and some areas even showed signs of being eroded and melted, as if he were truly disintegrating under the power of the Dragon Transformation Pool.

His eyes were tightly closed, his brows furrowed in pain, and his body occasionally twitched involuntarily. Combined with the smear of undried golden blood at the corner of his mouth, his acting was flawless.

Under the precise control of Wang Ling's invisible will, the vast and violent energy in the Dragon Transformation Pool was quietly tamed and filtered.

The pure dragon blood essence and primal power that were not tainted by the will of the outer gods, like a gentle stream, seeped into Wang Muyu's limbs and bones through the gaps in the rune chains, replenishing the energy he had previously consumed during his "outburst," and even slowly strengthening his foundation and expanding his meridians.

"Hmm...it seems...I've gotten a little stronger again?" Wang Muyu muttered to himself, feeling a little happy, but he immediately tensed up and maintained a pained expression.

"Dad is amazing! He can even turn the bad guy's bathwater into a tonic!"

His main focus, however, followed the "genetic resonance" that made him both excited and uneasy, carefully extending deeper into the energy channels of the Dragon Transformation Pool, seeking the true face of the Dragon God Altar.

Perception passed through the evil array constructed of wailing dragon spirits and skeletons, finally reaching the core.

It was a vast space that defies description.

The altar itself seems to be carved from a miniature version of the core of an ancient star, presenting a chaotic color of dark red and pitch black, and its surface is covered with artificially distorted law patterns.

Countless thick, dark golden energy conduits connect to it from all directions, pulsating like blood vessels, continuously injecting the power drawn from the entire Abyss of Fallen Stars into it.

Above the altar, a massive void nightmare's will loomed like dark clouds, its shadows, with countless tiny tentacles, hanging low. It merged with a pure yet chilling moonlight dragon power rising from within the altar, performing some kind of evil ritual that desecrated life.

The mere sensation of touch caused Wang Muyu's dragon soul to feel an instinctive repulsion.

But his attention was quickly drawn to the very center of the altar.

It was not empty there.

A giant egg, about the height of a person, was suspended in the air.

The giant egg's shell is not a physical object, but rather composed of constantly flowing dark red energy and broken dragon runes. It is semi-transparent, and the interior can be vaguely seen.

Right inside that giant egg—

Wang Muyu's senses jolted violently, almost causing him to lose control!

Inside... there was a huddled figure!

A boy who looks about seven or eight years old!

He had soft, short black hair, and his face... his face actually bore a six or seven-tenths resemblance to Wang Muyu!

The lines are just a bit colder and harder; under the tightly closed eyelids, the eyeliner is long and narrow, carrying a hint of evil that doesn't match her age.

More importantly, the throbbing and fear that Wang Muyu felt deep in his blood originated from this!

This boy exudes an aura that is the same as his, yet completely different!

They also possess the bloodline of the supreme dragon race, powerful and pure, but... they lack something crucial.

The aura was more like a meticulously crafted container filled with immense energy.

Cold, hollow, as if waiting to be infused with a soul, or... to be completely possessed by some will.

On the surface of the boy's bare chest, a faintly visible genetic chain mark, shimmering with a dim white light, was slowly rotating.

That familiar structure...

Wang Muyu had only ever sensed a similar Daoist aura from one person!

—It's Wang Ling's father!

"No...it's very similar, but not exactly the same!" Wang Muyu's heart pounded wildly as a terrifying thought flooded his mind.

Bai Zhe used some method to create a clone similar to his father's genes... and me? Or some other dragon genes...?

Is it aimed at Dad? Or is it... to replace me?

The immense shock and a sense of offended anger caused Wang Muyu's breath to ripple slightly.

Beside the Dragon Transformation Pool.

Absolutely powerful, molten gold pupils swept sharply into the pool!

Just a moment ago, the ultra-high precision monitoring system on board captured an extremely unusual, brief peak on Wang Muyu's energy fluctuation curve.

Although he immediately plummeted to the bottom, even weaker than before, that momentary abnormality was like a needle piercing through his recently calmed doubts once again.

"No!" A chilling voice suddenly rang out, breaking the silence deep within the hall.

Huang Yan was idly playing with a cluster of black flames he lit with his finger when he heard this, and he was taken aback: "What is it now? Jueqin, did you get scared out of your wits by that thing in the universe?"

His tone was sarcastic.

Shenming and Xunfeng immediately cast inquiring glances their way.

"The energy fluctuations of the target have shown an abnormal jump."

Jueqi stared intently at the Dragon Transformation Pool, data rapidly updating in his pupils: "Although it quickly recovered, the pattern doesn't match natural decay. Immediately increase the extraction power of the Dragon Transformation Pool! Activate deep soul scan! I need the most detailed data!"

His unease reached its peak once again.

That interfering entity in the universe, that feedback so perfect it was eerily obvious, plus this tiny anomaly at this moment...

The black dumplings didn't dare to slack off and immediately got to work.

Buzz—!

The energy liquid in the Dragon Transformation Pool instantly began to boil violently!

More runic chains emerged from the bottom of the pool, tightening the restraints!

A more domineering force, mixed with more of the corrosive will of the Outer Gods, began to forcefully impact Wang Muyu's Dragon Soul, attempting to penetrate its core and conduct the most thorough analysis!

"Uh..." Wang Muyu, who was at the bottom of the pool, suddenly felt a surge of pressure!

That force was no longer a gentle tonic, but had turned into a bone-scraping steel knife, frantically assaulting his disguise!

The will of the Outer God, like a maggot clinging to a bone, tried to penetrate his sea of consciousness!

The protective barrier left behind by Wang Ling began to fluctuate violently, emitting a faint hum.

Oh no! We've been discovered?

Wang Muyu's heart tightened, and he immediately focused all his energy on containing the absorbed energy within himself, desperately maintaining the illusion that he was about to be refined.

But he could sense that the scanning intensity this time far exceeded that of the previous scans!

...

Songhai City, Number 60 High School.

The bell rang signaling the end of the talisman class.

The students got up and started moving around, discussing the talismans they had just drawn.

Wang Ling's dead fish eyes looked out the window, his gaze seemingly piercing through layers of space, landing once again on that abyss.

He was acutely aware of the unusual activity at the Dragon Transformation Pool and Jueqi's vigilance.

"Good vigilance."

Wang Ling made a comment without any inner turmoil.

But that's all.

He needed Wang Muyu to stay there a little longer to gather more information about the altar and the "clone".

Now is not the time to close the net.

So Wang Ling did a very simple action.

He extended his right index finger and, seemingly casually, lightly tapped the air beneath the desk.

There was no inspiration, no fluctuation.

...

Dragon Skeleton Fortress, Dragon Transformation Pond.

Just as the deep soul scan of the Absolute Imprisonment was about to reach the core area of Wang Muyu's dragon soul—

"Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!"

The entire Dragonbone Fortress suddenly began to shake violently!

It doesn't come from inside, but from outside!

It was as if some enormous thing had crashed violently into the spatial barrier of the fortress!

The piercing alarm instantly resounded in every corner!

"Warning! Warning! Ultra-high intensity space shock wave detected! Source: Abyss of Meteor Fall, unstable fracture zone!"

"Warning! The external defensive barrier has been hit by an unknown force! Intensity level: Peak of the Celestial Ancestor level! Suspected large-scale spatial debris tidal eruption!"

The monitoring artifact flashed wildly, projecting the chaotic scene outside onto the screen—in the distorted void outside the fortress, countless star debris, swept up by spatial turbulence, crashed against the Dragonbone Fortress's protective shield wave after wave, like an out-of-control torrent, stirring up ripples all over the sky!

This sudden external attack instantly attracted everyone's attention!

"What?!" Huang Yan suddenly stood up straight, looking at the external monitoring screen, his dark red scales slightly opening with excitement: "Where did this blind thing come from? Daring to crash into my house?"
