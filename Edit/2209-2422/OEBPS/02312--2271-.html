Chapter 2271 The Arena and the Celestial King's Force Field

The deathly silence in the circular arena was broken by the echoing applause from Bai Zhe Liuli's clone.

"Welcome to my Crystal Martial Arts Arena."

Bai Zhe's clone spread his arms, his voice coming through the crystal shell, cold and mocking: "The rules are simple. I will send out my toys, and you, struggle to survive. If you perform well, you may die a quick death."

Before he finished speaking, a sickening scraping sound and a low growl came from the honeycomb-like cave entrances all around. Countless ape eyes lit up, filled with a bloodthirsty desire.

These subterranean creatures and glass structures, deeply corrupted by the will of the Outer God, are now watching the crowd with growing urges.

"You bastard! What do you take us for!" Li Changzhe roared, and the Myriad Scale Armor was activated again, but its light was noticeably dimmed.

Zhang Linyan silently nocked an arrow, drew the bowstring taut, and her eyes were sharp as an eagle's.

Su Xingyue gripped "Dawn" tightly, the sword humming softly. Chen Chao and Guo Hao stood back to back, their faces pale, but they also assumed fighting stances.

The students of St. Science College were trembling with fear, and despair was spreading.

Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, and Liu Qingyi quickly exchanged glances, their expressions grave.

They knew that this was no game, but a cruel trap set by Bai Zhe, intended to both exhaust and observe, especially to observe Wang Ling!

If they fail to provide adequate protection, these unsuspecting elite university representatives could be completely wiped out.

Sun Rong subconsciously took a step closer to Wang Ling, and the sword energy of the Aohai Sword gently surrounded her and Wang Ling like invisible ripples.

She looked at Wang Ling nervously, only to find him... putting the last bit of crispy noodle crumbs into his mouth.

Click, click.

The sound of chewing was particularly clear in the heavy atmosphere.

Bai Zhe's clone's gaze instantly locked onto Wang Ling. Liu Li's face was expressionless, but her voice was even colder: "It seems you still haven't realized your situation?"

He telepathically communicated with Wang Ling, but Wang Ling simply picked at his ear with his little finger.

Wang Ling swallowed the instant noodles, patted the crumbs off his hands, and then slowly raised his eyelids to glance at Bai Zhe's clone.

His eyes were calm and expressionless, even carrying a hint of displeasure at being disturbed from enjoying his meal.

Just as Bai Zhe's clone was about to be enraged by this disregard—

Boom!

On one side of the arena, a huge cave entrance suddenly exploded open!

A giant burrowing worm, far larger than any it had ever seen before, covered in sharp, crystal clusters and radiating a terrifying aura of a Dao Venerable-level peak, suddenly burst forth, letting out a deafening roar, and charged directly towards the most densely populated area of the crowd!

In an instant, Wang Ling's aura covered the entire area, enveloping everyone present!

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, Zhang Linyan, Su Xingyue, Li Changzhe, and even the unsuspecting representatives of the university elites were all protected by Wang Ling's invisible aura, which greatly reduced their fear of being overwhelmed.

Their cultivation level was far below that of the Dao Venerable Realm. Wang Ling knew that if he didn't intervene, they would be annihilated in an instant.

Under the protection of this invisible Immortal King's force field, no matter how powerful the creature, they would only assume that its cultivation level was around Golden Core, and that it was not completely invincible.

This illusion of being protected by the Celestial King's force field also served as cover for Wang Ling. Although he was called a mascot, many people actually believed he was not entirely without fighting power.

"Watch out!" Zhang Linyan's arrow left the bowstring, but only sparked at the crystal cluster, without causing any damage.

"Roar!" The giant worm opened its gaping maw to devour everything, sending a stench through the air!

Everyone was shocked and had no time to react!

At this critical moment—

"oops!"

Wang Ling seemed to be accidentally bumped by Chen Chao, who was nervously backing away. He stumbled, and the unopened bag of "Honey Spirit Flower Flavored" instant noodles that he had just taken out of his hand flew out.

The packet of instant noodles flew in a panicked arc, and right into the gaping maw of the giant burrowing worm!

The burrowing beetle: "???"

It instinctively closed its massive jaws and bit down—thud!

The sound of the packaging bag tearing was barely audible.

But the next moment, the giant worm's enormous body suddenly stiffened! As if struck on the head by an invisible giant hammer, its momentum came to an abrupt halt!

It twisted in agony, and the hard, glassy clusters on its body that were strong enough to withstand Zhang Linyan's arrows began to crack from the inside, oozing out strange, seven-colored light!

Boom!

With a muffled thud, the giant worm completely disintegrated from head to tail without any warning! It turned into a pile of shimmering, iridescent glass powder that quickly lost its luster.

Sold out in seconds!

Another bag of instant noodles!

The entire venue fell silent once again.

Chen Chao was still in the position where he bumped into Wang Ling, his mouth agape: "I...holy crap...Lingzi! Your instant noodles...are some kind of magical artifact?!"

Guo Hao muttered to himself, "From now on, I don't need to bring magical artifacts when fighting. I can just give Lingzi a truckload of instant noodles..."

Looking at his dimmed armor and then at the pile of powder, Li Changzhe fell into deep self-doubt.

Zhang Linyan and Su Xingyue looked at each other with suspicion and uncertainty. They vaguely felt that something was wrong, but they couldn't explain it.

The students of the Holy Science Academy looked at Wang Ling as if he were a god.

Bai Zhe's clone: "..."

Beneath the glassy shell, his will roared! He knew perfectly well what had happened! This was no luck at all... This man had easily resolved his probing with such a low-level fighting tactic in full view of everyone.

This is clearly an insult!

Wang Ling steadied himself, looked at the pile of powder, and at the right moment revealed a "what a pity for my new flavor of instant noodles" expression on his face.

He silently took out another packet of the new flavor from his pocket and started unpacking it.

Sun Rong suppressed a laugh, her cheeks flushed slightly, and she quietly breathed a sigh of relief.

She knew, of course, that it wasn't luck.

Ouhai had just clearly captured a fleeting glimpse of a terrifying will, condensed to its extreme and beyond her comprehension, the instant the packet of instant noodles flew into the insect's mouth.

Wang Zhen nudged Fang Xing with his elbow and transmitted his voice: "Tsk, did you see that? The new type of interdimensional strategic deterrent weapon—the Immortal King's Special Instant Noodles."

The black gemstone on Fang Xing's skull sickle gleamed slightly, responding: "Low cost, outstanding effect."

Liu Qingyi pouted and whispered to Wang Zhen, "Can't you learn from them? Even your fighting style is so creative!"

Wang Zhen: "...I can't learn this!"

Bai Zhe's clone finally snapped out of his rage and realized that he couldn't let Wang Ling continue to have these "coincidences"!

"Very good! Looks like we need something more exciting!" he said coldly, raising his hand and waving.

Buzzing—!

More than ten cave entrances around the arena lit up simultaneously!

Dozens of glass structures of various shapes, but without exception radiating powerful energy waves, and eroded subterranean overlord-level creatures surged out!

This even includes several glass beetle guards that have appeared before and can emit corrosive energy!

Their targets are no longer scattered, but—all aimed at Wang Ling!

Clearly, Bai Zhe intends to use sheer numbers and firepower to cover up all the "coincidences"!

"Protect the royal decree!" Sun Rong blurted out, the Ao Hai Sword hummed as it was drawn, its azure sword energy surging!

Wang Zhen, Fang Xing, and Liu Qingyi also sprang into action instantly, protecting Wang Ling.

Ryoko Kugimiya gritted her teeth and used the last of her nearly destroyed array plate's power to activate a small defensive array.

Zhang Linyan, Li Changzhe, Su Xingyue and others were puzzled as to why they were all targeting Wang Ling.

But they immediately adjusted their course and prepared to fight back.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao roared and stood on either side of Wang Ling, even though their legs were trembling.

A major war is about to break out!

Faced with this overwhelming barrage of attacks, powerful enough to instantly annihilate even an early-stage Dao God, Wang Ling, protected by everyone in the center...

He had just opened the packaging bag of "Spicy Dragon Scale" instant noodles.

The rich aroma of spicy numbing flavor wafted out.

He glanced at his companions around him, who looked as if facing a formidable enemy, and then at the surging tide of monsters approaching from the front.

Then, he lowered his head and focused on shaping the dough into even small pieces.

It was as if the surrounding destructive energy, the anxious cries of his companions, and the ferocious roars of monsters...
