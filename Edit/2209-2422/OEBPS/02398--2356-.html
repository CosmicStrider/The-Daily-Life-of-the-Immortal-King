8 PM, Training Area, Building A.

The training area of the Star Dome Esports Center is divided into several zones: general training rooms, team training rooms, holographic simulation battle rooms, data analysis rooms, etc.

Sun Rong booked a double training room.

The room wasn't big, but it was fully equipped.

Two of the latest psionic e-sports cabins were placed side by side, with a holographic tactical panel in the middle.

Wang Ling walked in, glanced around the room, and then naturally headed towards the gaming cabin on the left.

"Wang Ling, which hero do you want to practice?" Sun Rong asked.

"Either is fine." Wang Ling lay down in the gaming cabin and adjusted his position.

Sun Rong thought for a moment: "Then... shall we practice bot lane synergy? I'll practice a few protective supports, and you practice ADC?"

"Um."

The two players connect their devices and enter the game.

Training mode, custom room.

Sun Rong chose Bard, the astral wanderer, while Wang Ling chose Ezreal, the explorer.

"Where should I start training?" Sun Rong asked.

"Open a tunnel." Wang Ling succinctly stated: "Timing and angle."

"good."

Sun Rong took a deep breath and began to operate.

Bard's E ability, Magical Journey, can create a one-way tunnel in the terrain, making it an extremely powerful mobility and support skill.

However, if the tunnel is not dug properly, it can become a shortcut for the enemy.

Sun Rong had used this hero before, but she basically won by following Wang Ling's lead, and her proficiency with the hero was far from sufficient.

Now that she has this opportunity for specialized training, Sun Rong immediately puts pressure on herself to attend classes, because she doesn't want to disappoint Wang Ling.

However, with meticulous operation, controlling the tunnel proved to be far more complicated than Sun Rong had imagined.

On his first attempt, the tunnel opened a bit crookedly, and Wang Ling's EZ walked in, only to come out in a somewhat awkward position.

"I'm sorry..." Sun Rong said somewhat embarrassed.

"Continue," Wang Ling said calmly.

Fine beads of sweat appeared on Sun Rong's forehead.

She initially thought opening tunnels would be simple, but when she actually started practicing, she realized there were too many factors to consider: terrain, enemy and ally positions, teammates' movement habits, skill cooldown times...

Moreover, Wang Ling has extremely high standards, and he will point out every mistake.

"It was 0.3 seconds slower."

"The angle is 5 degrees to the left."

"Predict where my E skill will land."

Sun Rong gritted her teeth and practiced with complete concentration.

She didn't want to make a bad impression on Wang Ling.

Time passed by minute by minute.

Before I knew it, I had been practicing for an hour.

"Rest," Wang Ling suddenly said.

Sun Rong then snapped out of her highly focused state and realized that her back was soaked.

She quit the game and let out a long sigh of relief.

"Am I... stupid?" Sun Rong asked, somewhat dejected.

The training room was dimly lit, but Sun Rong could clearly see his eyes.
