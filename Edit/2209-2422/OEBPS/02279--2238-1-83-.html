Chapter 2238 Gathering of Heroes

Outside the Ling Shu Matrix's Xuan character area, the clamor of the public passageway, like a stone thrown into a calm lake, quickly shattered the tranquility of the various rest cabins. The argument between Li Changzhe and Lin Xuan of the Holy Science Academy attracted many elite representatives who had just settled in to peek out from their respective rest areas.

Looking through the observation array, Sun Rong saw that Li Changzhe's face was ashen, clearly affected by Lin Xuan's sarcastic "flattering stance," while Lin Xuan was smug, enjoying the gazes from those around him—including the slightly obsequious smirks of several followers from the Holy Science Academy.

Just as Lin Xuan was about to continue provoking, a clear and calm female voice rang out, carrying an undeniable authority: "Lin Xuan, that's enough. This is not the training ground of Shengke. Watch your words and actions."

The woman who spoke was tall and straight, wearing a well-tailored high-level research gown from the Holy Science Academy. Her temperament was as elegant as the moon. She was Su Xingyue, the team leader of the Holy Science Academy's current project to the center of the earth.

She toyed with a pill that emitted a soft white light in her hand, her gaze sweeping over Lin Xuan indifferently. When the latter met her gaze, his arrogant aura immediately froze, and his pale pink "flattering stance" dimmed a bit. He sullenly curled his lips and took a step back.

Su Xingyue turned to Li Changzhe and handed him the pill: "Student Li, the shelling just now took a lot of energy. This 'Concentrating Spirit and Restoring Qi Pill' can help you quickly recover your spiritual power. Consider it my apology on behalf of the Holy Academy for Lin Xuan's rudeness."

Her attitude was neither humble nor arrogant, which not only resolved the conflict but also demonstrated the demeanor of Captain Saint Co.

Li Changzhe paused for a moment, looking at the pill that looked quite valuable. His frustration eased slightly, but his forthright nature made him wave his hand and say, "Thank you, Captain Su. I can handle a little bit of the exertion."

Although he was heartbroken at losing the "Ten Thousand Scales Armor," his backbone remained.

"Hmph, putting on a brave face." Lin Xuan muttered softly behind Su Xingyue, but dared not raise his voice.

At this moment, Zhang Linyan, the representative of Emperor Shitian, also came over. She carried an ancient longbow, walked lightly, and had sharp eyes like an eagle.

She first looked at Li Changzhe and Lin Xuan with great interest, then her gaze swept over the representatives from various schools who were coming out of the rest area one after another, and finally landed on the Number 60 High School area, especially on Chen Chao and Guo Hao who had just been drawn out by the noise.

"Brother Li, don't be impulsive next time. Why bother arguing with a mad dog?" Zhang Linyan teased Di Shitian, clearly familiar with Li Changzhe. Then, she changed the subject, lowering her voice with a hint of barely perceptible surprise, "However, I just saw something interesting at the intelligence hub inside the base. Do you know the scoring criteria for that [Welcome Ceremony]?"

"Oh? What standard?" Gao Tianming from Fumo Number 1 High School also came over. Two palm-sized, exquisitely structured metal puppets floated beside him, performing various movements nimbly.

He had a calm demeanor, and his eyes held the focused attention of a researcher.

Zhang Linyan glanced around, her gaze lingering particularly on Chen Chao and Guo Hao for a moment, a look of disbelief flashing in her eyes: "According to reliable intelligence, the core of this [Welcome Ceremony]'s evaluation is—spirit stone consumption! In other words, the less spiritual power you consume by relying solely on your own spells and physical techniques to withstand or neutralize the artillery fire, the higher your score! If you use external items such as magic treasures, talismans, puppets, or pills, their value will be converted into spirit stone losses, resulting in a significant deduction of points!"

These words shocked everyone!

Li Changzhe's face instantly fell. Looking at his empty wallet, he wailed, "Damn it! My [Ten Thousand Scale Armor]... what a huge loss!"

He secretly regretted it, mainly because the shelling came too suddenly, causing his brain to short-circuit and preventing him from thinking of a better countermeasure. Now that he thought about it, if he had used the "Water Mist Immortal Technique" taught to him by Luo Lan to decompose himself and the immortal ship into water mist, his score would have been higher.

Unfortunately, it's all over now. Li Changzhe had no experience facing cannon fire, which led to a misjudgment of the situation. Now, thinking about it carefully, with his current control over the "Water Mist Immortal Technique," it should have been no problem for him to completely disintegrate himself and the Immortal Boat to protect the safety of Jingmen Number 8 High School.

Gao Tianming also frowned, subconsciously glancing at his two puppet dolls that had just played a protective role in the shelling; clearly, his score wouldn't be high either.

Gong Xuan, the representative of Sword God Academy, had a cold expression and his hand was always on the hilt of his sword at his waist. Hearing this, he just snorted coldly, seemingly quite confident in his performance of splitting the cannonball with pure sword energy.

Beside him, another sword god representative, Yi Zhiyang, seemed somewhat absent-minded, his gaze wandering. Especially when he saw the faint outline of the cannon muzzle in the direction of the base entrance in the distance, his body would involuntarily tremble slightly. His sharp object PTSD had not fully recovered, and he was still a little scared when he saw the pointed cannon head, so he did not perform well.

At this moment, Zhang Linyan's gaze fell on Chen Chao and Guo Hao again, with undisguised astonishment: "So, the highest overall score... belongs to Chen Chao and Guo Hao from Number 60 High School! The two of them, using only their physical bodies and runes, took the first shot without a scratch, and then joined forces to withstand the next three shots! And throughout the whole process... without relying on any external objects! How did you do that? Your improvement is terrifying!" Her tone was full of sincere shock, clearly finding the results of the two's special training in a short period of time unbelievable.

Previously, when she saw the list of nominees from Number 60 High School, she didn't take Chen Chao and Guo Hao, these two nobodys, seriously at all.

Chen Chao and Guo Hao were a little embarrassed by the praise, but more than that, they felt proud.

Chen Chao scratched his head and grinned: "Hehe, luck, luck! It's mainly because Guo Hao's runes are so powerful!"

Guo Hao also humbly said, "Without Chen Chao holding the line, my runes wouldn't have been able to hold up."

The two exchanged pleasantries, creating a relaxed atmosphere.

Just then, Sun Rong rushed out of her "Xuan-07" cabin, a hint of panic on her face, and went straight to Wang Ling's "Xuan-08" cabin next door: "Wang Ling! Wang Ling, what's wrong?"

The cabin door slid open, revealing Wang Ling with a face as pale as paper and an extremely weak breath. He leaned limply against the cabin wall, his eyes tightly closed, as if he had fallen into a deep coma. He appeared to be in a state of extreme weakness, with his spiritual energy severely depleted and his meridians damaged!

"Wang Ling!" Chen Chao and Guo Hao were shocked and rushed over immediately.

Chen Chao grabbed Wang Ling's shoulder, his hand feeling icy cold. He sensed a barely perceptible pulse and a erratic, weak breath. His eyes reddened with anxiety: "What happened? He was perfectly fine in the central hall just now! Did Old Man Teng's spiritual pressure injure him? That damned old man!"

Guo Hao was also anxious: "Quick! Sun Rong, Jiu Gong, didn't you bring emergency medicine? Give it to him quickly!"

Sun Rong squatted down beside Wang Ling, her fingers resting on his wrist pulse. The pulse she felt was extremely weak and chaotic, and there was a "pseudo-injury" fluctuation caused by the forced suppression of spiritual power.
