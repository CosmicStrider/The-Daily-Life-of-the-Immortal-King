Chapter 2258 The Whispers of the Earth's Veins

Inside the underground cave, time seemed to be smoothed out by Wang Ling's power, restoring a near-frozen tranquility.

Artificial sunlight mimicked the changes of dawn and dusk, and the fragrance of Jade Heart Juice mixed with the aroma of various spiritual plants and herbs, replacing the smoke of the previous battle and the strange aura of the Glass Clan.

Chen Chao's leg injury healed very quickly thanks to the excellent medical conditions in the cave.

He also benefited from the effects of the Earth Core Herb that Guo Hao and Zhang Linyan found. Now he can walk slowly without his crutches, though he still occasionally grimaces and complains about the extraordinary dangers of the Earth Core world.

Everyone had doubts about this mission, as if Project Core wasn't simply there to collect biological illustrations... it was more like they were going to wipe out all of these elite representatives in one fell swoop...

This is terrifying!

Moreover, communications at the surface command center have not yet been restored, leaving many college representatives involved in the project feeling uncertain.

Are we going to stay here forever?

Anxiety began to arise.

Of course, the sixty students performed exceptionally well.

Ryoko Kunomiya's skill in formations improved considerably under the pressure of actual combat.

She continuously optimized the perimeter of the cave and further integrated it with Sun Rong's Aohai Sword Qi Network, forming a psionic network that combines defense and early warning.

She even tried to use the characteristics of the energy residue of the Glass Clan recorded by scanning to set up targeted traps. Although she knew that it might have limited effect on higher-level beings, she was extremely serious about it.

Liu Qingyi seemed to have temporarily shifted her "supervision" of Wang Zhen to training, pulling Su Xingyue and some other students in good condition to spar in the training area where the cave had been opened.

The stated purpose is to "enhance the team's practical combat capabilities and prevent them from being impersonated by strange and unusual things."

In reality, it was an outlet for excess energy and a lingering jealousy.

This will also deplete Su Xingyue's energy, leaving her without the strength to talk to Wang Zhen.

Wang Zhen was happy to have some free time and took on patrol duties in the outer perimeter with Fang Xing. The two would often disappear for a period of time, and when they returned, Fang Xing's skull and sickle aura would sometimes become even more profound and unpredictable.

Sun Rong, on the other hand, was very busy.

She is not only the actual coordinator of the daily operation of the cave, but also has to maintain the stability of the Aohai Sword Qi imprint at all times.

In addition, she also uses her embroidery ability to silently observe the state of others and prevent possible emotional conflicts or internal crises.

Her gaze would always involuntarily drift towards the core area of the cave.

Most of the time, Wang Ling still nestled in his recliner, with the ever-read book "Creatures from the Earth's Core (Revised Edition)" spread out in front of him, and juice and a constant stream of instant noodles beside him.

But Sun Rong keenly noticed that Wang Ling seemed to have undergone a very subtle change.

He spent longer periods in a daze, and his dead fish eyes would occasionally pierce through the cave walls, gazing into the deeper darkness of the earth's core, as if listening to something. Even the frequency of his chewing instant noodles seemed to have decreased a little.

Most importantly, once when Sun Rong brought him freshly squeezed instant noodles, she inadvertently glanced at the open pages of his book. Instead of a guide to creatures from the earth's core, there were some extremely ancient and incomprehensible spatial geometric runes. In the blink of an eye, the pages returned to their original state.

What does he seem to be studying?

Or perhaps, something in the Earth's core finally caught his attention.

This change made Sun Rong's heart tighten inexplicably, filled with both worry about the unknown and a strange sense of anticipation.

Perhaps, something is about to happen that will make him truly serious.

This brief period of peace was broken on the third day at the center of the heavens.

It did not originate from an external attack, but rather from the Earth's core itself.

Buzz—

A deep, rumbling sound from the very core of the earth pierced through the layers of rock walls and the powerful barrier of the cave without warning, resonating deep within everyone's soul!

The sound was not noise, nor was it aggressive, but it carried an indescribable antiquity and heaviness, like the unconscious sigh of a planet that had been asleep for too long.

All the lights and spiritual energy devices inside the cave flickered slightly!

"What's going on?!" Chen Chao almost knocked over the petri dish in his hand.

"Abnormal fluctuations in the earth's vesicle pressure!"

Ryoko Kugimiya instantly rushed to the array plate. The data curve on the screen representing the spiritual pressure deep within the earth's core was fluctuating violently, but it quickly leveled off, except that the baseline was permanently raised a little bit higher than before.

"The strength is not high, but the depth of its source is unbelievable! Its penetrating power is extremely strong!"

Zhang Linyan and Li Changzhe appeared at the cave entrance in an instant, looking warily at the dark world outside.

However, apart from the distant veins appearing to be a little more active than usual, there was nothing unusual.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing had just returned from their patrol, their faces grave.

"The spiritual energy in the earth's veins outside has become much more active, even somewhat restless."

Wang Zhen, unusually, put away his nonchalant expression: "Some slumbering subterranean creatures seem to have been awakened."

Sun Rong felt a slight throbbing in the Aohai bracelet on her wrist. It wasn't a warning, but more like a resonance.

Her perception of earth vein energy was not as good as that of those who specialized in it, but the sound made her inexplicably feel uneasy.

All eyes once again unconsciously turned to Wang Ling.

Wang Ling had already sat up straight at some point, placing the bag of instant noodles in his hand aside.

He tilted his head slightly, and his usually unfocused, dead-fish eyes now seemed like precise radar, locking onto the depths of the earth beneath his feet.

His fingers tapped lightly on the armrest of the recliner unconsciously, with a peculiar rhythm, as if he were cracking some kind of code.

"Wang Ling?" Sun Rong couldn't help but call out softly.

Wang Ling did not respond. After several seconds, he turned his head very slowly, his gaze sweeping over everyone's faces one by one, finally landing on Sun Rong's face, where his brows furrowed almost imperceptibly.

Just when everyone thought he would ignore it or simply nod to indicate that nothing was wrong,

As if receiving a hint from Wang Ling's eyes, Wang Zhen's eyes lit up, and as if he had thought of something, he suddenly spoke, his voice carrying a particularly serious tone, but what he said stunned everyone: "It's awake."

"...Who? Who's awake?" Guo Hao asked instinctively.

Wang Zhen did not answer again, but picked up the thick book "Creatures from the Earth's Core" on Wang Ling's desk and quickly flipped through it, eventually stopping at a certain page.

What was drawn on it was not a creature, but a diagram of the earth's ley lines, which was as complex and intertwined as a neural network, with a huge vortex symbol marked in ancient script at the center.

Sun Rong, with her sharp eyes, noticed the small caption next to it that read: "Spirit of the Ancient Land!"

The cave was completely silent, with only the faint, low humming from the depths of the earth continuing.

...

The core of the Earth's core project base, the central hall.

"Report! Ultra-deep source psychic pulse detected! Depth cannot be calculated! Energy rating cannot be calculated! Residual psychic energy fluctuations are being analyzed!" The technician's voice was filled with shock and confusion.

Teng Luchen stared intently at the fleeting energy spike on the main screen. It nearly burst the detection limit, and his withered fingers clenched tightly.

Here we go again! It's that incomprehensible phenomenon again!

Can the source of the pulse be located?

"Unable to pinpoint the exact location! The area is too vast, almost covering the lower layers of the entire known Earth's core exploration area! It's like... the entire lower Earth's core moved at once!"
