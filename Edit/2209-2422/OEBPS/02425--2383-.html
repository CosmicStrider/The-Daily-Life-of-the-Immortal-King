Chapter 2383 The Order to Assassinate the King?

The Starry Sky Esports Center cafeteria was as noisy as ever. With the training and competitions these days, students from various universities in Songhai have gradually become close through their time together. Chen Chao, in particular, is very outgoing and manages to hook up with several new acquaintances every day, chatting and laughing in the cafeteria.

When Wang Ling and Sun Rong finished lunch and walked out of the cafeteria, it was 12:40 pm.

Chen Chao, Guo Hao, and a few others were still inside, bragging and discussing tactics for the afternoon's exhibition match.

"Wang Ling, is there any hero you particularly want to use this afternoon?"

Sun Rong held the packet of honey butter flavored instant noodles that Wang Ling had just given her and asked in a low voice.

Wang Ling thought for a moment and said, "Let it be."

"It's all about fate again..." Sun Rong couldn't help but laugh.

But then again... this guy seems to be able to play any hero.

The two walked to the elevator entrance, just as the elevator came down from downstairs.

With a "ding," the door opened.

Two staff members dressed in training camp uniforms were standing inside, pushing a cart full of cleaning supplies.

Upon seeing Wang Ling and Sun Rong, they nodded and stepped aside to make room.

"Thank you," Sun Rong said politely.

Wang Ling didn't say anything, but just glanced at the two of them.

These two employees look like ordinary workers.

A middle-aged man and a young man.

They exuded a faint scent of cleaning agents and subtle fluctuations of spiritual energy.

It's probably the early stage of Foundation Establishment cultivation, the kind of person who does odd jobs in the training camp.

But just as the elevator doors closed, Wang Ling frowned slightly.

The young man had faint black lines around his finger joints, like marks left from wearing some kind of magical artifact for a long time.

Although the illusion was well concealed, it could not escape Wang Ling's eyes.

Because of his previous involvement in the Shadow Stream incident, Wang Ling has now conducted some investigations into assassin organizations both domestically and internationally.

He recognized it at a glance; it was the mark of the Burial Mound Pavilion.

But Wang Ling's face remained calm as he pressed the button for the seventh floor.

The elevator slowly ascended.

Sun Rong stood beside him, humming an unknown melody softly, seemingly in a good mood.

She wore her hair in a high ponytail today, with a few stray strands falling onto her fair neck, swaying slightly as the elevator ascended.

Wang Ling's gaze lingered on that section of neck for half a second before shifting away.

The seal was slightly hot.

He reached into his pocket and pulled out a new packet of instant noodles.

"Didn't you eat enough for lunch?" Sun Rong asked, looking at him.

"An after-dinner snack," Wang Ling said.

"......"

The elevator reached the seventh floor.

The door opened, and the two walked out.

The corridor was empty; the other team members were either still in the cafeteria, practicing in the training room, or resting in their rooms.

Wang Ling's room 709 was at the end of the corridor.

Sun Rong's room 708 is next to his.

"Then I'll go back to my room to rest." Sun Rong swiped her card to open the door, turned around and said to Wang Ling, "Good luck in the exhibition match this afternoon."

"Mm." Wang Ling nodded.

Sun Rong closed the door.

Wang Ling was the only one left in the corridor.

He stood at the door of his room, took a deep breath, and then chose to swipe his card.

With a "beep", the door lock's green light turned on.

Wang Ling pushed open the door and went inside.

The room was exactly the same as when he left.

At least on the surface, there is nothing unusual about it.

The curtains were drawn, and the room was dimly lit.

The bed was neat and tidy, and there were several unopened packets of instant noodles on the desk. The tactical tablet was plugged into the charging dock.

There was a faint smell of disinfectant in the air, consistent with the smell of the cleaning agents used in the training camp.

Everything was perfectly normal.

The midday sun streamed in, illuminating every detail of the room.

It also illuminated those "little things" that were invisible to the naked eye.

...

Let's go back to before Wang Ling returned to his room.

At 12:35, a blind spot in the corridor on the seventh floor of Building B.

The young assassin from the Funeral Pavilion, Youzhe, who was pushing the cleaning cart, had already arrived at Wang Ling's room and started making arrangements inside.

Youzhe is a professional.

He accepted seventeen Xuan-level commissions, targeting everyone from Nascent Soul stage sect elders to Nascent Soul stage rogue cultivators, and he never failed.

The target this time is just a high school student, at most at the Foundation Establishment stage, and theoretically shouldn't even be able to be ranked at the Xuan level.

However, the fact that the Burial Pavilion gave him a rating of Level B of the Profound Realm and sent him, a mid-stage Nascent Soul assassin, to carry it out indicates that the target is definitely not simple.

Therefore, Youzhe is very cautious.

He used triple insurance:

The first level, [Spirit-Eroding Powder]: This is a colorless and odorless neurotoxin mixed in the filter of the air purification system. If the target breathes in the room for more than five minutes, the toxin will invade their spiritual meridians, causing disorder in the flow of spiritual energy and producing symptoms similar to demonic possession. The dosage is precisely calculated; it will not be fatal, but it is enough to render the target incapacitated for twelve hours.

The second layer, the [Soul-Binding Lock], is a high-level binding artifact disguised as a desk drawer. As soon as the target opens the drawer, the chain will instantly wrap around their wrist, releasing a soul-shattering impact that directly severs their soul.

The third level, [Shadow Strike]: This is Youzhe's own assassination technique. If the first two levels fail, he will emerge from the target's shadow and use a specially made [Soul-Capturing Needle] to pierce the target's back of the neck, forcibly capturing the soul.

Triple insurance, interconnected.

To ensure everything went smoothly, Youzhe did two more things: first, he hacked into the surveillance system of the seventh-floor corridor and replaced it with the looping footage from ten minutes ago; second, he set up a soundproof barrier around the room to ensure that no sound would get out.

Perfect.

Youzhe was very satisfied with his arrangement.

He even felt that the Funeral Pavilion was making a mountain out of a molehill.

Is it really necessary to make such a fuss over a high school student?

However, the commission is indeed generous.

The reward for this one order was equivalent to three of his usual high-level commissions.

"I'll treat it as a vacation," Youzhe thought to himself.

He then disguised himself as a cleaning staff member and evacuated Wang Ling's room. After hiding his tools, he climbed up the ventilation duct to the top of Wang Ling's room to monitor the situation inside through a peephole embedded in the duct wall.

The mirrors are specially designed to see through walls and furniture, displaying the flow of psionic energy and vital signs in the room in real time.

The room is now empty.

The goal is still to eat in the cafeteria.

Youzhe glanced at the time: 12:37.

According to intelligence, the target usually returns to the room around 12:50.

There are thirteen minutes left.

He closed his eyes, began to regulate his breathing, and completely blended his breath into the sound of the ventilation system.

The ability of a Nascent Soul cultivator to conceal themselves is akin to a dimensional reduction attack within the confines of such a mortal building.

Unless a cultivator of the same or even higher level deliberately searches for it, it would be impossible to be discovered.

Youzhe is very confident.

Of the seventeen commissions he accepted, the targets never knew how he appeared until their deaths.

This time will be no exception.

...

12:45.

Wang Ling walked to the desk, pulled out a chair, and sat down.

This movement is very natural.

But inside the ventilation duct, Yuzuri's heart skipped a beat.

The target needs to sit down!

Once you sit down, you'll be very close to the desk drawer!

Just pull out the drawer...

Youzhe held his breath.

However, Wang Ling did not pull out the drawer.

He picked up the tactical tablet and opened the training camp's intranet forum.

Several popular posts are displayed on the forum homepage:

Rational Discussion: Are the Sixty-Point Implementation Measures Reasonable?

Statement Debunking Certain Remarks Made This Morning

Vote for your predictions on the exhibition match final group stage!

Wang Ling clicked on the third post.

The voting results showed that over 70% of people believed that Wang Linghui and Luo Xinghe would face off again in the finals.

The comments section is very lively:

"Although Luo Xinghe was completely crushed this morning, the exhibition match is a best-of-three series, so there's still a chance!"
