Chapter 2376 Luo Xinghe's Nightmare

"Why are you throwing it!" Luo Xinghe shouted, his eyes red. "There's still a chance! We'll defend the high ground! Once he reaches his maximum size, he won't be able to move!"

In theory, being too large can impair mobility.

But Wang Ling showed him through his actions: sometimes theory is just theory.

Twenty-two minutes.

Wang Ling's Tahm Kench has stacked up to eight layers of [Tank Engine].

Size increased by 40%, health points increased by 40%.

Including the gigantification, the total size increased by 65%.

His model already occupies two-thirds of the screen.

When walking, you can hardly see your teammates behind you.

But he can still move.

Although slow, every step was earth-shaking.

He walked to the high ground on the other side.

The five people opposite huddled under the door tower, trembling with fear.

Wang Ling wasn't in a hurry; he just stood there and licked the tower with his tongue.

Each lick caused the tower to lose a small amount of health. Its enormous tongue moved nimbly in front of everyone, but Luo Xinghe's team had no way to stop it.

The two towering structures crumbled rapidly before him, like toys.

"Damn it! Fight him!" Luo Xinghe roared, clearly unable to hold back any longer. He had lost his usual composure and calmness as a top streamer and was too lazy to pretend anymore.

He used Kai'Sa's ultimate to dash in and used all his abilities!

LeBlanc, Garen, Rakan, Zilean...

All five players unleashed their skills on Tahm Kench in an instant.

Tahm Kench's health is starting to drop.

But this slight drop in health is insignificant; Wang Ling is now a man who can freely enter and leave the fountain...

Moreover, even if his health drops significantly, as long as Wang Ling activates his E skill [Thick Skin] and retreats slightly, his health will recover thanks to the equipment's regeneration effect.

He gave Luo Xinghe no chance to catch his breath, continuing his torturous acts, manipulating Tahm Kench to lick Kai'Sa with his tongue.

Instant deceleration!

At the same time, Heartsteel's stored damage is triggered!

Luo Xinghe's Kai'Sa instantly lost more than half of her health.

Then a basic attack, and he's down to almost no health.

W skill, Devour.

Wang Ling spit him out directly in his own camp.

The teammates reacted incredibly quickly.

Jinx fired a shot.

Olaf strikes with an axe.

Kasha, the fifth time she evaporated.

[Tank Engine]: 9 layers

Body size increased by 45%.

Now, Tahm Kench's model occupies three-quarters of the screen, and his entire body almost fills the entire high ground.

Luo Xinghe looked at his black and white screen, then at the giant Tahm Kench that filled his field of vision, and finally collapsed.

"This game... is unplayable..."

He lay slumped in the gaming cabin, his eyes vacant.

Twenty-four minutes.

Wang Ling's Tahm Kench has stacked up to ten layers of [Tank Engine].

Body size increased by 50%.

Including the gigantification, the total size increased by 75%.

His model has already taken up the entire width of the screen.

Its height also reaches two-thirds of the screen.

Where he walked, he could not see the terrain, the lanes, or even his teammates.

All that can be seen is a giant piece of meat, just like the giant toad that descended upon Songhai back then.

Wang Ling remained calm, and then he guided Tahm Kench to the opposite fountain.

Then, he did something that everyone will never forget.

He walked directly into the spring.

They began to tough it out under the laser beams emanating from the spring.

Thirty thousand health points—that's a terrifying number, a result of the tank engine's health boost and the extra health from Heart of Steel.

The spring water unleashed a frenzied laser attack.

My health is slowly dropping.

Thirty thousand, twenty-nine thousand, twenty-eight thousand...

But Wang Ling remained calm, because this small amount of health was practically useless in the face of his massive health bar, and with the shield added, the fountain laser was nothing more than a bath.

He stood in the middle of the spring, looking at Kaisa who had just been resurrected.

Q skill [Tongue Lash] is used again.

Because of its enormous size, Tahm Kench's tongue looked like a giant dragon, and it slapped Luo Xinghe's Kai'Sa hard.

Although he was invincible after being resurrected and didn't lose health, the visual effects were top-notch.

Luo Xinghe's composure completely crumbled.

"I..." Luo Xinghe opened his mouth, as if to say something.

But in the end, he didn't say anything.

He simply quit the game quietly.

The connection was forcibly disconnected.

"Victory!"

The victory sign lit up on the screens of every player on the first team.

After a deathly silence of ten seconds, the spectator hall erupted in a thunderous roar of exclamations and applause.

"My God... what did I just see?!"

"Tahm Kench... filled the entire screen?!"

"Did he go in and then come out again? The spring water didn't even evaporate instantly?!"

"Not only did he not kill him instantly, he also licked Luo Xinghe in the fountain! Although he wasn't hurt, it was extremely humiliating!"
