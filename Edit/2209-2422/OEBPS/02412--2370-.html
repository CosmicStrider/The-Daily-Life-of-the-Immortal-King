Chapter 2370 Xia Zhinu becomes an undercover agent

Wang Ling's ears turned red in that instant, like a drop of water falling into Sun Rong's heart. He always managed to stir his emotions in these unintentional ways.

The noise from the cafeteria, Chen Chao's shouts about going to the training area for extra practice that night, and Guo Hao and Li Changzhe's discussion...

In that instant, all the background noise turned into a blurry jumble of static.

For a moment, all was silent. Sun Rong could only hear her own heart pounding wildly, which almost killed her on the spot...

She didn't ask any more questions, but simply stood beside him with her eyebrows slightly curved.

She also noticed that Wang Ling was munching on instant noodles more often than usual.

Unfortunately, the seal acted like a dutiful firefighter, quickly extinguishing the unusual heartbeat and heat in its nascent stage, leaving only a complex feeling that even Wang Ling himself couldn't quite explain.

The lobby was now bustling with noise.

Aside from Zhuo Yi, no one noticed that Luo Xinghe had quietly left the scene in the corner.

Zhuo Yi stood behind the one-way glass in the second-floor monitoring room, the cup of spiritual tea in his hand steaming hot.

He looked down at Wang Ling and Sun Rong, who were surrounded in the hall below, and then glanced at Luo Xinghe's resentful back as he left. A knowing smile appeared on his lips.

"Hey, why did you have to mess with this man? Now look what happened, the shadow in your heart has expanded even further, and your mentality has collapsed, hasn't it?" He took a sip of tea.

Wang Zhencou stood in front of another screen, which displayed Xia Zhinuan's detailed report on how Luo Xinghe had instructed her to interfere with the draw and her negative behavior during the competition. The report also included the self-destructed USB drive.

"Xia Zhinuan has finally seen the error of her ways," Wang Zhen said, stroking his chin. "However, Luo Xinghe is petty and narrow-minded. He will not let this setback go unpunished."

Liu Qingyi crossed her arms and snorted coldly: "A clown jumping around. It's already merciful that Master Ling didn't completely break his mentality and make him quit the internet."

"The problem is that he doesn't see himself as a clown." Ryoko Kugimiya tapped her palm with her folding fan, her eyes sharp. "He only thinks that his master used unfair means, coupled with Xia Zhinuan's betrayal, which led to his failure. This kind of paranoia will either lead him to continue obsessively investigating, or... to use his public status to cause trouble."

Zhuo Yi put down his teacup: "The information guidance over there at Dark Eye Consulting is already done. He won't find anything real or useful; he'll only get a bunch of carefully fabricated fake information. But this might actually anger him and make him choose a more direct approach."

"For example?" Wang Zhen asked.

"For example, making sarcastic remarks during a live stream, implying that there's something fishy about the training camp, or that a certain contestant's skill level is abnormal." Zhuo Yi smiled: "He has millions of fans. Even if only one-tenth of them get caught up in the hype, it'll be trouble for Number 60 High School and for my teacher's peaceful life. Although it won't cause a big uproar, a toad on your feet won't bite, but it's disgusting."

Ryoko Kugimiya's eyes lit up: "Then, could we... give it a push?"

All eyes immediately focused on her.

"If Luo Xinghe wants to create public pressure, we'll help him make it even more convincing."

Ryoko Kugimiya swished open her folding fan, obscuring the lower half of her face and revealing only her eyes: "Then, create a scene where Ren and Rongrong have to face and overcome difficulties together. External pressure is often an excellent catalyst for internal emotions. Moreover, given Ren's personality, he would never allow Sun Rong to suffer unwarranted criticism because of him."

Wang Zhen slapped his thigh: "Brilliant! Let Luo Xinghe, the villain, become an ally of our Love Strategist Alliance without us even realizing it!"

Zhuo Yi pondered for a moment and nodded: "It can be done. But we have to be careful not to let our master and the reputation of Number 60 High School be really damaged. We also need to keep in touch with Mr. Wang Ming to ensure that online public opinion is within a controllable range."

"clear."

Ryoko Kugimiya closed her folding fan and began operating her tablet with great efficiency: "I'll go have an in-depth talk with Xia Zhinu first. She's a key witness now and the best source of information for us to understand Luo Xinghe's recent activities. By the way... give her some encouragement."

...

The small meeting room at the end of the corridor on the seventh floor of Building B in the Starry Sky Esports Center.

Xia Zhinuan sat on the sofa, holding a cup of hot water in both hands, her knuckles still slightly white. Across from her stood Zhuo Yi, whose expression was gentle, yet exuded an undeniable air of authority.

"Your honesty is very important, Xia Zhinuan."

Zhuo Yi said calmly, "It takes a lot of courage to stop a mistake in time and take responsibility. As for the training camp, considering your subsequent cooperation and performance, we can treat you leniently and not make it public for now, to observe the results."

Xia Zhinuan breathed a sigh of relief and said gratefully, "Thank you, Director Zhuo. I... I really know I was wrong. Wang Ling... he clearly saw through me, yet..." Her voice choked up again.

"Wang Ling, a student from Number 60 High School, has always been kind to others and excels in both character and academics. He never initiates arguments with anyone. After getting to know him these past few days, you should have already felt his character... He is of noble character and integrity, and never cares about worldly fame. Such a mindset is not something that ordinary high school students can have!" After Zhuo Yi finished speaking, he realized that he had gone a bit too far in praising him, so he immediately coughed twice in embarrassment.

"General Manager Zhuo is right..." Xia Zhinuan was stunned for a moment, then blinked: "But you said that Wang Ling disdains worldly fame, so what is Wang Ling's purpose in participating in this competition?"

"He just loves those instant noodles," Zhuo Yi said.

"..." This left Xia Zhinuan speechless. Although the reason sounded absurd, it was actually quite credible in Xia Zhinuan's mind. Every time she noticed Wang Ling, she would see him with a bag of instant noodles in his mouth, as if he could never finish them.

At this moment, Zhuo Yi smiled again, breaking the awkward silence: "Getting back to the point, I need your help with another matter now."

Xia Zhinuan immediately sat up straight: "Please tell me! I will do my best to make amends as much as I can!"

"Luo Xinghe won't give up. I need you to contact him proactively, out of remorse. Tell him you want to win him back, and then find out his next move."

Zhuo Yi looked at her: "Of course, it's not really about sending you back to him. We will provide you with the necessary protection and guidance; you just need to transmit messages. There will be some risks involved. Are you willing?"

Xia Zhinuan replied almost without hesitation: "I'm willing! It's what I should do. Besides..."

She lowered her head: "I also want to completely get rid of his influence on me. This incident has made me realize that there are some paths you can't blindly follow others on."

"Very good." Zhuo Yi nodded: "Jiugong will communicate with you in detail about the specifics. Remember, safety comes first. If anything seems amiss, immediately terminate the contact."
