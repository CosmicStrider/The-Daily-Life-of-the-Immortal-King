Chapter 2249 The Duo

The wind in the Netherworld Stone Forest howled as it weaved through the dark, bone-eroding crystals, carrying a chilling, deathly aura that penetrated to the very marrow.

Before the dust left behind after the spatial rift had completely dissipated, Wang Zhen had already returned to his carefree demeanor.

His fingertip lightly swept across the smart image guide terminal, saving the last record of "bone-eroding crystal bacteria".

"Boring......"

If it weren't for Wang Ling's sake, he wouldn't have bothered participating in this mission of Project Center of the Earth.

Suddenly, Wang Zhen narrowed his eyes and looked into the depths of the stone forest, where the darkness resembled solidified black ink. That was the direction that the "Soul Shatterer of the Hand of Judgment," whom he had "watched" disappear, had originally intended to explore.

A familiar scent approached from afar, accompanied by footsteps that blended almost completely into the ambient noise, causing Wang Zhen to raise an eyebrow.

Wang Zhen's usual lazy smile remained unchanged. He didn't even turn around, but said with a hint of teasing, "That skull sickle in your hand is quite interesting in this underworld ghost place. It has the same style as the magical weapon of the underworld messenger I just killed, Fang Xing."

A figure slowly emerged from the shadows.

He was just waking up.

He was wearing a uniquely styled, oversized hooded cloak, but the edges of the cloak still revealed the cuffs of the Number 60 High School uniform trousers, making him look somewhat out of place.

However, what is most striking is the bizarrely shaped weapon in his hand—a massive sickle crafted entirely from an unknown, ghastly white bone material.

The sickle blade was curved like a crescent moon, flowing with a dark luster, as if it could devour the already scarce light around it.

The handle is wrapped with dark red, vein-like patterns, and the end is inlaid with a slowly rotating, jet-black gemstone.

The dense, impenetrable death aura and soul fluctuations, like solid black flames, burned and leaped silently around the sickle, perfectly blending with the atmosphere of the Netherworld Stone Forest, yet emanating a unique and chilling pressure.

Wang Zhen's gaze lingered on the skull sickle with great interest, and he clicked his tongue twice: "Interesting. It's clearly a weapon for reaping souls, yet you've managed to make it feel almost alive. The work of those old guys in the War Sect's weapon-refining department?"

"It was originally passed down to me by my elders, but it was later improved. However, it is true that the material comes from the underworld. This organization has existed for a long time, but it has been lying dormant."

Fang Xing's face was not clearly visible in the shadows beneath his hood, but the enormous skull scythe felt as light as a feather in his hand.

Upon hearing Wang Zhen's words, the head under the cloak lifted slightly, revealing a jawline with clear lines that exuded an unusually cold and stern look.

"Wang Ming's encrypted channel."

Fang Xing's voice was no longer the clear, bright one he used to hear on campus; instead, it was deep and calm, carrying the stillness of a well that had been simmering for countless years: "He's having a blast up there. This time, the Underworld is serious; some old guys have descended to the Earth's core. But unfortunately for them, they ran into us, and Wang Ling too..."

"oh?"

Wang Zhen raised an eyebrow slightly, his face still bearing the expression that said "things have become interesting," but a hint of understanding flashed deep in his eyes.

He pointed to the spot where the [Soul Shattered] had just vanished: "Coincidentally, an overconfident old fellow came down. His bones are a bit brittle; they'd shatter at the slightest touch. He's probably at the Dao Venerable realm, and he's quite arrogant."

He spoke casually, as if he were commenting on a stale fruit.

On the skull sickle in Fang Xing's hand, the jet-black gemstone shone almost imperceptibly for a moment, as if it were actively absorbing and capturing the fragments of Dao Venerable-level soul power that had not yet completely dissipated in the air.

The dark red veins wrapped around the sickle throbbed slightly with excitement.

"Not enough."

Fang Xing's voice was calm and even, as if stating a fact: "One Hand of Judgment is just the outpost. There are bigger fish behind it. The psionic energy fluctuations of the Lord of the Underworld, Wan Shi, have already appeared in the Earth's core."

He paused, his gaze from beneath the cloak seemingly sweeping over Wang Zhen before returning to the depths of the endless black crystal forest behind Wang Zhen.

"We have to find it."

Fang Xing's tone was decisive and left no room for doubt: "You know, Wang Ling has never liked trouble. I don't think he needs to take action against an organization like this. Moreover, I also heard from Brother Wang Ming that the higher-ups have already asked Zhan Zong for help. Given Zhan Zong's style of doing things, rather than sending people to look for students who have wandered around, they are more likely to choose to strike at the heart of the problem and eliminate the source of danger directly."

"That's right." Wang Zhen gave a wry smile.

"Furthermore, as you've probably noticed, some of the creatures in the Earth's core carry rule-based seals. These seals are also closely related to the existence of the underworld."

After Fang Xing finished speaking, he lightly paused the skull sickle in his hand. The heavy bone handle struck the cold, black rocky ground with a soft "clang," which was particularly clear in the deathly silent stone forest.

The invisible ripples spread outwards from the point where the sickle fell.

The faint aura left by the [Soul Shattering] power, lingering on the surface of the bone-eroding crystals around it and emanating a low concentration of resentment, gathered like iron filings attracted by a magnet, coiling around the blade of the sickle and forming a layer of dim gray-black energy flow.

The skull sickle seemed to come alive, greedily absorbing and analyzing the remaining information.

Immediately afterwards, the spinning shadow inside the jet-black gem seemed to accelerate, projecting blurry and jumping coordinate fragments that pointed to a specific point of spiritual energy gathering deep within the stone forest.

Wang Zhen, observing the unusual movement of the skull sickle, deepened his smile, which held a knowing look and a hint of barely perceptible anticipation. "Oh? Pretty capable, huh? You followed the scent so quickly?"

He clapped his hands, brushing away non-existent dust, and walked casually to Fang Xing's side, his gaze following Fang Xing's as he looked into the absolute darkness deep within the stone forest, a darkness that seemed to devour even light.

"Fine, I'm bored anyway."

Wang Zhen stretched, his posture seemingly casual, but the languid smile in the depths of his eyes completely disappeared, replaced by an essential radiance as clear as the ice of the universe.

"Since we're going to form teams, Elder Fang..."

Wang Zhen turned his head, a playful smile curving his lips. His gaze lingered meaningfully on Fang Xing's oversized cloak and the skull sickle in his hand: "Are you... planning to stay in this form forever? I've heard... you have an even more fascinating side, and that sickle... will be a little different too?"

Fang Xing's face remained hidden in the shadows beneath his hood, unchanged.

He didn't answer directly, but his fingers gripping the skull scythe tightened slightly for a moment.

The dark light that had been flowing across the skull scythe suddenly blazed brightly!

Like a slumbering beast being disturbed, the gray-black energy flow swirling around the blade instantly became violent, emitting fine, sharp howls, like the wailing of millions of dead souls!

The jet-black gem at the end of the sickle spun at an extreme speed, almost becoming a miniature vortex that devoured light!

Fang Xing's aura showed no signs of sudden increase, yet it became as dangerous and unpredictable as a glacier about to collapse.

He took a step forward, his wide cloak billowing and rustling without wind, as if he and the sickle in his hand had become one, transforming into a terrifying blade pointing deeper into the earth's core.

"Lead the way."

A deep voice rang out, calm yet possessing a suffocating penetrating power; it was a sickle directed at him, and also addressed to Wang Zhen.

In the pitch-black, ghostly stone forest, two figures trudged through the bone-eroding crystal dust covering the ground, heading towards the core area where death and resentment were deepest.

The skull scythe hummed greedily and fiercely ahead, devouring the remaining aura of the underworld and guiding the death beacon to the underworld's lair in the earth's core.

【The Subterranean Cave (Wang Ling's Personally Built Version)】

A halo of light, interwoven with deep blue and warm yellow, flowed quietly through Wang Ling's cave base. The air was filled with the sweet scent of freshly squeezed Yu Xin juice and a faint aroma of crispy noodles and pepper.

Sun Rong sipped the cool, refreshing juice, the sweetness seemingly spreading from the tip of her tongue all the way to her heart.

Out of the corner of her eye, she glanced at Wang Ling, who was still "focused" on reading on the recliner. Even in this perilous depths of the earth, his presence brought a strange sense of peace that dispelled all gloom.

Her cheeks flushed slightly as she quietly described to Wang Ling the shapes of several strange, glowing mosses she had found along the way.

"Click".

Wang Ling tore open another packet of new flavored instant noodles with a blank expression and picked up a fan-shaped piece of the noodle.

He appeared to be reading a book, but his spiritual awareness was like an invisible giant net, silently enveloping the entire cave area and the movements further away.

"Good buddy, your standard of living is... tsk tsk."

Liu Qingyi unceremoniously took over another comfortable recliner shaped like a mineral, squinting her eyes to enjoy the environment that was so different from the outside world, while complaining about the Mountain Moving Team, "This is what a vacation should be like~ The mission to the center of the earth is too boring. If it weren't for learning from you, Master Ling... Wang Zhen and I would be too lazy to come here."

Liu Qingyi and Wang Zhen spoke exactly the same thing, which shows they are a perfect couple.

These two scions of noble families from the Divine Realm originally came to Earth to learn more about Taoist arts from Wang Ling, which is why they became ordinary cultivation college students at Number 60 High School.

Otherwise, with their qualifications, they would be qualified to be principals, and the school's history might not even be as long as either of them lived.

In the corner, the massive body of the Mountain-Moving Beast trembled slightly, and its huge single eye cautiously glanced around. It knew all too well its own position; it couldn't afford to offend anyone here.

Although Sun Rong's cultivation level was weak, the spirit sword she carried was no ordinary object, judging by the keen eye of the Mountain-Moving One. The aura of Heavenly Dao emanating from it far surpassed any spirit sword he had ever seen before. Moreover, the Mountain-Moving One could tell that this young girl had a close relationship with "Immortal Wang Ling," a fact that alone made him tremble with fear.

Liu Qingyi's strength goes without saying; she could easily handle it.

The only one who is a little weaker is the girl who speaks with a Sun Island accent... However, these three girls seem to have a very good relationship, so the Mountain Moving Sect doesn't dare to provoke the Nine Palaces at all.

Was it because it stayed in the Earth's core for too long?

Why are all the cultivators on Earth so incredibly powerful these days?

At this moment, with a hint of awe and curiosity, Kugimiya Ryoko was using her image-collecting terminal to scan and record the equipment, materials, and layout of the cave that Wang Ling had "casually" created.

She discovered that many of the alloys and energy circuits here were far beyond the scope of the technology trees currently known on Earth, and the spiritual waves emitted by the intelligent control core were even more exquisite.

"This... is simply a fortress ahead of its time..."

She murmured to herself, overwhelmed with shock.

Everything inside the cave was peaceful and warm.

The story of the Crystal Forest adventure brought by Sun Rong, Liu Qingyi's teasing, Jiu Gong Liangzi's exclamations, and the busy humming of the mountain-moving workers create a "vacation" scene that is completely different from the dangers outside.

However, this calm is merely a ripple in the eye of the storm.

Meanwhile, hundreds of miles away from the cave—

Deep within the Netherworld Stone Forest, the deathly black crystals are no longer a backdrop, but have transformed into a suffocating prison.

The darkness was like thick ink, almost swallowing all light.

The air no longer flows, but is frozen with the despair and resentment accumulated over millions of years.

Wang Zhen and Fang Xing walked one after the other on the rugged ground covered with crystal dust, their footsteps as light as ghosts.

Fang Xing walked in front.

The skull sickle in his hand was no longer a silent decoration; it was undergoing a chilling transformation—countless dark gold runes climbed onto the originally pale bone, pulsating like blood vessels and radiating a deathly pressure purer than the surrounding bone-eroding crystals.

Especially the jet-black gem at the end of the sickle, whose swirling vortex has transformed into a bottomless miniature black hole, greedily sucking up every trace of resentment and remnant mark of the underworld that permeates the stone forest.

It was like a hungry hunting dog, its deep hum transforming into a silent, soul-piercing shriek, fixedly pointing in a specific direction in the darkness.

The buzzing wasn't a sound, but rather it reverberated directly in the minds of Wang Zhen and Fang Xing—the prey was right in front of them, and there was a huge number of them!

"Resentment is gathering..." Fang Xing's deep voice echoed in the darkness, colder than before, as if devoid of any human warmth.

Beneath the wide hood, a faint, eerie green light seemed to flash and disappear, like will-o'-the-wisps flickering.

"That's quite a force to be reckoned with."

Wang Zhen's playful smile faded considerably, and his eyes sharpened like knives as he scanned the area ahead. "Looks like we've stirred up a hornet's nest. The Underworld has been operating here... for quite some time now."

He no longer concealed his scrutinizing gaze, which emanated from a higher plane, but instead looked in the direction locked by the skull sickle—a node in the Earth's core space distorted by immense deathly resentment, a... hidden outpost of the Earth's core underworld!

The atmosphere in the core hall of the Palm Cliff Earth Base is as tense as a taut bowstring.

Teng Luchen's eyes were bloodshot as he stared intently at the faint dot on the main screen representing "Wang Ling's blurred coordinates" and another large red dot representing "the energy bursting forth from the Netherworld Stone Forest".

His withered fingers unconsciously rubbed the alloy cracks on the edge of the control panel—the handprints left in his rage.

"Report! The War Sect has responded! They have confirmed that they have dispatched a crack force, led by Elder White Sheath, which is expected to arrive at the deep space anchor point in about three incense sticks' time. They request that we activate the guidance beacon!" The communications officer's voice was filled with excitement and a hint of awe.

"White Sheath?!" Jing Heqiu couldn't help but exclaim in a low voice, "Elder Bai has actually come in person?!"

Teng Luchen's eyes flashed with a sharp light, and he immediately ordered: "Prepare immediately! Activate the highest-level guidance beacon! Ensure the White Elder's fleet arrives safely! At the same time..."

His voice suddenly rose, "All recoverable detection units, focus on the Netherworld Stone Forest area! Suppress all movement in that hellhole! Once Elder Bai arrives, that will be their primary target!"

Just then, Wang Ming opened the independent terminal screen, the red light flashing, and a pop-up encrypted communication window with the highest privileges appeared. He quickly typed, "The base will restore monitoring immediately. You guys need to finish this quickly."

Recipient: Fang Xing.

After sending all this, Wang Ming's eyes sharpened, and his fingertips left afterimages on the keyboard, encrypting the information and synchronizing it to the main console. He then put on an expression of great distress: "Elder Teng, breaking news! A fierce battle has broken out at a suspected underworld stronghold, with strong psionic energy fluctuations."

"What the heck?"

Fujiro Chen was stunned.

Damn it...

In this inner world, there isn't a single peaceful place...

Teng Luchen's breathing became heavy instantly: "Dean Jing! Draw up a reserve team, led by Zhuo Yi, to prioritize finding and rescuing the group of students who were separated from Sun Rong and Wang Ling! We must bring them back safely before a larger-scale conflict erupts in the Earth's core!"

The immense guilt stemming from the misjudgment has now transformed into profound anxiety for the safety of the students.

Where is that little mascot right now in the storm?

Hopefully, he won't be swept away by the eerie winds of the underworld!

Fujiro Chen prayed in his heart.

He also expressed his deepest apologies to Wang Ling.

Wang Ling, my dear classmate... I'm so sorry!

The light was dim inside the deep, winding cave passages.

Li Changzhe half-carried and half-supported Chen Chao as they struggled to move forward on the rugged, rocky ground.

Guo Hao cautiously observed his surroundings, the talisman in his hand ready to be activated at any moment.

Zhang Linyan walked at the front, her sharp eyes scanning the passage ahead and the top of the rock wall like a falcon, her longbow resting loosely on her arm, the bowstring slightly taut.

"Hiss... Brother Li, thank you so much."

Chen Chao grimaced. The wound on his calf had stopped bleeding after being treated with herbs and Guo Hao's emergency talisman, but the pain was still excruciating. "If it weren't for you and Senior Zhang, we would have really died in that lava pit."

"Don't be so polite! We're all brothers!"

Li Changzhe, panting, spoke with a strong sense of loyalty in his Beijing accent, "Besides, all of you kids from Number 60 High School are top-notch! When we find that kid Wang Ling, we'll give him a good beating and make up for it!"

"Find Wang Ling..." Guo Hao interrupted anxiously, "Brother Li, Senior Zhang, you two are experienced, this inner world is too damn weird. Just now, that wave... did you feel it? Even though it was far away, my legs went weak! Wang Ling was already badly injured, and then he was thrown to an unknown place, I'm really scared of him..."

He dared not say any more.

Zhang Linyan slowed her pace slightly, her clear, cool voice echoing in the cave, carrying a hint of certainty: "The energy source is very far away, in a different location. At least we're not currently on that thing's path. As for Wang Ling..."

She paused for a moment: "Sun Rong, Jiu Gong Liangzi and Liu Qingyi's auras appeared faintly in another direction before. The fluctuations were brief but strong, and they may have already converged, or even... may have found a safe temporary camp."

"Really? That's great!"

Upon hearing this, Chen Chao's spirits lifted, as if the pain in his wound had lessened considerably. "They're both quite capable. I'll feel much more at ease if they find Wang Ling first!"

"Brother Wang and I have a long-standing friendship. He was particularly lucky during the selection test."

Li Changzhe also chimed in, his tone a mix of teasing and wistfulness: "He's a lucky man! If you feel you're all right, let's try to find our way in their direction! I can sense Sun Rong's spiritual energy mark here; it must have been left by her on purpose."

Guo Hao nodded, feeling slightly relieved, but a deeper unease quietly spread through him—what exactly was that power that could terrify even a nascent spirit beast in an instant?

Why does it feel... somewhat like the faint fluctuations that Wang Ling experienced during the stress test, which suddenly subsided?

Could it be...?

He shook his head violently, suppressing this absurd and insane thought.

Impossible! Wang Ling is just a Foundation Establishment stage mascot that needs protection!

It must be that the damn pressure at the Earth's core is too great, and I'm hallucinating.

The four continued walking in silence, with only the sounds of their footsteps and breathing echoing in the deep passage.

...

Chen Chao and Guo Hao, grimacing, were supported by Li Changzhe and Zhang Linyan on either side, and finally, following the faint spiritual marks left by Sun Rong, they stumbled through the last rugged rock passage.

When they stepped into the dark blue energy barrier, the sight before them made them instantly forget their injuries and exhaustion. Their mouths gaped open and their eyes widened in astonishment.

"I...I oh my god..." Guo Hao's jaw almost dropped to the ground.

He squeezed out a few words in a dry voice, his mind blank, only able to utter the most primal exclamation.

Even Li Changzhe, the well-informed captain of Jingmen Number 8 High School, completely lost his composure at this moment. His eyes quickly swept across the entire space: the ceiling was a flowing, realistic projection of a dazzling starry sky, and soft light shone down, illuminating the entire hall.

The surrounding walls are made of unknown luminescent crystals, with a warm and ever-flowing luster;

Beneath your feet is an incredibly comfortable spiritual carpet; its temperature automatically adjusts as you step on it, dispelling the chill of the earth's core.

The energy chair suspended in the corner has smooth lines and exudes a sense of futuristic technology;

The most outrageous thing is that there is a transparent bar that is floating there and automatically extracting some kind of spiritual fruit!

At the bar, Sun Rong, Liu Qingyi, and Jiu Gong Liangzi were sitting comfortably on the energy chairs, sipping their bright green juice.
