Chapter 2203 The scam begins

Lao Huang doesn't know what happened specifically. But he seems to have become the "spokesperson" of Wang Ling inexplicably.

In fact, he can feel the extraordinaryness of Wang Ling. And he can feel an inexplicable sense of intimacy in this young man.

And this feeling is not the kind of emotion that an elder brother has for his younger brother. This made Lao Huang a little embarrassed for a time, because when he faced Wang Ling, he really had the mentality of an old father.

How can anyone recognize his father as soon as he comes up? This is very rude. Besides, he knows that Wang Ling and Sun Rong are not ordinary high school students in the Foundation Building.

Lao Huang couldn't help but smile bitterly to himself. He was supposed to be conducting a practical training survey of high school students in the Foundation Establishment stage, but it turned out that he was the one who had come to do the internship.

Based on this natural intimacy and invisible tacit understanding. In addition, Lao Huang has an old father's mentality. He did not expose Wang Ling. Instead, he chose to cooperate and protect.

He cleared his throat and asked Huang Tingjian directly: "Do you dare swear to God that the information in this scroll is truly reliable?"

"Absolutely reliable. Senior." Huang Tingjian replied respectfully: "This information originally came from the hands of Zhi Jiunian. I have also checked it in private. There is no falsehood."

The scroll contained the coordinates of the common landing spots used by those stowaway aliens. The information was indeed reliable, but Huang Tingjian couldn't understand why this senior Huang needed to be affiliated with a company to earn a living. Therefore, he believed there must be a special purpose behind it, but the shady operations and thoughts of these powerful figures were beyond the comprehension of a junior like him.

As Huang Tingjian who has accumulated certain experience in the cultivation circle and is also a senior headhunter, Huang Tingjian understands the world of people better than anyone else.

He rolled his eyes. Then he suddenly faced behind him. He waved his sleeves and a pink mist spread out from his sleeves. Then it spread quickly.

The large group of system-ability cultivators in the rear were unable to react in time. Each one of them seemed to be in a fog. The holes gradually turned pink.

Wang Ling knows. This is an illusion. An illusion that can erase memories based on psychological suggestion.

"What happened here today. No one will remember that Senior Huang brought two high school students here... No one will remember what happened..." Huang Tingjian spoke to the people behind him. He spoke in an ethereal way. The voice was read silently three times.

Those people behind him fainted on the spot.

Then, he faced Lao Huang again and clasped his fists: "Senior Huang, please rest assured, I have taken care of everything. After these people wake up, none of them will remember seeing you. I know that you must have had some deeper meaning in your thoughts, so I don't want to ask too many questions. Also, I will not mention today's events to anyone. If you are worried, please erase my memory."

"..."

Lao Huang didn't expect Huang Tingjian to be so capable. But in fact, he really didn't have any so-called deep meaning. The original idea of coming here was just to find a reliable and high-paying job.

He was silent for a moment. He winked at Wang Ling and Sun Rong. Then he continued to raise his posture and asked: "You are very sensible. Little Huang. So, what are the questions of my two little friends? I wonder if you can also answer it?"

"This... is natural... I will certainly speak frankly and without reservation..." Huang Tingjian said.

Sun Rong glanced at Wang Ling. She quickly seized the opportunity and asked: "Is the system capability thing real? Does the system really exist in this world?"

This question is obviously a bit out of scope. And it's obviously not that Huang Tingjian doesn't know the answer. It's just that for some reason he can't directly answer Sun Rong's question.

Seeing Huang Tingjian's reaction, Wang Ling immediately understood. It was clear that Huang Tingjian was afraid of something; he was respectful because of Huang's level of cultivation. Just as Wang Ling knew the true inside story about the system's ability users and the powerful figures behind it, Huang Tingjian found himself in a situation where he dared not offend either side.

Because he couldn't tell which of the two sides was stronger or weaker.

At this time, keeping silent is the best choice.

"m sorry, Senior Huang, but there are some things... I'm caught in the middle, and it's not easy for me to handle them." Huang Tingjian looked embarrassed.

Lao Huang was not a person who liked to make things difficult for others. He did not continue to force Huang Tingjian. Instead, he turned around and left with the scroll.

Sun Rong also knows. In this situation, there is indeed no need to continue asking.

She thought of many things in an instant. She had many thoughts: "It seems that there is indeed something hidden behind these system ability users."

Wang Ling put his hands in his pocket. He looked at Sun Rong. He tilted his head. The breeze blew his bangs: "What do you think? Tell me?"

"I feel that there is no real system capability in this world. In other words, these so-called systems are preset scams from the beginning."

Sun Rong sighed: "Of course. I don't have any evidence. This is... a girl's intuition! Hmm! Intuition!"

"But there's one thing I haven't figured out. If these system capabilities are all a scam, then why does the Universe Infinite Group still recruit these people in the name of recruiting talented people?"

"The cultivation talents of these people are very mediocre. They have achieved this level by relying on the so-called system. When they were just in the factory, I thought their combat experience was not as good as our classmates from Number 60 High School."

"I think when Chen Chao and Guo Hao come out of retreat with their master from Huishuo Mountain, I'm afraid they will be separated by several levels."

"Miss Sun's words are indeed rough words. These system ability users are indeed empty-headed. Their own cultivation quality is not high." It's not just Sun Rong. Even Lao Huang has the same view.

From the system ability users they've encountered so far, Wang Ling, Sun Rong, and Lao Huang can clearly sense the weaknesses in these users... and this feeling is very similar to those rich kids who rely on drugs to constantly build up their abilities and achieve rapid growth in their cultivation level in a short period of time—a feeling of being all show and no substance.

After all, in the modern world of cultivation, it is not difficult to rely on modern technology and hard work to improve one's realm. Monks with extraordinary talents like Wang Ling are ultimately a very small minority among a very small number.

In order to provide their children with better educational resources, families with the means will have their children become dependent on medicine from birth. With all sorts of high-quality tonics being added to their bodies, they are like balloons that keep inflating in a short period of time. As for when they will burst, that is anyone's guess.
